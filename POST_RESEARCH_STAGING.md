# Post-research staging: mastery, strategic judgment, and practical contribution

Accepted direction: 25 October 2026
Status: agreed roadmap to apply after the first substantive Robotics Economics research edition. This is NOT a new automatically activated research goal or a requirement to wait for the original seven-day window.

## Purpose and destination

Use the initial report, working interactive dashboard, models, and subsequent evidence to build three connected outcomes:

1. **Intellectual mastery:** Amar can independently explain, derive, challenge and apply important technical and economic mechanisms of robotic deployment.
2. **Strategic judgment:** He can advise on which physical-labor end markets and value-chain stages to enter, with which embodiments, capability/supply conditions, fleet designs, customer organizations and commercial approaches, and when.
3. **Practical contribution:** Produce distinctive, evidence-grounded analyses and potentially a functioning coordination/decision system relevant to frontier robotics organizations.

The research should inform his prospective robotics strategy/deployment career, but not be reverse-engineered to justify a predetermined employer or immediate startup. Founding or owning AI-native industrial businesses remains a possible future outcome, NOT a requirement.

## Seven stages after the first verified research edition

**Stage 1 - Personal comprehension and critical review.** Read the integrated report and exercise the dashboard in guided weekend sessions; reconstruct its main assumptions and conclusions, work through bottlenecks and mathematical mechanisms, identify technical learning gaps, and explain the claims in Amar's own terms. Weekend emphasis is a practical preference, not a rigid schedule.

**Stage 2 - Thesis register.** Extract a small number of consequential, falsifiable deployment, scaling, data, coordination, adoption and ownership theses. For each record: mechanism, evidence and scope, strongest objections, uncertainty, decision implications, and what would change our view. Do not treat speculative examples as established findings.

**Stage 3 - Research by value of information.** Prioritize unresolved questions by which findings would most change understanding or deployment choices, not by sequential completion of the existing 55 questions. Launch focused research waves or empirical tests; incorporate failures and contrary evidence.

**Stage 4 - Decision system evolution.** Improve the dashboard and common calculation engine into a strategic deployment/portfolio tool relating capability, supply, supported fleets, customer economics and organizations, learning, penetration, and value capture. Keep evidence, scenario assumptions and unknown inputs distinct.

**Stage 5 - Practitioner validation.** With Amar's approval, use strong theses to formulate targeted conversations with researchers, deployment executives, operators and founders. Ask about intervention burden, commissioning, maintenance, economics, learning, integration, commercial contracts and bottlenecks. Separate firsthand observations from views and sales claims. Practitioner conversations are not automatically authorized outreach.

**Stage 6 - Robotics career application.** In parallel where appropriate, distill 2-3 exceptional decision-oriented analyses from the research for substantive dialogue with frontier humanoid/robotics labs, fleet/orchestration organizations or deployment businesses. Let findings inform employer/role selection; discuss opportunities without disclosing confidential employer material or publishing private work without approval.

**Stage 7 - Original practical contribution.** Select a research-backed problem and test a method or working prototype, for example a physical-digital fleet simulator with robots, agents, human supervisors, enterprise scheduling, failures and resource budgets. Define a falsifiable economic/control claim and simpler baselines. Ixion implementation or other-repository modifications require separate authorization.

Stages can overlap. Do not force outreach, thesis publication or implementation before adequate evidence. Never require a seven-day wait if the first edition passes its substantive acceptance criteria earlier. After the initial campaign, preserve a multi-month continuing roadmap and evidence-refresh triggers.
