## How to update the register

For each substantive new claim record the exact proposition, assumptions, proof or proof gap, strongest counterexample, closest prior mechanism, empirical discriminator, supporting files and observed input revision. Use “conditional result,” “conjecture,” “rejected,” “unidentified,” or “deferred” precisely. Numerical agreement alone is not a proof; a failed model may still clarify which premise matters. Do not promote the table's research questions into established claims.

## Installed obligations and the choice of operating environments

**Conditional result, November 25.** An additional site can reveal an otherwise missed diagnostic and require fresh checks on discoveries the operator would already have made. With fixed fleet size, current margins, search budget and feasible validation time, independent observation opportunities and a distinct additive check per environment, the increment from another environment falls with existing variety. Raising ordinary capture weakly reduces the smallest optimal number of environments, while reducing the cost of each check weakly raises it. The [derivation and assumptions](../autonomous_firm/The_Production_of_Capability.md#how-varied-should-the-operating-fleet-be) give the discrete proof. A site adding no new obligation removes the cost term at that margin. Two sites supplying complementary pieces of evidence can reverse diminishing returns. The earlier fleet-learning and transfer models price additional evidence. Here the same decision also changes the feasible uses of future discoveries.

The [field design](../autonomous_firm/The_Production_of_Capability.md#a-field-test-of-the-installed-base-mechanism) distinguishes discovery on held-out jobs from acceptance under live support obligations. It compares the last user of a configuration with another user of one that remains supported. The [replication](../autonomous_firm/replication/capability/installed_base.py) also compares fleet composition with direct enumeration of the evidence states. The original production and capital manuscript was read at [ed3b9fe](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/tree/ed3b9fec57cd87a5d953d06491553d5a3a826a21). The opening theory-week register was read at [ebc1a3c](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/tree/ebc1a3c02e4d4e3f43c2f721fe2cd4efb22ab035). ## How deployments select successive hardware generations

**Conditional results, November 25.** When a field report can change the next tooling choice, an unusual customer's extra probability of reporting in time is worth the incremental value of the resulting factory decision. The [site cutoff](../autonomous_firm/FULL_STACK_FIRM.md#when-a-different-customer-earns-the-next-deployment) is strict only inside a range of net switching costs. A common acceleration in reporting first increases and then decreases the advantage of the faster source if both sites eventually diagnose the same constraint. If silence is informative, use the posterior of every possible report. If a trained policy can itself be transferred, value that benefit even when the factory is already committed.

The [three-vintage solution](../autonomous_firm/FULL_STACK_FIRM.md#a-future-customers-data-rights-change-the-first-deployment) chooses sites, model training, tooling and service on both generations. Permission to reuse traces at a future customer can reverse the initial deployment. Under illustrative payoffs the first specialized build becomes more likely and the second less likely. The [pricing extension](../autonomous_firm/FULL_STACK_FIRM.md#how-much-future-demand-changes-an-earlier-machine) solves a buyer’s participation and reuse terms and gives a strict cutoff in the anticipated value of reaching it. Equivalent access on both hardware paths eliminates that particular earlier design premium. Classical information value supplies the single-decision benchmark. Here the firm creates the set of future experiments and offers by choosing embodied equipment.

[Exact checks](../autonomous_firm/replication/capability/full_stack_vintages.py) enumerate contingent plans and test the analytical comparisons. The [field design](../autonomous_firm/FULL_STACK_FIRM.md#what-would-distinguish-the-theory-in-a-fleet) follows otherwise comparable signals on both sides of known tooling commitments, including canceled orders, buyer refusals and old-vintage service. Analysis begins from the [existing full-stack manuscript](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/b784b262d7503a8baae573d4ff49efcaba48193c/papers/autonomous_firm/FULL_STACK_FIRM.md). The commercial magnitude and equilibrium response of rival suppliers remain unidentified.

## Complementary source portfolios and endogenous manufacturing

**Conditional results, November 25.** [Deployment portfolios and the next robot](../autonomous_firm/DEPLOYMENT_PORTFOLIOS_AND_DESIGN.md) closes the complementary-evidence exception flagged above and solves a further part of the full stack firm's allocation. | Claim | Assumptions and proof | Strongest boundary or counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A source with zero standalone target value can have positive value alongside another source | Independent Gaussian target and shared nuisance; known reference and mixed measurements; quadratic design loss. Gaussian conditioning gives the exact positive source interaction. | Unrelated nuisances, an already sufficient ordinary contrast, a cheaper equivalent experiment, or a decision unaffected by the target parameter. |
| Better precision at one source can raise demand for a different source | Fixed recipient exposure and costs; repeated independent measurement noise with a stable shared nuisance. Differentiate the marginal contrast value with respect to the first source's noise. | A nuisance independently redrawn with known mean removes the precision floor. Changing deadlines, demand or recipient survival need a new calculation. |
| Learning value depends on the manufacturing quantity it induces | Risk-neutral firm chooses design and continuous quantity after evidence; specified quadratic mismatch and expansion costs. Exact positive-part normal moments yield the optimized value and variance derivative. | Hardware committed before the signal, finite units, hard demand caps, nonlinear costs, or binding finance require the corresponding feasible program. |
| Neither source need pay alone for the pair to pay | Feasible simultaneous source menu, additive dated net source costs and the preceding continuation value. Compare all four portfolios. | Shared support obligations or cash/access constraints can make the pair costly or infeasible. |
| Current hardware can be chosen solely for cheaper later inquiry | Same direct service; priced onboard and equivalent external contrast; both alternatives buy selectively after the ordinary signal. The difference in conditional inquiry options gives the hardware premium. | An equivalent external option at the same cost erases the premium; a fully learned stable state gives no perpetual dividend. |
| Better information can raise value while reducing expected deployment | The replication gives an exact two-state counterexample with a hard demand cap, quadratic mismatch and expansion costs. | In the stated uncapped Gaussian model expected manufacture instead weakly increases; neither sign is an industry-wide theorem. |

These are applications of established Bayesian experimental-design, dual-control and investment principles, not a generic novelty claim. The distinctive research connection is between source interpretation, the experiment technology embodied in a current machine, and the design and scale of the next cohort. ## Shared-hour evidence reallocation and cross-market capital returns

**Conditional result, November 25.** [When a better source robot teaches another fleet less](EVIDENCE_REALLOCATION.md) proves that with substitutable recipient-specific evidence, scarce common hours, an exhausted incident pool, and positive reference work and service, the sign of recipient effective evidence is the sign of B' + (g/e-1)I'. A higher success rate can release hours and yet reduce transferable evidence. When source activity is optimized, the expansion response can overturn even a positive fixed-exposure sign. A binding robot-stage constraint transmits this to target physical MPK_r; profitability-bound entry may transmit it to the installation response, but other gates can prevent it. A competent fixed contingent procedure implements the same optimization; no necessary integration or recursive premium follows. The model uses standard concave allocation and implicit differentiation, not a new general theorem. The alternative evidence technology, shared-hour incidence, costs, and joint demand/qualification require matched evidence; no industrial coefficient is estimated. [Code](evidence_reallocation.js) and [results](evidence_reallocation_results.json) check the model and interior derivative against nested reoptimization. Next: non-substitutable evidence, portable inquiry and induced changes in demand and captured returns.
