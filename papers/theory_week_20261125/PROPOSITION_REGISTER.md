## How to update the register

For each substantive new claim record the exact proposition, assumptions, proof or proof gap, strongest counterexample, closest prior mechanism, empirical discriminator, supporting files and observed input revision. Use “conditional result,” “conjecture,” “rejected,” “unidentified,” or “deferred” precisely. Numerical agreement alone is not a proof; a failed model may still clarify which premise matters. Do not promote the table's research questions into established claims.
