# When a better source robot teaches another fleet less

25 November 2026 Eastern — theory-week synthesis, conditional derivation and synthetic CPU check. Input main 97c709f527e7312cc4023a3266db7753b0cdc522; production f25390d784b9721043b2eebe0eeb79b7f31f7ace; recursive firm ec919bd95c52521051414b5f99315854b9700879.

## Economic premises

A robot earns by delivering accepted work. Its failed attempts can additionally expose useful discrepancies; a technician can recover the order, inspect the discrepancy, or investigate a different source. Better execution changes both the time available for inquiry and the stock of cases worth investigating. In a mixed fleet the relevant question is not merely whether better execution generates fewer incidents. It is whether the operator can replace the *marginal transferable content* of those incidents with the scarce resources that execution releases, after accounting for other work competing for those resources. This links the evidence-supply problem, the economic value of shared support, the next installation and the production network.

The common [learning and capital paper](../learning_and_capital/README.md) has a locally fixed learning matrix L. [Production](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/f25390d784b9721043b2eebe0eeb79b7f31f7ace/research/theory_integration_2026_11_17/data_value/WHEN_CAPABILITIES_COMPACT_DRAFT.md) specifies dated qualified menus. The latter's [boundary study](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/ec919bd95c52521051414b5f99315854b9700879/research/recursive_firm/boundaries/README.md) compares integrated authority with feasible partners. ## 1. Allocation and timing

In one source window p is first-attempt accepted success probability, x is attempted source jobs, with 0<p<1 and deterministic fluid expected quantities (not an exact queueing model). A source attempt uses h(p)=h0+h1(1-p) shared hours, h0>0, h1>=0. These hours cover routine handling and recovery of failed attempts; failed source attempts contribute zero to the counted first-pass service in this restriction. If later recovery pays, its actual accepted order and cost must enter S below, not an extra acceptance count. Total shared time is H, and B=H-h(p)x>=0 remains. Lawful, eligible, nonduplicate incident review opportunities occupy at most I=r(1-p)x hours, where r converts incident counts into admissible review-hours. The original data already exist; processing time is still scarce. For this local calculation r and yields are fixed. Reference work can be a bench test, independent audit or deliberate sampling of normal work. It uses a different input stream and has no immediate service payoff here.

Allocate i hours to incident review, a to reference work, y to other currently accepted service (one unit per hour in normalized target-service units). Constraints: i<=I, i+a+y<=B, all nonnegative. The recipient's effective validated evidence increment is m=g i+e a, g>e>0, with units of a specified local learning input per hour. The strict ranking applies only to this recipient and starting corpus, not to every incident or modality. The same shared review capacity can deliver recipient service today. Let U(y) and W(m) be twice differentiable, strictly increasing, strictly concave net value functions, with strictly negative second derivatives in the interior. W is an optimized continuation value conditional on m (including ordinary future learning), not a second premium on future services already counted. For a private decision use captured values; for a joint benchmark use real net values. The complete window objective is S(p,x)+U(y)+W(m). S includes source current contribution and costs outside the explicitly allocated hours. The evidence costs are the foregone hours and any included real resource costs; no additional duplicate hourly charge. We suppress heterogeneous learning delays for this window and require all evidence arrive before the same relevant recipient decision.

## 2. The effective-resource result

Because g>e and W increases, a positive reference allocation can coexist only with exhaustive eligible incident review, i=I (otherwise shift one hour of a to i). When i=I, a>0, y>0, the budget is exactly

    y + m/e = E = B + (g/e-1)I.

E has units of effective reference-hours. It is not a new capital stock. The incident pool augments the attainable budget because its processing is more productive at this recipient than reference work. In the mixed interior, U'(y)=e W'(m). Strict concavity gives a unique optimum. Differentiating the constraint and FOC,

    dm*/dE = e U''/(U''+e^2 W'') > 0, and dy*/dE = e^2 W''/(U''+e^2 W'') > 0.

Thus for a change in source reliability, with x fixed or allowed to change, the sign of the recipient learning response is exactly the sign of

    E' = B' + (g/e-1)I'.

This is the net released shared time after replacing the lost *relative* evidence productivity. At fixed x, B'=h1 x and I'=-r x. Hence m rises precisely when h1 > (g/e-1)r, falls under the reverse inequality and is locally constant at equality. Both economies can have the same improvement in source accepted service px. The relevant difference is the time actually released and the effective incident/reference yield ratio. If source demand is already satisfied at D, x=D/p and E'=D[h0+h1-(g/e-1)r]/p^2, while source accepted quantity stays D. That is a separate operating regime.

The strict sign is not global: if incident review is the only active evidence technology and its pool is slack, a rise in B with fixed yields raises (weakly at corners) attainable evidence; if the pool binds and no reference work is bought, m=gI. For a precise finite calculation eliminate i,a by the minimum-hours frontier: t(m)=m/g until m=g min(B,I); if B>I, thereafter t(m)=I+(m-gI)/e. Optimize U(B-t(m))+W(m) over feasible m. Its concavity follows from convex t, increasing concave U and concave W. This handles zero service, no reference, zero incident and binding-pool cases without extrapolating an interior derivative. At a corner use finite differences or directional derivatives.

## 3. Endogenous operating allocation can reverse even the resource-release criterion

Put alpha=g/e-1 and k(p)=h0+(h1-alpha r)(1-p). In the mixed interior E=H-k(p)x. Let V(E) be the optimized U+W value there; V'=U'>0, V''<0. With source activity freely selected within x<=min(K,D/p,H/h), take S(p,x)=(vp-c)x-d x^2/2, d>0, a reduced-form decreasing marginal net contribution from additional original source jobs. This is not an estimated demand curve. The reduced objective S+V(H-kx) is strictly concave locally. At an interior optimum away from any switching surface,

    vp-c-dx = k V',
    dx*/dp = [v-k' V' + k k' x V''] / [d-k^2 V''],
    dE*/dp = -k'x-k dx*/dp.

Proof: differentiate the FOC vp-c-dx-k V'=0, using E'=-k'x-kx'; denominator is positive. For k>0 and h1>alpha r, the fixed-exposure gain -k'x>0 can be more than offset when the firm expands lucrative source work. More efficient operation crowds out recipient learning by making source production more attractive, not necessarily by making observations intrinsically worse. This is a signed policy-mediated term in the week's controlled transition. Conversely shrinking the source can release time and make more reference evidence attainable. The source expansion is an economic decision, not an exogenously fixed scaling exponent. Concavity of the full reduced feasible objective follows from the concave joint allocation problem with affine resource constraints at fixed p and strictly concave S; the one-dimensional solver respects all regimes.

Under U= u log(1+y), W=w log(1+m), the mixed optimum m=(e w(1+E)-u)/(u+w) when feasible. Synthetic examples (r=.5,g=3,e=1,x=10,p=.80 to .81) start at B=8,I=1,m=5. For h1=.5 the next m=4.975; h1=1.5 gives 5.025. Both gain .1 source accepted unit. The model also solves an interior endogenous x example with h1=1.5, h0=.5,H=8,v=.8,c=0,d=.08,u=w=1. At p=.8, x*=5.71686, m*=2.28494. Fixed-exposure derivative dm/dp=+1.42921, but dx*/dp=10.67097 and optimized dm*/dp=-1.77208 (centered numerical estimate -1.77194). Current source productivity improves, reference hours can be purchased, yet allocation redirects the freed resources. Units are synthetic. No benchmark-to-currency conversion.

## 4. From evidence to marginal product and deployment propagation

Suppose a later target stage has a locally validated rate A(m) with A'>0, and accepted work is min(D_T,A(m)K_T,H_T) at a given dated service standard. Away from ties the marginal physical product of the next unit of K_T is A(m) when the robot stage binds (for an infinitesimal addition) and zero when another stage binds. In the robot-bound regime its derivative with respect to source reliability is A'(m) m'_p. If demand/support binds it is zero locally, and the discrete change across a bottleneck can be opposite in sign. An increase in m can exhaust remaining demand and reduce the finite MPK of the next machine; compare complete schedules. This mechanism is a cross-site derivative, not the source robot's own physical marginal product.

For the common induced-deployment model let the next target starts be min(νF(v_T(m)), W_T/m_cash(m), S_T(m)), with symbols as in the common paper. If private profitability binds, local response is ν F'(v_T) v_T' m'_p; if cash-time or commissioning binds, only the corresponding derivative applies, including other state changes. A negative recipient learning derivative can create a negative cross-market installation response even with no negative transfer per admitted sample. A positive learning derivative need not add a single machine. With changed prices, rival actions or funds include all derivatives through the same state/decision system. Do not use the nonnegative spectral threshold for this signed response without further assumptions.

A firm that can redesign the learning procedure can change g, e, r or the information law: for example a retained reference procedure may raise e and reduce the incident premium g/e-1. A fixed competent state-contingent organization with identical feasible reference technology, hours and access implements exactly this optimizer. Recursion requires evidence of an economically justified change in that feasible technology/procedure, net of its design and upkeep costs; reallocation alone is not evidence of recursive superiority. Outsourcing an equivalent timely test at equal full opportunity cost replicates the relevant option. If it uses a separate scarce resource, include its price and limits rather than treating it as equivalent by assumption. A rival capturing some of the recipient return changes the decision maker's W (and potentially the active allocation regime); e.g. replacing W by a positive fraction of W lowers optimal m under concavity, but leaves the mixed-interior E sign conditional on remaining in that regime. Endogenous prices and competitive responses changing W itself add a separate term to the FOC derivative and are not signed here.

## 5. What to observe

In repetitive parcel work, compare original offered jobs, attempt/recovery burden, retained incident-review opportunities, independent reference work and support assignments. In automotive manufacturing, distinguish mechanical failures from calibration and fixture causes. For inspection/maintenance the independent reference may require costly ground truth after the event; an image is not automatically an informative label. For digital exception handling a routing improvement may suppress cases and release reviewer time while expanding cheap digital service. These are mechanism hypotheses, not estimated coefficients.

Test a matched source improvement with independently budgeted ordinary repeats vs deliberate reference, on comparable original offered jobs including zero-output shifts; hold downstream design/fixture/calibration/control decision rule fixed, record full hours, rights and test/qualification costs. Cross fixed source workload with flexible workload under preassigned capacity and order conditions. Measure g/e by the *same target decision and starting corpus*, not disparate benchmark averages; if evidence is complementary or cannot substitute, the effective-hours formula is invalid. Observe actual acceptance and subsequent target marginal capacity experiments under common demand; record the active adoption constraint and expired work. Distinguish a fixed reference rule from a learned portable rule on new sites. Compare a cheap external direct test. The claimed sign mechanism is rejected if effective-hours changes do not explain allocation in the specified mixed regime after matching the information law, price and constraints. Dynamic feedback from recipient adoption to source opportunity and strategic evidence capture is the next proof gap.
