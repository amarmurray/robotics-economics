# The production of capability and the allocation of robotic capital

*Opening theoretical scaffold for the November 25–December 2 RoboEcon week. This is a model specification and research agenda, not a claim that every proposed mechanism has been proved.*

## 1. The economic premises

The firm assembles different kinds of productive inputs. A physical machine, a commissioning crew and an operator occupy a place and a time. A validated policy or diagnostic procedure can be reused across compatible tasks, subject to permissions and qualification. A record is generated by an action in an environment; the actions selected by an increasingly capable system change the records it will encounter next. A customer's demand and acceptance conditions determine whether a technical change alters useful output. The party receiving the learning benefit can differ from the party paying for collection and from the party able to finance the next machine.

These premises imply distinct economic objects: installed productive capacity; reusable but task-dependent capability; information about capability and transfer; scarce resources that turn a candidate update into an authorized release; procedures governing future experimentation; and claims on the surplus that finances investment. Data volume alone is not an adequate common unit for these objects.

The question is how the firm jointly produces accepted service and future productive possibilities. The resulting choices affect other firms through goods and services, compatible learning, input scarcity and reinvestable earnings. Autonomy changes which choices an agent can execute, with what information and delay. Ownership, data permissions and autonomy must therefore be represented separately.

The present common paper already defines physical marginal-product columns and a matrix of induced deployments. The extension sought this week makes their changes endogenous. See [Learning and Capital in Robotic Production](../learning_and_capital/README.md), [the production paper](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/research/assistant-led-expansion-20261117/research/theory_integration_2026_11_17/data_value/WHEN_CAPABILITIES_COMPACT_DRAFT.md), and [the Recursive Firm program](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/research/recursive-firm-theory/research/recursive_firm/README.md).

## 2. A common state and an economically meaningful allocation

Index an operating node by task, embodiment, end market, firm and date. Let the state s contain installed capital K, task capabilities theta, retained evidence D, support and validation capacity H, organizational procedure psi, permissions R, dated cash commitments W and demand conditions z. These can be vectors or distributions.

An action chooses the attempted production plan, dispatch and support, evidence capture, training and validation, release, hardware investment and any redesign of the procedure. The feasible set depends on the information and authority actually available before each commitment. A joint plan can be conditional on a signal only if the signal arrives and an authorized actor can use it in time.

For a firm, the dynamic benchmark is

V(s) = sup over feasible policies of E[sum beta^t * net captured contribution_t].

All real expenditures, displaced work, receipts and continuation values must be counted consistently. Learning changes subsequent feasible production and costs; it is not an additional monetary reward for the same future output. Total real surplus and private value require separate accounts. Existing ordinary operations continue to learn in the comparison.

This dynamic formulation is established methodology. Its research content comes from the specified production and learning technologies, information, control and market responses, not from writing a Bellman equation.

## 3. Four transitions that must be coupled

**Production.** Accepted-service output is a vector Y, generated by a feasible joint schedule from K, theta, H and the available orders. A robot's physical marginal product is a dated finite difference or derivative of accepted service with respect to its installed capacity after dispatch reoptimizes. A private investment value compares optimized programs with and without the purchase, including acquisition cost exactly once. Neither object is a standalone task-accuracy score.

**Evidence.** The distribution of new observations is Q(operating policy, attempted opportunities, current capability, capture, retention, environment). Failed and successful attempts can supply different information. Duplicate events, common shocks and missing outcomes change effective evidence. The observation law must change when the production policy changes.

**Capability.** A candidate learning process maps existing evidence, new evidence, compute, embodiment and validation into a joint distribution of qualified capabilities. Transfer can be directional, redundant, complementary or harmful under a particular update. Retaining a previous feasible controller is an available alternative where rights and resources permit it.

**Organization and investment.** Experience can alter a procedure psi for deciding what to test, how to share updates, when to release and which resources to buy. Firms then select investments using their own captured returns, cash and complements. Suppliers, customers and rivals can respond. These responses feed back into demand, observation and investment opportunities.

The retained procedure must be tested on fresh decisions with matched starting execution models, evidence and budgets. An adaptive rule must be compared with a capable fixed state-contingent rule, not a deliberately unresponsive baseline. A complete feasible contract can implement the same contingent decisions; recursion alone is not proof of necessary integration.

## 4. The local propagation map must include allocation responses

Write a structural transition as s_next = F(s,a), suppressing shocks for the local argument. Suppose a specified organization's feasible policy is a = mu(s), and both maps are differentiable in a neighborhood. The controlled transition is T(s) = F(s,mu(s)). Its derivative is

D T = F_s + F_a D mu.

This chain-rule identity separates the direct technological/state effect from the effect mediated by reallocation. If prices, wages or counterparties' decisions respond, their specified response must enter F or the state and policy. Their effects cannot be appended as a second independent “multiplier” after already entering the transition.

Along a dated path, a marginal impulse propagates by the ordered product of the date-specific derivatives. A single constant matrix is justified only within a stable local regime. At a commissioning ceiling, a discrete release decision or a changed embodiment, use directional changes or solve the two feasible programs explicitly.

The current paper's nonnegative induced-deployment matrix supplies an interpretable restriction. This week asks when better capability changes its entries and their signs by reducing evidence supply, freeing or congesting shared resources, changing who captures the gain, or changing the active investment constraint. A spectral threshold from a fixed positive system is not automatically a global prediction for an optimizing economy.

## 5. Mechanisms to derive together

### A. Productive success and the supply of useful evidence

For an incident-based stream, the expected number of useful records is attempts times the conditional incident probability times the conditional useful-record yield. For positive differentiable quantities, the proportional change in this product is the sum of the proportional changes in those three terms. This is an accounting identity, not an estimated scaling law.

Therefore higher throughput can coexist with declining evidence if incident reductions outweigh expansion and improved capture. A recipient may lose a unique donor stream even when the source's current contribution rises. Ordinary-operation learning, deliberate audits, simulation and new task entry are alternative responses. The theory should solve which alternative is funded and how it changes future capital returns, not assume that every missing incident has a fixed monetary cost.

### B. Scarcity moves as capabilities improve

An execution improvement can remove a robot-stage bottleneck and leave support, commissioning, validation or customer demand binding. An organizational improvement can release scarce human time and make more physical capital useful. Which occurs depends on the current production recipe and the best alternative schedule.

The desired result connects this migration to both physical MPK_r and the private return to the next investment. It should explain when capital expands as its marginal product falls toward a competitive user cost; when a measured high marginal product persists because a complement binds; and when additional capability benefits customers but does not generate enough captured cash to fund the next installation.

### C. Faster learning changes both timing and the set of viable work

Moving an identical usable update earlier increases its discounted usefulness on an unchanged positive opportunity path. It need not enlarge eventual adoption. Deadlines, obsolescence, changing demand, commissioning constraints and cash tied up during qualification can turn timing into a change in the feasible opportunity set.

The common paper distinguishes these cases under a given regime. The next step is to endogenize the cost and selection of acceleration and let ordinary rivals learn. The return must be evaluated against the updated alternative, including displaced source work and any scarce validation queue.

### D. A better procedure can change what a deployment is able to teach

A retained test-selection or transfer-validation procedure can increase the yield of future field access. Its value is a difference between reoptimized future allocations with and without that procedure, after counting design, maintenance and validation. Its economic lifetime depends on how quickly tasks, embodiments, rivals and audit quality change.

The distinction is between a better current controller, better information about that controller, and a better technology for selecting future learning. The week's theoretical and empirical designs should isolate these margins. Merely recomputing an optimal dispatch after a model update does not establish a new retained organizational capability.

### E. More diffusion can strengthen the industry while weakening its evidence investment

Opening reuse can increase the number of compatible recipients while reducing a source owner's private return from exclusive learning. The resulting evidence effort, licensing choices and entry responses can offset or amplify the direct increase in reach. Conversely, exclusive rights can support effort but prevent a productive return path across firms.

A valid comparative static has to include both the direct reach change and the endogenous source effort and market response. This is a question to solve in a tractable competitive restriction; neither a general monotonic benefit nor a general harm is asserted here.

## 6. The first research choices

Develop the state-dependent learning/installation system first under a tractable ordinary-learning baseline, then relax the particular assumption responsible for each proposed effect. Keep a few coherent systems rather than adding unrelated toy economies.

Priority comparisons:

- Source improvement that preserves evidence access versus the same immediate improvement that suppresses a donor stream.
- Identical qualified execution with different commissioning, validation or cash-recovery delays.
- A retained procedure versus a capable fixed contingent procedure on fresh target tasks.
- Identical aggregate resources with different cross-firm learning and reinvestment links.
- A fixed rival versus a rival allowed to update its own learning and price policy.

For each comparison record the accepted-service vector, evidence and capability transitions, dated resource use, investment choices, private claims and total real value. Report which objects moved and which did not. A change in firm profit without a change in real production is a distinct result from a capability improvement.

## 7. Present status

Established inputs are the already published conditional models and their stated checks. This scaffold contributes a shared set of objects, an explicit distinction between structural and allocation-mediated propagation, and an integrated set of open mechanisms. The mechanisms in Section 5 are research questions until the relevant assumptions and derivations are completed. No new industrial coefficient, unconditional superiority of autonomous firms, or independent novelty claim is made.

The week should end with a coherent account of which feedbacks survive, why they survive, how they differ across firms and end markets, and which observations could distinguish them. See [the research plan](README.md) for milestones and publication ownership.
