## Economic premises and a tractable quotient

A firm may qualify an additional distinct workflow while serving today's customers. A qualified digital recipe uses zero human and two compute slots; the old digital recipe uses one human and three compute slots; the ordinary human recipe uses two human slots. Service is accepted or absent, one job per workflow per period. There are n distinct recipient workflows at each firm, but their accepted outputs are perfect substitutes in one pooled customer market with inverse demand P(Q)=A-Q. The firms independently choose current quantities and research. Customer surplus is Q^2/2. This replaces the parent's separate recipient-market pricing, not the production recipe. A shared pool of H human and C compute slots constrains both research and service. For v qualified and u old digital copies, coordination uses gamma(v+u)(v+u-1)/2 additional compute slots. Human and compute inputs cost 2 and 1 ticks. A committed lab costs one tick per date; scope, hardware, reference and report uncertainty are fixed here. Every recipient has a usable local archive with rights; no extra case retention changes its binary availability. Physical archives and local archives are inherited, and the local qualification and both-archive successor laws from JOINT_TRANSITION are known. Rights, operating capacity and cash are not assumed transferable across firms.

A retained procedure p permits any old method m<=p. With local data, one attempted qualification succeeds with probability (2+m)/4. An internal successor project under the same method has probability (2+m)/4 and becomes usable next date. One research slot per date; internal train/develop consumes (1,2)/(1,3) human/compute slots; contracts consume one human slot and fees 5/6; a guaranteed license uses one human slot and fee 10. All old methods, ordinary training, paid equivalent projects and licenses are feasible under each matched cap except the excluded successor generations. No research creates a terminal stake. Both firms begin unqualified with p=0. Reports are independently generated under the known local law, not conditional mixtures from an unknown common regime. This removes the information spillover channel deliberately.

**Cash quotient.** All expenses are prefunded; maximum per-date outlay is M=2H+C+11 (including maximum provider fee and lab). Since A>=2n, receipts cannot be negative. Starting each firm with B0=TM ensures every history can prefund every otherwise admissible action. Inherited cash adds a constant to every continuation payoff of that firm and to both selection rankings. Thus remove cash from the strategic state under the declared sufficient bound, then independently reconstruct expected net cash from dated receipts and costs. The result excludes initial principal; a binding credit restriction invalidates this quotient.

**Production and action quotient.** For k qualified workflows, any feasible labeled allocation is exactly represented by counts (v,u,l) of qualified digital, old digital and human jobs, with v<=k and v+u+l<=n. Every such triple can be assigned to distinct workflows. For a fixed quantity q=v+u+l and research action r, only the least costly feasible triple is needed: there is no retained evidence or future input stock changed by its identity, and all such triples induce exactly the same next (k,p) law and rival revenue. A dearer triple is strictly dominated; tied triples have the same payoff and transition. A training target can be any of the n-k unqualified workflows, because all have identical local laws and market access. This is an exact independent-count quotient *only because the output market pools sites*. It does not contradict the earlier joint-configuration theorem: under site-specific rival overlap, independent counts generally fail.

For h remaining dates the game matrix is current net cash plus the conditional expectation of the selected h-1 continuation. Enumerate all reduced actions, verify all unilateral deviations, and select the pure equilibrium with highest or lowest combined private cash, then highest firm-0 cash and deterministic action order. No pure equilibrium means this implementation explicitly fails, not that the game has no mixed equilibrium. At final date research has a strictly positive cost and no effect; omit it. The numerical representation is integer-scaled by 16^h for the joint game, 4^h for a one-firm separable case.

## Why abundant demand can restore successor value

For any feasible q<n and fixed research, append a human job at cost 4 when H>=2n+1 (the research uses at most one human slot). This does not add compute. The incremental gross receipt is A-(2q+1+q_rival)>=A-(3n-1). If A>3n-1+4, serving all n jobs strictly dominates every smaller quantity for that research, for every rival quantity. The earlier cash bound means this move cannot reduce subsequent action feasibility. This stage dominance holds inductively at every history. With full service, fixed rival output and independent local qualification, research transitions and net cash separate; each firm's own dynamic optimum is a best response at every state and both payoff-selection conventions agree. This proves a subgame-perfect equilibrium, not merely a central solution. In the saturated cases gamma=0 and C>=2n+3, so the least operating cost is exactly 4(n-k)+2k+1 plus research cost. Every qualified workflow saves two ticks per date relative to competent human service. Its timing matters. No terminal valuation or separate cohort G is inserted.

This yields an economically different regime from the staged independent-market model: service is concurrent with research and pays current invoices, but the market response is saturated and resources are ample. In the shared-coordination regime the marginal saving of substituting a qualified copy for a human job, with c existing copies and slack human capacity, is 2-gamma*c. Positive coordination can exhaust the private value of additional qualified targets even before all are qualified. With binding labor, the same substitution may instead release another scarce slot. In the unsaturated game the rival quantity also responds, so the revenue effect for a finite change is (A-q_i-q_j)Delta q_i - q_i(Delta q_i+Delta q_j) - Delta q_i(Delta q_i+Delta q_j). The sign is not the sign of technical productivity alone. A physically measured robot MPK is not present in this digital restriction; the corresponding accepted-output increment at fixed inputs is distinct from a private value gradient. The ordinary optimizer and fixed contingent implementation remain strong comparators.

## Verification and interpretation

[Code](pooled_recursion.js) and [dated results](pooled_recursion_results.json) contain 18 solved of 18 attempted matched cases. Every active-state quantity/research cost menu is compared with a separate labeled recursive enumeration; saturated menus are checked against the analytical cost identity. Every selected equilibrium is checked against every unilateral reduced action; the proof transports this to every labeled action. Independent forward replay enumerates four equiprobable uniforms per firm per date, not the backward transition routine; exact expected net receipts match the solved values and probability mass is conserved at every date. Dated expectation columns are displayed as numeric approximations of the exact mass sums; root cash fractions are exact.

The positive saturated result establishes that a concurrent, competitively priced economy can support a second successor from zero when usable reach and time are sufficient, even with paid equivalents and ordinary learning retained. It does not establish a positive cap-2 effect under actively changing rival output, binding funding, new evidence, public transfer uncertainty, positive coordination or manufacturing. The active low-coordination comparison is a separate regime, not a controlled estimate of the isolated effect of market demand. Source [deployment topology](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/8dba84f45e18fe5896e75cd9a98539e4b87a83fb/research/theory_integration_2026_11_17/data_value/DEPLOYMENT_TOPOLOGY_AND_REFERENCE_IDENTIFICATION.md) motivates economically usable reach and directed qualification; the [Economic RL antecedent](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/af1cabb95772524db1b34dda3b2667a54c203b43/research/recursive_firm/economic_rl/README.md) motivates keeping physical and private-value objectives distinct. This calculation is standard dynamic programming and finite Cournot best response, not a novelty claim or empirical calibration. A firm with a feasible fixed contingent implementation and equal technology/rights can replicate the policy.
