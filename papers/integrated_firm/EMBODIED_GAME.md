## Economic premises

An installed machine changes the set of jobs a crew and its digital support can complete. A paid parts kit is not that machine; a qualified recipe is not either of them. Their complementarity is dated: procuring a kit does not permit current production, assembly occupies scarce resources, and qualification results arrive only after current production. A digital workflow can relieve the same crew bottleneck as a robot. It is therefore necessary to compare manufacturing and research in one program, not sum separately computed returns. Rivals compete for final customers while each firm's cash constrains its own next investment.

Two firms choose concurrently, publicly observing the inherited state of both. At most one accepted physical and one accepted digital job per firm per date. Inverse demand for each market m is P_m(Q)=A_m−2Q; maximum total quantity is two and all prices are positive in these cases. Customer surplus is Q² per market per date. Accepted physical work can be human, arm or mobile; digital work can be human or agent. The original core's recipe requirements are retained, including reserve recovery, old qualified versions, instance replication and pairwise coordination. For each firm H=3 human and C=4 compute slots, priced at 2 and 1 cash ticks. Fixed lab costs one tick each date (zero-cost suspension only at zero inherited cash). No credit, interest, intermediate payouts, deterioration or market shocks. A local archive for each domain is inherited with retention rights; no new evidence enters this restriction. There is no hidden transfer type. The two firms' conditional qualification outcomes are independent.

State per firm is (K_A,K_B,I_A,I_B,M_P,M_D,psi,B), with installed capacity K and separate uninstalled kit inventory I binary, model levels 0,1,2, procedure levels 0,1,2 and cash B in integer ticks. Each kit is compatible with its respective embodiment. In the manufacturing menu, a missing kit may be purchased for 6 (arm) or 8 (mobile) before current receipts; it arrives next date. An inherited kit may be assembled with one human and one compute slot; its installation arrives next date. One procurement or assembly per date. The alternative cheap-supply case prices kits at 1 and 2. Procurement is admitted only with at least three dates remaining and assembly with at least two, an explicit contracting/horizon restriction; there is no strategic terminal disposal or late procurement. Unused kits have zero terminal salvage; installed arm/mobile have salvage 1/2 ticks respectively. These initial kit endowments are conditional assets, not an estimate of free manufacturing. An arm or mobile can be acquired only once; either can be used for the same physical market. The scope is fixed and procurement is from an elastic supplier at exogenous prices.

One research slot can coexist with manufacturing and service if resources permit. Any retained method m≤psi may train a local physical or digital model with success probability (2+m)/4. The same probability applies to producing the next procedure in the both-archive restriction. Training uses (1 human,2 compute); successor development (1,3); outside substitutes use one human and fees 5 or 6; a guaranteed procedure license costs 10 plus one human. No effect before next date, and no residual procedure/model payoff at liquidation. The strong ordinary planner uses the same old qualified versions and outside options. A cap comparison changes only the allowed procedure generations.

## Dated feasibility, local response and equilibrium

For production recipe x, research r and manufacturing choice b, human and compute totals are sums across the three uses. Current outlay c includes their paid resource costs, parts, provider fees and lab. Feasible actions obey h≤H, c_compute≤C and c≤B before customer receipts. The cash transition is B'=B−c+Σ_m q_im[A_m−2(q_im+q_jm)]. Installation follows kit conservation: order increments I next date; assembly decrements inherited I and increments K next date. No kit can be assembled on its order date. Research increments the specified qualified level only on success. The final value is B+K_A+2K_B. No cohort terminal stake or attributed productivity reward is used.

For an increase of one in own accepted quantity from zero, holding rival output fixed, the gross cash increment is A_m−2(1+q_jm). Holding own quantity fixed, a rival's additional accepted job decreases that firm's receipts by 2q_im. The change in tomorrow's feasible spending therefore follows from this exact cash increment, not from an assigned return to learning. The rival price/cash effect also means that a cheaper current recipe is not in general a valid strategic pruning when it changes tomorrow's public cash: the opponent may react differently. The implementation keeps distinct cash outlays and transitions at nonterminal dates; only actions with identical quantity, total cost, research-success target and probability, and manufacturing destination/effect are equivalent. No difference in unused resources carries across dates. At the terminal date, where no research or build is admissible and salvage is fixed, a lower-cost same-quantity recipe is strictly better (or tied). This is the only cost dominance used. A richer state with endogenous archive retention would invalidate the nonterminal equivalence unless its transition were included.

A local operating frontier illustrates the distinction between capacity and productivity. With H=3,C=4 and M_P=M_D=0, no installed machine permits one accepted job in total: the two human services would require five slots, and old digital-agent service still requires one human slot. Adding an arm does not enlarge the maximum total: arm plus human digital needs four humans, arm plus agent exceeds compute. Adding a mobile permits (q_P,q_D)=(1,1) using one human on the mobile, two on human digital and three compute. With a fixed equal-weight frontier and physical-first tie-break, the marginal vector of adding a mobile is (0,1), while that of adding an arm is (0,0). Qualifying physical version 1 changes the arm vector to (0,1) because arm plus human digital now uses three human and two compute slots. Thus the additional accepted job can be in the *other* market even with the same physical robot workload. This is a finite capacity-block comparison, not a robot-hour estimate, and its frontier is not a profit-maximizing allocation. At an inherited cash of 7 the assembly-plus-human-digital recipe costs 8 (or 9 with the old agent instead); cash 7 does not forbid idle assembly, but prohibits both concurrent recipes. That distinction is a financing opportunity cost, not a technological infeasibility.

For each public state and remaining horizon h the stage game includes terminal wealth as the expected continuation. Simultaneous actions determine the two current receipts; independent quarter outcomes determine the next joint state. With terminal wealth integer, scale each h-date payoff by 16^h and sum all conditional joint outcomes with exact integer weights. Enumerate best responses and pure Nash profiles; select highest or lowest combined private wealth, then greatest firm-0 wealth, then stable action order. Both selections are solved independently throughout the tree. Backward induction with verified unilateral deviations at every reached-by-enumeration subgame yields a public-state subgame-perfect pure equilibrium for each reported restriction. It does not enumerate mixed equilibria or all possible continuation selections. Any missing pure profile or profile-budget breach is reported open, not replaced by a planner. Exact Number integers fit below 2^53 for these horizons/payoffs. Cash is retained as state, not quotiented out. No obligation is discharged by a theorem for an unrestricted game.

With initial cash 7, allowing assembly changes neither selected allocation nor wealth. With cash 16 and identical inherited kits, one firm assembles a mobile while providing digital service, and supplies both markets next date. With a prequalified physical model, both firms assemble mobiles and serve both markets on the last date. The outcome is not a claim that only mobiles are economically useful: the arm is in the menu, and its local marginal product changes when the controller qualifies.

The three-date inherited-procedure case is a particularly important regime comparison. Without manufacturing, the selected firms have terminal wealth (30.5,27.5), newly generated cash (14.5,11.5), and zero salvage; one firm trains digitally on the first date. With manufacturing, both assemble mobiles on date one while providing digital work; neither trains; terminal wealth is (28,28), new cash (10,10) and salvage (2,2). Total accepted jobs rise from 7.5 to 10 and customer surplus from 10.5 to 20. Under the lower-total pure continuation selection, private total falls from 57.625 to 56 and customer surplus rises from 11.1875 to 20. This is a counterexample to extending single-agent optional-action monotonicity to a symmetric competitive menu change. It does not show an individual unilateral access change necessarily reduces private value, or that observed equilibrium selection is causal. Competition reallocates rents and removes the selected training investment; the two mechanisms need separate tracking. The private decline cannot be inferred from engineering MPK, nor does a lower private return imply a lower customer value.

The paid procurement comparison is stronger than an inherited-kit result. At ordinary parts prices neither firm orders in the three-date high-demand economy; both train initially. When parts prices fall to (1,2), one firm buys a mobile kit on date one while serving physical jobs, assembles it on date two, and uses it only on date three. The other trains digitally on date one. Both continuation selections yield (43,43) instead of (40.75,40.25) under the higher-total selection without manufacturing, or (40.5,40.25) under the lower-total selection. Customer surplus falls to 12 from 14.375/14.625, respectively. Lower parts costs therefore need not raise customer surplus when they also change the selected research and rival output paths. This is a synthetic equilibrium comparison, not a claim about industry-wide surplus: provider fees, worker alternatives and supplier costs need separate treatment before a social-welfare total is justified.

Starting from an inherited first-generation procedure, admitting a second in the same three-period competitive economy changes neither selected wealth nor research path under either selection, with or without manufacture. This is a matched cap-1/2 null with a sufficiently long clock for one successor to affect service if acquired immediately; it is not a test of acquisition from zero over four or more periods. A positive nonsaturated second-generation premium remains open. The present solver does not encounter a no-pure-equilibrium blocker; do not invent one.

## Verification and gaps

[Code](embodied_game.js) and [exact results](embodied_game_results.json): 32/32 solved matched cases, all unilateral deviations checked in each recursively evaluated subgame. Forward replay independently enumerates four equiprobable draws per firm per date, verifies probability mass, installed/kit conservation and the resource and cash ledger, and reconciles expected terminal wealth, salvage and cash. Customer surplus is accumulated from each realized joint quantity before averaging. The saved source was fetched and executed again and its generated JSON matched byte-for-byte. The local frontier calculation above is analytical; no claim of independently coded full action-menu enumeration is made here.

Obligations 1–7: (1) nests core recipe and priced-market restrictions but not latent-report or graph transfer; (2) jointly closes production, assembly, parts, training, procedure and cash in this fixed-right/scope restriction; (3) exact planning and equilibrium benchmarks only, no trained RL; (4) retained digital models separate from instance/coordination flows; (5) matched three-period cap-2 null and manufacturing displacement of ordinary learning, no general recursive superiority; (6) local frontier and global procurement/competition comparisons, positive active-rival cap-2 and a general mixed solver still open; (7) synthetic exact computation and conditional arguments, no empirical magnitudes or novelty assertion. Falsifier for the proposed mechanism is an equally capable matched recipe with slack crew/compute or no customer-price response, eliminating the cross-market capacity or rent channel. An alternative explanation of the private/consumer divergence is equilibrium selection: two declared pure selection rules preserve its sign here, but do not exhaust equilibrium correspondences.

Next: retain an exact dated procurement/assembly account while enlarging the number of recipient opportunities and the horizon so successive procedures can affect both qualified control and physical investment. Develop an equilibrium solver that either certifies deviations for mixed profiles or explicitly reports failure; preserve the November 27 pilot and other program gates. Restore endogenous evidence/rights and flexible scope only with state-compatible transitions and tractable exact restrictions; no assertion that all such features are jointly solved yet.
