## 1. Economic premise and precise clock

A customer order is not an inference instance; a qualification report is not automatically an accepted order. The four-block economy commits a service recipe and quantity for each of its n shifts, pays gross cost from inherited cash, performs one research and one hardware action (subject to the exclusive/concurrent regime and first-shift resources), and releases stock/scope changes at the boundary. Customer receipts arrive then. The old block does not identify a physical research completion duration. The new experiment stipulates completion and lawful release at the end of shift 1, with no new work admitted by that observation except the option to use a newly qualified version for **the same previously committed automated job and embodiment**. The resource price schedule is unchanged. Gross cost is reserved at block start; unused variable input payments are refunded at the boundary. This is a refundable-input contract, not an assumption that sunk wages or compute reservations are recoverable. No released capacity is reused within the block. Hardware, archive rights, further research, scope and collection dates are unchanged. The old recipe remains feasible; changeover has zero cost in this restricted test.

## 2. Exact locked embedding

Let h_Y,c_Y be service inputs per shift, g the inherited lab bill, and F the incremental first-shift resource and fee bill for research, build, retention and entry. Gross prepayment is C=n(2h_Y+c_Y+g)+F. The first shift has h_Y+h_R+h_K and c_Y+c_R+c_K+c_retention; subsequent shifts have the unchanged service requirements. Reserve C before the first shift. Each shift creates a receivable at the original market-clearing price implied by the committed quantities; the receivable is collected once at the boundary. Scope, hardware delivery/assembly, archive retention and research success follow the original transition law. No newly generated evidence can be used for the inherited research choice.

For every legal generated own action, rival quantity mask and research outcome, the independent event ledger reconstructs resource use, gross prepayment, receipts and boundary states. With release locked, all entries equal the old block transition. As market receipts depend only on the two quantity masks and the existing two firm-specific quarter-probability research draws are independent, the product of these checked single-firm laws equals the old joint kernel for every legal paired action, including deviations. Thus the game (not merely a selected root) is preserved. This proof applies to the accepted funded local-archive restriction; no common latent report is newly assumed independent. Existing justified terminal and funded penultimate reductions remain in scope. For the six primary locked runs there are 22,701 pre-duplicate-reduction own-action enumerations across menu states/calendars, 90,804 own-action/rival-quantity checks and 140,124 conditional outcome comparisons (counts include repeated cases across calendars).

## 3. One additional contingent response and safe reduction

For a successful train/contract that qualifies the target of an already committed automated job, the inherited physical and digital recipes reduce human use by one and compute use by one per remaining shift. With success probability p and n shifts, expected incremental net cash is

\[
G(a)=3p(n-1)\,1_{\{\text{training target is the committed automated job}\}}.
\]

For a release lag l, n-1 becomes (n-l)_+; for a nonrefundable resource payment or nonzero changeover bill the cash gain can be smaller. The formula has no additional reward for data or procedure value, and it changes no accepted quantity. It is a feasible cost saving derived from the same recipe. Refund is success-contingent and paid once. Neither failure nor merely developing a research procedure enables the qualified version. Outside training remains feasible.

The prefunded cash quotient remains valid because the same gross reserve covers all outlays and realized refunds are nonnegative. Two own actions with identical quantity and projected successor kernel can therefore be ranked by expected effective cost C-G(a); a discarded action cannot improve private value against any rival quantity/continuation in that class. Recompute this dominance before both menu and transition-class reductions, not after the original minimum-cost representative is selected. Dated cash replay credits actual refunds only on successful outcome branches; backward values use the exact quarter-weighted refund. All retained unilateral deviations are checked in each selected continuation. Off-path research, hardware, outside alternatives and old execution recipes remain present in the accepted menus.
