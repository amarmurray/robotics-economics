## 1. Economic premise and exact factorization

An independent refundable reserve is not first-shift consumption. In the both-open institution, write block length n, actual first-shift outlay including setup F_i, gross reserve per subsequent shift R_i, initial accepted quantity mask q_i, and qualified release outcome omega. The funded gross commitment is F_i+(n-1)R_i. If u_i is the selected per-shift suffix Nash payoff and T_i is the next-date value including subsequent competitive response and salvage, then

$$ W_i=r_i(q_i,q_{-i})-F_i+(n-1)\mathbb E[u_i(\omega;R_0,R_1)]+\mathbb E[T_i(X')]. $$

**Proof.** Start with n times first quantity revenue less gross commitment. The inherited correction for each outcome is (n-1) times the sum of the suffix revenue difference and refund R_i-c_i, where c_i is the actual suffix variable outlay. Expanding cancels hypothetical initial suffix revenue and reserved cash, yielding the identity outcome by outcome. The accepted funding quotient makes the next-date payoff independent of remaining cash throughout this domain. The stock transition still records first-shift retention, training, successor, hardware and scope. The two expectations need not be independent: linearity applies to their sum. No extra success draw or resource allotment is used.

The suffix is open in both outcomes and uses only inherited installed hardware, active scope, outcome-qualified masks and reserves. It has no further evidence, research, manufacturing, scope or collection event. Consequently expected suffix payoff is independent of first service quantity and actual first recipe once inherited operating types, release target/probability and independent reserve are fixed. The solver evaluates it once per pair of suffix signatures. Initial quantities and first outlays remain distinct payoff inputs; the next-date stock kernel retains its separate exact cache. The reserve breakpoints and all retained unilateral deviations remain. No claim that a larger reserve dominates a smaller one under rival response. This is a payoff factorization, not a new selection rule or action deletion.

In code, the zero-initial-quantity evaluation is solely a computational normalization of the inherited suffix correction, not a replacement legal first action. The forward ledger uses actual initial quantities and refunds; dummy-evaluation changed-quantity counters are not published as economic outcomes. Future experiments, first-service-dependent suffix evidence, scarce reserved crew/compute, binding financing or a selector depending on first quantities can invalidate the separability.
