## Economic premise and timing

The ability to use a retained archive depends on the source, recipient and release date. Two firms can run research and serve competing customer orders during the same period. Their observed qualification outcomes are informative about the same transfer regime. One firm's research can therefore change the other firm's subsequent evidence allocation without transferring a model or making any payment. This is a public-information restriction, not a private reporting or disclosure equilibrium. All current reports, retention outcomes, actions and state coordinates are public. No firm sees the latent regime itself.

Retain the parent digital one-job recipes, human fallback, old qualified versions, shared H=4 and compute C=7 per firm, wages 2, compute price 1, copy coordination c(c-1)/2 and dated cash. Each local joint type contains both firms' binary qualified digital model, local archive and legal retention right. Global state contains each firm's physical source archive, procedure 0/1/2, reference equipment, lab scope and cash, and a common demand state and posterior odds. Physical service and arm/mobile construction are held outside this restriction; the physical source archive is an inherited or purchased input, not a fictitious current service. Local archives can support their own recipient's training; physical archives support cross-domain training. Arbitrary transfer topology is not represented. Demand intercept is 8 or 12 and persists with probability 3/4. Accepted service is deterministic under the selected recipe. Each firm's accepted quantity earns the market price conditional on both firms' local quantities.

The full digital-restriction generator chooses a production recipe per site, optional paid legal retention where no archive exists, at most one research action, optional reference construction and next lab scope. It allows old procedures, internal and equivalent paid outside training/development, guaranteed successor licenses, exact and noisy audits, bought archives, idle closure and ordinary human production. It inherits the parent costs and report laws; reference construction and scope changes take effect next period. Development success depends on the inherited procedure and both archive types. Research cannot use the cases collected in the same period. Current outlays, including overhead and changeover, must be affordable before collecting current external revenue. Each research/retention/market report has probabilities in twentieths. The generator includes only one-job digital recipes and binary digital levels; this is a stricter menu than core.py.

## Exact joint likelihood and economic consequence

Given the full labeled public action profile a, let the observable report vector y consist of each active research report, retained case outcome and next demand state. Conditional on inherited public state and H, all these channel outcomes are independent in this restriction. For prior odds (u,v), define L_h(y) as the product of conditional report likelihoods. Then

    Pr(y|s,a) = (u L_1(y) + v L_0(y))/(u+v),
    posterior odds(y) = (u L_1(y), v L_0(y)).

Do not average the separately updated beliefs or condition on the latent regime itself. Apply the deterministic stock, scope and cash transition and then the realized qualification and case effects; only then map the whole public state to its canonical joint-type representative. Group identical next states *including the posterior*. If two report vectors produce the same qualified stocks but different beliefs, they need not have the same continuation value. Retaining the source/target association in each labeled action and report is essential.

A consequence is a public informational externality conditional on the availability of profitable contingent decisions. With a fixed common-information continuation menu and an isolated decision maker, costless access to an additional report weakly increases its optimized expected continuation value by the ability to ignore it. In the competitive continuation, access can change rival behavior, so no corresponding unconditional private-value monotonicity follows. A paid report is not a free asset. The effect of a firm's experiment on a rival is not in general the experiment's own private return, nor a physical marginal product. This distinction is important for the scope question: capturing a reusable procedure or archive does not automatically capture all of the informational consequences of operating it.

In the concrete check, the two firms train from physical archives with inherited methods 1 and 0. Strong-regime success probabilities are 3/4 and 1/2; weak-regime probabilities are both 1/4. From prior 3/5 the event of two successes has probability 1/4 and posterior odds 9:1. The product of marginal mixtures is (11/20)(2/5)=11/50, not 1/4. The joint law therefore differs from an independent draw of each firm's standalone transition, even before a competitive allocation changes.

## Computation and validation

[joint_transition.js](joint_transition.js) constructs feasible actions and exact integer masses with a common denominator, normalizing odds with arbitrary-precision integers. A separate reference implementation enumerates the latent regime first and all labeled bitmask report histories, reconstructing receipts, stocks and reports without calling the production kernel's event/base/effect helpers. It then aggregates the full observed public histories to canonical state and verifies equality of probability masses. Each tested distribution sums exactly to one and its posterior expectation equals the inherited prior. The tests cover degenerate priors, a cash-tight outside-scope case, retained evidence, internal and outside research, both old and new procedures, reference construction and scope. The permutation checks transport the full local action vectors and research target indices. The terminal cases omit all new research and retention. See [results](joint_transition_results.json): 1001 stratified concurrent joint profiles, 3003 relabeled profile comparisons, 2899 terminal profiles. The action menus are enumerated, but concurrent profiles are sampled for kernel checks rather than all possible game profiles solved.

This is an exact restricted transition law, not an equilibrium, an actual LLM test, or industrial calibration. It uses conditional independent channels, public outcomes, homogeneous recipient identities conditional on local types and an invariant common latent state. Private reports, strategic concealment, persistent relational graph edges, or correlated residuals require a different state/kernel. The source topology paper's noisy relation and bridge options cannot generally be reduced to these counts. The recursive Economic RL source's legal observations and matched ordinary-policy null continue to apply. Empirical discrimination needs paired public/owned qualification report histories, channel reliability, actual disclosure rules, and subsequent resource commitments, rather than fleet model counts alone.
