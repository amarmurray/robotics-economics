## Direct recursion has a clock; strategic wealth has a different clock

An inherited procedure affects only research laws. An accepted successor is available next period, training using it qualifies a model for the following period, and that model can only then change a service recipe. Thus from inherited psi=1, second-generation development at t, training at t+1 and service at t+2 is the earliest direct accepted-output route. From psi=0, two successive developments followed by training and service need four periods. This follows by the within-period inheritance constraints, not by any assumed acceleration. A two-period cap-1 versus cap-2 comparison cannot test the direct service benefit of the second successor. Earlier wealth effects could still arise via resources, informative public actions or equilibrium selection; do not infer an unconditional game-value monotonicity result from option-set nesting in the planner.

At fixed current action profile, a change in selected continuation changes W by the expectation of the change in continuation payoffs under the same joint report law. This is not an MPK or a change in training technology. In a game, the induced best responses can also change the current allocation. Therefore report selection separately when evaluating the private return to recursive capability, and do not assign a selection-driven gain to a procedure's productivity.

The last row is a nonempty option test: cap 2 adds two feasible root actions per firm (18 to 20), including development. The remaining time permits its direct training/service route, but the one-target stake and costly shared inputs do not justify first-period research in any reported root pure equilibrium. This is a null result under the two stated continuation selections, not a claim that all competitive recursive firms should avoid research. The two-period high-demand case has the same first actions (both human jobs at both sites) under either selection yet differs by 1/4 in aggregate terminal wealth. The three-period funded case changes the selected firm's first operating/scope decision when continuation selection changes: under the highest-total convention firm 0 closes and firm 1 serves, under the lowest-total convention firm 0 serves and firm 1 closes. The same technology therefore admits selection-dependent rent and resource allocation. No increase in physical productivity is needed. The selection is exogenous, not a prediction of which equilibrium an actual market chooses.

The strong ordinary contingent planner/contracting comparator survives. Current menus already allow the equivalent research technology through a paid provider, and no symbolic solver is a pretrained-LLM experiment. This game restricts competitive interaction to final output prices; it does not solve entry, supplier prices, credit supply or private reporting. The source financing-selection diagnostic at the pinned production commit motivates observing feasible opportunity sets and dated credit terms; the recursive Economic RL and organizational-recursion antecedents motivate keeping legal observations, retained methods and the direct versus successor route distinct.
