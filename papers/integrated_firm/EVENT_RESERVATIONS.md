## 1. Separate qualified capability, public information and market reopening

A qualification can reduce the bill for an already committed service, change what the firm can bid to deliver, change the rival's operating response, or leave every actual decision unchanged. These are different channels. The prior gate opened the suffix only after at least one successful execution qualification; failure left quantities locked. Hold the same physical clock, archive and local qualification kernel, initial service/research/hardware/scope menus and terminal continuation. Open the public suffix of the nonterminal long block at the end of shift one regardless of the two outcomes (even with no attempt). Failure and success therefore have the same reopening institution. The terminal block remains as before. No additional experiment, evidence capture, hardware completion, scope change, or receipt is released early. H=3 and C=5 per shift are unchanged. The first shift still shares its actual research, assembly and retention work with the old service. All settlements remain at the boundary.

The paired successor state and research outcome law do not change. The new correction to each old block action payoff replaces the committed suffix receipts and variable outlays with the exact public suffix equilibrium; conditional values are propagated before the earlier best-response comparisons. The public suffix game uses the previous finite exact potential, independent per-firm menus and the same highest- or lowest-total payoff selector, then firm 0 and mask tie-breaks. No claim that these selectors exhaust the correspondence.

Let the first recipe's variable bill including inherited lab scope be b_i, additional first-shift commitments be F_i, and the suffix length be n-1. In the tied institution the firm must reserve b_i per later shift. In the new independent institution it may choose an integer reserve R_i between inherited lab bill g_i and 12 (maximum possible per-shift operating bill 2H+C+g_i). Reserve may be smaller or larger than b_i. Old tied choices remain attainable. Gross dated commitment is

$$C_i^{gross}=b_i+F_i+(n-1)R_i.$$

After lawful public qualification (or failure), a suffix recipe is legal if it respects inherited installed hardware, qualification, H, C, and variable bill at most R_i. All unused reservation is refunded at the original boundary; no funds become available early. If c_i is realized variable bill, the refund is (n-1)(R_i-c_i). First-shift net plus suffix net thus equals first-shift receipts less b_i+F_i plus (n-1) times realized suffix payoff. Reserving cash does not itself consume real labor or compute. This is a *refundable cash commitment* contract; sunk crew booking, actual shared compute purchases, costly collateral, or binding liquidity would be different institutions. The existing funded bound covers the maximum old first-shift commitment plus 12 per remaining shift. The old feasibility check before independent reservation enumeration does not discard a first-shift action under that bound. No binding-finance extrapolation.

## 2. Exact reservation quotient

For a given initial state and release target j, denote by c(q,m) the least actual operating bill among legal recipes delivering binary quantity mask q at qualification mask m, before imposing the suffix reservation. Infeasible quantities have infinite cost. Only m and m union {j} can be realized by that single attempt (for nonqualification work, only m). Because a suffix recipe changes no state after the shift except actual receipts and outlays, c(q,m) is a valid sufficient statistic for the suffix best-response game. The feasible quantity set at R is {q:c(q,m)<=R}.

**Proposition.** In the funded, always-open, fully refunded, no-further-learning suffix with stated selector, it suffices to enumerate reserves at the distinct finite values of c(q,m) and c(q,m union {j}), including g. For any R choose the largest such breakpoint not exceeding R. This reproduces each possible released-state quantity menu and its least bills; hence it reproduces every reduced quantity payoff and selected equilibrium against any rival reserve/qualification. The first quantity, actual recipe, costs, all research/hardware/rights/scope choices, and stock kernel remain fixed. Since the unused reserve is refunded and future cash remains above the funding quotient, the total private payoff for either reservation is identical. The smaller gross reservation is feasible whenever the larger one was feasible. The old policy is represented even if its numerical reservation is not retained. Raw dominated suffix recipe aliases need not be retained as distinct strategies; every original feasible recipe is checked against the chosen stage equilibrium. This proof does not carry to binding financing, payment before the boundary, changes to raw recipe-dependent evidence, a selector depending on reserve level rather than payoffs/quantity menus, or realized shared capacity.

The independent check enumerates all 64 hardware/qualification/scope/eligible-target types, every integer reservation from g to 12 and both possible qualification masks. All 1,600 minimum-menu comparisons agree; 526 of 800 reservation-type choices are redundant in this quotient. An earlier enumeration of reserves at or above the first bill reached its 30,000,001-profile budget; the actual independent (also lower reserves allowed) exclusive game now solves using 8,682,797 reduced profiles. This is an algorithmic and economic state compression, not evidence of equilibrium nonexistence in the earlier run.

**Conditional resource reservation witness.** Reuse the same eligible arm firm, qualified physical recipe, digital archive, open lab, idle unreserved rival and internal digital training with probability 1/2 (1H+2C, bill 4) as in EVENT_SUFFIX. Both outcomes reopen. Five shifts; compare:

| First recipe and suffix reserve | First recipe bill | Gross commitment | Failure/success suffix net per shift | Expected block net |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cheap first, tied 5 | 5 | 29 | 11 / 11 | 51 |
| Old expensive first, tied 8 | 8 | 44 | 11 / 20 | 66 |
| Cheap first, independent 8 | 5 | 41 | 11 / 20 | 69 |

The third action keeps the first qualified recipe and buys the identical later feasible menu as the second at three fewer ticks of actual cost and gross commitment. All three share the same first accepted physical service, research attempt, retained stock kernel and rival first action; the future funded continuation is identical conditional on the outcome. There is no free increase in first-shift H or C. Relative to the tied cheap action, extra gross reservation of 12 buys 18 expected additional block contribution; against the tied expensive action the independent choice saves 3. This is a conditional comparison, not a selected initial policy or a guarantee that an expanded simultaneous game raises private equilibrium value.

## 3. Full continuation probability effect: a quantity-market counterexample

The archived funded-quantity comparison in [qualification_observability](../../research/theory/qualification_observability.md) finds no adverse equal-cost probability comparisons in its original technology and clock. That result is intact. The new reservation-constrained public quantity suffix has a distinct strategic institution. Holding the reopening rule fixed on *both* outcomes, the ranking of two otherwise identical attempts can be reversed by the selected continuation.

At a reachable off-path penultimate state of the accepted ten-shift economy, both firms have an arm, closed labs, sufficient funding. Firm 0 is unqualified, firm 1 has a qualified physical model and procedure 1. At the first shift firm 0 commits to a human physical job, bill 6 and tied reserve 6. Firm 1 commits to a human digital job, bill 4 and tied reserve 4, and contracts for digital qualification at fee 100 plus one human coordination unit costing 2. Both old procedures are legal; their success probabilities are 1/2 and 3/4 at identical cost, capacity, target, and scope. The rival first action and all other commitments remain fixed. Successful qualification releases only the digital bit. No hardware or other stock changes. First net of firm 1 is 12-4-102=-94; salvage of the inherited arm is 1. Initial cash principal is excluded from these continuation values.

| Realization | Firm 0 suffix market | Firm 1 suffix market | Suffix net per shift (0,1) | Terminal net per shift (0,1) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Failure | digital | physical | 8,12 | 8,18 |
| Success | physical | digital | 10,10 | 8,19 |

Under the higher-total selector there are two failure and three success suffix quantity equilibria, one terminal quantity equilibrium on each outcome. The suffix equilibrium changes which firm serves which market. Qualification adds a feasible lower-cost digital recipe, but the selected firm 1 private margin falls by two for each of the four remaining shifts, while the terminal per-shift saving is one for each of five shifts. The pure capability's terminal saving does not compensate for the intermediate strategic reallocation.

$$W_0=-94+4(12)+5(18)+1=45,\qquad W_1=-94+4(10)+5(19)+1=42.$$

Thus E[W]=45-3p. Moving from p=1/2 to 3/4 gives 43.5 to 42.75, an exact loss of 0.75. Both outcomes have positive probability and all values include the terminal quantity response. There is no borrowed external learning-value coefficient.

For the matched *fixed-rival* policy, hold the rival's failure-branch quantities (digital in suffix, physical in terminal block) across both outcomes and allow firm 1 to optimize its own lawful recipes/quantities. The old physical suffix job remains optimal at 12, while the terminal qualification saves one. The corresponding conditional values are 45 and 50; raising probability increases optimized value by 1.25. This fixed rival policy is a feasible comparator, not asserted to remain a best response after success.

More generally at such a fixed initial action and rival commitment, with independent binary training outcome probability p, continuation payoff is affine in p conditional on the other unchanged primitives and outcome-contingent equilibrium selection. Its slope is

$$(n-1)(u_1-u_0)+\Delta V_{\rm next},$$

where the latter is the value difference from the solved next-date decisions, including any dated cash or stock changes. An adverse suffix effect reverses the full ranking only when it exceeds the subsequent capability premium. This is a conditional probability derivative, not a globally reoptimized derivative over the initial action menu, or a general claim that improved execution is harmful. The broader selected support and root may change when probabilities change at every date. With correlated reports, p can also change the observation kernel/posterior; this affine expression requires the fixed conditional outcome law of this restriction.

The example compares two expensive feasible attempts; abstention with the same initial service and reserve saves 102 and keeps the failure continuation (147 instead of 43.5 or 42.75). Thus neither attempt need be an economically optimal research choice. Do not infer a negative intrinsic physical MPK, an adverse root adoption effect, or a reversal in a unique-quantity-equilibrium institution. Indeed the suffix before and after has multiple equilibria. A different equilibrium selection can change or erase this particular witness. The stronger unique-continuation result in EVENT_SUFFIX instead relies on the *success-only* reopening institution. Keep these mechanisms separate.

## 4. Link to payer authorization and enforceability

At the witness, a target-pass transfer b from a funded, consenting beneficiary, enforceable on a lawfully verifiable qualification, would change the operator's conditional difference from -3 to b-3, holding the given subsequent allocations and funding feasible. An outcome-independent advance cannot change that conditional difference in the prefunded restriction. A confidential verifiable target test and public market disclosure are not the same right. The other firm's conditional operating value gains eight ticks in the example (2 per suffix shift, unchanged terminal margin), while firm 1 loses three; a legally permitted transfer strictly between 3 and 8 would split the five-tick private continuation gain between the two relative to the matched lower-success attempt if all else is held fixed. This is a mathematical participation interval, not authorization of a transfer, a claim that competitors can or should make such an agreement, a competitive equilibrium with contracts, or a demonstration that a real payer receives this gain. Even the upper transfer bound cannot cover the witness's expensive attempt relative to abstention. Outside the witness, one must re-solve offers, incentives, target authorization, payer cash/credit and the fallback. The separate paper's private target record and advance/bonus comparison remain relevant: a record on which settlement cannot lawfully condition cannot deliver this incentive.

For the recursive firm, faster or more reliable procedure improvement must be valued through *both* the feasible production frontier and the surrounding firms' reaction. The retained procedure can alter the distribution over such responses. That is a claimant-value gradient, distinct from physical accepted service and input-saving gradients. An Economic RL agent may use physical MPK_r forecasts as lawful inputs, but should optimize the external dated account or a correctly telescoping shaped reward, not a probability proxy that rewards technical success in isolation. The pinned [Economic RL antecedent](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/af1cabb95772524db1b34dda3b2667a54c203b43/research/recursive_firm/economic_rl/README.md) preserves the capable ordinary contingent comparator. The [lawful signal timing antecedent](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/8dba84f45e18fe5896e75cd9a98539e4b87a83fb/research/theory_integration_2026_11_17/data_value/LAWFUL_SIGNAL_TIMING_AND_RECURSIVE_DATA_OPTION.md) holds the other program fixed under optional exogenous information; public strategic reopening need not inherit its weak value inequality.
