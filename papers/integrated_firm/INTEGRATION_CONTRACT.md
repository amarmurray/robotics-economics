## Method

Start from scarce and reproducible inputs, information and rights, accepted final services, dated cash, and the state inherited by the next decision. Define all units. Choose a small integrated economy whose restrictions are explicit. Solve all its retained decisions together before expanding the menu. Numerical simplicity is acceptable; treating separately solved modules as a jointly solved economy is not.

Retain ordinary production that learns, human production and fallback, old qualified models, capable fixed contingent organizations, and paid outside experiments. Free optional access preserves old feasible policies. Hardware and procedure changes compete for the same resources as present service. Data, installed capital, model capital, research procedures and operating flows are distinct. Avoid double-counting future output, internal transfers or recovered principal.

A Bellman equation is a definition until its primitives, constraints and transition law are specified. A finite solution is a valid restricted model, not an unrestricted theorem. A public-state competitive game is not private-information competition. Explain precisely which strategic and informational margins each extension closes.

## Research sequence

1. Write the common environment and map the existing studies into it.
2. Solve and independently replay one finite firm with endogenous service mix, digital/human use, embodied investment, retained evidence, training, inquiry, procedure investment, boundaries and dated cash.
3. Derive marginal-value identities and distinguish local technological effects, resource effects and changed policies. Test a small set of mechanisms that can change the conclusion.
4. Specify and solve a competitive restriction from the same technology; carry its price/allocation response back into investment where computationally and theoretically justified.
5. Study repeated successor procedures, changing representations and hardware vintages, then test whether conclusions survive broader end markets and longer horizons.
6. Separate exact planning from Economic RL approximation and measure regret against strong ordinary policies. Do not imply actual LLM evaluation from symbolic policies.
7. Maintain the cumulative paper, proof/claim register, results, unresolved dependencies and next action. Use scheduled execution for continued substantive work; suppress routine hourly updates.

## Completion criterion

The integrated core is complete when its production, learning and investment decisions are jointly determined from the stated primitives and resource constraints, with a reproducible solution and explicit boundary. The larger research program is complete only when its promised extensions and limitations have been assessed. A successful commit, elapsed time or an automation run does not pass a scientific gate.
