## Economic premise and analytical comparison

A qualified capability is a retained asset whose economic services are dated. A fixed total number of commercially useful shifts is insufficient to value a change to the research procedure: a shift occurring before a successor can produce a usable execution policy cannot earn that policy's marginal product. Consider two remaining investment dates, an existing archive, incumbent qualification probability p, successor probability q, and probability b of producing the successor. With ordinary retry, qualification probabilities immediately after each date are p and 1-(1-p)^2. With successor-first, they are zero and bq+(1-b)p. With a constant net captured operating increment D per qualified shift, n early service shifts after the first date, m late shifts after the second, and incremental nonservice cost C (recursive minus retry), the recursive program's private value difference is
[
D\{m[b(q-p)-p(1-p)]-np\}-C.
]
This assumes no earlier service effect of the procedure itself, independent conditional draws, unchanged rival policies/prices and same terminal noncash salvage; D is net operating rent, not physical MPK or welfare. With p=3/4, q=b=1 and C=0, positive value requires m>12n. Thus the earlier endpoint advantage of 1/16 is compatible with a negative economic return. At fixed n+m=S and positive endpoint advantage delta, the incremental value falls by D(p+delta) when one late shift is moved earlier. With heterogeneous markets/rival responses, replace D by dated state-specific conditional receipts and reoptimize; the formula is a fixed-program accounting identity, not a general equilibrium threshold. A competent ordinary program is essential. A service-only period before any recipient can use the improvement has zero direct qualification return.

## Interpretation and boundaries

This connects the production-of-capability source's dated original-customer orders with organizational recursion's distinction between retained inquiry and execution. The economically usable window is a **calendar of recipient-specific opportunities**, not simply total scale. More early demand may increase the return to acquiring an existing qualified model while decreasing the incremental return to improving the procedure that would eventually produce another model. This is a substitution mechanism, not proof of a novel universal law. The strong alternative is early outside qualification, ordinary retry or hardware that immediately releases complementary human capacity. Identification needs admission dates, actual accepted output by market, realized validation clocks, outside offers, and dated resource/funding constraints. The production source's common correlated recovery regime, directed archives and varying orders within a block are not nested by independent repeated service. The recursive source's physical MPK remains a vector, and captured D is distinct. No actual LLM or RL performance inferred.
