# When intelligence progress changes the operating date

Edition 0.33 joins the four funded intelligence arrangements to annual physical commitments and compatible parts in one execution. The decision question is whether a reduction in model/adaptation work actually advances qualified operation after the other necessary conditions and the human alternative are included. All coefficients remain declared synthetic assumptions. No new source establishes real adaptation productivity, staffing, rights, robot repair cost or target profitability.

## Service and comparison boundary

The complete obligation is 500 accepted cleaning zones each month from July 2026 through February 2027. All thirteen worlds deliver all 4,000 zones, including the digital-human-only case. Actual paid staffing stays at $32,000 for the eight months. The human operation has 588 whole-zone monthly capacity under the existing efficiency premise, so robot activity substitutes for already-paid work without proving additional service, avoided hiring or removed payroll.

The same logical-role capital allocations and physical objective underlie the four profiles. Distinct legal consolidation, assumed engineering, model resources and process integration can change which party runs short and which unit can operate. This is deliberately stronger than placing architecture cost tables alongside an independent supply forecast: every reported date refers to the same physical unit, supported cohort, part stock and actual payer ledger. It still does not estimate the real economics of any named architecture provider.

## A digital gain can expose a physical bottleneck

The original open-adapted profile requires 200 adaptation plus 100 validation engineering hours per initially qualified unit. Its 400-hour monthly engineering allocation admits only one of two opening robots in July. The existing installed-service priority blocks that month's new manufacturing attempt. The second opening robot qualifies later, but the missed start does not reschedule itself. More model capability, a source announcement or next month's spare engineers cannot retroactively execute that order.

The controlled productivity premise halves adaptation work to 100 hours per unit while retaining 100 validation hours, 400 hours of monthly engineering capacity, demonstrations and the same accepted-service assumptions. Both opening robots now qualify in July and the new batch can start. With unreserved annual resources, the four-robot fleet is supported in October: the open profile's two 105-hour integrations still exceed the 200-hour monthly allocation, so the second new unit follows the first by a month. Supported exposure rises to 27 robot-months.

With only 8,400 integration minutes available in 2026, the identical lower-adaptation premise supports one new unit in September, but its second new unit waits until January. Exposure is 24 robot-months. Further reducing adaptation to 50 hours lowers itemized resource cash but does not move that January physical date. This is the relevant distinction for substantial H: improved digital work may reduce one resource cost or make an upstream action feasible without shortening the remaining binding path. It requires an explicit unchanged-quality premise; an actual faster tool that worsens validation would need a different contract.

The licensed boundary is exact. Each new unit needs 87.5 hours, or 5,250 minutes, of physical integration. The full two-unit addition can be commissioned in September with 10,500 remaining 2026 minutes; at 10,499, one waits until January. The integrated profile's two 70-hour integrations fit the common 8,400-minute allocation. This ordering follows declared work requirements, not evidence that integration is intrinsically easier for one ownership structure. A measured target requirement, quality failure, different support priority or shared staff arrangement can reverse it.

## Qualified supply and payer liquidity remain independent gates

Moving board qualification and the manufacturing attempt to August moves the first licensed commissioning to October. Under the same annual integration reservation, the full fleet still waits until January and supported exposure falls to 23 robot-months. If qualification moves but the July order does not, no new units are manufactured: later inventory is usable stock, not an automatically executed order. Compatible receipts, qualification and an explicit action date are all necessary.

Eliminating model-supplier opening cash prevents the licensed profile's initial atomic qualification. Annual minutes, initial engineering, demonstrations and replacement parts remain unconsumed; fixed staffing and site obligations continue. A prospective fee or pooled company valuation is not automatically usable working cash for the payer at that phase.

Conversely, the model-led operator starts and pays for two new units but never successfully commissions them during this window under the original funding allocation. Its $72,000 manufacture payment, 48 manufacturing actuators and two boards remain consumed. Qualification failure does not refund successful upstream work. The two opening units still provide some supported service. These are different failure contracts: an unperformed atomic candidate consumes nothing, whereas an executed earlier action remains sunk. The separate learning-execution branch also retains actually performed failed R&D costs; it is not merged or overwritten here.

## Implications for the three decision packs

For hospital logistics, keep the complete deadline/custody service and feasible human dispatch beside every proposed model improvement. Identify whose workflow integration, testing, repair and site work is required, which pools are actually shared, and which entity must prepay them. A reduction in model work should advance a demonstrable qualified service date or reduce a relevant paid commitment before it enters the proceed case.

For utility inspection, preserve accepted due measurements, review and residual physical visits. A better onboard or remote model does not supply the operator's integration staff, qualified replacement part or valid service entitlement. The [inspection-readiness evidence](quadruped_operator_followup.md#rollout-retained-work-and-robot-repair-edition-032) leaves actual customer work and robot-maintenance coverage open; repeated activity cannot calibrate this cleaning model's resource requirements.

For automotive workcells, retain the same accepted kits, deadline and competent specialist/manual alternative. The [workcell and learning models](learning_execution.md) show why eligible data and physical validation matter; this arrangement adds the cash/resource interaction, but does not combine those independently executed cases into one feasible portfolio. No cleaning-zone output is converted into kits, hospital trips or inspection points.

The resulting recommendation is a conditional wait gate on unverified economics and a proceed gate for bounded measurement: document accepted service, configured qualification, retained work, shared versus distinct resources, compatible parts and every payer's dated obligations. Reject a claim that faster model work alone proves earlier complete service, removed payroll, self-funded expansion or a preferred ownership architecture. Unknown lifecycle costs, terminal assets, financing and real rights still prevent a defensible target ROI or investment ranking.

## Falsifiers and next evidence

The bottleneck result would change if integration were actually completed earlier, the lower-adaptation configuration failed the unchanged acceptance criterion, the same engineers were shared across supposedly distinct pools, the role receiving a fee could legally and operationally finance the needed payer, or human capacity could not meet the complete obligation. Collect dated per-unit admission, actual staff assignments/work, configuration/part releases, invoices and accepted service before claiming any of those changes. An executed target ledger or an explicit simultaneously conserved competing workload would be more valuable than another favorable scenario variant.
