# Calendar receipts: learner exercise
1. Derive the finite-horizon HJB when qualified production and development run concurrently; amend if same robot is taken offline.
2. From two equal exponential completion times derive exact A-only, AB and still-developing occupancy at t and integrate fixed-route net cash flow.
3. Show the nonempty interval where joint is better for short horizon and staged A-first for long horizon. Why is the interval not an optimal-policy theorem?
4. Solve long-horizon deficit recursion D(AB)=0 and state values with a=2,mu=1.2,v=4.5,W=10,c=1. Compare adaptive control and the previous terminal-only budget problem.
5. Determine peak required initial cash for cost buckets (1.5,1.5,1.5) and actual collections (0,2,2). Recalculate if receipts arrive only after third payment.
6. With B=.5,T=2,a=b=2,mu=1.2,c=1,v=4.5, derive target completion probability and the advance that gives joint the same completion probability. Does it buy equal profits or welfare?
7. Design a site-level negative control for the alleged productive intermediate service when qualification training uses shared hardware/supervisors and invoices can be delayed.
