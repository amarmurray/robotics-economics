# November 22 Models A/B bridge — production-generated versus deliberate data

**November 19 continuation, scientific gate still OPEN.** The linked [two-date chapter](TWO_DATE_DATA_ACQUISITION.md) combines the frozen observation/selection Model A with heterogeneous dynamic investment Model B without double-valuing data.

**Model A: fixed physical policy, qualified service.** Hold customer original-order population, Q, resource and operator requirements and entire production/recording intervention fixed. For a pre-action reporter S defined on every original order, selected O identifies only the interval from the [target selection chapter](TARGET_REPORT_SELECTION_THEORY.md) unless validated positive recording or independent target support exists. A conditional Blackwell information comparison is possible only when the reference state and physically approved decision menu match. Gross optional information has weakly positive value, but its incremental value falls when a robust embodiment substitutes for diagnosis, and rises when a new target-specific qualified service is only profitable in some market regimes.

**Model B: dynamic joint production-data-action investment.** A single Bellman objective includes date-0 productive service, rights-cleared source acquisition, independent nonactuating audit, stage-1 physical grounding, changed embodiment, joint customer qualification and terminal accepted service, with customer demand and operator/recovery impacts. A productive R can generate 100 selected traces that are diagnostically uninformative under an unknown/report-dependent sampling mechanism, while a deliberately sampled target audit improves the *economic policy choice*. In the synthetic example R=9/2, perfect A=24/5, whereas an equally informative free R byproduct reaches 6. An audit of accuracy α produces physical-choice transitions E→X at 13/18 and X→G at 5/6, but beats productive R only above 19/20. All thresholds are conditional and synthetic; they are not observed robotic training rates or rents.

The novelty objection is strong: Blackwell simulation, Bellman recursion, selective labels, data from production, qualified service optimization and complementary options have substantial prior art. The candidate paper contribution is a falsifiable joint **source-data/target-support/embodiment versus whole-customer-production** theory and evidence map, not an invented new named statistical theorem. No physical approval, signed original-order economics, source-to-joint-qualification calibration, complete BLS–BEA numeric crosswalk or independent proof review.
