# Student exercises — data-transfer acquisition options and production

All contributions below are complete hypothetical captured whole-network customer service units, not benchmark scores/dollars. Assume approved policies and tests unless challenged.

1. For potential qualification success probabilities p=q=1/2, find the feasible interval of cross-arm co-success t. With retained specialist S=2 and any qualified x or g yielding 4, compute gross service if both are tested before deployment.
2. Each test costs 1/5. Source x is tested first, then g only upon source failure, with no source→g carryover and unchanged customer opportunity. Derive the optimal threshold in t for ordering g. Evaluate staged and upfront values for t=0,1/4,1/2.
3. Explain why population-average policy means from a clean independent-network static four-arm randomized factorial do not identify the staged option value. Give one physically admissible experimental design targeting the missing conditional.
4. For x, g and new embodiment h, build two joint binary qualification distributions with equal success rates 1/2 and pairwise co-success 1/4 but different probability of any success. Compute total net value of all three tests with fallback 2, qualified policy 4 and each cost 1/5.
5. What happens if target customer orders expire after the first qualification fails? What if the g controller is not physically approved? Which of free optional data availability and mandatory paid rollout can be harmful?
6. Identify a pre-intervention customer-order denominator, matched complete qualification event, controller lineage, recovery/operator resource record and costs needed to distinguish static coupling from sequential learned transfer. Explain why marginal task benchmark success is insufficient.
