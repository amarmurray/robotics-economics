## 1. Economic premises and question

A firm can know a source task very well and still have no defensible mapping from that task to a new body or end-market context. Pairwise grounding is costly. Some relationships can be composed through intermediate bodies or contexts, whereas others are local and do not compose. A firm therefore faces two distinct investments in interpretability: increasing the **reach** of its collection of demonstrated relationships, and investigating **whether a proposed rule of composition is valid** for a class of future tasks. The second can change the economic value of already-retained evidence without adding a new source trajectory or improving any incumbent controller. It can also rule out a transfer route and redirect capital toward ordinary work or direct target learning.

Imagine an arm, a different gripper and a mobile manipulator solving matched handling subtasks. A pairwise experiment might identify a transformation that maps a correction across two configurations. Composition via a third configuration is a hypothesis, not a right to release an untested policy. A cross-check could test the common representation, even if the extra pair is not itself a valuable customer market. In manufacturing, inspection, or digital exception routing, the analogous question is whether an inferred correction transports through several interfaces or only works in the directly observed combination. These are illustrative mechanisms, not documented empirical measurements.

Keep four objects separate: source skill information; the pairwise interpretation relation; knowledge about the class of relations; and the dated qualified production menu. Rights, validation, demand, commissioning, support and funding govern the last. A competent fixed contingent procedure can implement all the Bayesian choices below: discovery of relation structure is not proof of recursive organizational redesign. Ordinary production that already produces paired comparisons belongs in the baseline.

## 2. A graph experiment in uncertain relative interpretability

Fix a class of n deployment contexts, indexed by embodiment, task family, operating policy and measurement conditions. Use binary signs only to isolate the distinction. Let H=1 mean a shared composable representation is valid in this class. With prior P(H=1)=p, assign independent uniform latent vertex labels Z_i in {-1,+1}. The exact pair relation M_ij=Z_i Z_j under H=1. Under H=0 the pair relations M_ij are independent uniform signs, local to their pair-specific matched experiment; they are not asserted to be globally consistent task labels. No absolute vertex sign is identified by pair comparisons alone. Each permitted matched relation experiment reveals one M_ij exactly in this benchmark, without observing H. The experiments are distinct edges; repeated readings of one edge are not independent checks. No real test is assumed perfect.

Let G be the graph of observed edges, with n vertices (including isolates), m edges, c connected components, and cycle rank r=m-n+c. An observed pattern is consistent if the product along every cycle is +1.

**Proposition 1 (reach and coherence are distinct).** Under the above priors, conditional on any observed consistent pattern,

    P(H=1 | G,M_G) = p 2^r / [1-p+p 2^r].

If an inconsistent pattern occurs, the posterior is zero. For an unobserved pair in the same connected component, the posterior mean of M_ij is this posterior times the product of observed edge signs along any path. For a pair in different components the posterior mean is zero. In particular a forest identifies relative path signs conditional on the shared representation, but gives no statistical evidence for its validity.

Proof. A spanning forest has n-c edges. Under H=1, the observed consistent pattern fixes all labels relative to one free uniform root per component, so exactly 2^c of 2^n label assignments fit. Its likelihood is 2^{-(n-c)}; an inconsistent pattern has likelihood zero. Under H=0, any m-bit pattern has probability 2^{-m}. Bayes' rule yields the displayed posterior. Under H=1, if i and j are connected their product is fixed by the path, otherwise independent root signs imply mean zero. Under H=0 an unmeasured edge is independent of observed edges. Averaging over H gives the result. This is a counting proof, not an inferred empirical scaling law.

Two marginal experiments therefore do different things. A bridge between previously disconnected components increases reach, potentially unlocking many as-yet unobserved pairs (up to the product of component sizes, less the one directly measured pair); it supplies no evidence about H. An additional edge closing an independent cycle in a consistent component increases r by one, leaving reach unchanged but testing the entire shared hypothesis. If it agrees with the existing path, posterior odds double; otherwise the shared hypothesis is rejected. The actual edge measured is directly known regardless of H; the posterior prediction formula concerns OTHER unmeasured edges.

This derives an endogenous transfer map for a class, not a fixed positive matrix. A class-specific cycle audit changes the beliefs governing every unmeasured connected pair in that class, while a bridge changes which pairs are even eligible for this route. Several independent classes have distinct posteriors; learning in one does not update the others unless an additional shared hyperprior is justified. With noisy observations the all-or-nothing rejection and cycle-rank sufficiency disappear; independent cycles, heterogeneous observation errors and model misspecification need a full likelihood. Exact sign synchronization is a prior statistical foundation, not our novelty.

## 3. Value of testing the common representation

Consider future fresh tasks using unmeasured pairs in a connected component. For a chosen task, let the source bit X be uniform and independent of H and the already-observed graph. The target bit is theta = X M_ij for that pair. A source report has symmetric reliability a, defined as 2 times accuracy minus one; a direct local target report has symmetric reliability d, independent of source error and relation conditional on theta. With known graph path product and posterior p (now shorthand for the current posterior), transfer is a symmetric target report of reliability a p. Two available independent symmetric reports and equal error losses give optimal target-choice correctness

    Q_j(a,p,d_j) = [1+max(a p,d_j)]/2.

Here the graph path is inferred, not the actual edge. Each fresh task uses a local decision; do not pool other tasks' reports before its dated validation in this restricted model. If a shared H creates dependence across recipients, joint qualification and later information must be modeled with their joint law, not independent Bernoulli draws. The max formula is a local decision result, not authorization to operate. An independent complete test in the synthetic production restriction below admits a correct choice and rejects an incorrect one, with a conventional fallback; no safety conclusion follows for actual robots.

An independent extra cycle check agrees with the current path with probability z=(1+p)/2. Upon agreement posterior p_+=p/z; upon disagreement p_-=0. For k distinct additional chords of an observed connected tree, with independently local edges under H=0, all k agree with probability z_k=p+(1-p)2^{-k}; posterior on that event is p/z_k. Any contradiction yields p=0. This experiment has fixed known cost and does not consume the service slots in the initial restriction.

Let V(p) be the optimized discounted captured contribution of a finite feasible future production and capital menu, conditional on this posterior, excluding the new audit cost. The policies can include source acquisition, a direct report, ordinary data, a new body, and old qualified recipes. With a common action/information menu across p and a fixed two-regime law, each fixed feasible policy has payoff affine in p; V is the upper envelope of affine functions and thus convex. This argument also allows future observations when the policy is a fixed mapping from histories. It requires the same feasible policies at each p (e.g., no posterior-dependent safety rule that removes an action); genuine changes in constraints require separately solved programs.

**Proposition 2 (option value of learning the transfer structure).** The gross gain from k costless optional checks is

    G_k(p) = z_k V(p/z_k) + (1-z_k)V(0) - V(p) >= 0.

Proof: z_k(p/z_k)+(1-z_k)0=p; convexity. For 0<p<1 and k>=1 the gain is strictly positive precisely when V is not affine on [0,p/z_k], by the equality condition for a convex chord at an interior point. In particular, a report which only increases posterior dispersion without changing the best feasible production program has zero gross decision value even if it reduces uncertainty about H. Never add G_k as a separate premium on top of an already optimized audit program: it is that program's incremental return before its cost.

In the simple one-target menu with a fixed source reliability a>0, direct substitute d>=0 and unit payoff on a verified success, the single-check gain is

    [ z max(a p/z,d) + (1-z)d - max(a p,d) ] / 2.

It is positive precisely for 0<d<a p/z (with 0<p<1); at d=0 the same linear rule is optimal, and at d>=a p/z the direct report dominates both posteriors. Thus increased evidence of composability is not a free multiplicative increase in *expected* source accuracy. The posterior is a martingale: the increase after a confirming result is balanced by the loss on rejection. Value comes from adjusting feasible decisions, not counting only successful reports.

With k available audits at per-check cost C, independent of dated service resources, the stopping value is U_0(p)=V(p) and

    U_k(p)=max{V(p), -C + z U_{k-1}(p/z) + (1-z)V(0)}.

After contradiction no further perfect coherence check improves p. The firm can stop to protect cash and use the direct route. If C=0, each further available check weakly raises value by information nesting; for C>0, even a high posterior may rationally go unaudited. When a chord uses the same commissioning window as service, replace V by the joint feasible program with one less remaining slot and the corresponding expiry/cash state. Gross posterior information is still nonnegative for a free optional report, but purchasing it can be inferior. An ordinary operation producing the same lawful report on the same date removes the incremental gross benefit of buying that report separately.

## 4. The composition of the next fleet

Take separate eligible customer orders for heterogeneous tasks j. Each needs one validation-and-installation opportunity before a common deadline. Available slots K are reserved before local reports; there is no resequencing after report, and qualification occurs independently of the audit and must precede the actual funded installation. A verified success yields discounted net contribution w_j relative to the fallback, including purchase/operating costs once. Failure leaves the fallback and its ordinary learning; common baseline contribution cancels. Prepaid slot costs may be included in the fixed menu, or subtracted separately once. Assume enough funded bodies for every selected verified success and no other binding resources in this restricted model.

Let u in {0,1} be a source investment that moves source reliability from a0 to a1 at net cost C_s (incremental cost less its independently measured current contribution, if any). The source can be a deployment producing field evidence, a teleoperation or simulation acquisition, or a data-processing investment; this example does not claim equal productivity across modalities. Its decision is made after the audit if purchased, before selecting target slots. Given p, the optimized restricted value is

    V(p)=max_{u,S: |S|<=K} { sum_{j in S} w_j Q_j(a_u,p,d_j) - u C_s }.

Already-qualified ordinary candidate orders can have fixed success 1. Other fixed local success laws can be included. This is an expected private contribution, not the physical accepted-service MPK vector. For a fixed u and distinct candidates, top K nonnegative expected contributions solve selection. With shared dated worker time, heterogeneous requirements or coupled customer recipes use the earlier joint schedule model.

Define expected starts N(p)=sum_{j in S*(p)} Q_j. Let e_j denote expected incremental lawful, usable successor evidence units conditional on an awarded installation, not gross trajectories. At a stated selection rule E_next(p)=sum_{j in S*(p)} e_j Q_j. For the audit the changes are the expected N and E_next under the two posterior-contingent allocations minus their ordinary values. They are signed differences of whole programs, not derivatives of Q alone. If future evidence value is already included in w_j, do not add it again; if e_j measures a separate unpriced spillover, calculate its downstream value under the beneficiary's actual dated menu, not a fixed universal record price.

Three synthetic witnesses, all computed by enumerating every source purchase and slot subset. Monetary units are abstract discounted net contributions; probabilities refer to the deliberately perfect verification restriction, not robot safety scores.

* **Source expansion.** p=.4, one target w=10,d=.3, a0=.4,a1=.8,C_s=.5. Without audit source upgrade is rejected (value 6.5). After a confirming check (probability .7) upgrade is bought (net 6.785714); after contradiction the direct report is used (6.5). Gross audit option gain .2, expected source upgrade changes 0 to .7, expected recipient starts .65 to .705. At a positive check cost below .2, the audit is privately purchased under the stated windows.
* **Source contraction.** p=.6, w=10,d=.3,a0=.2,a1=1,C_s=.5. Without audit the source upgrade is bought (7.5). Confirming result with probability .8 gives net 8.25 and purchase; contradiction returns to 6.5 and no purchase. Gain .4; expected source upgrade declines 1 to .8, despite recipient starts rising .8 to .83. The source and recipient capital responses can differ in sign.
* **Recipient and successor contraction.** p=.4,a=1, d=0, one shared slot. A relation-dependent market A has w=12 and e=0. B has ordinary verified success 1, w=9.2, e=1. Initially A's expected contribution is 8.4, so B takes the slot. Confirming audit gives A expected 9.428571 and displaces B; contradiction keeps B. Gross audit value .16, expected recipient starts 1 to .85, successor evidence 1 to .3. Any cost below .16 leaves the private audit attractive. If a separate beneficiary would generate real discounted surplus L per B-award evidence unit and this is not included in w, the real incremental surplus (taking w as current real contribution in this illustration) is .16-C-.7L. Even a zero-cost audit can reduce real surplus under privately chosen allocations when L>.16/.7. A planner with optional free report and old allocations can ignore it: the negative effect is an externality of privately reoptimized decisions, not negative value of information for an optimizing planner. If the two slots become independent, B need not be displaced; the mechanism relies on actual scarcity.

These examples identify distinct mechanisms, not three unrelated functional forms. An audit makes the prior transfer network contingent on its result, reoptimizes source and recipient investments, and changes the successor evidence portfolio. A productive deployment can also yield a future bridge or chord rather than a source bit. Hence the local induced-deployment matrix of the common learning/capital paper is a function of graph reach, posterior about relation structure, active slot constraints and captured continuation. A graph edge's private investment return is not proportional to the number of technical paths it creates.

## 5. A restricted market for shared structure evidence

The same cycle report can be read by many lawful beneficiaries, but producing it consumes dated resources. Distinguish that report from a direct route supplier, whose report concerns one recipient task. For independent beneficiaries with disjoint resources, no strategic entry response or other externalities, and a committed broadcast report, compute each gross willingness W_f = E[V_f(p')]-V_f(p) relative to its best ordinary and optional direct alternatives (including ordinary production evidence). Each W_f>=0 for the free report; they need not be equal, and any one can be zero. A fixed avoidable cost C of producing the report can be voluntarily covered by nonnegative contributions t_f<=W_f iff sum W_f>=C. Necessity is summation; sufficiency follows by assigning contributions successively up to each W_f until C is met. With transferable surplus and credible commitment this gives a coalition participation certificate, not a noncooperative provision equilibrium. A competing direct supplier changes the V_f outside option and may eliminate the coalition; a competing structure report must be evaluated by its joint likelihood and timing, not by a marginal success score.

For overlapping validation or strategic rival procurement this separability fails, as the shared-slot example and [prior strategic procurement](SCARCE_SHARED_METROLOGY_MARKET.md) show. There is no claim that Bertrand competition implements the efficient report type, that unrestricted coalitions can contract on future evidence, or that additive individual valuations remain valid with scarce shared complements. The next step is to solve a dated capacity/sponsorship game in which agents choose *which* bridges and chords to test, including joint validation and strategic participation, with ordinary graph learning allowed.

## 6. Evidence, intellectual positioning and falsification

Statistical foundations: Singer's [angular synchronization](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.3174) (2009 preprint abstract; 2011 journal abstract at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3003935/) treats noisy pairwise relative measurements and recovery up to a common phase. Baxter's [A Model of Inductive Bias Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0245) (2000 journal paper, arXiv abstract posted 2011) explicitly studies learning a common bias over related tasks. These precede our work; the cycle counting, Bayes, option value and portfolio envelope are standard mathematical ideas. No theorem about statistical learning novelty is claimed. [RoVi-Aug](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03403) (September 2024 preprint abstract) studies cross-embodiment and viewpoint data augmentation and reports physical-policy experiments. This supports treating representation/embodiment transfer as a technical research problem, not the exact binary cycle model, its validity or any commercial payoff. Only abstracts were consulted November 25 Eastern; no method replication or independent novelty review.

A discriminating prospective study would hold the initial source archive, embodied capacity and recipient eligibility fixed and compare: (i) ordinary matched production records; (ii) an added bridge to a new context; (iii) a redundant chord explicitly testing composability; and (iv) independent direct target grounding. Predeclare proposed transformation classes, record the actual compared task/embodiment conditions and independent relation checks, and test transfer on held-out, UNMEASURED pairs (not the purchased edge). Measure calibration of the predicted relationship separately from controller competence, and record complete authorized joint validation, not marginal skill metrics. Then independently follow the paid programs with original customer offers, fallback and actual accepted service, resource occupation, source capex and lawful successor observations. Record branch-specific refusals and ordinary training; do not condition the economic comparison only on successfully certified tasks. Repeated edges/common latent noise cannot be counted as independent chords. A chord intervention that improves only its own measured pair but not unmeasured within-class transfer rejects the proposed broad relation mechanism. A matched direct route achieving the same service for less cost rejects its specific investment case. An observed fall in starts is not evidence of harm absent resource/counterfactual and real surplus measurement.

No matched field panel of that form, contemporary official quantitative production backbone, independent review or field calibration is obtained here. The graph model assumes exact sign measurements, two regimes, fixed shared classes and known prior; real relations may be asymmetric, noisy, continuous, temporally drifting and correlated across classes. A finite number of consistent cycles cannot demonstrate a universal transfer law. The structural alternative with local context-specific relations is intentionally sharp; extending it to approximate and overlapping shared classes is the next proof gap.
