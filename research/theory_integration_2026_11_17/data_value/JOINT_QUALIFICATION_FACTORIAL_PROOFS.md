# Proof ledger: policy-level economic factorial, not independent skill statistics

**18–19 November 2026.** Scope: complete accepted customer service within the same finite opportunity horizon and policy-closed network; all technical/causal findings conditional. This ledger deliberately avoids adding a new numbered research question or a free data premium.

**1. Factorial accounting identity.** Write J_z=pi_z·f_z-c_z and s=(+,-,-,+) for 00,10,01,11. Then sum_z s_z J_z = (sum_z s_z pi_z)·f_00 + sum_z s_z pi_z·(f_z-f_00) - sum_z s_z c_z. This holds for any finite **joint** distribution and any properly defined complete-service value table. The reference table may be chosen differently with a correspondingly different attribution; the aggregate is invariant. Demand/dispatch effects and embodiment changes are captured in f_z, not a presumed externality. If f_00 is counterfactual on treated-only support, this is algebra rather than an identified structural partition.

**2. Marginal insufficiency is constructive.** For binary (q1,q2), consider distribution weights over (none,only1,only2,both) equal to (t,2-t,2-t,t)/4 for t∈{0,1,2}. Each marginal passes with probability (2-t+t)/4=1/2. Let arms 00/10/01 have t=1. With a joint-only service worth 4, t_11=2 gives J=(1,1,1,2), hence I=+1; t_11=0 gives J=(1,1,1,0), hence I=-1. The measured individual qualification rates are equal in all arms and both worlds. Independence is therefore a substantive identifying restriction, not harmless shorthand. A 2x2 production-recipe table can reverse the sign independently of this joint-law mechanism.

**3. A positive qualification interaction need not be a positive production interaction.** Keep t=(1,1,1,2), reference f(q)=4 if both qualify, zero otherwise. Then I_qualification=+1. If f_11(both)=2 while all other f_z=f_00, I_response=-1. All J arms equal 1 and I_total=0. This explicitly allows a change in original orders, capacity and customer response; it does not attribute it causally to an unobserved particular mechanism. The [exact JS](joint_qualification_factorial.js) enumerates 243 dyadic law/table cases and tests the identity with arm-specific costs.

**4. Randomization identification.** For k=1..N fix all Y_k(z) in advance for four safely admitted policies. Given independent positive-probability assignment and no cross-unit interference, E[1{Z_k=z}Y_k(Z_k)/e_k(z)] = Y_k(z). Summation, linearity and factorial signs yield E[I_hat]=(1/N)sum_k[Y_k(11)-Y_k(10)-Y_k(01)+Y_k(00)]. No independence of Y(00),Y(10),Y(01),Y(11) within a cluster is used. A complete balanced four-cluster randomized design has 24 assignments; exact finite enumeration reproduces the population contrast. This is classical design-based identification, not a newly discovered causal theorem.

**5. If 11 is unobserved.** Assume potential whole-network contribution Y(11)∈[L,U] for eligible and *defined* 11 outcomes. Subtracting identified other three population means yields bounds [L-mu10-mu01+mu00, U-mu10-mu01+mu00], sharp without restrictions on missing 11 potential outcomes. If 11 is physically inadmissible, it is not a deployment alternative and the hypothetical bound does not create one. If other arm values are uncertain, optimize a **joint** feasible set rather than subtracting independently chosen endpoints as though joint-world restrictions were known.

**6. Optionality.** An old feasible policy menu P is a subset of P' after genuinely free access, if old policies remain retained. max_{p∈P'}J(p)≥max_{p∈P}J(p), but a forced x=1 arm and acquired/trained source may reduce J; a factorial second difference has no monotonicity implication. Retained F and competent S must remain real admissible comparators.

**7. Causal-unit counterexample.** One dispatcher routes a fixed B order to either of two network “clusters.” A source-trained A site wins the order, reducing the other site's accepted B orders. Even with randomly assigned site labels, the potential outcome of one site depends on the other site's assignment; hence E[Y_k|Z_k=z] does not equal a stable Y_k(z) holding other assignments fixed. Independent site randomization estimates a particular saturation/allocation experiment, **not** a four-policy complete-network effect. Similarly, controller updates shared across sites destroy the assumed separate assignments.

**8. Data-level and evidence boundaries.** Full qualification Q is a joint, version-pinned held-out acceptance event, not a marginal benchmark score. A source-copy-only pre-admission experiment can estimate learning effects but not the target controller's qualified customer production. A non-trivial eta gap from previous first-exit theory is unnecessary under valid whole-network factorial randomization, but nothing in this proof establishes that such independent randomized units or safe treatments actually exist.
