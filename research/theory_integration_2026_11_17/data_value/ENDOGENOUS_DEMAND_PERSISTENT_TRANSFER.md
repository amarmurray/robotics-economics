# When Capabilities Become Production
## Endogenous customer demand, visited-state support, and one persistent transfer regime

**Conditional integrated research continuation — November 18, 2026. Private manuscript material, not externally published.** Follows [TRANSPORT_AND_SAFETY_IDENTIFICATION.md](TRANSPORT_AND_SAFETY_IDENTIFICATION.md) and [ROBUST_MODELS_AB_SYNTHESIS.md](ROBUST_MODELS_AB_SYNTHESIS.md). The exact unresolved question is whether a defensible production/grounding/embodiment choice can be bounded when the choice changes both the next customer's order and the robot states from which learning is possible, while one latent source-to-target regime persists across the entire history. This is a constructive continuation of the same qualified-production argument, **not** a fresh Q-number or assertion of original robust-control theory.

### 1. Why the prior transport certificate is insufficient

The earlier identity \(D_\mu=\sum_z\mu_z[b_z-k_z-\Delta_z(q_E(z)-q_R(z))]\) is valid when the eligible customer-order population \(\mu\), resource-window valuations, and route-specific acceptance definitions can be held fixed, or all changes are accounted for in pretreatment strata. It is not a total causal value formula if initial deployment affects repeat orders, referrals, service-price capture, state visitation, operator intervention, future qualification, or the customer opportunities on which later margins are earned. In particular, weighting **post-treatment observed orders** by a common \(\mu\) changes the estimand and can condition on a policy consequence.

Keep the already established objects. \(Q_t\) is the body/controller and retained qualified recipe menu, rights, learning stock, and approved safe operating resources. \(\omega_t\) includes the customer demand/order window, contract capture, outside specialist option, and shared production and organizational resources. \(V(Q_t;\omega_t)\) is the **single** optimal captured contribution from actually accepted production within those resources: \(V=\max_{y\in\mathcal Y(Q_t,\omega_t)}f_{\rm accepted}(y;\omega_t)\). Source experience \(x\), target grounding \(g\), and embodiment \(h\) alter the joint transition into \(Q_{t+1}\); they do not separately earn invented dollars per benchmark point.

Let \(\theta\in\Theta\) denote a persistent, potentially unobserved regime of source-to-target transfer, demand response and recovery. For policy \(\pi\) adapted to observed history \(H_t\), the **complete controlled kernel** \(K_\theta\) generates the next qualified recipes, realized customer requests, safe state exposure, reports/corrections, customer acceptance and peak recovery requirements together. A finite-horizon total captured-value calculation is
\[
J(\pi;m)=\mathbb E_m^\pi\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\delta^t\{v_t^{\rm accepted}-c_t^{\rm acquisition/embodiment}\}
+\delta^T V(Q_T;\omega_T)\right], \tag{ED1}
\]
where \(m\) is one admissible complete joint law, including one \(\theta\) trajectory and its initial prior. If accepted service for period \(T\) is already in \(V\), it is not also in \(v_T\); if contemporaneous receipts are counted as cash, do not count the same receipts as an independent "data premium." Specialized and old qualified policies remain feasible within the menu when rights/resource constraints allow them.

A policy is **uniformly pathwise feasible** if, for every \(m\) in the identified set \(\mathcal M\), every positive-support observed history and every supported physical continuation along which it actually attempts a service, its cumulative required recovery/operator/physical resources fit approved limits and a valid fallback exists. It cannot justify an unsafe branch by its low probability or high expected customer contribution. Costs of *unattempted* actions are not retroactively charged as observed overruns.

### 2. The correct robust comparison retains the same complete possible world

For two **fixed** feasible complete-history policies \(\pi,\sigma\), define
\[
L_{\pi,\sigma}=\inf_{m\in\mathcal M}[J(\pi;m)-J(\sigma;m)],\qquad
M_\pi=\inf_{m\in\mathcal M}J(\pi;m). \tag{ED2}
\]
\(L_{\pi,\sigma}>0\) certifies same-world uniform dominance of \(\pi\) over \(\sigma\), whereas \(M_\pi>M_\sigma\) only ranks worst achievable payoffs. Neither is a novel definition, and the latter **does not imply** the former. The same \(\theta\), customer response and potential transition law must be used for both counterfactual policies in each comparison; one cannot select an adverse world for \(\pi\) and a separately convenient favorable world for \(\sigma\) and label their difference a sharp paired bound.

**Proposition ED1 — exact persistent-prior reduction.** Suppose \(\Theta=\{H,L\}\), the full conditional controlled kernels \(K_H,K_L\) are fixed and common across route comparisons, an unknown **one-time** prior probability \(p=P(\theta=H)\) lies in \([\ell,u]\), and all complete policies compared are feasible under both regimes. Write \(w_{\pi H}=J(\pi;\theta=H)\), \(w_{\pi L}=J(\pi;\theta=L)\), with all policy-dependent orders, safe supports, reports and adaptations already included. Then
\[
L_{\pi,\sigma}=\min_{p\in\{\ell,u\}}\{p(w_{\pi H}-w_{\sigma H})+(1-p)(w_{\pi L}-w_{\sigma L})\}; \tag{ED3}
\]
similarly \(M_\pi\) minimizes the affine mixture of its two conditional values at those endpoints. Proof: a single initial \(p\) mixes two **complete trajectory** conditional laws, so both expectations are affine in \(p\). An affine function on a closed interval reaches its minimum at an endpoint. This reduction does not require stationary demand or equal visited-state support under the two actions. It **does** require specified conditional kernels, one shared regime prior and valid safe full-history policies; it is not a robust solution under arbitrary unknown feedback laws.

If only credible per-policy, per-regime intervals \(w_{\pi\theta}\in[\underline w_{\pi\theta},\overline w_{\pi\theta}]\) are available, a conservative (not necessarily sharp) paired lower bound replaces \(w_{\pi\theta}-w_{\sigma\theta}\) by \(\underline w_{\pi\theta}-\overline w_{\sigma\theta}\) in ED3. It remains valid when those endpoints are incompatible in the same physical world, but loses the power of paired evidence. When the law itself depends on an unknown common parameter \(\eta\), the correct object is \(\inf_{(p,\eta)\in\mathcal M}\sum_\theta p_\theta[w_{\pi\theta}(\eta)-w_{\sigma\theta}(\eta)]\), **not** separate minimizations of its stages.

### 3. One auditable two-period customer-production construction

All numbers below are **invented normalized captured contribution units**. They are neither dollars nor estimated robot performance. Take two dates, no discount, one authorized peak operator/recovery budget \(B=2\) for the terminal attempted service, and a persistent \(H\) (source aligns to target) or \(L\) (source misaligns), with \(p\in[2/5,3/5]\). The routes are R routine productive acquisition, G deliberate target grounding, E redesigned embodiment and S a qualified specialist/retained baseline.

| First action | First-period accepted contribution less acquisition cost | Next customer order / qualified and safely supported service under H | Next customer order / service under L | Total H, L |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| R | +2 | High B bundle order, target reached, jointly accepted B, recovery 2, captured +12 | Target B not safely reached/qualified; low surviving specialist order, recovery 1, captured +1 | 14, 3 |
| G | −1 | Grounding qualifies B, supported recovery 2, contracted B captured +8 | Grounding unlocks previously unvisited target support, qualifies B, recovery 2, newly contracted B captured +11 | 7, 10 |
| E | −1.5 | New body qualifies and safely produces B, recovery 1, captured +9 | Same accepted output, recovery 1, captured +9 | 7.5, 7.5 |
| S | +3 | Safe specialist service +3, then +3 | Same +3, then +3 | 6, 6 |

Every B in this **deliberately simplified** table is a *whole held-out accepted-service recipe* enabled in \(Q\), not a marginal motion-success percentage. Its captured value is calculated once from the same fixed-configuration \(V(Q;\omega)\) concept. The route itself changes \(\omega_1\): routine H produces strong follow-on demand; routine L leaves only a low-value safe fallback; grounding L can generate a qualified order previously unavailable. The paths are structurally separate potential histories, **not** exogenous site weights that can be transported by T1. Grounding's −1 is real opportunity/acquisition cost and S is a competent economic comparator. In R/L the disallowed B action is **not** attempted; the authorized specialist fallback is paid only if its remaining order exists.

The exact conditional vector is \(w_R=(14,3)\), \(w_G=(7,10)\), \(w_E=(15/2,15/2)\), \(w_S=(6,6)\). ED3 gives pure-route robust floors \((7.4,8.2,7.5,6)\). Thus **G is the best pure maximin route**, despite R delivering more in H and E offering full embodiment support. Also \(L_{G,E}=.7>0\), so G uniformly beats E in this particular identified set. By contrast the paired R−G value is \(-1.4\) at \(p=.4\) and \(+1.4\) at \(p=.6\); G is not uniformly superior to R. These distinctions prevent a worst-case ranking from being mislabeled a same-world theorem.

**Proposition ED2 — a qualified mixed allocation can dominate every pure robust floor.** If the operator can **ex ante** randomize across independent deployment opportunities or allocate a fractional fleet without shared-controller interference, use R on \(x=3/14\) and G on \(1-x=11/14\). Its conditional contribution is \(x14+(1-x)7=8.5\) in H and \(x3+(1-x)10=8.5\) in L. Hence its robust floor is exactly 8.5, above all four pure floors. No other mixture of the four routes has a larger worst-case expectation: the admissible prior \(p=1/2\) values R and G at 8.5, E at 7.5 and S at 6, providing a matching upper bound for **every** mixture. This is elementary zero-sum mixture/convexification mathematics, not a novel robotics optimization theorem. A single indivisible fleet upgrade, correlated sites, liquidity constraints, pooled data spillovers, or a physical ban on randomization invalidates the ex-ante mixture menu and leaves G the preferred pure maximin route.

The example is also a negative result about fixed transfer multipliers: routine's H/L technical reachability differences, policy-induced customer demand, grounder's new action support and E's new hardware all matter separately. Neither a single transfer scalar nor a source-data count can reproduce the total ranking without those transition assumptions.

### 4. Why rectangular robust Bellman updates can price the wrong world

There is a genuine information-structure distinction even when all actions are physically safe. Let \(\theta\in\{H,L\}\) persist; \(p\in[1/4,3/4]\) is selected **once**. A next-period order \(Y\in\{H,L\}\) is a fair coin **independent of \(\theta\)**. The accepted-service recipe demanded by H yields one normalized contribution only if \(\theta=H\); the recipe demanded by L yields one only if \(\theta=L\). The policy observes Y, chooses the order's feasible recipe and retains the same regime-dependent competence. Under **every** complete law with the one initial \(p\),
\[
\mathbb E_p[{\rm accepted\ contribution}]
=\tfrac12p+\tfrac12(1-p)=\tfrac12. \tag{ED4}
\]
A stage-/order-rectangular adversary instead splices \(p_H=1/4\) on the Y=H branch and \(p_L=3/4\) on Y=L, assigning the artificial value \(\tfrac12(1/4)+\tfrac12(1-3/4)=1/4\). This assembled law contradicts the stated common \(p\) and Y-independence. The extra conservatism is an *assumption change*, not evidence that learning is worthless. The example does **not** assert all rectangular algorithms are invalid: Iyengar's 2005 robust MDP and Epstein–Schneider's 2003 recursive priors deliberately impose rectangularity for their own well-defined formulations. The proper choice is to state whether nature can select separate conditional laws after different reports, or whether a physical persistent regime constrains the entire experiment.

More generally, posterior beliefs after report \(y\) under a known likelihood are \(p(\theta\mid y,\pi)\propto p(\theta)L_\theta(y\mid \pi)\), but posteriors at different reports are linked through the **same initial prior**. A single posterior interval cannot be independently reassigned to every node in a full-history maximin problem unless rectangular closure is explicitly accepted. Relaxing to rectangular closure **does** yield a valid conservative lower bound for a fixed policy if its induced set of complete laws strictly contains \(\mathcal M\), but may be loose or reverse a decision. This is standard dynamic ambiguity theory, not asserted original research.

### 5. Why more routine logs may not identify the comparison

**Proposition ED3 — on-policy observational equivalence.** Keep every R path, observed report, customer order, recovery, accepted output, E outcome and S outcome in the table unchanged. Construct world W+ with G/L next qualified-customer B contribution 11, as above. Construct W− with G/L contribution 5 instead, due for example to unobserved customer willingness to pay or weaker grounding qualification. If no G/L histories are observed and no bridge restriction ties their responses to R, both complete worlds generate the **same distribution of arbitrary quantities of R-only observations**. Yet G's robust floor is 8.2 in W+ and 5.2 in W−; E remains 7.5. Thus G outranks E in W+ and loses to E in W−. No amount of **additional R-only data** point identifies this route ranking. Proof is by the identical restricted likelihood and opposite inequalities. This is a positivity/off-policy identification limit, not a claim that deliberate G is technically superior: a safe randomized G intervention or an independently validated transfer restriction could discriminate.

The observed eligible-order denominator must include nonattempted, aborted and lost future orders. Merely observing "low R interventions" is insufficient to determine whether an unvisited G target state exists, whether recovery is approved, or what customer would buy after G. A forced inappropriate training recipe may create negative transfer; allowing an old policy to remain a **costless optional menu choice** cannot lower the optimized \(V\) under unchanged resources and rights. The observational countermodel does not contradict that free-disposal result.

### 6. Practical empirical discrimination and the November 22 synthesis gate

The design needs *joint* held-out service acceptance (00/10/01/11 for complementary tasks), independent safe exposure/gating/recovery audits, and the later customer-order response **under assigned first action**, not post hoc "successful deployment" sampling. Freeze controller lineage or randomize at the independent fleet-learning pool; record the full eligible customer population and calendar. Match R/G/E/S on site, initial order opportunity, rights, staffing, model version, safe diagnostic regime and resource budgets; record deliberate field probes, productive field trajectories, failures and recoveries, teleoperation, simulation and synthetic/public pretraining **as distinct sources**. Compare conditional customer service and next-period demand, not benchmark-to-wage arithmetic.

A defensible first test stratifies on baseline *pre-action* commercial demand and opportunity cost, randomizes or credibly instruments the first deployment choice within safe eligibility, follows all assigned units into t1 including zero reorders, and records the joint target acceptance and costs. Where G/L is not safely observable, report the supported lower/upper value decision bounds instead of pretending importance weights recover missing counterfactual support. The [companion design](ENDOGENOUS_DEMAND_EMPIRICAL_DESIGN.md) states a prospective data schema and falsifiers; no customer panel exists.

For November 22, the coherent argument is now: source learning and embodiment choice \(\to\) feasible visits, informative corrections and retained qualification \(\to\) jointly accepted production, recovery and current cash \(\to\) *endogenous* customer order/price opportunity \(\to\) next-period investment. Model A exactly embeds one divisible demand/capture margin; Model B is its deterministic, known-transition investment specialization. The missing scientific elements are observed customer/order and robotic state transitions, a credible baseline industry production matrix, an independent proof/prior-art review, and selection of a nontrivial *empirically testable* first-paper contribution. Mathematical existence of the above counterexamples alone passes **none** of those gates.
