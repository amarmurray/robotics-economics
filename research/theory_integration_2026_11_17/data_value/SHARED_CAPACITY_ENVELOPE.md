## Why the stratification result needs a qualification-to-production bridge
Section 4's summed bounds remain valid for actual whole-program accepted events with a common additive accounting measure. But independent extremizer assembly and the selective-grounding rule require independent feasible production and learning histories. In particular, potential per-order qualification is not actual per-order acceptance. Even with fully observed pre-decision qualification and zero technical interference, joint demand for a common robot or recovery worker can invalidate a sum of independently optimized stratum values. Shared learned controllers can alter the dependence of qualification across concurrent orders, not merely each marginal.

Consider one dated service window, n original simultaneously eligible, distinct customers, unit-resource recipes, K integer interchangeable slots, 0<=K<=n, fixed nonnegative net contributions w1>=...>=wn, and known eligibility/qualification/authorization A_i in {0,1} before dispatch. No other resource constraints or subsequent history in this restriction. All accepted receipts are counted once, incremental investment costs separate. Let p_i=Pr(A_i=1), N_j=sum_{i<=j} A_i, w_{n+1}=0, d_j=w_j-w_{j+1}. Model A's realized optimum f(A) selects up to K highest-valued qualified orders. If readiness or price is not observable before dispatch, this is an oracle objective, not an achievable guarantee. Conditional on a known pre-treatment demand/slot type, apply the result within that type, then average. No assumption of independent readiness.

## Result: sharp capacity-constrained economic envelope
For each prefix j, let T_j be the sum of its min(K,j) largest marginal probabilities. Then
L = sum_j d_j T_j <= E[f(A)] <= U = sum_j d_j min(K,sum_{i<=j}p_i).
These endpoints are simultaneously attainable over all joint readiness laws with the stated marginals. Intermediates follow by mixing laws. This is a weighted uniform-matroid rank specialization of established convex/concave closure mathematics, not a claim of independent theorem novelty.

Proof. By telescoping, f(A)=sum_j d_j min(K,N_j). For any subset of at most K prefix orders the count of its qualified orders is at most min(K,N_j); maximizing its expected count gives T_j. Jensen for the concave truncated count gives the upper bound. The same coupling attains all lower prefix bounds: take A_i=1{U0<p_i}, U0 uniform [0,1]. The K prefix orders with highest p_i contain all qualified orders whenever there are fewer than K and are all qualified whenever there are at least K (ties arbitrary), giving equality. For the upper, define q_j=sum_{i<=j}p_i, q_0=0. With U0 uniform (0,1), set A_i=1 iff the interval (q_{i-1},q_i] contains a point U0+m for integer m. Each interval length p_i<=1 so the marginal is p_i and a prefix count is either floor(q_j) or ceil(q_j), with mean q_j. Because K is an integer, min(K,.) is affine between those consecutive integers; hence each Jensen bound is attained. Nonnegative d_j permits summing. No independence, learning scaling law or additional data premium is used.

A useful exact decomposition is
E[f]=sum_i w_i p_i - sum_j d_j E[(N_j-K)_+].
Thus a learning program's economic effect under common weights is its weighted qualification gain LESS its change in weighted overflow, allowing K to vary by program if known at dispatch. Increased average qualification can be entirely redundant in busy windows; lowering correlated failures or shifting readiness into empty windows may be more valuable than another benchmark gain. With all K>=n overflow vanishes and the separable bridge is recovered. For arbitrary multi-resource/long-duration recipes return to Model A's feasible schedule or complete-history optimization, not this unit-slot formula.

## Investment certificate and counterworlds
For compared C (evolving routine coordination) and Z=CG, each with known K and qualification marginals, use L_Z-U_C-k <= incremental net <= U_Z-L_C-k, with k the incremental committed acquisition cost and all other J terms held fixed. These are safe separate-envelope bounds, sharp if arm joint laws can vary independently; do not claim sharpness under structural cross-arm couplings. When C realized production is identified replace its envelope by the observed expected value. A genuinely pointwise expanded feasible menu at fixed costs gives gross nonnegative difference, independently of the separate-envelope formula; retained controller code alone does not establish pointwise expansion.

Two contemporaneous customers A,B each worth 10, K=1. Under C, A qualifies in half of windows, B never; optimal mean contribution 5. Target grounding makes B qualify in half, leaves A's exact readiness unchanged. World 1: B always qualifies in the same windows as A. World 2: B qualifies in precisely the complementary windows. Both have identical marginal qualification improvements, pre-treatment demand classes, capacity, incumbent readiness retention, and incremental cost 2; optimal mean contribution 5 vs 10, net incremental -2 vs +3. All bounds are attained even with pointwise nested readiness C subset Z in this example. Independent stratum optimization predicts +3 in both, falsely recommending investment in world 1. This differs from the compact draft's single mutually exclusive opportunity example: here the *same* window contains two competing orders. No negative technical transfer is necessary. More generally even a marginal improvement for each order can coincide with a worse realized joint menu: disjoint A,B readiness with p=.5 yields 10, coincident A,B at p=.6 yields 6 with K=1. This latter illustration does not preserve old feasible policies; no contradiction to optional-data monotonicity.

## Empirical discriminator
In each original predeclared demand window record ALL concurrently eligible orders, their predispatch qualification/authorization and value net of recipe-variable costs, slot occupancy and release deadlines, the selection rule, actual accepted settlements and unserved alternatives. Preserve checkpoint/learner/rights lineage and independent complete catchments per arm, not per robot sharing a scheduler or model. To identify this restricted gross expected value one needs only E[min(K,N_j)] for each value prefix (or weighted overflow), not the entire copula. These are joint predispatch observations; accepted-only logs mechanically censor overflow. If qualification is unobservable for unattempted orders, use supported bounds or prospective safe diagnostic audits, never label unserved orders failures. If prices, slot costs, acceptance, rights or continuation change, compare full J, not a sum of these terminal estimates. The one-window restricted result neither identifies training causality nor authorizes any physical action.
