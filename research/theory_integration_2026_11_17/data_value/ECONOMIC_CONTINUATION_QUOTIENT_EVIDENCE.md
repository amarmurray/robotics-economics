# Prospective audit: economic continuation sufficiency (no field data)

**21 November 2026. Specification only, no customer access or experimental authority.**

The exact research question is whether original-buyer accepted/settled SG-versus-embodiment production can be valued using a compressed lawful learner/operator/customer/rights history. The existing licensed C=UVL statistic is not assumed sufficient. On a physically and contractually approved network, preserve exposure clusters and the complete original order-to-receipt trajectory:

1. At every dispatch decision: customer identity, **offered orders including zeros**, price/captured contribution, order revelation time, permitted service recipes, qualified robot/embodiment, support of old R and E, operator and fleet time slot. Distinguish rejected/unserved orders, recoveries and refunds from completed and finally cleared service.
2. For each candidate training source: F field success/failure/recovery, T licensed teleoperation, M simulation/synthetic, public/pretrained inputs, training/curation/compute costs, whether deliberately sampled or produced while serving a paid order, model checkpoint and embodiment lineage, rights holder, reuse permission and expiry/deletion. Do not infer L from a success log.
3. For joint customer episodes U,V and rights L, retain all eight *linked* cells and subsequent offered/qualified/accepted/finally-paid new buyer orders. Preserve the time new qualified options became physically available, operator contention and customer-demand feedback.
4. Before selecting an abstraction σ, enumerate all physically/legal actions per complete history (including old optional fallback) and audit whether merged histories have equal menus, **action-conditional** complete paid contribution and **next-abstract-state** transitions. Stratify explicitly by previously unrecorded buyer-order Z, rights renewal and shared resource occupancy. Historical average conditional on C is not enough.
5. If a matched licensed program cannot reach an action or state, mark positivity/permission failure. A strict finite-horizon sign certificate needs valid uniform ε_t,δ_t bounds over all **reachable** lawful program histories or independent supported floors and upper resource envelopes. Do not use training-only benchmark percentages as payment, invent intervention permission, or charge a second data option premium.

Theoretical discriminatory estimands are the difference between expected max paid contribution **after** knowing an offered order and max expected paid contribution **before** knowing it, and the joint effect of source-enabled service options with order revelation. A site experiment with a globally shared learner must account for network-level interference. Buyer demand and whether customer service was finally paid must be measured on the same original-market population for SG and E. A hypothetical 1/2 signal value is not an empirical estimate.

**Unavailable:** signed R/S/G/SG/E arms, original-buyer settlements and all zeros, legal same-episode L lineage, E physical approval, action-conditional learner/operator/customer transition support, official numeric BEA/BLS interindustry production coefficients and independent novelty review. Model A finite fixed recipes may be auditable earlier; Model B newly learned services and data-rights feedback require a longer full-horizon closure.
