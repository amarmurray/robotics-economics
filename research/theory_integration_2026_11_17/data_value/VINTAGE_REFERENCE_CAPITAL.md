## Premises

An archive can establish that a particular procedure worked on an older body without resolving the individual latent task requirement or the body's calibration convention. This is relational knowledge: the joint relationship is known more precisely than either component. A redesign can change the relationship without destroying the information in the old observations. A preserved reference configuration can enable a relative measurement across generations; another measurement on the new body alone might not recover the relationship. The firm chooses whether to carry the old reference, pay for a fresh external diagnostic, or use ordinary production and its retained data. It must still qualify the new installation safely and meet actual demand. Reference capital is a physical or reproducible measurement capability, not an automatic monetization of an old robot or justification for vertical ownership.

The technical prior is plausible, not calibrated: Stepanova, Pajdla and Hoffmann, [Robot self-calibration using multiple kinematic chains](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.07263) (May 2018 abstract) investigate intersecting chains and redundant measurements in simulation, reporting improved observability from combined calibration. [KITE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22113) (June 2026 abstract) separates transferable interaction intent from embodiment-specific decoding; [Behavior-Aligned Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27549) (July 2026 abstract) studies cross-embodiment representations and their interaction with prior data. These are separate studies and technical settings, not evidence that an older robot must be physically retained. Chen and Waggoner's [Informational Substitutes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08636) (2017 abstract) already develops informational complementarity and decision-value methods. Source checks 25 November; abstracts only, no verified commercial payoff or claim of historical priority. DDA §5 and Appendix B already model endogenous data and complementarity.

## A relational archive and a changed body

Use signs X,A,E in {-1,+1}, with X a persistent source task method, A the original anchor convention, E the convention of the next body. The appropriate candidate target procedure is T=XE. A relative bridge observes B=AE (possibly with error). The archive is the full joint posterior, with c=E[XA]. In the solved one-step restriction X and A are marginally balanced, while c can be nonzero. This can follow from safe tests that reveal the old appropriate policy without identifying either factor separately. The redesign transition is E=AD, with independent drift sign D and mean rho=1-2d, d in [0,1/2]. These signs are symbolic, not a physical calibration prescription.

A safe, single target qualification test selects one candidate sign; its maximum success probability given the belief is (1+|E[T]|)/2. Without more information this is (1+|c|rho)/2. Observing X perfectly or observing E perfectly leaves expected optimal success unchanged in the balanced restriction. With arbitrary joint posterior, a perfect observation of E instead yields (1+max(|E[X]|,|E[XE]|))/2; perfect observation of X yields the corresponding maximum involving E[E]. Proof: the unnormalized conditional signed means for the two observations of E are (E[XE]+E[X])/2 and (E[XE]-E[X])/2; sum their absolute values. The two-point identity (|a+b|+|a-b|)/2=max(|a|,|b|) establishes the formula.

A relative bridge report Y equals D with symmetric probability q>=1/2, with independent report noise. Set l=2q-1. The expected optimal success is

    p_bridge = (1+|c| max(rho,l))/2.

Proof: T=XA D, XA independent of D and Y. Hence E[T|Y]=c E[D|Y]. The unnormalized conditional means of D are (rho+l)/2 and (rho-l)/2. Summing absolute values gives max(rho,l). Thus the bridge is useful for this one-test action only when its reliability beats prior confidence about drift and c is nonzero. When d=1/2 and q=1, the old relation is restored fully even though neither old individual parameter is identified. If the task method itself independently flips with mean lambda, both signed means are attenuated by lambda; the bridge cannot restore the missing method. For correlated drift, endogenous observation errors, or changed action feasibility, use the full joint kernel rather than this formula. One may also replace the entire relational problem with a direct target diagnostic of T.

## From relational transfer to capital choice

Suppose one original B opportunity yields incremental captured net G>0 over the best ordinary A fallback after a successful test and commissioning. The two routes have the same dated resource occupation in this restriction and the only extra bridge cost is f. Gross bridge value is

    G |c| [max(rho,2q-1)-rho]/2.

It increases with d while the prior is sufficiently weak and the bridge quality and future exposure G stay fixed. The sign can reverse if redesign reduces remaining archive relevance, the future customer disappears, or the bridge itself becomes inaccurate. A new direct target experiment may supply the same decision at lower cost. Reference ownership cannot be inferred from these assumptions.

A design witness: c=1, stable design with rho=1 and incremental accepted B contribution 4, new design with rho=0, incremental contribution 6 and setup cost .5. Under ordinary testing stable earns 4, new earns 2.5. With a perfect relative bridge costing .1, new earns 5.4 and stable still earns 4 (ignore the bridge). This assumes the bridge can fit before the test, external alternatives either do not fit or cost at least 2.5 for an equivalent direct contrast, and both designs have unchanged fallback. It illustrates a strict redesign reversal from preserving relational evidence; it is not the result of the broader synthetic parameter grid below, in which no initial redesign reversal was found. If an equally cheap direct contrast exists, the physical anchor's special return disappears.

Let W_t(b,h) be value before the next design given full belief b and anchor availability h. An optional anchor can be retired freely at any date; retention costs k_t. If keeping it for the current decision creates no additional admissible action (e.g. insufficient validation capacity) and leaves current payoffs and signal law unchanged apart from k_t, the benefit is the optimized future opportunity difference, not the current bridge payoff. For fixed current action a it is beta E[W_{t+1}(b',1)-W_{t+1}(b',0)]-k_t. When keeping changes current choices, maximize both alternatives separately. The option difference is nonnegative before carrying cost because retirement remains feasible. A permanently retired physical reference is unavailable in this model; a recreated one is an extra competing route in a general model. An old archive alone may be enough if it contains measurements allowing an equally informative relative comparison. Retaining a historical machine in all cases is not the conclusion.

## Finite repeated-decision model

The source archive is retained as an eight-state sufficient belief over (X,A,E); source identity, generation and observed outcomes enter its likelihood updates. A fresh design chosen before the next observation changes E by an independent sign with design-dependent flip probability. This transition applies to the proposed configuration even if it is not commissioned; setup costs are sunk at that date. X and A remain persistent. The original service menu at each date comprises ordinary A and at most one prospective B installation (a dated cohort, no residual service counted outside its window). There are separate dated validation slots H and production/commissioning slots P. Each diagnostic uses one H slot, each safe qualification attempt another. A successful test permits an optional B unit consuming one P slot; otherwise A can fill all slots. Ordinary A remains available and generates evidence even without any deliberate diagnostic. Unqualified B never enters paid production. No capacity is claimed available on another date; all opportunities in this small example expire at period end.

Before each design the firm chooses retain or retire, then design, then optional none, source-only, absolute target, direct external parity, or relative bridge report. After the report choose a safe candidate test or skip, then upon a successful test choose B or A. All other slots go to A. Actual selected A and B service produces source-specific noisy reports on X with different lawful capture probabilities and accuracies; missing reports have known state-independent probability in this restriction. Those observations update the joint belief before next generation. Capture is conditionally independent given the persistent state and selected portfolio, not independent of selection unconditionally. The full program is solved by finite backward induction over the actually available histories, including preparation without current test when useful for later decisions. The optimum is implementable by a fixed, belief-state-contingent procedure; there is no claimed advantage over that competent benchmark. No data bonus is paid. Terminal value is zero; all costs and incremental paid contribution are already inside the optimized path. Prior archive volume is not an economic state substitute.

Synthetic witness: c=.9; H=(1,2), P=(1,1), beta=.95; stable/redesign drift (.05,.5); ordinary A contribution 3, B contributions (4,9) net of installation/operation, setup costs (0,.7); anchor carry .3 per date, bridge .1, source and absolute .4, external direct diagnostic 3. Bridge and external report perfect, source .9, absolute perfect; ordinary A capture .4 with accuracy .8 and B capture .2 with accuracy .7. These are modeled units, not data estimates. An initial one-slot H prevents acquiring and acting on a bridge before the current expiry. The optimized initial action with anchor is keep, redesign, no extra report. It later uses a bridge. Retiring immediately instead earns 10.677 discounted contribution and 1.08 expected B installations; the optimal optional reference earns 12.22 and 1.45 respectively. Both choose redesign twice, and the first-period policy is the same except for carrying the reference. A cheap direct external diagnostic in the checked parameter grid eliminates the reference return. The reference changes expected accepted allocation and next source mix, not just test accuracy. With one fixed original service slot each date, increasing B displaces A; this is composition of production and installed B, not a demonstrated increase in total physical output or MPK of an existing unit. In a real surplus account replace private captured values by matched real benefits and costs, excluding transfers, and solve again.

## What must be measured and what remains open

A factorial design should distinguish matched archive with absolute target contrast, relative cross-vintage contrast and direct independent target qualification. Keep accepted-task roster, old safe fallback and validation clock fixed; measure which cause the old archive resolved, the conditional reliability and time cost of each contrast, released versus merely passed policy, actually commissioned units, original paid output, and the source-specific new observations those units create. Keep lineage of sensor geometry, controller and reference versions. Randomize at sharing/validation catchment or explicitly record spillovers; include canceled orders and non-admitted cases. The model predicts a larger relative bridge benefit when old relational knowledge is strong and redesign weakens absolute predictability, not if a cheap sufficient native diagnostic exists. Cross-sectional success rates cannot identify this interaction.
