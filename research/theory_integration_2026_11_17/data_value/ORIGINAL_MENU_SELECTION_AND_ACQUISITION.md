## The precise unresolved decision

Return to the *same* synthetic original procurement parent with A priced at 4, B at 3, half of parents in budget-6 and half in budget-8 strata, and an explicitly qualified and jointly executable G policy **only as a hypothesis**. A retained R policy safely supplies A; E is an already qualified alternative for comparison in the toy economy. The original natural-menu incremental joint-B acceptance rate among the high-budget parents is theta_N. With the same buyer pool, unchanged operator/resource envelope, fixed posted net economics and the one-time G certificate cost already deducted,

    V_G^N = 7/2 + (3/2) theta_N;  V_E = 9/2;  V_R = 4.

G beats E only if theta_N > 2/3. A signed buyer contract C changes a treatment version (price, warranties, rights, information and/or obligations), so theta_C may differ from theta_N even for the same robot. No C-only observation establishes theta_N. More source X logs cannot fill absent original GG procurement exposure, even if those logs improve isolated robot accuracy. Without physical joint G admission, none of these financial screens authorize live G or X.

The remaining *scientific question* is whether original-parent evidence actually identifies demand on the relevant original menu when C solicitation is selective, and if not, which acquisition action could resolve the decision without pretending to learn the unoffered market.

## Proposition: sharp composition of missing response and menu-version uncertainty

Define a **complete preassignment frame of eligible original high-budget buyer parents**. Let R=1 mean a solicited C response is recorded, rho=Pr(R=1) its coverage in the full target frame, s=Pr(Y_C=1 | R=1) the conditional positive response, and u=Pr(Y_C=1 | R=0) the *unobserved* conditional rate. Importantly, no response is not a refusal; selection may depend arbitrarily on latent willingness. Then the law of total probability, without missing-at-random, gives

    theta_C = rho s + (1-rho) u,  0<=u<=1.

Suppose a **separately justified full-original-population treatment-version restriction** |theta_N-theta_C|<=epsilon (0<=epsilon<=1) applies to the *same frame, parent budget and action timing*. It is not learned from C alone. The **sharp natural-menu parameter set** is

    L_N = max(0, rho s - epsilon)
    U_N = min(1, rho s + (1-rho) + epsilon).

Accordingly V_G^N is in [7/2+(3/2)L_N, 7/2+(3/2)U_N] under the retained physical and economic assumptions. G robustly beats E iff L_N>2/3; E robustly beats G iff U_N<2/3. Equality is an unresolved tie. If the interval straddles 2/3, neither action is ranked by these observations. If E or R has uncertain value, compare the corresponding robust *whole-policy* envelopes instead.

**Proof and sharpness.** The first identity partitions the complete parent frame and makes theta_C range exactly over [rho s, rho s+(1-rho)] as u varies over [0,1]. For any theta_C in that interval, the declared transport restriction admits precisely theta_N in [max(0,theta_C-epsilon),min(1,theta_C+epsilon)]. The union of these nested, continuously varying intervals is the displayed [L_N,U_N]; endpoints are realized by u=0 or u=1 and the maximal permitted negative/positive natural-versus-C drift. For each pair, construct Bernoulli C and natural potential responses using a shared uniform variate, so that their disagreement probability is |theta_N-theta_C|. Keep the observed respondents, original budget frame and retained/mixed/E policy outcomes fixed; selection for nonrespondents may be unrestricted. Thus every point in the interval is compatible with these assumptions and observables. Affinity of V_G gives the stated contribution range. This does not assert that such versions are physically executable or that a causal cross-menu coupling has been measured. QED.

**Generalization.** If an independently sampled nonrespondent audit yields u in [u_L,u_U], replace the lower C rate by rho s+(1-rho)u_L and the upper by rho s+(1-rho)u_U, and then expand by epsilon and clip. If s is itself only bounded, use its lower endpoint for the lower bound, upper for the upper. For disjoint buyer/site strata h with known original-frame weights w_h and nonnegative incremental contribution g_h, the conditional separable lower/upper G production gain is sum_h w_h g_h L_h and sum_h w_h g_h U_h. For negative incremental margins interchange appropriate endpoints. Under joint acceptance or shared indivisible operator capacity these separable sums are generally **invalid**: construct a whole-parent joint acceptance law and optimize V over common resources, as in the existing joint-policy counterexamples.

## Exact counterworld and acquisition ordering

Take rho=3/4, every observed C respondent positive (s=1), and independently defended epsilon=1/8. Then theta_N is only in [5/8,1]. Its lower G contribution is **71/16 = 4.4375**, less than E=4.5, whereas the upper G is 5. All responding buyers agree, but the investment is unranked. World L sets all missing C responses negative and theta_N=5/8, meeting epsilon with equality. World H makes all missing C responses positive and theta_N=1. Both worlds preserve precisely the recorded C respondents, eligible frame, past R/mixed/E outcomes and (hypothetically) the same G competence, yet reverse the G/E choice. More samples of the same respondent segment cannot distinguish them.

If an independently valid *nonrespondent* audit supports u>=1/2, the lower theta_N rises to 3/4 and lower G to **37/8 = 4.625 > 4.5**. In this toy, the *minimal* conditional nonrespondent lower bound needed for robust G is u_L>1/6, because 3/4+(1/4)u_L-1/8>2/3. More generally with s=1 and no nonrespondent audit, robust G requires rho>2/3+epsilon; **if epsilon>=1/3, no rho<=1 suffices even with universal respondent acceptance**. Thus shrinking sampling error or adding C respondents is not equivalent to resolving a genuine treatment-version mismatch. No real target-audit value or data price is estimated by this construction.

These are falsifiable **measurement choices**, not a recommendation to solicit or train: improve known selection coverage; sample the absent buyer subpopulation with auditable response propensities and report type; or obtain lawfully admitted **version-consistent natural-menu** full-parent offers and receipts. The last route can identify theta_N directly with appropriate assignment/support but requires signed joint physical G and full original-order observation. A revised binding C contract could instead create a valuable *new policy*, with its changed terms valued inside V/J once; it is not a free passive data point.

## Sampling uncertainty is separate from identification uncertainty

Let rho be known from a complete original frame. Conditional on the respondent and nonrespondent strata, suppose independently sampled Bernoulli outcomes accurately represent s and u, with samples n_R and n_U. For jointly valid confidence 1-alpha, use Hoeffding radii

    h_j = sqrt( log(4/alpha)/(2 n_j) ), j in {R,U}

and intersect s_j in [max(0,hat_s_j-h_j),min(1,hat_s_j+h_j)]. A union bound makes both bands simultaneous with at least 1-alpha under those independence/representativeness conditions. Compose the *lower* two respondent bands and *upper* two bands with rho, then with the externally validated epsilon to obtain a confidence-enclosing partial-identification interval for theta_N. These are not confidence guarantees under strategic lying, unmeasured sampling propensities, cross-customer interference, nonstationary timing, or a misspecified epsilon.

For illustration only, rho=3/4, epsilon=1/8, n_R=n_U=400, hat_s_R=1 and hat_s_U=1/2, alpha=.05 yield h=0.07401036 and **lower natural acceptance 0.67598964**. The resulting lower G=4.51398446 exceeds E=4.5 *conditional on all assumptions and independent physical G admission*. Omitting the nonrespondent stratum instead yields lower natural acceptance only 0.56949223 and no ranking at this confidence level. These are invented survey statistics, **not actual prospective buyer observations**. No Bayesian EVSI is inferred from frequentist confidence coverage; one would need a prior and a likelihood for the distinct decision calculation.

## Integrated source-data / grounding / embodiment interpretation

The intervention alphabet remains **X** (additional specific source trajectories, field failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic or pretrained material, each with lineage, training choice and rights), **G** (target grounding, sensing, customer adaptation or safe admission), **E** (changed embodiment/qualified capacity) and retained **R**. X changes a potentially correlated joint qualification law; G may unlock old source representations via observation-closed recovery; E can change the feasible production column set. Customer procurement theta_N and operator pooling determine whether these technical gains become *bought, qualified, completed and paid* service. New parent observations affect the *belief* over theta_N, not physical competence; C commitments may additionally change the *environment*. Optimize one dynamic production value J with permitted old policies and once-counted acquisition/rights/compute/curation costs. A chosen policy's current production-generated data are already in continuation value, never a second data premium. The optional free-data monotonicity argument is not contradicted by poor results from a forced training recipe.

A crucial negative result is that this audit's mathematical bounds cannot be transferred to a new customer end market with different parent mix, menu, price, recovery architecture, operator capacity or correlated purchase/qualification outcomes without new transport assumptions. The single toy threshold is not a general robotic deployment break-even rule.
