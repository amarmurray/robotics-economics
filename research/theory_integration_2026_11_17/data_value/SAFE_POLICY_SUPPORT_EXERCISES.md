# Student exercises — qualified production with safe but incomplete network support

Work from [the safe-policy support theory](SAFE_POLICY_SUPPORT_THEORY.md). Synthetic values are normalized economic contribution, not robot task percentages. Do not consult [instructor solutions](SAFE_POLICY_SUPPORT_SOLUTIONS.md) until solving.

1. Derive SS1 from a pre-action two-stratum eligibility split. State precisely which potential-outcome assumptions make the interval sharp, and why an *unapproved* embodiment remains outside the deployment menu.
2. In the documented 3/5, 2/5 strata example, compute covered difference, uncovered mass and the worldwide p-versus-s bound when both unsupported outcomes lie in [0,2]. Recompute when the unsupported specialist mean is 1.
3. Compare H_p and H_s when both use an identical old-controller fallback on all noneligible pools. Prove the identity under no interference. Explain why this need not rank H_p against global specialist S.
4. Starting at covered gain 19/100, subtract separately verified full-network residual Gamma=2/25 and additional coordination cost c=3/20. What, if anything, is certified? If Gamma is instead unknown, what can be concluded?
5. Explain why a period-level G=9, R=13 observation from G→R does not identify the choice between G→R and R→R, and compute both two-period values. Identify the changing state.
6. A dispatcher can allocate orders across robot pools that do not share ML weights. Construct a transfer of one customer order that changes A's accepted output by +1 and B's by -1. What assignment/exposure unit would be necessary to learn the *total* economic effect?
