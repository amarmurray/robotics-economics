# Proof, counterexamples, and rejected shortcuts — shared learning and operator interference
**19 November 2026.** Standard conditional constructions tied to complete qualified service, not field evidence or independently novel math.

**P1: Production representation.** The constrained second-date operating LP for incremental qualified orders is maximize \(v(x_1+x_2)+\ell\,1\{q=2\}\) subject to \(0\le x_i\le z_i\), \(\sum_i x_i\le1+e\). Its optimum is \(v\min(q,1+e)+\ell1\{q=2\}\) if \(\ell\) is attributed to at least one served incremental order and the event q=2 genuinely creates that extra capturable contribution; add qualified unaffected incumbent \(2b\), subtract acquisition and E costs once to obtain (I3). **Important boundary:** if no incremental order is accepted, an automatic positive \(\ell\) is wrong. In this two-site model q=2 and capacity at least 1 guarantee one eligible incremental order; under customer demand/qualification uncertainty \(\ell\) must instead be conditioned on actually delivered complete service.

**P2: Source set cross differences.** Substitute q=0,1,2 into (I3) at e=0: \(F_0(0)=2b\), \(F_0(1)=2b+v-k\), \(F_0(2)=2b+v+\ell-2k\); second difference is \(\ell-v\). At e=1 the q2 value is \(2b+2v+\ell-2k-K\) and q1/q0 have the same constant \(-K\); second difference \(\ell\). Their difference v. Cost k cancels because additive, and K cancels because a constant under e=1. If E independently changes learning \(\ell_e\), the cross contrast becomes \(\ell_1-\ell_0+v\), invalidating pure congestion attribution.

**P3: Local/direct versus full adoption.** With one slot and fair ex ante allocation, a focal X site receives v alone, v/2 if a second X exists; technical pooled increment \(\ell/2\) accrues in expectation to each when both use X. G receives b. Conditioning on its own action and averaging the independently assigned neighbor produces (I6). All-X minus all-G site-average net value is (I7). Subtract to obtain (I8). This contrasts **different estimands**, not biased randomization of an identical estimand. A one-network all-X-versus-all-G design would compare (I7), assuming no outside spillovers.

**P4: Explicit rank reversal and portfolio.** With \(b=3,v=4,k=5/2,\ell=0,K=1\), the exact values are \(F_0(q)=6,15/2,5\) and \(F_1(q)=5,13/2,8\). Site-direct \(D(1/2)=1/2>0\), full-site \(T=-1/2<0\). Thus positive mixed-market own-X evidence does not imply profitable universal X adoption. The optimal deterministic portfolio from the six approved *hypothetical* menus is both X and E, with 8; one X and no E gives \(15/2\). All-X without E is costly but unchanged incumbent production stays feasible. Under independent p saturation, \(E[F_0]=6+2p(v-k)+p^2(\ell-v)=6+3p-4p^2\), maximized at \(p=3/8\), value \(105/16\). Fixed exactly-one selection achieves \(15/2\), demonstrating coordination rather than parameter reruns.

**P5: General interference bound.** For each i and a, start with \(w_i(a,\mathbf z_{-i})\). Turn other sites' coordinates one-by-one to all a. Uniform coordinate Lipschitz bounds yield \(|w_i(a,\mathbf z_{-i})-w_i(a,\mathbf a_{-i})|\le\sum_{j\ne i}\epsilon_{ij}^a1\{z_j\ne a\}\). Average under Bernoulli(p); for a=1 the mismatch probability is 1−p, for a=0 it is p. Sum the two component bounds and divide by N to obtain (I9). The two-site model has \(\epsilon^1=|\ell-v|/2,\epsilon^0=0\), and all differences have the same sign, attaining the bound. Missing bounds cannot be replaced with measured mean direct effect; this is sensitivity not identification.

**P6: Exposure Horvitz–Thompson identity.** Under well-defined stable potential outcomes \(w_i(a,e)\), correct exposure mapping, consistency and known positive probabilities of the two target exposure events, \(E[1\{Z_i=a,E_i=e_i(\mathbf a)\}w_i(\mathbf Z)/\pi_i^a]=w_i(\mathbf a)\). Taking arm difference and site average establishes (I10). A one-time experiment does not reveal both potential outcomes; variance may be huge or unidentified without sufficient joint exposure probabilities. If each site's target exposure requires all d neighbors plus own status and independent Bernoulli assignment, \(\pi_i^1=p^{d+1},\pi_i^0=(1-p)^{d+1}\); neither is usable at large degree.

**Rejected claims / alternative assumptions:** (a) Naive robot/site randomization identifies the policy-closed J (false with cross-site spillovers). (b) Pooling transfer \(\ell>0\) forces source complementarities (false when \(\ell<v\)). (c) More X trained sites must improve gross optimized work (false for a binding shared slot: gross saturates, but under optional policies cannot fall). (d) X costs prove negative intrinsic data value (false; paid conversion differs from optional free data). (e) A second embodiment always adds v (false unless it is qualified, has an eligible service and the constrained slot binds). (f) The factorial E contrast identifies pure congestion without no-learning-change exclusion (false). (g) Reconstructing the source-to-target technical kernel is necessary for whole-policy value (false under valid closed network randomization, but mechanism attribution remains unmeasured). (h) Cross-network spillovers necessarily reduce the value of training (false when shared learning \(\ell>v\)).
