# Instructor solutions — kept separate from student exercises

1. Write max(Qa,Qb)=Qa+(Qb−Qa)+. The Qa terms cancel between observing and not observing the buyer; the positive-part identity x+= (x+|x|)/2 gives one half weighted absolute difference minus absolute weighted difference. This assumes the same lawful menu and normal full-history observability at subsequent dates.
2. When d1 and d2 share a weak sign the expression is zero. In the opposing-sign quadrants it is min{p d1,(1−p)(−d2)} or its reverse, increasing away from zero in each coordinate. Maxima occur at the two opposing interval corners, leading to equation (3); min is zero if the rectangle touches a common-sign quadrant, else the nearest-to-zero opposing corner gives (4). Simultaneous optimized program payoffs may share demand and resource outcomes, making naive interval subtraction loose.
3. Without report the source menu chooses A reservation and earns 1/2 for q≤1. With report, it selects A after A and B after B, earning (1+q)/2. Benefit q/2∈[0,1/2]. Benchmark task completion does not identify buyer order arrivals, end-to-end qualification, acceptance, settled payment or future licensed production.
4. SG after costs earns (1+q)/2−1/10−1/20=7/20+q/2. E earns 3/4−1/10=13/20. Difference q/2−3/10, strictly SG> E iff q>3/5. The numerical threshold is wholly synthetic. Physical grounding can alter Q, right to deploy, buyer qualification and future transitions, not merely the order report.
5. An unapproved B controller is excluded from A_h; it cannot be assigned an uncertain Q and used to justify a test. A legally approved but unobserved B action may be assigned an independently justified full-horizon Q *interval*, not a fitted point estimate. A-only logging identifies no B accepted-paid response or source-transfer effect without transport/positivity assumptions.
6. For each z, every legal Q(z,b)≤r(z,b)≤l(z,a*)≤Q(z,a*). Thus a* is statewise optimal and a single blind dispatch is also optimal: regret zero for all compatible worlds. This does not prove that paid outcomes, learner transitions, source rights or legal actions remain equal after learning creates new future services; full Bellman state refinement remains separately required.
