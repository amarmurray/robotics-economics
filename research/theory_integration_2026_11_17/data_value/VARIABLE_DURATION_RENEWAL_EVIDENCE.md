# Lawful validation protocol: variable-duration G/K queues and deadline-conditioned buyers

23 November 2026. **Prospective, unpopulated specification only.** No authorization to access customer data, conduct G/K treatments, train models, spend money or contact buyers.

**Target estimand and interference unit.** Eligibility is one originally authorized *failed and still unpaid A ticket*, inherited genuine recovery and a stable queue cluster containing every technician, robot, affected customer and shared worker across G, K, GK, old R/P and independently staffed E. Include failed/blocked/no-invoice cases. Any exposure of customers to neighboring clusters or common workers must enlarge the cluster; do not assume independent units solely to enable a regret mixture.

**Data rights and source lineage.** Record failure-origin field trajectories for G and *separately* recovery-origin traces for K; timestamps, provenance, validation, retention, training, derivative checkpoints, deployment rights, privacy restrictions and rights expiry for each. Teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained and productive routine evidence are distinct inputs. Missing lawful rights are exclusion, not zero-cost training. Save original and updated complete-recipe qualification, model version, embodiment, acceptance test and costs.

**Physical timeline and observable predecision H.** Log the original failed A attempt, inherited recovery hours, G start and real finished duration g, precise time when g becomes visible to a K decision, K initiation or legally available skip, generated job identities, serial/parallel worker capacity, actual joint-versus-single program duration, release and deadline timestamps, safety precedence, robot time, independently staffed E resource claim and all unrelated queue events. Verification must establish whether G itself changes when K is subsequently selected and whether a g=1 status genuinely guarantees k^{GK}≤2. A guaranteed duration cap cannot be inferred from averaged test times.

**Original customer/payment and renewal.** A needs unique original offer, authorization, expiration, accepted service quality, finish time, actual contribution and failed/no-order records. B is a new, uniquely identified, newly authorized original order—record B offers, no-offers, accepted service and settled contribution with customer/deadline cohort and timing. In particular distinguish A completed at minute3, A completed at minute4, A missed minute4, and late B arrival. Selection on positive invoices is impermissible. Record customer refusal and demand spillovers, not only a post-service survey.

**Sequential support.** If legally and technically approved, assign G treatment at the true shared-queue/customer cluster. Among G-eligible clusters with valid observed pre-K histories H (including g), a prospective K-versus-skip randomized action with positive probabilities in each decision-relevant H cell could identify J(delta) by a sequential g-formula, provided interference, treatment consistency, logging and censoring assumptions hold. Blindly randomizing individual robots sharing workers violates the intervention unit. Separate G-only or K-only arms establish neither GK durations nor K effects conditional on g; historical P, R or technical Q cannot substitute.

**Minimum report and stop rule.** To compare the simple safe policy with E, document the availability and accuracy of g *before* K, worst-case job capacity bounds, complete A and B customer receipts and E's independently supported capacities/costs. To optimize decisions within the g=2 stratum, identify the policy-relevant conditional A/B paid difference of K versus skip and its expected costs. A lower bound is allowed instead of a point estimate when an intervention is unsupported. Zero positive support means **unidentified**, not negative expected effects. Do not quote E=13/4 or any model number as actual wages, customer WTP or measured ROI.
