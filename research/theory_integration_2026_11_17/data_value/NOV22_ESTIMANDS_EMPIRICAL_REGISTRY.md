# Prospective Nov22 registry: four estimands and original customer production

**Design only. No trial has been conducted, no customer permission or robot safety authorization obtained.**

**Primary unit:** a policy-closed customer/dispatch/operator/learning network, not a rollout, robot, or customer order nested in one shared dispatcher. Document all cross-network communications and demand substitution; if any edge remains, enlarge clusters or state the interference estimand. Define the customer universe at pre-source baseline, including all originally requested orders, refusals, reassignments, unattempted services and outside providers.

**Four prespecified contrasts:** (1) randomized static mean whole-policy Y; (2) t only from safe matched paired joint-Q outcomes or explicit Frechet interval; (3) regime- and H-conditional r_x from on-policy technical outcomes of assigned approved stage-two training; (4) policy-closed sequential complete-service J_x(pi) and its randomized direct comparison with retaining F, stratified by source regime. Report eligible and unstudied masses separately. If H-specific continuation absent but approved, construct economically justified partial-support bounds and label sharpness assumptions. If not approved, remove from deployable menu entirely.

**Policy-learning safeguard:** learn history-conditioned action rule on separate networks; compare it to fixed F/specialist, a no-source policy, an approved diagnostic-only policy, and source then optional grounding/body, all under the same cost, safety and market conditions. A positive before/after benchmark or completion rate does not establish any of these economic contrasts.

**Falsification order:** check network closure, baseline order comparability, rights and physical admission; then source-regime first-stage Q/order distribution; then stage-two eligible overlap; then conditional technical r; finally original-order whole-network contribution. The selection-only null posits unchanged conditional complete downstream outcomes on a *validated common prechoice type/support state* while first-stage changes failure composition. Rejecting it may reflect genuine transfer, new embodiment support, demand, recovery or policy-induced occupancy—measure those mechanisms rather than naming all of them 'positive transfer'.

**External technical route:** DROID https://droid-dataset.github.io/ and Open X-Embodiment https://robotics-transformer-x.github.io/ permit research hypotheses about source-mixture and held-out technical transfer under their actual licenses and versions; they do NOT provide this private accepted-order panel. Quantitative official BEA–BLS customer-industry backbone remains missing and may not be replaced by vendor promotional counts. No commercial inference without a joined order/capture panel.
