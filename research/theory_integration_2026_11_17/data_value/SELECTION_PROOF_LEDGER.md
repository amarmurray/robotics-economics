# Source-screening selection: derivation and negative-result ledger

1. **Population and actor:** fixed initial customer opportunity population; a unit includes the entire potential policy-closed physical, operator, dispatch, learner and customer network. Y counts accepted original and additional orders and all real costs exactly once, relative to retained competent F. No benchmark-to-dollar conversion.
2. **Dynamic policy identity:** after observing approved pre-B history H_x, the maximum expected continuation effect is max(0,d_x(G,h),d_x(B,h),...). Integrating over actual source-induced eligible histories yields J_x^*. Proof: conditional expectation and finite maximization over feasible action alternatives, with no costs outside Y. If irreversible costs arise before H, they belong in J_x(F).
3. **Four estimands:** static economic policy-arm means, cross-policy qualification overlap t, history-conditioned technical r_x, and direct eligible sequential economic effect d_x. Randomized static means do not identify t or r_x. A supported whole-network randomized stage two identifies d_x for its specific source policy only.
4. **Nested-screen identity:** F_0=F_1 disjoint union R. For common type-specific continuation g, pi_0*delta_0=pi_1*delta_1+m*delta_R; solve for delta_1-delta_0. Apply to g_a-g_b to obtain the action-ranking reversal condition m*A>pi_1*(-B), under A>0>B. No smooth learning function or cross-policy independence is needed, but nested potential success and unchanged g are strong unverified assumptions.
5. **Exact example:** two equal types, w=2, c_G=2/5, c_B=7/10, c_x=3/10. q_0=(0,0),q_1=(1,0),G=(1,0),B=(0,1). x0: G=3/5, B=3/10. x1: initial=1, incremental G=-1/5 and B=13/20, best gross=33/20, net=27/20. Diagnostic-informed x0: [ (2-2/5)+(2-7/10) ]/2=29/20. Thus diagnostic beats paid x1 for cost below 1/10. Checks use exact numerator units /20 and independent rational arithmetic.
6. **Negative identification:** stage-two outcomes among failures in one source regime cannot be blindly transported to another, because source choice changes both failure mass and failure mix. Conditioning on D_x=1 across regimes is generally conditioning on a post-source selector. Even a genuinely positive direct stage-two d_x is not the value of first-stage source x; source cost, passed qualifications, option menu and original customer orders also enter J.
7. **No unconditional technical transfer:** G and B effects are constant in the counterexample. If real transfer changes post-source technical response, regime-specific r_x, acquisition/safety support and customer Y must be estimated; the screening identity alone cannot separate mechanism.
8. **Safety and optionality:** every proposed continuation must be separately approved. Full choice with old qualified policy retained cannot worsen under freely optional data availability, but forced training or paying real costs can. An empirical randomization cannot itself create operational safety authorization.
9. **Prior-art boundary:** dynamic regime optimization (Murphy 2003), principal stratification (Frangakis/Rubin 2002), conditional-selection algebra, data-value dynamics in DDA 2026, LP/process flexibility and ordinary value-of-information are foundations. Originality of the integrated robotics-production contribution remains unverified.
10. **Validation scope:** scoped executable finite grid and independent Fraction arithmetic check synthetic equalities, not proofs, field evidence, regulator signoff, source-to-qualification effects or historical repository CI.
