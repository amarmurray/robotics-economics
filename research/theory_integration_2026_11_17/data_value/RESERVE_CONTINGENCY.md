# Joint training versus actual reserve capacity under endogenous recovery

24 November 2026. Continuation of [joint stochastic load](JOINT_STOCHASTIC_LOAD.md), in *When Capabilities Become Production*. A reserved human operator is productive capacity, not merely a risk parameter; its use can also replace an informative recovery trajectory with a different, possibly less reusable, lineage. Technical load distributions before recourse do not specify the economic transition after recourse.

## 1. Safe contingent menu and reserve option identity

Fix a whole-program law P and a controller investment g (including old versions). At a dated report h, let B_g(h) be the maximum expected accepted net receipts plus discounted optimized continuation over safe nonanticipating recovery actions without reserve. The action may stagger, abort safely, use the old controller, or defer an order subject to actual procurement and deadlines. Let K_g(h) be the analogous maximum over additional reserve-enabled actions. Each conditional value uses its own joint law of load, acceptance, backlog, rights and later qualification; no invariant recovery kernel is presumed. A safe action satisfies dated physical capacity for every certified disturbance; a missed commercial deadline may be allowed, unsafe execution is not.

Suppose buying an actual reserve slot ex ante costs k, does not change the distribution of h, leaves every no-reserve policy with the same receipts and transitions (including when idle), and adds only the specified reserve menu. The option's net value is exactly

    Delta_g(k) = E_P[(K_g(h)-B_g(h))_+] - k.

Proof: conditional maximization over the union gives max(B,K); subtract B and the one-time cost and integrate. This applies at the first decision with separate optimized continuations for the two menus only if retaining the baseline continuation is an admissible option; if reserve changes future menus, define B as the value of the *retained original program*, not merely its first action. For general multistage reserve reuse, construct full lawful contingency policies, not a sum of one-step option values. Compare training and reserve using whole-program net J, with training and reserve costs each paid once. The interaction for committed investments is Delta_G(k)-Delta_R(k); negative interaction means substitution in net program value, not a general technological substitution theorem. Positive interaction is possible when old bodies cannot serve a new order even with reserve, while a trained body can serve it only with reserve. An unused reserve is still paid for. An on-call option bought after h, if genuinely available at that time, has a different action menu and price.

For idle-program drift, let B'_g denote the value of the old-action family under the reserve program and K'_g the new-action family. If the predecision report law is unchanged and |B'-B|<=epsilon_B(h), |K'-K|<=epsilon_K(h) for a reference extension K, the actual gain differs from the reference positive-part gain by at most E max(epsilon_B,epsilon_K), by the sup-norm Lipschitz property of max. If the report law itself shifts, add an independently justified expectation-transport bound on the common reference max(B,K), relative to the original distribution; without a common report space and bounds, use full-program valuation instead. This is an upper/lower certificate, not an assumption that reserve leaves physics unchanged. Reserve use may improve paid receipts while destroying a future licensed data opportunity; the conditional continuation contrast, not immediate receipts, determines the decision.

## 2. Common-law identification and nonanticipativity

Let theta index finite controlled response types, each specifying all safe action-specific potential histories and economic outcomes on the same original cluster. Let P range over a common-law polytope {p>=0, sum p=1, Ap in [l,u]} where rows encode observed arm-specific and paired audit moments. A fixed lawful report rule pi has value c_pi'p minus committed costs. Consequently its sharp lower contrast to a fixed comparator is the minimum of (c_pi-c_comparator)'p over this polytope. The dual gives a signed combination of measured moments dominated pointwise by the response-type contrast. This is sharp only relative to the stipulated type set, not arbitrary restrictions on persistent kernels. If all alternatives are finite, a fixed witness beats an optimized alternative throughout the set if every pairwise lower contrast is positive. An upper bound on the optimized program is max_pi max_p J(pi,p); an implementable robust lower bound is max_pi min_p J(pi,p), with the same safe nonanticipating rule across common laws. Never choose a rule using unobserved response type. Unknown support must be conservatively included even when finite samples have zero events. The reserve identity holds law by law, but its optimizer need not be common across P.

## 3. Exact counterexample: forced recovery does not identify selective recovery

Two original orders each pay 4 net if timely accepted. Original R has one worker, two old controllers each need one simultaneous slot, and only one can meet its deadline. R=4. A separately reserved actual worker K costs k=7/8 and makes both old orders timely, without a successor; increment 4-k=25/8. Independently staffed embodiment E adds 3 net, independent procurement stipulated. Extra source X adds 1/2 net to the retained recipe but no changed recovery menu. These are synthetic units. The old menu is retained.

Joint target grounding G costs 1 and qualifies a staggerable fast recipe. A lawful predispatch binary congestion report O identifies whether simultaneous execution needs two workers (O=1) or at most one (O=0), with p=P(O=1)=1/4. On nonoverload, D executes both, net 8 plus 2D_s from an independently staffed, distinct future licensed qualified buyer opportunity; q=P(O=0,D_s=1)=1/4. On overload D is unsafe absent reserve. F safely serves one, net 4 and no successor. Stagger S safely serves one now and another at a later slot, whose customer deadline allows a net late payment 1 only for the trained variant; total 5 plus 4S_s for a different lawful failure/recovery lineage with distinct future paid service. Both later slots and successor staffing/costs are included; no double counting. S is also safe in nonoverload. S_s is potential under S and is observed only after action; a forced S audit identifies P(S_s=1)=1/4 in the original population. K used on overload serves both timely for 8 but replaces that lineage (no licensed successor); K is not used on normal paths and its idle presence leaves D and S unchanged. No hidden report predicts S_s. Reserve and stagger cannot serve a third copy of either original order.

Let j=P(O=1,S_s=1), sharp in [0,1/4] given these marginals. Attainment: allocate the S_s mass between overload and nonoverload, keep D_s law fixed (with any conditional coupling); all paths are safe for the selected menu. In nonoverload, D dominates S in conditional expectation since E[S_s|O=0]<=1/3 and 5+4/3<8. In overload, S dominates F, but may not dominate K. No-reserve trained program increment is

    G = 4(1-p)+2q-1 + p + 4j = 11/4 + 4j.

With reserve the trained program increment is

    GK = 5/2 + max(4p,p+4j) - k.

The purchased reserve is selected over stagger in overload iff E[S_s|O=1]<3/4 (tie at equality); thus fast direct recovery is not always economically optimal. The combined program never beats max(G,4-k) for k>=0: if K selected, GK=7/2-k<4-k; if S selected, GK=G-k. R+K beats G iff j<3/32 at k=7/8; equality at 3/32. In general G beats R+K iff k>5/4-4j. G exceeds E iff j>1/16. In the low world j=0, choose reserve; in the high world j=1/4 choose training and stagger. Both worlds have identical congestion distributions, D outcomes on normal paths, forced S full outcome distribution (5 or 9), old reserve outcomes and even the same total forced S successor rate. But only the latter world generates the stagger successor on the report-selected risk set. The missing joint economic audit is the overlap of the original report and the *action-specific* realized stagger successor.

A forced S sample retaining O and the complete later acceptance outcome can identify j under validated assignment/transport; the report-omitted full marginal cannot. A direct safe selective-S trial identifies j without measuring S_s on nonoverload. The fixed report rule D on normal, S on overload is safe in both worlds, so the maximin comparison is genuine (low G=11/4, known reserve 25/8). If the conservative report fails to exclude overload, the D policy cannot be deployed merely because its expected load is low. Free routine data that provide the identical trained menu/rights/report without acquisition cost raise G by 1; independence of E and actual reserve staffing are substantive hypotheses, not labels. If the reserve worker is drawn from the existing customer queue, subtract its displaced net production and recompute future law. If recovery generates a different lineage, use its actual successor state rather than reweighting the stagger data.

## 4. Interpretation, identification and prior art

A physical fleet with learned correlated failures has at least three economically distinct design variables: joint training that changes the feasible menu, operational recourse that changes the realized data-generating process, and priced actual capacity. The same aggregate autonomy and total data rate do not determine the investment ranking. Model A accounts for accepted customer recipes and dated resources; Model B chooses source/grounding/embodiment and operational recourse against the resulting successor state. Field trajectories, failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained inputs remain different lineages with their own costs, rights and transfer. This is a controlled response-type/option-value synthesis, not a novelty claim.
