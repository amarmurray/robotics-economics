# Evidence design: identify supported economic program rankings before fitting a cross-arm response copula

**21 November 2026. Prospective protocol only; no trial, customer contact, physical approval or dataset access is claimed or authorized.** Companion to [the identification hierarchy](PROGRAM_VALUE_IDENTIFICATION_HIERARCHY.md) and the continuing [Nov22 Models A/B synthesis](NOV22_MODELS_AB_ARGUMENT_AND_GATE.md).

## Estimands and randomized unit

**Primary:** for each legally executable whole program z in R, S, G-train, SG, E, the average original-buyer **net accepted and settled production** per predeclared customer/operator/robot/learner cluster over identical horizon, less program-specific true cost. Also the population factorial difference SG−S−G+R if those four complete arms are supported. The preassignment target market W, price regime, eligible cohort and timing must be comparable. If price is optimized or market exposure varies with z, the evaluated intervention must include the *actual* price/exposure/dispatch policy; do not transport a fixed demand table silently.

**Unit:** the smallest network encompassing shared robot policies/weights, update compute, supervisor/operator queues, customer procurement parent, outside orders and displacement. Record interference across sites, common learning runs and lead/lags; if interference crosses candidate clusters, randomization unit must expand or estimands/exposure mappings change. Do not assign one customer order to treatment when it changes operator capacity for others. Deliberate S source admission, G-train alignment of fixed source, G-observe predecision report, E physical embodiment and R retained-controller policies must be individually versioned and distinguished.

## Minimal linked record, row for every *eligible original offer* rather than just completed jobs

| Layer / fields | Why necessary |
|---|---|
| Preassignment cluster, timestamp, buyer parent, order/cohort eligibility, W, site, operator/fleet resource bounds, comparison-set version | Defensible risk set and interference closure |
| Z intervention, randomized propensity, seed/learner checkpoint/version, accepted training-update and compute budget, retention fallback and physical signoff | Identify actual program and avoid training-budget and safety confounding |
| Data origin, independent episode-family lineage, public/field/failure/recovery/teleop/simulation/synthetic tag, admitted transitions, rights, reuse expiry, proposed vs accepted sampling | Determine whether S changes *independent source information*, G training, or merely repeats the same archive |
| Per-complete-service A/B/E qualification on matched target panel; both-service joint cell, physical operator and recovery constraints, refusals/interruptions | Identify within-program co-qualification and signed service capacity |
| Before/after accepted offers, full procurement alternatives, price/terms, no purchase, cancellation, buyer segment, lost/deflected opportunity, outside operator orders and settlements | Capture endogenous demand and actual paid opportunity rather than guessed shadow price |
| Gross receipts, settlement delays, refunds, paid operating and operator costs, manufacturing/access/service warranties, capex/service-life assignment, insurance exposure | Economic program contribution, not raw task benchmark score |
| Production-created data episode, use right, time, downstream training admission, future original-buyer order and qualification, loss to follow-up | Within-program transition kernel and lawful dynamic-policy value |

Predefine whether treatments are *program assignment/offer* effects (intention to treat), effects of an executed compliant learner under a qualifying safety wrapper, or data-use mechanism effects. These are not identical causal estimands. The whole learner may be trained once per cluster; a task split inside the same checkpoint is not independent evidence.

## Identification triage

1. **Only R/S/G observed:** preserve SG/E missing-arm bounds. Cross-arm monotone/modular bounds may be shown as *scenario assumptions*, not data-derived certificates. Do not declare a robust best program on interval overlap.
2. **SG arm observed but Q_A and Q_B measured on disjoint task pools:** program paid outcome might still be measurable if complete paid services are observed, but a joint-qualification mechanism cannot be reconstructed from separate per-task marginals; preserve Fréchet or selection bounds.
3. **SG arm and E arm have lawful whole-network original-customer paid outcomes:** identify their average program comparison at a common target market without reconstructing casewise potential response correlations. A field observation with no assignment ignorability remains associational.
4. **Static arms available, future data policy untested:** do not claim optimal two-date value from period-one outcomes. Require stagewise support and complete history exposure to estimate transition kernels or compare fixed full-horizon programs.
5. **Shared-market spillovers or changing eligible buyer cohorts:** stop claiming transport. Enlarge cluster or state a different policy estimand; track all displaced original buyer orders and operator capacity.
6. **E lacks verified embodiment rights, signed service qualification or manufacturing economics:** label E unsupported and keep it as a conditional benchmark, not as an actionable offer.

## Falsification and diagnostics

Precommit a unit and timestamp manifest; verify no cross-arm learner/weights shared, no episode-family leakage, common accepted-update and compute budgets, target pre-treatment balance, known assignment propensities and W overlap. Report all eligible buyer refusals, no-purchase outcomes and incomplete settlement periods. Check within-arm joint A/B qualification rather than extrapolating from marginals; record the S×G *program economic* difference separately from per-task interactions. Test whether post-intervention buyer arrivals/segment shares, prices, outside-order receipts or capacity differ across arms before transporting a fixed F table. Preserve old-policy physical accessibility on each program; if not, free-data monotonic option logic does not apply.

For dynamic feedback, follow originating episodes into rights/retention and the actual *future* policy that uses them; a D–future-service association is not causal evidence of beneficial training. For a causal data-use effect, legally randomize or otherwise justify independent assignment of downstream data admission/grounding at the whole-learner cluster level.
