# Proof ledger — passive support and embodied decision frontiers

**18 November 2026. Scope:** one continued Q, omega, V, J argument; all figures synthetic unless labeled external. The source is [PASSIVE_SUPPORT_AND_DECISION_REGRET.md](PASSIVE_SUPPORT_AND_DECISION_REGRET.md).

1. **Distinct objects:** technology transition T_e, report law P_e, and safe qualified menu A_e. In a pure report comparison T, A, resources, deadline and original demand are fixed. In a developmental intervention the full J changes, so adding a separate EVSI premium double-counts continuation.
2. **Observational equivalence:** if the entire F-source report law is identical at theta- and theta+, no observation-dependent policy can have different aggregate selection probabilities across these worlds. This is a *nonparametric* missing-support argument, not a result that robots cannot generalize or that source data have zero technical value.
3. **Two-world minimax regret:** with A minus S = -L,+H, A selected with probability p gives (pL,(1-p)H) regret. Equalization gives p=H/(L+H), R=LH/(L+H). This is exact when A/S are the available approved choices, report laws identical and ex-ante randomization is permitted.
4. **Reuse of previous economic example:** V_A(x)=1/20+2x/5, V_S=2/5, x in [0,2]. Difference is (2/5)x-7/20; threshold x=7/8, endpoints (-7/20,+9/20). Minimax p=9/16, regret 63/320. For any random p, regret in x is affine within each region; maximum over compact [0,2] is at an endpoint. These are *normalized synthetic captured service* quantities, not robot dollars.
5. **Noisy diagnostic LP:** for P(+|theta-)=1/5, P(+|theta+)=4/5, let a=P(A|+), b=P(A|-). Regrets r-=(7/20)(a+4b)/5, r+=(9/20)(5-4a-b)/5. At (a,b)=(1,2/37) both =63/740. A dual supporting lower bound puts probability lambda=9/37 on theta-: lambda*r-+(1-lambda)*r+ has b coefficient zero, a coefficient negative, and minimum 63/740 at a=1. Since max(r-,r+) >= this weighted mean, the candidate attains the global minimax value over the square [0,1]^2. Its reduction relative to passive is 1323/11840; if an experiment delays demand or changes Q, this is *not* its actual cash value.
6. **No information != no technology improvement:** if an independently verified optional data-to-qualification transition raises A whole-service value by delta in both worlds, no report is necessary for A to dominate S when delta>=L. This construction does not assert that real extra source data achieves delta. Negative or costly forced training differs from retaining F/A options.
7. **Support cut:** for a required target event absent from observation support, construct two laws equal on every measured variable but differing on that event and hence on A accepted service; absent a validated invariant/constraint on the joint event, there is no point identified A service. Marginal competence on each component does not identify joint bundle/recovery/capacity. The conclusion is a standard identification limit, not a new robot-learning no-go theorem.
8. **Embodiment distinction:** E(a,h) may differ across bodies; switching h may remove a missing edge or add one. This creates no economic guarantee without newly certified safety, acceptance and whole-network production. Passive data, grounding and morphology are not reduced to arbitrary scalar transfer coefficients.
9. **Falsifiers:** verified cross-body invariance that identifies target service from F data; significant target-payoff information in F histories; passive corpus causing a proven Q shift; target signal errors not 1/5 per stratum; missed customer window; operator network noninertness; true specialist contribution not 2/5. Each invalidates the corresponding inference, not the old F fallback.
10. **Originality boundary:** binary decision regret and support nonidentification are pre-existing decision/statistical results. The proposed robotics-economic synthesis is a design and proof-obligation interface, not independently validated novelty. No real robot/customer dataset, full repo CI or physically approved diagnostics observed.
