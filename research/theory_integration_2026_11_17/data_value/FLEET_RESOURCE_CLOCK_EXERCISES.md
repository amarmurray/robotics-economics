# Exercises — fleet data and resource clocks
1. Given 96 scheduled robots for 413 minutes and an average of 65 executing, compute scheduled, executing and residual robot-hours. Under frozen other blockers, what is the direct upper bound from removing 8.3 incident hours? Why not divide a commercial revenue figure by the resulting share?
2. With a=4,l=4,f=h=2, calculate q and both isolated and combined increments. Derive the capacity cross-difference for arbitrary nonnegative f,h.
3. A single newly feasible episode may unlock a terminal service worth M. Construct a finite model in which the recovered clock is small but the economic advantage is arbitrarily large. What would make the Bellman certificate informative?
