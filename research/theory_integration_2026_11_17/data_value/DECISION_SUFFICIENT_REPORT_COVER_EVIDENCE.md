# Decision-sufficient report-cover: prospective original-buyer/learner evidence contract

**22 November 2026.** Planning-only, no external customer, robot, new hardware, nonpublic data or physical experiment. This is a refinement of the same SG/E Models A/B evidence protocol, not a stand-alone new field study.

**Decision unit:** one specific authorized post-observation action time for a common original eligible buyer-service cohort, preserving orders, no-orders, offered prices, accepted/rejected/refunded service and cleared settlement. Each row needs the original opportunity ID, cohort/market time window, learner checkpoint and included field-success/failure-recovery/teleop/simulation/synthetic/pretrained source lineage, training and report-use licenses, approved robot/body/tool/physical recovery status, available operator/resource slots, actual buyer order and no-order information when observed, report-availability timestamp, licensed reporting rights and the specific legal dispatch action. The terminal paid outcome is a net accepted-and-settled contribution plus documented later paid services, not a per-trajectory benchmark score.

**Support table:** build the genuinely available legal menu M(h) at each predecision record; do not interpret forbidden or merely proposed actions as zero-paying observations. Identify action-specific conditional whole-horizon Q from matched interventions, or report defensible joint intervals, including canceled/refunded attempts and lost operator opportunity; match actual learner lineage and the initial offered buyer population, and account for cross-fleet training/robot/operator interference at the whole network level.

**Certificate:** for each context h calculate credible optimal-action sets, uncertain possible-optimal sets and a jointly legitimate model of paid-Q dependence. Test multiway intersections of admissible optimal actions across the cells of the *actual implementable* report (not just pairwise contrasts). Compute an unrestricted action-cover minimum as an oracle **lower bound on report alphabet size**, then re-optimize over signals physically and legally observable before dispatch. If joint paid-Q bounds do not exclude a common action, do not certify positive regret; if outcomes or transitions are off-support, retain a broad identified set.

**Heterogeneous information:** source data acquisition is a training/qualification intervention; target physical grounding changes safe production ability; target order/physical-context reporting changes decision information; a new embodiment changes the menu/throughput. Report-provenance consent and data-use rights may themselves vary across contexts, preventing naive pooled policies. Field successes, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained inputs are distinct nodes in the lineage graph.

**Dynamic and economic audit:** only use the fixed one-stage formula where reporting neither changes the original cohort nor modifies producer action/learning transitions. Otherwise use whole-policy J and occupancy under the **candidate** report policy. Early paid pilot opportunities, deliberate target data, legal consent, compute, teleop, robot time, future learned services and any changed demand all belong in J exactly once. Compare gross report value against feasible sensing, reporting, curation and displaced-production costs. The older baseline controller remains optional where legally and physically admissible.

**Missing:** no real approved SG/E robot+operator action menus, original buyer offered/no-order panel, signed report rights or timestamped live eligibility, causal paid Q tables, full learner interference/occupancy, actual source-to-joint qualification mapping, E physical alternatives or BEA interindustry numerical flows. The synthetic minimum-symbol bound is not a field recommendation to buy sensors or modify controllers.

**Discriminating field hypothesis:** a coarsened customer record could be sufficient for R but not SG once S qualifies new services; a joint pair of legitimate signals can change economically optimal dispatch when either alone cannot. If measured original buyer/operator paid action rankings instead share a common lawful optimum throughout each pooled report cell, the hypothesized report value there is **zero** despite measurable classification information. This rejects any unconditional premise that more label information always creates productive economic value.
