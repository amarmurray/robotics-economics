# Source mechanisms versus whole-policy value when learning creates new embodiments

**20 November 2026 — continuation of the same November 22 Models A–B scientific gate.** This chapter complements the shared-learner exposure and sequential policy analyses. It is a conditional mathematical construction, not an observed transfer effect, certified robot architecture, identified dataset price or independent novelty claim.

## Question and common economic environment

When a source-training program X changes a shared controller and makes a new embodiment E commercially usable, does the observed profitability of the bundled X+E production program identify the separate economic contribution of its data, the new body, or target grounding G? **Generally no.** A well-supported *whole-policy* contrast can rank actual packages, yet its causal factor attribution needs separately implementable, qualified, supported components.

Retain J(pi) as the expected optimized accepted-and-paid contribution from the full prelisted original-buyer network, minus its actual data rights, collection, model compute/curation, manufacturing, operator, displaced-service and other real costs. Policies include original menus, qualification failures, scarce resources, fallbacks and future data generated through production. The continuation value is counted inside J, never appended as a data premium. For every qualified recipe, production V optimizes the admitted physical/organizational resource allocation and customer demand; signed policy admission, statistical exposure and paid original customer execution are different gates.

Let a subset T of {X,G,E} encode a *policy component intervention*, not a dataset count: X acquires and uses one specified lawful source mixture under a fixed development recipe; G makes a separately specified target-grounding/report protocol available at the correct decision time; E makes one specified manufacture-and-dispatch embodiment available. For each T, define v(T)=J(pi_T). All eight cells require a version-locked original market and a well-defined compatible controller/body/dispatch combination. If controller weights from X are irreversible or E does not work with the old controller, a missing hybrid cell may be physically undefined, not merely unmeasured. Do not treat a mathematically filled cell as authorized deployment.

## Proposition: total package return need not attribute its components

Suppose the only assigned and signed original-network production arms are T=empty and T={X,G,E}, with v(empty)=6 and v(XGE)=10 in *hypothetical normalized net contribution units*. Then the identifiable total policy gain under valid closed-network whole-arm assignment is 4; no causal component allocation or sign of interaction is identified from those two arms alone.

**Constructive proof.** Consider five admissible mathematical worlds sharing exactly those endpoints, with the same original cohort, observed bundled qualification and paid outcomes: (1) all four units appear whenever X is enabled; (2) whenever G is enabled; (3) whenever E is enabled; (4) only when all three are enabled; (5) once at least two are enabled. Their six unobserved mixed-arm outcomes differ, but the observed baseline and bundle laws do not. These worlds have opposite source-versus-body attribution, and distinct signs for three-way interaction, while preserving the same positive total economic return. No function of the observed two-arm law can select a unique component mechanism. QED. This is a standard observational-equivalence argument, not a new theorem of causal identification.

Even under a strong *free optional component* assumption that every addition preserves earlier legal controllers, body options, budget and safe original service, so v(T) is monotone on the entire Boolean policy lattice, this ambiguity persists. For each i define the usual three-component Shapley allocation
\[
\phi_i=\sum_{T\subseteq N\setminus\{i\}}\frac{|T|!(2-|T|)!}{3!}[v(T\cup\{i\})-v(T)].
\]
This is an order-averaged **accounting convention**, not an identified neural-learning or economic mediation pathway. It satisfies sum_i phi_i=4 and, under optional monotonicity, 0<=phi_i<=4. Each bound is sharp without hybrid observations: a world where all gain is enabled by i attains its upper bound; a world where another component alone enables the gain attains its lower bound.

The anchored three-way discrete interaction is
\[
I_{XGE}=v(XGE)-v(XG)-v(XE)-v(GE)+v(X)+v(G)+v(E)-v(\varnothing).
\]
With endpoints 6 and 10 and monotonicity, -8<=I_XGE<=4 is sharp, attained by the "any two suffice" versus "all three required" worlds. Thus even the **sign of source-grounding-embodiment complementarity** is not identified by a profitable package. The exact CPU enumeration checks all 15,625 five-level middle-cell assignments, of which 1,884 respect monotonicity, and confirms the analytic endpoints; the algebra, not enumeration, proves the continuous result. For the upper bound, match each singleton to a containing pair, implying sum pair increments >= sum singleton increments. For the lower, each pair increment <=4 and singleton increments >=0. Both extreme constructions are feasible.

### Two-component attribution under fixed grounding

Fix G at one qualified original-market protocol. Denote a source update by s=0,1 and a new embodiment by e=0,1. With all four complete-policy cells hypothetically defined, the symmetric source allocation is
\[
\phi_X=\tfrac12[(J_{10}-J_{00})+(J_{11}-J_{01})]
      =\tfrac{J_{11}-J_{00}}{2}+\tfrac{J_{10}-J_{01}}{2}.
\]
The body allocation is total minus phi_X; the interaction is Gamma=J11-J10-J01+J00. If only J00 and J11 are supported, even a perfectly estimated package value cannot identify phi_X, Gamma or isolated technical transfer. Independent externally justified bounds L10<=J10<=U10 and L01<=J01<=U01 yield the sharp conditional interval
\[
\phi_X\in[(J11-J00+L10-U01)/2,(J11-J00+U10-L01)/2].
\]
Sharpness requires **independent admissible** completion of the two mixed cells; cross-cell restrictions narrow the set. Under free-option lattice inclusion all four cell values lie between J00 and J11, so phi_X lies between zero and the total, not at a magically inferred split. With costly forced training, hardware lock, rights changes or shared compute, monotonicity need not hold; use actual policy-specific resource and fallback bounds instead.

## Why source-generated new embodiments complicate causal mechanism claims

A common natural policy is X -> trained weights W(X) -> successful qualification Q -> E(X) becomes eligible -> original customer production. Contrasting observed X+E(X) against baseline G+E(G) identifies the combined intervention at best. The putative counterfactual W(X) with E(G) may not exist as a physically meaningful and signed controller/embodiment combination. This is a treatment-decomposition problem, not a failure to calculate Shapley values. If cross-swapped weights/body states are valid and separately assignable, factorial *policy-component effects* become estimable; they still do not uniquely distinguish the contribution of field trajectories versus teleoperation versus simulator versus recovery without a separately randomized source intervention holding starting weights, compute, evaluation and embodiment fixed. Even a technical Q factorial does not identify original-market purchases or paid receipts.

Production can create second-stage data, but this affects future information states, not a bonus outside J. Whole-network shared controller updates and operators require randomization on the connected update/customer/dispatcher network, not robot identities. The earlier sequential g-formula identifies supported adaptive complete policies under valid histories; it does **not** manufacture unoffered hybrid component effects. Target grounding may unlock X data already held, while E can expand admissible recipes; their complement or substitution must be tested at unchanged natural customer offers and demand clocks.

## Prior art and what would discriminate the theory

Shapley allocations, factorial interactions, potential outcome under-treatment decomposition and separable-effect logic are established foundations. Stensrud et al. (JASA 2020), https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2020.1765783, explicitly require an interpretable treatment decomposition; their clinical competing-event estimands are not our robotic policy theorem. Aronow–Samii (2017), https://doi.org/10.1214/16-AOAS1005, addresses interference; Farboodi–Koh–Xia (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10127, already accounts for future data in production and complementary aggregation. The contribution here is an integration and precise **identification boundary**, with no verified independent scientific novelty.

The discriminating evidence is a prelisted original buyer–robot–operator cohort, immutable learner/update graph, legal source rights, frozen starting weights, training compute and data mixture versions; physical signoff of every *tested* source/grounding/body combination and safe fallback; explicit joint-arm assignment probabilities; independent held-out complete qualification (including failure/recovery); original unchanged offered/refused/unserved/accepted/paid orders, operator burden and timing. A successful base-versus-bundle test alone may rank two packages but cannot claim any modality caused the improvement. An offline shadow evaluation can identify a technical effect for a matched admissible source mixture, not its paid economic value. No real cohort, independent safety approval, field causal effect, BEA quantitative input-output cell or full CI exists in this tranche.
