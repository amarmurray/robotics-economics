# Prospective successive eligibility audit (unpopulated)

Define original clusters before the first acquisition; include all customers, worker pools, embodiments and affected jobs. Log original A/C offer IDs, accepted contributions, customer authorizations, failure/recovery lineage, actual K cost, and report creation including ineligibility, denied rights, missing buyer budget and absent worker time. Distinguish F failure trajectory and completed-recovery K, routine P, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained provenance. Record expiry/license and predecision report timestamps. For approved programs randomize early K versus retained R / genuinely available P at the cluster level; inside each eligible K/P history randomize G/skip with documented positive probabilities. G must include safety qualification before dispatch. Track every original cluster, new authorized B IDs, worker slots consumed, failed qualification, rejected/unpaid service and follow-up orders. Never extrapolate treatment from an arm with no lawful support. Estimate unconditional masked eligible-and-paid outcome contrasts and eligible masses using the original early-arm denominator; use joint confidence regions, not independent unadjusted bounds. The illustrative 15/64 and 18/64 are synthetic restrictions, not proposed universal targets. Cluster-level randomization addresses shared-resource interference within the cluster; cross-cluster interference needs a larger unit or explicit design. Record independently staffed E and its launch cost versus a shared-staff alternative. Keep data rights and marginal compute/curation costs explicit. No actual interventions, customer access or rights are authorized here.

Replication: node successive_riskset_exact.js > replay.json, compare with successive_riskset_results.json. Exact integer counts on 64 clusters and randomized policies on sixteenth grid; continuous-law proofs are in chapter. Scoped V8 execution, not whole-repository CI.
