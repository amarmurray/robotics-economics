# Proof, counterexample and decision-regret ledger: source-to-target report transport

**19 November 2026; standard conditional finite decision theory, synthetic mathematics, no independently validated novelty.**

1. **One fixed physical configuration:** p(z), y_c(a,z), approved M_c and time-consistent whole-network customer contribution are held fixed while K varies. For a report-conditional rule pi, put f_z(s)=y_c(pi(s),z). Its range is at most R_c(z). Since two normalized distributions differ in expectations of any range-R function by at most R times total variation, |E_{pK'}f-E_{pK}f| <= epsilon. Taking the max over admissible pi in either direction proves |W_c(K')-W_c(K)| <= epsilon. This is a valid bound, not a claim of exact sharpness in a specified menu.
2. **Stale decision regret:** let pi optimize K, pi' optimize K'. W(K')-E_{K'}pi = [W(K')-W(K)] + [E_K pi-E_{K'}pi] <=2 epsilon; its lower bound zero is by optimality at K'. No claim that training or reoptimization itself is free.
3. **More discriminating margin:** for each s,a, payoff gap D_K(s,a)>=0 by pi(s) optimality. Under K' the signed change equals sum_z p(z)(K'-K)(s|z)(y(pi(s),z)-y(a,z)); the triangle inequality gives the stated sufficient condition. It is not necessary and requires a joint single policy-closed network if scarce cross-unit resources couple s.
4. **Controller/report decomposition:** add and subtract W_1(K_0). This identity is not an identified natural/direct treatment decomposition if old K_0 lacks safe/meaningful support for qualified controller 1, or if p, y, customer demand or reporter semantics also change. Changing integration path changes component attribution.
5. **Synthetic table (unitless, already qualified by assumption):** with prior high=low=1/2, actions F=(0,0), S=(3/4,3/4), G=(3,-1), T=(4,-6), symmetric report accuracy q, W(q)=max{1,2q-1/8,5q-21/8} for q in [1/2,1]. This is the previous admitted-complement complete-service example, not a second generated market calibration. (G,S) value 2q-1/8; (T,S) value 5q-21/8; threshold 5/6. Direct enumeration of all 16 mappings verifies other rules cannot exceed the envelope. For q=3/5: values 43/40 and 3/8, regret 7/10; for q=9/10: values 67/40 and 15/8.
6. **Two indistinguishable target worlds:** available source-policy report validation reveals q_source=9/10 but does not measure the controller-1 target conditional kernel. Worlds q_target=3/5 and q_target=9/10 share the source report, baseline customer p, approved action table, and source observations, yet imply different best target rules and economic values. Target-policy labels (or justified transport assumptions) discriminate. This is a standard support nonidentification example.
7. **Robust choice:** if q_target belongs to [3/5,9/10], (G,S) achieves its minimum 43/40 at q=3/5. No stochastic policy can exceed W(3/5)=43/40 in that world, so 43/40 is the exact minimax guarantee. This proof avoids mistaking a deterministic enumeration for proof over randomized policies.
8. **Calibration information value:** with q ∈ {3/5,9/10}, equal prior independent of customer z, without revelation the predictive mixture kernel has q=3/4; W(3/4)=11/8. Revelation before the individual customer report yields (43/40+15/8)/2=59/40, an increment 1/10. Compare with one-world stale rule regret 7/10; they are different estimands and different planner information sets.
9. **Countermodel to overly broad monotonicity:** under target q=3/5, naive mandatory execution of the source-trained report rule (T,S) returns 3/8 below a constant no-report policy G=1. It does not contradict Blackwell optional-information value, since using T after a high signal is not the optimal informed policy. If a report is required to actuate physical behavior or adds resource cost, gross optional monotonicity itself may be unavailable.
10. **Boundary:** neither fixed-K theorem nor q-enumeration proves changed-controller target visitation, new-body physical qualification, approved recovery, source-to-target label transport or a real customer price. Only approved complete-network comparisons and joint original-order evidence can support those.
