# Competing source lineages: resource-feasible masked economic closure

23 November 2026 — continuation of [third-cohort moment closure](THIRD_COHORT_MOMENT_CLOSURE.md), within *When Capabilities Become Production*. Conditional mathematical synthesis, not observed robotics returns.

## 1. A resource-feasible decision certificate

The previous feature-span theorem propagated all selected moments under fixed lawful rules. Here we need less: determine the economic choice among competing source lineages and embodiments sharing the worker released by prior learning. Suppose a fixed lawful early program produces a predecision report h (including visible rights, original buyer authorization where known, workload and physical availability). On each h, A(h) is the finite set of **joint feasible schedules**, not a Cartesian product of separately admissible robot actions. Include the retained feasible fallback a0(h). A schedule chooses training/validation, dispatch, and a specified subsequent lawful continuation. Scores q_theta(h,a) are expected remaining net accepted-production contributions under one common structural law theta; include costs and new customer orders once, including all subsequent optimal production only if the continuation can actually implement it. A schedule cannot depend on hidden type or unknown law.

Let m_theta(h)=Pr_theta(h) on the original prefix denominator. For a rule rho(a|h) supported on A(h), define the masked contrast
    M_theta(h,a)=m_theta(h)[q_theta(h,a)-q_theta(h,a0)].
Then J_theta(rho)=B_theta+sum_(h,a) rho(a|h) M_theta(h,a), where B_theta is the value of the same prefix and fallback. Zero-mass cells contribute zero. Proof: subtract the two conditional expectations and average over the common prefix. Thus baseline plus masked contrasts suffice for evaluation of this menu, without identifying the entire technical transition kernel. Unknown laws cannot pick rho; compare fixed rules on the same feasible law set. If capacity is shared across original clusters, the feasible rule must instead be defined on their joint observed history, or satisfy the relevant physically executable joint schedule constraint; a per-cell independent maximization is not in general feasible. Cross-prefix comparisons require each prefix's own mass and baseline.

**Mask consequence.** A feature space closing open-loop source values need not close these contrasts. For all distributions on the finite full state, with constant feature, the fixed-rule row-span argument of the previous chapter requires the payoff pullback multiplied by 1{report=h} for every distinguishable supported action contrast. If it is outside the span, a left-nullspace perturbation preserves the observed moments while changing the value of that lawful rule. One may retain only differences between feasible schedules if the common fallback value is known; this is a weaker requirement than full forward-state closure. In particular, technical qualification independence is neither necessary nor sufficient.

**Constructive resource-aware residual bound.** For a given report, write x(a) in {0,1}^n for distinct scheduled activities, with x(a0)=0 in the optional-activity representation (the fallback resources and displaced work are already valued in the contrast). Assume x≥0, Ax≤b and x≤1, A nonnegative, and a verified representation
    q_theta(h,a)-q_theta(h,a0)=x(a)' d_theta(h)+i_theta(h,a).
Given d_hat and nonnegative component error bounds e_j(h) with |d_theta,j-d_hat_j|≤e_j, and |i_theta(h,a)|≤eta(h,a), the approximation x'd_hat has error at most e'x+eta(h,a). The interaction term is essential: joint qualification, queues and future buyer menus are not generally additive. For two schedules a,b the common coefficient error cancels on shared activities:
    |[q_theta(a)-q_theta(b)]-[x(a)-x(b)]'d_hat|
       ≤ e'|x(a)-x(b)|+eta(h,a)+eta(h,b).
One may use a tighter bound on the interaction difference if available. Proof: expand the difference and apply the triangle inequality. This is a conditional robust linear-score inequality, not a new assumption that production is additive.

For absolute score error over feasible schedules, the support function H_h(e)=max_(a in A(h)) e'x(a) can be computed by a small actual scheduling MILP. Alternatively any lambda≥0 gives the certified upper
    H_h(e)≤lambda'b+sum_j max(0,e_j-(A'lambda)_j).
Proof: e'x≤lambda'Ax+(e-A'lambda)'x≤the right side. This is a weak-duality bound, not an assertion of integer strong duality. With one worker slot and unit jobs, H=max e_j (include zero), not sum e_j. With multiple slots the exact support function depends on durations, skills and deadlines. Bounds for a difference of two schedules use the weighted symmetric difference; do not use the one-slot absolute bound for two disjoint schedules.

At a fixed prefix, integrate the selected-schedule error on its actual report occupancy; a uniform bound is sum_h m_theta(h)[H_h(e)+max_a eta(h,a)]. For unknown occupancy use a common-law upper bound, not independently chosen marginal extrema as a claim of sharpness. An identified lower bound on a fixed feasible policy exceeding an upper bound for another policy proves a ranking. To extend to multiple dates, choose approximate potentials vhat_t on lawful histories and define d_t(h,a)=r_theta,t(h,a)+beta E_theta[vhat_(t+1)|h,a]-vhat_t(h). By telescoping, J_theta(pi)=E_theta vhat_1+sum_t beta^(t-1) E_theta,pi d_t plus terminal approximation residual. Thus a verified resource-aware upper/lower bound on each d_t, including anchor residual, bounds value under candidate occupancy. If using the action-contrast representation above, apply it to d_t(h,a)-d_t(h,a0); separately bound d_t(h,a0). With different policies their occupancies generally differ, so the within-h cancellation cannot be carried across policies without a coupling/occupancy proof. This is a constructive residual certificate; neither a fitted residual on incumbent samples nor an oracle knowing the latent law establishes it.

## 2. Exact four-cohort witness with heterogeneous downstream buyer menus

Reuse the previous K/G prefix on the original denominator. Early A=3, K cost 1; half of clusters are eligible for G and the other half serve C=2; half of eligible G succeeds with B=8. Net prefix expected contribution is 3−1+1+2=5. Success clears an owed recovery job and releases exactly one t3 worker slot, probability 1/4; only these histories have the new licensed trajectory and available downstream t4 slot. The old t3 L order yields 2, the old t4 W order yields 2. Keep both as fallback. No further payoff on unreleased clusters. No unsafe dispatch after failed validation.

On released histories, the firm observes a licensed binary diagnostic H before choosing L, M or N. M is target grounding using the newly licensed recovery source; N is a distinct source-to-embodiment validation program (e.g. separate simulation-trained checkpoint). Their respective source rights are assumed on this report set. Each consumes the entire t3 slot plus explicit cost 1. Thus they are mutually exclusive and displace L. In t4 exactly one worker slot exists. A new buyer D_M orders and pays net contribution 14 if M qualified (Z=1) and Y=1; a different new buyer D_N pays net 10 if N qualified (Z=0) and Y=1. Y denotes a latent common contingent new-order opportunity, not a realized order, payment or known predecision report. Otherwise W still delivers 2. Once actual order and safe qualification are known at t4, take the higher feasible authorized job. The two target qualification profiles Z and 1−Z are a deliberately restrictive technical countermodel; not a claim that actual data sources are complementary in this way. Even if Z could be measured in an independently licensed paired proxy test, its transport to the production program requires separate evidence. H,Y,Z are assigned at cluster entry with the stated conditional law on the released population; no forbidden validation is imputed on nonrelease. All new orders are distinct from A/B/C/L/W. No data resale or premium is assigned.

Relative to L,W the realized remaining contrast is 0 for L, −3+12YZ for M, −3+8Y(1−Z) for N. All costs, displacement and new contributions have been counted exactly once. The mutually exclusive t3 actions and one-slot t4 production avoid summing revenues for two schedules. On released histories let (H,Y,Z) be uniform on even-parity triples with probability 1−p and on odd-parity triples with probability p. Each individual and every full pair distribution is identical for p∈[0,1]. Marginal technical success rates, joint technical profile, the overall M and N paid-outcome laws (omitting H), and pairwise H/Y/Z tests are unchanged. The **joint H-masked paid events** differ; do not claim equality of complete arm histories including H and realized paid outcome.

For H=0 the conditional contrasts (M,N) are (3−6p,−3+4p); for H=1 they are (−3+6p,1−4p). Derive by enumerating the two parity atoms per report, or using Pr(YZ=1|H=0)=(1−p)/2 and Pr(Y(1−Z)=1|H=0)=p/2, reversed for H=1. Each report has original-denominator mass 1/8. Thus:
    J_L=6; J_allM=6; J_allN=23/4.
    J_even=13/2−5p/4  (M if H=0, N if H=1).
    J_odd =21/4+5p/4  (N if H=0, M if H=1).
The known-law optimum in this restricted menu is
    6+[max(0,3−6p,−3+4p)+max(0,−3+6p,1−4p)]/8.
At p=1/2 no action in either report has positive contrast; therefore maximin over common-law mixtures is exactly 6, attained by L. Even a law-known decision without lawful H would choose L or all-M and achieve at most 6. If H is lawfully observed and p=0, the fixed even rule reaches 13/2; at p=1 it falls to 21/4. The counterworld reverses its comparison against a stipulated independently staffed E=25/4, built from retained A,C=5, an additional independently supported new order of 9/4 and cost 1. E is an assumed alternative, not field verified. E wins the maximin comparison over this menu. Both fixed arms alone are below E. For p≤1/8, however, a direct original-denominator masked paid audit certifies J_even≥203/32>25/4. No arbitrary threshold: it follows from the constructed amounts. If that audit provides a uniform bound p≤1/8, favorable-model component errors e_M=6/8 and e_N=4/8 apply in each report; single-slot support yields total error ≤(1/4)(6/8)=3/16, so J_even≥101/16>25/4 even without exploiting the selected action. The tighter selected-error calculation gives 203/32. No further claims for the full class of early K/G choices or general hardware technology.

The missing paid contrasts involve the triple H*Y*Z on the released risk set; even/odd signed measures annihilate all degree≤2 polynomials. Conditioning on release itself was necessary before applying the masks; pooling all preexisting field tests can fail even before that step. This is not simply higher technical transfer: the two lineages compete for the same resource, and the masked interaction decides which distinct later buyer menu is reachable. If we remove H's rights, even known p cannot license its use; if free optional routine P reproduces the *entire* K prefix and all downstream transitions at zero incremental cost, L gives 7. If E shares the sole C worker slot in its retained baseline and loses C=2, its committed value becomes 17/4, and retaining R=5 dominates it. These are structural alternatives, not empirical conclusions.

## 3. Interpretation, prior foundations, and next gap

The object connecting Model B to Model A is the expected difference of **joint feasible accepted-production schedules conditional on the lawfully available report**, not an independently summable list of per-source technical values. Feature closure for a restricted choice may be achieved by fewer contrast moments than full state prediction; conversely a new resource or report may enlarge the feature span. Standard weak LP duality and robust linear optimization already underlie the certificate (Bertsimas and Sim, *The Price of Robustness*, Operations Research 52(1), 35–53, 2004, [publisher abstract](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.1030.0065), checked 23 Nov 2026; only abstract/metadata read). The earlier predictive-state/decision foundations and DDA §5/Appendix B remain prior art. Our conditional application is to licensed, source-generated qualifications, physically executable shared worker schedules and actual original-buyer production, not independent novelty.
