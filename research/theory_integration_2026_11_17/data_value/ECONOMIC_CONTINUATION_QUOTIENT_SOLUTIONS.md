# Instructor solutions — economic continuation and buyer demand

1. At H both values vanish. If the next continuation is constant within projected next-state cells, the action-conditional expectation of that continuation depends only on the abstract transition law. Equal conditional net paid reward and equal **legal** action menu then give identical maxima. Induct backward. C alone does not reveal whether an A or B original buyer has offered an order, whether a shared operator is free, or whether a permission expires. Two full histories with C=1 and different offered buyer Z can require different actions.
2. Let D_t bound the value difference at date t. The maximum-of-actions inequality, conditional mean error ε_t, TV expectation bound 2B(H-t-1)δ_t and continuation error D_{t+1} imply D_t≤ε_t+2B(H-t-1)δ_t+D_{t+1}. With D_H=0, sum. A strict positive difference \bar J_SG-\bar J_E exceeding η_SG+η_E implies SG wins; symmetric negative implies E. An average logged discrepancy can vanish while individual lawful Z-conditioned action residuals are ±1/2; it does not meet the uniform requirement or establish unlogged action support.
3. J_R=p regardless of Z reporting; J_SG,hidden=max(p,1-p); J_SG,reported=1; J_E=max(3/4,p). The complementary economic value of source-enabled action B and the buyer-order signal is 1−max(p,1−p)=min(p,1−p). At p=1/2, R=1/2, hidden SG=1/2, informed SG=1, E=3/4; hidden SG−E=−1/4, informed SG−E=+1/4. The first-stage common original-buyer paid contribution cancels. The options do not assume a technical learning complementarity or a new data-accounting premium.
4. Let C=1 with probability one. In R only service A is legally qualified; SG additionally qualifies B through a legally licensed source-trained update. Both have the same date-0 complete paid events, yet a later original buyer requests B and can pay one unit if B is served. The R policy menu lacks B whereas SG's includes it, so a quotient calibrated only on R cannot transfer its action set. A minimum refinement records SG checkpoint/service qualification and the offered buyer B order before dispatch (and relevant rights/operator eligibility). This is a constructed existence example, not an identified physical robot effect.
