# Proof and negative-result ledger — endogenous demand/learning alignment
**19 November 2026.** This document describes conditional mathematical derivations; the executable checks examples, not the general proofs.

**P1 (sharp policy-specific three-way identification).** Given feasible six moments (a,b,d,x,y,z) of A, B, D *jointly defined under the same physically approved policy*, reconstruct all eight event cells as affine functions of t=P(ABD). The lower/upper maxima/minima E1 are exactly necessary and sufficient nonnegativity restrictions. This follows without conditional independence or additive transfer; every endpoint is attained by its reconstructed law. For 2 components plus original demand with shared operator interactions, these are not enough once operator state is stochastic.

**P2 (economic bound under one complete-service increment).** If the additional *captured* complete-service contribution is precisely g·1_ABD, g>=0, and a known incremental cost c is incurred once, the exact expected increment is gt−c; substitute E1 to obtain sharp [gL−c,gU−c]. A fixed scalar competitor v is robustly preferable when gU−c<v. If it is a production network nonlinear in several orders and capacities, this scalar formula cannot substitute for V; one must optimize over a joint state law/feasible Q and respect operator resource prices.

**P3 (matched pairwise evidence fails).** U and V both have (a,b,d)=(1/2,1/2,1/2) and (x,y,z)=(1/4,1/4,1/4), while t_U=0 and t_V=1/4. This explicitly disproves the conjecture that *even all matched pairwise qualification–demand correlations* identify the complete-service return after source training. Their one-way and two-way marginals are pairwise independent, but the triple is not identified. Source X net contribution 13/5 versus 23/5; grounding G=18/5 reverses optimal choice. A/B qualification must be validated on the **same original order** under X to identify the relevant triple, with no selective recording.

**P4 (optional noninterfering data).** With fixed environment and legal access to all previous feasible policies, expanding the data stock at zero cost without forcing retraining/dispatch change weakly increases the optimized objective by policy-set inclusion. This does NOT compare two controllers whose admission changes future orders, operator loads, physical risk or rights. U does not demonstrate negative technical transfer; it demonstrates an unidentified triple alignment and X's paid opportunity cost.

**P5 (conditional TV sensitivity).** For f∈[m,M], P,Q on the *same full payoff-relevant state space*, E_P f−E_Q f bounded in absolute value by (M−m)TV(P,Q). If all approved continuation rules share the menu and f_r bounds, apply uniform bound and sup inequalities. No common complete state/menu or defensible TV bound, no transport certificate. U,V are disjoint supports with TV=1 despite every pairwise moment matching. The three-way sharp interval can be materially tighter than an unconditioned TV bound.

**Verification scope.** Exact integer enumeration at atom denominator 8 covered all C(15,7)=6,435 nonnegative eight-cell compositions, finding 6,105 feasible distinct six-moment patterns. For each pattern, the minimum and maximum observed t equal E1; at the parity parameter t numerator 0,1,2 over 8, all six moments remain fixed and the G/X switch is exact. Independent Python Fraction arithmetic checked the two endpoint worlds and the interior t=1/8 law. This is scoped numerical corroboration, **not** an independent mathematical proof, physical replication or complete CI. The algebra above is the proof.
