# Exercises — cross-program transfer, queue conservation and economic data value

1. Given c=1/8,l=1/16,d=3/8,e=7/16, ε=1/8, identify whether a G event B≤W is feasible and compute the sharp interval for Pr(B).
2. Prove the one-sided worker inequality B≤W when a G calibration can add a recovery job but cannot clear a preexisting job, and give a concrete production mechanism under which it fails.
3. In the main synthetic P/G/E model with G(t)=5/16+2t, P=3/4, E=23/32 and conditional ε=3/8, solve the regret-optimal ex-ante randomization and contrast maximin production. Identify the legal action support necessary to make this an implementable option.
4. Explain why even a complete historically observed (A,W,old buyer Z) panel cannot estimate ε without a G intervention or defensible cross-action physical model. Give two laws consistent with old/P but opposite P/G rankings.
5. What does a certified engineering cap Pr(W=1,F_G=0)<1/4 imply if the proposed G scenario fixes Pr(W=1)=3/4, Pr(F_G=1)=1/2 and F_G≤W? Interpret falsification versus a better revenue forecast.
6. For several heterogeneous customer markets h, write the common-law polytope and distinguish a valid single ε average from marketwise ε_h caps. Why could dividing each marginal value by a different 'industry multiplier' produce a false P/E ranking?
