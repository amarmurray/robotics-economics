# Solutions: deadline-sensitive economic data transfer

23 November 2026. Instructor copy.

1. Let z=Pr(g2,k2). Then Pr(g1,k1)=z, Pr(g1,k2)=1/2-z, Pr(g2,k1)=1/2-z, and Pr(g2,k2)=z, with z∈[0,1/2]. Each marginal is unchanged. Under joint neutral processing, completion minutes are 3,4,4,5, respectively. Total accepted A receipts contribute 4(1-z), and newly ordered accepted B contributes z, so J=4-3z.
2. Without B the derivative is -4; B contributes Pr(g1,k1)=z, so the derivative becomes -3. More positive source-duration coincidence raises both the very early and very late mass, redistributing intermediate cases. There is no universal sign for the deadline-renewal adjustment.
3. Under shortening K minutes are 1 regardless of its standalone duration; when g1 a complete A plus B earns 5 and when g2 accepted A earns 4, yielding 9/2. Under elongation when g1, K still takes standalone 1 or 2 and receipts are 5 or 4; when g2 its extra minute makes A miss deadline, giving J=5z+4(1/2-z)=2+z. Both mappings retain exactly the same isolated potential histories and complete paired technical qualification.
4. Process K iff g=1. On g=1 the most K can take is 2 minutes under the stipulated envelope, so the A ticket earns at least 4 by minute4; on g=2 retain G alone, earning 3. Thus J≥(4+3)/2=7/2>13/4. G duration must be observed before the legal irreversible K choice; skipping K must be feasible without sunk remediation; customer orders, safety, worker sharing, cost assumptions and E staffing must also satisfy the stated environment. If any fails, the certificate does not carry.
5. Within histories H that legally precede the K decision, a lawful, sufficiently supported cluster-level K-versus-no-K intervention with recorded action probabilities, complete G/K repair durations, original A accepted/no-service outcomes, new B offers including no-orders, verified payments and true capacity identifies E[Y(K)-Y(noK)|H]. Require consistency, sequential exchangeability and no unmodeled cross-cluster interference. Conditioning only on invoices removes missed-deadline outcomes; benchmark Q does not observe repair duration or customer renewal. Unsupported strata remain partially identified.
