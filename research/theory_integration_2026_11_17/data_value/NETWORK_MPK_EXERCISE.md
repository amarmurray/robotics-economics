# Exercise: where is the next robot productive?
Two serial stages have capacities 2 and 2, with final demand 4 and common net margin 3. A learned controller changes only the second stage capacity to 4. Calculate current production value and the marginal gross value of another unit of first-stage capacity before and after learning. Then let two parallel stages of capacity (2,0) feed demand 3; an alternative learned route becomes capacity 1. Calculate the same contrasts. Explain why utilization alone is inadequate. In a two-date variant q=k, final upstream capacity k and final downstream capacity q under G or 1 under P, k=1 or 2, discount 0.8 and both margins 3: calculate the two gross marginal values, and decide whether incremental capital cost 4 is covered by either program. Do not assume a real service can be represented as a single-commodity network.
