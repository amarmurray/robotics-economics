# Prospective original-offer and recovery evidence for supported adaptive policies

23 November 2026 · Unpopulated protocol, **not** a customer dataset, consent, or approval to run experiments. Paired with SEQUENTIAL_ADAPTIVE_BUYER_WORKER_POLICY.md.

**Interference unit.** Register the entire customer/robot/teleoperator/learner-sharing cluster before assignment. Record A and C orders before robot offers and after, rejected and unserved demand, their real payment terms and lifetime contractual budgets. Independently verify E’s alleged separate support staff and cell. A worker shared across robots is not two workers because the jobs have separate IDs. Assigning a model update across clusters creates interference and can invalidate cluster-level comparisons.

**Rights and chronology.** Before any P/G/E/R program is eligible, record the customer's permission for data retention, training, source-to-target reuse, technical evaluation, commercial deployment and original-order auditing separately. Log model/robot/embodiment versions and the provenance of field successes, recovery trajectories, remote teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained data. Do not infer train rights from equipment ownership or invoice access. At t0 log original eligibility and assignment probability. At t1–t2 log site original offers, actual paid accepted units, verified quality, qualification results, compute, worker occupation and newly created repair claims. At t3 *before action* log buyer A/C offers and no-offers, remaining budgets, qualification, worker queue and which S/M/other actions were physically and legally feasible. Log action selection probability; do not make a worker free by recording two simultaneous assignments. After action log actual paid output, quality and postaction repair job creation. At t4, before dispatch, jointly log original offers/no-offers D and actual worker availability/clearance L (with exact job IDs, elapsed and human occupied time). Keep this register even if the buyer is declined. After dispatch log completed and accepted units, rework, payments, costs and censored unresolved jobs.

**Economically sufficient prospective experiment.** Only subject to actual permissions and safety approval, randomize program z across whole sharing clusters and randomize feasible t3 S versus M actions with known positive probabilities in the reachable G histories. Audit all eligible t4 original buyer offers or randomly sample reports at a known, history-based positive inclusion probability; sampling must not depend on the *unseen* order and must not induce buyer behavior. Record worker status on the same unit and date to estimate the joint D×L coincidence. Include failed and unserved efforts, not selected invoices alone. For alternative terminal t4 dispatch, independently qualify each recipe and preserve its action support. Neither an R-only panel nor a G/S-only panel identifies M's outcomes, even if both have exhaustive selected receipts.

**Measurement failure tests.** Check t0 assignment coverage, cross-cluster model sharing, effective positivity and posttreatment censoring. If M is not permitted, no M causal estimate is claimed. If workers are known blocked past the terminal horizon, buyer reports for that fixed terminal contribution may be optional; do not apply this shortcut to later horizons. Audit whether report acquisition itself changes customer demand (then treat reporting as an action). Keep source-to-target benchmark improvement separate from qualified customer paid service and from actual resource prices.

**No evidence yet.** Signed G use rights, lawful randomized S/M observations, joint customer original-order and worker clearance panels, verified independent E staffing, actual robot payments and official numerical BEA production coefficients have not been obtained. This protocol does not imply access to any confidential employer or customer data.
