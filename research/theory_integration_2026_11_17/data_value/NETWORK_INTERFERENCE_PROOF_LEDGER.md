# Proof and falsification ledger — network-coupled learning and production

**18 November 2026, private.** Companion to [NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_THEORY.md](NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_THEORY.md). These are elementary conditional mathematical deductions or counterexamples; none is measured robotics economics or claimed new network-causal-inference mathematics.

## N1 whole-network randomization

For fixed preassignment network k in d, define complete potential contribution Y_k(a), including all customer/qualification/coordination externalities, and I_k(a)=1{A_k=a}. By actual randomized probabilities p_k(a|d)>0 and consistency, E[I_k(a)Y_k(a)/p_k(a|d)|potential histories]=Y_k(a). Average over k. Positivity for an approved whole vector is mandatory; no intervention on an unsafe vector is recommended to establish it. If "networks" share a checkpoint/customer/order/operator, no-cross-unit interference fails. Local pool randomization cannot be relabeled network randomization.

## N1b sequential importance identity and limits

For any finite common approved history h_T under the behavior design p and target pi, its joint assignment/transition probability is the common transition likelihood multiplied by the behavior (respectively target) action probabilities. The product of pi_t/p_t cancels the assignment factors whenever target path support is contained in behavior path support. Summing Y(h_T) times the weighted behavior probabilities equals the target sum. Sequential conditional randomization and complete histories are not optional assumptions. If one target history has p=0, changing its unobserved payoff can alter target J while leaving all behavior observations unchanged, proving nonidentification without a bridging structural assumption. If a controller update changes the transition kernel itself through policy history, the kernel is conditional on the *same observed complete history*, not a history-independent device. This is standard sequential g-computation/off-policy evaluation, not a discovery.

## N3 selective rollout corrected for connected-network spillovers

Keep the complete pre-action target masses w_A=.7 and w_B=.3. Old route is 7 in both segments. Mixed approved A route has unchanged own gain g=.5. Let s=7-Y_B(A-mixed,B-old), per B unit, and c be actual additional total capture-equivalent rollout costs. Then J=.7(7+.5)+.3(7-s)-c=7.35-.3s-c and J-J_old=.35-.3s-c. For s in [-.4,1.5], monotonicity implies lower 7.35-.45=6.90 and upper 7.35+.12=7.47 (when c=0). The exact sign threshold is .35/.3=7/6 at c=0. The result is sharp in the stated unrestricted additive population response class: fix all baseline/A outcomes at the specified numbers, choose B's post-route net contribution equal to 7-s for either endpoint, and do not impose any other physical response restriction. Under richer dynamic controllers or endogenous entry, that sharpness need not transfer.

A separate causal attribution countermodel holds B's total change at -0.6 while changing mechanisms: (i) -0.6 in frozen-demand qualification with unchanged market orders, or (ii) +0.2 in frozen-demand qualification accompanied by -0.8 in net order opportunity, with the same total. Without held-out qual and original-order denominator, only the economic total is identified. This countermodel is intentionally not calibrated or a complete revenue contract.

## N4 noncomposition of safety

For each of two sites i=1,2, let U_i be binary peak recovery need with P(U_i=1)=.1. Joint world I: independent, P(U_1=U_2=1)=.01; the supported path consumes 2>B=1 and violates the hard shared peak rule. Joint world II: P(1,0)=.1, P(0,1)=.1, P(0,0)=.8 and P(1,1)=0. Individual marginals and expected totals agree, but the actual peak in world II never exceeds one. One can construct world II only under a physical scheduling/no-coincidence mechanism or verified support assumption, not by pretending no rare simultaneity has been observed. The event-existence distinction is independent of how accurate a point estimate of mean teleop hours may be. If events are staged across time, the temporal overlap graph must be included.

An upper envelope of each site's pathwise resources gives a sufficient network envelope by the triangle bound; it is not necessary because the maxima may occur on mutually exclusive histories. This is a hard-cap notion. Under an explicitly chosen probabilistic chance constraint, the conclusion can change but requires a different risk objective and verified tail information. There is no safety authorization in this construction.

## Prior-art objection and discriminating measurement

Hudgens–Halloran (2008) and Aronow–Samii (2017) already treat interference and design-based exposure effects. Leung (2022) develops approximate neighborhood interference under topology/remainder restrictions. Our N1/N1b are elementary cases of established inverse probability weighting and sequential design logic. Demand and resource externalities are ordinary production economics. Distinctiveness, if any, must come from justified physical source-learning/qualification mechanisms and actual customer production evidence, not a theorem renamed for robotics.

Two primary falsifiers: (a) complete checkpoint separation makes observed cross-pool skill-qualification effects vanish at sufficiently low economic cost (then reduced independent-pool model is viable); (b) full-network experimental follow-up finds no B order/qualification/capacity response to A rollout (then earlier 7.35 conditional certificate remains valid for the matched population). A negative benchmark under compulsory joint training is not negative marginal value of free optional data. The model's retained old policy prevents such an inference. All numbers in the worked countermodels are synthetic normalized contribution units.

**Evidence gap:** no observed full eligible-order denominator, no joined recovery schedule/independent safety signoff, no field source→controller→whole-qualification→captured production trace, no quantitative official BLS/BEA production backbone, no independent theorem/novelty review, no full repository CI. No stage acceptance is inferred from these derivations.
