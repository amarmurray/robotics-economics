## Single common qualified-production value

For a versioned, physically admitted whole policy and original eligible procurement parent, define A,B as binary natural-menu order acceptance. Let a=P(A), b=P(B), and q=P(A and B). Optimized gross contribution f depends on which accepted orders can be **safely completed and paid** under the physical and organizational resource envelope, with four values f00,f10,f01,f11. Directly expanding four response atoms gives

    E[f] = f00+(f10-f00)a+(f01-f00)b+gamma*q,
    gamma=f11-f10-f01+f00.

Gamma is the **economic production interaction** of accepted orders, not a fixed training-transfer coefficient or extra data price. Given only a,b, sharp joint demand bounds are

    max(0,a+b-1) <= q <= min(a,b).

Proof: nonnegative atom masses (p11,p10,p01,p00)=(q,a-q,b-q,1-a-b+q) are both necessary and sufficient; every q in the interval is realized. Extrema for E[f] follow by the sign of gamma. This is standard Fréchet–Hoeffding mathematics and a finite value-table identity, not a new mathematical theorem.

**Resource and demand countermodels.** If accepted A earns 4, accepted B earns 3 and both compete for exactly one operator slot, the optimizer chooses A when present. Then f=(0,4,3,4), gamma=-3 and E[f]=4a+3b-3q. Positive purchase correlation *decreases* realized gross value through simultaneous contention. If instead the buyer will pay 7 only for a qualified, indivisible A+B result, f=(0,0,0,7), gamma=7 and E[f]=7q. Positive correlation *increases* value by supplying a complementary order. The former is substitutable capacity; the latter is O-ring/complementary customer acceptance. Both are conditional on actually signed complete safe robot/operator dispatch and original contract terms. They are NOT two revenue streams to add together.

## Selection and C/N version drift do not identify the buyer copula

Let C be a distinct commitment/warranty elicitation menu, N the original natural offer. Suppose the eligible parent frame is complete, the C response-recording rate is rho, observed respondent marginal assent probabilities are s_A,s_B, **only separate marginal summaries are retained**, and missing C respondents may depend arbitrarily on latent purchase. Independently defend bounds |a_N-a_C|<=eps_A and |b_N-b_C|<=eps_B for the same parent population; these are only marginal mean restrictions, NOT a joint cross-version total-variation claim. Then

    L_i=max(0,rho*s_i-eps_i),
    U_i=min(1,rho*s_i+1-rho+eps_i).

The sharp original-menu joint response range is

    q_N in [max(0,L_A+L_B-1), min(U_A,U_B)].

The sharp one-slot expected **gross** contribution interval is

    [4 L_A+3 L_B-3 min(L_A,L_B),
     4 U_A+3 U_B-3 max(0,U_A+U_B-1)].

The sharp indivisible-bundle gross interval is

    [7 max(0,L_A+L_B-1), 7 min(U_A,U_B)].

**Proof of sharpness under these EXACT observables.** For each order separately, the missing-response partition gives a_C in [rho*s_A,rho*s_A+1-rho], and likewise b_C. Independent stipulated mean-drift restrictions enlarge these to [L_i,U_i]. Without paired respondent observations or joint cross-version restriction, compatible C respondent/missing distributions can realize all those marginal endpoints. For any feasible natural marginals a,b, the displayed four atoms allow all feasible natural q. In the one-slot model, substituting the largest q for the lower E[f] or smallest q for the upper gives expressions nondecreasing in each a,b because margins 4>=3>=0; choose the marginal L or U endpoints, respectively. For the bundle, extrema are the q extrema. Atom constructions attain all extrema. If actual paired C records, natural buyer joint records, selection restrictions or whole-parent cross-version bounds are added, these broad intervals may no longer be sharp and the feasible joint polytope must be reconstructed.

**Strong negative result.** Even observing C's *joint* q_C does not identify q_N from equality of C/N **individual marginal means**. Example: a_N=b_N=a_C=b_C=1/2, q_C=1/4. The natural N law may have q_N=0 (disjoint A-only/B-only parents) or q_N=1/2 (both-or-neither), both with the identical observed C distribution and zero marginal drift. One-slot gross is 7/2 versus 2, and after a hypothetical once-only G cost of 1/4 becomes 13/4 versus 7/4. A separately qualified and priced embodiment E value of 3 reverses the preferred option. For indivisible A+B, gross is 0 versus 7/2 and net -1/4 versus 13/4, reversing against E in the *opposite direction*. This E level is a conditional counterexample witness, not an estimated break-even threshold. A genuine independently validated joint |q_N-q_C|<=delta or complete-parent TV bound would tighten q, but two marginal restrictions cannot substitute for it. If the commercial outcome function f changes across menus, transport its costs/rights and resource effects separately.

## Technical transfer versus economically useful data

The existing dynamic model must use a **single** optimized V/J. R is permitted old qualified production; X is selected heterogeneous field, failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic or public/pretrained training exposure with rights, compute and curation; G is target grounding and certified observation/recovery and original-market alignment; E changes embodiment and feasible whole-service recipes. A technically improved X qualification marginal does not establish an improved expected paid order: even a qualification probability p and a buyer acceptance probability q leave P(qualified AND purchased) between max(0,p+q-1) and min(p,q) without a justified dependence assumption. A qualified new G recipe may add nothing when the original buyer never jointly orders or shared human capacity is exhausted. More unpaired routine source traces do not identify the natural original-parent buyer copula. Deliberate target-audit acquisition competes with more source learning and E inside the *same* J; production-generated future data already enter J continuation. Optional free extra data never lowers optimized gross value when old training policies remain feasible, unlike forced co-training or lost rights. An old controller available at the start is not a certified post-X fallback.
