# Empirical protocol: corrections, support, qualification and customer value
**Status: identification design, not executed robotics research. November 17, 2026 Eastern.**

## Unit of observation and target outcomes

The unit is an **attempted, complete customer service** with joint state exposures and any safety-qualified intervention episodes, rather than an isolated successful demonstration. Link through anonymized experiment IDs and dates: customer task and acceptance protocol; initial controller/embodiment and pretrained/source lineage; eligible task/site/body and usage rights; opportunity-state exposure opportunities versus actual visits; operator presence; gate requested/triggered/missed interventions; intervention content, duration, switch and cost; whether the episode was accepted with or without assistance; subsequent training inclusion; retained qualified configuration and joint qualification outcomes; complete-service cycle time, rework, safety and all-attempt cost; operator/customer alternative and actual demand. Privacy and explicit data use rights are prerequisites.

Do **not** ask a robot to fail in an unsafe live customer environment. Vary exposure only within approved and independently supervised safe states, or in simulation when appropriate; use independent audit/monitoring for missed-error detection. A safety-critical report must never be delayed to manufacture an information-timing treatment.

## Contrasts that discriminate mechanisms

1. **Exposure and gating:** factorial or quasi-randomized safe exposure eligibility and logged audit gate calibration; record X, detection opportunities, independent would-be error assessment and C. Estimate exposure e and gate recall g conditional on the task/site/body. The simple equation correction rate=e*g*error need is testable only if the gate produces negligible false positives. Report a sensitivity interval otherwise.
2. **Correction selection:** pre-specify target opportunity states and document inclusion propensities, including zero-support cells. A correction-only panel should be contrasted with an X-logged, independently audited panel. If apparent autonomy improvements arise only through smaller e or g, the underlying held-out error rate may not improve.
3. **Physical learning:** with safety-approved data already collected, randomize *training inclusion* at a fixed compute/curation budget and matched initial weights/seed protocol. Evaluate truly held-out target sites/bodies, autonomous and assisted service and **joint recipe qualifications**; preserve old policies so negative forced transfer is distinguishable from optimized optional use.
4. **Report timing and investment:** randomize only non-safety diagnosis availability ahead of a subsequent optional source/grounding/embodiment decision; hold actual corrected trajectories and compatible hardware constant. Compare adaptive acquisition against a competent ex-ante plan, not against an unaware deliberately poor agent.
5. **Production economy:** separately estimate revenue/price, accepted all-attempt service, retained demand, resource scarcity, customer surplus and provider captured contribution for qualified routes and a competent target-only specialist/human option. Feed the *joint* qualification distribution and customer opportunity states into the existing optimized production f(A;omega). No direct mapping of success-point changes to dollars.

## Falsifiers

- **Signal-likelihood falsification:** measured Pr(C|H,eligible exposure,gate) cannot be represented by (I1) at one common p_h across policies; include policy-dependent e_h, false positives and operator choice instead of fitting spurious transfer coefficients.
- **Learning falsification:** correction-only inclusion does not yield a durable, held-out increase in a qualified complete-service recipe even though assisted task completion improved; set the relevant persistent Q transition to zero or negative under the forced recipe.
- **Economic falsification:** a high-performing target-only or changed-embodiment service beats source reuse after common resource/cost accounting; the probe threshold fails because T is larger.
- **Observation-support failure:** some relevant states never appear in any safe approved collection policy; no inverse-propensity correction can identify their counterfactual learning.
- **Price/retention failure:** the same controller qualification has no incremental accepted order or provider contribution under the current customer demand and optimal recipe set. Report useful technical transfer but zero incremental economic value.

## Prior sources and reading boundaries

DAGGER: Ross, Gordon and Bagnell (2011), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html — PMLR abstract and metadata inspected November 17, 2026 Eastern; establishes policy-induced observation distribution, not this experiment's effect sizes.

ThriftyDAgger: Hoque et al. (2022), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v164/hoque22a.html — PMLR abstract and metadata inspected; explicit budget-aware novelty/risk gating. Earlier chapter separately inspected its physical-cable-routing Table 3 and emphasized assisted versus autonomous successes and reporting selection; do not use those trials as accepted-order economics.

IntervenGen: Hoque et al. (2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01472 — abstract and introduction inspected November 17; controlled generation of recovery examples from fewer human interventions is a substantive alternative to direct operator collection, with no matched customer market price.

DDA: Farboodi, Koh and Xia, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10127 — previous v1 selected HTML mechanisms; production-generated data and future-value accounting already exist there. Do not claim novelty in those principles.
