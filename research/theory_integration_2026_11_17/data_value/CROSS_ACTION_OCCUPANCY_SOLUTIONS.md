2. The inherited queue is a physical conservation state: a calibration that cannot remove queued recovery work cannot free its worker where the old queue remains occupied. Counterexample: G includes a real separately staffed recovery intervention or repairs equipment so the inherited job completes earlier; then F_G can be 1 when W=0, and the model needs a new transition, resource and cost.

A randomized G calibration is not legally implementable unless G is approved and technically possible; this exercise is a decision mathematical comparison, not a trial authorization.
