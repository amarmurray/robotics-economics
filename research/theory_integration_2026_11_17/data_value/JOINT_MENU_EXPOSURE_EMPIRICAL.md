# Identifying matched original-order menu/qualification transport
**Prospective evidence protocol; 20 November 2026 | no physical or customer action authorized**

**Unit and assignment:** one original eligible parent procurement opportunity and original service window, linked to all eligible offered services (including zero purchases), the legal precommitted price/quality/contract menu, controller version, parent budget, competing vendors, original order and refusal, time stamped operator demand, recovered failures, and whole network exposure. For cross-buyer interference, the assignment and analysis unit becomes the *complete shared learner/operator/parent cluster*. Never treat individual customer sites as exchangeable independent samples when they share exposure.

**Observed joint baseline law:** (C0,Q0) must refer to the same actual original buyer and preapproved network/capacity history under a credible legal purchase commitment and **safely** validated second-slot qualification. The original denominator must include declined buyers; conditioning only on training failures or sold orders creates selection. Q requires a jointly deliverable complete accepted-service recipe, not a benchmark or hypothetical body. C must be a paid/enforceable purchase at the offered policy-specific quality and price, not an informal intention.

**Cross-policy support is the indispensable gap.** To use u_C/u_Q one needs a credible upper bound on *same-original-case potential-response disagreement*, not just separate C/Q rates. A possible design, subject to independent authority, is pre-registered randomized whole-network cluster exposure with unchanged original registry, signed controller/manifests, identical buyer menu and auditable held-out qualification. Randomization estimates marginal treatment laws and average policy values; **it does not by itself identify individual cross-world switch fractions**. Obtain those only through independent structural/monotonicity/replay guarantees or use weaker distributional sensitivity bounds with their assumptions recorded. If no valid u_C,u_Q exists, keep G versus X unidentified. Product benchmark transfer scores cannot substitute for a causal budget on policy-induced actual purchase and safe qualification.

**Minimum linked fields:** original parent and eligible divisions; valid menu ID and permitted price/quality; pre- and post-offer parent budget/response and outside option; original no-sales; legally captured payments and refunds; source modality/version/rights and trained policy version; target grounding and body configuration; validated full recovery tail/operator-minute envelope; safe dispatch permissions; original order and controller network; precommitted positivity/assignment; data selection and report time. Stipulate whether a fixed first order H persists after changing offered capacity. Without that, return to full V rather than the two-slot formula.

**Preregistered discrimination:** after admissible estimates of q, baseline d,b,a,t, actual lawful price sets, k and justified u_C/u_Q, compute [L,U], project into full qualified production J, and compare supported intervals across R,X,G,E. Use entire original buyer/market population and cost/outside-option records; do not interpret BLS employment/output or BEA input purchases as robot-compatible service revenue. Audit uncertainty in drift bounds instead of treating them as known. No data, physical or buyer experiment conducted in this tranche.

**Falsification:** observe a verified C1Q1 rate outside the predicted interval in the same predeclared original population, or a changing first-sale/price budget that invalidates the reduction. Either rejects the corresponding transport restriction; it does not establish technical negative transfer in an otherwise optimally optional source-data menu.
