# Prospective signed original-buyer support and report-margin identification protocol

**21 November 2026 — unpopulated; no field permission or signed interventions obtained.** Companion to [the conditional proof](SIGNED_ACTION_MARGIN_SUPPORT_AND_REPORT_VALUE.md). The objective is **economic sign identification for a common physically admissible fine/coarse report experiment**, not forced collection of missing receipts or reclassification of source accuracy as productivity.

## Pre-register the causal object

- Economic unit: cluster of controller version × robot fleet × all shared operators × **original customer offer cohort** × queue/capacity state and temporal window. Predecision fine Y and coarse Z=φ(Y) must be shadow-loggable without affecting task arrival, while action-specific outcomes refer to the *same* original cohort. Do not condition away no-offer/refused/failed service.
- Two or more actual complete policies d: signed embodied recipe, operator/human fallback, safety stop, deadline, resource occupancy, qualified completion, customer acceptance/refund and captured paid receipt. The legal action menu is explicitly versioned at each observed report and resource state. An unsigned action is absent, **not** a missing randomization cell that should be filled unsafely.
- Fixed-learner estimand: L=V(Y)-V(Z), gross of actual observation cost, for common menu and potential outcome kernel. Training X, grounding G-train, embodiment E and changed market/customer exposure are **separate total-policy treatments**; their effects are not report-compression L.
- Report-cell probability p_y: empirical **predecision** frequency for full original offers, with a stated target cohort and observed assignment propensities. Inconclusive/delayed orders remain in the denominator. Version and rights provenance for source family, label eligibility and learner updates is logged separately.

## Minimal prospective data schema

| Object | Required keyed fields | Identifies / cannot identify |
|---|---|---|
| Original buyer opportunity | anonymized cluster/cohort ID, offered price/terms, no-offer/refusal, deadline, workload, accepted/rejected service, refunded and cash-collected amounts | gross contribution and market response at actual original-buyer denominator; not a technical benchmark |
| Predecision report | immutable Y vector, compressed Z mapping, learner/embodiment hash, task/physical/operator state, observation timestamp, missingness/rights | p_y and valid information order; late postfailure observations do not qualify |
| Signed action menu | report-independent and report-conditional approvals, full robot/operator action, recovery/retained R, capacity, cost and duration, forbidden cells | common policy inclusion \(\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A\); no field assignment beyond permission |
| Allocation/propensity | whole-cluster assignment, probability of each signed joint action, contemporaneous shared-customer queue, operator/fleet/network interference boundary | potential m_d(y) only on supported actions and appropriate overlap; per-robot propensity is insufficient if weights/operators shared |
| Quality and economics | joint physical qualification Q, completion, timeout/failure/recovery, human hours, acceptance, refund, original paid receipt, contribution accounting, future data effect counted once | conditional captured contribution; no accuracy-to-cash conversion |
| Data treatment | provenance and rights of field failures, teleoperation, simulation, public/pretrained, synthetic, source labels, filters, family IDs, admitted update counts | mechanistic source-transfer measurement, **not** permission for unplanned training |

Where an original buyer contributes to more than one network cluster, define and pre-register a larger closed interference boundary; don't infer independent customer outcomes or sum scarce operator allocations twice.

## What can be learned safely

For each signed action/report cell, record whether its assignment is **physically approved**, positive-probability **actually allocated**, and linked to complete paid/refunded service. Separate absent legal support from merely unobserved assigned outcomes. Estimate m_d(y) where conditional exchangeability/randomization with actual network-level support is defensible. If unsupported, use physically and financially justified response limits [l_dy,u_dy]; never treat unobserved alternative receipts as zero. Combine them into ℓ_y=l_Ay−u_By and u_y=u_Ay−l_By only if the mean-response identified set is rectangular (or obtain a joint polytope \(\mathcal M\)). Existing observed responses may yield narrower intervals. Record the exact assumptions supporting each endpoint.

A sign-certificate is operationally useful: an original-customer report y1 with ℓ_y1>0 and a report y2 in the **same** coarse cell with u_y2<0 certify strictly positive within-controller fine-report value when both signed actions remain available. Other unsupported report cells need not be filled to certify this lower bound. But no positive program-level value follows if the existing qualified E/R menu dominates, costs exceed the bound, the report arrives too late, or a whole-policy action is unsigned.

A proposed observational resource cost c=1/8 remains within the interval: neither an invest nor a reject conclusion is identified. Merely shrinking y3's unknown effect may tighten the upper end without proving L>c; the current lower bound is also capped by y1's forced positive mass. Calculate how each permissible cell-specific bound would alter (2)–(3) *before* requesting scarce operator time. If a cell is unsigned, leave its result as a bound. ## Whole-network experimental and falsification design

1. In shadow mode, record fine reports Y, coarse Z, original customer opportunities and signed action feasibility without changing dispatch. Document delayed reports, safety overrides and customer selection.
2. If separately authorized, randomize **whole-network** allocation among already approved, capacity-feasible actions at registered report/context cells and registered learner/robot versions; preserve fallback and buyer rights. Never direct this research to run a robot trial. If assignment is impossible, report identification bounds.
3. Estimate qualified **and** accepted paid outcomes conditional on Y and cluster, not only technical success. Use a held-out original customer cohort to test whether estimated A/B action-margin signs transport; include refund/cancellation and all natural no-service opportunities.
4. Negative control: a signal that improves target-task classifier accuracy but fails to alter any signed optimal action should have zero fixed-controller economic information value. Positive diagnostic: target physical/operator observation unrelated to task label but changing qualified original-customer action margins may be valuable.
5. Mechanism test requires a *different* whole-learner comparison: randomized source archive inclusion vs target grounding/training vs new embodiment where independently signed, with episode lineage, admission/update budget, rights, inter-robot shared-weight interference and matched customer service. Do not use the report-compression estimator for changing the learner.
6. Check across customers/end-markets whether ordering persists once demand, competing provider offerings, operator capacity, and network spillovers alter the optimized policy. Use the existing Model A LP shadow certificates only on audited active bases.
