# Instructor solutions — original-buyer offer support
**Separate from [student questions](ORIGINAL_BUYER_OFFER_EXERCISES.md).**

1. w_p≥L−d and w_p<U+d. The threshold acceptance convention is w_p≥t; thus t≤L−d guarantees acceptance, while t≥U+d guarantees w_p<t, hence rejection. Interior t admits values on both sides when the support interval is nondegenerate and endpoints accessible; always check empty/degenerated intervals.
2. Initial intervals w_A,w_B∈[5,9), policy drift≤1 and t=7 make each acceptance ambiguous. R=6; X=5+5a_A∈[5,10]; G=5+5a_B∈[5,10]; E=−1+5(a_A+a_B)∈[−1,9]. Worlds (8,5) and (5,8) with zero shift agree on all initial tests and reverse (X,G)=(10,5) versus(5,10). The restricted pure maximin chooses R=6, as X/G worst5 and E worst−1.
3. A∈[8,9) with drift≤1 implies w_A^X≥7. B∈[5,6) with drift≤1 implies w_B^G<7. Values: X10, G5, E4, R6. A marketing survey without an enforceable, accepted-service-standard offer is not a valid threshold observation and may confound quality/buyer composition.
4. L(x,λ)=Σc_ix_i+λ·(b−Ax), λ≥0. Max over x_i∈[0,1] independently gives λ·b+Σmax(0,c_i−λ·a_i), so infλ is a weak-duality upper bound on any feasible integer complete-order plan. Fractional splitting can violate indivisibility, resource/setup and joint acceptance constraints; obtain equality only with proven integrality/exactness.
5. At the two equally likely swap worlds, expected X or G = (10+5)/2=15/2, E=4, R=6. A perfect two-state report selects the accepted branch and yields 10; EVPI=5/2. This ignores audit cash, lost qualified production, right to use data, report correctness, shifting quality and market distribution; it cannot be quoted as the value of collected trajectories.
6. With only one qualified unit of robotic capacity but unrestricted eligible outside-buyer captured payment M, choose M above any finite proposed market bound; earlier R observations remain identical. A uniform provider receipt cap for **all potentially eligible orders** under the intervention, together with actual resource and rights coverage, restores a finite upper bound; alternatively prove market closure and a finite price cap.
