# Student exercises — conditional economic transfer (questions only)

1. Start with Q,D,J conditional transition kernels for source P and grounding G and state-dependent total-variation component caps alpha,beta,gamma. Prove the product upper bound for full transition total variation without imposing Q/D/J independence. State exactly which conditional kernels must be observable or externally certified.
2. A productive-source program earns current paid service but a grounding intervention does not. Derive the multi-date upper bound on their net paid contribution difference with separately accounted current receipts, variable customer value, real calibration costs and the available old-policy fallback.
3. Construct two fully physical common-noise potential-history laws with identical entire P and G *within-arm* distributions, but different Pr(Y_P != Y_G). Explain why a maximal-coupling TV calculation does not identify the unit-level cross-arm disagreement epsilon.
4. Prove the one-worker queue recursion Lipschitz bound. State which additional conditions permit a queue-distance bound to be converted into a paid-worker-free probability bound, and give a counterexample for continuous recovery durations near zero.
5. Suppose G demand has no action support and conditional beta=1, while Q and incident mechanisms are perfectly certified. Explain why the generic product certificate cannot prove G dominance or infer G's customer value. Design an admissible observable original-buyer statistic that would be sufficient *if* a lawful G trial were genuinely supported.
