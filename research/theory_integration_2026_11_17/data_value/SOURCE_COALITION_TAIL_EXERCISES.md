# Exercises: source-coalition tails and whole-market transport

1. For source types F,T,M, derive the exact third-order inclusion-exclusion expression for D(FTM), in terms of the empty, singleton and pair program contrasts. State when it is directly identified.
2. In normalized paid units, both SG and E have J(empty)=2, J(single)=2.1, J(pair)=2.5. If all sources are free optional and gross J is bounded above by 3.5, derive a valid SG-E interval for the triple source mixture. Is pairwise interaction information alone decisive?
3. Compute the third-order dividend of a paid service with technical score q(A)=|A| and payment 8 exactly when q reaches 3; compare with J(A)=min(|A|,2). What falsifies technical-pairwise transport?
4. Prove that if paid production is a sum of buyer-specific closed responses f_i(A intersect H_i) with |H_i|<=2 and source costs of order<=2, then all third- and higher-order dividends vanish. Provide a reason this may fail for a shared foundation learner or operator.
5. Buyers of classes c1,c2,c3 have whole-paid SG-E effects (3,0,-3). Compare island mixture (1/3,1/3,1/3) with target buyer composition (1/2,1/3,1/6). Explain why naive island averaging gives the wrong target choice, and when reweighting is itself invalid.
6. Design the least presumptive decision experiment if authorized whole pooled SG and E interventions exist. How does the evidence obligation change if the triple-source E policy is physically disallowed?
