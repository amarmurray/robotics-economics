# Student exercises — buyer choice, data transfer and qualified production

**Work independently before reading [solutions](JOINT_BUYER_PRICED_MENUS_SOLUTIONS.md).**

1. **Substitute sales.** One original procurement unit values two complete robotic services each at8 and can buy at most one. Both are offered at7, variable delivery cost0. Calculate revenues when each is offered separately and when both are offered together. What field records distinguish independent original orders from this procurement group?

2. **A menu-robust qualification certificate.** Let a buyer have w_A∈[8,9), w_B∈[5,6). Qualified source-X A has unit delivery cost1 and paid K_X2; target-grounded B has delivery cost0 and K_G1. Offers may only be 5,6,7,8 and each policy can offer its one service. Compute max_(price) min_(w) net contribution. Compare with retained R3. Does independence between w_A and w_B matter?

3. **Buyer-aware dual.** One original buyer could choose A or B, each worth7 to the provider, requiring one robot-hour. There are two physical robot-hours. Use a buyer-exclusivity relaxation and compute an upper bound. Repeat for two distinct original buyers with only one robot-hour.

4. **Endogenous price decision.** One buyer values A8, B6; X qualifies A at delivery cost3 and investment2, G qualifies B at delivery cost0 and investment1, E offers both at investment3. Each can choose a price from 5,6,7,8. Compare policy values at common price7 and after precommitted price selection. Why is w not revenue?

5. **Model objection.** Derive choice shares for an outside option and two equally attractive alternatives under an iid-Gumbel multinomial logit. Explain why a strictly positive interval margin ceases to guarantee actual purchase under unbounded noise. Specify the minimum original-customer evidence for a valid financial bound.

6. **Critical proof audit.** The operator observes all R customer purchases but cannot bound off-register source-induced customer prices. Construct observationally equivalent worlds invalidating any finite X revenue upper limit. Explain why a free optional dataset may still not lower optimized V, while forced shared-controller retraining can.
