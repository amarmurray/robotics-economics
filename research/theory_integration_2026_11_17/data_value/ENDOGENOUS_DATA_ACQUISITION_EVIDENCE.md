# Prospective original-buyer evidence: productive acquisition and deliberate learning

**22 November 2026 — study design only.** No real original-buyer receipts, lawfully signed P/D/SG/E arms, private robot logs, external outreach or paid interventions were acquired.

**Unit and clocks.** Start with a pre-assignment original *eligible offered* customer (including no-orders) and all affected robot/operator-site links. Timestamp eligibility, order/quote and pre-action demand, assignment to production versus data acquisition, robot/operator resource reservation, episode success/failure/recovery and teleoperator intervention, rights creation/revocation, licensed source mix/curation, actual learner checkpoint, qualification test, timely controller receipt of report, dispatch, acceptance/refund, final settled payment and subsequent original-buyer orders. An experiment that forgoes an eligible paying service must retain that opportunity in the denominator.

**Distinct data-source and legal lineage.** Separately classify field paid success; field failure/recovery; deliberate teleoperation demonstrations; licensed simulation; generated synthetic; and public/pretrained sources. Record source-to-target direction, checkpoint and robot body, curation/compute/operator/robot opportunity use, and rights **to record, train, pool, transfer, disclose, receive and use at dispatch**. A legal training input can be an illegal dispatch report. Data created after today's dispatch may qualify tomorrow but not retroactively today. Forced updating is not optional free raw-data access.

**Discriminating causal requirements.** Matched original offered-buyer policy assignment (only if authorized), lawful action menus and complete accepted-and-finally-paid Q including failure, rescue, terminal settlement, future newly qualified service, direct and indirect operator capacity, and demand feedback. Handle sharing/interference as whole-network treatment, not independent buyer/site outcomes. When outcomes are not supported, retain a joint feasible set of whole-program paid K values and state investment ranking **unidentified**, rather than imputing benchmark percentages or zero for forbidden actions.

**Core empirical contrasts.** Compare technically more accurate D data against P's production opportunity after *real* time and money costs; isolate target report rights from target skill grounding; test whether source qualifies a missing original customer recipe; compare E as a separate physical capacity choice; examine whether failure/recovery trajectories improve future accepted-and-cleared service at the expense of current paid throughput. Preserve demand no-orders and report-use rights.

**Missing:** all actual approved P/D/SG/E buyer arms, service settlements, learned network K, legal dispatch signal matrix and official numerical BEA/BLS interindustry requirements. The separate BLS 2025 sector output file is context, not an I-O technology coefficient.
