# Proof ledger — joint buyer choice and priced menus

**19 Nov 2026.** All propositions conditional and elementary; no scientific-novelty assertion. Companion: [joint buyer chapter](JOINT_BUYER_PRICED_MENUS.md).

**P1: robust unique-choice margin.** Fix a legally deliverable menu m and original buyer i. Suppose l_ij≤w_ij≤h_ij simultaneously for all buyer alternatives and admissible policy states. If l_ij−t_ij>max(0,max_(k≠j)(h_ik−t_ik)), then realized u_ij>0 and u_ij>u_ik for every other offered k, so buyer i uniquely selects j. Strictness makes the result independent of tie conventions. Conversely, selection of j requires w_ij−t_ij≥0 and ≥w_ik−t_ik for each k, hence h_ij−t_ij≥0 and ≥l_ik−t_ik. The conditions for possibility are necessary but may not be sufficient when quality/preference states are coupled or a candidate endpoint is not attained. It would be incorrect to call every candidate set jointly sharp.

**P2: choice/resource upper dual.** Fix m. For every original unit i define P_i containing every possibly chosen service. All feasible physically delivered complete-order assignments are feasible in the relaxation x_ij≥0, Σ_j x_ij≤1, Σ_ij a_ij x_ij≤b, and x_ij=0 for j outside P_i. Let c_ij≤cbar_ij and a_ij be resource minima across the covered state, and b a resource capacity upper envelope. For any λ≥0, add λ(b−Σa x) to the maximand. Maximization separately over each i's simplex yields max{0,max_j(cbar_ij−λa_ij)}. Weak duality yields the stated infimum upper bound. The assignment relaxation can be fractional and the candidate sets not jointly realizable, so this **need not be sharp**. Multiple resources replace λa by λ·a; complete bundles use one column per entire service. The observed market must be **closed** to eligible off-register purchasing or separately capped.

**P3: constructive lower floor and robust price timing.** Take one legally admissible precommitted price menu and an assignment of strictly guaranteed choices whose full accepted-service operator/robot resources are reserved for every covered joint demand state; ensure all other buyers cannot trigger unmodeled compulsory costs or displace reserved service. Each guaranteed pair is purchased, delivered, and collected at at least its stated nonnegative contribution. The sum minus K_p is therefore a lower bound for that menu's inf_theta value and hence for sup_m inf_theta. It is **not** valid for non-isolated shared controller recovery or obligations to serve all unforeseen acceptances. For any payoff function g(m,theta), sup_m inf_theta g ≤ inf_theta sup_m g; this follows because inf_theta g(m,theta)≤g(m,theta)≤sup_m g(m,theta) for every m and theta. Thus a price chosen with knowledge of latent customer state is an optimistic oracle, not a robust implementation.

**P4: double-counting counterexample.** One procurement unit can choose only one of two service alternatives; valuations8/8, prices7/7, outside0, both delivered separately if offered alone. Isolated offer receipts are7+7, but any simultaneous menu yields at most7. For two distinct buyers with one physical slot, x_1+x_2≤1 similarly caps7. The buyer-aware dual gives7 in the first case even with physical b=2; the physical dual gives7 in the latter case. For distinct buyers, demand and capacity restrictions are different economic mechanisms; do not conflate them.

**P5: price ranking reversal / support equivalence.** With single buyer values (8,6), cost(A,B)=(3,0), (K_X,K_G,K_E)=(2,1,3), ex-ante price choices {5,6,7,8}, and separately qualified X:{A}, G:{B}, E:{A,B}, compute per menu t−k−K if the service wins buyer choice, else −K. Under price7, (X,G,E)=(2,−1,1). Optimize over the finite grid: X=3 at tA8; G=5 at tB6; E=3 at B6, before R=2. This is a **price-selection** change, not claimed changed technical competence. Separate worlds (8,5) and (5,8) both obey original accepted5/refused9 quote bands; with cost A1/B0, KX2/KG1, their fully informed optimized (X,G) values are (5,4) and (2,7). Incumbent offer-only records cannot rank the pointwise optimized investments. These world-specific optima presume states can be learned before pricing; the maximin ex-ante policy may be different.

**P6: robust interval numerical certificate.** For wA∈[8,9),wB∈[5,6), costs (1,0), K_X2,K_G1,K_E5 and R3, the safe X menu tA8 yields 8−1−2=5 in all states. No X menu can yield more because A never pays price9 and every allowed price≤8. Similarly G menu tB5 guarantees 5−0−1=4 and raising to6 loses the sale in some admissible states; E cannot exceed2 since its highest attainable A contribution is 8−1−5=2 and B is no larger. Thus R3<X5, and X is uniformly robust best even if valuations are arbitrarily correlated within the support. The stored grid checks four half-unit support points, but this proof uses the full stated continuous intervals and discrete price grid. With forced shared-model adaptation invalidating R, discard R rather than taking free fallback.

**P7: stochastic-choice robustness failure.** In a one-buyer multinomial logit with outside and two products at deterministic utility 0, each alone is chosen with probability exp(0)/(1+exp(0))=1/2, whereas with both offered each product has probability 1/(1+1+1)=1/3. Summing separate receipts treats two disjoint assortments as simultaneous. Logit errors have unbounded support, hence a finite deterministic strict utility margin does not give sure sale. Replace such floor with a probability-aware bound derived from original no-purchase and assortment exposure, not just accepted sales.

**P8: off-register and feedback obstruction.** Fix any data on retained original buyers and physical robot hours. Introduce under X/G an eligible off-register buyer who buys one fully qualified service with captured payment M, while all R observations remain fixed. If M has no independently supported upper cap, take M→∞. No finite revenue upper certificate exists. Conversely if a supposedly retained R plan is physically displaced by a shared-model update or changed operator recovery, the monotonic free-option lemma does not apply. Both obstructions persist with perfect arithmetic and zero benchmark measurement error.

Rejections: (1) separate acceptance at t for substitutable service classes implies simultaneous sales; (2) incumbent quality and price anchors automatically transport to policy p; (3) optimally chosen price can be used when the state is learned only after commitment; (4) maximizing an LP relaxation establishes discrete feasible order fulfillment; (5) finite robot count alone caps provider receipts; (6) a static buyer-choice certificate establishes network-wide learning/dispatch closure. Closest prior art Talluri–van Ryzin 2004 and established LP/robust decision foundations; contribution originality unverified.
