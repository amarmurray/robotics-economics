# Instructor solutions — observation-constrained production

1. At t=0 every possible state must already be terminal. For t>0 a common legal old action across all indistinguishable states and successful continuation after every observed successor is necessary and sufficient by induction. alpha requires ra and beta requires rb, and either wrong choice can fault; no shared action certifies B={alpha,beta} though each singleton is winnable.
2. The refined controller can simulate the old coarse decision by discarding the extra observation, conditional on unchanged actions, clocks and resources. A nonzero-confusion report still supports both hidden states; sensor latency, legality or changed operator costs also defeat the no-cost-refinement premise.
3. The interaction is 6-4-4+4=2. If the improved complete service has no eligible purchasing demand, its optimized extra original-order production can be zero even with unchanged technical transition laws. Optional G's unavailable sales do not create a data premium. If G is forced and costly, net value may fall.
4. The upper p estimate is 1-0.05^(1/299)=0.00996914679, not zero. Every p>0 can generate n zero-fault observations with positive probability; model misspecification and distribution shift worsen the problem.
