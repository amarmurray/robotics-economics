# Exercises — qualification-weighted source dispatch (student edition)

These exercises use only the conditional synthetic model in [the chapter](QUALIFICATION_WEIGHTED_DISPATCH_AND_SOURCE_VALUE.md); no field economics or hardware permission is implied.

**1. Derive the production-optimal rule.** Assume θ∈{0,1} is equiprobable, three S_i=θ⊕E_i, each error pattern with k wrong has probability g_k. A is the signed whole service for θ=0, B for θ=1. If correctly selected A yields incremental accepted paid service with probability 1/4, B with 3/4, and a wrong selection yields zero, show the criterion at any report s with k ones. Identify when a k=1 minority choice is optimal.

**2. Sharp partial identification.** Given g_0=7/16−t, g_1=1/8+t, g_2=1/16−t, g_3=t and 0≤t≤1/16, compute q_X(t). Prove the sharp minimum and maximum using all admissible t, and contrast with majority technical prediction accuracy.

**3. Same actual investment menu.** With baseline R=2, X and G complete-program costs 1/4, successful signed additional service 10, G's report accuracy 7/8 and r_A=1/4, r_B=3/4, calculate J_X−J_G bounds and compare to the stipulated feasible E net 10. May a perfect t audit change the full investment choice?

**4. Audit timing and optionality.** If t is 0 or 1/16 equiprobably, calculate the gross value of a perfect t audit *after irrevocably choosing X but before setting the dispatch rule*. Explain why that value cannot be added to the return of E. Is moving from t=0 to t=1/64 a counterexample to monotonic value of optional free information?

**5. Real identification.** Design the smallest truthful evidence registry needed to distinguish positive source-transfer effects from high posterior score, physical qualification, customer acceptance, paid production and report-dependent dispatch. Which unsafe/missing support cells cannot be recovered just by inverse-propensity weighting?
