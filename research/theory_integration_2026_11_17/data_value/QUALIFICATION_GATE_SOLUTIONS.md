# Instructor solutions: source information and qualified economic value

Separate [exercises](QUALIFICATION_GATE_EXERCISES.md).

1. Pr(accepted complete service)=p r^++(1−p)r^-. Hence J=2+10Pr(acceptance)−1/4=7/4+10[p r^++(1−p)r^-]. p is only source/grounding inference.
2. J_X=7/4+10r(13/16+2t), J_G=7/4+35r/4. Difference 10r(2t−1/16), switch t=1/32 when r>0. At r=4/5, X_high=37/4, G=35/4 but independently qualified E=10 dominates.
3. Ex ante X average equals G. The audit value is [max(X_high,E)−max(G,E)]/2. X_high crosses E at r=22/25, G crosses E at r=33/35, giving 0 / (75r−66)/16 / 5r/16 on consecutive ranges. At r=4/5:0; 9/10:3/32; 1:5/16. It is a difference in maximized production values, not a second data premium.
4. At high t, X=7/4+10*(15/16)*(1/2)=103/16 while G=7/4+10*(7/8)=21/2 and E=10. G wins despite less accurate signal; original-customer paid and fully qualified acceptance conditionals distinguish the worlds.
5. For each arm, lower J=7/4+10[pℓ^++(1−p)ℓ^-], upper J=7/4+10[p u^++(1−p)u^-]; Bernoulli completions attain endpoints under rectangular assumptions. X−G bounds use lower X−upper G and reverse. With shared potential outcomes/action/learner interference, a joint feasibility set may shrink the possibilities.
6. Bounded-function TV gives each arm |J_customer−J_shadow|≤10ε, hence a shadow advantage >10(ε_X+ε_G) certifies positive X−G, conditional on fixed signed policies, same costs and the true ε bounds. A benchmark score change says nothing about full original-policy distribution shift or legal/safety permission.
