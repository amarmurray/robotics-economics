**20 November 2026. Public code and publisher records only, no dataset download or model training.** Original publisher https://rail-berkeley.github.io/bridgedata/ says CC BY 4.0 data, 60,096 total (50,365 teleop/9,731 scripted), 24 environments/13 skills; code MIT separately. PMLR CoRL landing-page abstract https://proceedings.mlr.press/v229/walke23a.html says 53,896 (unreconciled difference 6,200). TFDS official catalog https://github.com/tensorflow/datasets/blob/master/docs/catalog/bridge.md gives processed train 25,460 and test 3,475 (total 28,935); not equivalent to raw publisher episode totals without archive/version/transformation reconciliation.

Prospective, **unpopulated** manifest: archive and episode SHA/UID, publisher release, source generator, rights/consent, scene/task/time, camera/action and robot versions, original language presence/semantic validity, preprocessor output, deterministic scene- and learner-cluster-disjoint holdout, per-source proposed and admitted transition counts, compute/seed/learner graph, failed updates and recovery, heldout task pair qualification, grounding report before/after hardware lock, signed X/G/E/R complete controller/dispatcher/operator policy, original buyer offer/refusal/unserved/accepted/completed/paid receipt and resource expense. Don't fill gaps from raw counts.

Prospective test: if separately authorized and physically feasible, predeclare a 2×2 complete fixed-S inclusion (yes/no) × conditioning (goal-image/language) comparison, randomize entire shared-update clusters with positive full-policy probability, freeze unchanged heldout target/scene and development failure accounting, log source retention. A label-arm with zero admitted S exposure cannot identify a counterfactual equal-effective-share inclusion effect. A separate source-**generation** trial requires mutually feasible script/teleop collectors at a common source context, not archive labels. A real original-buyer production valuation requires signed whole robot-policy certification and actual complete accepted/paid service; offline training percentages are not currency. Nov22 scientific gate OPEN; no actual field evidence, BEA numerical production crosswalk, full repository CI or independent novelty.
