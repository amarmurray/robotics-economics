# Decision-specific joint recipe audit for marginal robotic capital
25 November 2026 Eastern · Continuation of [multi-resource MPK](MULTI_RESOURCE_MPK_PORTFOLIO.md), [resource incidence transport](RESOURCE_INCIDENCE_TRANSPORT.md) and the common Model A/B. No new standalone paper.

## Economic priors: why a recipe is the unit of the audit

The embodied fleet does not directly produce a scalar benchmark score. It supplies dated physical and organizational resources that may be combined to perform a customer's accepted service. Data capital changes which combinations are lawful and qualified, their resource use, and later sources of experience. A mobile robot, a manipulation arm and an inspection worker can be complements on one service and substitutes through an alternate recipe on another. A customer's mutually exclusive alternative recipes are options, not separately payable orders. The economic productivity of one more body therefore depends on the *joint* incidence and contribution of those options, not just mean utilization or the incidence of one input measured independently. This chapter asks which observations suffice to decide a given capacity portfolio rather than requiring a census of all robot behaviors. The optimization technology is classical LP decomposition/Lagrangian pricing; the application is a qualified production/data decision.

## 1. Price the residual opportunity, not every modality

Fix the original customer window, checkpoint/embodiment permissions, predispatch information, net contribution accounting and actual dated resources. Let i index mutually exclusive original-order groups, q_i their nonnegative demand quotas, R_i their full set of qualified, observable, authorized alternate recipes, each with joint incidence vector a_ir>=0 and captured net contribution v_ir. Include a no-service option of zero. A supported feasible subset S_i may be much smaller than R_i. Extra parent procurement, timing or cross-group constraints must remain in the master resources or in a valid relaxation, never counted twice. With divisible recipes, F(b)=max sum v_ir x_ir subject to sum a_ir x_ir<=b and sum_r x_ir<=q_i, x>=0. For indivisible orders the same certificate is an upper bound rather than automatic equality.

For a nonnegative candidate resource-price vector p define the local support statistic
    h_i(p)=max(0, max_(r in R_i) [v_ir-p'a_ir]).
For the observed subset define h_i^S analogously. Let e_i(p)>=h_i(p)-h_i^S(p)>=0 be an independently justified upper bound on the unobserved opportunity residual (if no bounded residual or complete safe menu exists, no certificate is claimed).

**Decision certificate.** For any such p,
    F(b)<=U(b,p)=p'b+sum_i q_i[h_i^S(p)+e_i(p)].
Proof: v_ir<=p'a_ir+h_i(p); multiply by x_ir, sum and apply both sets of constraints. This is ordinary weak duality with the order-price component eliminated analytically. In the finite divisible case with complete R_i and bounded feasible objective, minimizing the exact U over p>=0 recovers F by LP strong duality: at each p the cheapest feasible order quota price equals h_i(p). For integers this minimization may retain a relaxation gap. A verified feasible schedule gives L(b); no assertion that a finite sample proves e=0.

For portfolio d at fixed menu and exposure, evaluate independent feasible witnesses L0,L1 and possibly different certified prices p0,p1 at b and b+d. Then
    L1-U(b,p0) <= F(b+d)-F(b) <= U(b+d,p1)-L0.
Subtract incremental cost once. If both gaps U-L are zero, the marginal return is exactly identified in that restriction. If not, stop auditing when the resulting interval excludes the decision threshold, not when every recipe has been reconstructed. This supplies a sufficient decision-specific audit; it is not a universal minimum statistic or sharp interval for all partially known menus. If the baseline schedule is exact, a single endpoint certificate and feasible witness suffice. Upper planes can be tightened by alternative prices or valid jointly supported constraints. If original orders are latent at dispatch, the relaxation is an oracle bound, not a feasible lower witness. Unobserved prices are shadow certificates, not actual cash costs. A reported residual must cover every lawful recipe in the declared original order group; checking only attempted or sampled ones supplies a lower bound on h, not an upper bound.

The derivation is a special case of the earlier structural Lagrangian certificate, exploiting separable order exclusivity. Dantzig and Wolfe, *Decomposition Principle for Linear Programs*, Operations Research 8(1), 101–111 (1960), DOI https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.8.1.101 (publisher abstract inspected 25 November 2026), already develops the coordinating-price/subproblem structure and its decentralized firm interpretation. We claim neither the pricing algorithm nor the duality as novel. The candidate economic synthesis is choosing which joint technical evidence to collect using a capital decision's economically weighted gap, before evaluating the independently evolving learning program.

## 2. Why independently measured recipe coefficients cannot certify the portfolio

Continue the previous two-order example. A pays 6 and uses one r, one s and one inspection slot. B pays 4 under either of its alternate recipes. Capacities b=(1,1,1), optional portfolio d=(1,1,0). Two possible joint menus:
| B alternatives | r | s | inspection |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P, cheap body |1|1|1|
| P, expensive body |2|2|0|
| G, cheap body |1|1|0|
| G, expensive body |2|2|1|

All individual resource-coordinate multisets for B are identical. Both menus have same optimal original output and singleton increments (6 and zero), even in the divisible model. Both can serve B as an alternative to A. In the P world at expanded capacity A plus half the expensive B recipe earns 8; in G A plus the cheap B earns 10. The divisible optima are certified, not approximations: at original capacity p=(6,0,0) yields U=6 in either world; at expanded capacity p=(2,0,4), h_A=0, h_B=0 in P and h_B=2 in G, giving U=8 or 10. Feasible schedules meet both upper bounds. With a portfolio cost of 3, the net return is -1 versus +1. For indivisible B, P can earn only 6, G still 10; the P divisible upper bound 8 is valid but not exact. This is the same capital complementarity mechanism as before, now with identical marginal resource incidence summaries. Measuring the joint net residual at the two certificate prices, plus feasible witnesses, resolves this specific ambiguity. If a positive residual is all that is known, a feasible schedule still has to establish attainability; a positive local residual alone is not a positive realized portfolio return. The two menus are observational counterworlds, not a claim that G dominates P casewise; if grounding is optional and the old recipe is retained, include it and re-evaluate the union. In this illustration that union still earns 10 at b+d.

## 3. Direct capacity and induced learning are different interventions

Write W(k,a) for the expected discounted net contribution of a *specified lawful policy program*, including acquisition costs and terminal continuation once. k is the installed capital portfolio, a=0 denotes a predeclared common exposure/replay intervention (same permitted training histories, not destruction of required operational/safety records), a=1 denotes independently evolving retained-source training under that capital policy. The four cells can each have a distinct dispatch policy within stated information and budgets; for an optimized estimand require a feasible-policy witness and a valid program-wide upper, rather than optimizing on one realized tape. Define
    Delta_fixed=W(k+d,0)-W(k,0);
    Delta_evolve=W(k+d,1)-W(k,1);
    I=Delta_evolve-Delta_fixed.
The identity Delta_evolve=Delta_fixed+I is exact for the four defined policy programs. I is a controlled *interaction* with the specified learning regime, not a nonparametrically identified natural indirect effect of data. It may include learner compute cost, demand change, altered policy, later qualifications and other consequences allowed by the treatment. To interpret I as a learning-mediated return, define and justify exclusion of all changes other than lawful retained exposure and its downstream causal effects, comparable rights/budgets, common replay support, no shared learner contamination, and feasible intervention on each arm. If replay constrains allowable actions or does not retain the same safety policy, this is a different diagnostic. If one cell cannot be implemented lawfully, report partial ID or nonidentification rather than assume away the missing arm. For interval endpoints [L_ka,U_ka], take the signed sum of lower/upper endpoints to bound I (not generally sharp). No extra data premium may be added to Delta_evolve. A static residual audit is insufficient for counterfactual future states; extend the conditional bound to all reachable histories and use the existing Bellman residual certificate.

## 4. Concrete empirical design and bounded source review

For each fixed-exposure original opportunity window record (i) original order/alternative group, accepted service and actual captured margin, demand quota/deadline; (ii) controller/body versions and qualification/rights observed before dispatch; (iii) joint dated robot/operator/QC/compute/resource vector for each audited recipe, feasible schedule and actual resource reservations; (iv) certificate p, covered recipe subset, separately justified per-group upper bound on missing residual, and feasible witness. Store p before accessing outcome data or separate a held-out audit; audit selection must not masquerade as complete support. Record original refusals and unserved opportunities. The certificate can use signed independent engineering caps on attainable contribution and resource use, not merely performance averages. Price the relevant resource bundle on the same recipe and time window. Report where caps are unsupported. This is the smallest evidence package proposed for the present decision, not a claim of universal sample minimality.

For the independently evolving P/X/G/C/E comparisons additionally link each original and induced order to field trajectories, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, pretrained/public sources, lawful retention, curation/training cost, subsequent held-out joint recipe qualification and resulting accepted settlements. Distinguish a common-history capacity audit from the post-treatment selected sample of extra robot work. Use independent cluster assignment where approved, not individual robots sharing one learner or operator. Evaluate the four cells for a capacity contrast within each feasible learning program. If cheaper C or routine P supplies the same joint opportunity residual, feasible portfolio output and downstream value at lower costs, a G premium is unsupported. A positive engineering return can still fail the full economic contrast. No physical experiment is proposed as authorized here.
