# Proof ledger — shadow learning, B retention and target support

**18 November 2026. Every new result is conditional or a synthetic counterexample.**

1. **Definition and scope.** A complete accepted customer order is the economic measurement unit. F is a retained approved physical policy; H copies already generated F data and trains A offline. The same production value V, scarce resources and persistent regime θ apply. Never add an informational premium to the future production continuation.
2. **Pre-admission B closure.** With identical coupled exogenous disturbances, equal initial B state and no H-sensitive parent of any B transition/output/demand/queue node, induction gives identical B paths for all t<τ. This is **not** a B guarantee after A admission; nor does controller weight pinning imply absence of operator, compute, market or rights pathways.
3. **Optional data monotonicity.** If feasible policies, training recipes and resources for D are a subset of those for D∪{d}, the supremum over the latter weakly dominates. Counterexamples from mandatory checkpoint overwrites or sunk curation do not refute this under unchanged feasibility. Original policies must genuinely remain accessible.
4. **Observational equivalence.** Two model worlds agree on all F traces with only B action 0 and no A target action 1. They assign different accepted A service outcomes to action 1 (0 versus 2 normalized units), leaving F likelihood identical. Thus the A value is not point-identified by F. A post hoc simulator score is not a substitute for validated target-action invariance.
5. **Target-support interval.** Partition *pre-treatment* demand μ into identified cells C and unsupported U, and assume additive expected whole-customer incremental contribution by cell bounded [0,M(x)] and no shared unpriced resource interaction. Integration gives L=∫C m dμ and U=L+∫U M dμ. Under an unrestricted unsupported conditional mean, both endpoints are attained by setting 0 and M on U, proving sharpness in that identified set. For nonadditive bundles/capacity, restrict to the actual joint feasible set; the simple interval may not remain sharp. Hard safety is an eligibility condition, not a probabilistic contribution.
6. **Synthetic target mixture exact arithmetic.** L share 3/5 and accepted incremental mean 1/2 yields 3/10. H share 2/5 with incremental mean x in [0,2] contributes 2x/5. Cost 1/4: net=1/20+2x/5, ranging [1/20,17/20]. S net=2/5. Equality x=7/8; x above this favors shadow admission and below favors S, *if both physically approved*. No observed x, θ or robot sample enters these numbers.
7. **Two-date joint A qualification.** Essential qualification events p1=1/2 and p2=3/4 yield joint q∈[max(0,p1+p2−1),min(p1,p2)]=[1/4,1/2]. Full joint 2×2 tables exist at all q in the interval: P11=q, P10=p1−q, P01=p2−q, P00=1−p1−p2+q. At two remaining periods with accepted incremental value 1 each and training/validation cost 1/4, net=2q−1/4 ∈[1/4,3/4]. Conditional independence gives q=3/8 and net=1/2. Without a remaining customer window the forced return is −1/4, but optional F yields incremental 0.
8. **No purely informational EVSI from width.** The two worlds can agree in logged observables but have opposite optimal decisions versus S. Without a prior or decision objective, no probability-weighted value of a target diagnostic is identified. A grounding intervention can change A's actual q and service, so it is not pure information acquisition.
