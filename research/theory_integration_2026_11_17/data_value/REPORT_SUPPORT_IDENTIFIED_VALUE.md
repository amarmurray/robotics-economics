# Partial target report support and qualified customer production

**19 November 2026.** Continuation of *When Capabilities Become Production* and [signal-kernel transport](SIGNAL_KERNEL_TRANSPORT.md). **Result type:** finite standard decision-theory derivation; no real robotics/economic measurement, safety approval or originality claim.

## The same production environment with less assumed target support

Fix one independently qualified **whole-network** controller, operator/dispatch resources, specialist/legacy fallback, set of feasible complete customer-service assignments \(A\), and original pre-action customer-opportunity states \(z\in Z\) with distribution \(p(z)\). The nonactuating report \(s\in S\) has target conditional law \(K(s\mid z)\). An eligible decision rule \(\pi(a\mid s)\) chooses among already approved complete assignments, including retaining an old policy. Let \(y(a,z)\) be complete network contribution: accepted and displaced original orders, true customer participation, provider capture, operator/physical use and outside-service costs, not a classifier benchmark. The operative economic value is \(V(\pi,K)=\sum_zp(z)\sum_sK(s\mid z)\sum_a\pi(a\mid s)y(a,z)\), part of the existing production function and \(J\), not an extra data premium.

Suppose **target shadow validation** identifies \(K(\cdot\mid z)\) only for pre-action strata \(z\in H\). Every other row is an unrestricted simplex. This is genuine absence of source-to-target support, **not** a measured failure. Different field/autonomous recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained datasets can affect technical learning or report quality but do not certify these missing commercial target rows by volume alone.

## Sharp lower envelope and population-row certificate

For fixed possibly *randomized* eligible policy write \(y_\pi(s,z)=\sum_a\pi(a\mid s)y(a,z)\). Under **rectangular unknown rows**, its worst-case target contribution is exactly

\[
L_H(\pi)=\sum_{z\in H}p(z)\sum_s K(s\mid z)y_\pi(s,z)+\sum_{z\notin H}p(z)\min_s y_\pi(s,z).
\]

Indeed each unidentified row is linear on its own probability simplex and can independently concentrate on a worst report. The target-support robust value is \(\underline W_H=\max_{\pi}L_H(\pi)\), allowing stochastic rules (pure-rule enumeration alone does not establish a general maximin). If coupled structural constraints exclude simultaneous worst rows, the expression remains conservative but need not be sharp.

If an independently correct target shadow audit **exactly certifies** one old missing row \(z_*\), and fixes the same p, y, action/menu and reporter semantics, then
\[
0\le\underline W_{H\cup\{z_*\}}-\underline W_H
\le p(z_*)R(z_*),\quad R(z)=\max_{a\in A}y(a,z)-\min_{a\in A}y(a,z).
\]
The true row restricts the ambiguity set; the increase for a fixed rule cannot exceed the report-conditioned whole-service payoff range, which is bounded by \(R(z_*)\), and the difference-of-maxima inequality completes the proof. A qualified constant policy protects the old floor when reporting is optional. **This improves a worst-case certificate, not necessarily actual production or the Bayesian value of paying for finite labels.** Finite validation samples require uncertainty sets and selection analysis.

## Exact common service counterexample

Reuse the earlier **assumed qualified**, unitless customer-production menu with two equally likely original opportunity states H/L:
\(F=(0,0)\), competent specialist \(S=(3/4,3/4)\), grounded controller \(G=(3,-1)\), changed-target recipe \(T=(4,-6)\).
Suppose the *only* identified target report row is \(P(s=h\mid z=H)=3/5\). Low-row \(\theta=P(s=h\mid z=L)\in[0,1]\) is unobserved. The robust optimum is **G regardless of report, value 1**, even allowing randomization: the feasible world \(\theta=3/5\) makes the report independent of z, so every contingent lottery collapses to an unconditional lottery over contribution means \(F=0,S=3/4,G=1,T=-1\), bounded by G=1. Constant G attains it in every world.

If a separately justified **population** target calibration identifies \(\theta=2/5\), the now-known-kernel optimum is (G after report h, S after report l), \(43/40\); a fixed known-kernel linear program has a deterministic optimum, and the executable enumerates all 16 report rules exactly. The maximin **guarantee increment** is \(3/40\). The contingent G/S rule's *prior* missing-row floor was only \(22/40\); a rational baseline did not naively choose that rule. The 3/40 is NOT an ex-ante expected gross value of label purchases or a field-calibrated cost threshold.

Three *different* target report kernels, \((P(h\mid H),P(h\mid L))=(1/5,0),(1,4/5),(3/5,2/5)\), each achieve **60% pooled correct labels** under equal H/L opportunity mass. Their optimal *whole-service* values differ: \(44/40\) under (T,G), \(47/40\) under (G,S), and \(43/40\) under (G,S). Therefore pooled report accuracy does not identify the economic optimal service or conditional target transfer even with an otherwise fixed qualified controller.

## Interpretation, alternative assumptions and objections

The identified row must represent a pre-action **commercial opportunity**, not simply a post-action perception, observed robot success or accepted-work sample. Reporter observation must be nonactuating, rights-cleared and not change controller learning, dispatch, customer ordering or operator requirements. If source training changes policy-induced visitation, demand, the joint qualification law or the new embodiment's action/support set, all p, K, y and A may change; compare complete feasible acquisition policies through the original \(J\), including source-data, target physical grounding and new-body supply/cost. No free optional information theorem authorizes forced training, unsafe deployment or free labels. Neither technical benchmark improvement nor wages equal captured robotic contribution.

Nearest foundations: standard finite maximin/partial-identification decision theory; Blackwell (1953), https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032; distribution response to deployed prediction in Perdomo et al. (2020), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/perdomo20a.html; DDA https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10127 already values future data (§5) and complementary aggregation (Appendix B). These are **not** claimed novel theorems. What remains defensible is the integrated experimentally testable source–qualification–accepted-service interface and its actual evidence burden.

**Immediate obligation:** identify whether any independently approved, version-pinned target shadow report can label complete pre-action original customer opportunities conditional on z, with a frozen qualified physical policy. If not, retain explicit target row sets and complete-policy bounds; do not impute a source-domain accuracy or turn synthetic 3/40 into an investment recommendation.
