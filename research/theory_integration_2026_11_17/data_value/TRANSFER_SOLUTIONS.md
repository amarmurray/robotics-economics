# Separate solutions: endogenous transfer

1. Posterior precision equals prior precision plus independent observation precision for each Gaussian latent parameter. The posterior covariance for the independent linear combination is the sum of its parameter-weighted posterior variances, plus irreducible variance. Correlated priors/noise or a source representation that does not identify the target's shared parameter invalidate the simple additive precision rule; sample volume cannot repair an omitted relevant variable automatically.
2. Set \(u=a+x,v=b+g,M=B+c_xa+c_gb\). Cauchy–Schwarz gives \((A/u+C/v)M\ge(\sqrt{Ac_x}+\sqrt{Cc_g})^2\). Equality fixes the interior ratio, while strict convexity forces the clipped solution at boundaries. \(Wq(S)-B\) over *endogenously chosen* \(B\) need not use all available cash; fixed-\(B\) variance minimization alone cannot decide if investing is worthwhile.
3. \(q'(S)=-\tau\varphi(\tau/\sqrt S)S^{-3/2}\), \(q''(S)=\tau\varphi(\tau/\sqrt S)(3S-\tau^2)/(2S^{7/2})\). Since \(S_xS_g>0\) and \(S_{xg}=0\), \(q_{xg}\) has the sign of \(3S-\tau^2\). Non-Gaussian failures, multiple task/service requirements and shared physical resources change the composite interaction.
4. \(30[q(5.25)-q(9)]\approx 2.2908629\), below the cost 8. At \(X=2\), \(S_{\rm old}=4+1/2.2\), \(S_{\rm new}=.25+1/2.2\) and gross advantage is about \(12.0639116\), exceeding 8. The continuity argument proves a crossing between these states, not monotonicity at every stock.
5. Without the signal choose the target-only alternative for value 5; the prior reuse value is 3. The positive signal occurs with probability \(1/2\), updates the high-transfer probability to .8 and makes reuse worth \(5.4\); the negative updates it to .2 and leaves the alternative worth 5. EVSI is \(.5(5.4)+.5(5)-5=.2\). Net values are +.1 and -.1 at the respective costs. The diagnostic changes information and choice, not underlying skill in this example.
6. Perfectly identical qualification events both pass with probability \(p\), not \(p^2\). For \(0<p<1\), this can make a complete order requiring both substantially more likely than the independent model predicts. The sign/direction in other dependence structures is not established from marginal rates. Record joint held-out qualification outcomes.
7. Cross source-volume, target-pairing and representations under matched compute and task/site conditions, and include a changed embodiment arm with the same service acceptance rule. Distinguish fixed-budget mixture shifts from additional-spending factorial comparisons, and test both strict unseen-target and pretraining-exposed protocols. Accepted whole-order rate, recoveries/human interventions, customer demand net of best alternative, and realized operating contribution require additional measured panels beyond per-cycle scores.
