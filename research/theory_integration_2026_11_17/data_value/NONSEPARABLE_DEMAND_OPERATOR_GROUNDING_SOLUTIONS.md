# Instructor solutions: cross-demand and operator-saving G
19 Nov 2026. Kept separate from [student exercises](NONSEPARABLE_DEMAND_OPERATOR_GROUNDING_EXERCISES.md).

**1.** At (1/2,1/2), P_A=9+γ/2−1−γ/2=8 and P_B=7+γ/2−1−γ/2=6. Provider captured gross=8/2+6/2=7 in every world. Varying joint price/quantity opportunities and observing *all* eligible buyers, declines and substitution can identify a stable cross response; accepted-only tickets and endogenous prices cannot by themselves recover the structural γ. Price/offers must be genuinely exogenous or instrumented and market closure justified.

**2.** Strict concavity and the binding resource/cap KKT system give V_R=7, V_X=5+γ/2+(3−γ)²/(4−2γ), V_G=(65−γ)/8,V_E=9−γ/4, attained at (1/2,1/2),((3−γ)/(4−2γ),(1−γ)/(4−2γ)),(1/2,3/4),(1/2,1) respectively. Subtract the correct costs once. At γ=−1: R=7, X=83/12, G=143/20, E=141/20, so G. At γ=1: R=7, X=29/4, G=69/10, E=131/20, so X. The exact net X/G difference is (5γ²−21γ+2)/(40(γ−2)), with one interval root (21−√401)/10 ≈0.09750. The accepted baseline cannot identify γ and therefore cannot establish the preferred investment. The imposed single qualification window prevents automatically combining investments into an unmodeled choice.

**3.** Lagrangian L=F(q)+λ[O−(u_A−δ)q_A−q_B] plus multipliers for service caps, λ≥0. Envelope derivatives ∂_δV=λq_A*, ∂_OV=λ, under uniqueness/differentiability and fixed customer/demand law. A qualified G can have λq_A*>0 despite no new skill because saved scarce recovery time is reallocated to qualified service elsewhere. At an idle operator resource λ=0 and this immediate channel is zero.

**4.** In the separate-class model both G+E and E achieve q=(1/2,1). Thus interaction V_GE−V_G−V_E+V_R=7−V_G=−(9−γ)/8<0. For a required A+B bundle z=min(D,O/u), R=4,G=8×(1/(3/2))=16/3,E=8×(3/2)/2=6,G+E=8×1=8. Interaction=8−16/3−6+4=2/3>0. G/E substitute when both relax a capped, now-redundant resource; complement where added service scale raises the number of bundles benefiting from lower per-job effort. Saturation, prices, rights and physical approval may change this conclusion.

**5.** Pure minima are R7,X83/12,G69/10,E131/20; retain R. For equal X/G probability, direct algebra gives L(γ)=561/80+γ²/[16(2−γ)] so the minimum 561/80 occurs at γ=0, strictly above R's 7. The realized chosen intervention can still deliver less than 7. A shared learner changing target market γ with training X (or a recovery pool spillover) invalidates the common-γ fixed-world lottery analysis. With full support and additional risk-neutral assumptions the optimal X probability is (401−√401)/802, guaranteeing expected value (541+√401)/80; not required for the simple result.

**6.** Predefine a closed buyer/controller/operator catchment; measure eligible buyer populations and paired A/B quote exposures including declines, specialist switching, actual payments, full accepted qualification and recovery minutes. Randomize or instrument menu prices only with prospective approvals; separately approve G operator reductions and new E throughput, while recording controller/data rights and cross-cluster demand spillovers. Source X may change available output quality or differentiated customer expectations, so invariant γ is a hypothesis requiring validation, not a transfer rule. No approved field study is claimed.
