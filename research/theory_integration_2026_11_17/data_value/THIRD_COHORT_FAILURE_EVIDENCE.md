# Prospective evidence design: failure-origin grounding versus recovery-origin learning

This is an **unpopulated protocol**, not permission to use a customer, acquire data or conduct an intervention. Linked mathematical model: [third cohort failure/recovery](THIRD_COHORT_FAILURE_RECOVERY_ECONOMIC_OVERLAP.md).

**Unit and assignment:** One original buyer ticket, qualified robot/customer recipe, inherited worker pool and its actual repair queue; use closed noninterfering customer/worker clusters or explicitly measured spillover exposures. Register program assignment and available fallback checkpoints **before** qualification, buyer-response and worker-clearance outcomes. P is the first attempted paid service; assign follow-on R, G, K, GK and independently staffed E only with legal permissions and physical eligibility. If it is illegal to collect/retain/transfer the failure or recovery trajectory, that branch is excluded rather than assigned an optimistic success rate.

**Source lineage:** A timestamped failed *production* attempt L and exact resulting new worker job; a separate K recovery trajectory only if the job actually clears; any teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained assets logged with origin, controller, environment, human contribution, model version, semantic task, quality and rights separately. Do not infer a K trajectory on W=0 histories, or treat multiple clips of one failure as independent draws. Record data-use rights for observation, retention, training, cross-embodiment reuse, customer redeployment and revocation/expiry. Record deliberate G validation time, K processing time, compute/curation costs and source-selection checkpoint policy.

**Ticket and physical denominator:** Record **all original** eligible orders and no-orders, ticket ID, initial remaining monetary authorization, accepted early service (or rejection), whether budget/order closes, remaining deadline after failure, later original offer D before dispatch, acceptance quality, realized price, same worker's job arrival/clearance W and other queue demands. Record E independent staffing, manufacturing/qualification delay and cost; E is not worker-free just because a theoretical model calls it independent.

**Technical/economic audit:** With lawful matched checkpoint evaluation of the *complete target recipe*, preserve Q_G, Q_K and their **joint** response on the same well-defined risk set. Evaluate retained fallback checkpoint with no forced learning. Within cohort-1 failures estimate h=Pr(DW|F), individual paid coincidences x_G and x_K, technical overlap o, and decisive z=Pr(Q_G Q_K D W|F) where all terms refer to *one consistent authorized intervention regime*. Record unusable/negative transfer, validation rejection and absent tickets as zeros in the original denominators. Source-only success data and selected accepted-service receipts cannot substitute. Test explicit pre-offer masking/non-displacement and whether K training changes clearance, and test the selectable-checkpoint OR against actual inference/dispatch limits.

**Causal support:** If G, K and GK each change buyer order timing or repair queue, separate historical R data do **not** supply D and W potential responses. Randomized whole-regime clusters with rights, action positivity, consistency, matched task/customer and interference controls could identify whole-policy value directly; narrow cross-regime restrictions need real falsification tests. Absent those interventions, report sharp *conditional* scenario bounds, not measured field conclusions.

**Atlas / IO route:** original warehouse tote request, replenishment ticket, arm/mobile kit, customer support/recovery order; document each value chain's customer willingness to pay, qualified accepted units, operator support intensity and legal transfer. The numerical official BEA interindustry coefficient remains unverified, so no synthetic substitute.
