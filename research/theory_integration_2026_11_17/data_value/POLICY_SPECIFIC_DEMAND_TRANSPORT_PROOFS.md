# Proof ledger: policy-specific demand transport and incumbent information
**19 November 2026 | Companion to [main theory](POLICY_SPECIFIC_DEMAND_TRANSPORT.md).** Standard partial-identification, optimization and robust decision arguments, not independently novel.

**1. Valid domain and payoff normalization.** Conditional on the prior two-service qualified capacities, u_A, O, unchanged marginal own-price slopes 2, a_p=9+gamma_p/2, b_p=7+gamma_p/2, and one engineering qualification slot, the optimized *net* provider values are:
J_X(g)=19/4+g/2+(3-g)^2/(4-2g)=(27g-56)/[4(g-2)];
J_G(g)=(65-g)/8-11/10;
J_E(g)=9-g/4-11/5;
J_R=7.
Each policy's physically approved menu and full accepted-service customer contribution, not per-robot benchmark score, determine J. All money-like quantities are hypothetical normalized contribution. Proof of these optimized service recipes, caps and KKT conditions is in [preceding exact proof](NONSEPARABLE_DEMAND_OPERATOR_GROUNDING_PROOFS.md). Adding new quality/intercept changes without reoptimization is not authorized by the theorem.

**2. Derivatives and crossover.** J_X'(g)=1/[2(2-g)^2]>0, J_G'(g)=-1/8, J_E'(g)=-1/4 on [-1,1]. For D(g)=J_X(g)-J_G(g), D'(g)>0; D(g)=(5g²-21g+2)/[40(g-2)], zero exactly once in [-1,1] at g*=(21-sqrt(401))/10. Endpoint strict signs yield unique X/G ordering for any *common* g except the root. The earlier source/G cross-over was not estimated from customer data.

**3. Incumbent audit irrelevance absent transport.** Conditional on each possible observation r=gamma_R, assume policy-specific (gamma_X,gamma_G,gamma_E) can attain every vector in [-1,1]^3. Because each payoff set is unaffected by conditioning on r, pure minimax values are min J_X=J_X(-1)=83/12, min J_G=J_G(1)=69/10, min J_E=J_E(1)=131/20, R=7. Thus pure maximin is uniquely R, before/after perfect r revelation. A positive-cost audit cannot raise this criterion. This is not Bayesian irrelevance: a model with correlated future gamma_p and observed r can have positive expected information value. Nor is a physical incumbent fallback guaranteed if the audit itself changes access/controller capacity.

**4. Why the old common-gamma lottery fails.** Under one latent gamma for both X and G, 50/50 expected net has worst-case 561/80>7 (previous proof). Under rectangular *policy-specific* gamma_X and gamma_G, independently attainable after each realized intervention, a pre-action random t allocation has minimax expected value t*J_X(-1)+(1-t)*J_G(1). For 0≤t≤1 both endpoint net values are less than7, so every such mixture is worse than R in that criterion. This distinction concerns the uncertainty set, not the mathematical desirability of randomization universally.

**5. Sharp bound under transport coupling.** Assume common gamma_R=r∈[L,U], with conditional joint support gamma_p∈[c(r-delta),c(r+delta)] for p∈{X,G,E}, and independent attainable endpoint shifts within that support. c(z)=max(-1,min(1,z)). At a fixed r, since X increases and G decreases, the smallest D_XG=J_X(gamma_X)-J_G(gamma_G) occurs at gamma_X=gamma_G=c(r-delta), the largest at both c(r+delta). The common-function D is strictly increasing, and clipping is monotone, so the global extrema are D(c(L-delta)),D(c(U+delta)). Constructing these two endpoint worlds proves sharpness *inside this support model*. If the policy shocks must covary in another way, these remain outer bounds but may be loose. If the intercepts, prices, rights or resource constraints change beyond the stipulated model, they are not even valid outer bounds.

**6. Safe choice requires R/E comparisons.** For pure investment chosen *before* the unknown state and with physically feasible R, guaranteed values are:
underbar(J_X)=J_X(c(L-delta)), underbar(J_G)=J_G(c(U+delta)), underbar(J_E)=J_E(c(U+delta)), underbar(J_R)=7.
The max of these is the best pure maximin commitment for the **restricted** policy menu. A strong *state-by-state* X certificate uses lower X>max(upper G,upper E,R), where upper G=J_G(c(L-delta)) and upper E=J_E(c(L-delta)). A strong G certificate uses lower G>max(upper X,upper E,R), where upper X=J_X(c(U+delta)) and upper E=J_E(c(L-delta)). These are sufficient screening inequalities; independently maximizing across r may make them conservative when r uncertain. The pairwise D sign alone never ranks E or R.

**7. Exact rational cases.** delta=1/4,r=1/2: lower X=197/28>R=7; upper G=1119/160<7; upper E=539/80<7. Robust X before audit cost, lower incremental over R=1/28. delta=1/4,r=-1/2: lower G=1129/160>7; upper X=251/36<7; upper E=559/80<7. Robust G, lower incremental over R=9/160. Both are synthetic, and audit expense can erase them. In an off-support r near g* with delta=1/4, neither intervention's lower value exceeds R; hence zero worst-case incremental gross gain is possible even after a precise r audit. No ex ante positive data-acquisition price follows.

**8. Statistical resolution versus transport resolution.** If an audit certifies |r-rhat|≤epsilon, inserting L=max(-1,rhat-epsilon) and U=min(1,rhat+epsilon) yields a sufficient pairwise X>G condition rhat-epsilon-delta>g* and G>X condition rhat+epsilon+delta<g*. Even infinite sample precision cannot eliminate unknown delta. Under invariant a, T_A(q)=P_A+2q_A=a-gamma q_B. The two-context slope estimate gamma=-(T_A2-T_A1)/(q_B2-q_B1) has deterministic absolute error ≤2eta/|q_B2-q_B1| if both identified T_A values have errors ≤eta. This bound requires common eligible-buyer intercept and exogenous/identified variation; two selected completed orders do not suffice.

**9. Model failures that must survive the manuscript.** Unknown action-induced customer quality/intercept shift; endogenous original-eligible-buyer composition; nonrandom reporting; retained R infeasibility after changed learner or system setup; physically unapproved G or E; joint customer-service qualification and operator time not measured; changing retained specialist competition; shared-source training which changes other policies' gammas; unobserved rights/compute expenses; unbounded off-support captured receipts. All invalidate or weaken this simple transport certificate. Direct isolated whole-network policy comparisons and verified resource/receipt envelopes remain legitimate alternatives under their own support conditions.
