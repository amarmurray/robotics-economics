# Prospective original-buyer **joint choice** and priced-menu measurement

**Design only, not a request for private records, outreach or a performed experiment. 19 Nov 2026.**

**Original denominator.** Version the eligible *procurement decision*, not only each robot task. Register a persistent buying unit, order group, market/time, the full set of offered and **unoffered** technically qualified service alternatives, specialist/human fallback, whether those services are substitutes or components of a jointly accepted bundle, no-purchase outcome, noneligible customer entry and outside-option change. Multiple requests from one buyer with a shared procurement budget must not be misrecorded as independent accepted orders.

**Offer and choice process.** Record exact contractually enforceable posted menu, whether the buyer saw each service, posted time and currency, individual and joint offer variants, buyer's selected class or no-purchase, rejected quote, price revisions, quality/service-level guarantees, cancellation, renegotiation, and collection after acceptance. An accepted-only dataset has no identified denominator for a choice-share model. Apparent refusals when robot capacity was withheld or the offer was never presented are *not buyer refusal*. Preserve all rival/specialist quotes and competitor response to the new service.

**Matched robotics policy lineage.** R retained controller; X source exposures disaggregated into operating trajectories, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained data with rights and cost; G target grounding with held-out qualification **and** actual operator-saving effect; E new body/control/recovery/ manufacturing configuration. For each: frozen version, allowed old-controller fallback, policy qualification, signed physical admission, exact whole-service requirements, operator-recovery capacity and rights, selected/offered customer bundles, cash receipts and penalties. Record who collects revenue and when; do not price benchmark improvement as revenue.

**Identification pathways.** (a) Without experiments, jointly bound choice utilities from genuine original-buyer offer/refusal comparisons at the **same quality specification**, independently validate a menu-uniform policy drift cap, then construct strict guaranteed dominance and possible-alternative sets. (b) With approved clustered randomization, assign entire closed markets/learner-operator pools to complete X/G/E/R price-and-service policies, recording every original opportunity including no sales; estimate **whole-policy** contribution directly where randomization, safety, support and interference closure are satisfied. (c) If identifiable, test choice-based probabilistic alternatives (e.g. outside-option multinomial logit) against held-out menu changes, refusals, price variation and buyer heterogeneity; deterministic threshold certainty does not automatically transport.

**Discrimination and falsification.** If A and B are separately accepted but never jointly bought when both offered, observe substitution versus independently limited operator capacity. To distinguish, record entire choice sets and actual non-purchased alternatives at unchanged capacity, then perform a separately approved capacity change at unchanged choice menu. To distinguish X technical transfer from quality/price shift, compare matched target service qualification, supervisor recovery and policy quality at the *same offered price*. To distinguish G resource savings from revenue effects, instrument/measure operator minutes per **accepted full service** at matched service quality and eligibility. For shared controller/network spillovers, track model artifact IDs, stateful updates, operator roster and market routing across all nominal treatment pools.

**Falsifying conditions.** No finite bound if original eligible buying units/collected payments lack independently justified caps. No deterministic guaranteed sale if stochastic choice has unbounded shocks or menu-specific utilities exceed interval scope. No valid safe-menu lower bound if offers generate unlimited contractual obligations or shared operator capacity is displaced. No X/G ranking is causal from one baseline price or retrospective acceptance percentages. A real whole-policy trial without closed interference domains may not identify universal rollout value.

**Portfolio connection.** Join the procurement-group ID to the existing complete-order demand, qualification, output, data-transfer, contract/rights and operator fields in the atlas. Monetary sector input/output matrices will remain aggregate topology, not proof of specific robot service revenues or an assumed buyer choice model. Official BLS–BEA quantitative crosswalk remains unacquired; do not invent numerical quantities or substitute announcements.

**No field intervention, empirical policy-quality interval, actual menu randomization, official numeric backbone or external review has been performed.** November 22 models gate remains open.
