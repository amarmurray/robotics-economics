# Original-market menu transport: information versus intervention in the same qualified-production model

**20 November 2026 | private continuation of When Capabilities Become Production.** This addresses the *same* November 22 original buyer–robot–shared-operator acceptance problem from [complete-policy support](JOINT_NETWORK_POLICY_SUPPORT_AND_DATA_VALUE.md), not a new numbered question. The distinction matters even if whole-joint G and E are separately certified: a buyer commitment elicited under a new contract is generally **not** a passive observation of purchases under the original production menu. All results below are **conditional mathematics on an invented finite market**, not prices, certifications, field transfer, or established scientific novelty. X remains excluded from physical deployment without independent safety authorization.

## Common environment and exact intellectual question

A preassignment original procurement parent requests service A and service B, with posted contributions 4 and 3. Exactly half the synthetic parents have budget 6 and half budget 8. The old R operator route can fulfill only A for contribution 4; a jointly physically admitted G route could fulfill both, subject to the original parent's purchase decision; separately admitted G-A and G-B recipes do *not* establish joint G physical admission. E is an independently imagined qualified new embodiment with net contribution 9/2 after its own once-counted cost. A full joint G policy carries an **additional once-counted** fixed certificate/deployment cost of 1/2. An original-budget-8 parent purchases B jointly with A with probability theta_N, conditional on G actually being safe, offered on the **natural original menu**, and satisfying the resource envelope. A budget-6 parent cannot purchase both at these posted prices. Conditional on the stated no-interference and stable composition assumptions,

    V_G^natural(theta_N) = 4 + (3/2) theta_N - 1/2
                         = 7/2 + (3/2) theta_N.
    V_E = 9/2,    V_R = 4.

Here the G certificate cost is outside gross V and subtracted exactly once when comparing net investment choices; in the integrated dynamic J it belongs once to the chosen action's cost. Neither existing production-data continuation benefits nor new information value should be *added again* as a data premium. G beats the competent E if and only if theta_N > 2/3. If G is unsigned, it is not in the physically feasible action set irrespective of this inequality.

The unresolved observation from the previous counterworlds is the **joint natural-menu buyer response** theta_N, not isolated G task success. Denote theta_C the high-budget response to a prospective target **commitment or offer C**. The new action C may alter quality guarantees, deposit/refund rights, price menu, enforceability, cancellation and the buyer's information; theta_C and theta_N are responses to **two versions of treatment**. We must not assume equality because the same robot body is named G.

## Proposition: conditional sharp economic transport interval

Suppose an independently justified demand-transport restriction is

    |theta_N - theta_C| <= epsilon,   0 <= epsilon <= 1,

where theta_C is the *population conditional response law* under a precisely recorded commitment, and theta_N and theta_C refer to the **same original high-budget parent stratum**. Holding fixed the natural-menu price and the physical net-cost model above, define

    l = max(0,theta_C-epsilon),    u = min(1,theta_C+epsilon).

Then the sharp identified interval for the **natural-menu** net G contribution is

    V_G^natural in [ 7/2 + (3/2)l, 7/2 + (3/2)u ].

The criterion is strict and operational: G robustly exceeds E only if l>2/3. E robustly exceeds G if u<2/3. An interval straddling 2/3 does **not** rank them. R and any other qualified specialization enter the same maximum over alternatives, not a separate price of data.

**Proof.** The absolute difference restriction intersects the Bernoulli high-stratum probability parameter theta_N with [0,1], yielding exactly [l,u]. V_G is affine increasing in theta_N, so its range is the displayed interval. It is sharp under these assumptions: for every theta_N in the interval construct a Bernoulli natural buyer response with that probability; couple it with the contract Bernoulli response using common uniform U so the disagreement probability is exactly |theta_N-theta_C|, hence at most epsilon. The low-budget original order and all R/mixed/E outcomes remain as stipulated. No stronger bound follows from these conditions. This proof **does not** establish the restriction itself, finite-sample confidence, or the safety of the G policy. QED.

The parameter epsilon is a declared **cross-menu transport hypothesis**: no amount of contract-only observations identifies it without an additional matched source, independent rationale or legally enforced equality of versioned terms. It may capture preference changes under new warranties, quality uncertainty, deposits, timing or buyer selection; it is not a magical estimator of customer rationality. With different physical qualification probabilities, nonadditive shared operators or altered prices/costs, use a bound on the *entire* complete-policy outcome instead of recycling this formula.

### Two exact implications of the same synthetic network

* If theta_C=1 and epsilon=1/4, then theta_N is at least 3/4 and V_G^natural lies in [37/8,5]. A **physically admitted** G wins robustly over E=9/2; the 1/4 sensitivity limit is doing the scientific work, not the existence of a buyer report. At epsilon=1/3, the lower bound ties E.
* If theta_C=0 and epsilon=1/2, then theta_N is at most 1/2 and V_G^natural lies in [7/2,17/4], strictly below E=9/2.
* If theta_C=1 but epsilon=1, the natural response remains [0,1] and the G-versus-E choice is unidentified despite universal observed commitment. In two worlds, identical *contract* take-up theta_C=1, original orders, R/RG/GR/E and any contract-only records coexist with theta_N=0 or theta_N=1. This reverses G/E on the **unoffered natural** joint menu. One world could give an additional warranty under C that makes B attractive; the other would buy under either version. A binding C contract cannot by itself resolve the difference.

This last result is an original **application/counterexample synthesis** of treatment-version consistency and partial identification, not an assertion of a new impossibility theorem.

## Separate contract intervention from pure evidence acquisition

There are **three** distinct objects:

1. **Passive/nonbinding report S**: a report about a latent original-market purchase response, with a versioned likelihood P(S|theta_N). S has decision value only if its relation to natural-menu response is identified or assumed, and only if it changes the feasible investment.
2. **Binding commitment C**: a newly offered purchase/quality/payment contract whose own commercial and resource outcomes are Y(G,C), not automatically Y(G,natural). If C is enforceable and safely executable, it is itself a candidate **production policy** in V. Its discounts, refunds, exclusivity, access and execution costs must be booked once; there is no license to reuse a commitment as an unchanged natural-menu demand measure.
3. **Target grounding G / source data X / embodiment E**: interventions on physical learning, alignment and available safe production recipes. A buyer report is neither a robot qualification certificate nor an authorization to run an unsigned source-trained X. Distinct field, recovery, teleoperation, simulated, synthetic and public/pretrained data can alter the joint qualification law but do not by themselves measure the unoffered target procurement menu.

The setting can be generalized using a common complete outcome space S. If both versions evaluate the *same* bounded contribution g(s) in [a,b], and TV(P_N,P_C)<=epsilon under a justified coupling for the **whole original network**, then |E_N g - E_C g|<=epsilon(b-a). Proof: maximal coupling makes outcomes disagree with probability at most epsilon; their contribution difference is bounded by b-a on disagreement. Sharpness follows with endpoint mass shifts. But if a contracted price/refund or embodied resource set changes the **outcome function** itself, this TV inequality is inadequate until the difference in g_N versus g_C is separately accounted for. Nor is this a hard pathwise safety certificate.

This treatment-version distinction also rejects an apparent shortcut: customer demand commitments made under warranty C may be commercially valuable in their own right, yet cannot validate technical X transfer, automatically establish physical G certification, or causally identify an untested original G menu.

## A selective-information threshold with a competent embodiment alternative

Return to the two **natural-menu** counterworlds H (theta_N=1; net G=5) and L (theta_N=0; net G=7/2). Suppose, purely for decision analysis, the prior is P(H)=1/2 and a **passive** binary report S has symmetric accuracy alpha in [1/2,1], no causal effect on customer purchase or physical admission, and unchanged information kernel under subsequent actions. Baseline choose E=9/2, because prior mean net G=17/4.

After S=+, posterior P(H|+)=alpha; after S=-, it is 1-alpha. G is chosen after + exactly when alpha>2/3; after - E always weakly dominates for alpha>=1/2. Consequently the **gross expected value of sample information**, compared with E without S, is

    EVSI(alpha) = max(0, 3 alpha/4 - 1/2).

This equals zero for accuracy at or below 2/3, 1/16 for alpha=3/4, and 1/4 for a perfectly informative passive report. This is a direct standard Bayesian decision computation, not measured report pricing. Sampling/verification/rights costs subtract once within J; the report is not intrinsically worth the upper amount. A report can be statistically informative (alpha>1/2) but **decision-irrelevant** because E already covers the opportunity.

For an ambiguity interval P(H)=p in [p_low,p_high], with p_high>=p_low and a *perfect*, genuinely passive state-revealing report, the lower-envelope expected option value is 9/2 + p_low/2. The no-report maximin value is max(9/2,7/2 + 3p_low/2). Therefore the **maximin difference** equals p_low/2 for p_low<=2/3 and 1-p_low for p_low>=2/3. If p_low=0, a report has zero positive guaranteed improvement even though it might carry large Bayesian value at a specific prior. This assumes ambiguity is committed before the signal and the signal law itself is correct. It is not a minimax-regret result or an empirical valuation.

## Economic interpretation and next discriminating work

This is a single extension of the source-to-qualification-to-original-production chain. **Technical transfer** changes the distribution over independently qualified recipes and possibly observation-closed safe recovery. **Market transport** determines whether the new bundle is accepted at the actually offered price/quality/rights. **Operator complementarities** determine whether service recipes can be jointly executed. **Information** matters only when it changes the optimal feasible policy under these mechanisms. An embodiment E can lower the option value of additional target information even if it does not improve X learning; a contract C may both produce evidence and change the economy to which that evidence pertains. This is why raw trajectory volume, isolated benchmark percentage, a signed single-body robot test and a buyer survey are not substitutable proof objects.

Direct next testable question, not a new Q-number: can an actual original buyer–robot–shared-operator unit produce an independently approved **joint** physical G/E policy **and** a version-comparable original parent demand/receipt record? If not, can repeated *non-deployment* target offers plus independently justified menu-version restrictions provide non-vacuous epsilon while respecting procurement rights and sampling selection? No customer access, outreach, contracts, paid experiment or physical intervention was executed here; the [prospective discrimination protocol](MENU_TRANSPORT_EMPIRICAL_AND_GATE.md) is explicitly unpopulated. A missing original buyer response remains missing, not zero; source-trained X remains unsigned and inadmissible.
