| Claim | Scope and construction | Status / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| S1 selected reachable qualification: P_B=sum pi_h r_h e_h s_h | First correction necessary for B access; conditional exposure and qualification defined in that order | Proved by probability chain rule; not a claim about all available data streams |
| Covariance correction to naive independent bundle | e_h=1; r_h and s_h share latent regime H | Exact identity; unknown sign. World +/- respectively give 5/16 and 3/16 against independence 1/4 |
| S4-S5 optimized second action | A retained; B experiment and specialist mutually exclusive; same second resource calendar and Delta=f(A+B)-f(A) | Derived by Bellman maximization; no data premium added to terminal f |
| Two observationally equivalent first-episode histories reverse the selected first policy | Same pi, r_H,r_L, same marginal B success; conditional alignment varies; Delta=8,c_B=3,u_S=1,k=1/4 | Exact symbolic fractions, checked by two independent finite-state implementations; synthetic, no field inference |
| S2 off-support target qualification not identified | Zero B exposure for every logged policy, no bridging measurement | Proof by observationally equivalent environments; standard positivity limit |
| Sharp simple no-support interval | s_h in [0,1]; exposure e known and Delta>=0 | B option in [-c, Delta E[e|C]-c]; after option among B, specialist, old max cannot fall below competent fallback |
| S3 free optional expansion monotone | All old safe policies and rights retained; no new required cost or feasibility change | Feasible-set inclusion; does not apply to forced training |
| Controller improvement implies smaller economic data value | Not generally true; endogenous visitation and policy retention alter the action set | Rejected universal conjecture |
| First-step corrections imply safe autonomous competence | Unidentified without independently audited gate, held-out qualification and accepted service | Negative identification result, not robotics evidence |

**Proof audit:** Eq S3 requires correct **conditional** s_h, not merely raw B success probability. The World +/- values are for a hypothetical *forced* B attempt following a corrected first episode; under the optimized policy World - actually chooses the specialist, so 3/16 must never be presented as an observed deployment qualification frequency. Proposition S2 is only counterfactual nonidentification without any target-side bridge. A learned controller can avoid B entirely for safety; it need not be qualified for a service it never offers.

**Strong alternatives:** (1) routine productive work already reaches the same B states and produces usable retained learning for free; (2) target-only qualified specialist earns u_S; (3) new body/approved tool opens support without a correction; (4) extra safe sensor/audit reports identify error need without controlled failures; (5) fixed demand or a binding other recipe makes Delta zero; (6) alignment is anti-correlated with first correction, so selected data harms B learning while old optional routes survive.

**Technical validation boundary:** finite synthetic event enumeration and formula assertions verify calculations, not theory under arbitrary nonlinear control kernels, whole-repository CI, novelty, industrial market values or physical training. No official quantitative production backbone has been obtained.

**Next unresolved argument:** characterize multi-step routine support expansion versus deliberate target grounding and embodiment under identical safety/operator/calendar budgets when actions select H-dependent reachable states; identify testable joint accepted-order qualification conditions, not more first-step proxy thresholds.
