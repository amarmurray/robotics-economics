## Economic object and the source of missing identification

A cluster history is one draw from a **joint law** of potential buyer orders/no-orders, qualifications, worker queues, accepted-paid production and data/rights states under each legal policy, with all date-specific actions nonanticipative. In each preaction stratum H=h, let A be the P-program's downstream original-customer **qualified, demanded and paid-service feasible** event, W the inherited queue's worker-free potential at the downstream service date *before additional program-specific recovery*, and B the G-program's corresponding upgraded paid-service event. B is the intersection of G technical qualification, actual original target-buyer order, and worker availability after G calibration. Neither the G learner nor its buyer-order response is fixed to the P program. The economic objective is optimized captured qualified customer production minus all real resources, **including any future service effect of data exactly once**. A robot benchmark cannot be substituted for buyer willingness to pay.

Assume the following are documented **hypotheses**, not empirical claims:

1. **Conserved recovery capacity:** G cannot clear an inherited recovery job merely by being assigned. Its terminal paid target event satisfies B ≤ W pointwise. This applies to the fixed one-worker, one-slot service schedule without an independently resourced E worker or a genuinely worker-clearing G capability. If G actually repairs an inherited queue, the condition is false and the law must be enlarged.
2. **Bounded cross-program accepted-service discordance:** conditional on a preaction, legally linkable original-buyer/worker stratum, Pr(A ≠ B | H=h) ≤ ε_h. This is a sensitivity *budget* for changed learner qualification, changed buyer orders and changed operator incidents combined, not a historically measured effect. It must **not** be inferred from marginal benchmark transfer or from P logs when G has no action support.
3. The old/P law of (A,W,H), including no-orders where legally accessible and the genuine support/censoring process, is identified or else bounded by a separate lawful observation model. Cost and P/E comparison values in the numerical example below are stipulated complete program values; physical independence of E is a separate auditable premise.

These conditions do not require independence across P and G outcomes or rectangular selection of different adversarial worlds for different arms. They are restrictions on **one** joint law of counterfactual program histories. A true G prohibition removes G from the lawful policy menu; zero historical G propensity alone does not.

## Sharp two-event bound with a common worker queue

In a fixed stratum let c=Pr(A=1,W=1), l=Pr(A=1,W=0), d=Pr(A=0,W=1), and e=Pr(A=0,W=0), with c+l+d+e=1. Put a=c+l, w=c+d. If ε<l, the physical/sensitivity model is **infeasible**, because every A=1,W=0 history forces B=0 and already causes discordance l.

**Proposition.** Under exactly the observable (A,W) distribution, B≤W and Pr(A≠B)≤ε, with no other B restrictions, the sharp identified interval for b=Pr(B=1) is

    L = max(0, a-ε),   U = min(w, c+ε-l),     provided ε≥l.

*Proof.* Let x=Pr(B=1,A=1,W=1), y=Pr(B=1,A=0,W=1). All B mass is x+y, 0≤x≤c, 0≤y≤d. Discordance equals l+(c−x)+y, so it is equivalent to x−y≥c+l−ε. Minimizing x+y takes y=0 and x=max(0,a−ε). Maximizing takes x=c and y=min(d,ε−l). Each solution defines a feasible nonnegative common table with all other B=0; mixing the two attains every intermediate b. Thus the bounds are sharp, including the feasibility precondition. This is finite coupling/Fréchet algebra, not a new general mathematical theorem.

If P also cannot clear the inherited queue (A≤W), l=0,c=a and the interval simplifies to [max(0,a−ε),min(w,a+ε)]. If W is unavailable/selected, its feasible distribution must itself be included in the joint LP; do not substitute an unverified p(W) or multiply marginals.

These are **fixed service-event** bounds. When G qualification/demand/worker pairwise margins are additionally *hypothetically* certified, intersect with their true common-law restrictions and solve a single joint LP: an intersection of separate numerical intervals is not automatically sharp. If alternative G dispatches change later demand, value requires the full Bellman policy menu; this proposition cannot certify an unrestricted G optimizer.

## What old/P evidence can and cannot do

Without a **defended** ε, even a complete old/P registry does not recover the G success intersection. For every t∈[0,1/4], keep the G parity law and W construction, then draw P's A conditional on W with probability 2/3, independently of B. This retains the exact same old/P (A,W), common worker conservation, and G pairwise tables at all t, while G target success ranges over the original full interval. There is no historical G positivity. This is a constructive impossibility statement, not an identification strategy.

The cap ε could be externally justified only by a lawful source–grounding matched physical mechanism bound or *validated* causal response model across the same cluster/site/embodiment and buyer market. Separate robot technical benchmarks, a model generalization percentage, or a prior old-buyer complete log does not validate it. If G can physically free a worker, if target customers cancel old orders when G is advertised, or if the embodiment changes worker resources, update the potential histories and policy/action model rather than reusing B≤W. If ε is itself set-valued, take the union of feasible common laws before robust optimization.

## Economic significance and broader theory

The object being bounded is not the quantity of raw teleoperation or simulation data. It is the probability of **qualified and buyer-demanded customer service while real hardware/worker resources are available**, conditional on the chosen intervention. Data-rich source training can be worth less than a small target-grounding intervention if source data do not line up with those jointly feasible services; conversely, an E robot can dominate when extra capacity can overcome a shared-queue bottleneck. These comparative values depend on capture rights, actual resource costs, customer order response and production recipes, not universal scaling coefficients. Free optional licensed data leaves R feasible and cannot lower the optimized value without hidden costs; forced tuning can.

Stratify H by end market, accepted-service recipe, task-bundle joint qualification, customer offer/no-offer, physical recovery backlog, data rights and source modality (field trajectory, recovery, teleoperation, sim, synthetic, public/pretrained). Never interpret source modalities as interchangeable. Multilayer input–output propagation belongs **after** validated accepted-service changes, using actual BEA interindustry flows and supplier relations, not an exogenous robotics-market multiplier. E requires manufacturing lead times and independent labor/energy/maintenance; G rights must be legally verified. All future learning and downstream customer-demand changes remain within whole-policy continuation rewards, not a second data premium.
