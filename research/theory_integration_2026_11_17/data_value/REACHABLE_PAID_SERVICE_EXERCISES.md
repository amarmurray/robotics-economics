# Exercises — reachable paid service and missing grounding support

1. Starting from L1=max(0,L0+J-1) for L0,J in {0,1}, prove the worker-free event in each inherited backlog stratum. What changes if J can equal 2?

2. Prove the sharp bounds for s=Pr(A=1,J=0) given Pr(A=1)=q and Pr(J=1)=j. Construct all four cells of a joint distribution at both endpoints and identify the weakest needed assumptions.

3. Take p0=p1=1/2, v0=2, v1=4, q0=q1=3/4 and j1=1/2. Why can a policy observing only P histories not rank G against E=13/8, even if G's technical Q and original buyer D individual marginals are externally pinned at 7/8?

4. Calculate the ex-ante minimax-regret G share when fixed E=e lies between L and U, and compare it with maximin. Explain why observing L0 *after* investment is not equivalent to a precommitment policy conditioned on L0.

5. Suppose a certified, G-specific upper bound j1<=1/4 and G-specific joint QD lower bound 3/4 are separately justified. Establish the G lower value and state which empirical records are incapable of identifying these conditions.

6. In a two-date connected network, demonstrate a case where independent datewise selections of the worst queue law violate a conserved single-worker trajectory. State the finite scenario-LP remedy.
