# Economic value of discovering a qualified production graph

24 November 2026. Continues [joint lineage conversion](JOINT_LINEAGE_PROCUREMENT.md) in When Capabilities Become Production. An acquired edge is not a paid service; its economic value depends on how it rearranges the entire feasible schedule. Distinguish new physical qualification, observing latent qualification, customer authorization, and strengthening net contribution on an existing authorized recipe.

## Exact exchange identity

Let left vertices denote dated nonduplicable robot-worker capacity slots and right vertices distinct authorized buyer service slots. Edges are complete lawful, qualified, customer-admissible recipes, observed before dispatch; net weights additive and the only constraints are unit vertex capacities. Reusable licensed data are not exclusive vertices. Let old edge set E0 be contained in E1 and M maximize old weights w0. Under new weights w1 define anchor drift d(M)=sum_{e in M}(w1(e)-w0(e)). An alternating path or cycle C relative to M is admissible when flipping it produces a matching in E1; singleton insertions and deletions are included. Let g(C) be inserted minus deleted new weight. Then

    V(E1,w1)-V(E0,w0)=d(M)+max_{vertex-disjoint admissible C} sum_C g(C).

The empty collection is feasible. Proof: M xor N for any new matching N consists of vertex-disjoint alternating paths and cycles. Each component separately flips M feasibly (internal vertices exchange one edge, inserted endpoint edges touch unmatched vertices; common edges cannot touch changed edges). Conversely any collection of vertex-disjoint admissible flips composes feasibly. Summing the gains and maximizing gives equality. Negative gains may be discarded. This is classical matching exchange, not a new graph algorithm. The split is anchor-dependent, not a unique causal attribution to source data. A strengthened retained edge can subsequently be displaced; charge that loss exactly once. Under unit weights, exchange gain is extra matching cardinality, not number of discovered edges. Under heterogeneous weights it can arise without higher cardinality. Free optional edge addition preserves old value only if the old policy remains feasible with its old economics.

Generalize to finite binary recipes with nonnegative resource incidences A_re, capacities b_r, additive weights. Every parent procurement cap, operator/time/robot constraint and exclusive service claim is a resource row. Let M remain feasible under the new system. For any new feasible N join changed recipes in M xor N when they touch a common resource row. Each connected component independently flips M feasibly: an affected resource row is affected by exactly one changed component, so its usage equals N on that row; elsewhere equals M. Conversely row-disjoint individually feasible flips compose. The same identity holds using row-connected exchange components and row-disjointness. This does not guarantee tractable enumeration; a single buyer budget can connect the entire graph. Omitting that row invalidates separate exchange bounds. Two disjoint one-slot graph matches cannot both be paid under a shared one-slot authorization. When the changed resource system renders M infeasible, use an explicit new feasible reference and separately account for lost retained value.

## Dynamic audit

Let F_t(h) be a graph-level potential of lawful predecision history (e.g., a known dated graph's retained anchor plus feasible exchange gain), with F_T equal to terminal payoff or an upper bound. Use discount-in-rewards convention. Define D_t(h,a)=E[r_t+F_{t+1}(H')|h,a]-F_t(h). If D_t<=b_t uniformly over lawful reachable histories and actions, then J(pi)<=E[F_0]+sum_t b_t for every lawful pi by iterated expectation and telescoping (integrability assumed). Reverse bounds with F_T equal to or below terminal reward certify a specified implementable policy. Thus conditional economic exchange moments can replace estimating the complete hidden edge law when they bound the relevant potential residual and action support. Earlier static graph values are only potentials, not automatically continuation values. A future report's hindsight maximum is an outer bound, not a lawful policy. Do not add a second data premium.

Exact graph-state closure instead needs equal admissible action labels, conditional reward means and next-summary distributions for each action across all histories sharing a summary, and summary-measurable terminal reward. Backward induction gives equal values. Rights, budgets, report timing and lineage must be included whenever they affect the next action or transition. A final graph exchange moment may suffice for one last action without closing the full dynamic state.

## Two-probe graph discovery versus strengthening

At date 2 two capacity slots u1,u2 and two authorized buyer service slots v1,v2 share a parent budget of two dated slots. Old edge (u1,v1) pays one; candidate cross-edges (u1,v2),(u2,v1) each pay one. No (u2,v2). Each candidate alone adds zero to optimized output; both together permit two services. Optional G costs g=1/16 at date 0 and qualifies/reveals the first cross-edge indicator A before date 1. Optional second grounding/verification costs c=1/4 of forgone current net production and qualifies/reveals B before date 2; otherwise B unavailable. The potential A,B follow a preassigned common law, and no unobserved outcome is used for dispatch. No probe consumes terminal budget in this baseline. Let q=P(A), b=P(B), x=P(A and B) on the original cohort under the specified interventions. With old edge weight 1+s, 0<=s<=1, terminal value is 1+s+(1-s)AB (enumerate no service, old, each single and both cross-edges). After A=0 probing cannot help. After A=1 it pays iff (1-s)x/q>c (zero q means no such branch). Hence

    G_s=max(0, ((1-s)x-cq)_+ - g).

This is the optional net G increment above the strengthened baseline. Known law optimization uses only A before the second probe. With interval uncertainty the single predeclared always-probe-on-A rule yields a uniform lower certificate when the lower endpoint clears costs; do not optimize separately against unknown worlds.

For q=b=1/2 the four cell probabilities (x,1/2-x,1/2-x,x) attain every x in [0,1/2]. Anti-correlated, independent and correlated worlds yield G_0=0,1/16,5/16. Identical separate technical discovery rates reverse the ranking versus source X strengthening old weight by 1/8 (zero incremental cost assumed) and independently staffed/customer-funded E adding 1/4. In the independent world E wins, X beats G; in the correlated world G beats both. A supported joint x>7/16 is necessary and sufficient for strict G>E in this example; equality ties. The policy buy G and probe on A=1 yields x-3/16. No marginal B rate on A=0 is required to estimate it. In the correlated world combining X and G, if feasible, gives G increment above X of 1/4, total 3/8 rather than standalone sum 7/16. Strengthening retained production crowds out some rearrangement value even though new cross-edge qualification is unchanged. Thus technical complements can coexist with economic substitution.

One parent slot removes all G gain; if probing consumes one future slot, the same loss occurs on that branch. Routine free optional data reproducing both cross-edges and their reports with no costs have incremental x at s=0; at x=1/2 they beat E. E sharing resources must be reoptimized, not added independently. If cross-edge weights exceed old weight, individual gains may be positive; compute full max anew. If probes merely reveal latent qualification and old policies could already lawfully use those edges, the baseline changes. A truly free optional diagnostic on unchanged feasible policies weakly increases value; costly or mandatory probing need not.

## Interface with Models A/B and prior art

Model A is the dated qualified production and procurement graph; Model B selects data, grounding, strengthening or embodiment by optimizing its dynamic continuation. The interface is the distribution of *joint feasible exchanges*, not marginal technical discovery, source sample counts or benchmarks. Field, recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained sources remain separate interventions; no sign or additivity of their transfer is assumed.
