# From item routing to complete prescription recipes: applied joint residual audit
25 November 2026 Eastern. Empirical application of [joint recipe residual audit](JOINT_RECIPE_RESIDUAL_AUDIT.md), not a new paper.

## Priors
An embodied machine supplies a particular handling operation; a prescription often requires multiple medication lines, human handling and legally required verification. The relationship between item capability and complete service is a hyperedge: the required ingredients and resources must come together. Digital access to the order record, storage embodiment, staff authorization, and the downstream review stage are distinct complementary economic objects. Data about one line need not qualify the rest. Treating item-level penetration as whole-service penetration misallocates both MPK and the expected return to extending the machine's menu.

## Sharp grouping envelope
Condition on each dispensed prescription having at least one medication order, and each item assigned once to a prescription and classified as Drug Station (DS) or non-DS. For N prescriptions, M DS items and B=T-M non-DS items, let A be number of prescriptions with every item DS-routed. With a separately validated maximum H items per prescription (NH>=T>=N),
    max(0,N-B) <= A <= min(M,N-ceil(B/H)).
For no cap, H=T is redundant. Proof: each non-all-DS prescription needs a non-DS item; at most B such prescriptions; each all-DS prescription needs one DS item. At least ceil(B/H) non-all-DS prescriptions are needed to fit B. To attain any integer A in the interval, choose N-A groups containing a non-DS item and A pure groups. Place one required item in each group; B fits in nonpure groups and M fits into remaining total slots since T<=NH. Fill the remaining DS items in available slots. B=0 or M=0 are included. Elementary occupancy combinatorics, not an independent novelty claim. If counts do not partition as stipulated, no endpoint is certified.

Period 3 M/T=76.89%, yet A ranges from 28453 to 54821 (51.90% to 99.998% of dispensed prescriptions); N-A ranges from 1 to 26369. Even an all-DS prescription retains pharmacist verification and human handling. This is a sharp count-only bound on observed routing composition, NOT all originally offered or refused prescriptions, eligibility, or true qualification. Period 2 conditional range 25944–51481 (subject to the one-item discrepancy). A hypothetical H=4 would reduce period-3 upper A to 48229, but H=4 is NOT established by the paper. A prescription-to-item linkage or validated maximum bundle size is more discriminating than another aggregate utilization report.

## Conditional residual ceiling and MPK
Fix period-3 dispensed cohort, prices, incumbent feasible menu and dated resources b. Suppose a proposed catalog extension changes only non-DS item handling in the observed required bundle; no altered route or qualification on all-DS prescriptions, induced demand, shared-resource change or other learning spillover, and old recipes remain feasible. This does not follow merely from past DS routing. At most B=26369 groups have a non-DS item. Let each independently supported upper margin vbar_i bound nonnegative gross contribution of any new recipe, and h_i^S(p) the observed-subset local residual at nonnegative price p. The incremental omitted residual e_i(p) is bounded by max(0,vbar_i-h_i^S(p)) on affected groups, zero elsewhere, PROVIDED the old menu is completely accounted for or its missing residual separately bounded. A conservative aggregate new residual upper is the sum of the largest min(B,N) such ceilings; if each <=vbar, it is <=26369 vbar. Proof: nonnegative incidence makes v_ir-p'a_ir<=vbar_i, so maximize the sum over a subset of at most B groups. Weak duality as in joint recipe audit gives gross incremental static optimum <= p'd plus that sum for added capacity d if p is an exact incumbent price; otherwise add incumbent dual/witness gap. A feasible dated schedule is still needed for a positive lower bound. The result is specific to the restricted catalog intervention, not the value of faster handling or new customers. Neither that intervention restriction nor a value ceiling, dated resource prices or feasible incremental schedule is supplied. No numeric MPK or net G/P/C/E ranking is supported.

The observed median pharmacist time fell 69 to 23 seconds after trained staff were added, while median total changed 87 to 61. These are distributional changes, not an estimated 46 seconds per order of payroll avoided. Medians are nonadditive; absent a cap the upper tail of a nonnegative work-time distribution can grow arbitrarily with the same finite quartiles. Multiplying the median change by all prescriptions cannot establish aggregate resource release. Keep actual staff clock, active machine clock, overlaps, omitted review and redeployed qualified clinical services separate; value released capacity once. No causal effect of ML training or lawful retained-source rights established.
