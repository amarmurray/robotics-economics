# Prospectively discriminating independent source-family evidence from joint target transfer

20 November 2026. **Unpopulated, read-only source/experimental protocol** accompanying [the conditional theory](SOURCE_LINEAGE_HIGHER_ORDER_TRANSFER_AND_ECONOMIC_DECISION.md). Does not authorize new robot trials, model training, purchases, operator access, customer contact or publication.

## Two separate empirical objects

**Source archive/learner object.** A licensed version-pinned input and loader/revision manifest records every episode's raw digest, source modality (field / recovery / teleop / simulator / synthetic / public-pretrained), collection/generator ID, robot embodiment, controller checkpoint, operator/site/scene/time, skill/outcome/failed-recovery labels, training-ready transformations, processed transition lengths, proposed/accepted episode-of-origin weights, accepted-K and fixed proposal resource B. Include data ownership/training/derivative rights and parser-level task-label validity, not only nonempty text. Hold out at lineage **cluster** level; a random transition split is vulnerable to replay and near-duplicate contamination. This remains prospective: pinned Berkeley BridgeData loader code documents mechanisms but not the actual independent episode and admissibility manifest.

**Target qualification and original buyer object.** On an independently authorized held-out original-customer recipe/state regime, obtain paired predictions/qualification decisions for three source-trained checkpoints or specified signal modalities on the *same* target episodes. Record whether target regime \(\theta\) and ground-truth qualification are themselves valid; produce the full 8-cell target-conditioned error table, not just three accuracies or pairwise agreements. Record controller/version/embodiment/task per prediction and all policy actions/forgone actions. Target policies must first be physically signed; experimental shadow prediction alone is not live deployment permission.

For the binary illustrative mechanism, compute \(\hat t=n^{-1}\sum_c1\{E_{1c}=E_{2c}=E_{3c}=1\}\). With independent target *clusters* the conditional Hoeffding radius is \(\sqrt{\log(2/\delta)/(2n)}\); clip the inferred t support to [0,1/16] **only if** symmetry, calibrated 1/4 marginal errors and 1/16 pairwise wrong rates are supported. Pre-register tests of those assumptions; otherwise use general 8-cell partial-ID and end-to-end off-policy policy-value bounds instead of inserting t. Matched target probes may measure a diagnostic error mechanism but do not establish causal marginal benefits of acquiring new source data. Do not equate samples from one controller or producer with independent learners.

## Distinguishing the mechanisms

Randomize independently feasible, rights-cleared complete *learner clusters*: (1) baseline archive/no extra inclusion; (2) two new independent source episodes or *version-locked existing archive inclusion* (different acquisition estimands); (3) target grounding or admission/reweighting of existing episodes; (4) authorized joint X×G where physically feasible and budget-accounted; (5) approved new-body E as a **separate** deployment policy and opportunity cost. In a fixed-K learner design hold accepted updates equal and report proposal+curation cost; in a fixed-B design let accepted K differ, counting rejections and all compute. Do not condition on achieved K as if randomized. Shared model weights, replay, dispatch and operator pools determine the interference closure. If independent clusters or signed full-network arms are not available, no causal policy estimate is claimed.

Readouts have different meanings: (a) source lineage and target-conditioned triple-error t; (b) learned *joint qualified service recipes*, safety regressions and failure-recovery performance on each versioned target/embodiment; (c) each trained policy's own physically admissible state visitation and original-customer offered/unoffered orders; (d) eligible original buyer acceptance, invoicing, **paid** receipts/refunds, displaced old/manual production and all shared physical/operator/software/access costs. Only (d) under supported and signed policies can establish an economic J. Target labels and testing receipts are separate from customer revenue. No benchmark delta directly becomes dollars.

**Falsifiable contrasts.** When episode count/marginal coverage/pairwise errors are matched, does actual joint task qualification change with independently generated source-family coverage or three-way target error structure? Does G merely reweight/admit previously held data, or change the technical target map? Are recovery families independent after conditioning on upstream controller/simulator/operator and task regime? Does E add physically approved recipes that substitute for training, or new complementarity via operator bottlenecks? For source modalities, retain provenance/type-specific causal arms rather than imposing one omnipotent human-data factor.
