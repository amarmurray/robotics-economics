# Exercises: causal program-dependent data value

1. **Whole-potential-history law.** Derive equations (1)–(2) without granting two date-3 slots. Explain the different meaning of source T's paid current service and G's costful calibration.
2. **Pairwise insufficiency.** Verify every G marginal is 1/2 and every pairwise joint-success is 1/4 under family (3), yet the triple-success mass ranges over [0,1/4]. Give endpoint support sets.
3. **Market and physical response.** Calculate \(V_P\), \(V_{G,C}(t)\), \(V_R\), and the best *optional* G program. Identify the exact P/G switch and explain why G target order \(D_G\) cannot be replaced by observed old \(Z\).
4. **Regret vs guaranteed production.** Prove the maximin production choice and minimax-regret randomization. Distinguish what the controller knows before program commitment from ex-post oracle values.
5. **Audit design.** Show why complete old orders and operator logs cannot identify \(t\). Construct a lawful, connected-cluster intervention-specific joint evidence target, listing what must be observed and what an actual action-support zero prevents. State a physical assumption that would change the conclusion.
6. **Generalization.** If G data are freely optional, old controls stay feasible and there is no storage, training or legal cost, prove value monotonicity. Explain why this does not imply positive marginal economic value or that a forced bad G training recipe cannot be harmful.
