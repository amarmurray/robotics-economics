# Student exercises — simultaneous qualified orders, information and embodiment
Assume g_1≥g_2>0, one approved operator slot and two original customer orders.
1. Given p_1=2/5 and p_2=3/5, find all possible r=P(Z_1Z_2) and construct its four atoms. Why do full within-order success rates not identify it?
2. Derive precommitted U, predispatch-informed F_1 and safely two-slotted F_2. Prove I+C=min(g_1p_1,g_2p_2). Explain the joint reporter's physical permission requirement.
3. For p_i=1/2, g_i=8, c_A=1 and c_E=2, compare net retention, perfect audit and added body in exclusive and synchronized qualification worlds.
4. State sharp partial-identification bounds on net A−E and inequalities for robust strict choice from marginal data alone.
5. Design a safe prospective test distinguishing within-order qualification, across-order coincidence and whole-network economic policy value when only one order can be served.
6. Explain why a source-trained controller changing customer demand and operator calendars invalidates fixed-law transport to a new embodiment. What direct complete-policy estimand remains available under approved cluster randomization?
