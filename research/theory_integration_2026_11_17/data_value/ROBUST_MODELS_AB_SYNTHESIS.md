# The identification layer and Models A–B: November 22 synthesis preparation

**Read against research/theory/deployment_investment.md at the 18 November branch revision. This is a coherent bridge, NOT the accepted November 22 milestone or a new core model.**

Model A's \(G(a,c,K)=\max_{0\le q\le K}[(a-c)q-bq^2/2]\) and \(\rho\max(0,G-\Pi_0)\) instantiate one concave customer accepted-service value/capture schedule. The newer \(f(Q;\omega)\) is the more general qualified production/recipe envelope under resource and customer demand limits. For a **single** complete service recipe with divisible output and no additional shared bottleneck, one can specialize \(f\) to Model A's captured contribution; multi-recipe complementarities, discrete whole orders and finite operator caps need the richer formulation. No sum of provider fee, consumer gain and production cash may be counted twice.

Model B's first deployment \(i\in\{H,L\}\) yields **observed deterministic** \((a_i,c_i,K)\) and collected net cash \(m_i\). Its binary capacity expansion is feasible subject to \(B+m_i+f\ge I\) and repayment; its value \(m_i+\rho S_i(K)+\max\{0,\rho[S_i(K+\Delta)-S_i(K)]-I\}\) was only stated under feasible expansion. The robust theory changes **one element** first: acquired data, target grounding and embodiment move the qualification/cost/recipe state through a *joint controlled latent-regime kernel*, rather than a known shift \(c_i\); reports and recovery load are action-dependent. A single-policy realization with a degenerate identified set and ample operator capacity reduces to the known-transition Model B logic once timing/cash assumptions match. The newer expected terminal \(f\) is not an extra strategic option above Model B's \(\rho S\).

The prior synthetic Model B path ranking (H cash 8, L cash 2; credit cap 1 reverses preferred path due L's assumed cost/access benefit) is **not re-estimated** by the robust diagnostic illustrations. Its original payment feasibility and the counterfactual qualified specialist stay intact. With uncertain transfer it is no longer enough to plug an *average* \(c_L\) into a concave payoff: learning can change which complete bundles are qualified, its observation affects future choices, and a low-probability recovery may eliminate the route under a hard resource cap. Conversely, replacing uncertainty with a worst-case regime at every stage can understate value of learning about one persistent regime.

The shared interpretation is: current qualified accepted production and cash (Models A/B) enable or displace learning and capacity acquisition; selected source/target data alter future qualified production distributions; customers and scarce resources, including operator recovery, determine realized incremental contribution; contracts/cash dictate financing without creating additional aggregate output. Competitive specialist, retained old policy, demand expiry and changing markets must remain menu options.

**November 22 decision tests still needed:** (1) write one canonical notation linking \(a,c,K,Q,\omega\) and actor-specific capture; (2) reconcile production before/after learning without additive double-counting; (3) compare Model B's deterministic known-transition prediction against persistent-regime ambiguity and a safe matched test; (4) articulate the strongest prior-art objection to the combined claim; (5) identify one real audited accepted-service unit and industry production matrix route. None is passed by writing this note. The first-paper novelty remains unselected.


## 18 November continuation — canonical notation, population transport and demand-dependent capture

This extends the same November 22 **unpassed** synthesis, rather than opening another model. The intended single argument now uses four objects:

1. **Qualified physical configuration** \(Q_t\), containing body, retained policies, safety-validated recipes, eligible source experience/rights, and available certified human/operator resources. Eligibility is not a benchmark percentage.
2. **Customer demand/operating state** \(\omega_t\), including service opportunity, order/acceptance definition, price and contract capture, competitor/specialist alternatives, physical and organizational resource capacities, and time window.
3. **Fixed-configuration optimized operating contribution** \(V(Q_t;\omega_t)=\max_{y\in{\cal Y}(Q_t,\omega_t)}\operatorname{captured\ net\ accepted\ production}(y)\). The incremental data/qualification value is \(f(A;Q_t,\omega_t)=V(Q_t\cup A;\omega_t)-V(Q_t;\omega_t)\), with old policies in the menu and joint bundle feasibility respected.
4. **Policy-dependent complete history** under action \(\pi\): customer orders and retained production, source observations/rights and corrections, learned qualified body/control recipes, reports, peak operator resource use, and acceptance outcomes; latent transfer regime \(\theta\) persists where specified. \(J(\pi,\theta)\) discounts contemporaneous \(V\) or actually collected cash according to a clearly chosen actor and terminal \(V\), subtracting learning/embodiment costs once. Do **not** add an independent future-data dollar premium or accidentally include the same accepted service in both \(m_t\) and \(V_t\).

**Model A exact embedding.** For one eligible divisible service, one constrained customer, no shared multi-recipe bottleneck, and a two-part tariff, \(V(Q;\omega)=\rho\max\{0,G(a,c,K(Q))-\Pi_0\}\), where \(G=\max_{0\le q\le K}[(a-c)q-bq^2/2]\). An extra qualified capacity unit's incremental contribution is the difference of these positive-part operating surpluses, **not** an empirically assumed robotic hourly wage. \(a,c,b,K,\Pi_0,\rho\) are actor/window-specific and cannot be transported across customer mix without conditions.

**Model B exact degenerate transition.** Take \(T=1\), two known initial deployments \(i\), deterministically attained \(a_i,c_i,K\), collected \(m_i\), and only funded/repayable \(x\in\{0,1\}\) expansion. Then \(J_i=m_i+\rho S_i(K)+\max_{x\text{ feasible}}\{x[\rho(S_i(K+\Delta)-S_i(K))-I]\}\) at discount one. The original example (H 10.4 vs L 9.2 without credit, H 10.4 vs L 11.8 with credit) remains intact. General ambiguity does **not** retroactively alter these synthetic facts; its known-transition limit reproduces them **only when** service timing, financing and actor capture match. In general \(J\) uses *endogenous* joint qualification and recovery resource outcomes, and the deterministic calculation cannot be reused with averaged \(c_i\) or a scalar success multiplier.

**Transport into the same envelope.** A special site × window × order stratum \(z\) with one extra full qualified bundle of incremental captured value \(\Delta_z\), one incumbent contribution \(b_z\) preserved only under routine R, and real incremental difference \(k_z\) yields \(J_\mu(R)-J_\mu(E)=\sum_z\mu_z[b_z-k_z-\Delta_z(q_E(z)-q_R(z))]\). This is a restricted form of \(J\), not an independent paper-specific payoff. Full proof and sharp coverage comparison: [TRANSPORT_AND_SAFETY_IDENTIFICATION.md](TRANSPORT_AND_SAFETY_IDENTIFICATION.md). The earlier hypothetical *pooled* paired difference bound is insufficient for a different demand/site distribution; when target order strata have valid conditional bounds \(q_E-q_R\le u_z\), the lower economic certificate is \(\sum_z\mu_z(b_z-k_z-\Delta_zu_z)\). Missing target support and demand changes can reverse the ranking without changing any individual technical skill rate.

**Source/target learning map.** Denote source corpus/experience \(x\), target alignment/grounding \(g\), embodiment \(h\), and qualified action set \(Q(x,g,h;\theta)\) after validation. Technical transfer is a property of the controlled learning and evidence kernel, **not** a uniform fixed coefficient or guaranteed customer price. The original DDA task accumulation and planner already internalize future data value, so our enterprise-level addition is the measured/modeled accepted-service and physical-support interface, not the notion that data have option value.

**Fixed-regime and nonrectangular warning.** Reports update a posterior/identified set over the same persistent \(\theta\). A worst-world difference compares two feasible complete-history policies in *one* \(\theta\). A rectangular transition adversary after every report solves a different problem; no new robust Bellman solution is claimed. An intervention can change visited states, report support, qualified production, and future customer demand, violating both a static same-unit paired contrast and the exogenous target weights needed for the simplified formula. That is the next precise theoretical gap.

**What has been improved before the Nov 22 gate.** There is now one explicit \(Q,\omega,V,J\) map with two exact reductions, a transport counterexample under site selection, a demand-only reversal, a missing commercial-coverage threshold, and a report-conditional safety countermodel. The missing quantitative production/value-chain source, empirical accepted-service panel, independently certified recovery support, definitive first-paper novelty, full paper consistency review and external validation still prevent passing the gate.
