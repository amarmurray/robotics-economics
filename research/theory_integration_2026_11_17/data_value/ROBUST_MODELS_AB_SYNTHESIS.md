# The identification layer and Models A–B: November 22 synthesis preparation

**Read against research/theory/deployment_investment.md at the 18 November branch revision. This is a coherent bridge, NOT the accepted November 22 milestone or a new core model.**

Model A's \(G(a,c,K)=\max_{0\le q\le K}[(a-c)q-bq^2/2]\) and \(\rho\max(0,G-\Pi_0)\) instantiate one concave customer accepted-service value/capture schedule. The newer \(f(Q;\omega)\) is the more general qualified production/recipe envelope under resource and customer demand limits. For a **single** complete service recipe with divisible output and no additional shared bottleneck, one can specialize \(f\) to Model A's captured contribution; multi-recipe complementarities, discrete whole orders and finite operator caps need the richer formulation. No sum of provider fee, consumer gain and production cash may be counted twice.

Model B's first deployment \(i\in\{H,L\}\) yields **observed deterministic** \((a_i,c_i,K)\) and collected net cash \(m_i\). Its binary capacity expansion is feasible subject to \(B+m_i+f\ge I\) and repayment; its value \(m_i+\rho S_i(K)+\max\{0,\rho[S_i(K+\Delta)-S_i(K)]-I\}\) was only stated under feasible expansion. The robust theory changes **one element** first: acquired data, target grounding and embodiment move the qualification/cost/recipe state through a *joint controlled latent-regime kernel*, rather than a known shift \(c_i\); reports and recovery load are action-dependent. A single-policy realization with a degenerate identified set and ample operator capacity reduces to the known-transition Model B logic once timing/cash assumptions match. The newer expected terminal \(f\) is not an extra strategic option above Model B's \(\rho S\).

The prior synthetic Model B path ranking (H cash 8, L cash 2; credit cap 1 reverses preferred path due L's assumed cost/access benefit) is **not re-estimated** by the robust diagnostic illustrations. Its original payment feasibility and the counterfactual qualified specialist stay intact. With uncertain transfer it is no longer enough to plug an *average* \(c_L\) into a concave payoff: learning can change which complete bundles are qualified, its observation affects future choices, and a low-probability recovery may eliminate the route under a hard resource cap. Conversely, replacing uncertainty with a worst-case regime at every stage can understate value of learning about one persistent regime.

The shared interpretation is: current qualified accepted production and cash (Models A/B) enable or displace learning and capacity acquisition; selected source/target data alter future qualified production distributions; customers and scarce resources, including operator recovery, determine realized incremental contribution; contracts/cash dictate financing without creating additional aggregate output. Competitive specialist, retained old policy, demand expiry and changing markets must remain menu options.
