## Selection versus production, and report timing
Let N original eligible orders, S successful under a frozen incumbent, and K orders removed by a proposed dispatch screen, X of which would otherwise succeed. Conditional on the realized paired cohort and no interference, the new robot success fraction among admitted orders is (S-X)/(N-K). It strictly exceeds S/N iff X/K<S/N (cross multiply), while original robot completions weakly fall by X. With only marginal N,S,K known, X lies in [max(0,K-(N-S)), min(S,K)]; every integer in this interval is attained by selecting X successes and K-X failures. This is a sharp counting result, not causal evidence for a particular threshold. Original customer completion can nevertheless rise if separately feasible fallback or delayed service converts otherwise unsuccessful orders. Let F_i be the net value of that replacement action including its occupancy and actual completion, R_i incumbent net value on removed orders, and L the change in distinct discounted future licensed production across the whole cluster. Fixed unchanged retained orders imply delta J=sum_removed(F_i-R_i)+L-c, with incremental program acquisition c. If retained orders or the shared resource change, use the full policy contrast rather than this identity. Do not count released labor and the same downstream production twice.

For a single safely admissible original request with current lawful report H, define action returns Z_a=r_a+beta E[V(next)|full state,a], where r_a contains accepted service and actual operating costs once. All actions are feasible for every full state consistent with H (otherwise restrict menu to robustly feasible actions). A retrospective report F refining H gives the relaxed oracle value E[max_a E[Z_a|F]], versus lawful E[max_a E[Z_a|H]]. By the tower property and convexity of max, oracle >= lawful; choose an H-measurable incumbent maximizer to prove directly. Equality in the finite case iff an H-optimal action is also F-optimal almost surely within each H atom (nonnegative regret has zero expectation iff it is zero). Hold continuation functions fixed at a valid baseline or compare full policy sets if future information changes. This is standard value-of-information mathematics, not novelty. The novel candidate application remains the joint data-to-qualified-production comparison under lawful report timing.

Synthetic illustration: equal-probability uncongested and congested states, dispatch returns 2 and -1, safe manual fallback .5 in either. If the state is only known after dispatch, both actions have expected .5. If perfectly observed beforehand, optimum 1.25. Identical retrospective labels and success rates can therefore imply a zero deployable signal improvement or .75 gross information value. If safe failed attempts generate distinct licensed future net production worth 2 (already discounted), the congested dispatch return rises to 1 and optimal dispatch in both states eliminates the gross information value. Never interpret this as permission to expose patients or workers to unsafe attempts: unsafe dispatch excluded before optimization. In a real site the continuation number must be estimated from separately followed source policies, not imputed from observed failure count. An optional old policy remains available; costly forced training or compulsory screening can destroy value.

## Discriminating design and evidence synthesis
Prior Robin/Vulcan/LWD/CEL results establish different technical and resource denominators; this study adds actual original medication request exclusion and physical handoff plus a documented accompanying human. They do not jointly identify a data investment return. Compare P routine logging and lawful dispatch, G extra grounding/forecasting, and E changed embodiment or access, with an independent model and elevator/worker interference catchment per arm, or account for spillovers in a staggered cluster design. Record predecision forecast timestamp, realized congestion separately, original request urgency/deadline, rejected/queued orders, actual receipt and fallback, paid human escort/waiting clocks, downstream work and rights, learning lineage and acquisition costs. For the null, inexpensive routine traffic observations may yield the same lawful forecast and qualified service as expensive G; compare complete histories, not ROC or admitted-task success. Test whether G improves original timely acceptance and valued occupation relative to P before extrapolating to more sites.
