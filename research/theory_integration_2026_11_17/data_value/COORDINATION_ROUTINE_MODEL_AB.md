# Coordination, routine exposure and the economic marginal value of grounding
24 November 2026 Eastern. Integration of [focused evidence](FOCUSED_FIRST_PAPER_EMPIRICAL_SECTION.md) with [policy-closed data value](POLICY_CLOSED_DATA_VALUE.md), not a separate paper. Candidate mechanism, not empirical calibration.

## Model A/B bridge: a coordination rival is not necessarily a substitute
Model A evaluates actually admitted, accepted service with dated capacity and original customer demand. Model B chooses complete programs that also determine the law of future qualification. Add C: a fixed, nonlearning coordination-module change (e.g. dispatch/batching), with the incumbent routine production/reuse pipeline retained. C can change both resource occupancy and which trajectories that pipeline collects. Calling C "coordination-only" means no deliberate source acquisition or change to the learning algorithm, NOT freezing the ensuing data or controller histories. A frozen-controller C arm can separately diagnose the immediate physical mechanism but is not the economically relevant whole-program rival.

Use the same J and cost accounting. P is the retained routine program; G adds targeted grounding, C adds coordination, CG adds both when lawful, financeable and jointly feasible. Retain optional old policies; compare actual incremental costs, not four forced arm returns mislabeled optimal values. For every lawful G witness, if a feasible C/P emulator can attain its qualified paid histories with gross loss bounded by epsilon, the policy-closed stopping certificate gives V_G - V_C <= epsilon - (c_G-c_C). This is conditional on uniform emulation; matching eventual controller scores cannot certify it. Conversely, dominance of C over G alone does not imply that CG is unprofitable. The empirical test needs the marginal contrast CG-C as well as G-P.

## Exact restricted two-date model
At date 0 C can release an additional productive exposure opportunity (licensed learning inputs may be field trajectories, failures/recoveries or other modalities; no privileged human ontology). At date 1 one original target order can be safely accepted for fixed net contribution v>0 if H=1 (authorization, deadline and payment), qualification and resource feasibility all hold. L indicates baseline routine readiness; T indicates additional readiness attainable by G; S indicates additional routine readiness attainable from C's incremental production at date 0. W0 denotes baseline usable resource capacity at date 1; W1>=W0 denotes usable capacity under C. All indicators are binary and jointly distributed on a common original-order law. Realized qualification/resources are lawfully observed before dispatch; zero contribution from declining is always admissible. All indicators incorporate rights and certification. Assume no policy-dependent buyer response, no other competing services at date 1, no interference between G and the C-induced source pipeline, no unmodeled rework or delays, no residual terminal value, beta=1. W1 may reflect independently freed robot or worker capacity, not an automatic conversion of aggregate saved minutes. Early receipts and committed costs are additive across the two modules; write the net early C term a and the incremental G cost k. Include routine acquisition, processing and verification costs in the appropriate terms; if their cost depends on joint use the interaction below must include that difference. This is a special case of A/B, not a universal behavioral assumption.

For c,g in {0,1}, date-1 qualification is L OR (c AND S) OR (g AND T); capacity is W_c. Model A chooses the profitable target ticket iff H and both gates hold, hence

    J(c,g) = a c - k g + v E[H W_c (L OR cS OR gT)].

Let R=H(1-L)T, the original-order population for which deliberate grounding is both relevant and potentially sufficient. With binary indicators, the gross incremental benefit of G at given c equals v E[R W_c (1-cS)]. Thus

    J(0,1)-J(0,0) = v E[R W0]-k,
    J(1,1)-J(1,0) = v E[R W1(1-S)]-k.

Subtract and expand W1(1-S)-W0:

    interaction = v E[R (W1-W0)(1-S)] - v E[R W0 S].        (1)

This exact decomposition has two disjoint economic mechanisms. C complements G by opening a usable target capacity slot where routine learning still fails; it substitutes for G where routine learning now qualifies an already feasible service. Additional exposure that qualifies a target when capacity was absent and remains absent has zero marginal value here. No need to posit positive or negative technical cross-derivatives. This is a signed difference of conditional economic incidences, not an additive data premium. C's own contemporaneous production contribution cancels in the interaction. If G alters C's earlier exposure, demand or resource trajectory, replace this identity by the complete common-law J comparison; a cross-difference in other net costs must also be included.

## Sharp evidence requirement
Conditional on R=1 let a0=Pr(W0=1|R), b=Pr(W1=1|R), s=Pr(S=1|R), rho=Pr(R=1)>0. Set j=Pr(W1=1,S=1|R). W0<=W1 implies 0<=a0<=b. The conditional normalized interaction is b-a0-j. Given only these marginals, sharp bounds are

    v rho [b-a0-min(b,s), b-a0-max(0,b+s-1)].               (2)

Proof: W1 and S form a binary joint table with j in [max(0,b+s-1),min(b,s)]. Every admissible table can be extended by selecting W0 as a subset of W1 of mass a0 (split atoms as necessary), so no extra restriction on j follows from W0 nesting. Equation (1) proves the linear transformation of the interval; its endpoints are attained. R=0 yields zero. These are partial-identification bounds conditional on the structural common-law assumptions, not bounds from separate commercial arm aggregates without those assumptions.

The useful economic audit is the paired overlap of C-induced timely routine readiness with actual usable capacity in the original grounding-relevant customer population. A target-ready metric on all C trajectories, or aggregate freed capacity in another period, cannot establish j. Measuring the two mechanism terms in (1) separately diagnoses why the sign changes. A valid matched independent four-program cluster comparison can instead identify the economic interaction directly without measuring this cross-world latent R, and is preferred if feasible; it does not alone identify the mechanistic decomposition. Do not impose paired latent variables as observed facts.

## Counterexample and full-menu choice
Eight equally likely histories, R=1, W0 true on four and W1 on six, S true on three. Set v=8, k=4, net early C contribution a=0 (equal incremental early receipts and C costs, no duplicate displacement). Keep all marginal technical and capacity evidence fixed. World 1 places one S history in W1 and two outside; world 2 places all three S histories in W1. Then a0=1/2,b=3/4,s=3/8, j=1/8 vs 3/8. The four complete program values (P,G,C,CG) are (0,0,1,2) vs (0,0,3,2). Optional G alone breaks even; G after C pays +1 or -1. The optimal choice in the menu reverses between CG and C. Additional source X and independently staffed E can be appended as complete alternative programs, not duplicate sales; for example fixed net values 1/2 and 3/2 do not change the reversal, while a stronger rival can dominate both. All quantities are synthetic. In particular neither Amazon's reported distance saving nor its cost estimate identifies any of these probabilities. Pure C which releases capacity but no extra licensed learning (S=0) yields a weakly positive interaction; C that changes only learning (W1=W0) yields a weakly negative one. These distinct information/transition structures test more than a parameter grid.

## Prospective discriminating validation
Use the [compact original-order protocol](COMPACT_WHOLE_PROGRAM_VALIDATION.md). Where authorized and safe, assign independent interference catchments to P, G, C, CG before exposure, with a common baseline model and identical eligible customer universe and timing definitions; avoid pooling subsequent controller checkpoints across arms. Version scheduler, model, physical substrate, source and target rights, actual training/verification costs and accepted original-order receipts. Track paired dated utilization and qualification opportunities, not only mean distance per pick or eventual success. Capture the C-only frozen-controller diagnostic separately if feasible, and do not call it the complete routine program. Record independently measured cost differences and alternative X/E complete programs if in scope. No experiment, private customer access or approval is asserted. Fixed-program arm means identify intention-to-treat program returns under independent randomized catchments and adequate follow-up, not oracle-optimal V nor latent cross-arm overlaps. Use paired structural audits or signed conservative envelopes only where justified. Without independent full-program data this remains unidentified.
