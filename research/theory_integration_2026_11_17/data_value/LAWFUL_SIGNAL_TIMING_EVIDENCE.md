# Original-buyer causal evidence ledger: timing the lawful signal versus capital choice

**22 November 2026. Prospective, unpopulated. Not a field dataset.** Companion to [lawful signal and recursive data option](LAWFUL_SIGNAL_TIMING_AND_RECURSIVE_DATA_OPTION.md).

## Unit, factor and legal support

The connected assignment cluster is the union of original buyers/site/time windows, robot/controller lineage, checkpoint broadcast and rollback, operator/rescue queues, data-training permissions, E manufacturing and service capacity, and later customer-order feedback. Define **preprogram** Z or a stable validated Z proxy; if the alleged regime is affected by treatment or depends on observing a successful rollout, the common-factor model is false. Separate realized cluster Z reports from uncertainty about the underlying prevalence theta across clusters. Treat an unlicensed/customer-confidential signal as prohibited, not missing numeric data.

Capture Y timestamp, accuracy/likelihood calibration within Z, whether Y arrives before manufacturing/program commitment or only before later dispatch, who may lawfully see it, source provenance, costs and delays. A higher predictive q is not evidence of higher paid production: records must show *what decision actually changes*. Full buyer ledger: eligible offer **and no offer**, outside option, deadlines/unserved demand, independently approved qualified safe recipe, robot readiness, simultaneous supervision/recovery resources, accepted output, refund, settlement and cleared net receipt.

## Prospective interventions and identifying assumptions

| Claim / estimand | Distinguishing original-buyer comparison | Identification blocker |
|---|---|---|
| Early gross report W_early-W_none | Matched connected clusters, preassignment report and ex-ante P/D/SG/E commitments with same customer universe; account for unserved buyer orders | No verified licensable Y, report experiment, approved arms or buyer ledger |
| Program-locked W_late | Commitment randomized before lawful Y arrival; within-program adaptive controller qualification observed | No signed program commitment / conditional lawful policies |
| Value of source-produced versus deliberate data | Safe original-customer paid field activity vs separate teleop/sim/synthetic/curation, with full current and future paid service and costs | No matched source lineage, Q joint outcomes or paid no-offer data |
| SG target-grounding reuse | Same preexisting source data, authorized random target signal/grounding, held-out joint qualification and signed service receipts | No rights/target-grounding panel |
| E truly independent capacity | Same simultaneous buyer orders, independent available chassis and independent recovery/operator resources | No independent E physical throughput and rescue-capacity evidence |
| Shared factor robustness | Cluster-wide exogenous preassignment Z, conditional whole-network learner/offer/recovery returns under every supported assignment | No stable Z or positive whole-policy assignment support |
| Bayesian vs maximin criterion | Declare predictive factor distribution and θ uncertainty set **before** outcomes; record chosen decision rule and regret criterion | No justified theta set or credible source evidence |

Randomization, if ever approved, must be whole-connected-cluster and ethically/legal safe, with no external publication or private customer access under existing authority. Exposure mapping must track cross-cluster shared model broadcast, retries, teleoperation occupancy and rights. If positivity fails for an SG/E or sequential action, report an **unidentified** value rather than an extrapolated sign. Retained old R is a permitted alternative only where actually available. Preserve false negatives from failures and recoveries; successful episodes are selected observations.

## Falsifiers before any dollar conclusion

The exogenous report formula is wrong if observing Y itself reallocates a rescue operator, changes an offer, alters model checkpoint/qualification, imposes buyer delay, or is conditional on posttreatment success. The two-regime structure is wrong if conditional buyer/learning/recovery responses cannot be represented by one exogenous Z across arms or when supposedly independent clusters share a checkpoint. The capacity claims fail if E shares the allegedly eliminated bottleneck. Cost thresholds fail if measured incremental contributions, curation, procurement lead time or legal rights differ. The accuracy threshold is **not** a performance threshold on a task benchmark. Actual BLS sector output cannot substitute for unknown official interindustry use/supply quantitative coefficients.
