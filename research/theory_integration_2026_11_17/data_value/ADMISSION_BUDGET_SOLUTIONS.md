# Instructor solutions — admission and economically useful learning

Keep separate from [student exercises](ADMISSION_BUDGET_EXERCISES.md). Conditional standard probability/decision results; synthetic examples only.

1. The unweighted episode fraction is (1+0)/2=1/2, while the transition-weighted fraction is (1·1+9·0)/10=1/10. For equal proposals across S,T, R=(1/2)(1/10)+(1/2)(1)=11/20, admitted q_S=((1/2)(1/10))/R=1/11. Erroneously using 1/2 gives q_S=1/3. It also loses the fact that one sampled language can be broadcast to a whole episode's transitions.
2. At fixed B: E[updates]=BR and E[cost]=B c_p+BR c_u. At K accepted: E[proposals]=K/R; E[cost]=K c_p/R+K c_u. With source-specific proposal costs replace c_p with Σw_s c_s. These rely on iid proposals and stationary R; episode repeated states, batched decoding/real scheduling and variable accepted-step costs require separate accounting. Source-s accepted exposure expectations are B w_s r_s or K w_s r_s/R respectively.
3. Write R'=Σw_jr_j'. Then q_s'=w_s r_s'/R-(w_s r_s/R)(R'/R). This remains defined at r_s=0 if R>0 and differentiability holds. If r_s stays zero, w_s r_s=0 regardless of weight; alternate goal-image recipes and lawful annotation may change r rather than w. A label flag does not measure economically meaningful transfer.
4. By Fréchet, intersection P(A∩B) lies in [0,1/2], so union lies in [1/2,1]. Perfect positive coupling yields union 1/2, independence 3/4 and negative coupling 1. At return 4, expected source value is 2,3 or4; E's 5/2 beats positive coupling while source beats it under independence or anticorrelation. All effects are stipulated conditional qualification/production, not monetary inference from real benchmarks.
5. Let old feasible policy set P_0 and newly available options P_1 with P_0⊆P_1 and unchanged payoffs for old policies. Then sup_{π∈P_1} J(π)≥sup_{π∈P_0} J(π). Forced training, rights/compute obligations, altered old policy outcomes and irreversible capital can violate the nesting premise; none follow from free **optional** archive existence.
