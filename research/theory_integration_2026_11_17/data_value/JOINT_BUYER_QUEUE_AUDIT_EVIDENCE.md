# Prospective original-buyer/worker audit ledger: no observed data

**22 November 2026.** Companion to [the joint-buyer/queue audit theorem](JOINT_BUYER_QUEUE_AUDIT_AND_TIMING.md). This is a **research design only**. No customer, robot, employee, confidential firm data, authorized trial, signed right, payment, independently validated E, or empirical rate was accessed. None of the rational example's entries represent measured frequencies.

## Original qualified-service episode unit

The unit is an **original buyer's eligible opportunity in a linked service cluster**, not a selected robot-invoice, logged simulator success or a benchmark trial. A cluster includes its actual recovery workers, all robots contending for those workers, the original buyers they could service and lawful learner-broadcast boundaries. Cross-cluster pooling is permissible only if absence of shared capacity/learning/customer interference is defended. Use **all eligible no-orders and unserved orders in the denominators**, with an event-time clock.

### What can and cannot be inferred

A **rights-cleared timestamped linked A,Z sample** on supported old policies can estimate the conditional triple \(\Pr(H=0,Y=1,Z=1)\) with appropriate action/report support, not necessarily its causal value under G or a changed source learner. A pairwise-aggregated worker dashboard, site-order conversion and unlinked late-order table leave this moment unidentified even if each is exact. If reports depend on late paid success or earlier adaptive dispatch, a selected-linkage statistic is not the population b; measure masks/denominators and use justified randomization or nonparametric bounds.

The example additionally assumes deterministic one-job duration for T, inherited H clearing before t3, a completely qualified T task earning an original buyer's accepted net 3/4, *unchanged* terminal buyer demand across P/G, G bundle uplift k=1/2, G all-in cost 1/32 and independently resourced E net 9/16. These are **unverified causal/contractual assumptions**, not default imputations. Failure of any one requires a larger common-law potential-history set; independent arm-specific mean rates do not repair it.

### Rights-feasible decision audit candidates

- **A:** obtain only lawful linkage for A and terminal Z on all eligible original-buyer episodes, including blocked/no-order cases; compare full joint cells to the existing pairwise tables. Priced by the change in the P/G/E economic rank set, not generic label entropy.
- **B:** audit already permissible grounder/service qualification and original buyer settlements *under G*, if supported; otherwise record action support zero, not a fabricated G action propensity. A does not replace B.
- **C:** validate under actually authorized deployment the queue duration and simultaneous robot/workers for T and E; identify dispatch's effect on service eligibility and later learner changes.
- **D:** where downstream propagation matters, identify the original production bottleneck, substitutable human capacity and attributable customer value at each site; only use official BEA/BLS input-output coefficients if genuine numbers are retrieved and versioned.

No audit is authorized by this ledger. Acquisition costs, legal permission, customer reporting agreements, experiment design, statistical precision and within-cluster sample size remain unknown.
