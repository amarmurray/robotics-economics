## 1. The production problem and the economic objects

A firm does not choose data volume alone. It chooses which jobs to serve with which embodiments, which outcomes to observe, and which paired or repeated comparisons can reveal a relation that makes the archive useful elsewhere. A parcel-handling deployment can supply many grips but little evidence about whether a gripper correction carries to a different body. A matched operation on a second body can extend the reach of a representation. A cross-check on a third body may instead discriminate a shared relation law from local coincidences. An inspection fleet may need independent ground truth rather than more images; a digital exception-routing system may need matched input/output records before and after a policy change, rather than more tickets. These are possible production mechanisms, not field estimates.

Distinguish source competence, relation state (including validity, direction, uncertainty and drift), evidence-generation technology, dated authorized qualification, accepted service and the allocation of subsequent capital. A deployment can jointly produce service and several kinds of evidence. Its net incremental cost must subtract its own real or captured current contribution, as appropriate, and include any displaced service, additional obligations, compute, validation, rights and delays. No free dual use of an occupied slot. Compare against the best ordinary deployment with its own learning and direct target reports, not a frozen nonlearning rival. A capable fixed contingent rule can implement all policies below; changing the available experiment technology or retaining a better procedure is a separate economic investment.

## 2. An exact allocation problem: test the relation or extend it?

Start after all ordinary lawful reports. Condition on a shared representation hypothesis H in a specified class, with posterior p. There is an already connected reference component in which an unused cycle test is possible. A bridge to a different component would make previously unmeasured recipient pairs reachable (exclude the directly measured bridge itself from the incremental payoff). Conditional on H the bridge establishes the relevant path signs; conditional on not H it conveys no information about those unmeasured pairs. Its signal has the same probability under both regimes, so the bridge does not update p. An audit has conditional probabilities q1,q0 for agreement under H and not H; let z=p q1+(1-p)q0. The two posteriors are p+=p q1/z and p-=p(1-q1)/(1-z) for positive-probability outcomes. The perfect-cycle benchmark has q1=1,q0=1/2. Imperfect q1 and q0 allow nondecisive evidence. This is a specified signal law, not an inference that cycles in arbitrary real datasets are independent.

After both experiments can finish, let V0 be the best expected discounted captured contribution from the ordinary feasible menu (including direct reports, ordinary learning, and existing qualified recipes). For this restriction V0 is independent of H; those recipes use no newly reachable relation. Let V1(p) be the best value with the bridge, retaining every ordinary policy. Define B(p)=V1(p)-V0>=0. A fixed policy has expected payoff affine in p under a fixed conditional law and identical feasible policy set, so V1 and B are convex. This does not require independent recipient qualifications; joint history policies must use the joint law. If the ordinary menu itself uses other p-dependent relations, replace V0 by V0(p); B then need not be convex. The subsequent option inequality still works if the only effect of the bridge is to expand that same dated menu.

Let b>=0 be the net incremental bridge cost; c>=0 the test cost, measured in the same discounted contribution units. In this restriction both orders of acquisition leave identical terminal dates, resources, information and acceptance menus; the audit is available without purchasing the bridge. Exclude any other informational role for the bridge. The incremental values of the four programs are

    stop: 0
    bridge only: B(p)-b
    audit then optional bridge: A=-c+E[(B(p')-b)_+]
    bridge then audit: BA=-b-c+E[B(p')].

The audit alone contributes no direct gain in this restriction because V0 is independent of H.

**Proposition 1 (topological investment complementarity and option order).** If B(p)<=b and c>0, neither experiment is profitable alone. Nevertheless an adaptive bundle is strictly profitable iff E[(B(p')-b)_+]>c. Its audit-first advantage over precommitting the bridge before the same test is

    A-BA=E[(b-B(p'))_+] >=0.

Proof: at each positive-probability posterior buy iff its incremental menu benefit exceeds its cost; expectation yields A. The identity follows by x_+ - x = (-x)_+. Compare all four values, including stop. In particular no greedy rule limited to positive standalone returns can find the strictly profitable bundle in the stipulated case. The ordering gain is the expected avoidable cost net of forgone bridge benefits, not a second learning premium.

If the bridge itself generates a test of H, if obtaining it first makes the audit possible, if a factory or customer deadline expires before the second order, or if early source operation pays additional margin or creates other evidence, the ordering dominance can fail: solve the two actual programs, including those benefits. If the audit also changes the ordinary menu, compare against its optimized posterior-contingent value, not the old V0. Where a reference operation is already part of routine work, subtract only avoidable extra costs and compare against the routine information available by the actual decision date.

## 3. A tractable qualified-order model and a demand-dependent threshold

For each newly reachable customer opportunity j, suppose a source report has reliability a_j (twice correctness less one) and a direct independent symmetric target report has reliability d_j. Under the restricted two-regime path law, the optimal equal-loss decision succeeds with Q_j=(1+max(a_j p,d_j))/2 with the bridge, and (1+d_j)/2 without. The graph observation changes a proposed decision; only a separately authorized complete synthetic verification admits a funded installation. The customer retains its ordinary fallback if verification rejects it. Discounted net contribution w_j>=0 is the incremental payoff of a verified success relative to that fallback, not an observed accuracy-to-dollar conversion. Subtract costs of failed reserved slots as applicable. Assume those fixed slot costs common and already netted, and enough committed resources for successful starts. Reserve up to K joint test/installation opportunities before local reports. V1 maximizes sum w_j Q_j across feasible subsets; V0 solves the same problem with direct reports. With heterogeneous requirements, multiple periods, or dependent customer orders use the general joint recipe schedule, not top K.

If all n reached target orders have common a>0, d and w, and no other project competes for the K slots, put W=w min(n,K). Then

    B(p)=(W/2)(a p-d)_+.

For the perfect cycle audit, p+=p/z, p-=0, z=(1+p)/2. Suppose ap<=d<ap/z. The standalone bridge has no value, yet the test-first bundle is profitable exactly when

    W > 2(b+c/z)/(a p/z-d).

This derives the threshold in economically usable reachable work, not the number of nodes. The same relation has different private returns with different customer demand, capture shares and qualification windows. A larger archive with higher a can make the bridge valuable; a superior direct report can remove the entire option. If d>=a p/z the audit plus bridge cannot improve a target decision. If no suitable order arrives before validation expires, W=0. If scarce validation is the active constraint, increasing the number of reachable orders beyond K does not increase W. More reach does not guarantee profitable scale.

For heterogeneous candidate orders with shared K, B is the difference of optimized menus; the sum of isolated gains may overstate it because those gains may apply to the same scarce slot. A second transfer class with already viable incremental return D>=0 (same scarce capacity, exclusive alternative program) changes the option value of probing the first class to E[max(D,B_A(p')-b,0)]-max(D,B_A(p)-b,0)-c. This follows by reoptimization, not a fixed class-centrality score. If classes share latent structure their posterior must update jointly, not independently. The option value is thus portfolio-conditional even when class beliefs are statistically independent.

Constructed witness: p=.4, a=1, d=.5, four identical orders of w=10, K=4, b=.8, c=.3. Audit agreement has probability .7; posterior becomes 4/7. B(p)=0, B(p+)=10/7. Audit-first net=.14; bridge-first with audit=-.10; bridge-only=-.8. With K=2 the best program stops; with q1=.9,q0=.5 the noisy test is not worth its cost; with captured w=7.5 rather than 10 it is also not worth buying. All keep the same ordinary direct reports. Under four independent original orders, the ordinary expected funded starts are 3; conditional on agreement and bridge purchase, 22/7, and after contradiction 3. Thus ex ante expected recipient starts are 3.1, and the bridge purchase (if a distinct source robot) occurs with probability .7. These are counts in a synthetic verification menu; no actual robot deployment inferred. With a cheap external direct test, reoptimize the ordinary and bridge menus: if it delivers the same decisions for lower total cost, the specific bridge option loses its economic advantage.

## 4. Why the firm may need repeated references, not more sites

The preceding exact sign model has a sharp shared-vs-local distinction. A softer model reveals a separate identification problem. Let each context i have an independent uniform latent label Z_i in {-1,+1}. Let the true relation on edge ij be M_ij=Z_i Z_j U_ij, where U is an independent persistent edge-specific deviation with mean m in [0,1]. A reading R_ij=M_ij E_ij has independent sign error E of mean n in [0,1]. Across distinct edges, U and E are independent conditional on labels. The single-reading joint law on any graph depends only on t=mn because U E is itself an independent sign of mean t. Thus arbitrary graph coverage with one reading per edge cannot separate imperfect composability (m) from imperfect measurement (n). In a simple cycle of length l, E[product R]=t^l by cancellation of vertex labels. A triangle identifies t in this homogeneous nonnegative restriction.

For a path of length l in an observed forest, the posterior mean of the unmeasured TRUE chord relation is m t^l times the observed path product. To see this, the uniform labels induce independent fair true label-products along forest edges; after each noisy reading their posterior means are t R. Their product gives the endpoint label product; independent unmeasured deviation contributes m. Hence identical historical single-reading laws can imply different unmeasured transfer success. The distinction matters if the firm's decision uses an actual target relation rather than predicting the next noisy reading.

**Proposition 2 (reference identification).** Two independent readings of the SAME persistent true relation have E[R1 R2]=n^2, because M^2=1. With n>0, repeated readings and an identified t yield n=sqrt(E[R1 R2]), m=t/n. Repeating the measurement of the same stable object is not another independent cycle test. If the relation drifts between readings by an independent sign of mean rho, the repeat moment instead becomes rho n^2; without known rho, the proposed recovery fails. Correlated sensor errors or nonhomogeneous relations also invalidate it. A retained older configuration, external benchmark, or a timely independent reference can supply a stable comparison; a high-volume site fleet without those controls may not.

Example: (m,n)=(.5,.8) and (.8,.5) give t=.4 and identical distributions of all single-edge readings. With a two-edge forest path, unmeasured true relation reliability is .08 vs .128; repeat agreement sign moment .64 vs .25. A direct substitute with reliability .10 can dominate inferred transfer in the first world and not the second for a perfectly observed source bit and equal loss. The data do not justify choosing either world. This is a bounded measurement-identification result, not a claim that real cross-embodiment transfer is binary, stationary, or isotropic. The joint law of multiple recipient decisions remains necessary. The prior exact-graph p and soft-model m are different objects; do not equate them.

## 5. From topology to MPK_r, endogenous demand and recursive structure

Let the firm's state include K (installed embodiment/site capacities), D (lawful source histories), P (joint relation beliefs, with separate classes), Q (qualified recipe set), H (support and commissioning), W_cash (dated cash), and g (governance/experiment technology). For action u, the observation law O(Y|s,u) changes because production selects which tasks, incidents and paired/repeated comparisons occur. The subsequent menu Q' depends on D', P', hardware, training, independent validation and rights. Relation evidence is a productive input to that map; it does not by itself manufacture a capability. The firm's Bellman value is the captured contribution of actual production net of costs plus discounted continuation; the staged option derived above is already part of that continuation. A physical MPK column is the vector of finite differences of accepted output when one specified installed body is added at fixed qualified state and dispatch is reoptimized. An ex ante private body return includes all the resulting state changes and acquisition cost once. The source bridge may have low current MPK but positive dynamic private return because it enables an otherwise missing relation; another high-throughput body may be redundant for this information law.

For a recipient with qualified rate A_j and original service demand D_j, accepted service y_j=min(D_j,A_j K_j,H_j) in the single-stage restriction. Within the machine-binding regime physical MPK equals A_j; otherwise it is zero for an infinitesimal addition. Relation information can change the selected qualified recipe and thus A_j; it need not raise the MPK of every body, because demand or support may already bind. Compare finite programs at new entry and bottleneck changes. The dated funded-start response is limited by eligible demand, contribution threshold, cash and commissioning. An audit or bridge that changes only the date of an otherwise inevitable release accelerates starts; a customer expiry or tooling freeze can change the eventual set. The example's .1 incremental expected funded starts applies to its single expiring window, not a long-run multiplier.

The first-order induced deployment column now depends on which experiment the source makes feasible: it can create a bridge, generate a diagnostic chord, separate persistent from transient error, or displace an existing high-value evidence stream. A prospective additional recipient installation is itself a source of later paired comparisons, support demand, collected cash and component orders. At date t the response of the full controlled transition T(s)=F(s,mu(s)) has derivative F_s+F_u Dmu where smooth; for a discrete bridge switch use two whole-program trajectories. The production, learning and cash routes may lead to different firms. In an upstream equipment market, incremental target starts imply orders according to the actual BOM and available inventory; in a downstream workflow, additional accepted units may release labor or relax a bottleneck; shared technicians can instead crowd out another stage. These are mappings conditional on the real network, not universal positive spillovers. Include collected margin in the appropriate payer's next-period cash state (with actual settlement and capital cost), not all customer surplus. An increase in current throughput can lower the supply of relation-revealing incidents; the shared-hour reallocation result on main is the corresponding signed allocation channel. No fixed spectral growth claim applies to this signed, switching network.

The organizational implication is specific: an integrated manufacturer/model/deployer can choose a paired experiment, preserve a stable vintage and change the next design if it controls the required dates, rights and staff. A partner with equivalent timely access can replicate the feasible policy. The minimum viable stack is the minimal resource/right combination that can meet the service and revision promise, not a compulsory list of owned layers. In this model the maximum incremental willingness to buy a more capable experiment/authority package is the value difference of the best dated feasible programs; compare it with the full setup, overhead and exit costs. When a portable repeated-reference procedure becomes available externally, the premium for ownership of the reference operation can fall, even as the value of the model archive rises. Conversely drift can revive the need for a reference cell. A recursively revisable inquiry technology must earn a premium over a capable fixed contingent rule with equal information and budget; no automatic autonomy advantage is proved by the present allocation result.

## 6. Competition and the market for a structure report

The same audit can serve several lawful beneficiaries. In the nonstrategic disjoint-resource restriction, firm f with its own bridge opportunity B_f and net cost b_f has willingness to pay E[(B_f(p')-b_f)_+]-max(B_f(p)-b_f,0), before an audit fee. A common report can cover avoidable cost c via nonnegative participation payments if their sum reaches c (with individual contributions no greater than willingness). This follows by the same constructive allocation of payments as in the prior chapter; it is not a noncooperative funding equilibrium. For example, split the four-order witness into two firms each with two slots and b_f=.4; each has gross audit willingness .22, so neither sponsors .3 alone but the two can cover it with .15 each. If only two slots in total exist and each firm's bridge is separate, at most one two-order bridge contributes in the favorable branch under the stipulated exclusive allocation, making gross joint benefit .22<.3. Adding separate willingness amounts would be incorrect. If only the report is shared, but individual validation or action competes, solve joint resource allocation. Competition can reduce captured w without changing actual real w; the preceding example loses private sponsorship when w falls from 10 to 7.5 even though real bundle net stays .14. Conversely business stealing can increase private willingness beyond real gain, as in the prior strategic qualification chapter. No new competitive equilibrium is claimed: supplier choice, rival entry, joint qualification dependence and public-vs-private signal correlation are unresolved beyond the earlier explicit games. A priced independent local report changes the outside menu and can substitute for a shared report.

## 7. Evidence and falsification

Primary technical anchors (consulted November 25 Eastern): Yang, Finn and Sadigh, [Data Analogies Enable Efficient Cross-Embodiment Transfer, arXiv:2603.06450v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06450v2), revised March 20 2026, abstract distinguishes appearance/viewpoint variation from morphology and reports benefits from paired demonstrations for morphology. Sridhar et al., [Cross-Embodiment Transfer via Behavior-Aligned Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27549), July 30 2026 abstract and introduction, motivate explicitly testing embodiment-invariant representations and report transfer experiments with limited target data. Neither establishes the binary relation law, the economic threshold or any commercial return. Dawid and Skene (1979) [observer error estimation](https://doi.org/10.2307/2346806), abstract, is relevant measurement-error prior art. Existing Bayesian experimental design, contingent investment, graph synchronization, DDA's future-data valuation/complementarity, and inductive-bias learning are prior foundations. The candidate synthesis is the joint investment in reach, reliable inference about transfer, and qualified downstream capacity; no independent novelty determination.

Design a factorial experiment with ordinary field learning retained in all arms: (1) repeat same known relation under independently instrumented readings; (2) matched bridge to unfamiliar embodiment; (3) relation test on already reachable but previously unmeasured pair; (4) bridge plus audit with randomized feasible ordering; (5) direct target evidence at matched cost and date. Hold source execution, initial archive, permissions, target offered tasks and verification protocol fixed in the technical contrasts. Record selection and refused opportunities, timing, repeat drift, common errors, latent class claims and held-out actual relations, not merely agreement on the measured edge. Then follow whole economic programs with actual customer acceptance, original demand and fallback, dated support/compute/validation, manufacturing choices and receipts, subsequent eligible evidence and claimant. A cross-check that helps only its measured pair rejects broad shared inference; an independent direct route with the same dates and payoffs at lower cost rejects that bridge investment; a repeat that cannot separate measurement and relation variation rejects the restricted identification argument. Compare stable vintage vs reconfigured hardware separately. No field calibration or new official numeric production backbone obtained.
