# Prospective audit of partial source-policy invariance

**18 November 2026; no field study performed or customer data accessed.** The estimand is the captured whole-network original-order and new-qualified-service contrast between two **independently admissible** version-pinned source-copy/no-copy complete policies, plus whether a justified eta_j < h_j-l_j holds after a *complete* matched exit label. Do not substitute robot-only task success or train/test similarity.

## Record the causal unit and verified mechanisms

- **Pre-policy registry:** timestamped original B and A customer opportunity/order IDs, promised complete recipe and SLA, eligibility, rejection/unattempted orders, alternatives including specialists, contract capture and cost attribution; record dates before assignment.
- **Configuration lineage:** signed controller hash, embodiment and tools, data-source hashes/rights, source-use versus availability, training/curation/compute budget and checkpoint hash; separately signed acceptance and physical admission approvals with stated supported states/recovery.
- **Network closure:** dispatcher and scheduling version, shared-controller or update links, operator shift and simultaneous call capacity, maintenance/charging/compute dependencies, customer substitution/demand feedback; include any edge reachable under either complete policy.
- **Within-policy target log:** each checkpoint's own safe first unsupported *whole-network* history/action label, p_ij with denominator and uncertainty, K_i including matched whole service and source costs, joint service acceptance, retained F/S alternatives, censored outcomes and time-window expiry.
- **After-exit matched evidence:** independent safe evaluation/structural proof of remaining original B orders, A accepted services, recovery/operator peak, dispatch and customer cancellation, considering both policy versions in the *same* matched market/resource/remaining-time stratum. Explicitly bound absolute *difference* in captured contribution u_aj-u_bj rather than treating separate confidence bands as a direct joint bound.

## Falsification sequence

A. Does a read-only copy leave the original B controller/data/control/dispatch/compute/operator and demand-response law unchanged through the full horizon, rather than merely before the first read? Test I/O latency, operator shifts and customer substitution; counterexample if any path persists.

B. Can both policies safely reach the same fully labeled exit state with approved fallback and unchanged customer-opportunity census? A common robot pose is not enough. When matched states lack positivity, record **not identifiable** rather than a zero difference.

C. Is a purported eta_j actually an audited *joint policy* restriction valid for every feasible market/transfer regime, not the absolute value of a noisy sample mean? If not, use eta_j=h_j-l_j; do not improve certificate based on conjecture. If p,K uncertain, build one joint uncertainty set and optimize it, not point-value formulas.

D. Compare source-copy/no-copy, productive routine, target grounding, independent embodiment and competent specialist using **qualified and physically permitted complete policies**. Record p,K again for each; an informative diagnostic changes the decision but does not create data-training value, and physical grounding changes the learning process itself.

## Closest available public evidence and what it cannot establish

The verified 2019 ICML/PMLR paper *Safe Policy Improvement with Baseline Bootstrapping* (Laroche et al., https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/laroche19a.html) treats offline policy improvement with a baseline on data-uncertain state-actions. It is relevant prior art for the *fallback concept*, not proof of actuation-level, multi-robot, full-customer-order rollback. The prior PMLR DAgger paper and 2023 partial-identification work concern distribution shift and incomplete support, not a signed B-order/whole-market structural exclusion. Public ROS 2 logging and Open-RMF primitives discussed in earlier protocol only show possible components.

**Evidence status:** no signed customer production ledger, paired safe target trial, source-policy-specific target exit distribution, validated residual eta, safe fallback approval, external independent review or numeric official BLS/BEA crosswalk. This protocol cannot authorize or claim any real-world deployment.
