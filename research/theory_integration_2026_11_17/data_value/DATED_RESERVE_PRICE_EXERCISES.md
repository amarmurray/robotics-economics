# Exercises — dated reserve and source-to-production

1. With d=q1=q0=1/2, derive the gross optimum and the G/E threshold if E adds net 1 and G costs 1/2. Why does the expected capacity constraint fail?
2. Derive the minimum deterministic dated-price upper and the report-contingent exact upper. Explain why the report does not violate nonanticipation.
3. If q0=1/4, q1=3/4, d=1/2 and j is unknown, derive sharp gross value bounds. Show why reusing the skip price revenue is invalid.
4. State the conditional residual assumptions needed to use separable site potentials in a coupled network. Is an expected price-revenue bound from the incumbent sufficient after training?
