# Student exercises — observation-constrained production

1. State and prove the finite C_t information-support recursion. Why can alpha and beta both lie in perfect-state W_1 but the information cell {alpha,beta} fail C_1?
2. Prove that certified no-cost observation refinement cannot reduce the admissible set. Give two reasons a 99%-accurate but nonzero-false-report detector does not certify the same worst-case safe action set.
3. In the stipulated original-order optimization with R=X=G=E=4, XG=6 and XE=5 normalized net units, compute the X/G complement and give a changing-demand counterexample.
4. With n=299 independent zero-fault trials, calculate the one-sided 95% binomial p bound; explain why this is not a proof p=0.
5. Specify a defensible original-buyer/operator cluster treatment assignment and list one estimand it identifies and one physically safe-retention claim it cannot identify.
