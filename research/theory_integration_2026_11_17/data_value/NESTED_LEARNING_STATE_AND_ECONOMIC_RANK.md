# Nested production and learning nulls: when does economic data capital need more than one dimension?
25 November 2026 Eastern · Supporting theoretical note for [When Capabilities Become Production](WHEN_CAPABILITIES_COMPACT_DRAFT.md), not a separate paper or a novelty determination.

## 1. Economic priors and the object of the test
A robot body commits rival dated resources. A lawful experience stock is potentially reusable, but only after a controller, embodiment and target workflow have been jointly qualified. Thus the useful state for a capital decision need not be cumulative production, cumulative demonstrations, or even a scalar weighted data stock. The economically relevant question is whether two histories with equivalent current service and resource commitments can produce different **vectors** of attainable future service, and whether the difference survives competition for dated resources and the cheaper evolving rival. A source-specific readiness gain that never changes feasible paid service is not evidence of economic dimensionality.

We nest two restricted but competent nulls rather than pretend that learning-by-doing in general is scalar:
* Fixed process flexibility: conditional on a configured plant/body to product/recipe graph, each date solves A with realized demand, capacities and qualified arcs. No data action changes the feasible future graph, costs, information or demand law. Under risk-neutral additive periods with no intertemporal resource constraints and observable demand at dispatch, B reduces to choosing a configuration before demand and summing expected static optimized values less configuration costs. With shared dated resources use the time-expanded menu. This is ordinary configured flexibility, not a new theorem.
* Scalar learning-by-doing: cumulative usable experience e follows e'=(1-d)e+ell(x) (or a common weighted exposure index), and all source-dependent future transitions, feasible actions and rewards depend on history only through e and the nonlearning state b (including demand, capacities, bodies, organizational state and rights where relevant). There may be endogenous physical output, changing capacity, flexible recipes and demand. With matched action sets, payoff and transition kernels in (e,b), backward induction makes the Bellman optimum a function of (e,b). No history beyond that state improves it: condition on the compressed state, apply the same continuation value and maximize over the same actions. A count-preserving, lawful source-composition intervention that does not alter e or b cannot change continuation value. If e is an unknown index, equal total counts do not establish equal e. Organizational knowledge transfer, forgetting and heterogeneous learning are already part of the learning-by-doing literature.
* Combined null: allow arbitrary smooth scalar e(z) of the source vector z and arbitrary panel-specific response functions after production optimization. This is stronger than a known cumulative-count null. A fixed flexibility graph plus scalar learning can have nontrivial capital complementarity, demand dependence and nonlinear responses. The test below isolates what a single locally smooth knowledge index cannot represent; it does not reject a general multidimensional learning-by-doing or DDA model.

A sufficient dynamic state reduction is stronger than observing equal terminal outputs: the compressed state must preserve rewards, transition probabilities and available actions for all feasible policies, not just the historical policy. If training cost, licenses or curation capacity differ by source, either match those conditions for the diagnostic or model them separately. The full B comparison includes those costs and does not assume this reduction.

## 2. An observable economic dimensionality restriction
At a common lawful checkpoint with all nonlearning primitives b fixed, let z in R^m be the usable source mixture after a matched early production prefix. Let D contain k independent feasible local composition directions; each column sums to zero for a fixed-size corpus experiment. Directions need not be orthogonal. Predeclare J>=2 terminal economic panels differing in customer offer, admissible resource budget or embodiment, each assigned or observed under common pre-treatment design. Let Y_j(z) denote the expected net variable contribution of complete original offered services under a specified independently feasible downstream policy or a justified optimized A. Match baseline prices, rights, cost accounting and information timing **within** each panel across composition arms. Common training acquisition costs are not counted twice; isolate gross downstream value before incremental acquisition costs for this diagnostic. The panels may differ from one another; do not add their values together if they are alternative uses of the same body.

**Proposition (nested scalar restriction).** If the source-dependent state is e(z), scalar, and the downstream panel values are Y_j(z)=F_j(e(z)) with both maps differentiable at the same point (and no separate direct composition effects), then the J by k matrix M with row j equal to the derivatives along D satisfies rank(M)<=1. Proof: chain rule gives M = (F'_j(e))_j [grad e' D], an outer product. In particular all 2 by 2 minors vanish. This is true even if F_j is a different nonlinear economic production function in each panel. If a panel optimum is nonsmooth at the checkpoint, work inside a verified smooth regime or use a separately derived nonsmooth null; two-sided finite differences crossing a policy/capacity kink are not a valid rank test.

In the general source-specific model write q(z) for jointly validated readiness descriptors, conditional on b. If q is sufficient for downstream panel value Y_j=F_j(q), then M=J_F J_q D. More generally use the full relevant state vector including joint resource incidence and readiness law, not marginal task success. The observable rank is a lower bound on the dimension of a differentiable sufficient source-dependent state at that point, not an estimate of the total number of tasks or latent data factors. Economic projection can erase dimensions: a demand-saturated panel, unqualified complete recipe or identical bottlenecks can make rank zero or one even when the technical map has rank greater than one. A single aggregate revenue outcome can never supply a rank-two test. Technical rank alone does not establish economic rank. This is standard chain-rule/reduced-rank reasoning; the applied test is on original-service economic panels with independently controlled composition and dated resources.

**Finite-resolution audit.** For two panels and two directions let rows of the central-difference estimate be a_hat,b_hat and suppose independently justified Euclidean error bounds eps_a,eps_b relative to the local derivative (including sampling and finite-step approximation). Under the scalar null,
    |det(a_hat,b_hat)| <= eps_a ||b_hat|| + eps_b ||a_hat|| + eps_a eps_b.
Proof: expand det(a_hat-e_a,b_hat-e_b)=0, apply |det(u,v)|<=||u||||v||. If each directional derivative is Lipschitz with constant L in its one-dimensional step parameter on [-h,h], central differencing has absolute deterministic error at most L h/2 by averaging deviations of the derivative. Combine with a simultaneous sampling interval for each entry and then row norms; no independence assumption is needed for the deterministic bound. With no justified smoothness bound, report a finite intervention contrast, not a rejected differentiable scalar null. Baseline randomized panels must not be selected from post-treatment successes. Repeated same-cluster testing with shared models requires interference-aware assignment and uncertainty.

## 3. How the mechanism alters the investment, rather than simply rejecting an index
Consider a two-date lawful program C that changes early work mix or which equally costly records are retained/curated while preserving early accepted service contribution and total usable corpus size. Relative to P, the effective stock for target A changes from s0 to s1. G is an additive target-grounding input of delta>0 in this deliberately restricted learning technology. The terminal expected contribution of a dedicated available target service is w_c f(s_c+g delta) for g=0,1, where w_c>=0 is supported economic availability times contribution, not a benchmark-to-dollar multiplier. This restriction assumes target readiness independent of the availability/value event conditional on the fixed panel and arm; alternatively interpret f as conditional on that event. It is not a model of arbitrary coupled qualification laws. c also permits a separate late capacity change, represented by w1 versus w0. Early accepted contribution cancels in the cross-difference, costs are additive.

The incremental gross value of grounding after c is w_c [f(s_c+delta)-f(s_c)]. Thus CG beats C after a common committed marginal cost k iff this expression exceeds k (subject to the other feasible alternatives). If f is concave, s1>s0 implies routine source conversion lowers the incremental target-grounding gain at fixed availability. A larger usable late resource budget works in the opposite direction. For positive w0 and positive gains, complementarity occurs iff
    w1/w0 > [f(s0+delta)-f(s0)]/[f(s1+delta)-f(s1)].
For exponential saturation f(s)=1-exp(-s), the threshold is exp(s1-s0). For rational saturation f(s)=s/(1+s), it is ((1+s1)(1+s1+delta))/((1+s0)(1+s0+delta)). Both follow by subtraction. These are a smooth illustration of the existing signed capacity/routine mechanism, not a universal functional form. If capacity remains fixed and the G gain straddles k, the equal-count routine intervention changes the preferred G decision even with identical early output. A scalar index with unknown weights can fit one target response; two independent economic panel directions are needed to discriminate that more flexible null. If grounding instead unlocks old data through a multiplicative or endogenous conversion map, replace f(s+delta) by the actual lawful counterfactual q_g(z); neither sign is implied by generic concavity alone. Costly forcing and optional free menu expansion remain distinct.

## 4. Model and exact numerical diagnostic
Use three normalized source quantities (not three exhaustive modalities), z=(1,1,1), and two target conversion rows T_A=(1,0,1/4), T_B=(0,1,1/4). Directions d1=(1,0,-1) and d2=(0,1,-1) preserve size. T D has determinant 1/2. With q_r=f(T_r z), the determinant of the local technical derivative matrix is f'(1.25)^2/2. Dedicated panel A measures expected accepted service on A only, panel B on B only, each with known unit contribution and available resource, yielding the same positive determinant under the restricted economic model. If instead both measured panels are proportional aggregations of A+B, economic determinant is zero despite positive technical rank. The C mixture (2,1/2,1/2) has the same total but increases A's effective stock from 1.25 to 2.125; compare marginal G with delta=.5. These quantities are constructed, not estimates.

## 5. What is actually measurable
Randomize lawful source inclusion at a common checkpoint, equalize curation/compute budgets and fixed early production where possible, preserve exact source lineage and checkpoints, and randomize or block predeclared original-service panels with identical task, offer, resource and release conditions within each panel. Measure at least two independent composition directions across at least two economically distinct panels, with enough samples to bound joint uncertainty; use multiple step sizes to investigate the local approximation. Record failures, refusals, unserved orders, recovery, dated body/worker/compute clocks and actual settlement or independently supported value bounds. This is a controlled transfer/production diagnostic, not the whole-program causal contrast: the main P/G/C/CG comparison must still follow separately evolving exposure and successor states. A rank-two result rejects the restricted scalar reduction, not the multidimensional alternative in Data-Driven Automation; an economically rank-one result can still leave profitable G in some regimes. DDA (Farboodi, Koh and Xia, June 2026, NBER WP 35320, https://www.nber.org/papers/w35320, abstract rechecked) already includes heterogeneous task-specific spillovers and production-generated data. Argote and Epple (1990, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.247.4945.920, abstract) and Levitt, List and Syverson (2013, https://doi.org/10.1086/671137, abstract) already address transmission/aggregation of manufacturing knowledge. Jordan and Graves (1995, https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.41.4.577, bibliographic abstract) study fixed configured process flexibility. Do not claim a new general rank theorem or that these papers are scalar restrictions.

**Footwear warehouse baseline audit.** The public [2025 data paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12269467/) §3.1/Table 1 and §3.2/§4.1 describe four levels, 2,293 storage locations, and a 27-item trolley cap. The published file listing includes customer orders and picking waves; the documented class diagram links waves to orders, operator and product/location. But the methods generate waves from real demand patterns and describe simulated alternative inventory configurations. The abstract refers to temporal data while §1 explicitly denies timestamps/continuous tracking; §3.2 describes wave durations. This discrepancy must be resolved from actual fields before treating the corpus as a dated human-service ledger. These records may support a fixed-flexibility routing baseline and jointly grouped original-order incidence if the join keys and provenance survive anonymization; they do not establish customer-accepted robotic recipes, captured value or an independently evolving learning comparator. [Data DOI v1](https://doi.org/10.17632/pf2w725pw3.1), listing and article inspected 25 November; raw files not retrieved, no new official IO edge. Do not treat reconstructed routes or simulated slotting as observed treatment effects. Commercial causal identification remains absent.

**Next scientific action.** Test whether available matched technical source-mixture evidence contains two independent equal-budget composition directions at a common checkpoint, plus supported complete-service panel denominators; assess what level of the restriction can actually be tested. If technical-only, explicitly confine the claim to technical dimensionality. Retain fixed flexibility and heterogeneous learning as strong alternatives, rather than accumulating new synthetic certificates. Seek the raw warehouse order/wave schema through genuinely available public routes to build the fixed-routing baseline; do not label it a robot ROI dataset.
