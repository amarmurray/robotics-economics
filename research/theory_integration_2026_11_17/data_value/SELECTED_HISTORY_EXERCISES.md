# Exercises — selected original-buyer histories and economic data value

Use the [theory chapter](SELECTED_HISTORY_AND_DECISION_SUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.md). All data are synthetic normalized net paid-production units; distinguish policy oracle from implementable policy. Solutions are intentionally separate in [instructor material](SELECTED_HISTORY_SOLUTIONS.md).

1. Starting from B3=J+H and B4=max(0,B3−1)+K with t3 target service only when B3=0, derive the T-wait and T-serve incremental payoff rows in terms of p00,q0,q1. Explain which original buyer offers can be omitted without affecting this **particular** fixed menu.
2. Given fair binary marginals and all pairwise uniform J,H,Y tables, derive the parity distribution and compute p00,q0,q1. Show explicitly why the T-wait policy cannot optimize the full P menu despite being optimal among T controllers in a subregion.
3. In each queue-one cell 01 and 10, the selected Y log has unconditional masses Y=1 logged 1/32, Y=0 logged 1/32 and Y masked 3/16. All remaining queue histories have masked Y. Find the sharp compatible theta interval **conditional on** the pairwise parity restriction. Construct the missing-data allocations at both endpoints.
4. Calculate the sharp full P and P−E intervals. At what theta does P beat E? Distinguish (i) an oracle that knows theta, (ii) a fixed P controller under maximin, and (iii) a lawful early report of theta net of its acquisition cost.
5. Prove the affine row-span criterion for fixed-policy point identification on a strictly positive equality-restricted simplex. Give one reason it cannot be applied unmodified at a binding inequality and one reason point identification of each policy is not required for a robust investment ranking.
6. A prospective original-buyer registry takes a Y audit with known probability rho>0 independently of Y conditional on pre-recording history; T assignment has lawful positive known propensity e. Derive a one-stage IPW expression for q1 and explain why it fails when auditing systematically excludes unsatisfied customers whose original orders were blocked by workers. Identify what changes with adaptive t3 action selection and cross-site interference.
