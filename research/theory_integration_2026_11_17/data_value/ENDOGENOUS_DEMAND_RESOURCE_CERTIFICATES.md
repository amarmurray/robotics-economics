# Endogenous buyer demand, source data and embodied capacity
**19 November 2026 | *When Capabilities Become Production* integrated continuation. Conditional theory; no observed robot economics or novelty claim.**

## The exact question
The preceding market-support and LP dual chapter offers a potential upper production bound with an unknown source-to-qualification learning law. But a fixed number of qualified *jobs* is not an upper bound on *capturable provider cash* when source training X, target grounding G, or a new embodiment E changes buyer willingness to pay, original customer-order volume, substitution and the complete service menu. This continuation keeps the original R (productive incumbent), A (target information), X (heterogeneous source learning), G (grounding), and E (new body/capacity) within **one** qualified customer production objective. No benchmark percentage is converted to dollars. Field/failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained sources remain separate technologies and rights constraints.

## Proposition 1 — policy-consistent demand-and-resource dual
For an approved complete-network policy p and a jointly reachable learner/operator/buyer history h, let y≥0 be all potentially qualified complete-service recipes, \(\phi_{p,h}(y)\) their captured *current-period* customer contribution after appropriately accounted variable costs, and \(A_{p,h}y\le b_{p,h}\) actual resource constraints (buyer rights, refused demand, accepted-order limitations remain feasibility constraints). Suppose a physically/contractually verified *history-uniform* envelope satisfies \(A_{p,h}y\ge\underline A_p y\), \(b_{p,h}\le\bar b_p\) and \(\phi_{p,h}(y)\le\bar\phi_p(y)\) for all possible qualified recipes and policy-induced customer states. For every λ≥0,
\[
V_{p,h}\le\lambda^\top\bar b_p+\sup_{y\ge0}[\bar\phi_p(y)-\lambda^\top\underline A_py].\tag{D1}
\]
Taking the infimum over λ tightens the bound. It is valid even without a fitted learning transition but becomes vacuous if demand/capture or newly qualified recipe space lacks a verified finite envelope. Sum actual stage contributions over the horizon with discounting, subtract investment costs once, and compare the resulting whole-policy X upper with a genuinely feasible R/G lower. Never add a separate data-premium atop the already included future production. An E body changes resources; X/G may create new recipes or new buyers: revalidate every dual coefficient. A free optional nonactuating data copy that preserves all old feasible policies weakly enlarges optimized value, unlike paid forced conversion.

## Proposition 2 — exact revenue-sensitive capacity shadow price
For a *uniform-price provider*, not a social planner collecting the integral of buyer willingness to pay, inverse customer demand \(P(q)=\alpha-\beta q\), \(\alpha,\beta>0\), and at most C qualified service units, gross receipts are
\[
 V(\alpha,\beta,C)=\max_{0\le q\le C}q(\alpha-\beta q),\quad q^*=\min(C,\alpha/(2\beta)),
\]
\[
 V(\alpha,\beta,C)=\min_{\lambda\ge0}\left[\lambda C+\frac{(\alpha-\lambda)_+^2}{4\beta}\right],\quad\lambda^*=(\alpha-2\beta C)_+.\tag{D2}
\]
The dual is exact by concave maximization and complementary slackness. A physical C alone cannot bound receipts without α/β or a separate valid captured-price ceiling. A provider may optimally leave a qualified E slot idle as demand becomes price-elastic.

## Proposition 3 — identical observed trained service, opposite physical-investment ranking
Suppose the only X-trained fully qualified production observation is one slot q₀=1 sold at price p₀=8. Under a *hypothesized*, not validated linear price family \(P_X(q;\beta)=8+\beta(1-q)\), β∈[2,4], every world reproduces the same source-trained accepted-service outcome at q1. Give the retained R controller \(P_R(q)=8-2q\). Let X source conversion cost K_X=3/2; certified new E embodiment expands capacity from1 to2 for K_E=1. Then R net=6; X net=13/2; E (without X) net=7. In world β=2, optimal X+E gross=12 (q*=2), net=19/2; X+E beats E by5/2. In world β=4, optimal X+E gross=9 (q*=3/2), net=13/2; E beats X+E by1/2. **Both** produce price8 at q1 and the same technical qualification. The economic source–embodiment gross interaction \([V_X(2)-V_X(1)]-[V_R(2)-V_R(1)]\) changes sign from +2 to −1. Under the *restricted* β interval and retained conditions, the sharp X+E versus E net difference is [-1/2,+5/2], not an identified investment recommendation.

On C≥q₀, if β≤p₀/q₀, every optimal q*≥q₀ and \(R_\beta(q)=p₀q+\beta q(q₀-q)\) decreases in β over q≥q₀. This proves endpoint sharpness within the stated family. With only one price/quantity observation and downward sloping but otherwise unrestricted demand, even a **finite** revenue upper bound may fail: a nonincreasing price schedule can equal8 at q1 but be arbitrarily expensive for a tiny unobserved q<1. A capacity ceiling is not a captured-cash ceiling.

## Proposition 4 — when target grounding G and capacity E are genuine complements
Under the narrower *verified* mechanism that G raises a qualified, monetizable demand intercept α by δ≥0 while leaving β, physical resource requirements, rights and model/customer closure unchanged,
\[
V(\alpha+\delta,\beta,C_2)-V(\alpha,\beta,C_2)\ge
V(\alpha+\delta,\beta,C_1)-V(\alpha,\beta,C_1),\quad C_2\ge C_1.\tag{D3}
\]
Because \(\partial_\alpha V=q^*\) almost everywhere and q* is nondecreasing in C, integrate over δ. This is one **conditional** channel through which target grounding unlocks existing representations and makes more embodiment economically useful. If G instead reduces recovery minutes, X changes price curvature, E brings cannibalizing buyers, or body learning changes recipe feasibility, the statement need not hold; compare the complete J(R),J(X),J(G),J(E) and combinations. This is not fixed positive additive transfer or a general complementarity theorem.

## Information versus productive learning
In the two equally likely hypothetical β worlds, choosing among R,X,E,X+E without A yields expected X+E value (19/2+13/2)/2=8. A perfect noninterfering preinvestment target audit A allows X+E only in β2 and E in β4, expected (19/2+7)/2=33/4; gross EVPI=1/4 *normalized service contribution units*, before collection cost, timing, sample selection and safety. An imperfect report cannot be worth more under these assumptions. This is not a field data price and requires a real prior. Free R production data might provide the same off-support target demand evidence if sufficiently supported, while selected accepted tickets usually do not.

Farboodi, Koh & Xia, *Data-Driven Automation* (arXiv:2606.10127 v1, June 2026), already values endogenous data and cross-task spillovers.
