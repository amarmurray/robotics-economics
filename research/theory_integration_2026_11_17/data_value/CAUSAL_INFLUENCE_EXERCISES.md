# Student exercises — conditional influence, productive data and safe admission

All inputs are synthetic normalized production units. Work from the theorem assumptions, not robot field evidence. The [separate solutions](CAUSAL_INFLUENCE_SOLUTIONS.md) should remain separate from the assigned version.

1. **Vector coupling:** Two models share e0=0; a three-node controller shock has b0=(0,.05,0); only C1_(B,X)=.4 is nonzero. Derive e1, e2 and a terminal B captured-value error with weight .3×10. Explain why the result is a bound, not a claimed realized loss.
2. **Network tails:** A remote shock β=1/20 propagates along at most two directed paths per step with per-edge conditional influence at most 1/5. For radius r=1 and T=4, compute finite and infinite tail bounds. Why may a shared controller invalidate the chosen radius?
3. **Same-world choice:** With retained value 7 and competent specialist upper 7.08, compute the open and serial A/B route economic lower bounds using the chapter table. Which strict rankings follow, and what must be established before any route is physically admissible?
4. **Joint laws:** True and approximate transitions each have fair binary individual marginals U,V. True U=V, approximate U=1−V. Why cannot separately matched node marginals justify the C1 joint coupling for a customer bundle requiring U=V?
5. **Feedback and unsupported states:** A retained controller never broadcasts. A target-grounding policy unlocks a new customer and broadcasts weights to every site. Explain the error in using incumbent fitted C=0 as a policy-uniform guarantee. Suggest two fields necessary to reconstruct a target support map.
6. **Hard resource invariant:** Prove that an exclusive reservation token caps simultaneous active human recoveries at one *only if* all recovery consumers are gated and any refused start reaches a verified operator-independent safe state. Why do near-zero simultaneous-event estimates fail to prove this condition?
