# Target transport and conditional recovery — empirical identification protocol

**Designed November 18, 2026. No commercial/customer pilot, physical safety certification, robot access or source data acquired.** This note extends [ROBUST_POLICY_EMPIRICAL.md](ROBUST_POLICY_EMPIRICAL.md) without substituting an unverified dataset or changing the full production-atlas roadmap.

## Decision unit and linked panels

A single unit is an **eligible and contracted customer service opportunity**: site × qualified body/controller lineage × whole accepted task bundle × scheduled demand/window × relevant operator/recovery capacity. Define study sampling and target demand denominators separately. Keep a complete calendar of eligible orders, declined/aborted orders, service saturation, price/capture schedules, qualified specialist fallback and rights/curation costs. Active production logs alone omit failed, untouched and inaccessible service opportunities.

Join three datasets under safe, approved rules if ever permitted: (1) randomized/frozen-controller technical source/grounding/body interventions, including field trajectories, interventions, deliberate target trials, simulation and public input *separately*; (2) independent state-exposure, gate miss and recovery/abort audit; (3) customer-accepted whole-service 00/10/01/11 and provider/customer output/accounting under matched demand/resource windows. None exists as a joined panel in this project.

## Design before regression

1. **Specify target weights** \(\mu_z\) from actual eligible customer demand/order universe, not robot action frequency, and separately record validation enrollment weights \(\nu_z\). Stratify by site/shift, full recipe, customer demand/captured price, staffing, body/controller version, storage rights and support capacity. Keep uninterpretable/unregistered site mass as **unsupported**.
2. **State intervention and lineage.** Comparison R (routine productive byproduct), G (target grounding), E (changed embodiment), and S (qualified specialist/retained old policy) must use a credible randomized, parallel or fully documented causal design. Independent or frozen controller lineages prevent cross-cohort updates from contaminating potential outcomes. Where crossovers are used, randomize order, allow washout only if technically real, audit carryover, and report cluster uncertainty.
3. **Record joint held-out qualification**, not marginal completion percentages: attempts and accepted B,C outcomes 00, 10, 01, 11, conditional on identical contract acceptance and within the planned customer window. Record real supported alternatives. Do not count correction success as automatic qualification, and do not convert benchmark percentages to wages or dollars.
4. **Audit selection and positivity.** For every target stratum \(z\) with \(\mu_z>0\), ask if both route outcomes are identifiable and transportable. Compare \(\nu\) and \(\mu\); disclose insufficient overlap and set a partial-identification bound for missing strata using verified marginal evidence or conservative [−1,1] bounds, never silently drop them. The target upper bound on \(\sum\mu_z\Delta_z d_z\) should be calculated from conditional matched estimates with simultaneous uncertainty and site clustering, not from a pooled \(\nu\)-weighted figure.
5. **Audit physical joint support** in report × eligible state × latent gate miss × operator intervention × recovery-tail × accepted-service cells. A missed correction may have no logged intervention; an unchanged averaged recovery distribution is not evidence of safe policy conditional on a report. Only an independent authorized procedure can determine approved fallbacks/reserves, including abort paths.
6. **Compute the economic comparison once** using accepted service opportunities, avoidable retained work, \(f(Q;\omega)\), customer outside alternative, resource prices and actual capture. Record \(\Delta_z\) and \(b_z\) *by demand window*. Do not add future data value again if the continuation qualified-production value already includes it; distinguish technical marginal source effects from captured provider contribution.

## What the hypothetical threshold means

In the synthetic two-site illustration, selected study mix \((.9,.1)\) would suggest R beats E by +.36, but target mix \((.4,.6)\) implies R loses by −.84; neither number is an estimate. If validated target covered strata have a matched E-minus-R upper difference .1 and other sites have only component marginals (.75 R, .9 E), at most \(1/12\) of target order mass can lack coverage before the *strict* positive R certificate disappears, under the *specific* \(b=1,\Delta=8\) assumptions. The actual decision requires a fresh estimated customer envelope and confidence/tail bounds; a finite sample can neither prove structural zero recovery support nor certify a robot's safety.

## Discriminating measurements / countermodels

**Selection falsifier.** Re-estimate route differences under the intended customer order distribution. If uncovered service opportunities or a newly recruited target site have larger E relative gains, a previously favorable aggregate R comparison can reverse. Transport must handle site-specific demand and cost, not only covariate shift in technical success.

**Demand falsifier.** Keep the same frozen controller and acceptance law but compare two pre-registered accepted-service value/capture schedules; see whether ranking changes as the qualifying bundle becomes binding or saturated. This can be a price/margin and demand-counterfactual evaluation, not a claim of customer randomized price manipulation.

**Recovery falsifier.** Under an independently authorized safe diagnostic, record conditional report × peak recovery requirement; any positive-support branch with resource requirement above the allocated cap invalidates that route's pathwise certificate absent a safe abort or additional preapproved reserve. Report conditional recovery tails with uncertainty, not just average human minutes.

**Competent alternative.** Simultaneously value qualified specialists, retaining trained controller releases, free routine byproduct data, and safe simulation/target proxy. If all required rare-state support is freely captured by ongoing productive deployment, the costly grounding plan loses its asserted incremental acquisition value.

**Confounding and interference.** Private site skill and operator competency can affect route assignment and qualification; shared training across target sites violates isolated potential-outcome notation. Predefine cluster-level learning pools and freeze versions before evaluating per-site \(q_j(z)\). Do not condition on achieved autonomy or the controller's chosen state-visit distribution as though pre-treatment.
