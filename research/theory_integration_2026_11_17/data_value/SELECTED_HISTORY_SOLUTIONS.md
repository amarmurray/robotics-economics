# Instructor solutions — selected original-buyer histories

Companion to [exercises](SELECTED_HISTORY_EXERCISES.md). Entirely synthetic, not field evidence.

**1.** If J+H=2 the single worker leaves one job before t4, so Y cannot pay. If J+H=1, a job is served at t3, no target t3 service can create K and t4 Y can pay. If J+H=0, waiting permits t4 Y but serving X immediately earns 1/2 and creates K=1 blocking Y. Thus from a P acquisition cost of 1/8: wait=−1/8+q0+q1, serve=−1/8+p00/2+q1, safe P-R=5/16; add baseline 6. Y at J=H=1 is absent from these specific policy rewards, but can matter for learning, demand evolution and a larger policy menu.

**2.** Walsh expansion of a distribution on three binary variables with all pairwise uniform tables kills every 1- and 2-factor coefficient; a single 3-factor term yields probability theta/4 for even parity, (1−theta)/4 for odd. Therefore p00=1/4,q0=(1−theta)/4,q1=theta/2. Wait=1/8+theta/4, serve=theta/2, R=5/16. Wait≤R when theta≤3/4; for theta≥1/2 serve≥wait. Their union covers [0,1]; wait is never globally strictly optimal.

**3.** In 01 and 10, each complete positive atom weighs theta/4 and each negative atom (1−theta)/4; observed positive and negative masses 1/32 each imply theta≥1/8 and theta≤7/8. The missing positive mass is theta/4−1/32 and missing negative mass is (1−theta)/4−1/32, summing to 3/16, and both are nonnegative exactly within this interval. Hide all Y in 00/11. Theta=1/8 leaves zero missing positives; theta=7/8 leaves zero missing negatives. Identical observable masked distributions support opposite full laws.

**4.** P−6=max(5/16,theta/2) ranges from 5/16 to 7/16. E−6=13/32; P−E∈[−3/32,+1/32]; P strictly dominates E if theta>13/16. An oracle picks T-serve at theta>5/8; without any timely report, fixed P-R guarantees 5/16 while no mixture can exceed that at theta=1/8, so under maximin E's 13/32 prevails. A lawful precommitment report may change the Bayesian or robust decision, but must be charged actual acquisition/rights/compute opportunity cost and cannot modify the past investment.

**5.** On an equality-only affine feasible set with a relatively interior positive law, any small feasible perturbation is in null(A); v gives invariant expectation iff v·d=0 for all such directions d, or v belongs to row(A). At boundary-active inequalities, some null directions are infeasible; use exact min/max LP instead. An optimized policy maximum can be constant even if unchosen payoff vectors vary. To certify only a sign, a common-support LP bound excluding zero is enough.

**6.** With valid action randomization/exchangeability and conditional independent masking, q1=E[1{A=T}1{J+H'=1}S Y/(e(H)rho(H,J,H',T))] using the registered cluster population and no interference outside the modeled cluster; H is preaction history, H' the second job. The expectation is across genuine original orders, not invoices. If S depends on Y after conditioning, weighted complete cases are biased and the missing history cannot be recovered by nominal rho alone. For adaptive actions weight all selected action/audit stages, require sequential overlap in relevant histories, and model the entire interfering learner/customer/operator cluster as the exposure unit.
