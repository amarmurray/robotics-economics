# Instructor solutions: finite-sample original-buyer SG/E economics

1. E weakly dominates throughout its envelope iff 12k−1≤3 or k≤1/3. SG *strictly* dominates throughout iff 12k−1>5 or k>1/2, impossible. For 1/3<k<1/2 rankings vary with E, and at k=1/2 SG weakly dominates and may tie. If h=Pr(qualified)=1/2 and buyer/settlement marginal also 1/2, Fréchet bounds imply k∈[0,1/2]. Concordant and discordant distributions attain both, yielding SG net 5 and −1 with identical marginals.
2. Write \(\hat\Delta=\sum 12X_i/n_S-\sum 8Z_j/n_E\). Independent summand squared ranges total \(144/n_S+64/n_E\), hence r=sqrt{log(2/alpha)(144/n_S+64/n_E)/2}. On coverage an incorrect empirical sign has true gap magnitude at most max(0,r−|estimated gap|); if |estimate|>r the ranking is correct on coverage. This is neither a guarantee of benefit from running the experiment nor a measurement of robot performance.
3. Cauchy–Schwarz gives \((144/n_S+64/n_E)(n_S+n_E)\ge400\); equality requires n_S/n_E=12/8=3/2. With heterogeneous costs optimize under the budget to get n_S/n_E=(12/8)sqrt(c_E/c_S). Dependence between learners, variance heterogeneity, demand feedback, rights and physical cost may invalidate or change the optimal design.
4. SG assigned to first, E to second produces D=11−(−1)=12; reverse produces D=−1−7=−8. Fair mean 2 equals the average of the two clusters' SG−E potential differences. Each D∈[−8,12], so m independent flips give radius 20sqrt{log(2/alpha)/(2m)}. At m=300 and alpha=.05 it is 1.5682; independent unpaired 360/240 Bernoulli clusters yield 1.1089 under stronger iid assumptions. Matching can nevertheless reduce actual variance.
5. Shared learner updates transmit S/G experience into the putative E/control group, and shared operator/parent markets move demand across arms. Assign at the full interference-closed learner–operator–buyer level or model exposure, including no-uptake, rejected orders, recovery and refund. Join data modality, rights, training checkpoint, joint complete qualification, offered original buyer order, signed physical service, payments and subsequent training-driven services. Fold those future services into J exactly once, rather than granting data an additional premium.
