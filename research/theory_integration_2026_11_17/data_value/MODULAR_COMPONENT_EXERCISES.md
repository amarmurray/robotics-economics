# Exercises — source, grounding and embodiment attribution (student copy)

All contributions below are synthetic net normalized units and all signed-policy permissions are *hypothetical*.

1. The old complete original-buyer policy has v(empty)=6. The jointly trained X+grounded G+new-body E policy has v(XGE)=10. Construct two monotone policy-value tables that agree on these endpoints but assign all four units to different components. Can the observed total identify a source-data value?
2. For any monotone set function with these endpoints, derive the three-component Shapley value formula. Prove its bounds and its efficiency without assuming additive technical transfer.
3. Calculate the three-way discrete interaction for (a) only all three components produce the additional four units and (b) any two suffice. Explain why benchmark gains and total package returns cannot determine its sign.
4. Under fixed grounding, suppose source-only and body-only complete-policy values are in [L10,U10] and [L01,U01]. Derive sharp bounds on the symmetric source allocation. What fails if one of the cross-arms is physically impossible?
5. Propose an evidence design that separates (a) total bundle return, (b) source/body policy-factor effects, and (c) causal transfer from field trajectories versus teleoperation, without treating a held-out benchmark score as a paid customer receipt.
