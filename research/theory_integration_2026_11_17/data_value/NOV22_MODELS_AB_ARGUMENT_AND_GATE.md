## One mechanism, two analytical layers

**Model A: signed qualified production.** Fix learner checkpoint, embodiment(s), original offered-order cohort, customer/competitor willingness to pay, rights and operator/robot capacity. Each feasible complete-service recipe is a column that consumes robot/operator time, access and verification. Production/dispatch optimization uses realized demand, available qualified recipes, fallback, payment terms and resource constraints. Formally, if \(q\) is a vector of jointly qualified recipe states and \(d\) original buyer requests, \(F_{e,d,N}(q)=\max_{\pi\in\Pi_{\rm signed}(q,e,d,N)} C(\pi)\), where \(C\) includes accepted/settled contributions and rejected/refunded orders and counts outside-slot displacement once. Existing LP resource-price certificates, O-ring service bundles, process flexibility and joint qualification partial ID are preserved. **Model B: endogenously changing capability and feasible production.** Let \(S\) acquire/allow new licensed and lineage-identifiable source episodes, \(G_t\) train on or align an existing archive with target evidence, \(G_o\) provide a new *predecision report* for an unchanged trained learner, and \(E\) alter embodiment/manufacturing and feasible services. \[
J(z)=\max_{i\in\mathcal I(z)}[-K_i+\mathbb E\{C_i(z,\xi)+\beta J(z'_i)\}],
\]
where \(z\) includes qualified policies, rights and data mixtures, operator access, buyer demand, upstream manufacturing, and next-round production-generated data. The source-to-qualification map generates a *joint* distribution, not only per-task accuracy. Current and future paid production are valued exactly once. ## A compact rigorous bridge

With two complete-service qualifications \(Q_A,Q_B\), four signed optimized after-certification production payoffs \(f_{00},f_{10},f_{01},f_{11}\) admit an exact expansion
\[
\mathbb EF=r+a p_A+b p_B+\kappa h,\quad h=\Pr(Q_A=Q_B=1).
\]
The source–grounding interaction at a fixed demand/resource/embodiment table is
\[
\Delta_{S,G}\mathbb EF=a\Delta p_A+b\Delta p_B+\kappa\Delta h.
\]
Here \(\kappa=f_{11}-f_{10}-f_{01}+f_{00}\) can be positive for an end-to-end order or negative when one robot/operator slot makes qualified recipes substitutes. Fréchet lower/upper limits on \(h\) produce **sharp conditional bounds** if no stronger cross-arm constraints exist.
