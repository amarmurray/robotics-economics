# Joint lineage conversion under a shared procurement budget

24 November 2026. Continuation of [endogenous authorization growth](ENDOGENOUS_AUTHORIZATION_GROWTH.md) in *When Capabilities Become Production*. The previous token-stock certificate permits joint action columns, but the stock of individual authorized opportunities need not be a sufficient state for a controller that changes compatibility, reports and the generation of further orders. Here we make that distinction constructive. All numbers are synthetic.

## 1. From typed stocks to joint conversion

Let H_t denote the lawful predecision history of one original interference cluster. In addition to distinct dated order IDs, it records licensed source lineages, controller version, compatibility hyperedges (complete jointly qualified recipes), remaining procurement balances by buyer, and worker/robot availability. A hyperedge is a candidate *joint action*, not another order to count in revenue. Reusing a data file is not consuming an order. A controller can create a new qualified service type or enable a new report, but neither qualification nor a parent license implies new customer authorization. Actual successor orders have distinct IDs; parent links may be many-to-one. Charge the same downstream accepted service once.

For a fixed joint schedule a, define the conditional joint-generation envelope K_t(h,a) as a set containing every admissible vector of conditional means (r, f'), where r is discounted net reward and f'=E[F_{t+1}(H_{t+1})|h,a]. F is a chosen history feature vector, not necessarily additive: include stock, compatible bundles, report classes and remaining customer procurement rights or their interactions. A common law must produce all these conditional means; K may be a conservative outer set. Costs include training, curation and displaced production exactly once. If a resource is consumed before the next decision, update its balance; e.g. b'=b−c for a buyer's remaining authorized spend/slots, with c≤b pathwise, and add any verified exogenous replenishment separately. When buyers can authorize new amounts, use a lawful action-specific transition, not a fixed budget assumption.

**Joint-generation potential.** Let U_t(h)=alpha_t+theta_t·F_t(h), with unrestricted coefficient signs. Assume integrability and terminal U_T≥terminal reward. If for every admissible predecision history and whole schedule,

    alpha_{t+1}+sup_{(r,f') in K_t(h,a)} [r+theta_{t+1}·f'] ≤ U_t(h),

then E[U_0] bounds optimized discounted net production under every compatible common law. Proof: the actual conditional mean is in K, hence r+E[U_{t+1}|h,a]≤U_t; integrate under any nonanticipating rule and telescope through T. Optional retained-controller policies remain in the action set. For time/resource charges use the same proof with nonnegative resource prices and a valid original-denominator budget on their total use; do not also debit those prices as actual cash. The previous linear token potential is a special case with F=stock, separate carry/expiry inequalities and nonnegative prices. Here negative interaction coefficients are permitted because the *whole* joint transition is bounded, not a componentwise relaxation with signed weights. For finite polytopal K the inequality checks its vertices; a finite reachable state graph can use tabular F. Neither representation guarantees tractable separation on general hypergraphs.

**Compression failure.** If two histories share F but one admits a valuable conversion and the other does not, then any exact value representation by F alone fails when their optimal values differ. Even having the same counts by lineage and controller is insufficient: A1 and B1 can be mutually compatible at one customer, or refer to disjoint fixtures/rights at two different customers. One cross-source qualification count computed from distinct compatible pair IDs distinguishes the two-state example below. In larger exclusive-use settings, counting every hyperedge still overstates simultaneous dispatch: a matching or packing of disjoint authorized service claims and physical resources must be feasible. Reusable source data need not be disjoint; enforce exclusivity only on actual rights, orders and scarce capacity. State compression requires action/transition closure or a valid outer bound; adding a pair feature does not in general make the process Markov.

## 2. Exact shared-buyer example

Dates 0,1,2; undiscounted units. Retain an old controller R. At date 1 each of two eligible licensed lineages A,B can support a separate qualified old service paying net 4. A single worker at date 1 permits only one. At date 2 an ordinary retained service paying 2 is always available. One original buyer has a *shared* procurement authorization for at most two service slots across both dates, with at most one each date; slots are not reset by creating a new order. Receipts are fixed conditional on these slots, not a dollar budget. No other receipts. Thus R=2+4·1{A or B} at each history. Date 0 grounding G costs 1/2 including displaced initial contribution. It retains R and enables a different complete service J only when the two lineages are jointly compatible and licensed for the new controller. The date-1 J has distinct buyer authorization, pays 3, and uses one slot. Performing it can create one new licensed, qualified and authorized date-2 order D paying 8; success is reported before date-2 dispatch. The firm can perform D or the ordinary retained 2, not both. The J decision precedes the successor outcome; an optional lawful predecision report S may inform the probability. No speculative payment for unperformed D. An independent embodiment E adds 1 net to the *same* R on a separate customer, staff and budget, not to G. Extra source X without compatibility or new paid menu has zero incremental value before costs in this constructed environment; that assumption is not general.

Let C denote original-cluster compatibility and predecision eligibility of J (requiring A and B); q=P(C), and Y indicate whether choosing J would generate the usable D. Define m=P(C,Y=1), and q_s=P(C,S=s), m_s=P(C,Y=1,S=s). The old route earns 6 on C. Choosing J earns 5+6p_s in conditional expectation where p_s=m_s/q_s. The opportunity cost of J is one unit today and the incremental value of D is 6, not 8: the retained date-2 service is displaced. For zero q_s set contribution zero.

    G−R = max(0, sum_s (6m_s−q_s)_+ − 1/2).
    E−R = 1.

The outer max is the date-0 option to retain R without buying G. Conditional on G bought, the sum selects J only in report classes with positive expected advantage. It does not use Y before Y is reportable. Derivation: condition on lawful S, subtract the old route's feasible two-date receipts, then maximize separately at date 1 because the date-2 choice is already optimized; take expectations and subtract grounding cost. No second data premium. Without reports the sum is (6m−q)_+.

**Additive token failure.** With known success, full budget and compatible A,B, the post-acquisition production value is 3+8=11. With A only or B only it is 6; with neither it is 2. The unique affine singleton-calibrated predictor is 2+4A+4B, giving 10 rather than 11. For R its same prediction gives 10 rather than 6. The pair interaction changes sign from −4 under R to +1 under successful new controller. This simultaneously exhibits capacity substitution and technology-created complementarity; singleton marginal source values are not a valid general upper or lower bound. The exact binary-state expression on full compatibility for known success is 2+4(A+B)+(−4+5Y)AB. If compatibility is not automatic replace AB in the +5Y term by C, leaving the −4AB procurement correction intact. For a lawful predecision conditional probability p the +5Y term becomes (6p−1)_+; do not use latent Y. If the total buyer budget is only one slot, J cannot produce a paid D and old production dominates (4 if any lineage, otherwise 2). This is a change of economic value without necessarily changing technical transfer.

## 3. Sharp report-conditioned audit bounds

Given q,m, any lawful S satisfies

    (6m−q)_+ ≤ sum_s (6m_s−q_s)_+ ≤ 5m.

Lower: positive part is subadditive, summing conditional contributions. Upper: m_s≤q_s so each positive part ≤5m_s. Both are sharp across report structures: independent report on C attains lower and a lawful perfect precursor of Y attains upper. Perfect foresight is an outer limit, not presumed implementable. With a binary report and known positive class mass s=P(C,S=+), x=P(C,Y=1,S=+) is restricted to [max(0,m+s−q), min(m,s)]. The value is

    L(x)=(6x−s)_+ + (6(m−x)−(q−s))_+.

Every x in the interval has a valid joint law of (C,Y,S) by its four nonnegative cells; C false histories may have a constant null report. L is convex in x, so the exact upper given these margins is the larger endpoint value. The lower is the no-information bound, attained by x=ms/q for q>0; q=0 means zero. With an interval for x, convexity still gives the upper at endpoints, but lower may require interior kink or endpoints. This is a fixed-law optimal rule, not a robust prescription for an unknown law; a single predeclared rule with a uniform lower bound is needed for that.

Two causal worlds: A=B=C with probability 1/2 and neither otherwise. Conditional on C, a potential successor under J occurs with probability 5/8. The positive predecision report has probability 5/8 on C in both worlds. In one world S is an independent diagnostic with x=25/128; in the other it is a lawful perfect precursor with x=5/16. Specify the joint four-cell distributions before date 0; the potential J outcome is revealed only after J, R's actual outcomes are identical, and C and S are observed before selecting J. Report marginals and potential successor rates match. The first world yields sum=11/8, G−R=7/8, below E−R=1. The second yields sum=25/16, G−R=17/16, above E. This is a rank reversal even holding compatibility, aggregate successor rate and original report marginals fixed; the missing information is their joint predecision relationship. A randomized forced J audit on C observing S and Y would discriminate these worlds; separate marginals or an incumbent-only audit cannot.

A policy-uniform cap m≤1/4 yields G−R≤3/4 under *any* report refinement, hence E beats G. In contrast, an independently supported always-J-on-C policy with 6m−q>3/2 ranks G above E without requiring additional report refinement. A supported positive report class with 6m_+−q_+>3/2 also suffices. Costs and baseline matched. At the first world's m, a free optional routine source reproducing the full G transition, menu, diagnostic, rights and qualification without cost gives incremental 11/8>1; the data's source modality is not the decisive property. If E consumes the same retained capacity and displaces an additional 1.5, optional E increment is zero, not one. Additional data that are free *and optional* preserve the old optimum; forced training and forced dispatch do not.

## 4. Meaning and prospective discrimination

The economic unit of recursion is a qualified, authorized and budget-feasible opportunity transformation under a lawful joint policy. It is not a parent sample count or a benchmark. This extends the previous stock envelope by admitting a controller-dependent conversion graph and signed joint features while conserving procurement across service generations. Field, failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained data can enter the lineage graph separately; only the actual matched intervention establishes the direction and magnitude of transfer. No claim that those sources have fixed positive additive effects. A controller can make a new report informative while leaving successor frequency unchanged, changing which deployment opportunities are worth creating.

[Evidence protocol](JOINT_LINEAGE_PROCUREMENT_EVIDENCE.md) distinguishes predecision report availability from later qualification, tracks original-buyer budgets and distinct successor IDs, and preserves no-service and failed paths. This is established Bellman super-solution, conditional expectation, Fréchet bound and finite-action convexity mathematics. Bounded prior-art check 24 Nov UTC: [Brown and Zhang, Operations Research 2023](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.2022.2287), abstract on separable and Lagrangian upper bounds for weakly coupled DPs; [Brown, Smith and Sun, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.1090.0796), abstract on nonanticipativity relaxation and penalties. Neither is asserted to establish this robotics-specific accounting example; no independent novelty review. DDA section 5 and Appendix B already cover future data and complementary aggregation. Synthetic checks do not validate any real transfer law, approved trial, procurement behavior or economic returns.
