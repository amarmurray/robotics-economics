# Endogenous exposure and qualified order-alignment — prospective validation registry
**Not executed.** No customer data permission, robot physical approval, field exposure, experiment or original-order sample obtained. This is a measurement and falsification specification only.

**Unit and population.** Freeze a pre-action signed/registered original customer-order frame for each *policy-closed* controller/dispatch/operator/customer network; include missed, rejected, never-admitted, substituted and failed orders. Annotate demand type, customer price/collections and qualified complete-service acceptance independently of robot selection. Track all interference clusters and version IDs. If target demand responds to service history, record the time and path of original orders *under each approved policy*, not just a frozen after-action panel.

**Mechanism observables.** For each authorized source/training recipe (field trajectories; recovery/failure; teleoperation; simulation; synthetic; public/pretrained, separately provenance/rights-labeled), collect initial controller and data lineage, training/curation/compute cost, held-out A manipulation and B recovery *jointly*, after-action D original customer eligibility, time-window match, operator/dispatch slot, human safety intervention, recording indicator and missingness, accepted completed service and alternative displaced incumbent order. Merely knowing independent A and B rates or pooled benchmark success is insufficient; link all coordinates at the same policy/unit/time window.

**Prospective comparisons, requiring independent approval.** Preserve competent R and its optional free noninterfering logs; evaluate nonactuating independent target audit only on the original-order sampling frame; source-X, physical grounding-G, and changed-embodiment-E each require own qualified complete-service and rights/physical approval. Cluster-level assignment or a justified partial-interference design is required whenever shared training, operators, customers or dispatch affect another arm. Compare *direct complete-policy accepted-order contribution*, not a manufactured data value; include lost date-0 production and staged budgets. A fixed-reporter paired shadow analysis may estimate E1's six moments but identifying t requires a synchronized joint record on counterfactual X-qualified order opportunities; assignment plus a new source-target qualification audit may be necessary.

**Failure modes to test rather than assume away.** Report selection on accepted production can delete low-score/failed orders even with known denominator; policy-dependent original customer demand makes R's order shares nontransportable to X; a changed embodiment can change which service demand exists; qualification correlation can reverse the best physical investment while all marginal/pairwise rates are unchanged; shared operator capacity makes independent order extrapolation invalid; production records may be informative as training inputs while uninformative as regime diagnostics (or the reverse).

**Design-based estimands.** Under justified network-level randomized assignment with treatment consistency and bounded/no cross-cluster interference, the mean policy-closed contribution contrast includes demand, qualification, operator and displacement effects without requiring an identified intermediate triple. If actual assignment is not safe/permitted, record the unsupported cells and retain E1/selection intervals, do not pseudo-randomize historical logs or execute an unapproved intervention. Sample-size and confidence methods must use independent network clusters and appropriate design-based uncertainty. A selected rollout is not a replicate.

**What would disprove the motivating mechanism.** Repeated independently approved, versioned complete service trials showing demand-qualified alignment t at its independence benchmark with strong precision across interventions would undermine this particular higher-order explanation; evidence that protected incumbent plus copied source data identifies the same post-X opportunity law would strengthen a simpler transport/coupling architecture. Pairwise agreement alone proves neither. Hold out original customer orders for validation rather than labeling qualified robot motions as commercial value.

**Atlas/data backbone.** A source-specific robotics deployment/opportunity atlas remains separate from the required **quantitative official BLS–BEA production bridge**. No valid new official numeric crosswalk was obtained in this tranche; prior vendor demonstrations are not substituted. This protocol is not permission to reach out, purchase, obtain confidential employer data, train controllers or deploy hardware.
