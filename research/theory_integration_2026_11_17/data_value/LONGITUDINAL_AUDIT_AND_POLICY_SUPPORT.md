## Economic question and environment

When a source-data pilot changes a shared learner, target-specific grounding unlocks legally reusable data, or an independently supported embodiment adds a production recipe, **which original-buyer and worker histories must be lawfully observed to rank these investments under an adaptive controller?** The preceding static selected-history theorem identifies a three-moment payoff quotient for its fixed queue-policy menu, but it does not establish that a record of invoices or customer successes supports *longitudinal interventions*. Here program assignment, recovery, intermediate original demand, eligibility/qualification, the adaptive intervention, the terminal demand process, and two reporting decisions form **one** globally feasible causal history.

Let a connected, interference-closed customer/learner/operator cluster have a precommitted program (A_0in{P,G,E}) and a later admissible dispatch/validation decision (A_1). The common full potential-history law (mu) contains all program- and action-specific learner checkpoints (L), original eligible buyer **offers and no-offers** (Y_1,Y_2), qualification (Q), queue jobs (J_1,J_2), lawful source lineage, robot-time and operator budgets, and paid accepted-service increments. (P) obtains source experience, (G) performs licensed target grounding on held data, and (E) includes **additional** embodied and recovery resources. The data modalities remain typed: ordinary field trajectories, recovery failures, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained/public inputs are not interchangeable. A retained safe action (R) remains in every legal program menu. Program capital/compute/rights costs are subtracted once; future accepted service is the continuation value, **not** an additional independent data premium.

An audit (S_1) reveals original buyer (Y_1) for an adaptive controller, with registered probability (rho_1), after the program and initial incident but **before** (A_1). A separate audit (S_2), independently sampled before viewing terminal buyer (Y_2), reveals that offer/no-offer even when the worker is busy. Observability does not imply dispatchability, acceptance, cleared payment or transfer rights. The behavior program has registered assignment probability (p_0(a)>0), and action probability (e_1(bmid H_1,S_1,S_1Y_1)>0) for each *legal* target-policy action on its reachable histories. Audit probabilities may depend on already recorded operator state and previously chosen action; they may not covertly depend on the unrevealed buyer outcome after conditioning on that registered state. The audit itself must not change qualification, customer orders or worker durations **except through recorded downstream actions**. Actual program-policy causal comparison further requires connected-cluster consistency, sequential randomization/exchangeability, and every relevant intervention/exposure's legal and physical feasibility. These are design assumptions, **not verified field facts**.

## Proposition 1 — sequentially selected original-buyer production can be valued without an oracle kernel

Fix a legal program $a$, a full-information adaptive policy $\pi(H_1,Y_1)$ selected *after* the first audit, and finite total paid contribution $R^{a,\pi}$ (including upstream accepted services and one copy of program costs). Under the assumptions above, a logging law with independent audit draws and action-support probabilities identifies

$$
V(a,\pi)=
\mathbb E_{\mathrm{log}}\left[
\frac{
\mathbf1\{A_0=a,\ S_1=1,\ A_1=\pi(H_1,Y_1),\ S_2=1\}\,R_{\mathrm{obs}}
}{
p_0(a)\,\rho_1(H_1,a)\,
e_1(\pi\mid H_1,S_1=1,Y_1)\,
\rho_2(H_2,a,A_1)
}
\right].
\tag{1}
$$

Here $H_2$ is the **predisclosure** second-audit history, not a future settlement used to choose $A_1$; all denominator quantities are evaluated at the actually realized history. Condition on the complete latent cluster trajectory under $(a,\pi)$: integration over $A_0,S_1,A_1,S_2$ cancels four strictly positive registered probabilities in chronological order, leaving its causal value. Integrate over the *same common* $\mu$. This is the standard sequential importance-sampling / randomized-observation identity, not a newly discovered estimand. In a longer dynamic problem, the product extends over the actions and history reports needed by that policy; an unobserved state that determines the next dispatch cannot be discarded. The formula is an expectation identity, **not** a finite-sample confidence interval and not a solution to physically unsafe exploration.

If $S_1$ depends on unobserved $Y_1$ at fixed recorded $H_1$, $\rho_1$ computed from observed cells is not an outcome-independent audit propensity; (1) fails without a separately justified selection model. Likewise $e_1=0$ on a reachable required intervention path means no inverse weight or observed counterfactual there. A customer registry cannot repair an unassigned robot action, and a randomized action cannot repair missing original no-offers when reporting has unknown outcome-dependent censoring. The earlier common-law LP bounds remain necessary in such cells.

## Proposition 2 — exact scope of a minimum economic reporting requirement

For the fixed finite menu and horizon, suppose a terminal queue record proves a particular attempted original buyer service **cannot** be accepted or paid and its unreported buyer demand does not affect any subsequent learner, demand transition, policy, or contract. Its terminal reward is then known to be zero without seeing that buyer variable. On this stratum one may replace (S_2/rho_2) in (1) by **1**, scoring already-known earlier accepted receipts and program costs with their original weights. Conditional on the complete pre-(S_2) history, this replaces an independent mean-one random multiplier by its expectation; the estimator's second moment weakly falls (strictly when a nonzero known contribution and an imperfect audit coexist). This is conditional Rao–Blackwellization. It **does not** license ignoring unserved demand in a longer, recursive firm: if it changes future target acquisition, market discovery, qualification, or demand-response learning, its continuation value can be nonzero. Nor does it prove a global minimum-variance estimator: fixed known costs could be removed from the random weighting separately.

More generally, given a finite potential-history support and **fixed** legal policy menu, reporting is payoff-sufficient if every legally feasible completion in a recorded observation fiber has the same whole-policy payoff vector, or—under stipulated linear structural constraints—if that vector is identified on the compatible law polytope. The coarsest partition by whole-menu payoff signatures is an abstract economic reporting quotient for a saturated full-history model; it is not automatically attainable as a field measurement or stable when new policies or future periods are added. A causal action-support condition remains separate from reporting sufficiency.

## Exact synthetic three-date counterexample: naive selected logs reverse the legal program choice

To demonstrate rather than calibrate, take two common latent bits (z,win{0,1}), independent and fair in the displayed counterexample (the executable checks also enumerate *all* 165 four-atom laws with integer masses /8). Assign (P,G,E) with probability (1/3). Under (P), learner checkpoint (L=z), first recovery job (J_1=w), and intermediate original offer (Y_1=z). Under (G,E), let (L=1,J_1=0,Y_1=w). A permitted (T) at the intermediate date requires (J_1=0) and can be productive or a licensed deliberate validation; (R) remains safe. One worker clears an existing (J_1) within the intermediate date. If (T,z=0,w=1), a new (J_2=1) arrives for (P/G), making the shared worker unavailable at terminal service; (E) is stipulated to have truly separate recovery capacity. Qualification at the final date is (Q_2=1{L=1 mathrm{or} (T,w=0)}). Final original demand is (Y_2=1{z=1 mathrm{or} w=1 mathrm{or} (T,w=0)}) for (P,E), or (1{w=1 mathrm{or} (T,z=0)}) for (G). Thus trials can alter learner qualification and original demand, but incident queues can block paid production. Intermediate qualified/accepted original service earns (1/2), terminal qualified/accepted original service earns (1) only if (J_2=0). Program costs are respectively (1/8,1/5,1/4), subtracted once; the common safe production baseline of 6 cancels in comparisons. These are **stipulated normalized** payoffs and laws, not observations or estimates.

Respect a deliberately narrow rights/physical menu: (P) is production-only (retain (R), or (T) only when (L=1,J_1=0,Y_1=1)); (G) includes an independently *licensed* deliberate target probe (T) whenever (J_1=0), even if (Y_1=0); (E) has independent hardware/worker but no deliberate target-probe right (retain or original-buyer adaptive). This rights distinction is a **synthetic assumption** to test the legal menu; allowing (P/E) to probe changes the ranking and invalidates the example's claimed *full-menu* ordering. No arm may choose (T) while (J_1=1). The behavior controller chooses T with probability 0 when J1=1. For P/E, where only production with a positively audited original buyer is licensed, T has probability 3/4 when S1=1, Y1=1 and L=1 and 0 otherwise; the log cannot use Y1 if the audit was not performed. For G's licensed deliberate validation, T has probability 3/4 after an audited positive Y1, and 1/4 otherwise. Each logged action is feasible under its program's stated physical/rights restrictions. The first random audit has (rho_1=1/4) when (J_1=1), (1/2) otherwise. Terminal (rho_2=1/4) when (J_2=1), (1/2) otherwise; audits are independent of unreported offers conditional on their queue variables.

For precommitted legal program+controller packages, direct entire-history evaluation and (1) agree exactly:

| Program and legal best policy | True incremental net production | Naive matched complete-case mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| P, original-buyer adaptive | (1/2) | (41/88) |
| G, licensed deliberate probe | (11/20) | (99/130) |

**True:** E > G > P. **Naively observed:** G > E > P. The bias is caused by joint action selection, outcome auditing, learner state, and the incident queue—not because a benchmark skill change was converted into money. Correctly registered sequential sampling recovers all seven legal policy means **exactly in expectation**, including both positive and zero original demand. The synthetic G-probe second moment of the whole-program IPW contribution falls from (391/25) to (151/10), a reduction of (27/50), when the unnecessary terminal audit is bypassed on a known zero-service (J_2=1) history.
