**20 November 2026. Design only.** No field test, private customer access, customer recruitment, hardware intervention or purchases are authorized.

**Boundary and denominator.** Register the pre-policy original eligible customer–robot–operator–learner network, including refusals, zero sales, specialist displacement, common buyer procurement parent, contractual prices, service quality and receipts. Randomly selected accepted motion clips are **not** a representative original customer denominator.

**Physical input audit before statistical transport.** Obtain, from separately authorized engineering review, versioned old R, X first-step intervention semantics, safe/unsafe states, all reachable disturbance successors, residual service clock and operator schedule after each outcome, post-failure workpiece geometry and environment state, licensed rights to invoke R and any newly embodied spare, and whether recovery is permitted after an unsafe fault. Require an independent completeness/soundness argument for the **conservative** transition abstraction, not merely successful simulation. Separate a nominal graph that omits rare X failures from one with all plausible physical tails; document how the winning region and admission differ.

**Three nonexchangeable observations.** (1) Baseline original R qualified service and old success distributions; (2) signed X/fallback game support and the safe goal predicate, including entire shared network; (3) actual buyer paid accepted service and net contribution under a specified policy/menu. Only (1)+(2) could support a conditional physical retention assertion. (3) and true external resource opportunities are needed to value the intervention economically. Randomized complete-network arm means can identify expected original market policy value under valid assignment, but cannot recover a casewise counterfactual successful replay of an irreversible order.

**Data acquisition discrimination.** Register field successes, failures/recovery, teleoperation trajectories, deliberate target probes, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained inputs separately. For each proposed X or G update: lineage, legal data rights, compute/curation and operator cost, qualified target-state changes, changed state-occupancy/exposure and joint accepted production. Explicitly log whether production-generated data exists only because a risky policy was admitted; safe gating changes the data-acquisition process. New embodiment E must document manufacturing delay, hardware qualification, staffing and changed customer service windows.

**Failure criteria.** If no signed network-closed disturbance and recovery model exists, the finite-state winning set is a mathematical illustration, not a safety certificate. If no buyer/receipt panel exists, do not assign it dollar returns. If original-network arms cannot be assigned independently, do not extrapolate a site-average randomization to market-wide displacement without a specified interference/boundary model. If the qualified physical intervention is not approved, use non-actuating evidence and conditional theory only.
