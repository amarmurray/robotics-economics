# Student problems: transporting source-data information to qualified customer production

**Separate solutions:** SIGNAL_KERNEL_TRANSPORT_SOLUTIONS.md. These exercises use conditional, synthetic complete-network contribution—not field robot evidence. Preserve signed physical approvals, original orders and retained specialist choices.

1. **Report transport inequality.** For a fixed approved network and c, derive |W_c(K')-W_c(K)| <= sum_z p(z)R_c(z)TV(K'(.|z),K(.|z)). Explain why using the maximum absolute contribution instead of statewise range is often loose. Which hidden learned-policy changes invalidate the comparison?

2. **Decision margin rather than generic error.** Prove the pairwise sufficient condition ensuring that a source-optimal report-conditioned qualified service stays optimal for a second kernel. Explain why identical policy maps do *not* imply zero value from improving report accuracy.

3. **Exact stale-policy regret.** With high and low equally likely and S=(3/4,3/4), G=(3,-1), T=(4,-6), symmetric report accuracy q, derive the values of (G,S) and (T,S), their crossing, and their expected outcomes at q=3/5 and 9/10. Distinguish policy regret from physically unsafe action.

4. **Robust target transport.** Assume q_target is unknown in [3/5,9/10]. Prove the maximin report rule and guarantee against *all randomized report rules*, not only the sixteen deterministic policies. Verify the no-report benchmark.

5. **Calibration value versus naive regret.** An unobserved target accuracy equals 3/5 or 9/10 with prior 1/2, independent of pre-source customer state. Compute the ex-ante economic value of learning the target kernel *before* an individual customer report. Why is this not 7/10? Describe how a free calibration mechanism changes a paid target-grounding decision.

6. **Causal identification and new bodies.** Design the minimal (prospective only) approved source-reporter/target-controller cross-play audit needed to identify K0 and K1 for the same opportunity z. Explain where a new embodiment changes the feasible qualification set and how complete customer orders should be carried through the economic evaluation. State what remains unidentified when target shadow reporting lacks approval.
