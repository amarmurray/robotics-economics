# Prospective original-buyer, learner, rights and shared-worker evidence protocol

**Unpopulated, not authorization to experiment.** No actual matched original-buyer panel, signed cross-site data rights, cleared payments, probe or intervention is available. Linked theory: [three-date buyer-budget and queue analysis](THREE_DATE_BUYER_BUDGET_QUEUE_AND_GROUNDING.md).

The unit is a connected customer/robot/worker cluster and its time-stamped t=0 investment, t=1 dispatch and t=2 purchase horizon, not a sampled trajectory frame. A original buyer has a documented lifetime contract budget and actual offered/no-order status for early and late eligible windows. C is a separate original buyer. Log both unserved and accepted *original* A/C orders under the same available price/SLA terms; distinguish hypothetical modeled demand from real customer offers, rejected service, settled paid service, reimbursements/refunds and retention. Capture conditional margins and paired A/C demand **jointly** within permitted clusters, not only two independent aggregated conversion rates.

Pre-register qualified task-bundle recipes (acceptance gate, safety and model version); Q_C0 and date-2 Q_C; provenance and separately costed field, recovery, paid teleoperation, simulated, synthetic and pretrained streams; algorithm/update timing, true model adaptation, grounding-to-target alignment and possibility of harmful *forced* training; optional retained R. Require actual data license scope by source, target end market, embodiment, permitted derivative model and revocation. G is *forbidden* when licensing or physical authorization is absent, not an arm with an estimated zero effect. E must prove separately manufactured robot-time, staffing, deployment lag and power, not free parallel throughput.

Recovery evidence: t=1 event time, job size and severity, worker shift boundary, work units actually cleared, t=2 outstanding backlog and whether t=2 robot-time could legally be used during repair. Explicitly test the example's L2=J with one worker unit clearing a two-unit job. Track all other arrivals, staff overtime, incident failures and censored downtime; if these violate assumed conservation, replace the kernel with the full observed queue network.

Minimal policy row is (eligible A early offer and accepted paid decision u, A remaining budget, original A late D2, original C late C2, date-2 qualified status Q_C, early incident J and unresolved L2, lawful precommitment report S and timestamp, settled A/C payment, E independent resources, data-use rights). Cluster stratification: relevant markets, customer demand volume, segment, service recipe, hardware, learner version, worker configuration, season and spillovers. The necessary G target statistic here is x_G=Pr(D2^G=1,C2^G=1) plus G qualification/incident joint outcomes under the relevant *policy*, not just Q accuracy. Pilot-induced changes in original demand and offers must be recorded causally, not assumed invariant.

Identification design, subject to **separate** physical/ethical/legal approval: a truly supported prospective G assignment at connected cluster level, ideally paired with P/old control and a full original-buyer/no-order audit, records no-service sites and failed orders. Ex-ante assignment probability e>0 and predeclared observed-audit propensity rho(h,d,c)>0 are needed where used for weighting; if rho depends on unobserved demand or is zero because recording is forbidden, do not use a missing-at-random estimator. Within an allowed design, verify consistency, conditional exchangeability, interference/exposure mapping, stable buyer terms, independent E capacity and timing. Weighting identifies a *supported population policy*, not rights-ineligible customers. A G classifier's 3/4 regime accuracy in the witness is hypothetical and must be calibrated on actual legally observed G-specific paired buyer responses before using any reported information value.

Economic discriminators: price paid net of refunds/overhead, unique A budget consumed, C contribution conditional on overlap with A and worker downtime, added manufacturing/grounding/training/curation/license costs, lost opportunity to keep older R or dispatch P, and marginal robot-time per cluster. Compare the P data-learning marginal with G's reuse of existing licensed data and E's additional C slot using *equal paid acceptance recipes*, not percentages multiplied by labor wages. Treat t=2 customer demand after t=1 sale as potentially endogenous. The t=0 report must arrive before commitment to count as program-choice information.
