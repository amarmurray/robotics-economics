# Instructor solutions — cross-date worker occupancy and data value

**Separate from** [student problems](WORKER_CARRYOVER_EXERCISES.md), 22 November 2026.

1. On an admissible t=2 history h, the target-minus-R full paid return is (r2T-r2R)+beta*d3[Pr(F3(S31 or S32)|h,do T)-Pr(F3(S31 or S32)|h,do R)]. The controller chooses T iff this is positive (either on tie if indifferent); otherwise R. It observes t=2 predispatch signed Q, actual eligible orders, contract expected-pay category and H2, not future t=3 demand, H3 or t=2/3 cleared payment.
2. Put p_a=q_a-j_a. Any fixed q=3/4 and H marginal 1/2 has j in [1/4,1/2], making p in [1/4,1/2]. For old R with p_R=3/8, d3=2 and target immediate premium delta2=0, target is preferable if j=1/4 but not if j=1/2. These joint laws exist by Fréchet coupling. This is an *economic* response, not technical-negative-transfer evidence.
3. H2=1 makes T infeasible and t=3 target paid opportunity probability (1/2)(1/2)=1/4; future return 2+2+2(1/4)=9/2. H2=0 with 00/11 leads R with 2+3=5. H2=0 with 10/01 produces T at t=2 and signed expected t=3 target, yielding 9/4+4=25/4. Reweighting by h and z gives 5-(1/2)h+(5/4)z. B t=1 pays 7/4 versus R's 2, so with L probability1/2, J_P=13/2+(1/2)[-1/4-(1/2)h+(5/4)z]_+.
4. Let A be a singleton eligible order and F a free worker. z=P(A∩F); P(A)=1/2 and P(F)=1/2 give z∈[0,1/2]. The bound is attained by assigning busy to 10/01 (z=0) or 00/11 (z=1/2), each at probability1/4. The optimal B when z=0 would give date1-to3 13/2 versus R7, so it is not chosen and J_P=13/2. At z=1/2, B conditional on L gives57/8, so J_P=105/16. E=209/32 lies strictly between. Joint B and H histories, not their marginals, determine the investment ranking. Mixtures attain intermediate z.
5. Selected target service requires H2=0 and S2 nonzero; lost opportunities under busy operators and the no-offer categories are conditioned out. The adequate prospective panel includes cluster assignment, t0 source/modalities/rights/checkpoint, t1 L/W and B/R eligibility/assignment, predispatch t2 Q1/Q2/O1/O2/expected paid classes and H2, t2 actual choice and intervention, t3 predispatch Q/O/H3 after each t2 action, independent R/E physical capacity and all after-service acceptance/refunds/cleared payments. A counterexample to the model is discovery that R itself uses the target recovery worker (destroying its stipulated free-busy baseline).
6. Two workers alone do not enable simultaneous execution when the one robot-time slot remains binding. More robot-time plus genuinely independent worker slots can create additive simultaneous output (subject to task deadlines and other bottlenecks). An LP resource-price certificate values a **specified** additional feasible slot conditional on a known opportunity table, but cannot estimate unknown joint Q/order laws or predict extra customer paid opportunities without causal evidence.
