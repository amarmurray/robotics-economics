## Economic premises
A corrective improvement does two things to the next production period. It changes the observations generated by routine work, and it changes the supply of qualified human or digital support before a new customer or manufacturing commitment expires. These objects are distinct. An incident can consume the same engineer time needed to ground an update or commission a new body. A reusable source record may replace a slower target reference experiment, but does not eliminate the need for a qualified target and a physical body. The economic value of an observation depends on the *joint timing* of these inputs, not just the transfer rate. The previous [dated-order audit](DATED_CAPACITY_ORDER_WITNESSES.md) fixed endowments; here the source policy generates them.

A concrete motivation is parcel manipulation. Li et al.'s RSS 2023 [Robin study](https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss19/p023.html) describes learned ranking using production pick observations. A related [ISER 2025 author publication abstract](https://www.amazon.science/publications/learning-to-optimize-package-picking-for-large-scale-real-world-robot-induction), inspected November 25, reports over two million evaluated picks and a 20% relative reduction in pick failures using a learned adjustment to heuristic-sampled picks in test workcells resembling Robin. This is technical evidence that a policy can change which failures arise; it establishes neither downstream retention, released crew time, target qualification, settled service nor returns. Do not equate its relative failure reduction to a dollar saving or claim commercial causal evidence. DDA already studies production-generated data and future economic value; duality, dynamic programming and Fréchet bounds are classical. The candidate synthesis is the endogenous alignment of data substitution with a dated investment bottleneck.

## 1. A minimal common production/learning system
Two dates of operation and a single final customer window. At date zero a feasible source controller (routine or improved) executes current jobs with ordinary learning enabled. Its net captured source contribution M includes actually incurred operating/capture costs and displaced work. It creates a joint law of (H,E,Q,F) observed before date-one choices:
- H: whole crew/validation slots available before deadline, after prior recovery and support obligations;
- E: a lawfully retained, usable donor record, not merely an incident; it may come from a recovery, successful trajectory, teleoperation or other eligible source;
- Q: target B is already qualified through ordinary routine learning; the old policy remains usable on A;
- F: a body purchase can actually be ordered and paid for in time. Hold it fixed where studying crew constraints; financing here is not an additional profit.

The recipient has one installed flexible body and two original opportunities, A worth u and B worth v in *net captured contribution*, each requiring one body-window. A is already qualified. At most one additional body can be purchased for c; commissioning consumes d_c crew slots before the service window. With Q=0, B becomes qualified by either grounding a retained record (E=1) for g and d_g slots or buying a deliberate target reference for x and d_x slots. Both routes are assumed equally effective and lawful after completion; their costs include compute, adaptation and verification once. Both routes are available in both programs, not a deliberately weakened ordinary comparator. Qualification and commissioning use the same dated crew; all other inputs and demand are fixed in this benchmark. Neither action requires running unsafe failures. No value from extra training or speculative resale is omitted in this finite horizon; to extend it add common successor service. Use the best feasible route, including no route.

Let b be the body purchase (0 or 1), with b=1 feasible only if F=1 and H>=d_c. Let R_b be the minimum feasible qualification cost: zero if Q=1, otherwise min(g if E=1 and H-b d_c>=d_g; x if H-b d_c>=d_x), with an empty minimum infinity. With positive u,v and one order of each type, the terminal optimized value is
    W_b = u + [v-u+b u-R_b]_+ - b c .
This follows by choosing A alone, B alone, or (only with two bodies) A+B. If Q=1 and v<u the same expression still applies. Let V=max of W_0 and feasible W_1; ties choose no purchase. The net purchase condition is
    [v-R_1]_+ - [v-u-R_0]_+ > c .
This is a **conditional investment return**, not the physical MPK of a free already commissioned body. Commissioning may displace qualification so the gross expression can even be negative. Physical MPK at fixed qualified recipes and already commissioned capacity is a vector of accepted A and B service changes; if both qualify and v>u, adding a body yields one extra A and no extra B. Captured dollars and physical orders must not be interchanged.

## 2. The evidence-dependent admission window
Restrict to 0<c<u, 0<=g<x<v-u, integers 0<d_g<=d_x and d_c>0. Both alternatives to make B ready are worth doing whenever feasible. Because a retained record unlocks the cheaper and no slower route, R_0=R_1 whenever both finite. Then the optimal start indicator is exactly
    I(H,E,Q,F)=F * { Q 1[H>=d_c]
                    +(1-Q)(1[H>=d_c+d_x]+E 1[d_c+d_g<=H<d_c+d_x]) }.
Proof: with B already qualified the extra A earns u>c if commissioning fits. Otherwise, if a qualification route and commissioning jointly fit, the additional A earns u>c at the same minimum qualification cost. If no joint route fits, buying the body completes at most A and costs c; retaining the incumbent can still complete A. The two disjoint windows for B-not-ready are exhaustive. This proves the claim including exact corners under strict inequalities. Equal durations eliminate the evidence-dependent admission window. Higher reference costs, other alternative service, arbitrary order books or staggered capacity require W_b or the full joint production menu, not this indicator.

**Theorem (conditional admission and substitute delay).** A useful retained record changes new-body admission only in the middle window, conditional on no native target qualification and sufficient dated funds. It can still have positive economic value outside that window. In this restriction, if source improvement removes E=1 but raises usable H by at least d_x-d_g, holding Q,F fixed, any previously profitable start remains feasible and profitable: H>=d_c+d_g implies new H>=d_c+d_x. This protects the binary start, not value, since reference costs more. If time is released after the window, or less than this difference, a lost record can instead cancel a start. This is not a global monotonicity theorem for learning or for acquisitions carrying obligations.

For the smallest clocks d_c=d_g=1,d_x=2, and Q=0,F=1, the conditional gross value of access E (including optimized acquisition choices but before any differential source capture fee) is
    H=0: 0
    H=1: v-u-g
    H=2: u-c+x-g
    H>=3: x-g.
At H=1 the record enables a substitution on the existing body. At H=2 it both saves the reference expense and permits a new body. At H>=3 the alternative reference fits and the value is only the cost saving. Hence the value at H=2 strictly exceeds that at H>=3 despite more available support at H>=3. Its ranking against H=1 is ambiguous. If F=0, the H=2 and higher value is x-g; with Q=1 it is zero. A single constant data value per record or a smooth everywhere-positive transfer multiplier misses this switch. Optional evidence availability is never harmful in the matched state. Source improvement may alter that state and its probabilities.

## 3. Coupling this window to the source choice
For source program r let p_r denote the joint law of observed H,E,Q,F. It is generated by current allocation, safe exposure, capture, rights and ordinary training. For independent opportunities of a common type with incident probability f and conditional usable yield k, an incident-only stream has P(E=1)=1-(1-f k)^A at A attempts; do not use that expression under shared failures or when source/target transfer depends on their joint state. Successful trajectories, simulated records and external data can be separate routes, not intrinsically equivalent to incidents. For an instrumented successful job, write e_0 and e_1 for ordinary and enhanced *usable* record probabilities conditional on a fixed observable stratum and equal service. With known conditional H,Q,F and cost a, the incremental value of capture is beta(e_1-e_0)(V_1-V_0)-a. If capture must be committed before H resolves, use the joint expectation under the actual intervention law, not perfect foresight. If instrumentation itself changes timing, re-solve complete programs.

Define source r's total value and induced-start rate
    J_r=E_r[M]+beta E_r[V(H,E,Q,F)] ; i_r=E_r[I].
Any initial acquisition cost not included in M is subtracted once. Optimize over the lawful old and improved routine plus optional instrumentation and external acquisition; no extra "data premium". Each program learns and has its own state distribution. The exact response of start rate is the difference of three joint masses:
    Δi = ΔP(F,Q,H>=d_c) + ΔP(F,not Q,H>=d_c+d_x)
          + ΔP(F,not Q,E,d_c+d_g<=H<d_c+d_x).
This links directly to a dated entry of the common [induced-deployment map](https://github.com/amarmurray/robotics-economics/blob/97c709f527e7312cc4023a3266db7753b0cdc522/papers/learning_and_capital/README.md). It is an expected first-generation installation response per source decision in this restriction, not a constant G coefficient, spectral prediction or additional value term. To obtain a response per installed source body compare with the best no-addition program, including displacement of other source jobs, and divide by the increment. A customer or rival price response changes u,v and can remove the maintained strict region. If F becomes endogenously correlated with evidence, retain its joint occurrence rather than multiplying funding and capture marginals.

### Sharp identification and economic-weighted measurement
Suppose Q,H,F are observed jointly but E is recorded only as an overall rate e. Write w=P(F,not Q,d_c+d_g<=H<d_c+d_x). The remaining unknown start mass is j=P(E=1,window), exactly in [max(0,e+w-1), min(e,w)]. Both ends attainable by nesting or disjointly allocating E; convex mixtures attain intermediate values. For expected *availability* value, hold the other state law fixed and let d(s)=V(s,E=1)-V(s,E=0)>=0. Then V=E[V_0]+E[E*d(s)] (E in the product denotes the binary event). Given only the total rate e and the state distribution, sharp upper/lower bounds assign E first to largest/smallest d(s), with fractional allocation at the boundary. Exchange proof: moving a small positive mass from a low to a high weight weakly increases the objective. These bounds are for a matched state, not a counterfactual that preserves endogenous capture while changing recovery. With joint constraints on E,Q or lawful retention, restrict the feasible allocations. Compare independently evolving program bounds with each other; do not interpret this diagnostic as a natural mediated causal effect.

## 4. Constructive source policy comparisons
All quantities synthetic, beta=1. u=4,v=10,c=2,g=1,x=3,d_c=d_g=1,d_x=2; Q=0,F=1 throughout. The optimized recipient values/start indicators are
| Dated crew H | No donor (value,start) | Donor (value,start) |
|---|---|---|
|0|4,0|4,0|
|1|4,0|9,0|
|2|7,0|11,1|
|3|9,1|11,1|

Consider a class of source jobs currently completed using recovery. The old process produces a donor record and H=1. The improved process avoids recovery, releases one *usable dated* crew slot, and adds 0.5 to current source contribution. Routine successful capture produces a usable record with probability 1/4 (not zero); enhanced capture raises this to 3/4 and costs 0.5 per such job. Both can buy the external reference. Old value is 9; improved ordinary is 8.5; improved with optional enhanced capture is 10. Optimizing across the alternatives selects the instrumented improvement and raises start probability from zero to 3/4. This does not depend on deliberate harmful failures; all alternatives satisfy the same safety/acceptance constraints.

Conversely, suppose old recovery already leaves H=2; the improvement releases time outside the relevant window (H remains 2) and adds 4 to current contribution. Same ordinary success capture rate 1/4, enhanced 3/4, but capture fee now 3. Old value=11, improved ordinary=12, enhanced=11. The firm chooses improved ordinary; expected starts fall from 1 to 1/4 while value rises. These are strict optimized comparisons, not merely reruns of a parameter: the relevant dated resource transition and capture alternative differ. In either case keeping the old program is admissible. When the reference route is instantaneous or cheap with equal dated occupation, the particular data-dependent admission effect vanishes. If B has no remaining demand or an installed body cannot serve both types even after qualification, re-evaluate the actual qualified portfolio.

## 5. Empirical contrast and next intellectual question
The critical row is not "failures per hour". It is an original source cohort with actual attempted and unattempted service, ordinary capture on success/failure, permitted reuse, target-native readiness before additional grounding, matched deliberate reference route cost and dated duration, and the same crew's actual free commissioning interval. Link accepted original A/B work and original offers/refusals to installed/commissioned bodies and dated payments, never count a planned start as accepted service. A matched capture × source improvement design should preserve all old safe policies, same customer denominators and learning budgets, and allow independent evolution and target reference in every arm. Cluster at the shared release/crew level. Test whether extra retained records alter starts mainly within the intermediate dated window and whether the effect disappears after native qualification. Record direct output, avoided real support costs and private contribution separately; for interfirm welfare, remove transfer payments and model external effects. In inspection an infrequent fault may supply the record but scheduled inspection windows constrain commissioning; in manufacturing a QA/fixture engineer may be both reference and commissioning resource. These are application hypotheses, not claims of observed role overlap.

The next dependency is to endogenize the choice of the reference technology itself: can a new source embodiment or digital diagnostic reduce the extra dated slots d_x-d_g, changing which market receives the next body and the subsequent mixture of production-generated evidence? Compare a stable external reference, shared or partially compatible reference, and learned grounding on the same feasible joint production menu, with heterogeneous cohort demand and an explicit no-addition path. Derive the local response of the induced-deployment matrix and distinguish acceleration from permanent opportunity expiry. Empirical commercial identification and contemporary official production backbone remain open. No novelty claim for the Fréchet, allocation or Bellman machinery.
