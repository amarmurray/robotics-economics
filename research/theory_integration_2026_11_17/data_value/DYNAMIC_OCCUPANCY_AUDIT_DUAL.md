# Dynamic occupation-measure audit dual: pricing a coupled successor opportunity constraint

24 November 2026 UTC (23 November Eastern). Continuation of [reoptimized policy feature frontier](REOPTIMIZED_POLICY_FEATURE_FRONTIER.md) within *When Capabilities Become Production*. This chapter supplies a way to bound optimized values without enumerating complete contingent rules. It develops the earlier [dynamic paid resource certificate](DYNAMIC_PAID_RESOURCE_CERTIFICATE.md): the new constraint is not simply an additive worker budget, but an **original-population, jointly auditable upper bound on successful licensed customer successor flows across distinct policy-reachable report classes**. The distinction from a rectangular collection of separately valid conditional transition bounds matters. All numerical examples are synthetic.

## 1. A common-law outer flow certificate

Let a finite time-expanded history graph represent admissible whole-cluster production/learning decisions. A node is a history of information lawfully available before the next decision, including original buyer identity/order, license scope, model version, robot and operator resources, deadlines, queued work, and already earned cash. A terminal node records remaining admissible salvage if relevant. Use a common original eligible-cluster denominator; do not condition the population on sales or successful reports. A coarse graph may be an outer relaxation if it contains the images of every feasible history, action and transition and overestimates conditional receipts; it is not automatically a sufficient Markov state.

For each edge e=(h,a,h') let z_e>=0 denote its unconditional probability flow under some common population law and some nonanticipating policy. Let x_ha=sum_{h'} z_e. Outflow from each nonterminal node equals its incoming flow plus the fixed initial distribution μ; terminal edges may lead to a zero-value sink. Time-discounted edge payoff ρ_e bounds above the actual discounted conditional reward, including earned accepted customer receipts, resource/acquisition/curation costs and terminal salvage once each. If the reward itself depends on unobserved details, the bound must be valid under the selection of histories/actions represented by that edge.

Conditional kernel restrictions such as l_ha,h' x_ha <= z_e <= u_ha,h' x_ha are linear. General finite polytopal conditional restrictions can be written similarly by multiplying by x_ha; zero action occupancy needs no conditional distribution. Additional inequalities Bz<=b are allowed only when established for **every** feasible candidate policy under **every** law in the evidence set. They may be physical or contractual restrictions, or original-population probability bounds on jointly licensed, authorized, timely, technically qualified and accepted successor activities. A cap on actual paid successor flows can sometimes be inherited from a common parent order/eligibility envelope, even when different actions visit different report states. An incumbent-only conditional frequency or a survey of unpaired successful reports generally cannot provide that uniform cap.

Let P be the polytope given by conservation and the valid local/coupled restrictions. Every feasible (π,q) induces a z in P, by the law of total probability, regardless of which report states π creates. Therefore

    sup_{q in Q, π in Π} J(π,q) <= max_{z in P} ρ·z.

The LP is an outer certificate: it may mix inconsistent conditional laws, fractional schedules, or grant an outer coarse state access to unavailable information. Its optimizer need not be implementable. It bounds the *law-informed* optimum and hence any robust implementable optimum. A valid lower certificate is a single lawful fixed candidate rule with a lower bound uniform in q; it is not the optimum of an arbitrary lower relaxation.

**Dual / Bellman form.** For B including all local inequality rows, choose λ>=0 and node potentials u with terminal u=0. If each edge satisfies

    ρ_e - (B^T λ)_e <= u(h)-u(h'),

then conservation implies Σ_e z_e[u(h)-u(h')]=Σ_h μ_h u(h). Multiplication and summation give

    J(π,q) <= μ·u + λ·b.

This is weak duality by direct telescoping. Finite feasible bounded P gives equality of the primal LP optimum and its linear-programming dual, not equality with true policy value. With local row polytopes separated from B's cross-node audit rows, fix λ for only those audit rows and solve an optimistic Bellman recursion on the local polytopes with adjusted edge payoffs ρ_e−(B^T λ)_e; summing the local Bellman inequalities against action occupancies proves the same bound. Minimizing the resulting bound over λ recovers the outer LP optimum by finite LP duality (assuming the local conservation/kernel-flow relaxation is feasible/bounded and the additional constraints feasible). Rectangular upper recursion alone corresponds to λ=0. Complexity is in represented nodes, edges and constraints rather than the count of complete policies; no claim of polynomial complexity in the original number of latent task variables or full histories. A compressed representation needs a separately established outer mapping or sufficient state.

**Elimination.** A currently less profitable action cannot be pruned merely using its present receipt gap or the incumbent's occupied states. For a history, a conditional upper on *all* successors of the action may exclude it against a feasible alternative lower from that same history and law family. At the root one can discard an entire investment if its outer upper falls below another investment's implementable robust lower. Conditioning a global cap on a low-probability reached history can increase its conditional allowance; do not reuse the unconditional b at that node. Reachability of all candidate histories, not incumbent occupancy alone, enters the conservation constraints. Unreached states need structural bounds or prospective supported observations, not zeros.

## 2. Three-date grounding and two report-specific successor routes

One incumbent worker and body per original cluster, one service slot per date, all cash net of variable expense and β=1. The baseline R supplies retained service 4 at date 0, 1 at date 1 and 1 at date 2, hence 6. G replaces date-0 retained service with a grounded paid recipe net 3 (includes grounding/curation cost); it creates one licensed predecision report H=A or B, each with probability 1/2. H describes which of two heterogeneous target lineages is relevant to this cluster. At date 1 the worker either performs retained service 1, or verifies the indicated lineage instead (zero date-1 receipts, no extra cost in this example). Verification can qualify a distinct original customer's authorized premium service at date 2. If the jointly rights-cleared, timely, qualified and accepted successor event occurs, it earns 1+p instead of retained service 1. Otherwise the retained date-2 service is available. At most one date-2 service and one premium opportunity per cluster; no double counting of fallback. The date-2 premium report is available before date-2 dispatch, not the date-1 verification decision. There is no extra data premium. These are assumed feasible schedules, not safety approvals.

Let q_h denote the unconditional probability of H=h and success if verified in that report class, with each q_h<=1/4 (conditional success <=1/2). A common law can be constructed by independently assigning the other potential programs' outcomes and drawing H and a latent success bit with these joint masses. The only decision information at date 1 is H; conditional verification randomization, if admitted, is independent of the latent bit. Then a fixed pure rule verifying in class h adds p q_h−1/2 to G's base 5. Optimal G within this example is

    V_G=max{6, 5+max(0,pq_A−1/2)+max(0,pq_B−1/2)}.

The initial 6 option declines the investment. This is crucial: after committed G, its own no-verification value is 5, not 6.

Let x_h be verification flow and y_h joint paid premium-success flow. Then 0<=x_h<=1/2, 0<=y_h<=x_h/2. Any law-independent original-population authorization/eligibility bound that all successful premium paths inherit gives y_A+y_B<=c. For example, only a fraction c of original eligible clusters can obtain the one premium successor authorization under any admissible G continuation, even if verification improves technical capability. This last hypothesis requires actual structural or matched complete-program evidence and cannot be inferred from sales under R, which never creates this route. Under a forced-verification-both audit in the stipulated graph, a bound q_A+q_B<=c implies the same inequality for every selective verification policy because it is a subset of those potential successes and there is no cross-cluster or other policy-dependent change in the law. Without that bridge, a forced audit alone is not enough.

The committed G objective is 5−x_A−x_B+p(y_A+y_B). The local inequalities imply −Σx <= −2Σy. Thus the coupled outer certificate is

    U_G=max{6, 5+(p−2)_+ c},       0<=c<=1/2.

For p>=2, a global dual multiplier λ=p−2 on the success cap and local multiplier 2 on each inequality y_h−x_h/2<=0 remove all positive residual from the variable objective. For p<2 the same local multiplier alone leaves a nonpositive success coefficient, with λ=0. This proves the bound without enumerating policies. It is attained in the flow relaxation by splitting y totaling c between classes, setting x=2y for p>=2, or selecting x=y=0 otherwise. Rectangular recursion without the global cap gives max{6,5+(p−2)_+/2}. The common-law cap can be much sharper even when every individual transition bound is valid.

At p=6 compare independently staffed E, which leaves R unchanged and adds a separate contract net 1/2, value 13/2. X buys additional source data for 3/2; while preserving retained work its distinct date-2 premium succeeds with probability 1/4, so its value is 6−3/2+p/4; optional X at p=6 earns 6. Assume mutually exclusive acquisition authorizations over this horizon. In a low-law world q_A=1/4, q_B=0, G optimally earns 6; in a high-law world q_A=q_B=1/4, G earns 7. Both respect the same report-class frequency and local conditional upper bounds, and have identical complete R observations. We do not claim they have identical measured conditional success rates. Rectangular upper 7 cannot settle G/E. If a validated uniform original-population cap c=1/4 holds, the coupled bound is 6, strictly below E=6.5, attained by the low-law world. If instead a complete lawful verify-both audit supports q_A+q_B>=1/2, the single verify-both policy earns at least 4+6(1/2)=7 and strictly beats E. More generally that fixed rule wins when the supported lower exceeds 5/12. No unidentified law-informed choice is needed.

An instructive relaxation gap: at c=3/8, local caps imply the best pure-law-informed committed value over compatible laws at p=6 is max{5,6,4+6(3/8)}=25/4, with the upper endpoint reached by q_A=1/4,q_B=1/8. The flow LP gives 13/2 by choosing verification and success masses that need not arise jointly under a lawful report-only rule and a common law. Randomization independent of latent success cannot exceed the best pure rule for a fixed law. A tie from an outer certificate does not identify a tie in achievable value.

Current economics alone ranks G's date-0 3 below R's 4, and verification's date-1 0 below retained 1. The high-law counterexample reverses both myopic comparisons after licensed successor service. This is the concrete reason old-price/current-output dominance is not enough to eliminate a source action.

## 3. Transport, free routine data and competing embodiments

For exogenous p with the same buyer law and admissible menu, c=1/4 certifies G<E for p<8 (assuming constant E=6.5); at p=8 upper ties. With c=1/2 a high-law verify-both rule beats E for p>5. X's own value varies with p; these are G/E contrasts, not a full-menu ranking at every p. A customer-facing price or queue shortening can change the set of eligible premium orders, report timing, rights or technical qualification; then c and the local row bounds can change. At p=6, if a supported upper on increase in original eligibility cap is η, replace c with min(1/2,1/4+η), provided all other bounds still hold. The strict G<E certificate survives η<1/8. A smaller mean queue duration alone gives no such transport bridge.

Free optional routine production P that reproduces **the entire G transition, verification menu and costs except the date-0 lost contribution** earns one more than committed G. In the low-law world selective verification gives 7, beats E, and demonstrates that the economically costly grounding action—not data availability—is what the negative G bound concerns. This exact transition-equivalence assumption must be tested; free files do not imply it. Optional old policies ensure optimized value nondecrease when the free input expands the feasible menu without displacing resources. If E instead competes for the date-0 worker, its added 1/2 replaces the retained 4 rather than supplements it; absent spillovers its committed value is 5/2 and optional E reverts to R=6. Thus independent staffing is an essential economic assumption, not a body-count proxy.

## 4. Prior foundations and proof boundary

The general flow, dual and Lagrangian results use established constrained MDP occupation-measure methods (Altman, 1994, https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.19.1.169; finite-state treatment in *Constrained Markov Decision Processes*, https://www.routledge.com/Constrained-Markov-Decision-Processes/Stidham-Jr-Altman-Scarsini-Shaked/p/book/9780849303821). Rectangular uncertainty and robust dynamic programming are established: Iyengar (2005), https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.1040.0129; Nilim and El Ghaoui (2005), https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~elghaoui/pubs_rob_mdp.html. Publisher/author abstracts and book contents checked 24 November UTC; not a full novelty review or proof attribution of this particular optimistic bound. DDA §5 and Appendix B already treat future data value and complementary aggregation. The candidate synthesis here is an economically meaningful *policy-uniform successor-flow audit* that composes rights, accepted demand, shared capacity and learning with the previous policy-feature frontier. It can settle a portfolio choice without reconstructing every source transfer kernel. The prior dynamic paid resource certificate handles additive resource envelopes; this chapter isolates a separate cross-report eligibility moment and demonstrates both the gain over independent local bounds and relaxation gap.

Actual original customer data, field approvals, paid accepted recipes, rights, independent E capacity, empirical conditional row bounds and numerical official BEA interindustry backbone remain absent. These are mathematical sufficient conditions and synthetic counterexamples, not measured returns.
