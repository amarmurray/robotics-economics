# Instructor solutions — conditional economic transfer

1. At the same x, couple Q maximally, then conditioned on matched q couple D maximally, and conditioned on matched q,d couple the full incident vector J maximally. Successive matching probabilities are bounded below by (1-alpha),(1-beta),(1-gamma), hence joint mismatch at most 1-(1-alpha)(1-beta)(1-gamma). Mapping through a deterministic shared queue cannot increase TV. If any conditional q/d state lacks an identified G row, its uniform conditional bound is not certified; three separate Q,D,J margins do not suffice.
2. Let V_a(pi)=sum_t E_a[r_t^a]-C_a with r in [0,M_t]. For uniform same-state action reward deviation k_t and transition envelope delta_t, path coupling yields h_t=1-product_{s<=t}(1-delta_s); absolute value of the *paid-service* difference plus cost contrast is bounded by sum_t(M_t h_t+k_t). Record the current P receipt once in its current reward or known direct deviation; do not add a future data premium. If G is truly optional and free with R retained, optimized V_G>=V_R, although forced calibration can have negative net value. This fixed paired-policy inequality does not automatically rank different adaptive policy menus or independently resourced E.
3. With fair Z let P=(Q,D,J)=(Z,Z,1-Z). Let G be either identical or (1-Z,1-Z,Z). Each within-arm triple has exactly the same distribution; all jobs enter an initially empty conserved queue before the target cutoff. P paid event is Z and G is Z in the first world but 1-Z in the second. Therefore actual cross-arm discordance is 0 or 1 while the full transition TV is zero in either world and expected paid service 1/2. Total variation controls an *optimal constructed* coupling, not the historical latent potential-outcome pairing.
4. For f(x)=max(0,x), |f(u)-f(v)|<=|u-v|. Apply with u=L_P+J_P-S and v=L_G+J_G-S and iterate. If queues are integer nonnegative job units, |1{L_P=0}-1{L_G=0}|<=|L_P-L_G|, so a genuine matched-structural distance expectation bounds worker-free disagreement. With continuous queues L_P=0,L_G=epsilon>0, the free-worker indicator gap is 1 for any epsilon, even as |L_P-L_G| approaches zero; need a margin or threshold-duration condition instead. This also demonstrates why matching physical queue timing matters.
5. Delta=1-(1-alpha)(1-1)(1-gamma)=1; the envelope is vacuous. A P-only buyer panel cannot estimate any counterfactual G buyer response; to estimate G conditional demand one needs all original eligible offer/no-offer histories at a lawful, actually supported G exposure, linked with preaction x, qualification Q, incidents, accepted-service and payments, with assignment/report propensities and physical queue carryover. In its absence report unresolved bounds. A contract assuming unchanged demand is a falsifiable extra premise, not an empirical conclusion.
