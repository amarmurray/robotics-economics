## The exact question and answer

The earlier terminal-t2 result made an original-buyer audit worthless **at permanently blocked terminal states**. But a t2 recovery job can clear before t3, t2 service can create another job, and accepted t2 service can change both t3 original demand and the probability of an invoice being recorded. Which histories suffice to price a feasible G (licensed target grounding), P (productive source learning), E (genuinely independent embodiment) or old R policy *before* the response is known?

**Result.** A lawful t2 report can be economically valuable even when shared-worker backlog prohibits all t2 production, because it can select a feasible t3 qualification or dispatch policy after the backlog clears. Moreover, the joint event "t3 original buyer demands accepted work **and** inherited worker is free" is a decision-sufficient moment for one fixed downstream recipe, but its two individual marginals do not identify it. Fréchet bounds give a sharp, decision-usable conditional range; invoice-only histories, or R histories with zero G service support, cannot supply the missing response kernel. This distinguishes **temporal recovery**, **economic demand**, **technical qualification** and **reporting selection**.

## 1. One physical, nonanticipative three-date law

Let programs a∈{R,P,G,E} be alternatives with one common primitive population (buyers, tasks, incidents and exogenous shocks) and separately specified legal access, embodied capacity and information structures. At t0 select a and a permissible contingent policy π before unobserved G outcomes are known. G may train on retained data *only if its lineage, evaluation and deployment rights permit*. The old R policy remains feasible where capacity and safety allow.

At t1, A's accepted/paid unit Y_{A1}∈{0,1} uses one unit of its lifetime purchasing budget; technical success is a separate quantity. An early event can create **two distinct** repair obligations J_A,J_X∈{0,1}. Assume a single eligible worker clears exactly one inherited unit during t1 whenever any is pending (otherwise replace one by its measured eligible clearance), so

L_2=(J_A+J_X-1)_+,\quad B_{A2}=1-Y_{A1}.

At t2 the firm sees lawful original A/C offers O_2 before dispatch, but some reports may be selected or forbidden. It chooses u_2 after only information available by that deadline. Production on the common robot-time slot requires L_2=0, a qualified accepted-service recipe and a buyer with remaining budget. For C, let Y_{C2} denote **actually accepted and paid** work (not a controller pass). C has one unit of purchasing budget across t2/t3. A t2 attempt may produce a new job J_2∈{0,1} at the **end** of the worker's t2 clearing window. Thus

L_3=(L_2-1)_++J_2,\quad B_{A3}=1-Y_{A1}-Y_{A2},\quad B_{C3}=1-Y_{C2}.

Set J_2=0 if no eligible attempt occurred in this minimal menu. This convention matters: an incident created after the t2 clearance cannot be magically cleared in the same worker slot. It may prevent t3 service, even if t2 service paid immediately. In a fuller fleet, μ_2≤min(L_2, available eligible worker slots) replaces the unit clearance; add synchronized jobs to the same conserved queue, not an independent worker draw.

At t3 the **original** A and C offers D_{A3},D_{C3} are recorded before the firm decides whom to serve. They can depend on actual t2 paid quality, pricing, publicly observed service, and unresolved jobs, under an explicitly defended causal law. Let q_{A3},q_{C3}∈{0,1} indicate independently verified legal and technical qualification of the actual accepted-service recipes. For one shared terminal slot and nonnegative captured per-unit contribution v_A,v_C,

f_3(h_3,d_A,d_C)=1{L_3=0} max{v_A B_{A3}q_{A3}d_A,\ v_C B_{C3}q_{C3}d_C}.

Here history h_3 contains quality, prices, rights, worker backlog, task/embodiment and learner state. E can add a physically separate eligible robot and worker (with costs charged once); it may then serve both buyers, but cannot create a second purchase from the same buyer. A successful learning update may also change q_3 or J_2; these effects belong in the *same law*. The whole-program value is

V_a(π)=E_a^π[r_1+r_2+f_3-C_{training}-C_{hardware}-C_{rights}-C_{coordination}].

This objective counts present service, continuation and scarce resources once. It does **not** add an independently priced future-data premium to the terminal contribution already improved by learning.

## 2. Backward induction: why a blocked-state report can matter

Let F_2 be the legally observed information before the t2 decision and let S be an optional lawful t2 report delivered before a t2 investment decision. With future optimized terminal value J_3 and permitted t2 decision set U(F_2), define

Q_2(u;F_2)=E[r_2+β J_3(H_3)|F_2,u],\qquad J_2(F_2)=max_{u∈U(F_2)} Q_2(u;F_2).

When the same actions remain feasible and the report can be ignored, its *gross* value is

EVSI(S|F_2)=E_S[max_u E[r_2+β J_3|F_2,S,u]]-max_u E[r_2+β J_3|F_2,u]\ge0.

**Proof.** For any optimal no-report action u*, the report-contingent decision can select u* at every S, so its expected value is at least the unconditional value. This is a standard information-option result; it says nothing about report cost, approval delay or change in rival behavior. If no feasible action or continuation payoff changes with S, equality follows.

**Counterexample to extending terminal irrelevance across dates.** Let L_2=1, and permit the single worker to clear that inherited job in t2, making L_3=0. No t2 service can be accepted. Nevertheless, t2 has one independent validation slot: qualify either buyer A's or buyer C's t3 recipe. Exactly one of A/C will present an eligible original order at t3, with equal probabilities, and accepted service returns four. A timely lawful report perfectly indicates which buyer will order. Without it, either fixed t2 validation earns expected contribution two. With it, conditionally validate the arriving buyer and earn four. Thus the **t2 report is worth two**, even though t2 service is physically impossible. If validation also needs the same worker while it clears the inherited repair, this counterexample fails: separate the compute/validator and worker capacities in the evidence ledger. If the signal arrives after validation, its value for this menu is zero. The old theorem's zero audit value was correctly restricted to a *terminal* blocked state.

## 3. Sharp joint paid-production bounds from incomplete G evidence

Condition on a t2 history H in which G has a licensed, qualified C t2 recipe and no inherited backlog L_2=0; A has not spent its purchasing budget, no A t2 service is offered, and C has one remaining purchase. Suppose action u=1 accepts one C unit at t2 for contribution c and can generate one new repair job J_2. This job arrives after the t2 worker clearing window and, if unresolved, blocks A service at t3. C's paid budget is now exhausted. Let D=D_{A3}^{G,u=1} denote **A's actual original t3 offer**, including unserved or refused orders; let J=J_2^{G,u=1}. Let v denote A's captured contribution if its t3 unit is accepted. Then the whole conditional two-date contribution of this fixed policy is exactly

V_{G,serve}=c+v x,\qquad x=Pr_G(D=1,J=0|H,u=1).

A safe skip/no-t2-service policy with no new repair and t3 original A demand probability d_0 instead gives V_{G,skip}=v d_0 (other policy-specific costs may be subtracted once). Write d=Pr_G(D=1|H,u=1), j=Pr_G(J=1|H,u=1). Because D and J are *jointly* generated by the same t2 quality/incident/customer causal history,

max(0,d-j)\le x\le min(d,1-j).    (1)

**Proof and sharpness.** The four atoms for (J,D) have masses (p_{00},p_{01},p_{10},p_{11}) with x=p_{01}, d=p_{01}+p_{11}, j=p_{10}+p_{11}. Nonnegativity gives x≥0, x≥d-j, x≤d, x≤1-j. Conversely each endpoint is attained by an admissible 2×2 coupling with these marginals, so the bounds are sharp *conditional on no further cross-date physical or causal restriction*. The exact interval for the fixed serve policy is

c+v max(0,d-j)\le V_{G,serve}\le c+v min(d,1-j).    (2)

For this menu, a *robust* recommendation against safe skip exists if its lower bound exceeds v d_0; safe skip dominates if the serve upper bound is smaller than v d_0. Between those inequalities an unidentified **joint original-offer/repair-response** determines the policy ranking. The result is a standard Fréchet bound composed with physically gated paid production, not a general scientific-novelty claim. It does not identify d,j from an unsupported G action.

For a fixed network policy with more customers/embodiments and repeated training, this single x is no longer sufficient: the estimand is the value-weighted occupancy of *joint qualified, requested, accepted and resource-feasible service histories*. Minimize/maximize whole-program values across one common feasible scenario-law set. If state transition ambiguity is nonrectangular (one latent quality/recovery regime ties dates and arms), independent statewise worst-case Bellman choices can be infeasible and too pessimistic.

## 4. One-law observationally equivalent worlds, ranking reversal

Set c=1,v=4,d=j=d_0=1/2; no extra cost in this *conditional* witness. Let a common latent U be fair Bernoulli. Under actually observed retained R, the firm skips the t2 C service, generates no new job, and original A t3 order D^R=U is served when present. Hence R contributes two in expectation. R invoices and **all original R buyer orders** are identical in both worlds.

Under hypothetical, licensed G service at t2, both worlds have:
- one accepted C unit at t2 (contribution 1, consuming its budget);
- new job J_2^G=U (one worker, arrival after its t2 clearance);
- original A3 demand marginal 1/2, the same verified Q_G, price, early service, and G incident marginal 1/2.

In world L set D_{A3}^G=U: the t3 original offer happens **when the worker is busy**, so x=0 and V_G=1. In world H set D_{A3}^G=1-U: the offer happens only when the worker is free, so x=1/2 and V_G=3. These are two *alternative complete causal laws* for G response with the same observed R law, not two realizations that a firm's controller can discriminate before commitment. The difference is a joint response channel, not technical success.

For an illustrative competing independently staffed/embodied E with net value 9/4 and productive P with net value 11/5 (both held fixed across worlds), E beats G in L, but G beats E in H. The safe skip policy at two and the right to preserve old procedures remain available. An oracle choosing serve only in H is inadmissible without a lawful early regime signal. G-specific evidence sufficient to distinguish L and H needs both *original t3 offer* and *t2 repair-state* on matched assigned histories, not merely the two marginal failure/order rates. The companion script exhaustively checks the sharp 2×2 coupling inequalities and this one-law witness using exact integers.

**A negative observed transfer under one forced learner update is not evidence that freely available optional records reduce the optimized menu value.** If old training/dispatch actions remain admissible, adding a free usable record only expands the feasible policy set. A firm can still rationally decline to train, because training, curation, validation, risk or rights may be costly or displace stronger policies. Conversely, a physical embodiment or a grounding retrofit can outperform more data by changing the feasible qualified recipes directly.

## 5. Selected reporting and the limits of conditional transport

Let full history H_t include originally eligible offers/no-offers, accepted service and quality, retained rights, versioned Q, buyer budgets, repair jobs, physical clearance, and lawful pre-decision signals. Let M_t be the **separate** observation mask: invoices can be generated *only when a service is accepted*, and a job report can be suppressed for a robot that never runs. A two-period causal factorization under a fixed a and π is

p_a(h_2)\,K_{a,2}^{offers}(o_2|h_2)\,
K_{a,2}^{report}(m_2|h_2,o_2)\,\pi_2(u_2|F_2)\,
K_{a,2}^{service}(y_2,j_2,q_3|h_2,o_2,u_2)\,
K_{a,3}^{offers}(o_3|h_2,o_2,u_2,y_2,j_2,q_3)\,
K_{a,3}^{report}(m_3|h_2,o_2,u_2,y_2,j_2,q_3,o_3).

This is an ordered model, not a product of independent unconditional qualification/order/worker marginals. Later offers are **potential original buyer requests**, not inferred from the invoices the deployment chooses to generate. The law may condition on the complete causal quality and publicly observable service history; whether those variables are actually measurable is a separate empirical obligation.

A longitudinal R-to-G g-formula can evaluate a fixed G policy only if:
1. Every G-reachable buyer, worker and action stratum used for transported kernels has support in the source design, and effects of approved G interventions on earlier accepted/quality/queue states are established by genuine supported evidence rather than lab scores.
2. The specific later kernel being transported (including causal response **after** the t2 service, not merely initial offers) is invariant conditional on the complete history, or a defensible sensitivity restriction replaces equality.
3. Original A/C offers and no-offers, **including unserved buyers**, can be lawfully measured or are observed through a known positive, conditionally ignorable audit mechanism. Masked receipts alone are not original demand.
4. Actual qualification, worker eligibility, embodiment, customer budgets, prices, rights and versions match, and cluster interference is either modeled or ruled out with a defensible boundary.
5. The policy uses only information legally observed before each decision and preserves the optional old menu.

If R cannot qualify and serve C at t2, R has **structurally zero action support** for the G service-response kernel K^{service}_{G,2} and post-service K^{offers}_{G,3}. Even a perfectly complete original R buyer ledger does **not** identify G's induced t3 order/repair association. A valid comparison would require an approved matched intervention (possibly another program with a genuinely equivalent accepted C recipe) or an independently defended invariant structural mechanism. Do not cite a numerical benchmark pass rate as customer-paid value.

An independently randomized, lawful original-offer audit M with known positive sampling probability η(h,u)>0, where selection is independent of the original offer conditional on the recorded pre-audit history and assigned u, can identify the relevant original-order mean via

E[D|h,u]=E[MD/η(h,u)|h,u].

This identity is a standard conditional inverse-probability calculation; it needs complete exposure denominators and **action support**. Invoice-triggered M=1{paid} is not ignorable because payment depends on demand, Q, worker availability and customer acceptance. If an audit is not legally possible, preserve a partial identified set rather than impute forbidden original orders.

## 6. Closest foundations, rejection tests, and the next specific gap

This composes established dynamic programming, marked recovery queues, Fréchet bounds, missing-data identification and longitudinal causal transport. In particular Robins (1986, DOI 10.1016/0270-0255(86)90088-6) developed longitudinal causal inference; Pearl and Bareinboim (*Statistical Science* 2014, arXiv:1503.01603) give explicit conditions for transporting causal information across environments; Farboodi, Koh and Xia (*Data-Driven Automation*, NBER w35320) already model forward-looking production data and complementary aggregation. Do not claim those foundations, future-data value, or aggregation complementarities as new.

A field test must measure the same assigned cohort from t1 paid customer quality through t2 repair clearance, t2 original service/offer and accepted C outcome, t3 original A/C order/no-order and actual acceptance/payment, with legal recording and cross-embodiment rights verified for every source. Deliberate teleop, naturally produced successes, failures/recoveries, simulator, synthetic, and public/pretrained sources require distinct provenance and matched controls. An independent pre-dispatch audit of refused/unserved buyers, alongside matched legal G action support, is the discriminating identification input. Check new jobs induced by service; otherwise using a fixed worker-availability table creates a false production gain. The official BEA InputOutput API and supply/use tables are a suitable production-network backbone (https://apps.bea.gov/api/ and https://www.bea.gov/itable/input-output), but **no quantitative BEA use/make coefficients or task-level customer payments** were retrieved for this model.
