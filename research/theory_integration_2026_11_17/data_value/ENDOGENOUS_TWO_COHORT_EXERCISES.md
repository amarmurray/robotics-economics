# Exercises — endogenous data, original demand and qualification

1. Derive the four P history moments Pr(F), Pr(D_P), Pr(W_P) and Pr(Q_P D_P W_P) from F,D0,R0 independent fair. Why does multiplying three marginal means not value an accepted order?

2. With F/T 20-atom cell counts (k,10-k,10-k,k), show that both separate source qualifications are 1/2 and derive J_G(x) when G demand and worker clearance are independent fair. Find the exact G/E crossover and explain which historical evidence is missing.

3. At x=1/4 compare G submodel I (D0,W0) against submodel II (F,T). Show that Q_G,D_G,W_G each have the same marginal in both, yet the program ranking reverses. State a lawful audit that would distinguish the worlds.

4. Let OR and AND architectures have conditional precommitted contributions 19/20−x_O and 3x_A−1/20. Derive the portfolio share canceling overlap when x_O=x_A and prove there is no overlap-invariant mix if (x_O,x_A) varies freely in a two-dimensional interior.

5. Find the minimax-regret precommitment between G and E in submodel I across x∈[0,1/2]. Contrast maximin and the ideal two-world report. Explain why a no-cost free optional corpus is not the same as compulsory licensed acquisition.
