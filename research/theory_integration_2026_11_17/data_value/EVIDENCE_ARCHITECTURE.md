# Evidence architecture: from local capability to a capital decision
25 November 2026 Eastern · Synthesis of the existing first paper, not a new paper or scientific scope lock.

## Economic priors and organizing claim

A robot is an embodied provider of scarce dated services, not a scalar stock of tasks. A learned controller and reusable data can change the set of lawful production recipes and their joint resource requirements. Customer willingness to pay and an authorized settlement determine the contribution of a completed recipe. Neither a count of trained skills nor the sum of prices of intermediate activities measures that contribution. In particular, a data improvement may make a new body more productive, make it redundant, or leave current production unchanged while changing its successor opportunity set. The controlled program is the investment object; the physical production network and learning law determine its payoff. This is the connective argument across Models A and B. DDA's future-data and complementary aggregation results are acknowledged; the proposed emphasis here is qualification, joint dated capacity and cheaper independently evolving alternative policies, not novelty of valuing data or standard LP duality.

## One decision criterion rather than another proxy

For a fixed original-order population and lawful predispatch state, let S_i be supported recipes for each order, q_i its demand quota and h_i^S(p) its nonnegative maximal reduced contribution at resource price p>=0. Suppose an intervention changes only a subset of original groups C; existing recipes retained and all changed recipes in C, and known capacity increment d. For each group, independently bound the incremental residual from new recipes by e_i>=0. For overlapping audited group sets A_j suppose at most m_j of C lie in A_j. Define t_i=q_i e_i. The total incremental residual is bounded by

    M = max sum_i t_i z_i
        subject to 0<=z_i<=1, sum_(i in A_j) z_i<=m_j.

For individual indivisible groups, z_i is the indicator of affected group; the fractional LP is a safe upper relaxation. For any multipliers eta_j>=0, an explicit upper certificate is

    M <= sum_j eta_j m_j + sum_i max(0,t_i-sum_(j:i in A_j) eta_j).

Proof: multiply each count constraint by eta_j and add the positive residual bound from z_i<=1. By LP duality, optimizing eta yields the relaxed optimum (not necessarily the integer optimum). This reduces to the prior largest-B ceiling with a single whole-population cap. It also avoids double-counting a group that appears in several audits (e.g., formulation and authorization). Include a whole-population cap if supported; otherwise unaudited groups retain their full e_i. Do not treat an observed route as a proof that the new recipe cannot affect other groups. If the baseline dual witness has gap gamma, the fixed-exposure gross incremental optimum is bounded above by p'd+M+gamma. Subtract incremental investment cost once. A feasible incremental production witness is still needed for a lower return bound, and changing learning histories require full J. No cross-study combination of counts, costs or margins is permitted.

In Kobe the available published count supplies one constraint m=26,369 only under the restricted catalog-extension premise. No independently supported t_i, dated resource p or baseline witness gamma is supplied. The published numbers therefore do not identify the economic threshold. In Amazon the physical ceiling is instead a resource-occupation moment; it cannot be substituted for a count of affected customers. Both fit the same decision architecture.

The repository metadata mentions timestamps while the article's limitations on traceability explicitly distinguish pick sequence from continuous time tracking; inspect actual fields before relying on any dated clocks. This is a promising source for the *human* feasible-route baseline and wave composition, not evidence of robot ROI. Do not substitute it for the missing official contemporary production-network backbone.

## Prospective scope and discriminating next action

The strongest first-paper candidate is the decision rule for economically qualified data investments against P and C with independently evolving routine histories. The LP, network cut and bundle ceilings are supporting restrictions, not separate claims of novelty. The four-arm comparison identifies a randomized fixed-policy economic interaction if the joint original demand/accounting population is retained; it does not identify every optimal policy or latent cross-arm event. For a defensible causal result, prioritize an original opportunity ledger containing group ID, offered quantity/deadline, all supported recipe alternatives with qualification version, dated body/worker/compute occupancy, accepted/fallback settlement and cost, and successor data rights/lineage. Audit an independently supported omitted-menu residual ceiling and a feasible production witness before increasing technical sample size. If neither is available, present a conditional result and the precise measurements needed rather than pooling proxy values. No new official numeric edge.
