# Student exercises: stable resource prices and data report value

**Exercise 1.** In max c_y x subject to 0≤x≤1 with c_1=1/4 and c_2=1/2, prove that the optimal basis can be the same for both reports while the slot shadow price differs. Explain why neither is automatically a receipt from a displaced original buyer.

**Exercise 2.** In max x+7z/10 subject to x+z≤1, x≤d, x,z≥0, solve d=1/5 and d=6/5. Give the slot's dual price in each case and explain a nonlocal one-slot displacement.

**Exercise 3.** Take p=(1/2,1/4,1/4), margins (1/2−λ1,−1/4−λ2,λ3), λi in [0,1/2] and range(λi)≤2ε. Derive P,N and sharp lower/upper bounds on L, including endpoint witnesses for ε=0,1/16,1/8,1/4. Distinguish proof over real ε from grid enumeration.

**Exercise 4.** Suppose report acquisition has synthetic contribution cost c=1/32 and is pure G-observe. Derive the exact ε interval guaranteeing positive net report value. Explain why this conclusion does not follow for G-train, X-new source or E and why a uniformly dominant E eliminates full-menu report value.

**Exercise 5 (evidence).** Propose a legal method to bound λ_i using original customer offers, accepted paid outside orders and physically approved shared-controller policies without treating an unused slot as an actual receipt. State what should be done if original buyer demand changes with Y.
