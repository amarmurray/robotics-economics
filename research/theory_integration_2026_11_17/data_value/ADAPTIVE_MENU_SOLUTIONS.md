# Instructor solutions — adaptive source/grounding/embodiment acquisition

Do not distribute as part of the student exercise sheet; these are conditional hypothetical calculations.

1. Feasibility requires P11=t, P10=1/2-t, P01=1/2-t, P00=t, so 0≤t≤1/2. Expected gross optimum across both qualified policies and fallback is 4-2t.
2. Upfront net equals (4-2t)-2/5=18/5-2t, giving 18/5,31/10,13/5. Source-only net=2+2×1/2−1/5=14/5. The value of testing g on the failed-x branch is max{0,2(1/2-t)-(1/5)(1/2)}=max{0,9/10−2t}. Buy g iff t<9/20. Thus staged values are 37/10,16/5,14/5.
3. Static randomization identifies each J_z but never jointly observes a network's Q_x,Q_g or learns Q_g after source training. An isolated, approved matched offline qualification of both candidate controllers could bound t but not automatically establish service revenue or safety; an approved sequential experiment on x-failure histories, with original-order accounting, directly targets r and the staged accepted-service payoff.
4. Even parity equally weights 000,011,101,110; odd parity equally weights 001,010,100,111. Both have marginal successes 1/2 and each pair co-success 1/4. At least one passes with probability 3/4 versus 1. Three-test net is 2+2×3/4−3/5=29/10 versus 2+2×1−3/5=17/5.
5. With no remaining g opportunity or physical admission, the second action is unavailable and its value term vanishes (source-alone 14/5 under these assumptions). Free optional availability cannot decrease optimized policy value if old options remain, but paying testing costs, losing customers or forcing an unsafe/unqualified rollout may lower actual contribution and is not authorized.
6. Use policy-closed network as unit; pre-register original B and new A orders and deadlines; version both controllers, training and data-copy rights; observe whole accepted-service joint qualification with approved recovery under both alternatives where feasible; record failure-conditioned sequential grounding, operator/dispatch interference, customer cancellations, total opportunity costs, hardware delivery and captured service value. Marginal benchmark pass rates omit dependence, demand and original-order response.
