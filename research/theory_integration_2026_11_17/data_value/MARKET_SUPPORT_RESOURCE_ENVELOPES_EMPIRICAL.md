# Prospective policy-support and whole-service resource audit (design only)
**19 November 2026 — no actual customer/robot experiment, physical signoff, rights grant or paid data collection.**

Predeclare a market catchment and fixed horizon *before* assigning data/source/grounding/embodiment policy. Version all original offered, refused, displaced, repeated, completed, unsupported, newly induced and canceled complete-service orders; the full accepted recipe, buyer alternative, capturable economics, physical inputs and unique order attribution. The raw number of moves or accepted-only motions is not customer production.

Audit **closure**: controller/model checksum and shared update broadcasts, learner gradient/data rights, recovery/teleoperation operator calendars, dispatch and battery/tool pools, inter-site referrals, prices and other demand spillovers. Treat the entire jointly affected group as the candidate closed unit. An X-only robot in a mixed G fleet is not a complete all-X exposure. Independent market-level randomization requires approval and proof of no cross-market policy interference; it has not occurred.

For each R/A/X/G/E policy version, separately register field trajectories, failure/recovery, human teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained inputs, curation/compute/rights costs and safe rollback. Audits/reporters A are not physical qualification. Verify all new service recipes X/G/E could enable, every hard operator/embodiment constraint, maximum offered-order volume and contribution span over policy-induced demand, and a valid upper dual vector for *every admissible history*. Record approved existing fallback's real ability to serve the same buyers after an X intervention; storing weights does not certify its economics. One E body can alter both transfer and capacity.

Classify evidence: (1) approved and closed *complete original-order whole-policy outcome*; (2) physically verified whole-service upper/lower bound; (3) local matched qualification; (4) selected accepted-only benchmark. Never promote (3)/(4) to (1). Unknown rare operator→buyer links prevent a zero-effect declaration. A genuinely valid capacity certificate can reject costly X without estimating source transfer; if none can be validated, the correct whole-market bound is potentially vacuous. Test falsifiers: an extra eligible recipe, an unregistered new buyer, a shared model cross-catchment effect, a displaced old fallback, or higher captured value than the cap.

Still missing: all actual robot and original customer-order observations, physical and data-rights approvals, independent full policy market closures, validated capacity and payoff spans, full repo CI and quantitative official BLS–BEA industry input-output crosswalk. Keep the atlas and broader multilayer economic roadmap open.
