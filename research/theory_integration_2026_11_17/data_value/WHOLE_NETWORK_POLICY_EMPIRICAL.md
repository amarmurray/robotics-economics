# Prospective approved-network measurement contract: data, demand and operator recovery

**Protocol only; no recruitment, physical experiment, purchased data or outreach.**

1. **Freeze the pre-policy target frame.** Identify a network unit large enough to include all shared operator crews, dispatch queues, model/controller updates, robots, sites and affected buyer orders. Retain a fixed pre-policy market/site denominator. List isolated strata, adjacent markets and spillover edges; record changes in demand as **outcomes** rather than conditioning trial inclusion on them.
2. **Define treatment as a complete approved regime.** R incumbent productive controller and optional logging; A target independent report/audit; X source training from separately versioned field/recovery/teleop/simulation/synthetic/pretraining streams; G grounding and source reuse; E physically altered embodiment/extra slot. Log rights, licenses, compute/curation, physical safety and customer acceptance; keep specialist old policy feasible. If any arm lacks approval, exclude it from randomized menu rather than impute it.
3. **Link all original orders and operator histories.** At each stage record offered/unserved/rejected/canceled demand, task and site, price/contribution, original order time, competent fallback and displaced accepted service; complete recipe joint qualification, unsafe/excluded actions, operator hours, failure/recovery and queue blocking; controller training lineage and target-report missingness and timing. Do not infer hidden unattempted qualification from accepted logs.
4. **Target estimands separately.** The primary \(\Delta_{XG}\) is **net full-network policy** contribution per fixed baseline network over a predeclared horizon, including induced demand and learned future skills once; stratified stage outcomes and jointly attempted qualifications are secondary mechanisms. Record acquisition/rights/cash costs consistently. Randomize network closures—not one robot—only when independently authorized, or use valid exposure mappings if graph interference cannot be avoided.
5. **Support and validation.** Record arm assignment even on failed/nonadherent deployments; preserve complete rejected-original-order denominator. Pre-specify missing networks and payout envelopes; report partial bounds when full samples or approval are absent. A direct randomized regime mean does not prove source-to-target transfer; additional pre-action shadow labels plus matched held-out complete-service tests are needed for attribution.
6. **Quantitative atlas as an orthogonal input.** Only insert official observed BEA use/supply by identified vintage/NAICS and BLS employment wages/duties after numeric extraction and units reconciliation. Industry monetary links are not robot task qualification or customer receipts. Prior official source-route failure unchanged; no substitute vendor press release.

**Falsifiers for model transport:** source X changes demand/order composition, reporter affects controller behavior, high-incident networks leak recovery crews, physical E changes task qualification rather than only capacity, time windows couple market sites, and rights prevent optional use of a source. In all such cases the earlier fixed-law identities are *diagnostics*, not transport estimators.
