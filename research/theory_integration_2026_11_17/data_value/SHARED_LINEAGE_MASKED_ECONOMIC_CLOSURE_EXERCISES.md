# Exercises — shared resource and masked learning

1. Derive both H-conditional M and N success coincidences in even and odd worlds, then compute the value of choosing M at H=0 and N at H=1 on the original denominator.
2. Prove the robust optimum is 6 over all randomized policies in the stated local schedule menu. Why is knowing the law but losing H's lawful-use right insufficient?
3. Given p≤1/8, derive a uniform absolute error bound using the one-slot support function and a tighter policy-specific bound. Compare both against E.
4. Show why the same one-slot support bound cannot directly bound the difference of M versus N; give the weighted symmetric-difference bound. Explain what changes if two customers' jobs share a single fleet worker across clusters.
