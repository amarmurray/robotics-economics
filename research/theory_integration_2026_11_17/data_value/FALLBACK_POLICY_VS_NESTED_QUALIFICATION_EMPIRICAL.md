# Prospective original-customer fallback qualification and direct-policy evidence design

**20 November 2026 | Research protocol only. No outreach, buyer records, safety approval or hardware experiment.**

## Which claim can the actual field architecture support?

**A. "Old policy selectable."** Inspect controller, firmware, body allocation, operator schedule, support contract and customer SLA in the intended production window. Show the full original R service policy can still be chosen *before the new policy is admitted*, with unchanged rights, original orders, costs and market interference. This supports the fixed-environment expected policy-set inclusion argument only; it is not a casewise Q_X>=Q_R finding.

**B. "Old qualification never lost under implemented fallback F."** Enumerate original complete physical/network histories, including failure/recovery tails, late operator contention and contracted customer windows. Version a *signed* policy-level safety case for an observable gate, a physically non-destructive retry, or independently resourced redundant bodies. Record where the old recipe can **actually** be invoked after the X intervention. Test the gate on joint old-success/new-failure cases within approved regimes and preserve unsupported high-risk cases as failures of proof. Requiring no casewise regressions cannot be replaced with a fixed expected-loss tolerance.

**C. "Economic source data transfer."** Define original eligible parent and whole-network opportunity registry including zero orders, refusals, out-of-contract buyers and absent precommitted offers. Link preintervention Z; legally supported buyer order B/m, quality/price/refund/rights terms; frozen R and source-trained X controller lineage; G grounding and approved E body; individual and *joint* safe qualifications; operator recovery resources; actual collected contribution net of refunds; future production-generated data. A benchmark source result does not establish an accepted paid order.

## Two legitimate, different estimands

**Ex-ante policy-level value:** On physically permitted whole learner/operator/customer network clusters, randomize complete R/X/G/E policies under fixed horizon and original registry. Estimate arm-specific actual payment and complete accepted-service means with appropriate interference/support accounting. This does not require joint individual potential outcomes, but does need policy-level market closure, physical approvals, positivity, comparable customer compositions and a competent retained/specialist baseline.

**Casewise safe addition / mechanism:** The stronger nesting premise and zero R-success/X-failure safety claim need a physically guaranteed selector/recovery architecture or much stronger verified structural restrictions. Separate randomized R/X averages cannot identify latent regression probability even with perfect samples. A matched audit on safely reversible/replayable states may help but must address intervention state mutation, customer availability and shared recovery network. Never assert a physical cross-world test where two potential histories cannot both occur.

Pre-register source mixtures by field production trajectories, failures/recoveries, targeted teleoperation, simulation, synthetic/public/pretrained inputs; track compute, curatorial/legal rights, target-grounding labor, new embodiment/fit, controller safety, and **cost of fallback/redundancy**. For retrospective selected logs, report source exposure propensities and missing buyer/qualifications; do not pretend missing outcomes are zero.

## Falsification and paper decision

Audit specific real safety mechanism, not generic feature flags. If signoff is absent, the November 22 claim is explicitly **policy-value identification without established casewise safe nesting**. If full-network approval and original eligible buyer exposure are also absent, keep a structural candidate and inconclusive applied ranking. This is an admissible honest research result, not a gate pass.

Official industry-demand topology still needs vintage-appropriate BEA use/supply numerical flows linked to the existing verified BLS employment/output rows; the BLS rows alone are not a quantitative flow network or market revenue benchmark. The initial original-sector atlas remains synthetic/unpopulated until the real data are accessible. No private customer access, physical trial, outreach, paid compute, public submission, or confidential employer data are authorized.
