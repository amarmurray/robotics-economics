# Endogenous source production, customer feedback, and the limit of a static mixture hedge

23 November 2026 · *When Capabilities Become Production* · continuation of [adaptive source-mixture topology](ADAPTIVE_SOURCE_MIXTURE_QUALIFICATION_TOPOLOGY.md) and [supported heterogeneous qualification](FROM_HETEROGENEOUS_EVIDENCE_TO_PAID_PRODUCTION.md). **The November 22 scientific acceptance gate is OPEN.** This is conditional mathematical theory with synthetic rational examples; not measured robotics economics, proven transfer or an independent claim of novelty.

## 1. The same firm, but a controlled source distribution

At date 2 a firm with an already admitted field source F, incomplete customer-task qualifications, legal-use/transfer rights, scarce worker time, and original customer purchasing opportunities chooses productive field service P, deliberate target grounding G, separately staffed embodiment E, or safe retained policy R. Productive P can earn a receipt, create a field record only when work is actually performed, change future buyer interest, and generate recovery workload. G deliberately collects or validates a distinct source T under a separately signed right, possibly unlocking previously held F, but takes support resources and need not produce an early sale. E changes the physical feasible resource menu after its build/qualification delay; R stays available where its physical fallback remains valid. **The second date's evidence law is chosen endogenously**, not the fixed joint F/T distribution of the previous four-cell calculation.

For a finite physically feasible state s, a legally permissible action a and joint next outcome z=(H,L,Q,D,W,B'), let K_a(z|s) be the action-specific causal kernel. H is accepted early service and its quality; L is typed source lineage (field success, field failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic or public/pretrained, each separately with permissions and version); Q is *joint* held-out acceptance qualification for the target customer recipe; D is the original buyer offer including no-order, deadlines and cash budget; W is actual free worker/robot capacity net of the recovery queue; B' is the next available buyer budget. A one-unit terminal paid service has contribution m QDW only if Q,D,W are indicators and one original order is available. With multiple services, replace m QDW by the **optimized feasible accepted-production value** V(s') from the common LP/worker-resource system; do not sum mutually exclusive orders. For the two-cohort example,

    J_a = E_{K_a}[r_early(a,H) - c_a + m_a Q_a D_a W_a].

For longer horizons the same object is r_early + E[K_a J_next], **not a separate added “data premium.”** The future price of a source is already inside the chosen feasible continuation. Marginal technical transfer, source count and model benchmark improvements are not sufficient statistics for J_a.

**Proposition 1 (two-date causal production).** If a policy is fixed before its own source/worker/customer outcomes are observed, K_a has well-defined consistent potential responses, the legal and physical fallback set is recorded, and all receipts/costs are counted once, the displayed formula equals the policy's expected incremental customer contribution. If cluster-level lawful assignment of a has positive conditional probability on each economically reachable pre-action history, complete original buyer/no-order and worker records plus matched joint qualification can identify the right-hand side by the sequential g-formula under consistency, exchangeability and no unmodelled cross-cluster interference. Historical P-only outcomes cannot identify K_G without additional **substantive** transport restrictions, even if P source success and a separate G benchmark marginal are known. Proof: condition on the assigned pre-action history and apply consistency and conditional random assignment, then the law of total expectation. With P assignment probability zero for G, alter every G potential response on a common latent population without changing any observed P variable; hence lack of identification.

This extends, rather than replaces, the existing exact multi-period worker/demand analysis. It also makes a limitation explicit: an independent embodied-and-staffed E program must still be priced from its **own qualified production menu**. Our scalar E comparison below is a stipulated normalized comparator, not a claimed staffed robot site.

## 2. How endogenous production changes the corpus, buyer and worker simultaneously

A fully enumerated two-cohort witness uses 20 equally weighted pre-action source types F,T and three independent fair latent binary draws D0,R0,W0, making 160 joint atoms. F is an earlier retained productive-field qualification; T is the potential result of a *different*, licensed target-grounding intervention. Neither success indicator is a generic dataset count. Let k range over integers 0,...,10 and assign the F/T cells (00,01,10,11) masses (k,10-k,10-k,k)/20. Thus Pr(F=1)=Pr(T=1)=1/2, while x=Pr(FT=1)=k/20 is undetermined by their separate marginals. P is observed historically, G has no assignment support. All original P histories remain identical for every k.

Under P, early service H=F is accepted and generates a field record S=H; in a site without F qualification no productive field record is manufactured. The early accepted contribution is 1/5 per success. Early service changes original late buyer interest to D_P=F OR D0 (so Pr(D_P)=3/4), but a service-originated repair can occupy the one shared worker: W_P=NOT F OR NOT R0 (Pr(W_P)=3/4). Its later complete customer recipe qualifies under Q_P=F AND S=F and earns margin 2 only when Q_P D_P W_P=1. Hence

    J_P = (1/5)Pr(F) + 2 Pr(F(1-R0)) = 3/5.

It is **incorrect** to infer paid completion from independent P marginal multiplication: Pr(Q_P)=1/2, Pr(D_P)=Pr(W_P)=3/4, while the actual paid coincidence is 1/4, not 9/32. This is a concrete endogeneity of demand and worker occupation induced by productive learning.

Under G, the firm instead acquires/grounds T, with all-in validation, curation, license and displaced-resource cost 1/10. The matched late alternative recipe qualifies if Q_G=F OR T; there is no early accepted buyer service, and in **submodel I only** the original late offer D_G=D0 and clearance W_G=W0 are both independent fair draws. A complete qualified paid order has normalized margin 3. Thus

    J_G^I(x) = 3 Pr[(F OR T) D0 W0] - 1/10
             = (3/4)(1-x) - 1/10 = 13/20 - (3/4)x.

A separate staffed E has stipulated net contribution J_E=31/50, already net of its own resources and investment; R has incremental J_R=0. The matched program values are:
 
| Law x | P productive | G targeted source (submodel I) | E independent | R | Preferred |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 3/5 | 13/20 | 31/50 | 0 | G |
| 1/4 | 3/5 | 37/80 | 31/50 | 0 | E |
| 1/2 | 3/5 | 11/40 | 31/50 | 0 | E |

Every value is a conditional normalized **customer-production increment**, not dollars inferred from an ML benchmark. The G advantage against E disappears when x exceeds 1/25; the k-grid crosses between k=0 and k=1. The first service, generated corpus, changed original demand and newly owed recovery job are part of the *causal path*. G cannot be assigned the P-generated customer demand or P's operator workload merely because both have positive model-transfer scores.

**Proposition 2 (two nested observational equivalences).** (a) All k share the *complete P history law* (including early accepted service, original late buyer offer, repair and paid completion) and individual F and potential T qualification marginals. Nevertheless k=0 and k=10 reverse the G/E investment ranking. (b) Even knowing the entire joint F/T law need not identify G. At k=5 construct **submodel II** on the same 160 atoms, leaving P unchanged, setting G original demand D_G=F and worker-free W_G=T. D_G and W_G still each have marginal 1/2 and Q_G has the same marginal 3/4 as in submodel I, but the paid event is Q_G D_G W_G=FT. Consequently J_G^{II}(1/4)=3(1/4)-1/10=13/20, which beats E=31/50, while submodel I has J_G^I(1/4)=37/80, below E. Both G demand/worker mechanisms are physical possibilities: demand can sort with site competence, and failed deliberate acquisition can produce an unresolved support job. They do **not** assert observed G operation. Proof: all identities follow by expanding the 160 finite atoms; P is a function only of F,D0,R0,W0, whose law does not depend on k, and the coupling variation changes only G potential buyer/worker transitions.

There is no costless-source paradox. G is a costly, possibly worker-displacing intervention. The old R/P actions remain selectable *ex ante*. A negative forced-G contribution in an alternative hypothetical law does not imply that making free optional lawful data available lowers the optimal value with an unchanged menu. A post-hoc optimizer aware of the true unobserved G regime is not an implementable historical decision maker.

## 3. Investment certificates and a lawfully timed report

Under the **restricted** ambiguity set from submodel I (the G buyer and worker independence restriction is assumed, not observed), the entire unobserved x range is [0,1/2]. P=3/5 is dominated by the independently feasible E=31/50 in this synthetic comparator; its capacity may still matter if E is infeasible. The best G advantage over E at x=0 is A=3/100; E's advantage at x=1/2 is B=69/200. Before any x report, minimax regret mixes independently comparable deployment clusters into G fraction A/(A+B)=2/25 and E fraction 23/25; worst expected regret is 69/2500 normalized units. Maximin guaranteed contribution instead selects E. These are **different objectives**, not an oracle switch after learning x. If only two endpoint worlds had a stipulated equal prior and a lawful *perfect precommitment* report, its gross expected benefit relative to E-only would be 3/200; any actually implementable paired audit also has delay, sampling error, privacy/rights and physical opportunity costs. This is no evidence that such a perfect report exists.

Submodel II invalidates the preceding regret guarantee because it violates the assumed G buyer/worker independence, while maintaining source-pair data and single outcome marginals. With only p(Q_G)=3/4 and p(D_G)=p(W_G)=1/2, sharp generic Fréchet bounds give 0 ≤ Pr(Q_G D_G W_G) ≤ 1/2, so even the **joint source success audit** by itself leaves a huge paid-service interval. A G-specific original-order/worker audit must capture the **same paid-risk-set joint**. Sparse old invoices cannot provide a valid substitute.

## 4. What becomes of the previous 3/4–1/4 hedge?

The preceding source-topology note supplied a precommitted architecture mixture constant at 7/10 because both OR and AND alternatives shared one fixed overlap x. Let x_O denote F/T qualification overlap *under the policy that produces the OR-source cohort*, and x_A the overlap under the policy that produces the AND-source cohort. Those are distinct causal potential distributions when field work and deliberate grounding create different data, select customers and consume worker time. In the interior where acquiring the second source is precommitted, the previous margins are

    O = 19/20 - x_O,       A = 3x_A - 1/20.

For mixture weight lambda on O the directional sensitivity is -lambda dx_O + 3(1-lambda) dx_A. If the supported common law entails x_O=x_A, lambda=3/4 cancels a **one-dimensional** uncertain overlap and gives 7/10. If x_O and x_A may vary independently across feasible common-latent causal worlds, there is no lambda for which this differential vanishes in both directions: that would require lambda=0 and lambda=1 simultaneously. Hence the static hedge is not generally a robust hedge after source production becomes action-dependent. The A policy's optional precommitment to skip a negative-value acquisition changes its piecewise payoff but does not restore a global cancellation.

This is not a formal impossibility of all hedges; additional cross-program restrictions, genuinely informative precommitment signals, or other qualified recipes can support one. **Constructive feasibility:** take F fair, and for each program a draw a distinct independent uniform U_a. Put T^a=1 conditional on F=1 when U_a≤2x_a and conditional on F=0 when U_a≤1-2x_a. For any (x_O,x_A)∈[0,1/2]^2, each T^a has marginal 1/2 and the desired F/T^a overlap, simultaneously on one common latent population. Thus independent uncertainty is not merely an invalid collection of unrelated action marginals. The general result is a rank condition on the action-specific **economic sensitivity matrix**, not a universal claim that mixed strategies fail.

## 5. Economically sufficient prospective evidence, not automatic causal identification

Three evidence levels must remain separate. A matched **technical intervention** measures lawful, versioned F-only, T-only, combined and unchanged-policy target *qualification* under comparable models, compute and final held-out acceptance. An **original-buyer/worker validation** additionally records unselected buyer offers including no-order, quality/rejection, the exact shared worker/recovery queue and complete accepted paid service **under the same program**. A **closed network assignment** prospectively randomizes admissible whole P/G/E regimes over non-interfering robot/customer/worker clusters; otherwise spillovers require an explicit exposure structure. The smallest source audit under submodel I needs a matched paired F/T success association (plus separate validation of its conditional buyer/worker independence). Under unrestricted submodel II it is not enough: the decision-relevant extra object is a G-specific Pr(Q_G D_G W_G), with rights, causal assignment and positive support. An accepted-only invoice panel or an F=1-only productive source archive is selected and cannot reconstruct it.

This evidence program differentiates paid learning from deliberate teleoperation, production failure/recovery, simulation, public/synthetic and pretrained sources by their *actual* admission, lineages and causal opportunities. It tests conditional value after worker/capital/curation displacement without converting robot benchmark accuracy to revenues. A source that expands t4 competence could change the t5 source mixture again; the same K_a state formulation recurses with a richer corpus and embodiment state rather than imposing stationary transfer. Full project continuation: worker/production-networks and end-market prices, P/G/E/R and E-build delays, legal ownership/rights and manufacturing fleet investment, empirical atlas and teach-back are still active, not displaced by the example.
