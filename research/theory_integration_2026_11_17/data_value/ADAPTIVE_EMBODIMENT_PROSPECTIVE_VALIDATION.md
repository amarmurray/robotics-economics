# Original-network X/G/E embodiment-option discrimination — prospective, unpopulated

20 November 2026. This is a measurement contract only. No employer or customer data, outreach, robot deployment, hardware purchase, unauthorized training or experiments were performed.

**Research unit:** one original eligible procurement parent, its *original natural offer* (not a newly binding warranty/deposit), both requested service types, physical service windows and robot/operator schedule, predeclared policy version and preassignment treatment eligibility. Record the full denominator: reports that arrived before or after the irreversible hardware/adaptation decision, refusals, nonresponses, cancellations, incomplete service and actual buyer receipts/refunds/captured contribution. Restrict net analysis to the same original parent network or explicitly identify spillovers across shared pools.

**Physical authorization before observations are treated as interventions:** independent signoff for R, source-trained specialist EA, specialist EB, robust body E0, observation/diagnostic report, their permitted whole-network switching/recovery sequences and operator workload. Unsigned alternatives are only model scenarios; original R being in a folder is not certified post-X recovery. Source-only shadow studies may be offline only within authorized public/permissioned data and compute; no unsafe live policy assignment.

**Matched technical factorial:** freeze baseline weights, source licenses, compute, curator effort, target validation/qualifier and original customer tasks. Vary production field data vs selected failure/recovery vs teleoperation vs paired analogies vs simulation/synthetic/pretrained sources as distinct *treatments*; within each, vary source quantity versus grounding/alignment separately and report source costs. Hold out original customer–robot–operator states and report the **joint** qualification table for EA/EB/robust/recovery, not separate benchmark pass rates. Source-program learning-generated new embodiments require versioned morphology/controller manufacturing design provenance and independently approved complete recipe.

**Matched policy/economic factorial:** compare (i) retained R, (ii) approved EA-only, (iii) approved EA/EB, (iv) approved E0, (v) approved EA/EB/E0 where actual one-body switching/dispatch permits; and (a) no G or deliberately withheld report, (b) target G report **before** embodiment/dispatch lock, (c) identical-quality report **after** irreversible lock. Predeclare natural order population, full parent-level lawful assignment/propensities and operator interference clusters. Same nominal report accuracy is not enough: estimate state- and menu-conditional report calibration and missingness. Record approved fallback feasibility and actual changeover/lead time. Report post-commitment versus pre-commitment effects separately.

**Economic estimand:** compare E[u(approved whole policy, original N customer demand)] at the original eligible-parent level, net of operator time, physical depreciation, source rights, training, compute, curation and truly incremental embodiment expenditure, including any displacement. Report J's postpolicy production-data transitions, avoiding an added data premium. An exposure/offer violation blocks point identification; use existing partial-identification and C/N transport bounds, not artificial response independence.

**Falsifications:** (1) if E0's original-customer contribution is high enough, paired source and G may have zero incremental value despite better held-out technical transfer; (2) if a one-body precommitment binds, source pairing loses its flexible option premium; (3) if G arrives after deadline, it cannot change a locked embodiment, even if it perfectly diagnoses target regime; (4) if original buyer demand differs from technical holdout frequencies, benchmark-implied rankings can reverse. Measure all under actual physical approval and policy assignment, not hypothetical robot trials.
