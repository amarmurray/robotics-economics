# Shared reserve capacity as an endogenous economic complement to controller learning

24 November 2026. Continues [reserve contingency](RESERVE_CONTINGENCY.md), [joint stochastic load](JOINT_STOCHASTIC_LOAD.md), and the common qualified production J/V model. A controller can make fewer calls for human intervention yet increase the marginal economic value of the first shared reserve worker. The mechanism is the distribution of simultaneously competing, economically valuable calls; controller learning changes that distribution, the net benefits of actions, and their downstream data trajectories. Neither average intervention count nor isolated call value identifies the portfolio capacity decision.

## 1. Common whole-program environment

Let g denote a trained controller menu (retained R, extra source X, target grounding G, or embodied E); let k be actual reserve capacity committed before dispatch. Let h be the complete lawful predispatch report available to the dispatcher, with law P_g^k on the original buyer-worker interference population. The report contains certified demand/qualification and physical support sufficient to admit each candidate action. A missed commercial sale may be admissible; unsafe action is not. Actions a=(a_i) choose fallback, stagger, abort, dispatch or a reserve call in each original cluster. A_g^k(h) includes only jointly admissible nonanticipating actions, with dated worker conservation, shared buyers and rights. k may also change arrival reports, original eligibility and current queues. Retain a common original denominator; declined and unserved original opportunities remain in the ledger. New distinct authorized buyers enter the successor state rather than being counted as copies of prior orders.

Define F_g^k(h,a)=E[r(h,a)+beta V_{t+1}(s')|h,a,g,k], where r is accepted net contribution less actual call expense and all other variable costs, and s' includes licensed field/recovery/teleoperation and other source lineages, subsequent qualification, customer demand, and dated worker backlog. V is the optimized feasible continuation including future costs once. The state must include remaining reserve availability if workers carry over. Full value is J_g(k)=E_{P_g^k} max_{a in A_g^k(h)} F_g^k(h,a)-c_g-C(k), with one-time training c_g and committed reserve cost C(k) charged once. A worker drawn from elsewhere is not new capacity: its displaced service and learning are reflected in F or C, not both. An isolated local call score from a different population, report timing or action does not define F.

Fix a lawful no-call vector b(h) available both before and after reserve acquisition. It need not maximize the no-call family after acquisition. Let B_g^k(h)=F_g^k(h,b(h)). If a common safe fallback does not exist, do not use this decomposition; optimize the full menus. Define W_g^k(h)=max_a [F_g^k(h,a)-B_g^k(h)] (nonnegative with b available). Then, exactly,

    J_g(k)-J_g(0) = E_k B_g^k-E_0 B_g^0
                       + E_k W_g^k-E_0 W_g^0 - (C(k)-C(0)).

This is a subtraction identity, not an invariant-baseline assumption. The first difference includes idle reserve effects, composition/report drift and the designated baseline's own continuation drift. The second includes changes in noncall reoptimization as well as calls. With the same report law, the first difference can be bounded by E sup_h |B_g^k-B_g^0| or the sharper E |B_g^k-B_g^0|. If report distributions change, add a bound for expectation transport of the reference B on a common report space, or retain the exact separate expectations. When b is the optimized no-reserve rule and is still executable, W_g^0=0. Do not charge C again through B.

## 2. Local-to-pooled coupled certificate

For each lawful h, let l_i(h,a_i) be a reference conditional local incremental value relative to b_i, with l_i(h,b_i)=0. This may be built from a matched isolated audit only with an explicitly tested transport assumption. Define the residual

    D(h,a) = F_g^k(h,a)-B_g^k(h)-sum_i l_i(h,a_i).

D captures cross-cluster call costs, changed uncalled clusters' receipts, shared procurement, and changed joint continuation; it is not a new premium attached to a trajectory. Suppose D<=d_+(h) on all admitted vectors and D>=-d_-(h) on a particular lawful witness a^w(h). For an additive relaxation with per-action nonnegative resource loads u_i and capacity H (including k), and any nonnegative resource-price vector lambda, all feasible vectors satisfy sum_i u_i<=H. A valid upper and lower certificate is

    sum_i l_i(h,a_i^w)-d_-(h) <= W_g^k(h)
      <= d_+(h)+lambda.H+sum_i max_{a_i in relaxed_i}
                                  [l_i(h,a_i)-lambda.u_i(h,a_i)].

Proof: apply the residual bounds to the true objective; replace the witness objective by its bound and maximize for the lower inequality; for the upper, add lambda.(H-sum u_i)>=0, then relax joint constraints and independently maximize. If load varies within a report, use certified upper consumption for a sufficient physical witness and a genuinely necessary resource inequality for an upper relaxation; a bound built from sufficient admission restrictions is not automatically an upper relaxation of the actual feasible set. Pointwise prices may depend on lawful h, not latent state. Integrate and insert in the exact identity. Nonrectangular constraints and jointly safe fallbacks remain in A, not replaced by an expected worker budget.

A constructive way to bound D in dynamic use: take reference component functions U_i(s'_i) and a common terminal V, and suppose on every reachable next state |V(s')-sum U_i(s'_i)|<=epsilon. Use matched conditional immediate receipt and continuation component increments to define l_i. If their summed incremental prediction of r+beta sum U differs from the actual conditional increment by at most delta(h,a), then |D|<=delta+2 beta epsilon by subtracting the two conditional errors for a and b. This does not require independent successor states; it does require a supported additive approximation to their joint economic value and transport of incremental predictions. A whole-program residual may instead be measured directly. Recursively constructing an upper potential and a feasible lower policy gives a dynamic certificate; adding a second learned-data bonus would double-count V. At a terminal stage with truly separable payoffs and no shared budget except reserve, epsilon=delta=0.

For a single simultaneous unit-demand reserve pool with k slots, independently executable calls, additive exact conditional premiums w_i(h) (negative premiums may be skipped), and no other joint constraints, the upper and lower coincide:

    W_k(h)=sum_{j=1}^k w^+_(j)(h),
    W_k(h)=min_{lambda>=0} [k lambda+sum_i (w_i(h)-lambda)_+],

where order statistics are descending, padded with zeros. Proof: select the k largest positive premiums; any lambda between the kth and (k+1)st positive premiums gives equality in the dual, with endpoint lambda=0 if capacity exceeds positive calls. Thus local option values sum to the actual pool value iff all positive calls can be served jointly, or the excluded calls have zero premium. For k smaller, the loss is the expected sum of omitted positive premiums. With approximate additivity and a safe top-k witness, the gap is bounded by the residual bounds above; without the witness the price expression is only an upper bound. A joint recipe that pays 3 only if both clusters are recovered has W_0=W_1=0, W_2=3. Concavity in integer reserve capacity therefore fails under joint qualification/procurement even with nested feasible menus.

## 3. Training and reserve complementarity: a distributional theorem

In the exact additive special case, hold report law, baseline, remaining menus and call premiums fixed across k for each g. The gross marginal value of slot k is E_g w^+_(k). By the tail integral identity for nonnegative integrable random variables, it also equals integral_0^infty P_g(N_z>=k) dz, where N_z=sum_i 1{w_i>z}. Consequently the training-by-reserve cross difference at k is the integral of the difference in these tail probabilities (and any separately justified difference of acquisition cost or drift terms). This is a distributional criterion for economic complementarity, not a statement about average technical performance. If trained premiums are coordinatewise no larger on a common coupling of reports, each order statistic is no larger, so training and each extra slot are substitutes in this special case. But lower individual mean premiums, or even lower marginal distributions, are insufficient. Desynchronizing remaining calls can increase the chance that one worker is useful even while reducing total calls. With a fixed number n of clusters, if total expected positive premium decreases while the first marginal rises, at least one later marginal must fall, by summing the order statistics. This is a shift of the capacity-demand schedule, not uniformly higher or lower value of human labor. It need not imply higher optimal staffing when acquisition costs, safe admission and other investments differ.

For general D and endogenous report/baseline laws, define the cross difference as [J_G(k)-J_G(k-1)]-[J_R(k)-J_R(k-1)]. Apply the certificate separately to the four programs on a common law, retaining drift and costs. Bounds on the paired residual contrast can sharpen four separate bounds. The additive tail criterion alone is not valid when a recovery call alters other sites' future data, qualifications or original buyer budgets. In particular, a trained action can destroy a valuable joint lineage or unlock a jointly admissible recipe; the cross difference has no universal sign.

## 4. Exact counterworld: fewer calls and lower isolated value, but a more valuable shared worker

Two original buyer clusters, one potentially useful reserve call per cluster per simultaneous window. All no-call actions are safe and retained; a call is admitted only with the actual free slot. Values are incremental to the old no-reserve whole-program value (normalized to zero). No reserve means no actual extra slot. The reserve costs 9/4 ex ante, and even if idle coordination consumes 1/8 of net baseline contribution, in each controller arm. These costs are separate. Old controllers have call indicators A,B both equal 1 with probability 1/2, both zero otherwise. A call produces incremental accepted net receipts 5 minus variable labor cost 1, hence 4; no joint lineage interaction. One reserve worker yields gross 2, net -3/8. Independently staffed E adds net 1/16, with distinct procurement and resources stipulated; X adds zero in this example.

G changes the call distribution and gives new recovery lineages; its optimized no-reserve program net of training costs is -1/8 relative to R (G can be forgone ex ante). In both G worlds, P(A=1)=P(B=1)=3/8. With just one local call opportunity, using reserve yields 5 paid incremental contribution minus 1 variable labor cost and minus 1/2 of displaced future licensed stagger data contribution =7/2, relative to the safe trained noncall menu. This is a difference of full continuation values, not a separately added premium. With both clusters reporting a call, using either sole worker incurs an additional 1/4 call coordination expense and displaces 1/4 of the other cluster's licensed continuation contribution; net 3. This is a state-dependent call cost and cross-cluster externality, not two workers. No further action can serve both timely with one slot. All values conditional on the report; no foresight of future random receipts beyond these known conditional means. All successor value uses distinct authorized paid service and feasible resources. Training costs 3/8 and raises optimized trained noncall gross by 1/4, leaving net -1/8. Preserve the entire old program after the sunk training cost, including R+K when K is purchased. Assume a pool-wide compatible controller version must be chosen before its report is generated, so hybrid or report-contingent old/new version switching is not an additional action in this specific example. The old program remains an ex ante option, while safe local noncall recourse is always available. A richer hybrid menu must be valued separately; these values are not upper bounds for it.

Let j=P(A=B=1) in G. On the common original population, mass vector (neither, A only, B only, both)=(1/4+j,3/8-j,3/8-j,j), so j in [0,3/8] sharply. The old law is fixed in each world and can be coupled independently with these G reports; same-world joint potential histories exist. Both worlds have identical marginal call counts and isolated full call/stagger outcomes, including isolated successor contributions. They do not have identical full pooled histories; the missing evidence is exactly the joint report exposure and coupled call outcome. G has expected calls 3/4 rather than 1 and each isolated expected call value 21/16 rather than 2. Yet

    gross G pool option = (7/2)(3/4-2j)+3j = 21/8-4j,
    trained-version K increment = -1/8 + 21/8-4j - 9/4 - 1/8
                                = 1/8-4j,
    J_G(1) = max(1/8-4j, -3/8-3/8) = max(1/8-4j,-3/4).

The second term retains the complete old-version reserve program after paying for training. For no reserve J_G(0)=max(-1/8,-3/8)=-1/8. A fixed trained-version result is not the optimum of the retained menu.

At j=0 (disjoint), the first slot has gross 21/8>2 and net G+K=1/8>E=1/16. At j=3/8 (synchronized), the trained-version slot has gross 9/8 and its net is -11/8, but the optimized G+K arm retains the old reserve version and achieves -3/4, so E wins. The training-alone and reserve-alone arms both have negative committed net values. They are genuine complements in the disjoint world: the combined optimized net minus the sum of separate increments is 5/8, positive; in the synchronized world it is -1/4. If acquisition must be robust against all j, E is the known positive feasible witness under this menu; the oracle choice in the favorable world is not a robust policy. Exact G+K comparison against E is j<1/64, equality a tie. This is a model-implied economic crossing, not an empirical threshold. A same-population report audit that bounds j and a matched pooled call/lineage audit identifying the incremental payoff in simultaneous windows can close the comparison; isolated conditional call value alone cannot.

In the no-interference variant remove the extra 1/2 loss on simultaneous reports. Gross is (7/2)(3/4-j), so the sign reversal survives even without spillovers (synchronized optimized combined value still -3/4). Conversely, if training simply deletes calls on the same sample path and never raises a premium, the order-statistic proof rules out a rising first marginal in that additive model. The mechanism is endogenous timing/dependence and conditional economic value, not an inevitable result of robotics learning. For a continuing pool, dated service durations and future availability replace this one-window cardinality model; the shadow value must use the coupled continuation. New embodiments may relax simultaneous worker needs or compete for the same reserve and original buyer budgets; the independent E comparison is only the stated benchmark.

## 5. Synthesis and provenance

Model A's qualified production recipes and resource-price certificates now apply to shared, stochastic human recovery supply; Model B's source/grounding/embodiment allocation changes both the distribution and economic magnitude of reserve calls. One source of value for joint training is changing the joint temporal demand for scarce recovery workers, not just increasing individual capability. This can alter the slope of the economic data-value surface as the firm scales pooled deployment. Field, failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained sources retain separate lineage, licensing and acquisition costs. No direct benchmark-to-dollar map. Free optional new data with retained old policies cannot decrease gross optimized value; the negative G commitment above includes cost and is not a contradiction.
