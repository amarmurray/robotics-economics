# Instructor solutions: shared queues and original-customer production

**1.** Let Z_i=(-1)^bit_i. Expanding the eight-point distribution in Walsh monomials, fairness and pairwise independence imply that every one- and two-way coefficient is zero. The only free coefficient is c=E[Z_J Z_H Z_Y] in [-1,1]. Write theta=(1+c)/2; every even-parity atom has probability theta/4 and odd-parity atom (1−theta)/4. The (0,0,1) triple cell is odd and takes every value in [0,1/4], despite identical complete pairwise data.

**2.** The physically conserved queue is B3=J+H, B4=max(0,B3−1)+K. When B3=0, t3 serve yields 1/2 and K=1 prevents t4 customer service; waiting permits t4 Y. Because Pr(Y=1|J=H=0)=1−theta, serve iff 1/2≥1−theta, i.e. theta≥1/2. With B3=1, the worker clears the job and a t4 Y can pay; with B3=2, one job remains. Before deducting P's 1/8 source cost, T-wait earns (1+theta)/4 and T-serve earns 1/8+theta/2. A decision using Y itself at t3 is an illegal future-order oracle.

**3.** After source cost, P-R=5/16, P-T-wait=1/8+theta/4, P-T-serve=theta/2. The middle policy is never a global optimum once R remains optional. J_P−6=max(5/16,theta/2), so the sharp oracle P−E interval is [-3/32,+3/32]; E>G>P at theta=0 but P>E>G at theta=1. P's theta-informed winner threshold is theta>13/16. At theta=0 every P policy pays at most 5/16, while P-R obtains 5/16 everywhere. Therefore no ex-ante randomization improves that robust P guarantee; robust E=13/32 wins over robust P by 3/32.

**4.** Write max_i A_i−max_j B_j = min_j max_i(A_i−B_j) = max_i min_j(A_i−B_j). For the lower endpoint enumerate j, minimize z over Gamma subject to z≥A_i(mu)−B_j(mu) for all i. For the upper enumerate i, maximize z over Gamma subject to z≤A_i(mu)−B_j(mu) for all j. If the initial policy mixture is x, maximize z with x in its simplex and z≤sum_i x_i A_i(e)−B_j(e) for every vertex e of Gamma and every j. Positive z certifies a feasible fixed policy; armwise rectangular optimization may combine impossible shared causal shocks. Forbidden policies must be *excluded from* the legal menu, not assigned a speculative zero revenue.
