1. R=2Pr(AW)=1/2. Conditional on independent fair Q, P=2Pr(AW)+2Pr(QCW(1-A))=1/2+[Pr(CW)-Pr(ACW)]=3/4-t. G=2Pr(W(A or C))-1/8=2(1/4+1/4-t)-1/8=7/8-2t. E=2Pr(C)+2Pr(AW)-3/4=3/4. G's program cost is 1/8; E's independent resource expense is 3/4. No benchmark-to-cash conversion is used.
2. Even parity (000,011,101,110), odd parity (001,010,100,111), each equiprobable within its group, have all singles 1/2 and pairs 1/4. The 111 atom has probability 0 versus 1/4. Any mixture preserves the margins and varies t through [0,1/4]. R pays only when A=W=1, probability 1/4 in both. Neither paid incidence nor its amount reveals the triple.
3. E-P=t>=0, E-R=1/4>0. G-E=1/8-2t, so G wins at t<1/16, E at t>1/16 (ties at the threshold). Worst G=3/8, P=1/2, R=1/2, E=3/4; maximin E. Ex ante G probability x yields regrets (1-x)/8 in t=0 and 3x/8 in t=1/4. Equalize to get x=1/4, worst 3/32, unique continuous minimax solution over G/E. P and R are weakly dominated by E.
4. In low parity G-E=1/8; in high parity G-E=-3/8. After a low report, posterior G advantage is [a-3(1-a)]/8=(4a-3)/8; choose G only for a>3/4. With equal prior, report-low probability 1/2, so gross value max(0,(4a-3)/16), reaching 1/16 at a=1. If investment is irreversible and the report informs no other feasible action, postcommitment report has zero program-choice value.
5. If d=B^Tlambda, d^Tmu=lambda^Tb for every Bmu=b. Conversely if d is not in row B, a nonzero null direction v with d^Tv != 0 exists; a strictly positive feasible mu permits mu±epsilon v for sufficiently small epsilon. Both preserve observed moments but give different contrasts. A sign certificate needs only min d^Tmu>0 or max d^Tmu<0, not equal values across laws.
6. Fix full R triples to even parity in two worlds, with all R payments identical. Under G one world leaves A/C/W even; the other changes t2 original demand or creates a worker repair so the G triple is odd, with all three G individual and pairwise margins still fair. The same old R audit is compatible with G value 7/8 or 3/8. Only a physically lawful, appropriately assigned G-specific full original-offer/worker/outcome intervention or an externally defended exclusion can rank G; if no such exposure is permitted, G's causal postservice response remains a structural missing-support quantity, not a zero effect.
