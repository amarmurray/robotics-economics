# Prospective evidence: original-buyer feedback versus qualification

**Unpopulated and not authorization for customer contact, experiments, purchases or confidential data.** Companion to [buyer-response theorem](ENDOGENOUS_BUYER_RESPONSE_AND_ORIGINAL_OFFERS.md). There are presently no cleared customer receipts, rights-cleared G deployments, observed G orders, independently verified E worker/cell or numeric official input-output edges.

**Measurement unit.** A connected customer/robot/operator group at dates t0 commitment, t1 accepted source quality/recovery/announcement, and t2 original A/C order cutoff, dispatch and final cleared payment. Identify actual buyer entity and full initial budget for A across t1–t2. Independently enumerate A and C original offers and no-offers **before** routing or deciding which to serve; preserve unserved and declined offers separately from paid sales, abandoned requests and refunds. Capture contemporaneous quote, delivery promise, competing supply, product state, market publicity and all relevant deadlines. Record whether willingness to buy depends on observed pilot success or service-level failure.

**Grounding mechanism and rights.** Record time-stamped independent Q_G validation for the exact task/embodiment/site/acceptance gate, the right to use its field/recovery/teleop/simulation/synthetic/public sources, a verifiable distinction between actual target alignment and benchmark improvement, cost and old-R policy preservation. A missing or prohibited G action is recorded as excluded/unsupported, never as zero return. A proposed pre-order exclusion test requires that no G-related service improvement, availability, price, advertisement, hidden contractual change or rival interference can reach either original buyer before their offer deadline. If it cannot be maintained, use G-specific causal experiments or a sensitivity set; do not transport R demand under an unsupported assertion.

**Support and embodiment.** Document robot-time, worker eligibility and exclusive occupation; t1 repair job sizes; time physically cleared; late backlog; which tasks are qualified and their accepted service. Check whether E actually adds a separately manufactured/commissioned robot and worker, rather than rebadging the shared queue. Track site and customer demand when the new embodiment displaces rather than augments an existing purchase. Trace finance, installation, coordination and energy costs exactly once.

**Identification route A (pre-offer exclusion).** Rights- and safety-approved target qualification Q_G on matched contexts plus a complete original R A/C paired buyer registry, with proven timing/exclusion and no t1 job under G_skip, evaluate E_R[max(2D,3Q_G C)] in the existing optimized production function. An R accepted-invoice ledger alone is insufficient; G cost and the E alternative must also be accounted for. This route cannot identify post-disclosure adoption or t1 quality-induced new demand.

**Identification route B (action-specific observation).** If genuinely lawful and ethically authorized, cluster-assign real G versus R/P regimes while freezing disclosed prices/SLA comparisons and verifying interference boundary; independently audit all original offers, including no-offer and unsatisfied cases. Positive assigned probability and audited observation support in each analyzed stratum are necessary for sequential policy estimation. Revenue and resource totals measure whole-program value. Matched buyer orders plus training/qualification/worker histories distinguish which effect is buyer response. Do not randomize unsafe or forbidden deployment. Record original customer/referral/other-market response that may violate within-cluster independence.

**Identification route C (partial identification).** If no G arm exists, report baseline x_R and the unconstrained feasible G interval. An analyst-supplied delta bound on |x_G-x_R| is a transparent sensitivity parameter, not an empirical confidence interval. Evidence to *defend* it would require a narrowly matched, lawful disclosure test, physical exclusion verified before the original offer cutoff, or an approved action-specific order panel. A Q_G engineering score, R-only buyer audit, customer survey after selection, or vendor announcement does not establish delta.

**Broader portfolio tie.** Collect independent qualified accepted-service recipes across digital-digital, digital-physical and robot embodiments. Distinguish source field, failure/recovery, paid teleop, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained pipelines by admissible lineage, latency and compute/curation. Record all original buyers' payments/physical capacity and downstream price response, not benchmark-to-dollar conversion. Update the multi-end-market empirical production network only when original BEA/BLS numerical cells are actually retrieved and versioned; preserve the documented official route without inferring missing coefficients.
