# Exercises — cross-cluster occupancy

1. Starting from conditional Bellman residuals, derive the signed occupancy identity for two policies with distinct report distributions.
2. Prove the sharp weighted L1 residual bound with |e(z)|<=b(z); explain why a coupling preserving a prepolicy covariate need not be the true joint counterfactual law.
3. Enumerate the even/odd triple laws and compute P, G, E and the illegal independently summed G score.
4. With p>=7/8, delta>=-1/4 and an identical arbitrary X-dependent anchor error, compute a lower bound for G-E. What if anchor errors are not identical?
5. State precisely when free routine data dominates paid G. Why does a new robot sharing the same worker not replicate independent E?
