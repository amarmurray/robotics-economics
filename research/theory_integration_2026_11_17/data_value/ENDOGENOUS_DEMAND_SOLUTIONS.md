# Instructor solutions — endogenous demand and persistent transfer

**Separate from [ENDOGENOUS_DEMAND_EXERCISES.md](ENDOGENOUS_DEMAND_EXERCISES.md). November 18, 2026. Synthetic, not empirical.**

### 1. Robust floors and a critical logical distinction

\(E_pR=3+11p\), \(E_pG=10-3p\), \(E_pE=7.5\), \(E_pS=6\). Their minima over \(p\in[.4,.6]\) are respectively 7.4, 8.2, 7.5 and 6; G is the best **pure maximin**. But \(E_p(G-R)=7-14p\), which is +1.4 at p=.4 and −1.4 at p=.6. Thus G does **not** dominate R across worlds. \(E_p(G-E)=2.5-3p\), whose minimum at p=.6 is +.7: G does uniformly beat E in this synthetic identified set. Maximizing a policy's own lower envelope differs from finding a nonnegative paired difference in every possible common world.

### 2. The equalizer and upper certificate

The H value of the mixture is \(14x+7(1-x)=7+7x\); its L value \(3x+10(1-x)=10-7x\). Equate: \(x=3/14\). Both values equal 8.5. The prior \(p=1/2\) is admissible and assigns R/G each 8.5, E 7.5 and S 6, so no randomized mixture of all four can exceed 8.5 **at that prior**, hence no route mixture can have a worst-prior value above 8.5. The equalizer attains that bound. A single nondivisible expensive embodiment, limited number of independent customer units, shared-learning interference or correlated operational commitments may prevent this fractional action: the pure-route maximin is then G with 8.2.

### 3. Preserve the same prior in both order branches

For one p: \(P(Y=H,\theta=H)+P(Y=L,\theta=L)=.5p+.5(1-p)=.5\). The worst complete-law value is .5. A branch-spliced adversary takes \(p_H=.25,p_L=.75\) and gets .5(.25)+.5(1−.75)=.25. But since Y is independent of one persistent θ and p is chosen once, using different conditional p on the two branches is outside the admitted model. A rectangularity assumption changes that model; it does not discover evidence that physical θ changed.

### 4. Exact consumer-service envelope

Here \(G(K)=\max_{q\le K}(10q-q^2)\); unconstrained maximum q=5, so for K=2,4 the capacity binds. \(G(K_0)=20-4=16\); \(G(K_1)=40-16=24\). With outside option zero, capture is \(0.5G\): values 8 and 12, incremental contribution **4**. With \(\Pi_0=18\), captured values are .5 max(0,16−18)=0 and .5 max(0,24−18)=3, incremental contribution **3**. These come from physical/customer resource feasibility, demand shape, capture and outside alternatives, not a percent change in technical correctness. For discrete B/C services, one must use their jointly qualified recipe envelope rather than assuming this divisible example applies.

### 5. On-policy likelihood equivalence

Define both worlds with identical R transition, exposure, customer, observation, report, recovery and acceptance kernels and identical E kernels. They differ only on the counterfactual G/L outcome never visited under R. All R-only sample likelihoods are then equal regardless of sample size. In W+, G values (7,10) give min \(.6(7)+.4(10)=8.2\), above E=7.5. In W−, G values (7,4) give min \(.4(7)+.6(4)=5.2\), below E. A safely permitted randomized grounding intervention in that baseline stratum, or a separately validated structural bridge linking G to observed data, could distinguish them. Reweighting R cannot synthesize a missing treatment potential outcome. If the trial is physically unsafe, only bounds and safe proxies are warranted, not compelled exploration.

### 6. Source experience to captured accepted production

Measure source type/rights and curation/compute inputs, effect under matched training recipes on target skill and its uncertainty, independent target grounding and changed-body arms, safely reachable state support and recovery tails, full held-out customer-accepted service bundles, qualified production under shared human/physical resource caps, baseline and future eligible orders (including zero reorders), demand/price/capture/outside alternatives, and acquisition opportunity costs. Compare an old qualified controller or competent specialist, free productive routine data, and the redesigned embodiment. Positive benchmark transfer need not create new qualified capacity; even qualified capacity can be worthless if scarce bottlenecks or saturated demand prevent additional accepted service. A **free optional** dataset may be ignored after testing if it offers no positive net benefit under the existing policies. Forced training can cause negative transfer and does not violate the option-value monotonicity result.
