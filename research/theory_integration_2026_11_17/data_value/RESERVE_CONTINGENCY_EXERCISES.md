# Exercises: reserve contingency

1. Show the exact reserve option formula under unchanged idle baseline and predecision report law. Why is a purchased reserve not free when unused?
2. Given p=1/4 and P(S_s)=1/4, construct both extreme joint laws, preserving q=1/4. Compute G, GK, R+K and E at k=7/8.
3. Find the conditional successor rate at which stagger dominates reserve in overload. Explain why a full forced stagger outcome marginal fails to identify that rate.
4. Give a counterexample to training and reserve always being substitutes, and identify the extra evidence needed if reserve is shared across two sites.
