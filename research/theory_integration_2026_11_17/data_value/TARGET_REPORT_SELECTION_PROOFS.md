# Proof ledger — selected target reports, denominators and demand

**19 November 2026.** Standard finite missing-data/sampling results and conditional original-order production application; no independent originality claim.

1. **Sharp nonparametric identified set.** Observed category probabilities a=P(O=1,S=h), b=P(O=1,S=l), m=1−a−b. Hidden high mass t can be any value [0,m], giving q=a+t. Conversely any latent report/selection joint law giving (a,b,m) must have q in [a,a+m]. This proves S1 sharp; finite panels yield lattice bounds. A finite-panel fraction is not the target population q without sampling assumptions.

2. **Recording-rate set.** With ρ_h=P(O=1|S=h), ρ_l=P(O=1|S=l), total probability gives a=qρ_h and b=(1−q)ρ_l. Membership in allowed propensity intervals gives S2; for feasible q construct those conditional probabilities. Zero q or 1−q forces the relevant selected mass to zero. S3 follows with strictly positive endpoints. Known N is a count, not a known ρ. Unknown post-action report potentials invalidate the estimand itself.

3. **Finite confidence.** For each original-order observation define H_i=1{O_i=1,S_i=h}, L_i=1{O_i=1,S_i=l}. Even if O depends on S, each is Bernoulli with mean a or b. For N independent opportunities, two-sided Hoeffding gives failure ≤2 exp(−2N ε²) per indicator. Across m strata and two indicators, choose ε=sqrt(log(4m/δ)/(2N)). With union coverage ≥1−δ, q≥a≥H/N−ε and q≤1−b≤1−L/N+ε. Fixed m and predetermined N matter. Known propensity bands intersect inequalities over confidence intervals, but the resulting outer set is not asserted sharp for finite n.

4. **Two-world equivalence.** World A has P(selected h)=qρ_h=(2/5)(1)=2/5, P(selected l)=0 and P(no record)=3/5. World B has (3/5)(2/3)=2/5, same others. Product iid original-order law yields identical selected record counts and labels for **every** N, even when N is observed. Independently given H-state q_H=3/5, exact complete-service maximization over 16 pure report-action pairs yields W_A=43/40 (G/S) and W_B=1 (G/G); randomized policies cannot improve a fixed known kernel because the value is linear in the report-specific lotteries. With q_L in [2/5,3/5], constant G achieves 1 and at q_L=3/5 all rules have at most 1, certifying the **full randomized maximin** equals 1. Mere pure-policy enumeration is not a general maximin proof.

5. **Demand and report sensitivity.** At fixed π and q, expected within-state contribution v_z lies in common [y_min,y_max]. The difference of p and p' expectations is at most common span ×TV(p,p'); for fixed p', |V(π;p',q)−V(π;p',q')|≤Σ_zp'_zR_z|q_z−q'_z|. Supremum over P×C, use optimal π* at truth and the usual minimax lower bound. This proves S5. At a boundary where a particular policy's report-specific payoffs have smaller range, a refined policy-dependent bound can be tighter; S5 is a valid broad certificate, not asserted sharp.

6. **Information value caveat.** A better verified label set weakly raises maximin gross value only when it is a *nested* refinement of a frozen approved action/kernel environment. Recentered confidence intervals need not nest; paid, actuation-interfering or forced collection may reduce net continuation. Any actual Bayesian EVSI requires a prior and a preposterior experiment model, neither supplied by a confidence-width change.
