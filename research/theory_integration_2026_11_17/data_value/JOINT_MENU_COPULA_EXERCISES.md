# Student questions — demand dependence, data value, qualified production

1. Original buyers A and B demand qualified robot services at marginal probability1/2 each. They compete for one safely allocatable operator slot with net margins6 and4. Derive E[production] as a function of t=Pr(A and B), find the feasible t range and its effect on investment X versus operator-saving grounding G that admits both at margin5 each and pays upfront1. Explain what a benchmark task success percentage leaves unidentified.

2. With three different buyers, fixed-menu marginal probabilities (1/2,1/2,1/4) and service margins (6,4,2), derive the sharp one-slot expected captured-contribution bounds over all dependence structures. Explain event constructions attaining each and give one operational change invalidating the executable lower floor.

3. Suppose the two feasible regimes t=0 and t=1/2 have equal prior probability. The investor also has E with expected net2 and a genuinely retained R net2. Calculate information value of a perfect preinvestment paired-demand report and explain why an arbitrarily precise unpaired survey of marginals cannot substitute.
