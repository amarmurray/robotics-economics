## Prospective experiment 1 — fixed archive inclusion, the feasible offline mechanism candidate

**Treatment definition:** two lawful, frozen and hash-listed source pools from publisher archives, teleoperation and scripted-pick-place, with a common **original task/scene/robot/action** target where held-out evaluation is actually supplied. Independently log collection method, skill, scene, camera configuration, action parameterization, language completeness and failure/recovery tags. Predeclare an exact inclusion intervention, e.g. substitute equal approved training-compute budget of scripted episodes versus teleoperated episodes or add an optional source on top of a fixed baseline. Equal episode counts alone do not control trajectory length, entropy or curation. Use a frozen starting weight hash, optimizer schedule, compute and seed policy, unchanged test cohort and independent target holdouts. Do not re-use training scenes or nearly identical episodes in the held-out target set.

**Correct estimand:** assignment-level change in joint task qualification from *including those particular archived files in that learner at that budget*. Both regimens must have positive complete *learner-update network* assignment probability in a future approved design. Record all intended task regimes, failures, recovery attempts, safety-stop classifications, confidence intervals and any negative transfer; retain pretrained/public baseline as a separate factor. Offline evaluation never establishes deployment admission or paid service. If the original model/update design makes training compute exceed authorized scope, keep this as a preregistered protocol and do not train.

**Bias guards:** Collection method was not randomized across BridgeData scenes/tasks, scripted samples focus on pick/place, changing camera availability may correlate with collection date and class; matching is not enough to claim a "pure human versus synthetic" source-generation effect. If source splits differ in scene, state or action representation, qualify a narrower population or explicitly model transport rather than silently treating every record as exchangeable. Treat user-reported missing language labels as a pending audit, not a dataset-wide assertion.

## Prospective experiment 2 — new source generation, *not* identified by experiment 1

At the same context, randomize a signed and feasible teleoperation versus scripted data-generation policy (where both can genuinely act); control scene/task, starting robot state, success/failure, collection cost/attention and stopping rule. Freeze a downstream learning protocol and measure common target qualification. If one generator cannot safely perform a context, mark it **inadmissible**, not a missing-at-random observation; the source-origin effect there is undefined for available policies or a separate hypothetical effect under explicit engineering changes. If generator assignment is absent in the archival logs, only observational or partial-identification claims are supportable.

The [sharp source-origin support theorem](SOURCE_ORIGIN_VS_INCLUSION_SUPPORT.md) is deliberately conditional: with target weights \(\mu\), supported mass \(\rho\) and outcome in [0,1], unsupported context contributions can reverse any positive observed supported contrast. **It does not bound target transfer from fixed off-task scripted data**; that is experimentally testable with a held-out target even without scripted collection on that target task.

## Prospective experiment 3 — original customer production and decision value

Only after independent physical/organizational approval for the *whole* controller/embodiment/dispatcher/operator policy: randomly assign complete original-buyer X/G/E/R policies on non-interfering network clusters where signed and supported; collect original offer price/version, refused/unserved/accepted/completed/paid receipts, operator attention, customer restrictions, safe fallback, costs, rights and second-stage production data. For a matched held-out service, estimate the full joint qualification law rather than converting model benchmark success percentages into money. Explicitly record irreversibility and report arrival relative to hardware commitment. If hybrid X/E policies are unsigned, only value the implementable whole programs; no Shapley source mediation inference.

## Exact current research decision

**Feasible now:** cite and audit the original-publisher rights statement, archive partition and technical prospective estimands; run a small metadata-only CPU proof/check. **Not feasible/authorized here:** touching raw recordings, training models, collecting new episodes, robot operations, paid customer trials or full BEA numeric replication. The strongest bounded next step is a permission- and compute-compatible *metadata manifest* for an identified individual BridgeData V2 archive version, with task labels and held-out evaluation representation checked before any future independently authorized training; then put its technical result through the existing joint qualification and original-customer policy gates. Do not mislabel this ledger as field identification.
