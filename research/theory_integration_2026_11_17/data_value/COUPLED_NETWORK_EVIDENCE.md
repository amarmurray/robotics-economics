## Target economic estimands

For lawful program a, legal full-history policy pi and preassignment connected-network regime Z=z, the estimand is complete accepted/cleared net customer production v_(a,pi,z). For the unknown common preassignment regime prevalence theta, the candidate full-optimized value is J_a*(theta)=max_pi[(1-theta)v_(a,pi,0)+theta v_(a,pi,1)]. Joint uncertainty C contains **one** theta shared by all compared a, pi, original buyer sites and their linked learner/recovery roster. A differential certificate needs its genuine support and the conditional table; ex-post history selection or sitewise independent rectangles do not define the same economic experiment. Actual intervention values and the common-factor restriction are entirely unsupported so far.

## Minimum causal and legal exposure graph

- Define entire interference cluster *before* randomization: original buyer accounts and service windows, robot embodiments and dispatch, shared learner checkpoint lineage and broadcast / rollback, trainer / operator / rescue queue, upstream manufacturing slots, shared software or data reuse permissions, and customer demand feedback. Note external cross-cluster links. If broadcast is global, independent site-level arms are not randomized experiments.
- Store Z or a licensed preintervention proxy **before** intervention and do not let model training, sales contact, delivered service, payment, or a downstream failure define Z retrospectively. Ensure analyst availability is distinct from a signal the deployed operator may lawfully observe at decision time.
- Assign P customer-paid field episode (including success, failure and recovery), D deliberate target/teleop, SG licensed source+grounding, E genuine independent physical embodiment, and R retained controller at whole-connected-cluster scale with positive assignment to ethically/safely feasible arms. Match original service windows and customer buyer universes. No assignment, data access, safety approval, or receipt permission is currently signed.
- Record original buyer offer/no-offer, willingness to accept service, deadline, cancellation, displaced or unserved demand, service eligibility and independently qualified safe controller, offered physical/worker capacity, learner and rights state at dispatch, accepted delivery and final cleared net payment/refund. Count unserved original offers, not just successful deployments.
- Record raw learner/source trajectories by modality and provenance, target grounding, licensed retention and reuse, operator queue and simultaneous recovery, model update broadcast exposure, industry/task/NAICS candidate crosswalk, hardware availability, and later customer demand shifts. Never collapse production data, teleop, simulator, synthetic and pretrained input into one undifferentiated success variable.

## Identifying a common exogenous factor

A stable common theta is defensible only if preprogram exposure variables provide a factor that is truly unaffected by assigning P/D/SG/E and is defined over the **connected** network. Preassignment demand regime, exogenous scheduling/weather shock or pre-existing market segment may be hypotheses; none is verified. The fact that two sites shared a successful checkpoint is *posttreatment* and insufficient. A two-type model is falsified when conditional net outcomes, joint order/recovery data or adaptive learner transitions cannot be represented by two conditional whole-network laws invariant in their mixture across programs. Mechanisms can respond to interventions **conditional** on Z; distribution of Z cannot.

To estimate v_(a,pi,z), guarantee whole-graph exposure mapping and treatment propensity for actually legal policies. A cluster-randomized design can identify **mean** program differences without observing both potential outcomes on the same cluster. A complete policy comparison also requires sequential support and lawful action menus on histories the optimized policies can reach; logging R-only histories is not enough. Partial identification (bounds by missing service outcomes, censored offers or valid conditional response restrictions) must be used when positivity fails. Frozen model broadcasts, independent recovery shifts, and site separation are interventions requiring separate operator and rights approvals, not assumed counterfactuals.

### Decision-sensitive falsification

Test whether source learning changes the prevalence of the proposed factor; whether an "independent" E chassis still shares a recovery bottleneck; whether source pilot success changes later original buyer order arrival; whether lawful data rights invalidate presumed qualification; whether a buyer conditional paid contribution is only estimated from selected successes; and whether a single P policy is executable across unknown states. If any fails, the illustrative robust [1/10,1/2] program gap is inapplicable. At theta=0 no fixed P pilot mixture exceeding E* can have pilot probability above 1/5; at theta=1 it must be at least 1/2. Thus the conditional optimized ranking is *not* a predecision controller guarantee.

## Distinguishing static, dynamic and aggregate evidence

Once evidence supports a conditional response table, compute both the exact common-theta envelope of optimized full-horizon J and the **implementable** information-adaptive policy value under the actual observation filtration. Use finite-history Bellman residuals when Z is observed later or the network's transition law has multiple stateful factors. Do not identify conditional table entries from technical benchmark percentages, marginal learner accuracy or general sector output. Macro BLS 2025 sector output is context, not a production-flow or robot use coefficient.
