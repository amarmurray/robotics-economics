## Conditional cell proposition

Take calendar horizon T, one worker's usable clock A<=T, manual station time h>0 per unit, a downstream QC obligation q>=0 per unit performed by this SAME worker, and robot station clock r>0 inclusive of allocated downtime. Under the robot program the worker spends l>0 per unit on all loading/unloading/maintenance/recovery support (not on QC). D>0 is the common eligible demand cap. Units divisible, homogeneous, certainly accepted after QC, no other binding resources, no precedence/queue losses beyond r, fungible worker time, independent worker and robot occupancy except for captured support; steady-state relaxation, not a short-horizon schedule guarantee. Then manual Q_M=min(D,A/(h+q)), robot Q_R=min(D,T/r,A/(l+q)). These are Model A resource constraints; actual sale/captured margin and all cost/rights are a further layer. A strict increase in completed units occurs iff simultaneously

T(h+q)>Ar, l<h, D(h+q)>A.

Proof: if Q_M=D, strict improvement impossible. Otherwise Q_M=A/(h+q). A minimum of three numbers exceeds it exactly when all three exceed it; multiplying positive denominators yields the conditions. Thus slower nominal/effective machine production can raise full-cell throughput when QC competes with manual assembly for the worker, and even perfect station speed has no strict value through this channel at a demand or human-support bottleneck.

Using the paper's reported gross clock as a frozen illustrative r=340*60/108, T=3600 and A=3000 from the stated duty assumption, h=141, the first condition becomes q>16.4074 seconds per motor. This is a conditional threshold, NOT measured QC time or a prediction of the factory. With synthetic l=60 and nonbinding D, q=0 yields manual 21.28 vs robot 19.06; q=20 yields manual 18.63 vs robot 19.06. If the manual line uses a separate QC worker, this threshold does not apply. If the robot's support time l rises with QC concurrency, re-estimate feasible joint recipes instead. Throughput alone cannot establish cost avoidance or sales.

## Transfer versus additional data: two channels and a falsifier

Let target grounding G change the physical upstream handoff and/or retargetable representation, so downstream data can be reused across targets without collecting a separate corpus for each. Distinguish: (i) interface standardization changes the distribution of downstream inputs; (ii) representation changes the controller conditional on inputs. A paired ablation must hold the other channel fixed. Equal corpus counts do not equal acquisition cost; pretrained inputs, human trajectories, field failures and routine production remain separate. For fixed common demand and other constraints, a learned source X may reduce r while G reduces l; the cross-difference can be positive even if both unilateral improvements are zero. Synthetic T=A=60, D=10, q=5, r=15,l=10 gives Q=4. X to r=10 gives 4; G to l=5 gives 4; both gives 6. Proof by substitution; complementarity arises from simultaneous binding constraints, not a claim of neural source synergy. A new embodiment E can independently change r/l or add a second staffed line; compute feasible joint resources, not a second copy of the same worker. With positive unit net margin m and fixed costs, the incremental gross complementarity is 2m in this example; full J includes once-only learning costs and lawful successor payoffs. Free optional old policy retained; forced retraining may reduce physical output. If inexpensive routine P yields the same interface and service by deadline at no higher full cost, emulation rules out a deliberate grounding premium.

Prospective evidence: log paired interface states before/after alignment, per-target data lineage and same-budget technical variants; retain full program arms and original orders (incl nonattempts), stage acceptance, QC rejection/rework, gross occupation and support/QC clock overlap, rights and actual settled margins. Test whether support and QC genuinely compete for one worker and whether the additional service is actually demanded. With independent QC, revise the constraint instead of forcing the theorem. Standard serial bottleneck/resource LP and task decomposition (authors discuss modular alignment/interaction prior work); this is conditional economic synthesis, no independent originality or field economic calibration.
