# Lineage dependence: when three source observations create one economically actionable capability

20 November 2026 Eastern. Conditional extension of the **same** *When Capabilities Become Production* source X / target grounding G / new embodiment E / retained R production choice. This does **not** add a new value premium, invent a new research question, or assert scientific novelty. It sharpens the previous [episode occupancy result](EPISODE_SUPPORT_OCCUPANCY_AND_ECONOMIC_TRANSFER.md): independently named episode families, accepted transition shares, collision probabilities and even **all pairwise source/error relationships** need not determine whether an additional source mixture changes customer-relevant qualification and optimized paid production.

## What is being valued

For a fixed physically approved original-customer policy menu \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{signed}}\), customer regime \(\theta\), signals/data \(Z\), and jointly qualified recipe outcomes \(Q\), retain the existing optimized whole-network contribution
\[
J(\mathcal I)=\max_{\pi\in \mathcal P_{\mathrm{signed}}(\mathcal I)}
 \mathbb E[V(\pi,Q,\theta,\text{original orders, resources, rights})]
 -C(\mathcal I,\pi).
\]
The information set \(\mathcal I\) indicates **when** data is observed relative to irreversible decisions. \(V\) already accounts for captured service contribution and, when present, the correctly discounted endogenous continuation; no independent data royalty/premium is added. The following signal example isolates an *inference/selection* channel holding the two underlying specialist physical abilities and their original customer payoffs fixed. It is **not** a claim that real source-trained controllers learn via binary error-majority aggregation. Changed weights, hardware, service demand or customer network membership would require the full map \(Q=\mathcal L(X,G,E,\ldots)\) and a new qualified-production table.

A useful strict distinction is: **file IDs ≠ lineages ≠ independently informative task evidence ≠ jointly qualified service ≠ paid contribution.** True source data can be teleoperation, failed/recovered production, simulation, synthetic episodes, pretrained public data or other channels; none is privileged by the construction. Shared upstream generator, scripted recovery, controller checkpoint, environment seed, operator or task regime can induce dependence among even different provenance families.

## Proposition: pairwise verification is insufficient for the three-source production choice

Let a latent original-customer regime be \(\theta\in\{0,1\}\), equally likely. There are two separately stipulated signed specialist service policies, A and B. If the policy selected for \(\theta\) matches the regime, the qualified accepted-service contribution is 12 normalized units. Otherwise a **separately certified, available R** contributes 2; if no such R exists, the example's payoff table is invalid. A signal \(S_i=\theta\oplus E_i\) is a version-locked, target-relevant signal whose error \(E_i\in\{0,1\}\) may depend on the errors of other episodes. Conditional on \(\theta\), the vector \(E\) has the same exchangeable law in both regimes. Each signal has error probability 1/4, and every distinct error pair has \(\Pr(E_i=E_j=1)=1/16\). Thus **every pair is independent**, with identical full pairwise conditional distributions across the worlds.

The unknown third-order moment is \(t=\Pr(E_1=E_2=E_3=1)\). For any particular three-bit error pattern with \(r\) wrong bits, its probability is
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
r&0&1&2&3\\
\Pr(\text{that particular pattern})&
7/16-t&1/8+t&1/16-t&t
\end{array}
\]
where the entries for r=1 and r=2 each occur in three distinct patterns. This table follows by inclusion-exclusion from the one- and two-way moments plus t; it sums to one. **Nonnegativity is necessary and sufficient for the sharp interval \(0\le t\le1/16\).** Every t in the interval generates a valid exchangeable three-bit law with all the stated pairwise observations. There is no hidden assumption of three-way conditional independence.

The Bayes-optimal decision given all three signals is **majority vote** for every such t. To verify this rather than assume it: for three matching signals, the weight for the matching regime is \(7/16-t\) versus t; for a two-to-one split, the majority-matching weight is \(1/8+t\) versus \(1/16-t\). The former weakly exceeds the latter throughout the interval. Hence its accuracy is
\[
p_X(t)=\Pr(\text{at most one error})=
(7/16-t)+3(1/8+t)=13/16+2t
\in[13/16,15/16].
\]
This is a *sharp* partial-identification interval conditional on the stipulated signal law and finite signed policy table. Pairwise error testing — even perfect pairwise testing, not merely hashing episode names — cannot shrink it. Conditional independence of all three errors is only one special assumption: t=1/64, which yields \(p_X=27/32\). The end worlds t=0 and t=1/16 have exactly the same single and pairwise signal accuracy, but 13/16 and 15/16 three-signal accuracy.

**Proof status.** Inclusion-exclusion, pointwise nonnegative eight-atom completion and likelihood comparison above establish the algebra. This is standard higher-order dependence and Bayes-decision mathematics; the executable checks below are diagnostics, not the proof. Distinct episode-family *count* is insufficient because a shared latent process can generate dependent failures. Conversely even strongly correlated-looking raw images may carry new target information; independence must be defined relative to the task/learner and conditioning set, not by heuristic byte similarity.

## One common original-customer investment menu, and a reversal

Keep the same original buyer, original accepted service, qualified-policy capacities, fallback, captured contributions, rights and target regime distribution in every synthetic world. There is already one 3/4-accurate signal and its approved specialist-selection policy, giving R-inclusive expected contribution \(2+10(3/4)=19/2\). The firm may *optionally* choose among:

* X: collect/admit **two additional** apparently distinct episode signals, then use the Bayes-optimal three-signal selector, for cost 1/4. Assume all three signals can be observed before specialist selection, and the complete policy is signed.
* G: instead buy a target-grounded report with accuracy 7/8, conditional-independent of the existing 3/4 signal given \(\theta\), for cost 1/4. With two symmetric signals, the more accurate G report wins any disagreement and has accuracy 7/8. This is a distinct **information-acquisition** path, not extra robot competence.
* E: invest in an independently, completely qualified new embodiment policy with net captured original-customer contribution 10, with no claim about how it was qualified. Keep the baseline old specialist policy and R available.

At the stipulated common investment window X and G compete for one resource allocation; the joint X+G combination is **not** separately feasible/costed here and must not be smuggled in as dominated. Costs in this paragraph are normalized *complete program costs*; this is not data episode pricing, observed customer money or a benchmark-percent conversion.

Then
\[
J_X(t)=2+10p_X(t)-1/4=79/8+20t,\quad
J_G=2+10(7/8)-1/4=21/2,\quad J_E=10.
\]
The robust comparative interval is exactly
\[
J_X(t)-J_G=20t-5/8\in[-5/8,5/8].
\]
X outranks G **iff \(t>1/32\)**; G wins otherwise (tie at equality). In the t=0 world G=21/2 beats E=10, X=79/8 and baseline=19/2; in the t=1/16 world X=89/8 beats G=21/2. At actual conditional-three-way independence t=1/64, X=163/16 and G still wins. All three worlds share the **entire single and pairwise error distribution**, and the same captured customer payoff table. These are conditional decision claims, not an identified real-world investment ranking.

For one **stipulated** equal prior on just the two endpoint worlds, committing ex ante to either X or G yields 21/2; perfect target-valid information on t before committing yields \((21/2+89/8)/2=173/16\), so the gross precommitment value of a perfect audit is 5/16. It is zero for the already irreversibly committed contemporaneous choice, and any paid audit must net its own costs, interference and opportunity displacement. There is no separate data value added on top of optimized J.

### Negative results and domain limits

1. **Free optional observations are not harmful in a fixed information decision problem.** If old information and signed actions remain available and a new signal can be freely ignored, the feasible decision rules expand and optimized gross value cannot fall (Blackwell/EVSI logic). Forced admission, additional compute, changed policy weights or foregone customer work can decrease *net* contribution. The t example prices a costly X **choice**, not free optional archive access. It is not an instance of negative transfer under an optimally optional free dataset.
2. **Triple dependence is not an entire robotics learning law.** Episode errors here select a previously feasible policy; learned physical competence, gradient interference, non-iid rollouts, new customer demand, and changed embodiment are held fixed. If new data changes feasibility, conditional independence, original offers or whole-network resources, both the distribution and \(V\) must be re-identified rather than transporting \(J_X(t)\).
3. **Pairwise comparisons are not an independent-episode certificate.** The constructed errors are pairwise independent in all worlds yet not collectively independent except at t=1/64. Conversely observing high three-way conditional dependence alone does not prove clones; shared task difficulty, a common simulator fault, correlated policy errors or conditional target shifts may explain it. Episode lineage inspection and held-out target-conditioned tests answer complementary questions.
4. **Information is economically useless when it cannot change a signed production decision.** If the physical/customer policy set contains only the same R action in every regime, even perfectly informative signals have zero incremental production value. Target-relevant information value is therefore conditional on the qualified service and buyer contribution menu, not a generic entropy-to-dollars mapping.
5. No archive was downloaded or hashed and no learner, source customer, embodiment, actor or paid-production experiment was run. Official BEA–BLS numerical production-flow evidence, full historical repository CI and independent publication-novelty review remain missing. This theory does not resolve those gaps.

## Observable, discriminating follow-through

A version-locked independent held-out cohort with **paired three-signal outputs on the same original-customer target and reliable regime/qualification truth** directly measures t by the fraction with all three errors, provided the cohort is a valid independent sampling unit. With n independent matched target probes, Hoeffding gives a two-sided \(1-\delta\) radius \(\epsilon_n=\sqrt{\log(2/\delta)/(2n)}\), clipped to [0,1/16]; a robust signed X-versus-G ranking follows only when this interval lies entirely above or below 1/32 and the specified policy/payoff model has survived validation. Dependence across target probes or customers requires cluster-aware inference; one cannot count millions of correlated transitions as n independent trials.

For the actual *source-training* claim, this diagnostic is insufficient. Use the already proposed fixed-budget and fixed-accepted-K **whole-learner** source inclusion X × grounding G design with lineage-blocked splits and source-generating-method controls, plus matched joint held-out service qualification, changed learned occupancy, R regressions, original buyer offers/refusals, operator/dispatch interactions and paid/refunded contribution. Separate prospective source-generation from passive archive access. E changes feasible service recipes and must be evaluated as a whole production policy. Source rights, target licenses and compute/curation costs remain explicit.
