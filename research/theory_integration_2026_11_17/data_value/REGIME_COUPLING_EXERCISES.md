# Student exercises — regime-conditioned data value

Use the complete synthetic R/E_A/E_B original-customer model in [the chapter](REGIME_CONDITIONED_QUALIFICATION_AND_FLEXIBILITY.md). Treat all displayed contributions as hypothetical.

1. **Regime coupling.** Holding P(Q11)=q, P(Θ=A)=π and the unconditional full four-cell Q distribution fixed, prove the feasible x=P(Q11,Θ=A) interval. Construct full Q×Θ laws attaining both endpoints.
2. **Embodiment lock.** With Y symmetric conditional accuracy α and an independently certified R, calculate expected F and L contribution *on the Q11 event* by separately enumerating y=A and y=B. Show why their premium need not equal (2α−1)q.
3. **Information measurement.** If q=2/5 and α=3/4, compute m=P(Q11,Y=A) in the balanced x=1/5 world and skewed x=7/20 world. Under what condition does m identify x?
4. **Sharp envelope.** For π=1/2, α=3/4, find sharp flexibility-premium bounds at q=2/5 and q=4/5. If flexibility costs 3/20 in the same accepted-contribution units, classify robust acceptance, robust rejection and unranked cases, without treating the cost as an additional data premium.
5. **Unsupported original service.** Explain how to formulate lower and upper production-value optimization when the original buyer paid-contribution row of an otherwise physically admitted whole policy is not observed. Distinguish physical admission, conditional transfer qualification and original-market assignment overlap.
