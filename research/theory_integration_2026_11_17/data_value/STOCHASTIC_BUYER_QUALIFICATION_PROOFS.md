1. **Buyer-response tail identity.** For any nonnegative integer-valued Y<=h, min(Y,h)=sum_{k=1}^h 1{Y>=k}; take expectations. At h=2, E[N_2]=P(N_2>=1)+P(N_2>=2), E[N_1]=P(N_1>=1), subtract. For a common pre-admission N under invariant buyer preferences and menu, choose Y_h=min(N,h): the first h tails cancel and only P(N>=h+1) remains. With a distribution on N the fixed-response marginal is nonincreasing in h; this does not extend to changing menus, prices, provider quality, or other resource constraints. Six-cell exhaustive exact-integer test is a diagnostic, not proof.
