## Empirical claims the paper can defend

The common mechanism is that changes to the data mix or grounding affect joint controller qualification, which changes the set of executable service recipes, their dated resource requirements and customer choices. A separate optimization layer converts this altered menu into feasible accepted production. A learning-induced expansion in the menu need not have a positive net economic return after acquisition, qualification, staffing and training costs. The appropriate object is the incremental optimized captured contribution over an independently evolving lawful program, not a benchmark score, cost per demonstration or the number of installed robots.

Three groups of evidence support different links of this argument. First, Robin, VLAC-Cut, CEL and Learning while Deploying demonstrate heterogeneous technical effects of source composition, curated autonomous experience, fleet size and online learning respectively. Common-checkpoint comparisons are not complete learning-program comparisons; score and throughput definitions differ, and small CEL fleets can regress. Second, Vulcan, the motor cell, medication delivery and clinical laboratory evidence establish that assignment, execution, completion, downstream quality and deadlines have distinct denominators and clocks. Hospital cost estimates show why nominal labor release need not yield realized payroll savings. Third, Cainiao provides observed original-demand and conditional mode-choice denominators. Selection into use cannot be removed by conditioning only on successful delivery. These are complementary demonstrations of mechanisms, not links in one identified causal chain. See the evidence matrix and validation protocol for source versions and unresolved discrepancies.

## New production-side anchor: algorithmic coordination can substitute for installed capital

Allgor, Cezik and Chen, *Algorithm for Robotic Picking in Amazon Fulfillment Centers Enables Humans and Robots to Work Together Effectively*, INFORMS Journal on Applied Analytics, online 5 January 2023, DOI https://doi.org/10.1287/inte.2022.1143. Inspected publisher HTML abstract, Trade-off Between Multi-item Order Shipment Cycle Time and Pile-on, Implementation, Benefits and verification letter, 24 November 2026; no raw production records or full appendix replication.

The authors describe a deployed operations-research scheduling intervention, NOT an ML source-data intervention. Their implementation followed simulation and incremental field pilots; the new picking module was deployed while retaining the legacy assignment module. The picking window traded batching efficiency against order cycle time; the production setting was fixed after selection against operational requirements. Authors report a 62% reduction in drive travel per picked item, 31% fewer drives required and about $500m in direct drive-cost savings evaluated over 2017–2020. They report no deterioration in operational efficiency, but do not supply in these passages a complete original-order settlement panel, matched learning histories, implementation cost or customer-level deadline distribution. The savings are the firm's reported avoided drive cost, not independently reconciled net profit, learning ROI or an observed marginal price for data. Separate warehouse space benefits are not added to this figure. The source matters because it demonstrates a concrete alternative to buying hardware: reorganize physical work, use a better controller/dispatch module and preserve complements where replacement is not necessary.

Under a deliberately restrictive continuous fleet requirement model, constant throughput, unchanged availability/utilization, homogeneous drives and unchanged nontravel work, initial drive work is H+D and final work H+.38D. A .69 requirement ratio then implies D/(H+D)=.31/.62=.5. This is an algebraic conditional explanation, NOT a measured composition of Amazon drive hours. If throughput/availability ratio changes by factor g, the requirement ratio is g(1-.62f); absent g, f is not identified. Real peak-dependent capacity, integer drives and congestion further break the ratio. The study does not estimate those quantities for this model.

## A compact resource-realization certificate for the existing production model

This specializes Model A to fixed qualified service commitments and one fungible capacity resource across dated windows, without carryover or queue changes. Normalize capacity to integer units of serviceable drive-time per window. Window t requires h_t+d_t units, with h nontravel and d eligible reducible travel. The qualified service, buyer deadline, worker resources and all other recipes are held fixed; assume each unit of travel saving can be removed from its window without violating those conditions. For reductions s_t in [0,d_t], sum s=S, required installed capacity B0=max_t(h_t+d_t), B1=max_t(h_t+d_t-s_t). Let slack a_t=B0-h_t-d_t. Then

    B0-B1 = min_t(a_t+s_t).

Proof: subtract the maximum from B0. This exposes why aggregate engineering improvement is insufficient to infer avoided capacity. For a fixed total S and independently supported d_t, the sharp lower endpoint is

    k_min = min_t [a_t + max(0,S-sum_{j≠t}d_j)].

Each s_t is at least the positive remainder after exhausting other windows. For the minimizing t, allocate as much as possible outside t, then its minimum; feasibility follows from S<=sum d. The sharp upper endpoint is the largest integer k>=0 satisfying both

    k <= min_t(a_t+d_t),
    sum_t max(0,k-a_t) <= S.

Necessity: all a_t+s_t>=k, so each lower requirement is feasible and their sum fits S. Sufficiency: start at those lower requirements and allocate the remaining S within unused d capacity; total capacity is sufficient. Continuous units use the same inequality with real k; integer capacity rounding is a separate modeling assumption. No empirical bounds supplied by this paper justify the d_t or constant-service premise for an actual deployment. This is a standard peak-resource/water-filling argument, not a claimed novel optimization theorem.

Synthetic example: two windows, h=(2,0), d=(8,8), S=4; baseline capacity 10. The same four travel units released can reduce peak required capacity by anywhere from 0 to 3. A reduction allocated (0,4) leaves the peak at 10; (3,1) equalizes residual workload at 7. For installed nonrecoverable capacity K and optional new capacity costing c per unit, actual avoided incremental purchases are c([B0-K]_+-[B1-K]_+)=c min(B0-B1,[B0-K]_+). At K=8 the avoided purchase range is [0,2c]; at K>=10 it is zero. If the units can instead be redeployed to a genuinely valuable other service, optimize that option once in Model A, not in addition to the same avoided purchase. If capacity is contracted, reusable or salvageable, replace this cost curve with the relevant lawful opportunity cost. No conversion of Amazon reported percentages to dollars is needed.

Compare X additional source data, G target grounding, P routine production-generated reuse and E new embodied access. A source or grounding intervention can change the dated s distribution, while E changes K or introduces new qualified routes. The incremental economic ranking depends on binding windows, baseline installed/committed capacity and full service feasibility, not just aggregate technical gains. When a learner changes demand or deadlines, resource simultaneity or support, replace this static restriction with the original controlled-law J; the above bound cannot certify safety or preserve customer service by construction outside its premises.

## What is needed to falsify the first-paper mechanism

In addition to the compact validation protocol, record baseline and successor required capacity by dated demand window, distinguish installed, committed, new and reusable capacity, capture configuration/dispatch versions and software/qualification effort, and record actual capital orders or documented redeployment. Preserve the retained policy and identify whether a cheaper P program or coordination-only intervention attains the same service and usable capacity. This is a genuine competing explanation: a non-learning scheduling change may make additional data acquisition redundant. Compare all arms at the same service, resources and horizon or explicitly account for changed service and continuation in J.

No source found in this bounded search follows independently evolving data programs with jointly observed original-order settlements, paid clocks and acquisition costs. Thus the paper can defend a conditional mechanism with empirical anchors, not an estimated universal data value or causal ROI. The current official BEA/BLS landing pages confirm the annual and historical table routes but do not expose a newly verified contemporary numerical industry edge in this search (https://www.bea.gov/itable/input-output ; https://www.bls.gov/emp/data/input-output-matrix.htm ; accessed 24 November 2026). Do not use a secondary repackaging as an official new edge. The historical 2009 example remains separate.
