# Composition panels: what finite experiments can actually discriminate
25 November 2026 · Companion to [nested learning states](NESTED_LEARNING_STATE_AND_ECONOMIC_RANK.md) and [When Capabilities Become Production](WHEN_CAPABILITIES_COMPACT_DRAFT.md).

## Economic priors and finite evidence
A source stock is not a sufficient economic object merely because it predicts an observed aggregate. It must retain the distinctions that matter to alternative uses of embodied capital and scarce attention. The previous local derivative-rank restriction requires a common checkpoint and a smooth neighborhood. Contemporary learning studies instead report a handful of widely separated recipes. We need an economically interpretable restriction testable with such finite data without pretending that finite chords are local derivatives.

Let z_i denote a lawful fixed-size composition or reweighting arm at common nonlearning state b. Y_ji is independently measured gross downstream net-variable contribution from original offered services in predeclared panel j. Each panel separately solves A or uses a fixed feasible policy; panels are alternative environments, not revenues to sum. Hold the within-panel offer law, rights, dated capacities and acquisition accounting fixed for the diagnostic. Different arms can induce different future production in the complete B comparison; this experiment does not replace that comparison.

**Proposition 1 (finite unrestricted scalar nonidentification).** For any distinct finite z_i in R^m and arbitrary finite real Y_ji, there exist smooth scalar e and smooth F_j satisfying Y_ji=F_j(e(z_i)) at all observed points. Choose a linear projection a avoiding the finite union of hyperplanes a'(z_i-z_k)=0; its projected coordinates are distinct. Interpolate the values for each panel with a univariate polynomial at those coordinates. This proof does not assert feasibility of extrapolation or a sufficient dynamic state. If outcomes must lie in a bounded interval, use smooth disjoint bump functions centered at distinct coordinates and interpolate between admissible values and a common admissible baseline, preserving the bounds. Thus finite response rank alone cannot reject an unknown unrestricted smooth scalar, even if measured without noise. A local derivative-rank rejection requires a quantitative neighborhood restriction. This is classical interpolation, not a novelty claim.

**Proposition 2 (finite ordered-menu scalar test).** Suppose a higher scalar state e permits all previously qualified recipes and feasible policies at unchanged resource requirements, contribution and demand, possibly additional ones. Optimized gross Y_j is nondecreasing in e by feasible-menu inclusion (also true for dynamic B if all old policies retain the same law and payoff, not merely nominal permission). This is a substantive economic restriction, not a claim that actual learning is nested. Fixed forced retraining, changing costs or endogenous demand can violate it. Under this restricted null, no two composition arms can have opposite strict economic rankings across panels.

More generally suppose F_j is monotone with known direction sign s_j in {+1,-1}, allowing flats and discontinuities; or enumerate signs if unknown. Create a directed edge i→k whenever s_j Y_jk > s_j Y_ji for some j. An exact finite scalar monotone representation exists iff the union graph is acyclic. Necessity: strict outcome change requires strictly increasing latent index in that direction; a directed cycle is impossible. Sufficiency: take any topological order, assign strictly increasing scalar indices, and interpolate each signed response monotonically (piecewise linear or smooth monotone step interpolation). On the finite sample there is no additional restriction from smoothness. Known common nondecreasing orientation is the nested gross menu case; allowing panel-specific negative signs is a weaker ordered-state comparator. With simultaneous intervals, build edges only for strict separated intervals; a cycle is sufficient to reject under every compatible outcome array, absence of a cycle is not a general statistical acceptance procedure. A simultaneous confidence procedure must respect shared training seeds, common panels and clustered trials.

### Investment interpretation
Opposite preferences between two equal-size source compositions across two customer panels, if measured on original-service contribution and resource-feasible schedules, reject a globally ordered nested-menu scalar even at a capacity kink. They imply neither a universal technical rank nor unrestricted scalar impossibility. For a firm facing uncertain demand, the composition choice must condition on the buyer mix rather than maximize a universally ordered knowledge index. Costs, acquisition time, shared body constraints and evolving rival P/C/E remain in B; gross response vectors do not price data on their own. A larger source pool with old policies retained can still weakly improve optimized value even when forced replacement makes a particular panel worse.

## Evidence: ReMix supplies two finite input directions, not local economic output derivatives
Hejna et al., arXiv:2408.14037v1, 26 August 2024, §§4.1–4.4, Table 2, Appendix C, https://arxiv.org/html/2408.14037v1 (HTML inspected 25 November); published CoRL proceedings https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/hejna25a.html (2025) abstract differs numerically from preprint, so use only versioned preprint details here.

The preprint compares three OpenX RT-X sampling schemes (uniform per state-action pair, expert weights, learned ReMix weights), with 11 domains. Final policy training is 400,000 gradient steps, common small in-domain co-training allocation, and 10 real trials per task. Weight construction and subset selection change the experimental program; these are matched training budgets, not demonstrated identical initial trained checkpoints or equal paid data acquisition costs. In the 25% subsetting comparison, physical subset size is matched but actual sampled proportions still use assigned weights, and feasibility constraints on rare-domain removal mean corpus shares are not necessarily equal to these sampling weights.

Two finite sampling directions are demonstrably independent. Table 2's RT1/Toto percentages are U=(40.9,3.42), H=(26.8,4.13), R=(42.5,16.3). Normalize each full 11-coordinate row by its reported sum (99.97,99.91,100.08, rounding). For directions H−U and R−U the RT1/Toto minor is approximately −0.0182364 in fraction units. This shows independent **input** changes, not rank of a derivative. The preprint reports aggregate success 82.5%, 52.5%, 37.5% for ReMix, human and uniform on its four RT-X subset tasks; an aggregate is a single output row. The task-level figures are not transcribed here; even with them, distant recipes, learned weights and sparse evaluations would not identify the local derivative-rank restriction absent quantitative smoothness and uncertainty. The 25% subset result is technical, not evidence that costly collection, storage or retraining is economically optimal. This study is an excellent template for a finite composition experiment using original service panels but not a commercial economic rank estimate. In particular, 400k gradient steps do not imply matched compute for the weight-selection stage (the authors note two full training passes).

## Evidence: source use is itself a technical choice
Wei et al., Ambient Diffusion Policy, arXiv:2606.12365, dated 10 June 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12365, §7.2 Table 1(b) and Appendix G.3, inspected parsed text 25 November. Neural motion planning compares filtering, co-training and frequency-selective data usage. Point estimates of success are 46.0%, 59.9%, 65.9%; squared acceleration 3.9, 42.7, 31.4 (lower better). The point-estimate rankings of success and negative acceleration cannot both be monotone functions of a scalar even allowing opposite orientations: the former orders filter < co-train < ambient, the latter co-train < ambient < filter. This is only an illustrative technical point contrast: success uncertainty overlaps/touches for the latter two arms, smoothness uncertainty not supplied in the table, outcomes are not economic panels, and training treatment changes rather than just fixed-size source composition. No statistical rejection asserted. The planar-pushing experiment is a source-to-target **simulation** proxy as clarified in Appendix G.3, not observed sim-to-real commercial deployment. This evidence motivates distinguishing raw source holdings from training-time use rules in the firm's choice set; it does not justify direct benchmark-dollar conversion or an assumption of monotone transfer.

Existing Robin has one fixed-corpus replacement direction and a distinct live ranking trial; VLAC-Cut compares matched recovery counts but adds curated segments and resets both arms to a selected checkpoint. Neither supplies two independent local source directions with matched economic outcome panels.
