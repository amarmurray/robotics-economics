## Economic premises
A learner may improve execution at a present workcell, qualify previously unreachable target recipes, or improve the economics of using one more body. These need not produce the same ordering of learning investments. A scalar ranking all feasible production menus by inclusion is a strong economic benchmark: old recipes survive with unchanged requirements, value, information and rights. Under arbitrary scalar interpolation finite-panel rejection is impossible. Nested menus preserve the order of optimized *levels* even at a production-frontier kink; their slopes with respect to embodied capital need not preserve it. A reversed ranking of MPK_r, by itself, does not establish heterogeneous nonnested knowledge.

Consider alternative equal-size lawful source mixtures i at a matched checkpoint. Each market panel j represents original customer opportunities, not revenues to sum across alternative uses. Let k index a predeclared vector of dated body, operator, validation and commissioning endowments. F_jki is optimized gross downstream net-variable contribution at that endowment, before differential data/body acquisition cost. Match demand, prices, rights, information and accounting across arms within each panel. Physical MPK fixes the recipe law and perturbs installed capacity, measuring accepted-service vectors (or contribution-weighted service under fixed weights). Private investment instead compares independently evolving programs including acquisition once, receipts and successor histories.

## 1. Finite ordering certificate
For each cell derive L from a feasible schedule with the actual dated endowment and supported recipes, and U via the joint residual certificate: at nonnegative resource price p, U=p'b+sum_i q_i(h_i^S(p)+e_i(p)), with independent e_i covering all omitted recipes' additional reduced contribution. No oracle observing late information can provide a feasible predecision lower witness. If no supported upper residual exists, use a valid looser bound or report nonidentification. For stochastic panels bounds must cover expected values under the feasible information structure and simultaneous uncertainty.

Nested scalar menus imply one common source-arm order along which every F_jki is nondecreasing. With only independent closed cell intervals, draw edge i→i' when U_jki<L_jki'. A cycle rejects the null. **For this interval-only relaxation**, acyclicity is also sufficient for an array consistent with some common monotone order. Proof: take a topological order and, in each panel/capacity sequence, assign each arm the largest lower endpoint among itself and all predecessors. It cannot exceed that arm's upper endpoint, since an earlier lower endpoint greater than the latter upper endpoint would force the reverse edge. This proves compatibility of a value array, not existence of actual nested production menus nor statistical acceptance.

Paired audits can contain information omitted by independent boxes. Suppose they bound F_v-F_u in [l,h], where u,v are cells of a panel. Encode F_v-F_u<=h by edge u→v of weight h and F_u-F_v<=-l by v→u of weight -l. Absolute bounds use an anchor fixed at zero. Free optional capacity with no additional obligations implies F_expanded>=F_base; a purchased support commitment need not. A proposed common source-arm order adds zero-weight edges from later to earlier arms in each panel/capacity. The system is feasible iff the graph has no negative-weight directed cycle. Necessity: sum inequalities around a cycle. Sufficiency: add a zero-edge super-source, take shortest-path distances, subtract the anchor distance from all values; the resulting potentials satisfy all edges. With finite absolute bounds and a feasible graph, the shortest path from u to v is the sharp upper bound on F_v-F_u in this relaxation, attained by shortest distances from u translated to the anchor. A negative cycle before ordering constraints denotes inconsistent evidence, not rejection of the economic null. After checking consistency, test all permutations of a small predeclared arm set; no feasible order means rejection. The result is exact for difference inequalities, not arbitrary nonlinear joint constraints.

Classical prior art: Dechter, Meiri and Pearl, *Temporal Constraint Networks*, Artificial Intelligence 49 (1991), 61–95, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90006-6 (publisher and Technion abstracts inspected 25 November 2026). Difference-constraint reasoning is not a new algorithm. The synthesis combines dated production witnesses with economic state ordering, not a claim of novelty for data complementarity or future data value (already DDA).

## 2. What an MPK comparison reveals
Let M_i=F_i^1-F_i^0 at expanded versus original capacity. Exactly,
    F_A^1-F_B^1=(F_A^0-F_B^0)+(M_A-M_B).
If simultaneous baseline difference bounds are [-d,d], and M_A-M_B>=m>d, A ranks strictly above B at expanded capacity. A reversed result in a second panel rejects the common nested scalar order even if baseline level intervals overlap. Crossed marginal rankings without baseline evidence do not suffice.

Counterexample (constructed):
| Panel | Low state: base → expanded | High state: base → expanded | marginal low,high |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4 → 8 | 8 → 8 | 4,0 |
| B | 4 → 4 | 4 → 8 | 0,4 |

Implement A with two 4-contribution orders, low state consuming one body-period per order, high state offering faster recipes completing both within a single body-period. B has one common 4 order and one additional 4 order qualified only in high state, each consuming a body-period. Expanded endowment is two body-periods. High retains all old recipes. At a body cost of 2 the preferred incremental installation market reverses, although high weakly dominates every level. With common unit contribution these differences are proportional to accepted-service units. No conclusion about market shares or aggregate elasticity follows.

## 3. Dated bottleneck and incomplete audit
Two alternative markets have the same two dates. Both arms retain a common early order worth 4; the late opportunity is worth 6 and consumes a late body slot and late verification input. A qualifies the late recipe in market 1, B in market 2. A new commissioned body provides a late slot; it cannot retrospectively do early work. Late verification capacity is one unit. Base has no late body slot.

| Panel | A: base → expanded | B: base → expanded |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 → 10 | 4 → 4 |
| 2 | 4 → 4 | 4 → 10 |

The high arm's two-order feasible schedule provides L=10. At the original endowment the absent late slot's dual price of 6, early order residual 4, zero other prices, certifies U=4 for the stated menu. If at expanded capacity an independently justified residual ceiling of 1 covers all omitted low-arm late alternatives at p=0, U_low=5 suffices to establish the strict ranking without reconstructing every recipe. If verification is unavailable late, both exact menus deliver 4 even after expansion despite technical qualification differences. Aggregate operator-hours do not resolve the date of availability. Free lawful availability of both controllers permits union of their feasible recipes and weakly dominates either; forced composition replacement is not optional free access.

A less complete audit uses base levels each in [4,6], expanded levels each in [4,12]. Panel 1 incremental gross intervals A:[5,6], B:[0,1]; panel 2 reversed. Raw level boxes never separate. Joint difference constraints imply high-minus-low >=2 in each expanded panel; no common nested order. At an incremental body cost of 3 the high arm's fixed-exposure net operating return is positive, low negative, absent any other costs or continuation changes. Real investment still requires the full independently evolving P/G/C/CG/X/E comparison; the gross result is not a dollar estimate from benchmark accuracy.

## 4. Relation to propagation and next empirical discriminator
A source can change the next funded body destination even with nested scalar learning (the slope counterexample). Conversely the dated-bottleneck test identifies a restriction on economic levels across markets, not a number of physical skills. Threshold crossings affect induced-deployment entries only if the body is funded and commissioned and no other scarce resource or alternative investment absorbs the return. Faster commissioning may reveal an ordering reversal by changing when a body becomes usable. Treat alternative commissioning dates as different predeclared panels or complete programs, not free changes to the same panel. The gross diagnostic holds nonlearning state fixed; the full dynamic decision includes source work, data rights and independently evolving alternatives without an added data premium.
