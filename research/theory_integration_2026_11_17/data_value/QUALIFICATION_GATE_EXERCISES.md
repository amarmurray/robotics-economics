# Exercises: source information, qualification and embodiment choice

[Theory](QUALIFICATION_GATE_AND_DATA_VALUE.md). Separate [instructor solutions](QUALIFICATION_GATE_SOLUTIONS.md). All values synthetic.

1. Given fallback contribution R=2, complete accepted original service=12, cost 1/4, predictor correctness p and success conditionals r^+,r^-, derive expected original-customer net contribution. Explain why p alone cannot rank source X versus grounding G.
2. For p_X=13/16+2t (0≤t≤1/16), p_G=7/8, common r correctly selected acceptance and zero wrong acceptance, calculate X−G and identify its threshold. Show how E net=10 can defeat both.
3. For equally likely endpoints t=0 and t=1/16, prove the exact three-piece value of a free precommitment t audit with X/G/E. Evaluate r=4/5, r=9/10 and r=1. State when the audit has zero value despite technically useful source data.
4. Keep identical high-t source signals but set X correct service qualification=1/2, G=1, both wrong=0. Calculate X/G/E net values and describe the missing empirical estimand.
5. Derive sharp bounds for r^+ and r^- belonging to separately feasible intervals and known p, and identify why joint source-policy interference can prevent sharp simultaneous X/G endpoints.
6. For complete paid-service laws with TV distances ε_X and ε_G from authorized shadow counterparts, prove a sufficient condition for robust X>G. Why cannot ε be inferred from benchmark accuracy improvement?
