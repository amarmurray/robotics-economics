# Observable recovery, data grounding and embodiment within qualified customer production

**20 November 2026 UTC | continuing the November 22 Models A–B acceptance audit.** The preceding [full-state physical recovery game](ROBUST_RECOVERY_GAME_AND_DATA_VALUE.md) establishes an exact conditional recovery criterion *when the correct post-X physical state is observed*. Its missing premise is precisely the availability, reliability and permission of that observation at the actual original customer, robot and operator network. This chapter develops the finite information-support version and integrates heterogeneous data and original-order production. These are known partially observed reachability/shielding mechanisms, **not** new mathematical control theory or a physically approved deployment.

## Original network and proof

A state s in a finite network S includes original buyer order and service clock, physical workpiece/robot, safe and terminal status, shared operator and specialist capacity, dispatch and data rights, and controller versions. Let K be the safe states and T subset K the absorbing *physically qualified* completed service states. Customer willingness to pay and actual receipts are distinct predicates. A_R(s) are the legally available incumbent-R rescue actions; F_R(s,a) is the nonempty **complete** set of successors under disturbances, including faults, operator loss and service deadline failure. An observation O(s) is only what the controller/operator can reliably distinguish under the signed deployed sensor and communication stack. The controller must choose the same action from observationally indistinguishable histories.

For a nonempty possible-state set B consistent with a common observed history, define the next observation-conditioned state set U_o(B,a) as the union over all s in B of successor states in F_R(s,a) displaying observation o. Define C_0 as all nonempty B contained in T; then C_t includes C_(t-1) and every nonempty B contained in K for which there is *one common action* a in the intersection over s in B of A_R(s), all successors stay safe, and every nonempty U_o(B,a) lies in C_(t-1). Sensing or action-switching with positive time and resource use must consume modeled steps and budgets.

**Proposition 1, exactness under a sound observation model.** C_t is exactly the set of information supports from which some legally permitted, nonanticipating observation-contingent incumbent strategy robustly completes the original qualified service within t remaining steps against *every* admitted disturbance. Proof by induction: at zero time all possible states must be completed. At a positive time, indistinguishable states force one common first action; necessary continuation must work after every possible observation. Conversely a common qualifying action and recursively winning observation-contingent continuation construct the strategy. This is the finite belief-support reachability-game recursion, not a novelty claim.

**Proposition 2, corrected X-first admission.** For a one-step X source-trained proposal from the original network, enumerate the entire possible first-outcome support S_X. X-first/R-only after-X rescue is robustly admitted with t total steps iff the initial action is safe and every nonempty observation class of S_X belongs to C_(t-1). This is an iff **only** for the specified complete disturbance and observation support, clock, permissible physical action class, and independently certified sensor. Merely verifying that every s in S_X lies in the *perfect-information* old winning region W_(t-1) is necessary but in general insufficient. A stored R executable is not an observation policy, and a policy still selectable before X is not proof of recoverability after X.

**Proposition 3, free refinement and its boundary.** If a certified new observation O' reveals at least as much as O, the old observation is a deterministic garbling of O', and the physical transitions, legal action set, delay, operator use and costs are unchanged, then every old robust strategy can be simulated by ignoring extra information. Recoverability cannot shrink. Target grounding that needs an operator probe, physically changes the state or has unbounded error support does not satisfy this free-refinement theorem. A classifier with a positive probability of confusing two rescue-critical states does not establish a hard worst-case distinction simply by reaching 99% average classification accuracy.

## Exact original-service counterexample

After the source-trained X action, the only possible physical outcomes are done, alpha and beta. 'Done' is terminal. Both alpha and beta are safe and individually fully recoverable within one more step, but alpha needs old action ra and beta needs rb: the wrong action enters a modeled unsafe fault. A coarse observation labels both states simply 'retry'. Hence {alpha,beta} is within the elementwise full-state winning region but **outside C_1**. X is not admitted under this field observation; an optimistic oracle picking the right rescue from the hidden state would be invalid. A separately *certified target-grounding sensor* G revealing alpha/beta before action permits both correct rescues with unchanged X technical behavior. Alternatively a separately qualified new embodiment E adds one universally feasible rescue u from both states (operator load, rights and deadline must also hold). An additional possible X unsafe fault invalidates either hard guarantee.

The committed [JavaScript demonstrator](observation_closed_recovery.js) and [stored results](observation_closed_recovery_results.json) enumerate all seven nonempty first-step subsets of {done,alpha,beta}. Coarse observation admits five; certified fine observation or E's common old-compatible rescue admits all seven. The *fully supported* three-outcome X action fails coarse and passes fine/E. Executed 38 scoped V8 assertions; independent Python set enumeration reproduces 5/7/7 and full-support verdicts. These are synthetic test results, **not** physical safety signoff.

## Market value, endogenous data and non-identification

Under a fully specified fixed original-buyer purchase/menu, provider captured-payment and shared resource constraint, let V be the existing optimized accepted-production program. Consider a *stipulated, not empirically estimated* normalized net contribution table including costs, specialist outside options and old feasible fallback: V(R)=4; V(X only)=4 because X is not admitted; V(G only)=4; V(E only)=4; V(X+G)=6; V(X+E)=5. Then the conditional source-learning/target-grounding complement V(X+G)-V(X)-V(G)+V(R)=2. This is a *production option unlocked by sufficient information*, not a dataset's intrinsic market price and not a new data aggregation theorem. If original customer demand is absent, R is no longer feasible, G takes too long, or shared operators displace a more valuable order, the table and sign may change. An actual policy value comparison requires common original buyer order support, lawful menus, receipts and independently approved whole-network R/X/G/E/specialist arms. Even randomized arm means do not identify casewise old-policy successes or cross-policy rescue failure supports.

Distinguish X source-trained field/simulation/teleoperation/public inputs, G target-specific alignment and sensor evidence, E embodied interface/redundancy, and deliberate probes versus production-generated trajectories and failures/recoveries. Distinct acquisition costs, rights, data curation, sensor-error support, capacity and target transfer apply. Information from a policy excluded by the physical admission gate cannot be credited as *observed future free field data*. Learning's only future value is inside the existing Bellman continuation J; do not append a second data premium. Policy-set monotonicity for optional, costless data is separate from forced training and new commercial conditions.

**Negative statistical result.** If a rare unsafe state has stationary independent probability p>0, every finite n no-fault sample has probability (1-p)^n>0. Thus no number of such observed successes establishes p=0 or complete worst-case transition support. Under an *explicit iid assumption*, the one-sided 95% binomial upper p bound from zero failures is 1-0.05^(1/n); 299 samples give roughly 0.009969, below 1%, **not** an absolute safety guarantee, especially with distribution shift or selected trajectories. A risk-tolerant probabilistic admission policy is a different, separately authorized objective. This argument is established statistical mathematics, not publication novelty.
