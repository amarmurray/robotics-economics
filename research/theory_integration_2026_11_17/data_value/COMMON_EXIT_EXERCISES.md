# Student exercises — common unsupported continuation

**Do these using the conditional chapter, not any measured robot return. Separate instructor answers are in COMMON_EXIT_SOLUTIONS.md.**

1. Two policies have known terms K_a=27/20,K_b=7/5 and first-exit probabilities 1/2,1/4 into the **same** unknown complete-network residual u in [0,4]. Compute (i) the exact possible a–b contrast, (ii) the interval from subtracting individual bounds, (iii) the crossover value of u. Explain why one interval is narrower.
2. Generalize to finitely many exit strata. Derive the exact paired box lower/upper and the formula for inward endpoint improvement over separately taken bounds. Explain what makes sharpness fail with a persistent latent customer regime coupling u_1 and u_2.
3. Suppose p_a=p_b=1/4 and K_a-K_b=1/5. What would common-residual comparability imply? Give a different post-exit controller/customer-production law with the *same* observed pre-exit probabilities and K's but a negative contrast.
4. Distinguish a safe target diagnostic that refines u from target grounding that trains a new checkpoint. Which observed objects can change in each case? Why is neither an extra data premium added outside the production objective?
