# Multi-step safe support — separate solutions
All quantities are hypothetical net accepted-service units; no physical or dollar inference.

1. A positive-probability terminal event in finite discrete space contains at least one positive-probability path. Conversely a path of safely feasible positive conditional edges and fallback actions defines a policy with a positive terminal event. A mixture can obtain all its mass from one H; under another H the same edge may be impossible. Even positive probability epsilon does not satisfy an arbitrary customer's reliability requirement, and f can be flat after B is obtained.

2. Both B and C marginals equal (7+1)/16=1/2 in each policy, and all four outcomes have positive probability. Joint complete-bundle probabilities are 1/16 under R and 7/16 under G. Terminal gains are respectively 16/16=1 and 112/16=7; after G's displaced 4, net G=3, beating specialist=2, while R=1 does not. For additive f=8*1_B+8*1_C, each policy's expected terminal gain is 8, so net G=4 versus R=8. Measure held-out *joint* qualified and customer-accepted orders and the relevant optimized demand, not only component marginals.

3. After first failure the stage remains j=0; after success j=1, but second R from j1 has zero transition to j2. Thus R,R never reaches j2 by deadline. First R then G qualifies with probability (1/2)(3/4)=3/8, and first R then E qualifies with unconditional 3/5, assuming stated unchanged E kernel. These are **precommitted** route probabilities: the optimal policy conditions on first information and can select E only on failure.

4. First A output 1 is common. Given first success, compare R=1,S=2,G=.75Delta-.25,E=.6Delta-2; given failure only R,S,E are feasible. For Delta>=0, G-E=.15Delta+1.75>0; S dominates R. G>S iff Delta>3 and E>S iff Delta>20/3. Therefore V=1+.5max(2,.75Delta-.25)+.5max(2,.6Delta-2), equal to 3 for Delta<=3, 15/8+(3/8)Delta for 3<Delta<=20/3, and -1/8+(27/40)Delta for larger Delta. At Delta=7, V=1+.5(5)+.5(11/5)=23/5. Without G: 1+max(2,11/5)=16/5. Without E: 1+.5(5)+.5(2)=9/2. Specialist baseline equals 1+2=3.

5. Routine after success yields 1+rDelta versus G=.75Delta-.25, so R>=G iff r>=(3/4)-(5/4)/Delta for Delta>0. For r>=3/4, this holds at all Delta; the retained incumbent service alone dominates both at Delta=0. Source skill scores do not specify safe target visitation after q1, gate detection, training eligibility, or independent joint accepted target service; each conditional factor must be measured on the matched second visit.

6. Couple the induced distributions so total net accepted M_R>=M_D and jointly qualified Q_R contains Q_D with probability one; monotone terminal f then implies realized M_R+f(Q_R)>=M_D+f(Q_D), hence expectation and optimization. Equal support alone admits probabilities p and epsilon in the same full-support cell; one policy may still sacrifice larger current production. Without a coupling or a specific customer f, neither dominates all demand environments.
