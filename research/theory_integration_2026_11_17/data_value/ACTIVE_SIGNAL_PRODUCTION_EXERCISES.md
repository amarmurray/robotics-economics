# Student exercises — active learning, lawful report and paid production

Work in normalized complete original-buyer contribution units. No task benchmark percentage is a dollar value. Assume approved safe T only if predispatch qualification Q=1; R is always available *before* slot reservation.

1. **Conditional production.** Derive G=2+sum_y[4s_y-2q_y]_+ from a policy that sees Q,Y but not B,F. Identify the strict T dispatch criterion and explain why the missing R fallback matters after an irreversible slot reservation.
2. **Identification.** Given by report stratum m_y,q_y,b_y,f_y, derive sharp upper and lower bounds for s_y and G. State a realistic shared-network restriction under which separately taking the two extremal couplings would cease to be sharp.
3. **Two counterworlds.** With Z fair, P Q fair independently, report a fair independent coin, B=F=Z, r_P=+1/2; D Q=1,Y=Z,r_D=-1/4; compare D world I B=F=Z with world II B=Z,F=1-Z. Calculate G, J and compare E=12/5. Why does perfect Y not rank D economically?
4. **Information versus physical effects.** Evaluate I=G(K_D,L_D)-G(K_D,L_P)-G(K_P,L_D)+G(K_P,L_P) in both worlds. Explain why attributing a separate exogenous “data premium” would double count future production.
5. **Falsification.** Design a minimal matched original-buyer and rights/worker ledger to distinguish the two worlds without assuming that selected robot successes represent all eligible customer offers. Distinguish a finite-stage proof from validation of an adaptive shared-learner fleet.
