# Policy-support and physical-envelope proofs and rejected shortcuts
**19 November 2026 | Conditional finite-population support, standard weak duality.**

**P1 (support).** Write each closed catchment's target potential contribution Y_c(p), fixing its entire original-order and endogenously affected network history *under p*. Summing observed values and unsupported lower/upper bounds proves S1. Rectangular independence of the missing potential outcomes attains both extremes. Cross-catchment shared operator, model broadcasts or resource coupling invalidate sharpness and possibly even individual cluster counterfactual definitions. Under cross-policy restrictions, difference of individual intervals is valid but generally not sharp.

**P2 (resource price).** For every nonnegative feasible complete-recipe y and λ,ν≥0 with Aᵀλ+Bᵀν≥v, vᵀy≤λᵀAy+νᵀBy≤λᵀb+νᵀd. Maximization on the left proves S2. Uniform application needs a complete outer recipe universe under every X history, validated maximum customer-order demand, dispatch/recovery capacity, captured contribution and rights. Policy-induced demand growth, new G-qualified work or new E-body capacity can invalidate a reused upper bound. Gross future customer production already reflects source learning: do not add a second continuation-value term.

**P3 (two potential-outcome worlds).** Fix observed G/G=6, X/G=G/X=7 under one-slot gross cap7. Let unsupported X/X=0 in a forced, incompatible joint model world, or X/X=7 in a qualified world. Both give the exact same observed full supported policies. At cost K=1/2, net differences are -13/2 and +1/2. An *operative, rights-cleared, customer-law-preserving* rollback rules out X/X=0, raising gross lower to6, but a free data copy alone is not forced training. Mere backup controller weights cannot guarantee no downstream buyer/queue displacement.

**P4 (domination despite missing learner law).** With all-X gross≤7 from a verified single operator and single order cap, G/G feasible at6, and incremental all-X cost K, difference≤1-K. Thus K>1 strictly rejects X. This conclusion needs no positive/negative source-transfer identification. It is void if the order cap, margin cap or resource constraint is false. A physically approved E expansion changes b and can escape this conclusion.

**P5 (known finite register).** One observed full-X gross 13/2 and two independent unknown all-X catchments each in [6,7] imply gross [37/6,41/6]. Subtract G=6 and K=1/2 to get [-1/3,+1/3]. At K>5/6 the upper is negative. One additional fully observed catchment reduces width by (7-6)/3=1/3. This is not a statistical confidence interval, nor an estimate of EVSI.

**P6 (rejected statements).** Reject local mixed-site effects as the all-X estimand under joint interference; zero marginal robot/operator TV as proof of zero joint change; accepted-order denominators as original demand; a dual using only *currently* qualified recipes as a safe future X/E cap; a positive benchmark as positive net service; and shrinking an uncertainty interval as necessarily profitable paid target data. The method is an application of classical partial ID, coupling caution and LP dual certificates, not established scientific novelty.
