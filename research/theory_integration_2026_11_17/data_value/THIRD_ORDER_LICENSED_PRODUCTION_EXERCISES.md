# Student exercises — licensed third-order production

All numbers are synthetic normalized complete paid-production units. Solutions in a separate file.

1. With binary U,V,L having marginal 1/2 and all pairwise joint-1 probabilities 1/4, write all eight joint masses in terms of t=P(111). Prove the sharp interval and give two pairwise-indistinguishable counterworlds.
2. Derive the expectation of an arbitrary signed full-horizon policy payoff f(U,V,L), calculate its third finite difference, and give a necessary and sufficient condition for pairwise identification in this family.
3. Using J_R=33/32, J_G=17/16, J_S=1+max(1/32,t/4), J_SG=1+max(1/32,t/2), and J_E=35/32, derive both SG/E endpoints and its indifference value. Show free optional-data monotonicity even if a forced new recipe fails.
4. For a common law-independent policy set with invariant conditional continuation, why is optimized J(t) convex? Why is this not a claim of monotonically increasing economic returns to more data?
5. Specify the minimum additional *linked* original-buyer evidence needed beyond all pairwise U,V,L tables, and describe what invalidates a rights-only projection in a dynamic learner/operator/customer network.
