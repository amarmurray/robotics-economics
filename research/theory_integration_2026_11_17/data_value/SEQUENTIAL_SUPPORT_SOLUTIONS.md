**1.** P(C1)=1/2(3/4)+1/2(1/4)=1/2. Bayes gives P(H|C1)= (3/8)/(1/2)=3/4. In positive-alignment world, joint probability P(C1,B qualified)=1/2(3/4)(3/4)+1/2(1/4)(1/4)=5/16. In negative-alignment world it is 1/2(3/4)(1/4)+1/2(1/4)(3/4)=3/16. The independent product is 1/2 times 1/2 =1/4. Correlation of first correction with second B validation changes the joint event even though the marginal numbers are the same.

**2.** Given C1, the chance of B qualification is 5/8 or 3/8. B net is 8(5/8)-3=2 in positive world; 8(3/8)-3=0 in negative world. The specialist yields 1 in either. Without C1 only specialist is available. Probe value relative to constant old A base is -(1/4)+(1/2)(2)+(1/2)(1)=5/4 in positive world; in negative it is -(1/4)+(1/2)(1)+(1/2)(1)=3/4. Routine without a relevant correction yields 1. Choose probe only in positive world. This uses *one* second slot for B or specialist, not both, and includes first foregone accepted service in the probe cost.

**3.** The first sample likelihood is determined only by pi and the common first exposure/gate/correction kernels, identical across worlds. Any sequence of first samples therefore has the same joint law. For a stronger result, choose two target-state validation kernels that differ only at B while every logged policy assigns probability zero to B; their observable likelihoods coincide for any sample size but a new B-reaching controller would generate different qualification and customer-production value. Approved randomized B coverage with independent gate audit and held-out complete-service validation is one discriminator. A declared structural smoothness or transport assumption could narrow bounds, but is not supported merely by an abundance of source data.

**4.** The old correction signal may be informative while the new binary report is constant, yet new physical production possibilities can dominate. If the prior feasible action and data options remain free and legal, the optimizer can retain the old plan, so the enlarged menu cannot lower maximum value. Forced model replacement, lost rights or retraining costs invalidate feasible-set inclusion. Do not equate negative transfer under a forced recipe with free optional data causing a value decline.

**5.** Delta=0 makes B net = -3, so choose the +1 specialist in this single terminal window. Technical qualifications still matter if later different demand is possible; put that possibility in continuation f, not an added premium. If B service additionally requires C, f(A+B)-f(A) may remain zero. The economic event is the **joint** held-out qualification and accepted recipe {B,C} (possibly with resource and site acceptance), and its support-conditioned probability; multiplying independent marginal benchmarks is not justified.
