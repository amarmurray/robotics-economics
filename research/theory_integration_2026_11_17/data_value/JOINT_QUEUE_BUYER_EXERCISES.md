# Exercises: data acquisition in a shared remediation queue

Student problems; answers are in a [separate instructor file](JOINT_QUEUE_BUYER_SOLUTIONS.md).

**1.** There are two worker slots. G and K independently generate one mandatory validation repair job V and Z when active. A genuine valid original ticket A requires one slot and an accepted complete service recipe. With a=Pr(V) and b=Pr(Z), derive sharp partial-identification bounds on Pr(A paid under joint GK). Construct endpoints with coherent joint potential jobs.

**2.** A accepted at t3 earns m, then generates a distinct authorized B order at t4 with probability γ; paid B generates n discounted by β. Derive joint-program value and its derivative with respect to z=Pr(VZ). Explain why adding an independent speculative data premium on top of continuation would double count.

**3.** With a=b=1/2, G-only worth 3 and GK worth 5 when both repairs do not collide, suppose V is lawfully observed before K can be activated. Derive a *fixed robust* nonanticipative policy independent of z and the value of an optimized policy if z were known. Distinguish the information requirements.

**4.** E has genuinely independent capacity and normalized value 17/4. Compare its ranking with full GK across the sharp coupling interval. Under independent comparable customer clusters and only that ambiguity set, calculate the minimax-regret GK fraction. Why is it not a field investment recommendation?

**5.** Analyze the same witness with three available worker slots, or with B demands independent of previous A service. What causal assumptions, field measurements and data rights would be needed if the joint GK regime itself changes repair generation?
