# Student exercises: buyer response and economically useful grounding

1. Under a single t2 service slot with b=2,c=3, Q_R=0 and certified Q_G=1, derive V_G in terms of p_D,p_C and x=Pr(D=C=1). Explain why equal C technical qualification and marginal order rates do not imply equal paid value.
2. Construct the eight-atom common law for D,C_R,C_G with D and C_R independent fair binary and Pr(C_G=1|D=1)=2x, Pr(C_G=1|D=0)=1-2x. Prove it leaves the full R paired original-buyer distribution unchanged for all x in [0,1/2].
3. Suppose x_R=1/4 and a defended |x_G-x_R|<=3/20, with equal G buyer marginals. Give sharp G value bounds, compare with E=21/10 and P=2, and derive the ex-ante minimax-regret G lottery. State why the bound is not an estimate from R invoices.
4. Show the exact decomposition of G's total gain into a baseline-opportunity qualification effect and an action-induced buyer-demand effect. Which condition permits baseline R original orders to evaluate G_skip without a G customer deployment? Which observations and permissions are still required?
5. A policy-randomized trial records all settled revenues and costs but not every refused buyer offer. Explain which total program contrast it can identify and which mechanistic counterfactual transport it cannot.
