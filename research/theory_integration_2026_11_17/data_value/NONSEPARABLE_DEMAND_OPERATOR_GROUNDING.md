# Nonseparable customer demand and operator-saving grounding
**19 November 2026 | Continuation of *When Capabilities Become Production*. Conditional mathematical composition; not calibrated robotics evidence or a novelty claim.**

## Same question, stronger customer-market structure

A qualified technical improvement from heterogeneous X source training, target-specific G grounding, or E new embodied capacity acquires economic value only through complete customer service, endogenous original orders, competing specialist alternatives, feasible shared operator/controller resources and actually capturable provider contribution. The prior [demand-aware certificates](ENDOGENOUS_DEMAND_RESOURCE_CERTIFICATES.md) used a single uniform-price output, a demand-intercept interpretation of G and a physical service ceiling. Here **two separately qualified service classes face nonseparable substitute/complementary buyer demand**, while G instead lowers recovery/operator time. This changes both the ranking of X/G/E and the *sign* of physical investment interactions without introducing a second data premium.

Let q=(q_A,q_B) be counts of fully qualified customer service of classes A,B from the **same predeclared market and horizon**, not benchmark success fractions. A symmetric illustrative inverse captured-price schedule is

P_A(q)=a-2q_A-γq_B,  P_B(q)=b-2q_B-γq_A.

Total provider receipts (or variable-cost-adjusted captured contribution if coefficients incorporate actual costs) are

F_γ(q)=a q_A+b q_B-2q_A²-2q_B²-2γq_Aq_B.

γ>0 represents substitution/cannibalization across the two outputs; γ<0 a complementary buyer response. For |γ|<2 the Hessian -4[[1,γ/2],[γ/2,1]] is negative definite. A **different** calibrated demand model need not be quadratic, symmetric, or uniform-price. Do not interpret an inverse-demand schedule as buyer surplus, and do not add a buyer-surplus integral to the provider revenue objective.

Qualified feasibility requires 0≤q_A≤c_A, 0≤q_B≤1, u_Aq_A+q_B≤O. O is a joint *approved embodied-throughput and recovery-operator equivalent* resource, not automatically a count of human staff or robots. R retains c_A=1/2, u_A=1, O=1. One engineering qualification slot permits **one** first-stage alternative: X learning raises qualified A capacity c_A to1, G grounding lowers A operator burden u_A to1/2 while retaining its qualification cap, or E adds a certified body-plus-recovery package increasing O to3/2. All other resources remain fixed *only in the synthetic exercise*. Incremental costs are X=1/4, G=11/10, E=11/5 normalized contribution units; combinations are outside the one-slot choice set, but G+E is also evaluated as a mathematical interaction. Free optional source data that preserves R cannot reduce the optimized gross policy value. Paid forced conversion can.

## Proposition — identically observed service, distinct optimal investments

For γ∈[-1,1], set a=9+γ/2 and b=7+γ/2. Every γ-world yields **the identical retained original-market observation** q_A=q_B=1/2 and customer prices P_A=8, P_B=6, so captured incumbent receipts are7. The off-support cross-demand effect γ is not identified by that observed mix, including when both service counts and their receipts are recorded.

Optimizing the qualified constrained service portfolio gives, exactly,

- R: q=(1/2,1/2), V_R=7.
- X: q=((3−γ)/(4−2γ), (1−γ)/(4−2γ)), V_X=5+γ/2+(3−γ)²/(4−2γ).
- G: q=(1/2,3/4), V_G=(65−γ)/8.
- E: q=(1/2,1), V_E=9−γ/4.

All are **gross optimized complete-service provider receipts** before the stated alternative-specific cost. The analytic optimum is valid over the whole indicated interval and exact-arithmetic polygon-edge enumeration checks 81 rational γ points; it is not a universal source-to-qualification law.

| Feasible first-stage choice | Net if γ=−1 (complementary market) | Net if γ=+1 (substitutable market) |
|---|---:|---:|
| R retained | 7 | 7 |
| X source learning | 83/12 | 29/4 |
| G operator-saving grounding | **143/20** | 69/10 |
| E embodied/recovery capacity | 141/20 | 131/20 |

Thus **G is uniquely preferred at γ=−1, but X at γ=1**, despite matched baseline prices, original customer service and technical qualification. Under the particular bounded family, the exact choice crossover solves

V_X−1/4=V_G−11/10,
γ*=(21−√401)/10 ≈0.09750.

G wins for γ<γ*, X for γ>γ*. E and retained R do not exceed the winner anywhere on [-1,1] under these assumed costs/capacities. This is an *assumption-specific indifference threshold*, not a robotics benchmark or price of a training trajectory. There is no finite robust recommendation if the cross-demand and policy support assumptions are unconstrained.

## Proposition — resource-saving data can be valuable without new qualification

For an approved convex feasible production set with a unique optimum, strong regularity and differentiability, let λ be the shadow value of the operator-equivalent constraint. If G only saves δ units of A operator burden, u_A→u_A−δ, holding the demand law, qualification and rights fixed, the envelope identities are

∂V/∂δ = λ q_A*,  ∂V/∂O = λ.

Grounding's value can therefore arise entirely from freeing **scarce deployment supervision** for another qualified service, even when no new A task becomes feasible. A source training intervention that only raises the technical eligibility cap c_A instead has gross marginal value given by that cap's shadow price, which may be zero if the capacity is redundant. Neither is a primitive "data price"; both are endogenous system resource valuations. With binding caps, nonunique multipliers or discrete trials, use directional derivatives/subgradients or exact finite differences rather than these smooth formulas.

## Proposition — G and E are not invariably complements

In the two-service example, combining G with E does not raise optimized gross receipts above E alone: with q_A capped at1/2 and q_B capped at1, extra operator release is redundant. Hence for either γ-world

V(G+E)−V(G)−V(E)+V(R)=V(R)−V(G)<0.

The relevant G/E economic interaction is **negative** even though G is technically helpful and both individually relax the same bottleneck.

But suppose A and B are *joint required steps of each single accepted customer order*, so q_A=q_B=z, unit contribution p, shared operator use (u_A+u_B)z, capped original demand z≤D. Then
V(O,δ)=p min{D,O/(u_A+u_B−δ)}.
If none of the four R/G/E/G+E allocations is demand-saturated,

Δ_GΔ_E V = p ΔO[1/(u_A+u_B−δ)−1/(u_A+u_B)]>0.

An exact demonstration with p=8, D=1, baseline O=1 and total operator requirement2, G saving δ=1/2 and E adding ΔO=1/2 gives R=4, G=16/3, E=6 and G+E=8, **positive** interaction2/3. A/B components then must both be *jointly qualified per original order*; separately reported success rates cannot justify the coupled output. Saturation, customer substitution, rights, a different E body, or policy-induced demand can reverse this sign.

This is the precise boundary between a model where G relieves the same scarce resource as E (substitution) and a model where E increases the effective market scale over which per-bundle G savings operate (complementarity). It is not a general theorem that grounding and embodiments are complements.

## Identified-state choice versus ex-ante robust decision

With only γ∈[-1,1] and **one indivisible engineering qualification slot**, the pure-policy worst-case net values are R=7, X=83/12, G=69/10 and E=131/20. Thus **retaining R is the unique pure maximin decision**, even though the ex-post best qualified alternative is G or X in every world. This is a negative result: ignorance of cross-customer demand can erase the defensible gross economic value of source training or operator-saving grounding.

There is an information-structure qualification. If a decision maker is risk neutral, can randomize *in advance* between fully approved X and G, and both policies face the **same fixed latent γ** regardless of the realized intervention, the 50–50 lottery has expected net
L_{1/2}(γ)=[J_X(γ)+J_G(γ)]/2=561/80+γ²/[16(2−γ)]≥561/80=7+1/80.
More sharply, the optimal risk-neutral maximin lottery over this menu assigns X probability p*=(401−√401)/802≈0.47503 and G otherwise. Its guaranteed *expected* net value is (541+√401)/80≈7.01281, with the worst case at the policy-indifference γ*. Strict convexity of J_X, linear J_G and tangency prove the saddle; no numerical grid is needed. **Neither lottery supplies a pathwise safe revenue floor.** It is not valid when shared learning/operator/customer spillovers make γ depend on the realized intervention, when a single action irreversibly destroys the incumbent, or when randomizing G/X has unapproved safety or setup costs. Those possibilities return the model to the earlier complete-network support/retained-policy framework.

## Identification and the next actual research dependency

The observed retained price/service pair pins a and b *conditional on γ* but not γ itself. A valid full-market off-support joint price/quantity variation with fixed composition could identify γ, e.g. ∂P_A/∂q_B=−γ; actual prices are endogenous, so merely regressing receipts on accepted orders is invalid. Unseen refused quotes, customers choosing specialists, induced orders and shared-market spillovers must be included. Training X, grounding G and E also change support/qualification; if their price laws differ from the retained one, **baseline cross-elasticity alone is insufficient** to evaluate policies. Collect original eligible customer opportunities, offer/quote/decline events, simultaneous order substitution, operator minutes, paired accepted-service qualifications, actual payments and versioned controller/data rights under prospective approved controls. No such measurements or physical approval exists here.

This remains a finite conditional countermodel for the preceding demand-aware resource certificate: the buyer cross-effect and the shadow price of physical recovery are complementary *objects of measurement*, not reducible to technical benchmark improvement. It preserves the existing heterogeneous field/failure/recovery/teleop/sim/synthetic/public source distinctions, data rights, future production counted once and the R retained fallback. A broader empirically identifiable cross-price/multiperiod customer network, with interference, remains unproved.
