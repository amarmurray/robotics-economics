# Original-buyer policy-response and data-value measurement protocol
**19 Nov 2026. Prospective design only; no customer contact, approval, purchases, physical experiments, or private data.**

**Question.** Do baseline original-buyer cross-class quotes/refusals and engineering source-transfer measurements support any economically meaningful restriction on the *policy-specific* customer demand and qualified operator network for X training, G target grounding or E qualified embodiment? A measured high benchmark-transfer rate is not a buyer demand transport restriction.

**Unit/denominator.** Freeze original eligible buyer opportunities by independent catchment and horizon *before* any physically approved intervention. For each opportunity record paired A/B price offers, declines and nonpurchase, competing specialist/outside options, project cancellation, feasible whole-service qualified recipes, recovered/failed attempts, operator/recovery work, customer acceptance, collected payments, penalties, service/version lineage and rights. Keep zero offers/orders, unattempted qualified work and cross-market migration. Avoid accepted-only denominator selection.

**Distinct evidence needed.** (1) Under R: credible exogenous/supply-instrumented A/B offer variation, not observed accepted-price regression, to estimate the stable cross-demand gamma_R or a partial interval. (2) Under approved X/G/E in the same eligible market: post-policy service quality/price intercept, cross-demand gamma_p, original-buyer composition changes and observed no-purchase/specialist substitution. (3) Under matched qualified interventions: validate c_A, A recovery/operator savings, E physical capacity, shared dispatcher constraints, same-horizon captured contribution and competent retained policy. (4) Measure policy/source identity and rights for distinct field, recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained input. Compute/curation cost and productive time foregone enter J only once.

**Transport certificate evidence.** A user-specified bound |gamma_p-gamma_R|<=delta must be justified from independent approved policy comparisons or credible structural constraints *with uncertainty*, not declared from closeness of benchmark percentages or an incumbent-only confidence interval. Confirm whether the matched policy price-anchor a_p=9+gamma_p/2,b_p=7+gamma_p/2 is tenable. If not, record policy-specific intercept/quality intervals and redo the production maximization; the chapter's narrow fraction thresholds must not be reused. A paired original-buyer market design with isolated learner/operator/customer exposure is a requirement, not existing authorization.

**Measurement of paid audit A.** Pre-specify report content, statistical precision epsilon, full rights/compute/curation price, delays/foregone service, effect on buyer demand and information available before irreversible X/G/E choice. A realized beneficial r does not establish ex ante positive value; obtain credible report probabilities to compute the integrated downstream J expectation. An unapproved physical X/G/E deployment is excluded from feasible action set. If only retrospective R data exist, maintain an inconclusive X/G/E ranking and R only when independently qualified/available.

**Fallback evidence routes.** If post-policy quote support is absent, compare *entire* approved network policies under true market isolation and full eligible-order outcomes, or certify a conservative captured-receipts envelope with verified physical resource capacity. Neither a public vendor capability announcement nor a new success-percentage dashboard fills the missing numerical BLS–BEA official opportunity atlas. Public research can guide hypotheses only, not stand in for commercial payment observations.
