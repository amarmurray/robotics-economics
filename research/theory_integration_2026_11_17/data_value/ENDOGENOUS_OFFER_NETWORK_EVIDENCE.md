# Prospective original-buyer eligible offers, operator capacity and learning lineage

**22 Nov 2026; not actual customer data and not permission to run experiments.** Companion to [the same Models A/B research result](ENDOGENOUS_OFFER_NETWORK_AND_CAPACITY.md).

**Connected program/exposure unit:** map all controller/model-update lineage and broadcast, robot/embodiment slots, human rescue/operator schedules, dispatch scheduler, original customer demand and contract/rights connections. The comparison P productive work/data, D deliberate target collection/grounding, E new independent body/operator slot, and R old retained policy needs lawful physical safety and contractual approvals. Without independent complete-network clusters, robot-level randomized episodes are not a valid stand-in for full-market effects.

**Record before any dispatch or selection:** all incoming eligible original orders (including requests later refused, canceled, delayed or not served) with customer/site/task/window, competing original-buyer order offers and prices, customer decision/acceptance where legally available, timestamp; exact robot/learner checkpoint and qualified A/B recipes, signature and held-out acceptance criteria; safety/rescue fallback; licensed information/observation rights and which signals arrived early enough to dispatch; availability of the prior R controller; all actual shared operator, learner and new-body capacity constraints, including proof that an E second slot does not use the same scarce recovery resource.

**Record after:** attempted and accepted services, safety stops, failures/recoveries and interventions, actual cleared settlement plus refund and direct expenditure; rejected/unused buyer orders and reasons; learner version/source and rights lineage, permitted retention of customer traces, next-period target qualification and new-service offers. Differentiate routine production trajectories, failures, teleoperation, simulation and synthetic/public input, curation/training compute and target grounding costs; do not treat either failure data or human data as universally valuable.

**Causal/production estimands:** primary total discounted cleared original-buyer whole-network value J_P,J_D,J_E including productively earned pilot receipt and all true costs. Conditional diagnostic m_A,m_B,j under each arm (Z means offered AND actually qualified AND lawfully usable, not selected served); technical validation/field target lift, shared operator utilization, order substitution, and dispatch loss when both buyers qualify and demand simultaneously. Customer price and rights can themselves respond to the intervention, so use the complete policy-specific joint law K, not a task success percentage.

**Validation gate and evidence ladder:** verify timestamps and original-buyer coverage first; independently verify service receipts and signed qualification/rights; map all learner/operator/buyer joint edges; secure physically approved comparable whole-network exposure/assignment or defensible conditional causal assumptions with explicit sensitivity; then estimate J, correlation and lost orders. Do not label observational order differences as causal without assignment or a valid structural transport mechanism. Future customer actions and paid data rights are not inferred from selected routine logs.

**Known failures:** unserved B offers invisible to robot logs, model version broadcasts between nominal arms, single recovery worker for multiple E robot bodies, source-induced customer order changes, data rights excluded or too late, forced training overriding old R, shifting prices/conditions or a real new physical embodiment affecting previously assumed service feasibility. Missing/unapproved arms are not "zero outcome." No such signed comparative experiment, original-buyer receipts, joint K or numerical official BEA/BLS interindustry production coefficient was obtained.

**Public prior-art scope checked 22 Nov 2026:** Talluri/van Ryzin 2004 capacity and customer-response book overview (Springer); Aronow/Samii 2017 published interference abstract (Yale); Hernán/Robins 2024 text excerpt on selection. No entire source replication or independent scientific review.
