# From heterogeneous evidence to qualified paid production: supported kernels and decision-relevant audits

## Intellectual question

The preceding result proved that a compressed history is sufficient for a known finite decision if every compressed cell retains a common optimal action, and provided a stronger controlled-state condition for dynamic sufficiency. That condition cannot be *estimated* merely by reading past invoices: source-specific service or recovery choices can generate different evidence, training eligibility, joint A/C embodiment qualification, next-period original customer offers and shared-worker occupancy. Under G, a licensed grounding program, some legally possible actions have not yet been performed. Which economically sufficient kernel can actually be learned, which comparison can be certified before full identification, and what lawful intervention would close the gap?

The result is an operational **support-aware transition certificate**. For a specified finite P/G/E/R program menu, one can project each supported action's joint outcome law onto the payoffs of *qualified accepted production*. Where action support is missing, an explicit set of compatible kernels produces an interval, not a point estimate. If the resulting action-value intervals separate, the optimal operating action is certified without learning the full joint law. When they overlap, the prospective audit must identify the joint *production event* or a sufficient expected reward, with the right original-offer and worker risk-set denominator. Retaining single technical success marginals or data row counts is not generally enough. This combines standard linear identification, Bellman optimization, and missing-support causal logic; none is claimed as new general mathematics.

## 1. Environment and causal chronology

At t0, choose an investment program z in {R,P,G,E}. R retains the safe old policy and may be selected again whenever technically and legally feasible; P buys additional source evidence; G funds a licensed target-grounding path using some existing records; E provides a physically verified independent embodiment and service capacity. Each program's incremental capital, data-rights, evaluation and operational costs enter once. Alternative ownership and contractual structures are permissible whenever they supply the same timely rights and resources.

Before t3, a legally available signature h includes original offers (including zero and refused), remaining buyer budgets, accepted earlier production/quality, model/embodiment versions, qualification and rights, distinct field successes, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained records, timestamps, worker jobs, and queues. Origins and licenses are not interchangeable. Let a be a feasible pre-outcome action, including S (serve a qualified original order) or M (produce or validate target-relevant recovery evidence), with one exclusive worker/validation capacity accounted for. An M action is not feasible simply because an R or G/S log exists: it requires actual permission, qualified safe implementation, and the appropriate intervention resources.

After a, a **joint** outcome ω includes newly generated signed data; model update/release; arm A and mobile C qualification including the joint kit recipe; original next-period buyer offers, budgets and quality/acceptance; worker recovery and availability; and report inclusion. The order of operations matters. The firm sees only admitted signals at their actual times and reoptimizes a later dispatch on qualified recipes. Write U_z(h,a,ω) for current accepted contribution plus discounted optimal later accepted contribution, net of real resources used once. U is not a technical score converted mechanically into money. A failed qualification or blocked worker may force the safe recipe, a different order, or no service. For fixed z,h,a define

Q_z(h,a)=Σ_ω k_{z,h,a}(ω)U_z(h,a,ω),

where the conditional intervention kernel k depends on **z and a**, not on a transplanted R or G/S history. If z changes the pre-t3 distribution, that program's own history law μ_z(h) must also be supported or bounded. Current work's causal decision analysis is conditional on an independently identified μ_G and recorded feasibility; it does not equate μ_P, μ_G, μ_E and μ_R.

## 2. Partial identification of the economic transition

For a fixed program, history and action, let q be the vector of probabilities over finitely many physically possible ω. Suppose independently defensible measurements, structural impossibilities and lawful randomized trials impose linear restrictions:

K_{z,h,a}={q:q≥0, 1ᵀq=1, A_{z,h,a}q=b_{z,h,a}, q(ω)=0 for certified impossible ω}.

The rows of A may represent *joint* target qualification, same-episode original buyer and worker coincidences, a known sampling distribution corrected for independent reporting, or separately known marginals. They may not silently transport R action responses into G/M. If no lawful G/M action has been observed, its causal law remains unconstrained by G/S observations unless a separately defended invariant mechanism supplies a valid restriction.

For any specific action, linear optimization gives its sharp conditional value interval:

L_a=min_{q∈K_a} q·U_a,     U_a^+=max_{q∈K_a} q·U_a.

Both extrema exist for a nonempty finite compact polytope. Every feasible q is one lawful conditional counterworld under the stipulated constraints, so the bounds are sharp **for that specified set**, not universal. A fixed admissible policy using action a therefore needs only the payoff projection q·U_a, not every underlying transition probability. In the relative interior, q·U_a is identified if and only if U_a annihilates the linear null directions of the supported restrictions (equivalently, on the allowed support, it lies in the row space of A augmented by normalization and certified zero constraints). If no relative interior exists, use the direction space of the feasible affine hull instead.

**Action certificate.** If L_a>U_b^+ for every competing feasible b, a is optimal for every compatible kernel. If U_a^+<L_b for some b, a can be discarded under the stated independent action-row uncertainty. If all candidate intervals overlap, their endpoints alone may be insufficient to rank them: solve the *common* feasible-law program rather than comparing values achieved by incompatible laws. If the unknown rows are independent conditional on each h and a (action-and-history rectangularity), the optimal value before observing ω has exact bounds:

Σ_h μ_z(h) max_a L_{h,a} ≤ V_z^* ≤ Σ_h μ_z(h) max_a U_{h,a}^+,

and both endpoints are attainable by selecting each row's extremal law independently. Proof: for every h, max_a Σ_ω q_a(ω)U_a(ω) is bounded by these maxima. To attain the lower endpoint, choose a minimizer for **each** action row simultaneously, then the maximum equals max_a L_a; the upper endpoint selects a maximizing row of an upper-best action. History cells can be combined because μ_z is fixed and the rows are rectangular. Without that assumption, these formulas remain valid outer bounds but can fail to be sharp, because worker, original buyer, upstream learning or a common latent response mechanism couples histories/actions. Multi-period nonrectangular settings require a common occupation-measure or joint-kernel feasible set and an explicit dynamic program. A post-treatment risk set must not be held artificially constant across programs.

**Optional old action.** If G only expands a free feasible menu, has zero new training/implementation costs, and leaves the old policy/corpus feasible, its optimized value weakly increases. Costly G acquisition or forced mixing is a different treatment. Negative transfer of a particular update is not evidence against the optional-data result.

## 3. A two-embodiment synthetic witness with support, not just a new parameter draw

Suppose the source-provenance signature is F (a field trace) or T (a qualified recovery/teleoperation trace), each occurring with probability 1/2 **under G's own stipulated predecision law**. Every history has a known safe G/S policy worth 21/10 normalized units in *total current plus future net contribution*. G/M is legally feasible only in T because that stratum has an admissible target-specific recovery record; its future causal response has not been observed. F must use S. The alternative supported program values, under their own cost and independent-capacity assumptions, are R=3/2, P=2, E=12/5. E's own worker and embodiment are genuinely separate; its value is not added as if it simultaneously used G's occupied worker.

Within T, action M uses a paid and independently completed qualification process on an arm and a mobile controller, possibly enabling a **joint kit recipe** by the next deadline. Let binary QA, QC record both target qualifications, D denote a new original kit-buyer offer with budget for one unit, and W denote an actually free shared worker. A different pre-existing buyer has one guaranteed original fallback job that pays 1 when W=1. The *same* worker slot can serve the qualified kit instead of the fallback, not both; accepted kit contribution is 6 if QA·QC·D·W=1, and otherwise the fallback earns 1 when W=1. The two embodiment stages are sequential within the one booked service window, not simultaneous uses of one exclusive worker. M's real incremental training, qualification and acquisition cost is 3/5 and is deducted exactly once. Thus its actual expected service contribution is

V_M=Pr(W=1)+5Pr(QA=QC=D=W=1)−3/5.

Even granting strong hypothetical **G/M-specific** singleton measurements Pr(QA)=Pr(QC)=3/4 and Pr(D)=Pr(W)=4/5, we have not learned the economically crucial conjunction p=Pr(QA QC D W=1). The sharp four-event Fréchet interval is

max(0,3/4+3/4+4/5+4/5−3)=1/10 ≤ p ≤ min(3/4,3/4,4/5,4/5)=3/4.

So V_M=1/5+5p lies in [7/10,79/20]. Neither extreme assumes independent qualification. Both are attained by the *20-atom physical laws* in the executable model: the lower world makes four failure sets disjoint with sizes 5,5,4,4, leaving two atoms with all events true; the upper world nests all four success events so 15 of 20 atoms qualify and can be served. Every event in these two worlds is determined before dispatch by exogenous factors and only observed in its lawful chronology; no policy anticipates the realization. Both worlds share all four singleton marginals and the same supported G/S histories. Technical qualification and economic conversion are distinct.

Given T's observed rights and provenance, the optimized G investment is

V_G(p)=(21/10 + max{21/10,1/5+5p})/2.

The G/T dispatcher chooses M exactly when p>19/50 (=0.38), otherwise its safe S fallback. This gives V_G=21/10 in the low law and 121/40 (=3.025) in the high law. Independent E yields 12/5 (=2.4), so the *program* ranking changes precisely when p>1/2. These are consequences of the joint qualification, original buyer/worker timing and one scarce service slot, not changes in an invented benchmark-to-dollar factor. The outcomes are synthetic and do not estimate real pass rates, order volumes, prices, or operator minutes.

**What is actually minimally informative?** For this fixed M value and specified fallback, the two scalars Pr(W) and p are sufficient to price M; the full sixteen-cell law is unnecessary. For just this fixed payoff vector, even their weighted sum suffices. If fallback prices and kit prices vary independently across legitimate end-market demand, both moments must generally be retained; if the customer has no remaining kit budget, p drops out and an expensive qualification audit is not worth buying for this one terminal decision. Separate qualification rates are not sufficient because the joint accepted-service event matters. A proven capability link may have positive technical transfer yet zero economic transfer if the buyer does not order, worker cannot serve, the due date has elapsed or a better opportunity occupies the shared slot.

**Do not confuse hypothetically known M marginals with historical support.** The singleton restrictions above are an *illustrative stronger information set*. They could only be used operationally after legally supportable program-specific evidence or independently justified structural restrictions. G/S records do not supply them. Without those restrictions, the G/M interval is wider, although its safe old action remains available.

## 4. Prospective design and a lawfully estimated statistic

The observation/assignment unit should be closed under shared model versions, buyer order responses, worker queues and data sharing: a buyer–worker–model-sharing cluster, not an individual successful invoice. Register G rights, eligible T provenance, actual menus, safe capacity, original buyer opportunities and budget *before* randomization. Prospectively and with permission assign some G,T clusters to M and some to S with documented propensity e(h)>0. After t4, randomly sample original buyer offer/no-offer, worker availability, and validated **joint** qualification with an audit inclusion propensity η(h,a,preaudit)>0 that does not depend on hidden original orders or recovery outcomes conditional on the named preaudit variables. Keep a frozen, matched model/hardware/compute baseline and verify the target joint kit and prior fallback on independent admissible evaluation resources; a qualification audit that consumes the same terminal service slot must be accounted for as an intervention, not a free report.

For an eligible T sample of size n_T, let A_i be assignment, O_i be the audited-event indicator, and Y_i=1{QA=QC=D=W=1} observed only where admissible. Under consistency, sequential randomized exchangeability, positivity, independent audit sampling and no untracked interference across clusters, the inverse-probability estimator

p_hat=(1/n_T) Σ_{i:T_i=T} 1{A_i=M,O_i=1}Y_i/[e_i η_i]

has expectation p_T. This identifies an intervention-specific **joint economic moment**, not the outcomes of untried methods or another program. S must remain available for safety and commercial fallback. Track nonpayment, refusals, zero-order clusters, worker cases unresolved at censoring, rights limitations, incremental validation cost, and independent E staffing; invoices alone are selected reports.

If e=η=1/2 in the synthetic homogeneous T stratum, the contribution of one T cluster to p_hat is 4·1{M,audited,joint success}. Its variance is 4p−p². The estimator is unbiased but may be imprecise, especially for rare joint success. An information-only provenance-display experiment can test the separate effect of whether the allocator *recognizes* T; it must not be mislabeled a qualification intervention. A source-generation or grounding intervention requires its own randomized action and outcome accounting.

The actual economically informative comparison is accepted paid kit service and displacement of the fallback, plus verified target qualification and downstream original offers. The decision objective can be selected *before* seeking every high-dimensional data statistic: audit only when the admissible ambiguity crosses an action boundary and the value of information exceeds acquisition and implementation costs. If a lawful G/M arm is unavailable, state that there is zero causal support, use the feasible supported policy and report decision bounds rather than estimating G/M from R.

## 5. Mathematical limitations, source relationship and next test

The sharp interval assumes four marginals for the same G/M risk set and a four-event binary terminal system. Real embodiments have correlated multistage errors, shifting customers and multiple worker classes; their joint distribution may be restricted by actual engineering compatibility or exclusion. Common capacity and budget constraints must be represented as certified structural zeros in the feasible set, not assumed after seeing data. When training outcomes feed new demand or another next-date source, the correct payoff U_a includes the next optimized continuation and its observation policy; this chapter isolates one step within that broader Bellman model.
