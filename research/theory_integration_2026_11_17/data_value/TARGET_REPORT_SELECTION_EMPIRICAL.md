# Original-order selection ledger: prospective nonactuating observational design

**Status 19 November 2026: prospective protocol only.** No independently approved reporter, whole customer-order panel, robot/controller physical trial or customer permission observed. This design authorizes none.

## Design and inclusion hierarchy

1. Define each original customer order **before** robot eligibility, acceptance, dispatch or reporter output. Include refused, expired, displaced and never-dispatched service; record versioned customer-order ID, site, original timestamp, end market, customer counterfactual, required complete-service acceptance and verified state z. The pre-action source of z must not depend on acceptance or a selected report.
2. For the *frozen, physically approved* controller/embodiment, version reporter and its no-actuation boundary, code hash, safety fallback, compute/network isolation, operator/dispatcher and actual report-generation time. A report emitted only on accepted actions does not itself define a pre-action counterfactual report on rejected orders. If that architecture cannot be validated, the frozen signal model is not empirically available.
3. Record for **every** original order whether S was generated, whether it was retained in the dataset and why. Preserve high and low categories, missing/ineligible categories, known original-order N_z, and all selection-stage denominators. If the observed record set is accepted-only, estimate a_z,b_z against original N, **not** against only accepted orders.
4. Certify any claimed inclusion propensity ρ_z(s) from independently documented routing/randomization and signed reporting logic. Logging only high reports with an unknown acceptance filter gives unknown ρ_h and yields S1, not the inverse-probability estimator. Positive inclusion support is a property of the *whole routing/logging architecture*, not an assumption about technical transfer.
5. If permitted, add an **independent, prospective nonactuating target audit** on original opportunities with precommitted sampling probabilities and compute/operator cost controls. Shadow validation must certify that audit does not alter existing customer service, dispatcher response or operator attention; otherwise define an entire audit intervention within dynamic J and measure its production cost. No paid labeling or physical intervention permitted under this research authorization.
6. Measure complete qualified y(a,z) for approved actions/fallback: accepted customer orders and service windows, missed/displaced orders, recovery, fleet/human time, unit contribution and captured value under actual contract. Document potentially connected customer-demand networks. Estimate p_z from the unconditional frame and do not transfer the frozen p,y across changed controllers.
7. For a separate source/grounding/new embodiment program, version source provenance (routine trajectories, failures/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained), rights and curation, controller and physical-policy lineage, joint qualifications, approved safety/recovery and whole-network original-order effect. An inert diagnostic cannot by itself validate new qualified capability. Only independent policy-closed network comparisons can isolate whole-policy economics if interference extends beyond the customer site.

## Identification ladder

- No original-order denominator or known sampling frame: only selected-law facts; no target q estimate.
- Original denominator plus selected high/low counts but unknown report-dependent logging: S1/S4 partial row coverage.
- Validated conditional propensity bounds with original denominator: S2 and confidence-set intersections.
- Exact known positive report-specific inclusion or independent audit: identifiable frozen target q (with sampling uncertainty).
- Independent **approved** complete-policy customer networks and original service ledger: prospective economic value for physical source training, target grounding and changed embodiment, not yet observed here.

Keep statistical coverage separate from actuation safety, customer consent, contract valuation and investment feasibility. Cluster or time-dependent original orders require alternative inference, not inflated iid counts. The economically relevant missing support is weighted by original-order probability and the span of qualified whole-network service alternatives, never raw success-rate percent or wage alone.

## Boundaries and route audit

No current rights-cleared signed target reporter, original-order sampling frame, approved whole-network comparator or calibrated y has been verified. Public DROID and Open X-Embodiment are technical trajectory sources, not original commercial orders. BLS industry/occupation matrices and BEA input-output vintages remain public routes to a **separate** production atlas, not substitutes for missing customer-order numerators/denominators or a numeric crosswalk. No vendor marketing claim is a signed policy/eligibility record. Closest scientific baseline is missing-data selection/partial identification and design-based sampling; none proves new robotics capability or a first-paper publication contribution.
