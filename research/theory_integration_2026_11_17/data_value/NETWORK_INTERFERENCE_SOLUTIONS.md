Private full worked solutions to [NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_EXERCISES.md](NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_EXERCISES.md). Do not mistake mathematics for a robot safety certificate or measured cashflow.

**1.** Complete target captured value is J=0.7(7.5)+0.3(7-s)-0.08 = 7+0.35-0.3s-0.08=7.27-0.3s. The difference from old service 7 is 0.27-0.3s, decreasing in s. The identified interval for the difference is [0.27-0.3(1.1),0.27-0.3(-0.2)] = [-0.06,0.33]; the route is not strictly robustly preferred. Condition s_U < 0.27/0.3=0.9 is needed (with strict inequality and unchanged other constraints). Recording only A improvement drops a potentially negative B externality; a changing customer-order registry can mask harm even while held-out controller qualification improves. Also verify safety and the legitimacy of 0.7/0.3 *pre-action* weights.

**2.** Define I_i=1 if network i is assigned a. HT(a)=[I_1(10/0.2)+I_2(12/0.8)]/2 =25I_1+7.5I_2. Its expectation is 25(0.2)+7.5(0.8)=11. The four assignments (I_1,I_2) yield estimate 0,25,7.5,32.5; the joint probabilities are 0.16,0.04,0.64,0.16, respectively. Therefore weighted total 0+1+4.8+5.2=11. Randomizing within a connected pair changes joint controller and operator potential outcomes, and per-robot i.i.d. errors are inappropriate. Only one potential outcome per network is observed, so joint individual (Y_i(a),Y_i(b)) dependence is not identified, even though each supported policy's population mean is.

**3.** Independent events: P(0,0)=0.81, P(1,0)=0.09, P(0,1)=0.09, P(1,1)=0.01. The last path requires two operators and violates the hard cap. Mutually exclusive: P(0,0)=0.8, P(1,0)=P(0,1)=0.1, P(1,1)=0. Mean use is 0.2 in both; each site mean is 0.1. Expected use alone is inadequate. Verified nonoverlapping time windows or an independently certified abort-before-recovery mechanism may make joint peak <=1; simply observing no failures does not establish that support. Conversely summing per-site maxima 1+1=2 is only a sufficient conservative criterion and may reject a genuinely disjoint-safe schedule.

**4.** Both mechanisms have total -0.6: (-0.6)+0 and (+0.2)+(-0.8). Keep starting body/checkpoint, approved controller data sources, rights, training recipe and actual compute/curation cost matched; randomize safe *whole connected networks* to either shared or isolated checkpoints and comparable source/grounding additions. Independently test held-out end-to-end qualification at frozen customer demand, then audit the original all-eligible customer-order registry including zero orders, new orders, substitutions and recoveries. The shared-isolated technical difference identifies controller-mediated influence conditional on that intervention, while order changes measure commercial externality. Demand may still respond to controller route and qualification, so causal attribution needs the validated design. Do not price a skill improvement by a fixed benchmark-to-dollar factor.

The same whole-network hard resource and eligibility support must be respected.
