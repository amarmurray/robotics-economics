# Two-date original-order policy comparison — prospective validation registry

**Status: no field evidence, intervention authorization or customer consent obtained.** This is a bounded, *prospective* identification plan only, not an instruction to run customer or robot experiments. It extends [TARGET_REPORT_SELECTION_EMPIRICAL.md](TARGET_REPORT_SELECTION_EMPIRICAL.md).

**Units and versions.** The independent experimental unit, if ever permitted, must be the policy-closed controller/dispatch/operator/customer network, including shared foundation-model training pools and demand spillovers. A robot trajectory and a customer order inside a common dispatcher are not independent replicas. Predeclare the original-order universe before controller qualification, admission, routing, collection or report filtering. Record rejected, expired, incumbent-protected, never-eligible and substituted orders with complete acceptance definitions and counterfactual competitor service.

**Arms for an approved future study.** R: retain qualified incumbent and routine service/data, capturing all current contribution; A: rights-cleared independent target shadow report without actuation and with measured displaced production/operator time; X: separately versioned and held-out adaptation to heterogeneous additional sources, with actual training/curation cost, source rights, controller lineage and joint qualifications; G: physical target grounding with independent safety gates and changed occupancy/recovery; E: new body/tooling/manufacturing with qualified complete-order production and actual delay/capital burden. Cross-arm pooling is forbidden unless the experiment establishes safe network separation or describes the interference. Every arm retains lawful fallbacks and authorized aborts.

**Measurements that distinguish the theory.** For every original order and opportunity window, record regime proxies and reporter versions before action; source S and its observation O if well-defined; report creation, recording, dropping and selection probabilities (including low/missing), not just accepted logs; source modality and distinct exposure units; qualified stage-by-stage task/service bundles, safety/recovery and operator load; original-order accepted/missed/displaced service; net captured contribution and staged cash budget; customer demand and dispatch allocation after training. Validate target report quality on independently sampled pre-action original orders, not after conditioning on robot acceptance. Qualifying a movement is not qualifying a complete customer service.

**Discriminating contrasts.** (1) Estimate the real inclusion law P(O|original regime, S, controller) and test whether recorded routine data identify the relevant target stratum; the synthetic 2/5 equality is a null *example*, not a forecast. (2) Compare the independent audit report with routine logs under a held-fixed approved controller/dispatcher: is a Blackwell coupling credible at the same target opportunity distribution? (3) Match X, G and E on held-out joint qualified service and original-order economic outcomes after their actual costs, rather than treating an increase in benchmark score or trajectory count as contribution. (4) Compare *complete* R/A/X/G/E policies if any of them alters demand, operator resources, access or learning history; frozen-row confidence bounds cannot be transported. (5) State what credible observed policy separation could disprove the proposed information-quality-driven switch among X and G.

**Identification limits.** When the original-order reporting propensity is unknown, retain sharp selected-report intervals instead of imputation. When the target reporter is not pre-action-defined on unaccepted orders, abandon the frozen-report estimator and use whole-policy counterfactual bounds. When safety approval or within-network interference forbids randomization, document the missing support; no synthetic revenue estimates or independent pseudo-replication. Source rights and licenses must be documented before any offline model usage; this authorization permits no purchase or training.

**Broader agenda link.** Keep the multipayer cash and rights feasibility, multi-layer customer/robot/operator production network, demand evolution, physical embodiment and manufacturing constraints inside the full J. The official numerical BLS–BEA crosswalk and actual commercial end-market opportunity atlas remain unverified separately. No vendor story repairs those numerical gaps.
