# Prospective cross-arm qualification and endogenous buyer-market audit

**Design only, 21 November 2026; no physical trials, customer contact, data acquisition or confidential inputs authorized.** Companion to [cross-arm theory](JOINT_QUALIFICATION_CROSS_ARM_SUPPORT_AND_DEMAND.md) and the [existing whole-learner experiment protocol](JOINT_QUALIFICATION_CHOICE_EVIDENCE.md).

**Experimental unit and versions.** For an original target/customer task panel, pre-register its eligibility, target order timing, environment/hardware/operator/access tuple, signed controller/fallback, safety envelope and deadline. Across R/S/G/SG, compare same architecture and total accepted-update/compute budget; S uses genuinely independent rights-cleared episodes, G-train aligns/grounds the pre-existing archive, SG combines them at controlled budget. Separately record field trajectories, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained origins; log lineage identifiers and proposed/admitted transitions. G-observe is a fixed-learner *report*, not G-train. E requires its own embodiment manufacturing, capital, signed recipes and customer eligibility; do not treat E as an accuracy multiplier.

**Which qualification moments are directly identified?** Random assignment of whole-learner/operator/customer clusters may identify each arm's marginal \(p_A^z,p_B^z\) and **within-arm paired** \(h_z\) if the two complete-service tests are both supported on the *same* target units. It does **not** reveal each unit's four counterfactual \(Q_j^z\) response types. Pathwise monotone and pathwise lattice-homomorphic restrictions therefore need separate structural justification, matched cloned learner comparisons with controlled interference where legally possible, and falsification checks; observing zero average S×G task interaction does not imply the stronger modularity. Shared weights, overlapping customer markets and operator spillovers require cluster-level exposure mapping; a split by episodes is inadequate.

**Customer-demand response.** Predeclare the eligible *original* market cohort before offer/acceptance selection. Preserve offered, rejected, canceled, delayed, accepted, refunded and settled paid service at the same economic scope. Capture segment \(d\) (complementary bundle vs substitutive shared-slot, or real task types), prices/terms, inbound order intensity, marketing/channel exposure, competitor alternatives, buyer adoption and capacity pressure, with rights for data use. Identify \(\pi_z(d)\), actual order rate \(\Lambda_z\), segment-conditional \(p,h\), complete net \(m_z(d)\), and whether demand changed because of technical reliability signals, prices, commercial treatment or exogenous selection. Record paid outside-order availability and actual displaced receipts; shadow prices are not receipts.

**Decision ledger.** For every original offered task segment, keep a row with physically/legal signed R/S/G/SG/E whole policies, joint qualification (00/10/01/11), admissible fallback, signed accepted-service production value, incremental training/capex/access cost, observed demand contribution and missing-counterfactual code. Report the original **unrestricted Fréchet** decision interval, optionally monotone or modular cross-arm bounds **only after** defended assumptions, and composition/intensity sensitivity. Prespecify buyer quality/safety and revenue tests; missing policy support is not evidence of zero outcome. Avoid benchmark-point-to-dollar conversions.

**What would reject the theory's useful restrictions?** A legally matched target context where S or G destroys a previously qualified *same service* violates pathwise task monotonicity; an AND/OR response type violates pathwise modularity even if population main effects cancel. Significant shifts in observed order arrivals, segment mix, willingness to pay or paid outside work reject the one-fixed-demand table. If a single robot/operator action changes capacity elsewhere in the network, update the full signed production set; do not multiply per-robot uplift by fleet count. If only separate-arm observed moments exist, maintain the broader identified set regardless of theory elegance.

**Official production atlas:** retain typed customer–operator–integrator–manufacturer–data network edges and vintage/crosswalk requirements. No numerical BEA/BLS sector relationship has been obtained from a checked table, so no macro coefficient is asserted. Existing source-route failures are not cured by unrelated vendor deployment news. No customer outreach, unapproved study or public publication is requested.
