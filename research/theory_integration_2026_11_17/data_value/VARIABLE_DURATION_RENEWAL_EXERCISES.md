# Exercises: deadline-sensitive economic data transfer

23 November 2026. Student copy; independent worked solutions live in [solutions](VARIABLE_DURATION_RENEWAL_SOLUTIONS.md).

1. With Pr(g=2)=Pr(k=2)=1/2 and an unknown coupling, derive the four joint g/k cell probabilities from z=Pr(g=2,k=2). Show why no individual duration histogram identifies z, and derive the neutral paid-GK value under A deadline4 and separately authorized B requiring completion by minute3.
2. Remove the B renewal ticket. Compare the derivative of total paid production with respect to z to the version with B. Explain why the earlier binary all-or-nothing renewal amplification cannot be extrapolated.
3. Suppose activating K after G changes K's repair duration to max(1,k-1), and alternatively to k+1 whenever g=2. For each, compute the whole-program J over all z and explain why paired technical Q does not identify it.
4. Let the firm legally observe g before choosing whether to process K. Give an implementable lower-bound policy robust to the three duration regimes, compare with E=13/4, and list at least two nonoptional physical or rights assumptions needed.
5. Formulate the minimum cluster-level sequential evidence for choosing K within g=2 without assuming neutral durations. Explain why collecting only invoices or paired benchmark scores cannot estimate it.
