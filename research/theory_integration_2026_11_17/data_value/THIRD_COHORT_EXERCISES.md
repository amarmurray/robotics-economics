# Exercises — third-cohort economic source overlap (student)

Use the conditional failed-cohort model in [the chapter](THIRD_COHORT_FAILURE_RECOVERY_ECONOMIC_OVERLAP.md). Do not consult [instructor solutions](THIRD_COHORT_SOLUTIONS.md) before attempting.

1. Starting from one original buyer ticket, write a three-cohort pathwise contribution expression when early success closes the ticket, failure creates a recovery job, and a late service can use one of two qualified checkpoints but can never be billed twice. Derive Eq. (1).
2. Prove the union identity x_GK=x_G+x_K-z and the three lower and three upper restrictions in Eq. (5). State why their simultaneous sharpness need not hold under arbitrary additional causal restrictions.
3. In the eight-atom witness, calculate every unconditional technical joint Q_G/Q_K cell and the two **separate** paid coincidences. Show precisely which overlap changes between worlds.
4. Calculate the ex-ante cost threshold for adding recovery-origin K to already authorized failure-origin G, and derive the robust mixing fraction against independent E.
5. Suppose joint gradient training displaces the retained G checkpoint. Which of the derived monotonicity claims survive? Specify a legally and physically credible validation intervention that would test the selectable-checkpoint assumption.
6. Suppose G-targeted training changes W through extra operator validation. Identify exactly which quantities cease to be transportable from old-policy buyer/worker histories, and propose a cluster-level treatment schedule.
