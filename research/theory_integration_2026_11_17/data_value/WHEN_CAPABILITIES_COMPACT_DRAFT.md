# When Capabilities Become Production
## Learning, qualification and the allocation of robotic capital
Working first-paper draft · 25 November 2026 Eastern. Conditional theory and proposed empirical design; no calibrated return or independently established novelty. [Detailed technical appendix, preserved November 24–25 draft](COMPACT_DRAFT_DETAILED_APPENDIX_20261125.md).

### Abstract
A learned capability is a possibility for production rather than an output. A robotic firm invests in data, embodied capital and coordination to transform possibilities into qualified, customer-accepted work. Because production generates further learning opportunities, changes in coordination or hardware also alter the rival learning program against which a data investment must be valued. We develop a common economic comparison of independently evolving feasible programs, show how routine learning can either displace deliberate grounding or amplify its value through capacity release, and derive conditional decision bounds from incomplete original-service evidence. Shared dated inputs make marginal qualification and individual robot returns insufficient for portfolio investment. We distinguish direct technical comparisons, commercial program effects and optimized capital returns. Available studies support elements of the mechanism but do not identify a complete commercial learning contrast.

## 1. From intelligence to economic production
Two properties of contemporary robotics matter jointly. A physical body cannot perform simultaneous incompatible uses, but a controller or lawfully reusable experience may support multiple bodies. Neither reproducibility nor nonrivalry means that an additional deployment is free: actuation, sensing, verification, computation, recovery and attention consume dated resources. Experience generated in production can improve subsequent competence, but the location and content of experience are determined by the production policy. A successful routine may eliminate failures that would otherwise teach a rare skill. A scheduler can expose a different mix of work. A new embodiment can make a previously absent action or observation possible. These facts motivate the following primitives.

**Embodied capital** supplies mechanically feasible actions and dated service capacity. **Controller capital** is the versioned reusable policy that turns observations into actions. **Data capital** consists of lawful reusable source-specific histories: field trajectories, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained inputs, each with its own learning and curation technology. **Qualification** is a relation among controller, body, target, accepted workflow, time and rights, not a scalar competence score. **Organization** determines feasible joint use of bodies, workers, compute, validation and releases. **Customer demand** determines the economic use of that joint menu; it includes original offers, refusals and new opportunities induced by past service. A recipe is a complete admissible customer service with joint resource incidence and acceptance standard. The relation between inputs and outputs is a production network of such recipes; transactions, physical flows and learning transfers are distinct networks.

This is built on heterogeneous endogenous data and spillovers in Farboodi, Koh and Xia's [Data-Driven Automation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10127), the competence/coordination distinction in Imas, Koh and Legg's [Organizing Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05417), and older task allocation, organizational complementarity, process flexibility and learning-by-doing. [Positioning and contribution audit](CONTRIBUTION_HIERARCHY_20261125.md) states what is already known. We do not claim discovery of data spillovers, complementarities, feedback loops or LP duality.

## 2. The common value object
At a lawful observed history h, let A(h) describe dated joint resources per qualified recipe, B(h) the original demand incidence, K(h) usable capacity, d(h) demand, v(h) net captured contribution per accepted recipe. In a fixed, divisible, one-period case,

    F(h) = max v(h)'x  subject to A(h)x <= K(h), B(h)x <= d(h), x >= 0.   (A)

Do not list an unqualified recipe as feasible. For indivisible orders, queues or time-dependent obligations use actual time-expanded feasible schedules; the LP is then only an upper relaxation. Internal physical intermediates may be added with flow conservation, counting final contribution once. Planner surplus and a producer's captured returns are different objectives.

Let program g be a committed intervention and a set of feasible nonanticipating policies with its own training, lawful retention, resource and information constraints. R retains the old policy; P allows routine production-generated reuse; X adds source data; G deliberately grounds a target; C changes coordination with its routine learner still evolving; E acquires a new embodiment or capacity. Lawful combinations are candidates. A controlled law m describes original and induced offers, resource state, learning and qualification under each policy; it need not be invariant across programs. Write

    J_g(pi,m) = E_m^(g,pi)[sum_t beta^t r_t + beta^T phi_T] - c_g,
    V_g(m) = sup_(pi feasible, nonanticipating) J_g(pi,m).              (B)

Here r_t is net contribution from accepted work (including actually incurred variable costs), c_g is incremental committed program cost and phi_T is distinct successor production. Account for acquisition, curation, compute, verification, alternative uses and manufacturing/installation at their actual dates once. No separate premium for data already valued through successor production. Heterogeneous investment competes in one budget and menu; a positive standalone return does not prove optimal allocation. Under a common known law the marginal return to G depends on the retained program and the full set of alternatives, not the magnitude of technical transfer alone.

**Result 1 — economic emulation.** If every G policy admits a feasible P policy with discounted gross loss at most epsilon, then V_G-V_P <= epsilon-(c_G-c_P). Subtract costs and take suprema. A law-independent emulator and uniform bound also support a robust comparison over uncertain laws; a law-informed optimized policy is not necessarily executable. The same argument applies to C. Free optional access that retains all old lawful feasible policies and adds no obligations cannot reduce the gross optimum; costly or forced training can. [Full policy-closed formulation and first-failure certificate](POLICY_CLOSED_DATA_VALUE.md). A frozen controller comparison is a technical diagnostic, not the cheaper independently evolving competitor.

**Nested comparators and observable source dimensionality.** Fixed configured flexibility is a special case of A/B when data never changes future feasible menus. Scalar learning-by-doing is a special case when a single experience index and the nonlearning state preserve all available actions, rewards and transitions. Even allowing arbitrary differentiable panel-specific production responses, a scalar source index implies rank at most one for the local matrix of economic responses across independent fixed-size source-mixture directions and predeclared service/resource panels. Rank above one rejects that restricted index, not heterogeneous learning-by-doing or DDA. Technical rank can disappear under economic bottlenecks; match original offers, lawful checkpoints, acquisition budgets and independently feasible dated resources, and account for smoothness and sampling error. [Nested models, rank restriction and saturation model](NESTED_LEARNING_STATE_AND_ECONOMIC_RANK.md). This is a controlled diagnostic rather than a substitute for the evolving whole-program contrast.

**Finite composition evidence.** Arbitrary finite responses at distinct source mixtures can always be interpolated by an unrestricted smooth scalar index, so finite chord rank is not a local derivative rejection. Under the stronger nested-menu null, however, all gross optimized panel values are nondecreasing in that index; separated opposite panel rankings reject this restriction even at a kink. More generally oriented strict ranking edges must be acyclic. ReMix's published sampling weights give two independent finite input directions, not an economic rank estimate. [Finite order diagnostic and technical evidence](COMPOSITION_PANEL_EVIDENCE_AND_ORDER.md).

**Capacity-dependent ordering.** Cross-market reversal of marginal embodied returns does not alone reject nested scalar knowledge: higher knowledge may make another body redundant in one market and productive in another while preserving every optimized level. Compare levels at matched original and expanded dated endowments. Feasible witnesses and simultaneous upper bounds on omitted qualified recipes establish strict rankings; paired incremental bounds can resolve overlapping level intervals. Common monotone order is feasible in the difference-constraint relaxation iff some order admits no negative cycle. [Proof, counterexample and prospective capacity audit](DATED_CAPACITY_ORDER_WITNESSES.md).

## 3. Why coordination changes the value of data
To isolate the mechanism, consider two dates with one final authorized service, fixed contribution v>=0, no other competing orders, stable demand and additive costs. At date 1, H is authorization, L is timely baseline qualification, T is incremental qualification from G, S is incremental routine qualification generated by C at date 0, and W0<=W1 are binary usable capacity before and after C. All indicators are jointly distributed; qualifications and authorization are lawfully observable at dispatch. Assume qualification composes by OR with no further interactions. Let R*=H(1-L)T. The gross incremental grounding benefit at C status c is v E[R* W_c(1-cS)].

**Result 2 — signed complementarity.** With additive early net contribution and acquisition costs, the four-program interaction is

    I = J_CG-J_C-J_G+J_P
      = v E[R*(W1-W0)(1-S)] - v E[R* W0 S].                             (1)

The first term is newly usable capacity in a market where routine learning still fails. The second is grounding made redundant by C-induced routine learning on formerly usable capacity. Proof: subtract v E[R*W0] from v E[R*W1(1-S)] and rearrange. Both terms have a production interpretation; neither requires an exogenous positive transfer coefficient. Coordination that only releases capacity (S=0) weakly complements G; coordination that changes only routine readiness (W1=W0) weakly substitutes. General demand, costs or training interactions require the full J comparison. [Proof, assumptions and matched-marginal counterexample](COORDINATION_ROUTINE_MODEL_AB.md).

The investment implication is not simply to buy the largest observed technical improvement. G can beat P yet fail against an evolving C; C can beat G yet make CG worthwhile. X and E can dominate both. There is no universal ranking of source modalities or embodiment choices. The economic effect of data is relative to the dated qualification failures still left by the best attainable rival.

## 4. Which measurements resolve the investment?
We distinguish fixed-arm intervention effects from policy-optimal effects. A valid randomized fixed-program trial identifies a mean contrast between assigned policies. It does not identify the optimum over untried policies. For a supported policy witness L_g and a valid upper U_g on V_g, a sufficient adoption criterion is L_g > max_(k != g) U_k under common accounting. A rejected upper versus another feasible lower can rule a program out. Under uncertainty the witness must remain feasible without knowledge of the latent law. A partial order is a defensible outcome when intervals overlap. This is the general decision architecture; the following are supporting restricted certificates, not independent contributions.

**Missing combination.** Under the single-order restrictions of (1), define F0 subset F1 as lawful usable-capacity events, and p,g,c,z as nonnegative policy-invariant economically weighted masses of accepted P,G,C,CG service. Let f0,f1 be capacity masses and d=f1-f0. If P is retained in G and C, CG retains G and C, and there is no joint-only qualification inside F0, then

    max(g,c) <= z <= min(f1,c+g-p+d).                                   (2)

The lower follows from retention; inside F0 CG can add to C at most g-p, and outside F0 at most d; total is capped by f1. Nested and maximally separated acceptance sets attain endpoints for divisible masses under the maintained assumptions. Thus the incremental net G return after C lies between max(g,c)-c-k and min(f1,c+g-p+d)-c-k for incremental cost k and no other changed J terms. Three arms cannot establish strictly positive interaction using only these bounds. Separate weighted budgets on retention regressions and joint-only readiness relax the restrictions; pre-treatment strata tighten bounds where their masses can be summed. [Detailed proof, structural sensitivity and strata](COMPACT_DRAFT_DETAILED_APPENDIX_20261125.md). Neither randomization nor retaining controller code establishes these cross-arm event restrictions.

**Capacity and portfolios.** Multiple orders may compete for the same body, worker or deadline. For K interchangeable unit slots and observable ready set, expected optimized production is weighted marginal qualification less weighted expected overflow across value-ranked prefixes. Identical marginal qualification can have different economic value because readiness is joint. Accepted-only logs also censor unserved qualifications and may not represent optimal dispatch. [Shared capacity](SHARED_CAPACITY_ENVELOPE.md), [censored capacity](CENSORED_CAPACITY_ENVELOPE.md), [nonoptimal dispatch](ECONOMIC_OBJECTS_AND_ORACLE_GAP.md). For the fixed finite divisible recipe LP, marginal capital in direction d is the minimum p'd across the incumbent optimal dual price face, so summing individual directional returns is exact only with a common minimizing price. A learned recipe can change this face before output changes. Finite increments, indivisible capital and changing histories require full portfolio contrasts and feasible witnesses. [Network cuts](NETWORK_MPK_CUT_GEOMETRY.md), [multi-resource portfolio MPK](MULTI_RESOURCE_MPK_PORTFOLIO.md), [joint residual audit](JOINT_RECIPE_RESIDUAL_AUDIT.md).

These results explain why a technically improved robot can leave whole-program contribution unchanged, why a new robot can unlock future data, and why adding up local returns is not a production-network aggregation model. The embodied return vector is indexed by body, capital scale, program and end-market demand. Its dynamic counterpart is the increment in B including induced data and future service, not a second payment for the same data. The aggregate equilibrium and multipayer accounting extensions remain part of the broader roadmap, not solved by this paper.

## 5. Empirical scope and falsification
Existing evidence identifies separate links in the mechanism, not a matched causal chain. Robin and common-checkpoint recovery studies support technical source effects; fleet learning and online deployment show possible endogenous exposure. Vulcan's assignments/attempts/extractions and pharmacy prescription-line linkage demonstrate different production denominators; the motor and hospital records distinguish occupied and released clocks. The Amazon deployed scheduler is a concrete coordination rival, not a learning ROI. Cainiao records show that mode choice among selected deliveries differs from penetration of all original inbound opportunities. [Evidence matrix](EVIDENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [source evidence section](FOCUSED_FIRST_PAPER_EMPIRICAL_SECTION.md) retain versions and limitations. No source here jointly establishes a data intervention, all original customer offers, resource occupation, lawful successor exposure and net paid production. Historical official BEA cells are separate from the missing contemporary BEA–BLS backbone.

Preferred design: randomize independently evolving lawful P/G/C/CG over interference catchments starting at matched checkpoints, predeclare the original opportunity population and horizon, retain all offers/no-service/refusals, complete recipes, accepted settlements, dated body/worker/compute use, data lineage/rights and all incremental costs. Follow each arm's own exposure and successor demand. Include X and E where feasible. A frozen-controller C arm is a separate physical diagnostic; it must not replace evolving C. Tests of (1) need lawful dated residual qualification and capacity evidence or direct four-arm economic contrasts; the latter do not alone identify latent mechanism terms. For omitted recipes bound their economically reduced contribution independently and pair with a feasible schedule. For missing CG validate (2)'s assumptions or report nonidentification. [Validation protocol](COMPACT_WHOLE_PROGRAM_VALIDATION.md).

Falsification operates at three levels. If an asserted technical transfer fails on matched target qualification, that transfer mechanism fails. If qualification improves but the hypothesized joint resource/service opportunity does not materialize, the production mechanism fails. If a cheaper lawful evolving program delivers equivalent net service and successor value, the distinct grounding investment fails for that menu. A statistically null four-arm interaction is not proof of no technical learning; a positive interaction does not guarantee either investment beats other alternatives. Distinguish sampling uncertainty, structural partial identification, and accounting uncertainty.

## 6. Contribution and scope
The candidate contribution is a theory of economic data value in **learned, jointly qualified production**, with an evolving organizational and production-generated learning counterfactual. The focal test is the sign and magnitude of incremental G after C (and versus X/E), not a generic claim that data is valuable. The decision certificates specify which observations can discriminate the mechanisms and which do not. All restricted theorems use established mathematical foundations; independent novelty, field support and contemporary network calibration remain open. The broad RoboEcon synthesis, manufacturing, autonomy, ownership and production topology are retained. [Contribution audit and scope decision criteria](CONTRIBUTION_HIERARCHY_20261125.md).
