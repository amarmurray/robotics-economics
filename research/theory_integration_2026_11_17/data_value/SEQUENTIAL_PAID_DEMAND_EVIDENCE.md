# Prospective connected original-buyer, worker and learner evidence

**Status:** design only, unpopulated. No user-authorized outreach, commercial customer access, robot deployment, private records, training purchases or publication is implied.

**Observational unit.** A connected customer A/C–robot–support-worker cluster with a t1/t2/t3 timeline, originally eligible opportunities before any dispatch, separately licensed evidence streams and independently settled paid work. Keep unoffered, refused, attempted-but-unqualified, missed, rejected and completed orders. Document offer/decision/acceptance/payment times. Keep a zero-denominator audit of eligible exposures, not just invoice outcomes.

**Physics and budget.** Original A t1 service spends one A lifetime purchase; original C t2 service spends one C lifetime purchase. Track J_A,J_X at t1; the single eligible worker's actual one-unit clearance; L2; t2 accepted C work; J2 arising *after* the t2 clearance window; L3. Distinguish worker active minutes, occupied capacity, repair downtime, robot-time, human fallback and later clearance. Capture simultaneous jobs and common shocks, not independent repair availability for each buyer.

**Causal response.** Record what buyers actually learned about t2 quality, price, availability, recovery, publicity and rival offers before their *original* t3 request deadline. Preserve original t3 A/C offer and no-offer records even if the robot is broken, C budget is spent, or Q=0. A t2 job that blocks t3 can be correlated with t3 buyer demand; measure the joint incident/order table, not just marginal rates.

**Selected reports and rights.** Separate M_t (selection into telemetry, invoices and buyer audits) from original outcomes. Document feasible audit permissions and its randomization design/known positive sampling probabilities by cluster history. Signed rights to record, retain, train, derive and deploy each source type are separate. Forbidden actions have structural zero assignment probabilities; do not use inverse propensity weights there. A simulated G qualification is not evidence of accepted G service or induced customer demand.

**Matched technical / economic check.** Fix original task opportunity, embodiment, model checkpoint, hardware gate, compute, cost and rights in matched source-to-target learning comparisons. Observe frozen target qualification and joint accepted-service recipes, not a scalar benchmark-to-cash mapping. Study original t3 demand and paid throughput in a distinct legally supported customer study. Check a worker-capacity relief channel separately from technical transfer.

**Resource allocation after a blocked t2.** Document whether a validator or compute slot can operate independently while the single recovery worker clears the inherited job. Record a valid pre-t2 report's timestamp relative to qualification commitment. Only then can the two-unit example of information value in a blocked production period become a testable prediction.

**Empirical boundary.** The 2026 official BEA source route is https://www.bea.gov/itable/input-output and https://apps.bea.gov/api/ (InputOutput Make/Use/Requirements). Official quantitative interindustry coefficients are still missing; no substitution from a vendor pilot. No real G service, payment, lawful original-buyer panel, independently proven E resources or signed transfer rights is represented by this protocol.
