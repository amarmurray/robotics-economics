# Prospective matched original-customer, menu and operator audit

**19 Nov 2026; design only, not a performed intervention or an outreach request.**

Register ALL eligible original procurement groups in the same policy-controllable original market and decision period, not merely signed or fulfilled sales. Fields: stable buyer and parent budget ID; offered assortment, price and quality/service guarantee; unoffered substitutes and rival vendors; no-purchase/refusal; eligible-but-capacity-censored demand; actual order time and common opportunity; service substitution versus independent orders; robot/controller/policy version; held-out target qualification; operator/resource pool and concurrency; recovery times, safe dispatch eligibility, refusal rights, service-level liabilities, gross payer receipts and collection/variable costs. Log selected and unselected offers and maintain a common original denominator.

**Identification of t.** For a precommitted fixed X policy/menu and two eligible groups, sample matched simultaneous A/B *requests* independent of capacity censoring; estimate joint probability t and its interval, rather than multiplying accepted-only individual rates. Original buyer groups may belong to one parent budget; if so, impose that structural support on the response-type distribution rather than claiming unrestricted Fréchet sharpness. Record time and common demand shocks. The t=1/4 example is synthetic and has no external calibration.

**Causal X/G/E/R design.** Before transporting customer propensities between policies, separately verify physically approved operator-slot changes, source-data permissions/compute costs and target adaptation, robot-body qualification, identical or identified offered-menu/price alternatives, and versioned controller updates. For scarce recovery, measuring mean operator minutes is insufficient if simultaneous recovery tails violate the two-slot G construction; measure joint deadlines and full accepted-service recipes. Isolation across shared operator/customer/controller networks needs an approved whole-network experimental design or a defensible exposure restriction.

**Falsification:** if pre-dispatch orders cannot be observed before allocation, W=max is an optimistic oracle and cannot be used as a guaranteed realized lower bound. If buyer choice depends on new menus, baseline p_i is not usable for X/G. If E does not provide an approved independent capacity slot, its comparison fails. If actual prices or customer population have no supported bounds, no finite captured-cash certificate. Do not call vendor claims or benchmarks an identified data-to-dollar effect.

Connect prospective fields to multilayer atlas/task value chain and official BLS/BEA aggregate topology, which is not an individual customer panel.
