# Prospective empirical discrimination — static means vs adaptive data/grounding/body value

**Research design only. No physical experiment, private data or customer panel was obtained.**

*Unit and scope.* Start with independently delineated policy-closed customer markets (controllers, shared learners, operator/dispatcher and customer demand all included). Register original B and prospective A orders, non-attempts/cancellations, deadline, demand strata, gross service values, capture/payment, fallbacks, machine versions, qualified service recipes and pathwise physical/operator budgets *before intervention*. Records that lack the actual service denominator do not identify economic value.

*Three estimands need three distinguishable designs.* An approved randomized source/no-source × ground/no-ground experiment on independent whole networks estimates static J_z and the source-grounding interaction I, not cross-arm alternative qualification overlap t. A *separate* approved, isolation-verified paired shadow qualification of x and g on the same market opportunity may measure cross-arm Q_x,Q_g conditional on customer stratum, but is not whole-service field revenue and may fail if training changes the later policy. A prospectively approved and sequentially randomized grounding decision **among first-stage source failures** can measure r=P(Q_ground_after_source=1 | failed source, history), the actual continuation of qualified customer orders, and the opportunity cost of the delay. Sequential support/positivity, stable implementation, physical approval and network noninterference are necessary. Refuse to infer a physically ineligible arm's value.

*Lineage and original-order records.* Preserve market_network_id, independent_network_group, customer_demand_stratum, original_order_registry, order_deadline, source_type, versioned_data_rights, read_only_copy_flag, source_training_flag, controller_model_hash, embodiment_bill_of_materials_version, target_action_support, frozen_full_customer_recipe, independent_qualification_approval, Q_x/Q_g/Q_h_joints_if_actually_matched, x_failure_history, conditional_next_stage_Q, A_accepted_service, B_retained_displaced_orders, failed_and_unattempted, operator_recovery_minutes, dispatch_cross_edges, learning_and_testing_time, manufacturing_delay, curation_training_rights_cost, qualified_specialist_comparator, captured_contribution. Mark missing, unapproved and inapplicable separately from economic zero.

*Tests that could reject the theory model.* (1) Does the measured alternate-policy joint t differ materially from what marginal independence assumes within customer strata? (2) Does observed grounding-after-source r differ from static P(Q_g=1 | Q_x=0)? (3) Do offline qualification signals transport to the complete field customer recipe? (4) Does protecting B customer orders while copying data actually preserve operator/customer-market interference closure? (5) Do costs and expired orders eliminate nominal option gains? (6) Is a third embodiment available/qualified by the promised commercial window? (7) Does competent specialist S or production-generated no-extra-cost data dominate?

*Partial reporting.* Without physically approved paired/sequential designs, use Fréchet and complete-history identified-set bounds only, not imputed t/r. The quantitative official BLS–BEA production backbone and industry task atlas remain separate missing evidence; industry wages and technical robot pass percentages cannot be converted directly to customer contribution. No source claims a field calibration here.
