# Instructor solutions: transport and resource feasibility

Companion to [TRANSPORT_EXERCISES.md](TRANSPORT_EXERCISES.md). Synthetic arithmetic and standard conditional proof, not robot evidence.

**1.** For symmetric marginals \(p\) and full-bundle \(q\), cells \((P_{00},P_{10},P_{01},P_{11})=(1-2p+q,p-q,p-q,q)\). Site A R gives (.25,0,0,.75); A E gives (0,.10,.10,.80); site B R gives (0,.25,.25,.50); B E gives (.05,.05,.05,.85). Each has nonnegative cells summing to 1. Differences \(d_A=.05\), \(d_B=.35\). Source \(\bar d_\nu=.9(.05)+.1(.35)=.08\) and \(J_\nu(R)-J_\nu(E)=1-8(.08)=+.36\). Target \(\bar d_\mu=.4(.05)+.6(.35)=.23\) and \(J_\mu(R)-J_\mu(E)=1-8(.23)=-.84\). Reweighting requires conditional effects stable and measured under both site strata (with overlap); directly applying the *pooled* matched difference from source to target is not justified.

**2.** Valid R complete-bundle bounds are \([.5,.75]\), E bounds \([.8,.9]\); maximum difference is .9−.5=.4. Therefore \(D_\mu\ge1-8[(1-\varepsilon).1+\varepsilon .4]=.2-2.4\varepsilon\). Strict positivity holds for \(\varepsilon<1/12\), with weak bound at equality. The bound is sharp under independently attainable sitewise endpoint laws: covered (qR,qE)=(.7,.8), missing (.5,.9). Negative lower bound is *not* an E dominance result; it only admits unfavorable possible worlds.

**3.** \(1-\Delta(.1)=0\) gives \(\Delta=10\); Δ=8 implies +.2 and Δ=12 implies −.2. Model A has \(a-c=6\), unconstrained optimum q=3, so both K bind. At K1=1, \(G=6-1=5\); at K2=2, \(G=12-4=8\). Captured expansion increment .8(8−5)=2.4. Model A is a one-divisible-service operating special case, not a general full B/C bundle formula; the latter requires customer recipe combinations and joint qualification outcomes.

**4.** World W0: \(P(good,low)=.5,P(good,high)=0,P(bad,low)=.4,P(bad,high)=.1\). World W1: (.4,.1,.5,0). Both have good=.5, high=.1, sum 1. Only a good-report target action is considered; with W0 it costs 1+1=2 along every supported good path; W1 admits positive good/high mass costing 1+2=3, above 2.1. The W1 target action cannot be chosen without a safe reserve or abort. Budget 3 repairs only this *stylized* capacity inequality; it cannot certify actual robot gates, reachability, failed recovery or authorization.

**5.** For multiple recipes, write \(E_\theta[V(Q_R;\omega)-V(Q_E;\omega)]\) or the original complete-history \(J\), using joint outcomes of accepted service and scarce shared resources; do not attach a constant \(\Delta\) if it changes with which other skills qualify. Sample selection needs a transport/positivity argument, controller-induced state visits are post-treatment, pooled retraining causes interference across units, and an old qualified specialist changes the incremental operating frontier. The mathematical tools (Fréchet bounds, standard causal transport, minimax/robust support) are well known. The unresolved empirical robotics-specific assertions concern the controlled source/grounding/body learning kernel, route-dependent safe visit support, and accepted customer production demand and contribution. No per-trajectory economic price is identified by a benchmark percentage.
