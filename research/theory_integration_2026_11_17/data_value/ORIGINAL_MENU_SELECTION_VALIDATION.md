## What each unit must establish

The preassignment unit is the **complete procurement parent**, comprising all A/B requests that jointly consume the same robot/operator resources. Store eligible parents with ordered and *declined* requests, absent responses, budget/price/quality/rights state, operator/site/time segment, and alternatives. Before any target report, write a consistent hash of the natural GG offer (price, delivery window, warranty, cancellation, refund, operator and controller version); separately hash commitment C. If labels or rights differ, their outcomes are different treatment versions. Record a complete response flag **R** and known solicitation denominator; never code R=0 as buyer refusal.

## Gated acquisition decision tree

**Physical gate.** If there is no independently approved complete joint G (or E) robot/operator observation/recovery policy, there is no live action and no implied certified production. Analyze declared counterfactual demand as a hypothesis only. X remains unsigned until matched technical/physical validation.

**Original-market gate.** If physically approved full-parent original GG offers are permitted, predeclare original parent frame and cluster-level assignment with positive propensity, correct timing and complete refusals/receipts. Estimate natural theta_N directly, *not* by an individual robot-task success percentage. If mixed-only assignment is lawful but full GG is absent, previous support counterworlds still apply.

**C-only acquisition gate.** If only commitment C is observed, first validate full-frame rho and s with selection/finite-sample uncertainty. Second independently audit nonresponse u or bound it transparently. Third defend cross-version epsilon from truly comparable matched original-menu evidence or a legitimate enforceable equality; C-only data can never estimate epsilon. If any scientific assumption fails, retain the full identification interval and show unranked decisions. A binding contract should enter V as its own production policy rather than stand in for N.

**Decision-value gate.** Compute interval [L_N,U_N], then common V/J for physically admitted R/G/E and optional safe X. If it straddles a choice threshold, identify which *approved* observation could split the still-compatible worlds; do not price a passive report absent a validated outcome kernel/prior. Compare cost of target sampling, new grounding, source transfer and embodiment with no continuation double count. Never interpret the threshold 2/3 outside the exact two-order toy economics.

## Falsifiable original-versus-C comparison

A simultaneous randomized menu-version trial on the same eligible parent population, where *both offers are legally and physically approved*, could directly estimate each version's response and expose C/N differences. A paired repeated offer without random ordering is not automatically valid: demand may be changed by previous offers, publicity or learning. Compare buyer/parent strata, prices/quality, receipts, price menu and operator capacity. Predeclare a maximum epsilon and its uncertainty/transport scope from external data; do not calibrate epsilon by assuming it. Track interference across shared parent resources, so the cluster, not isolated A/B task, is the analysis unit.
