## Economic premises

A reference workcell has a use beyond its paid output: it can make a measurement of a later embodiment interpretable in the coordinate system of an earlier archive. But the firm also produces *new* experience in the newly commissioned embodiment. Which reference configuration should remain available is therefore a capital-allocation decision, not simply a depreciation assumption. A physical anchor, a portable measurement representation and a contracted diagnostic are distinct ways to supply the same information, potentially at different dates and costs. Each must compete against ordinary operation that also learns and against alternative original buyer orders. A new anchor can be valuable even if the older archive remains intact; retiring an anchor can leave some archived data economically dormant.

Technical motivation (abstract-only checks 25 November): Stepanova et al., [Automatic self-contained calibration of an industrial dual-arm robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07548), December 2020, report physical dual-arm experiments using multiple complementary calibration methods with laser-tracker comparison; Mu et al., [One-Policy-Fits-All](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14522), March 2026, describe a shared geometry-aware action latent and retargeting decoder across embodiments. These support the possibility of contrasts and portable representations, not the assumed binary model, costs or commercial return. Blackwell comparison of experiments and Chen–Waggoner [Informational Substitutes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08636) are prior foundations. ## Two relational archives, two competing anchors

Let A,B,C denote sequential embodiment conventions, represented by signs. Define D=AB, E=BC; the old-to-current bridge is DE. Let R=X_L A and N=X_N B be the retained task–embodiment relations for two task families. Their current C policy signs are T_L=RDE and T_N=NE. D is not necessarily identified by prior qualification of one task: task methods may differ. The state is the joint belief over (D,E,R,N) and the actually feasible reference routes. For an explicit restricted comparison condition on the inherited history and assume independent signs, E balanced, and means d,r,n for D,R,N in [0,1]. A signed report of its stated contrast has symmetric accuracy q, independent conditional on the sign. Write l=2q-1. The ordinary one-test success probability is 1/2 for both families. Qualification itself is a safe independent local test of the proposed candidate; the model does not permit deployment after failure.

**Proposition: anchor renewal rotates the learning network.** Under those restrictions the one-test optimal probabilities after an old-anchor (DE) or renewed-anchor (E) report are

| | Legacy task L | New task N |
|---|---:|---:|
| Old | (1+r l)/2 | (1+n d l)/2 |
| Renewed | (1+r d l)/2 | (1+n l)/2 |

Proof: both report signs are balanced; conditional means of their reported contrast are +l or -l. For the old anchor, E=D(DE) and D is independent of DE because E is balanced. Thus the conditional target means are r E[DE|report] and n d E[DE|report]. For the new anchor they are r d E[E|report] and n E[E|report]. A sign test succeeds with probability (1+absolute conditional mean)/2 when its candidate is chosen optimally. Except when d=1 or the relevant archive is uninformative, renewal improves N and worsens L. Both experiments can be informative but neither generally dominates. If the drift signs or archive relations are posterior-correlated, recompute using their full joint law. A perfect portable recording of D makes the two relative contrasts equivalent, given matching reliability, because E and DE become invertible recodings. A portable representation that only predicts one task need not do this.

With independently actionable nonoverlapping target opportunities, no binding shared resource, and one incremental test per opportunity, let W_L and W_N sum their incremental captured qualification-contingent service values. Equal bridge costs and reliabilities imply the gross new-minus-old value is

    (1-d) l (n W_N-r W_L)/2.

The sign follows by weighted subtraction; private weights differ from real service surplus. The threshold is a portfolio composition threshold, not a universal technical definition of obsolescence. It holds when qualification probability is the only altered margin and no shared constraint or alternative route intervenes. If one test after the report is all that fits, take the maximum across original eligible order/route pairs of their contribution times the appropriate probability instead of a sum. If one buyer can use either route, never count it twice. Sequential tests share the latent signs and themselves reveal information, so even this maximum is not a formula for multi-test schedules. Under the balanced restriction the unconditional target covariance equals E[T_L T_N]=rdn (both individual means zero). A record containing marginal success only loses the joint information needed to condition later tests. This covariance formula is conditional on the stated independence assumptions, not a universal property of joint qualification.

## Reference renewal, capacity and feedback

The two dates use one common production-value framework. Date 1 has H1 shared shift slots. One accepted A or B source job is performed; the chosen route supplies a lawful report of R or N with state-dependent probability. Both ordinary operations learn. Missingness is observed and its likelihood is retained. The firm can retire, keep A, renew to B if B was used, keep both, or prepare a portable representation. Renewal/portable setup occupies a dated shift and has a separate fee; spare slots go to A service, which also learns. The first site choice is committed before its report; anchor choice is conditional on that report. Existing records remain retained in all arms.

Date 2 has H validation slots and P separately funded commissioning slots; a diagnostic occupies one validation slot and may be skipped. Old and new anchors enable different contrast reports; portable yields noisy reports on D and E within one explicitly assumed slot. Direct external target diagnostics are available at a price to every arm. Both-held offers either contrast in the single preparatory window (not two unlimited reports). All programs can use the same native fallback and decline extra training. There are L-only and N-only original orders and optionally one shared order with either route; each has incremental net contribution relative to its ordinary fallback. Each original order gets at most one test, success permits one optional new installation, and a failed test updates the belief but never enters paid production. A successful test can also be declined in favor of later orders. No later ability to retry expired orders. A model test fully reveals that particular symbolic policy parity, not generic field competence.

For terminal optional C service at date 3, a further lawful record permits one funded installation earning w net of its cost, with ordinary fallback record probability u0 and route-specific useful-record hazards conditional on D. Given the selected counts k_L,k_N and latent state, probability is 1-(1-u0)(1-u_L(D))^k_L(1-u_N(D))^k_N. Conditional independence is assumed only for these new draws. Its expectation uses the actual posterior after the chosen tests and actions; unconditional count products would be invalid. Date-3 service is distinct and expiring. The objective discounts its net contribution once; no separate imputed data revenue. Monetary flows are discounted, counts are not. All financing is already feasible in the stated P slots; an actual dated cash constraint would change the feasible menus.

Let W(b,h) be the optimized value at date 2 including the distinct successor service. With inherited belief b and a fixed date-1 report, the choice of renewal rather than retention compares optimized continuation values after the spare-slot reports of each action, including current shift displacement and fees. Thus, writing a for the anchor action, its value is m_A times spare shifts minus its fee plus beta E[W(b',a)]. The expectation uses the observation law from that action's actual additional routine service. In general this cannot be written as a fixed transfer multiplier times a fleet size. The finite recursion first maximizes over safe local test choices and conditional acceptance, then over optional diagnostics, then anchor choices and source site. It is implementable by a fixed belief-state contingent procedure. Adding an optional renewal route can only increase optimized value, as the old policy is still feasible. It need not increase any particular installation coordinate. The relevant controlled installation response is E[k_j | renewal available]-E[k_j | legacy menu], each under its own optimized policy; this is a finite policy perturbation, not automatically installations per marginal source robot. To form an induced-deployment column, compare a defined one-unit source acquisition with its best feasible counterfactual, using this admission problem and excluding descendants from that column. The common network's local coefficients then change with reference choice and archive state; no constant positive matrix is implied.

**Ownership boundary.** If an outside route can reproduce each owned reference experiment with identical conditional information, lawful reuse, available dates and capacity occupation, and no greater cost, replace the owned experiment by that outside route at every history. By backward induction the resulting attainable continuation is at least as large, and positive carrying/setup costs make redundant ownership unnecessary (if no separate service or residual value). Equivalence of average accuracy alone does not imply this result. Nor does a portable decoder automatically reproduce the joint conditional report law. A reference body with independent production use must be compared as a joint asset, not charged twice.

The first two examples hold technical inputs fixed and change P only. With tight commissioning, choosing renewal raises total date-2 installations; with room for two, the same diagnostic consumes one of the two validation slots that the legacy program can use for two tests. Renewal raises value but can reduce total starts. The C probability column is a potential funded successor under the stated hazard law, counted in value only when w>0; when w=0 it is a reported eligibility probability, not a claim of actual investment. The shared original-order case maintains exclusivity by an order bitmask. In the 48 configurations, 18 had strictly positive renewal premium and positive renewal incidence. No first-site reversal found in this grid; no claim that every proposed recursion occurs. Equivalent external old/new contrasts at the same date-2 cost and no ownership preparation exactly remove the renewal advantage in the checked case: 16.7635 in both programs (portable prohibitively expensive in this ablation).

These are expected commissioned *robot routes*, not additional final physical service if human fallback satisfies the same order. MPK_r at fixed controller and commissioned stock is the separately defined vector of accepted original service differences after dispatch. Real surplus requires actual avoided resources and customer benefits, not the captured contribution table. A change in private weights from rivalry can rotate the preferred anchor even at fixed technical probabilities; the price response remains to be solved, not assumed.

## Empirical discriminator and next question

Measure a matched cross-vintage contrast panel: preserve the same source archive; compare A-retained, B-renewed, portable and independent external contrasts before a common cutoff. Track two separate target families whose archived task relations were learned on different hardware. Record hardware/control lineage, rights, diagnostic likelihoods including missing reports, all original overlapping offers and safe qualification outcomes, actual validation occupancy, commissioned orders and ordinary fallback. Track displaced paid work at source and successor evidence by actual service route. Randomize at the shared crew/release group if feasible. A shift toward new-family performance accompanied by old-family losses under equal diagnostic reliability supports the rotating-anchor mechanism; a cheap portable mapping that preserves both families at matched resource use rejects the physical-anchor advantage. Repetitive handling, multistage manufacturing and sparse-event inspection differ in task diversity, recurrence and available reference contrasts; no common numeric parameter is inferred. The cited technical studies do not report this joint economic panel.

Next: characterize when multiple sequential reference links and portable measurement representations preserve decision-relevant joint beliefs under uncertain common drift, with a finite validation budget and rival access. Determine whether common external calibration can reduce private reference investment while raising total accepted service.
