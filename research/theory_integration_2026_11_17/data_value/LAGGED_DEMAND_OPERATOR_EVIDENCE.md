# Prospective field evidence: lagged demand, shared learning and recovery

**22 November 2026. Prospective design only; no actual customer, robot, operator, legal permission, approved randomization or cleared payment data was accessed.** Companion to [the derived conditional result](LAGGED_DEMAND_LEARNING_AND_OPERATOR_OCCUPANCY.md).

**Comparison/exposure unit:** a physically disconnected cluster of original buyers, sites, learner checkpoints/broadcast domain, embodied robot fleet, dispatch queue and shared rescue/teleoperation workers. Draw the actual adjacency graph before defining randomization units. If two ostensible clusters share a common training update, human recovery, customer orders or supply constraints, the no-interference design is invalid. The comparator is complete production P versus deliberate target D versus physical/independent recovery E, with retained legally executable old R controller.

**Before intervention / date 0:** signed P/D/E program scope; qualified old A recipe and per-buyer prices, physical safety evidence, operator rescue capacity and lawful rights. Register **all** original buyer offers/declines/cancellations (not just robot-served episodes), timestamp, buyer/site/task, service window and preoffer exposure; pilot success/failure and accepted paid receipt, subsequent original buyer willingness to offer and whether A/B orders overlap. Register field trajectory vs failure/recovery vs manual teleoperation vs simulated, synthetic and pretrained inputs separately, plus provenance, consent, retention, curation/compute, embodied hardware, checkpoint and target grounding. Validate target B qualification using a predeclared safe matched service recipe.

**At date 1:** collect A/B original offer panel including refused/no capacity events, Q_B qualification and precisely when the customer was informed, lawful order-report delivery before dispatch, dispatch selection/recovery, paid settlement/refund and excluded work. Observe whether data generation changes **future customer demand** conditional on pilot success rather than inferring demand solely from robot completion. Track operator/recovery time in physical slots and all coupled sites, including E independent second-slot certification.

**At date 2:** measure whether B productive accepted service actually creates legally reusable data that qualifies new C, with a held-out original target-service acceptance test, not benchmark percentage proxy; observe full C buyer offers **including no-orders**, A standing offers, robot and human recovery H, scheduled service overlap, safety, customer settlement, all true training and incident costs. Timing: Q_C, H and C order jointly precede the production action that earns its receipt; post-dispatch reports cannot be used as predispatch policy signals.

**Primary estimand:** discounted net cleared original-buyer whole-network J_P, J_D, J_E (including pilot receipt and every real resource cost), not the proportion of successful selected robot episodes. Diagnostic identified or bounded joint probabilities: Pr(Q_B, O_B, not-O_A) and Pr(Q_C,H | productive B). Keep separately B technical lift, lawfully usable source/target data, original buyer market-demand feedback, displacement of A service, recovery/operator scarcity and verified independent E simultaneous capacity. If actual buyer receipt/signature data are absent, label estimates unpopulated and investment ranking unidentified.

**Discriminating interventions:** connected-network assignment where legally/physically approved; alternatively clearly stated structural conditional exchangeability and interference restrictions, instrument/exposure support and joint-kernel sensitivity. Match service recipe, time window and buyer prior histories where possible. Observe pilot outcome and customer information exposure jointly: differentiates market feedback from latent seasonal demand; randomizing robot episodes within a common model/dispatcher does not. For Q_C/H coupling, record resource schedules even for no dispatch and failure; only measured per-event qualification and gross operator utilization cannot distinguish opposite date-1 optimal policies.

**Refusal/rights/independence audit:** no consent -> event not a lawful training input; no signed C qualification -> do not infer from model benchmark; no observed unserved buyers -> three-way Fréchet bounds cannot be populated empirically; no certified E recovery capacity -> do not remove the one-slot constraint. Optional R must remain physically executable, not merely written in a policy manifest. Inability to support any arm leaves J comparison unidentified rather than zero.

**Prior-art validation:** Wang et al. (May 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00416) and Yu et al. (October 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25736) report technical fleet adaptation with real robots; no customer-cleared order-response data from these papers were used or assumed. DDA §5 / App B already examine future data value and aggregation. BEA/BLS 2025 sector-output totals previously recorded are macro context, not interindustry flow coefficients or robotics opportunity sizes.
