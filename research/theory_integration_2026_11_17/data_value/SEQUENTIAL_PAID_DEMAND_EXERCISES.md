# Exercises — sequential production and data value

1. A client A has one remaining unit of demand capacity. At t2 a robot serves C, receiving c immediately and consuming C's lifetime one-unit budget. A t2 incident J2 arrives after the support worker's clearance window. At t3 A originally orders with indicator D and pays v if accepted when J2=0. Derive sharp bounds on the value of serving C given marginals d=Pr(D=1) and j=Pr(J2=1), with *no independence assumption*. Find conditions under which the fixed t2 serve policy dominates safe skip, whose t3 original A-order probability is d0.

2. Build two joint laws with d=j=1/2 and equal t2 C receipts but opposite paid rankings against an independent embodiment worth 9/4. Explain why R customer invoices and even correct independent G demand/incident marginals do not recover the missing event.

3. Suppose L2=1 forbids all t2 production. The worker clears one existing job in t2. A separate t2 validator can qualify either A or C for t3. Exactly one buyer offers an order, equally likely, worth 4 if qualified. A lawful t2 signal perfectly forecasts the eventual buyer. Price the signal. What changes if validation requires the same blocked worker, or the report arrives after the validation deadline?

4. Suppose original A3 offers are observed only when paid. Explain why M=1{paid} need not satisfy conditional missing-at-random. State a positive lawful randomized audit design that allows E[D_A3|H,u] to be estimated by inverse probability weighting. Does auditing R alone identify untried G t2 service effects?

5. Show that adding freely available optional source records cannot reduce the maximum feasible J if all old policies, costs and rights remain unchanged. Give a counterexample where forced training on that source does reduce realized value, and explain why a second price on the same future production gain double-counts data.

6. Extend the model to two candidate robots with a shared recovery worker. Explain why independently minimizing each stage's uncertain event kernel can select combinations incompatible with one common latent law, and propose a joint sensitivity / physical-rights audit instead.
