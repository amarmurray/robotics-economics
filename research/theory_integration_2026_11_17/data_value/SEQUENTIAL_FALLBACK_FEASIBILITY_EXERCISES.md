# Student exercises — sequential fallback, qualification and economic value

**Synthetic mathematics only. Work independently before reading separate solutions.**

1. An original network has `r=1/2` old-policy qualification, `x=3/4` source-trained first-attempt qualification, and `b=1/8` after permissible retry failure. Find the sequential qualification rate, and the **sharp** possible original R-success regression interval without paired information. Explain why retaining R weights does not prove the regression is zero.

2. In the X-failure stratum `k=1/4`, suppose a validated common-initial-state design establishes `a=1/8` old R-success/X-failure mass, with `b=1/8`. Calculate the refined regression interval. Repeat for `a=1/4`. What additional engineering claim would certify zero physical regression on every original eligible case?

3. In the nested eight-history market example, an original R order has captured contribution 4, and R qualifies on four of eight equal original opportunities. F qualifies on seven, but costs two additional normalized per-original-opportunity units for source/curation/recovery. Compare net F versus R if every buyer still accepts. Then compare with cost zero but F customers pay only on histories 1–3. Why does a positive safe-qualification gain not identify a cash gain? Discuss where production-generated retry data enter a two-date J.
