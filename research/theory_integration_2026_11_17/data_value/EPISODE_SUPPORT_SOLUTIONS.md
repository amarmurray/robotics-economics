# Instructor solutions — episode support and qualified production

**1.** With one source and fixed eligibility, `p=(9/10,1/10)`. With genuinely implementable equal episode weights, `p=(1/2,1/2)`. The latter does not generate another independent B, fix a wrong semantic label, change physical certification or establish transfer. The pinned raw converter applies a date-dependent latency shift before checking `traj_len`, so count processed transitions after alignment.

**2.** Failure to draw both classes means all A or all B; disjoint events, hence `1-a^3-(1-a)^3=3a(1-a)`. At `a=9/10` it is `27/100`; at `a=1/2`, `3/4`. Interpreting this as qualification requires the explicit counterfactual learner map and separate held-out, physically permitted joint-service certification.

**3.** Choose one of three A slots, then ordered distinct B slots have mass `(1/2)^2-9(1/18)^2=2/9`. Thus `3*(1/2)*(2/9)=1/3`. With a single B family the distinct-B mass is zero irrespective of sampler weights.

**4.** `V=2+10Q-cost` for each training option, E fixed `19/5`. Context presence gives baseline `47/10`, G `91/10`, X `81/10`, E `19/5`: G wins. Novel-family gives R/baseline `2`, G `8/5`, X `59/15`, E `19/5`: X wins narrowly by `2/15` over E. The continuation contribution is already in 2/12; no extra data premium. With free optional availability, old feasible training/policy remains and the max cannot shrink. Mandatory retraining may have a worse realized return.

**5.** `Q_N=3a(b^2-Σ_Bp_j^2)`. Cauchy gives `Σ_Bp_j^2≥b^2/m_B`; concentrated B implies `Σ_Bp_j^2≤b^2`. Therefore `Q_N∈[0,3ab^2(1-1/m_B)]`. At a=b=1/2 and m_B=9 it is `[0,1/3]`. X's possible net is `[2-7/5, 2+10/3-7/5]=[3/5,59/15]`, crossing E `19/5`. The within-B collision mass `Σ_Bp_j^2` together with a and b identifies Q_N **under this learner map**; semantic independence/family validity still needs observation.

**6.** Achieved K depends on treatment, labeling, proposal cost and source difficulty; conditioning on it may open selection differences. Fixed-K randomization assigns the entire feasible stopping policy, including rejected proposals and budget. Randomize connected learner/controller update and shared operator/customer clusters, with physical R/X/G/E approval. Record full original buyer price/offer/refusal, joint task qualification and capacity, accepted service, paid receipts/refunds and resource displacement. A benchmark score alone never identifies production contribution.
