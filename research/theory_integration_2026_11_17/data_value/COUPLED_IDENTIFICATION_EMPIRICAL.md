# Coupled source-to-customer identification — prospective matched evidence protocol

**18 November 2026.** No robot data, private customer access or intervention is available or claimed. Companion [theory](COUPLED_IDENTIFICATION_AND_AUDIT.md) and [proof ledger](COUPLED_IDENTIFICATION_PROOF_LEDGER.md). This adds a *decision-relevant joint measurement* to the existing [policy-dependent demand protocol](ENDOGENOUS_DEMAND_EMPIRICAL_DESIGN.md).

## Which quantity is unmeasured?

The table in the theory uses a persistent aligned-transfer indicator H within each independent learning pool and a **pre-action** target demand/opportunity stratum D. The missing quantity is their same-population joint frequency t=P(H,D), coupled to how route R/G/E/S changes **future** orders, complete service qualification and recovery. Marginal H-test accuracy, marginal D counts and average robot recovery are not sufficient to reconstruct this joint relationship or claim a robust R/G investment ranking.

The fully general counterpart is not simply t. Record, for each initial eligible site/pool and policy, the *joint response type*: (qualified controller/embodiment, future customer orders including zero and cancellations, whole-service joint acceptance, safe attempted-state support, peak/operator resource and fallback), under one consistent version/time. A structured identified set is acceptable when outcomes are not observed; do not implicitly fill missing alternatives with independent draws.

## Observational unit, assignment and outcomes

- **Unit:** initially eligible commercial customer opportunity attached to a controller-learning pool; freeze time window and initial accepted-service contract. Pool rather than task is the statistical unit where learning spills across tasks.
- **Pre-action registry:** task/service requirements and joint qualification rubric, baseline D, customer price/capture and competitor/specialist outside option, site/robot body, rights, training/curation, approved recovery caps and operator availability. A D marker measured only after successful deployment is **not** baseline D.
- **Assignment when ethically authorized:** R productive field rollout, G deliberate target grounding, E embodiment intervention or S existing qualified alternative, randomized or credibly assigned over independent pools. Keep precommitted full policies, version lineage and abort rules explicit. Record intention-to-treat and per-protocol cautiously; no unsafe missing-positivity experiment.
- **Learning:** distinguish routine field trajectories; false-negative intervention gates; operator corrections; failure/recovery data; purposeful teleoperation; simulator experience; synthetic/public pretraining. Store controller policy retention and which data rights actually permit transfer.
- **Future demands:** register all eligible customer opportunities even with zero follow-on orders, declines, lost orders and cancellations. Record accepted prices, capture, specialist substitutions, customer site and timing. Never reweight by posttreatment successful orders as though orders were pre-action.
- **Accepted services:** held-out whole-service 00/01/10/11 joint acceptance, proof of safe route feasibility, peak recovery/remaining budget on actual attempted paths, aborted/denied target states and fallback. Report service hours and scarce robot/operator resources independently of benchmark technical rates.

## One potentially discriminating report, with explicit validation obligations

A binary safe transfer report Z can narrow the joint frequency only if there is **independent** evidence that its sensitivity s and false positive f to a well-defined qualified transfer regime H are stable *across pre-action D strata*. Then \(m=P(Z=1,D=1)=fP(D)+(s-f)t\). A safe bench test, permitted held-out target validation or historical site-level test can potentially supply Z; a simulation-only success is **not** by itself an established field-invariant H label. Separately validate the missed-error gate and recovery distribution; do not train/test on the same episodes and label a correction a full-service qualification.

A **synthetic illustration** uses s=.9, f=.1, P(D)=.5 and m in [.34,.40], giving t in [.3625,.4375] and R−G captured-contribution lower 0.625 within that model. This result is **not evidence** of any real robotics accuracy, opportunity share or commercial value. Unknown calibration or D-dependent errors require an identified-set inversion over all admissible (s,f,m) rather than point substitution; if s=f, Z provides zero information about t. Conditional independence is an empirical hypothesis.

For Bernoulli audited \((Z=1,D=1)\) events across independent pools with known report calibration, Hoeffding permits a finite-sample confidence radius. The toy n>=289 calculation assumes exact hypothetical mean .37 and fixed s/f; a real study must account for within-pool clustering, zero-order attrition, response-type heterogeneity, report generation, repeated controller updates, denominator errors and multiplicity. An audited target H label might not be ethically or operationally available. No study is authorized by this protocol.

## Safety and alternative controls

A diagnostic is **not operationally allowed** merely because expected operator/recovery use is low: positive-support peak 3 when remaining authorized budget is 2 invalidates an attempted action under the program's hard-support criterion. Pre-registered abort/fallback before exceeding capacity may create a genuinely safe, possibly less informative alternative. Report report×high-recovery support or clearly mark it unidentified; finite zero sightings cannot certify zero support. Do not assume approval for physical testing from statistical sufficiency.

Include a genuine qualified S, old controller retain/no-retrain arm, E revised body and R free production-generated byproduct. Keep deliberate G's foregone service, compute, curation, access and rights costs. No double count of future data value: it changes the distribution of Q and future accepted V; it is not an independent cash receipt. Distinguish objective of maximin captured contribution from minimax opportunity regret: they can imply different allocations and different gross value of a population report.

## Analysis and falsification

(a) Estimate randomized **route means** on the registered target opportunity population if assignment/positivity and full follow-up permit. Marginal potential-outcome covariance is *not* necessary for the mean contrast. Report target-site transport limits and missing support before proclaiming a causal estimate.

(b) For the joint law where not all regimes/actions are observed, build a consistent response-type moment matrix A and compute same-law pairwise lower/upper contribution using a *single* q across all policies and histories. Record observable moments that support each dual certificate. Do not treat an LP outer relaxation as physically sharp if conditional kernel restrictions (independent sensors, site interference, controller retention) are nonlinear.

(c) Independent test of whether R changes visited state support and future orders rather than only lowering intervention rates; deliberately and safely vary source mix, target alignment and embodiment where permitted. Pre-specify physical held-out customer acceptance and real recovery resources; this directly tests the transfer-to-production mapping instead of dollarizing benchmark improvement.

(d) Look for outright falsifiers: changing s/f with D or robot version; specialist already meeting orders; target-only controller beating the transfer route; no incremental grounding benefit under useful free R data; pooled R/G fleet learning that defeats fractional additivity; a previously unmeasured recovery tail; the order registry systematically omitting canceled/zero customers.
