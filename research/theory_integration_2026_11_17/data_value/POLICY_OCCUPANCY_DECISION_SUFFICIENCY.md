# Policy-occupancy decision sufficiency for recursive paid robotic production

**22 November 2026.** Continuation of *When Capabilities Become Production*, within the November 22 Model A/B synthesis. The scientific acceptance gate remains **OPEN**. The theorem below is a conditional finite-horizon dynamic-programming identity and a candidate application to robotics economics, **not** independently established mathematical novelty, measured paid robotic production, or a certified robot policy.

## Why this is the next step

The coupled original-buyer paid-Q chapter asks when matched learner/checkpoint, operator, rights and buyer data justify narrowing an economically feasible joint response set. The one-step decision-refinement chapter observes that hiding histories is harmless at one decision if they admit a common continuation-optimal lawful action. Strong full-state quotient theorems instead require equal legal menus, expected returns and projected transition laws for *every* action. These are very different standards. We need the intermediate, genuinely **recursive and policy-specific** test: what must a compressed buyer/learner/rights record preserve for the *optimized whole-program* source-plus-grounding (SG) versus new embodiment (E) decision?

This chapter answers without making source data a homogeneous scalar, inventing future demand, treating technical benchmarks as paid returns, or adding a second future-data premium. It explains why identical safe first-stage actions can mask large later losses if licensed buyer reports are discarded, and when a weak empirical record can only bound—rather than choose between—investments.

## 1. Complete-history economic environment

For each investment program k in {R,S,G,SG,E}, fix a finite horizon H, initial original eligible-buyer population and capital/operating cost accounting. Full **available information** history h_t includes observable buyer orders and no-orders, offered prices, accepted and refunded service, actual cleared settlement, learner checkpoint/source lineage, licensed rights, physical robot embodiment and status, operator and queue capacity, failure/recovery state and permitted training/dispatch observations. It does **not** include an unobserved buyer need simply because an econometrician knows there is a latent buyer.

At h_t let M_k(h_t) be the physically approved, legally permitted action menu. An action may dispatch an existing retained controller, perform safe production or teleoperation that generates data, acquire a deliberate target report, train a source model, recover, or allocate a new qualified embodiment. The complete contingent law P_k(h_{t+1}|h_t,a_t) may depend on earlier endogenous production data, customer order response, shared fleets and rights. Period contribution r_k(h_t,a_t,h_{t+1}) includes **only actually qualified, accepted and finally cleared service** and once-counted production, operating, compute/curation, teleoperation, rights and coordination costs. Full future qualified services generated by learning are in later rewards, not appended as an independent data-value term. Upfront I_k is deducted once if not already in period rewards.

For a finite lawful full-history policy class Π_k, define
\[
V^*_{k,t}(h)=\max_{a\in M_k(h)}Q^*_{k,t}(h,a),\quad
Q^*_{k,t}(h,a)=\mathbb E_k[r_{k,t}+V^*_{k,t+1}(H_{t+1})\mid h,a],\quad V^*_{k,H}=0.
\]
Full net investment value is J^*_k=E[V^*_{k,0}(H_0)]-I_k. The expectation is taken over the same declared original eligible-market cohort when comparing k, but its **program-induced subsequent demand** is allowed to differ by program.

A compressed decision record φ_{k,t}(h_t), e.g. pooled quality and a licensed-data count without actual buyer order, defines a subset Π_k^φ of history-dependent policies measurable with respect to the retained record (including clock and the controller's legitimate remembered actions). It must choose an action legal in **every underlying full history where it may be deployed**; where menu intersections are empty the abstract controller is not admissible until the record is refined or a signed common safe action is supplied. Let \(\widetilde J_k=\sup_{\mu\in\Pi_k^\phi}E_\mu[\sum r_k]-I_k\) and \(R_k=J_k^*-\widetilde J_k\ge0\). The old policy remains available wherever physically and legally valid. Mere free *availability* of optional raw data cannot reduce the maximum over a nested feasible policy class; forced updates and costly acquisitions can.

## 2. Exact adaptive occupancy-regret identity

**Proposition (complete-history Bellman occupancy).** For every lawful compressed policy μ and finite H, define the full-information Bellman shortfall
\[
g_{k,t}(h,a)=V^*_{k,t}(h)-Q^*_{k,t}(h,a)\ge0.
\]
Then exactly
\[
J_k^* - J_k(\mu)
=\mathbb E_{\mu,k}\left[\sum_{t=0}^{H-1}
g_{k,t}(H_t,A_t)\right],\tag{1}
\]
where both terms use the same initial market distribution and I_k cancels. The expectation is under **μ's own induced histories**, including all buyer demand and data-learning transitions that μ changes. If Π_k^φ is finite, R_k equals (1) evaluated at its best compressed policy \(\widetilde\mu_k\).

*Proof.* Write \(g=V^*_t(h)-E[r_t+V^*_{t+1}(H_{t+1})\mid h,a]\). Integrate under μ, sum t, telescope the intermediate V* terms, use terminal V*_H=0, and subtract expected μ rewards. The initial full V* remains. No independence between qualifications, source types, buyer demands, rights, or operators is used. Exactness requires the action to be lawful and the expectation to use its actual controlled kernel. Q* must include optimal future responses—not a one-step benchmark or a return observed under an arbitrary fixed fallback. QED.

**Corollary (reachable-action criterion).** R_k=0 iff there exists a lawful compressed policy μ such that, at every decision time, its chosen action lies in \(\arg\max_{a\in M_k(H_t)}Q^*_{k,t}(H_t,a)\) for μ-almost every *reached* full-information history. For randomized μ every chosen action with positive conditional probability must be optimal. With finite menus this follows from nonnegative summands and existence of an optimal compressed policy.

The qualifier "μ-reached" is essential. The set of every *conceivable* history sharing a dashboard row need not have a common optimum if mutually conflicting histories can never both occur under an optimal compressed policy. Conversely, knowing a common optimal action at the **first** decision does not preserve later performance after production-generated learning, demand signals, rights changes and failures. The requirement is dynamic and program-specific.

For a policy-independent global dashboard guarantee, stronger controlled-kernel/rights/menu equivalence is sufficient; the present criterion instead permits lossless compression under a single original market and particular optimizing policies even if nonselected actions have different kernels. It does not transport automatically to another buyer mix, another source modality, another embodiment or a changed contract/approval regime.

## 3. An investment decision with missing observation—and no technical negative transfer

**Synthetic original-buyer reference table.** Two original buyer types A and B have 1/2 probability each. At t=0 the retained safe "pilot" service is itself finally accepted and paid, yielding 1/5 contribution, and—only if licensed target reporting G_info has been acquired—produces a correct buyer-order report for the next dispatch. The alternative is to skip. At t=1 the retained recipe a clears contribution 1 only for A. Source training S qualifies an additional recipe b clearing 1 only for B; old a remains free and lawful. A new E embodiment qualifies a generalist e clearing 3/4 under either buyer. Each has one operator service slot; service values already net period operating costs. These are **stipulated normalized customer contributions**, not benchmark-percent conversions or observed prices. To isolate information, the pilot's report does not itself change customer demand, rights or payoffs. G_info is not physical target alignment G_train.

The two-date complete-history optimization gives gross retained R, S-only and G_info-only values 7/10; SG with the buyer report gives 6/5; E gives 19/20. Assume once-counted incremental SG cost 1/5 and E cost 1/20. Thus **full** J_SG=1, J_E=9/10, so Δ*=+1/10. A dashboard that hides the licensed buyer report—but still allows every physical action—gives compressed \(\widetilde J_{SG}=1/2\), \(\widetilde J_E=9/10\), incorrectly ranking E with \(\widetilde\Delta=-2/5\). At t=0 both SG policies take the same paid pilot: the coarsening loss arises **entirely at t=1**, where true A- and B-reports require incompatible optimal dispatch actions a and b. In source+report SG the report-discard regret R_SG=1/2, while E's generalist e is a common optimal action and R_E=0. No learned source recipe is destroyed and no negative transfer is inferred. The report has an economic value *conditional on the learned menu*.

Equation (1) gives the exact correction \(\Delta^*=\widetilde\Delta + R_{SG}-R_E=-2/5+1/2=1/10\). The paid pilot is counted once as period production; its information value is not added to the full optimization. The experiment cannot justify any actual physical SG/E ranking: signed menus, target rights, buyer responses and shared-policy transition support are all fictional.

**Counterexample to a blanket common-action test.** Consider a prior lawful choice that selects a productive branch and makes one of two later hidden histories unreachable. The two later histories may have incompatible optimal actions and be stored in the same summary, yet a compressed policy that commits to the productive branch and acts optimally there loses nothing. Hence requiring common argmax across *all conceivable histories* is sufficient but not necessary for program-specific sufficiency; their occupancy under the candidate policy matters.

**Counterexample to safe-only observational inference.** Suppose only the retained a action was dispatched at both buyer types and the licensed report was discarded. Its observed paid success and pilot receipt are identical in the report-using and report-blind counterworlds; the missing counterfactual b dispatch under B supports a loss anywhere within its legitimate paid-Q bounds. The identical safe logs alone do not identify the coarsening regret or SG/E sign. An approval-uncertain b is not coded as an observed missing-zero treatment; it is excluded from lawful menus until certified.

## 4. A robust ranking certificate with incomplete joint Q evidence

**Proposition (program-specific coarsening bounds).** Suppose empirically or structurally justified bounds yield \(0\le R_{SG}\le U_{SG}\) and \(0\le R_E\le U_E\) for the **same original eligible market and complete paid horizon**, and \(\widetilde\Delta=\widetilde J_{SG}-\widetilde J_E\). Then
\[
\widetilde\Delta-U_E\ \le\ J^*_{SG}-J^*_E\
\le\ \widetilde\Delta+U_{SG}.\tag{2}
\]
A strictly positive lower bound establishes SG preference; a strictly negative upper bound establishes E preference. The interval is an **outer** bound and need not be jointly sharp, because a shared learner/operator/rights network may couple SG and E regrets. No cross-world independence, shared scalar quality, or new hypothetical E action menu is implicit.

*Proof.* Substitute \(J_k^*=\widetilde J_k+R_k\), and maximize/minimize the difference over the stated nonnegative bounds. QED.

A conditional way to obtain U_k is to certify action-conditional *whole-horizon paid* Q intervals for each **lawful** action at each reachable full history:
\[
g_{k,t}(h,a)\le \max_{b\in M_k(h)}\overline Q_{k,t}(h,b)-\underline Q_{k,t}(h,a)=:\bar g_{k,t}(h,a).\tag{3}
\]
For a specified lawful compressed candidate policy, an audited upper bound on its own occupancy yields \(J_k^*-J_k(\mu)\le\sum_{t,h,a}\bar\rho_{\mu,k}(t,h,a)\bar g_{k,t}(h,a)\). This bounds R_k as well because \(\widetilde J_k\ge J_k(\mu)\). If only a uniform worst-history bound exists, use that, not a fabricated occupancy estimate. A data set from only an old logging policy does **not** certify occupancy under μ. Histories with new learner-generated qualified actions, adaptive recovery, changed customer demand, new embodiments or rights expirations must appear in the envelope, not be silently assigned zero.

In the synthetic case, knowing only \(R_{SG}\in[0,1/2]\) and \(R_E=0\) converts the misleading coarse point contrast \(-2/5\) to the valid outer range \([-2/5,+1/10]\): **unidentified**. Knowing the exact conditional Bellman gaps recovers +1/10. An empirical benchmark score or an unjustified one-factor paid-Q model cannot bridge that difference.

## 5. What to observe, what to split and what not to assume

A field design must identify or conservatively bound (i) the original eligible buyer/order/no-order and offer population, (ii) full-information report availability at *each* actual decision, (iii) same shared checkpoint lineage and legally trainable/retainable sources, (iv) physically approved action-menu intersections at every aliased history, (v) shared operator, robot and recovery capacities, (vi) action-conditional qualified buyer acceptance and final settlement plus downstream production, and (vii) the **compressed policy's own occupancy** including policy-induced demand. At minimum split a dashboard row whenever it contains alternative lawful actions with materially different paid-Q rankings and both hidden histories are reachable under a policy relevant to SG/E. Rights or physical-menu conflicts require splitting *before* optimizing a fictitious common policy.

Source families stay distinct: retained field successful episodes; field failures and recovery demonstrations; teleoperation; simulation; synthetic data; public/pretrained material. Target G_info reveals market or context signals; G_train physically qualifies learned actions and may alter menus; E introduces genuine embodiment/throughput/footprint options. Production-generated observation competes for scarce robot/operator slots and may also earn present paid service, so value it inside J. Forced training may have negative returns even if optional free raw data would be harmless. Future learning-generated embodiments, cross-site pooling and digital coordination remain in the controlled transition law, not in an extra premium.

## 6. Relative position in the integrated theory and prior art

Model A (fixed qualified recipes and original buyer demand) can compute or bound per-history paid-Q using the existing production LP and scarce robot/operator resource dual. Complementary qualification bundles and shared constraints can make Q nonseparable. Model B (endogenous data, demand, learning, qualification, manufactured embodiments and rights) instead requires full histories and admissible transition support. The present result unifies the economic contrast while **not** requiring an unvalidated scalar source-transfer coefficient or independence of qualifications.
