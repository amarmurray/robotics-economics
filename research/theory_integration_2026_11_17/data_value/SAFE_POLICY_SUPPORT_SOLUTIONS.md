# Instructor-only solutions — safe support and network qualification economics

Separate from [student exercises](SAFE_POLICY_SUPPORT_EXERCISES.md). Every statement is conditional on the stated illustrative assumptions; no observed robotics economics.

1. Law of total expectation gives sum_d w_d[e_d delta_E,d+(1-e_d)delta_U,d]. With each unsupported p and s outcome in [L,U], delta_U,d in [-(U-L), U-L]. Summing yields SS1. Sharpness follows by assigning unobserved potential outcomes to opposite endpoints without changing the measured eligible response law. Structural transition, demand or monotonicity assumptions can narrow it. A physically unapproved p is not made admissible by a mathematically bounded hypothetical outcome.
2. Covered A=(3/5)(3/10)+(2/5)(1/4)(1/10)=19/100. Uncovered share=(2/5)(3/4)=3/10. Max absolute unknown effect is (3/10)(2)=3/5, so worldwide interval is [-41/100,79/100]. If specialist unsupported mean is 1, p∈[0,2] gives delta_U∈[-1,1], hence [-11/100,49/100]. Both span zero: even perfect enrolled observations do not rank worldwide p.
3. On E=0 both H policies deploy F, so their effect is exactly zero; on E=1 they differ by p−s. No cross-pool controller/demand/operator influence and unchanged treatment environment give J(H_p)-J(H_s)=19/100. Comparing H_p to global S instead adds the separate outside effect J(F)-J(S), which is not implicitly zero.
4. Before added c, lower=19/100−2/25=11/100. With c=3/20, lower=11/100−3/20=−1/25; strict dominance cannot be certified. If Gamma is unconstrained, even the initial 19/100 is an experimentally identified *isolated* mixture contrast, not a bound for a globally connected rollout.
5. G first yields 9 and creates next-period capability for R=13, giving 22; R→R yields 10+10=20. The following R period runs on a **post-G qualified controller**, not the initial R controller, so 13 versus 9 is not an initial-policy comparison. R→G gives 19 in this horizon and makes the sequence issue explicit.
6. Shared dispatcher changes the allocation of an otherwise identical order: A gains one (+1), B loses one (−1), total zero. A-only measurement reports fictitious new system output. Assign the complete dispatch+customer network or justify an explicit exposure map plus residual spillover accounting; ML independence alone does not suffice.
