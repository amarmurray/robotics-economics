# Joint stochastic recovery load and licensed successor value

24 November 2026. Continuation of [learning-induced resource incidence](RESOURCE_INCIDENCE_TRANSPORT.md), in *When Capabilities Become Production*. Learned controllers may reduce each robot's mean recovery demand while synchronizing residual demands that destroy safe pooling. The joint execution also determines which reusable data lineages actually enter the successor learning state.

## Conditional scenario resource certificate

At a lawful history h, a finite structural action catalogue C(h) specifies job commitments and contingent recovery policies, which may depend only on observations available at execution time. Action a and disturbance z induce dated resource loads A_k(h,a,z), capacities b_k(h,a,z), accepted net receipts r(h,a,z), and successor h'(h,a,z). Load may be nonlinear in joint action: overlapping recovery, inspection, procurement and shared operators need not be additive. A certified conservative conditional support S(h,a) must cover physically relevant disturbances, not merely observed positive frequencies. Safe actions satisfy A_k<=b_k on every supported disturbance, together with qualification, rights, viable recovery and structural constraints. If actual overflow can be safely handled, include the authorized recourse and its costs; a service probability cannot by itself authorize a violation.

For a bounded continuation potential F, let W(h,a)=E[r+beta F(h')|h,a]. For any nonnegative scenario prices lambda, define
    U(h)=max over a in C(h) [W(h,a)-sum_{k,z in S(h,a)} lambda_{k,z}(A_k-b_k)].
Every safe action's penalty term is nonpositive, so U>=max_safe W. Action-dependent nonnegative prices also give a valid upper bound if all safe actions are included. Unsafe outcomes may use a justified optimistic extension of W, not booked as receipts. A safe relaxed maximizer with zero priced slack certifies equality. Alternatively replace each signed violation with its positive part (A_k-b_k)_+; this gives a different upper relaxation, exact with sufficiently large penalties on every unsafe action in a finite bounded catalogue. Proof: safe actions then have zero penalty and each unsafe action can be penalized below the feasible optimum. Ordinary signed prices need not be exact: the empty schedule can earn a fictitious slack credit, a negative result retained in the executable check. Neither penalty makes an unsafe maximizer executable.

If terminal F_T dominates terminal receipts and F_t>=U_t at every supported history, backward induction bounds the optimized safe finite-horizon value. Adding upper residual epsilon_t per step gives an upper error sum discounted by beta^t; this follows by recursively conditioning on h,a. A lower certificate instead exhibits a single safe nonanticipating policy and evaluates its accepted-service trajectory. License and data value enter once via actual successor h' and continuation, never as a second premium. Free optional data with retained safe policy and unchanged receipts cannot reduce gross optimized value, while training costs and forced retirement may.

For unknown common whole-program law P in M, use a supported union of disturbances at each common information history for the admissible policy class Pi. The same policy must work across laws; sup_pi inf_P J(P,pi)<=inf_P sup_pi J(P,pi). A policy chosen separately for each hidden P is an upper relaxation. A fixed common safe witness yields inf_P J(P,pi), preserving coupled laws across actions and times. Rectangularizing conditionals can loosen certificates and should be labeled, not assumed empirically valid.

## Exact two-controller example and sharp overlap bounds

Two distinct original orders each pay 4 net if accepted. Retained controllers each require one shared worker recovery slot, so only one order can execute; R=4. Procurement permits both. G costs 1, retains R and qualifies two variants with binary worker load L_i in {0,1} each having mean 1/2. Both orders can run only if L_1+L_2<=1. All required recovery is included, no additional backup. A lawful report before dispatch may certify their joint load. If both accepted jobs execute, a joint licensed successor bundle generates a separate future qualified customer service worth 2 net if H=1. H is observed after execution, includes rights, subsequent held-out qualification and distinct authorization, with the later capacity and costs accounted for. Singleton execution does not generate this bundle in this toy. H is potential under dual execution; its marginal on blocked paths is not observed.

When L_1,L_2 are reported before dispatch, dispatch both if feasible, otherwise one. Define p=P(L_1=L_2=1), q=P(H=1,L_1+L_2<=1). Optional G increment equals max(0,4(1-p)+2q-1). X instead increases retained net receipts by 1/2 with no load change; an independently staffed E adds 3 net with separate verified procurement. All quantities synthetic. Under half marginal loads, p in [0,1/2]. With h=P(H=1), sharp conditional bounds are max(0,h-p)<=q<=min(h,1-p): subtract the overlap with the overload atom, and attain either extreme by allocating H mass between feasible and overload strata. This preserves the entire load distribution. With h=1/2, antialigned loads give G=4, aligned loads with H only on blocked or feasible paths give G=1 or 2. Identical expected worker hours and license marginals reverse the E decision.

Even a full joint load audit can be insufficient. At p=3/16, with load probabilities (3,5,5,3)/16 on (00,01,10,11), H marginal 1/2 permits q=5/16 or 8/16; G=23/8 or 13/4. G>E exactly when q>3/8. The required economic moment is actual feasible licensed successor overlap, not the frequency of a technical capability alone. If H marginal comes from deliberate dual execution with backup staff, transport to original G is an additional causal assumption. The fixed safe policy can instead directly identify q without identifying H on blocked paths.

## Information certificate

Let C be the event that a lawful predispatch report cell's conservatively certified conditional support excludes overload 11. In this toy, both can safely run precisely on C; elsewhere one can always run. Let c=P(C), d=P(C,H=1). Optimized optional G=max(0,4c+2d-1). Proof: common action across each indistinguishable cell; dispatching both is dominant when admissible, impossible otherwise. Take conditional expectations and sum. A certified binary congestion flag is sufficient without predicting each load. With p>0 and no signal, C empty and G=0. With p=0 and independently certified exclusion, even no signal suffices. Under ambiguous laws including overload and no signal, union safety excludes dual dispatch even in the true antialigned world; finite sample zero events do not certify exclusion.

For a coherent refinement with unchanged physics, demand, rights and costs, C can only expand (every subcell of a safe cell remains safe). The gross report gain is 4P(C_new minus C_old)+2P(H=1,C_new minus C_old). This is not a claim that more training data improve physical qualification; grounding that changes load law changes c,d themselves. Two actual slots or certified flexible deadlines remove the overload constraint; then net G=3+2h=4 at h=1/2, before any additional backup cost. One parent purchase cap removes dual production even with abundant workers. Free routine data yielding the same variant, report and rights gives gross 4c+2d without deliberate acquisition cost. A shared-staff E must enter the same menu, not be added.

## Integrated economic interpretation

The economically relevant object is a report-conditioned feasible dispatch menu with successor transition value, not individual autonomy percentages. A shared representation or environment may correlate residual recovery; the mechanism is a hypothesis, not measured here. Model A values qualified, safely feasible production; Model B chooses heterogeneous source X, target grounding G, coordination, deliberate or production-generated data and E by altering joint menus and subsequent learning states.
