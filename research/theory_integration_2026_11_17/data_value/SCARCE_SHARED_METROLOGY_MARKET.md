## Economic premises

A portable representation can make the result of an old calibration reusable, but acquiring a new physical contrast occupies a device, staff and a dated window. A reference provider can sell that access across different markets. A bidder that buys a contrast must still spend local validation resources before claiming that its machine meets a buyer's requirements. Rival qualification affects the value of buying such access, and an award rather than a successful lab test can determine which future customer histories are collected. Therefore scarce reference capacity is not simply a neutral multiplier on a fixed matrix of technical transfers.

Technical feasibility anchor: NIST's [Robotics Test Facility](https://www.nist.gov/laboratories/tools-instruments/robotics-test-facility), updated June 24, 2024, describes external collaborative use operated by staff and possible cost reimbursement; its [Manufacturing Robotics Testbed](https://www.nist.gov/laboratories/tools-instruments/manufacturing-robotics-testbed), same update date, describes reconfigurable metrology and real/virtual experiments. [API's commercial robot calibration offering](https://apimetrology.com/api-robot-performance-measurement-and-calibration-solution/) describes physical accuracy measurement and use of measured corrections in robot programs. Pages consulted November 25, 2026; none establishes queue lengths, price schedules, a proprietary shared-report market, economic returns or the assumed qualification likelihoods. Relevant economic prior art: [Levin and Smith, Equilibrium in Auctions with Entry (1994)](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/aecrev/v84y1994i3p585-99.html), abstract consulted, treats costly endogenous auction entry; [Kamien and Tauman, Fees Versus Royalties and the Private Value of a Patent (1986)](https://doi.org/10.2307/1885693), publisher abstract consulted, treats outsider licensing to competing firms. We do not claim novelty for costly entry, strategic substitutes, licensing, or decision-theoretic data complementarity. DDA section 5 and Appendix B remain prior art. The narrower synthesis is a reference-dependent qualification game whose awards change both the identity of the installed capital and the effective evidence available to subsequent markets.

## 1. Qualification is an economic choice, not an automatic consequence of access

One original order can be served conventionally for C or by either of two qualified robotics firms A and B at the same real incremental cost C-D, D>0. Include all body, commissioning and service costs in the comparison; for this benchmark the procurement cost saving is fully available for division under the pricing rule. Each firm can pay real local test cost c_i before discovering whether its candidate qualifies. A passed test is required, and one funded new robot is commissioned only on award; the two firms have separate feasible local windows. Testing without winning generates no new production robot. Both can use their ordinary archive and retained policies; their ordinary test probabilities need not be zero. Conditional on a publicly committed reference route, potential binary test outcomes have marginals p,q and joint success j, with max(0,p+q-1)<=j<=min(p,q). This is a reduced-form likelihood/qualification map as in the preceding joint-belief chapter, not an assumed independence of transfer. The law is unchanged by test participation except for which outcomes are observed. All tests are simultaneous, before private results; no late retries. Price is reverse second price with conventional fallback; the only qualified bidder receives D above its cost, two qualified bidders earn no current margin and the award is allocated by a fair tie. Fallback fills the same original service. Real saving is from replacing fallback, not increasing final-order count.

If e_i is the discounted additional *private* continuation contribution conditional on its awarded deployment (relative to no award and ordinary future learning), then entry gains conditional on the rival's entry indicator y are

    A: D(p-jy) + e_A(p-jy/2) - c_A.
    B: D(q-jx) + e_B(q-jx/2) - c_B.

These follow by enumerating A-only qualification, both-qualified qualification with fair award, and no qualification. For pure entry (x,y) in {0,1}² the expected award vector is (px-jxy/2, qy-jxy/2). Current real surplus relative to conventional service is D(px+qy-jxy)-c_A x-c_B y. Add a subsequent real continuation contribution once, not the private e plus the same real output. Equilibrium entry is a pair of best responses; with e_i>=0, higher rival entry weakly discourages own testing. When both entry gains cross zero strictly between rival nonentry and entry, there are two asymmetric pure equilibria and an interior mixed equilibrium. For j>0 its entry probabilities are

    x = [q(D+e_B)-c_B]/[j(D+e_B/2)],
    y = [p(D+e_A)-c_A]/[j(D+e_A/2)].

Derivation: set the other firm's expected marginal entry gain equal to zero under independent mixed strategies. The interior formula requires both ratios strictly between zero and one; otherwise evaluate pure best responses and boundary indifference. A shared public report can shift both marginals and j. Even known report availability need not imply a unique participation pattern. A strong precommitted contingent qualification procedure can execute these choices; no comparison against a deliberately unresponsive organization.

## 2. A better reference can crowd out robotic production while increasing current real surplus

Restrict to e_A=e_B=0. Initially both strictly prefer testing against entry: c_A<D(p-j), c_B<D(q-j). Exclusive reference access makes A's potential success event expand from A0 to A1, so its marginal becomes r>p, and the joint with B becomes k. B's potential event is unchanged; nesting implies 0<=k-j<=r-p. Assume c_A<D(r-k) and c_B>D(q-k). These strict inequalities give unique both-enter baseline and unique A-only upgraded equilibrium: A enters regardless of B; B's best response then changes. The reference improves a technical possibility, but B rationally stops paying for its test. If r<p+q-j, total expected robotic awards decline. The customer continues receiving the same accepted service through fallback when nobody qualifies.

A's willingness to pay for the exclusive access, given the committed reference menu and ordinary baseline, is

    W_A = D(r-p+j).

Current real gain before reference cost is

    R_A = D(r-p-q+j)+c_B = W_A-(Dq-c_B).

Proof: subtract the equilibrium payoffs and real costs. Under the stated conditions R_A>0: holding both tests fixed, the nested upgrade cannot reduce coverage; allowing B's exit saves more than the lost residual coverage D(q-k). Moreover W_A>R_A because initial strict entry gives c_B<D(q-j)<=Dq. Thus the difference between the licensed firm's private gain and current real gain is exactly the lost B-only margin plus lost buyer saving, accounting for B's saved test expense. This is a business-stealing wedge; it can exist even though the qualification change is technically useful and dropping the duplicate bid is currently efficient. Do not infer a welfare loss from reduced robotic award count alone. Conversely, allowing the owner to sell a reference costing between R_A and W_A can make the provision privately profitable but socially costly even before considering future learning.

The nesting assumption is substantive: a shift that changes the joint potential outcomes without a feasible coupled monotone improvement needs fresh evaluation. With independent outcomes j=pq,k=rq is a special case. With compulsory test entry, negligible costs, a buyer subsidy, or equalized outside access the strict participation result can disappear.

## 3. The source of the next generation's learning is endogenous

Suppose a distinct next-date customer X is ordinarily robotically served with probability p0. An awarded B deployment supplies a lawful useful record with probability u conditional on that award; independently of ordinary qualification, it enables X's qualified installation when ordinary learning misses. The beneficiary has separately available validation and body capacity, and X's one original order expires. Let discounted net real saving conditional on this additional qualified order be d_X>0, including its acquisition and release costs, with no extra payment to B in the initial benchmark. The incremental X award probability is (1-p0)u times B's award probability. Ordinary learning is preserved in all arms. The A-exclusive access therefore changes the next market's expected robotic awards by

    - (1-p0)u(q-j/2),

and its total real contribution changes by that quantity times d_X. This is a specific omitted feedback in a current-only qualification game; it is not an imputed data premium atop already counted subsequent orders. The sign can reverse if A also generates transferable evidence, if C teaches X, or if X has no demand or commissioning headroom. With several award-dependent evidence routes, use joint original opportunities and their conditional distribution, not independent addition of overlapping wins.

If B receives private continuation e_B per award, its post-upgrade test remains attractive against A precisely when e_B >= [c_B-D(q-k)]/(q-k/2), for positive denominator. A lawful contingent compensation from downstream beneficiaries can alter the current equilibrium; complete internalization does not guarantee the same procurement/production allocation because price capture and real savings also differ. A positive e_B belongs in both the old and new entry calculations. A strict initial both-entry equilibrium remains under an increase in e_B>=0 in this benchmark.

This connects the current transfer network to the next one: the same improved A report can raise A's expected installations, displace B's installations and erase an evidence source for X. The response includes entry, awards and exposure. It is a finite difference in expected installations per extra usable reference slot, conditional on this market menu; it is not yet a per-source-robot coefficient. To obtain such a coefficient, couple a marginal source robot to the dated report law and the provider's supply decision. Physical MPK_r at fixed qualified controller, demand and complementary stocks is a separate accepted-service vector; the transfer premium, auction fees and realized investment responses are not physical MPK.

## 4. Endogenous capacity and allocation of an outside reference provider

Add distinct market C, with one original order and a single robot supplier. Its ordinary and improved qualification probabilities are p_C and r_C, real saving D_C, captured share alpha, and local test cost c_C. Assume both routes strictly justify testing, and no overlapping resources with A/B/X besides the outside metrology provider. Its access willingness to pay and real incremental gain are W_C=alpha D_C(r_C-p_C) and R_C=D_C(r_C-p_C). Both markets' ordinary programs remain available. Reports for A and C require distinct physical visits before the tooling/qualification deadline; the report is compatible only with its intended recipient in this solved menu. The provider chooses zero, one or two visits before expiry, with real cost F(n), including staff and metrology occupation. It commits to a menu of target-specific offers; known participation values, no strategic private type, credible commitment, acceptance at the participation price. No resale; an arbitrarily small discount makes acceptance strict without changing strict plan rankings. Each firm can decline; the supplier cannot compel training or entry. This is a complete-information price discrimination benchmark, not a claim about observed market prices. A feasible shared broadcast would be an additional offer with its own joint outcome law and equilibrium selection, not two physically identical visits.

Under the stated independent market menus the outside supplier maximizes sum of accepted W_i minus F(n), and a constrained social evaluator choosing the same menu but counting current and successor real contribution instead maximizes sum of R_i (plus the actual successor differences) minus F(n). We compare the same downstream equilibrium response, not a fully coordinated planner with extra authority to choose entry. Capacity and allocation both endogenous. A private first slot goes to A rather than C whenever W_A>W_C and profitable; the real first slot goes to C if R_C>R_A plus A's successor effect and profitable. This difference persists even with known demand and no financing friction. With scarce capacity, adding a reference asset can shift learning toward a market where it protects private rents, away from a market with greater real returns. A fee is a transfer in the aggregate, while the slot's real cost is not.

Synthetic witness: D=10,p=.5,q=.6,j=.3,r=.7,k=.42,c_A=1.5,c_B=2.4. Initially both test and expected awards are (.35,.45), with A margin .5 and B margin .6. After A access, only A tests; its award probability .7 and net private current value 5.5. Thus W_A=5, R_A=1.4 and current robotic awards decline .1. Choose p_C=.3,r_C=.8,D_C=8,alpha=.5,c_C=.5; W_C=2, R_C=4. For X let p0=.2,u=.5,d_X=10; B's lost .45 awards reduce X qualification probability .18, or real continuation 1.8. In this witness B captures none of X's gain; a private continuation per award exceeding 20/13 would prevent its post-upgrade exit. Let F(0)=0,F(1)=.8,F(2)=3.3.

| Committed reference menu | Provider profit | Aggregate real gain vs ordinary | Current A,B,C expected installations | Next X expected installations |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| None | 0 | 0 | .35,.45,.30 | .38 |
| C | 1.2 | 3.2 | .35,.45,.80 | .38 |
| A | 4.2 | -1.2 | .70,0,.30 | .20 |
| A and C | 3.7 | .3 | .70,0,.80 | .20 |

The supplier chooses A, while the real comparator chooses C. The former builds one reference slot, raises A's technical readiness, reduces current industry robotic deployment by .1 and next X by .18, and lowers discounted real contribution even though the same source improvement before reference costs and future feedback raises current real surplus. The alternative creates .5 extra current robotic installations without suppressing B's future evidence. These are expected finite-cohort quantities, not forecasts. If the second slot instead costs less than W_C=2 at the margin, the private provider adds it (given A); since the A route has negative real gain before its real slot cost once X is included, the real comparator still rejects A. Hence capacity scale as well as location can differ.

As a separate access-structure stress test, allow a shared report with p=.7,q=.8,j=.58 and the same current test costs. Then both incumbents prefer to test alone, neither wants to test against the other. Two pure equilibria and an interior mixed equilibrium coexist. The mixed entry rates are about .966 and .948; its expected current robotic awards sum to about .903. The A-only and B-only equilibria give .7 and .8. No unique signed response follows from those higher marginals. The previous prepaid-test shared-access result is not automatically robust to costly strategic qualification. This does not solve a price menu offering that shared report; equilibrium selection and contingent licensing with public/private rights remain open.

## 5. Identification and next dependency

A field panel must distinguish (i) the report's effect on held-out potential qualification, including matched local test protocols and their incremental labor cost, (ii) who chooses to test after access and price are announced, (iii) the joint set of qualified bidders and actual awards under fixed buyer acceptance/fallback, and (iv) the records and subsequent accepted work generated by *awards*, not by tests. Document the provider's requested/reserved/used/refused slots, real measurement labor, dated capacity, compatibility, fees and ability to resell the result. A customer with only one qualified bidder need not reflect intrinsically low rival competence; that rival may have selected out of testing. Independent access windows and matched quotes could discriminate crowdout from missing technical transfer; an equivalent no-cost contrast and matched permitted reuse are negative controls. In repetitive handling, distinct production shifts may generate substitute diagnostics. Multistage manufacturing may share calibration equipment while its recipient workcells require different local qualification. In sparse inspection, the award may be the only opportunity to observe a consequential future condition. These are hypotheses tied to original service records, not measured magnitudes. No benchmark is monetized.

The comparison narrows the theory-week dependency: endogenous evidence supply is not merely a within-firm incident rate; award selection can change the firm/task origin of future data. Next solve endogenous competition between alternative reference sources and broadcast versus exclusive licensing when the report changes joint qualification and each actor's subsequent evidence; explicitly allow rational refusal, multiple equilibria and a low-cost portable substitute. Examine whether an empirically distinguishable paired service/evidence panel can reject the crowdout mechanism. Preserve the core first-paper evolving-counterfactual argument and the existing December 25 deadline.
