# Prospective original procurement/budget and robot qualification evidence protocol
**19 November 2026 | research design only; no customer contact, robot interventions or permissions asserted**

### Original eligible buyer register (denominator independent of fulfilled sales)
Record stable eligible customer ID, common procurement parent/budget ID, service buyer/division ID, original decision period, market/end-market and competing alternatives. Preserve all original quotes, refused offers, no-purchase, accepted-before-dispatch requests, capacity-censored offers and timing. Register *both* requesting and nonrequesting divisions; acceptance-only receipts bias demand estimates. Keep data rights and person-level privacy boundaries.

### Budget and menu timing
Register each parent's **verified maximum legally spendable budget for these services**, budget source/contract term and period, unused and competing spend, and whether it was revealed before price/qualification/admission commitment. Observe which schedule was legally permitted: one uniform posted menu, parent-specific price, or no permissible change; retain chosen and unchosen menus and outside options. A parent spending6 under price6 **does not reveal** whether its budget was7 or8. Separate verified affordability from behavioral purchase commitment; a parent might decline additional units even with remaining budget. At multiple price menus measure actual parent choice, including the extent to which spending on one robot service displaces another service or vendor. No invented quote/cash floor.

### Physical and technical match
Version every X source-training recipe, actual source type and rights (field trajectories, failure/recovery, teleop, simulated, synthetic, public/pretrained), evaluation split, training/compute/curation costs and controller checkpoint. Independently verify complete-service acceptance, operator dispatch/recovery minutes and worst-case simultaneous deadlines for X and G, new E embodied slot safety/qualification and real retained R feasibility. Distinguish two per-division services in the *same parent budget* from two independent original purchasers. Log model-sharing/interference and demand spillovers across sites, not only robot success.

### Discriminating intervention and identified contrasts
Conditional study, subject to independent physical and commercial approval: for comparable original eligible parent/time strata, observe joint (H,B) at the same policy/menu, actual legally feasible price4 versus6 buyer responses, and whether G really frees operator capacity. Match the same original customer service recipes, quality/price, payment capture and vendor alternatives; do not compare a benchmark with revenues. At baseline price6, the synthetic B7/B8 worlds are observationally identical; a *paired budget-and-request* audit distinguishes them. With q,b but missing pair t, report the sharp four-type conditional [max(0,q+b−1),min(q,b)] interval instead of a policy winner. If X/G policy qualities or rivals differ, collect policy-specific potential support; no transport of q,t without a justified exposure/choice law. Whole-network randomization or a defensible controller/operator/customer graph is required when pools interfere. Do not implement physical experiments on this authorization alone.

### Atlas schema and aggregate backbone
Join: original_order_group -> parent_budget_period -> policy_menu_offer/refusal -> safe complete_recipe qualification -> robot/controller/operator network -> actual fulfillment -> captured receipts and collection costs -> candidate learning-data source. Store both original-buyer eligibility and no-sale censoring. Sector/end-market BLS 2025–35 and BEA IO tables can contextualize labor/task opportunity and business-network topology; aggregate statistics do NOT identify the joint parent budget/qualified-service law. The numerical vintage-matched BLS–BEA task crosswalk remains unavailable and must not be replaced with another vendor launch anecdote.

### Falsification/limits
The theory's G floor fails if parent budget is unverifiable or buyer commitment is not established; the two-slot implementation fails if joint recovery tails violate approval; the price information mechanism fails if parent budgets cannot legally be used before offer; the economic bound fails if eligible purchasers/payments are unbounded. Record each as an explicit missing parameter, not as zero. No customer or physical evidence is claimed.
