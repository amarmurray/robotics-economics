# Exercises — third cohort moment closure

1. With the eligible mass fixed at 1/2, construct both parity worlds and calculate all six one/pairwise moments on the original denominator. Find the missing paid-event moment.
2. Derive the exact M-versus-L incremental contribution and the exposure threshold against E. What if M has a further cost c per attempt?
3. Prove that the feature-span criterion is necessary for all distributions, but may be unnecessary for a singleton known occupancy. Explain why statewise maximization of latent-state payoffs is not a feasible rule.
4. Suppose the total discounted uniform residual certificates for G,M and E are 0.08 and 0.04. If their fitted values are 6.10 and 6.00, is the ranking certified? What if the first is 6.15?
