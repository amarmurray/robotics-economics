# Exercises — endogenous eligibility
1. Derive the five-type feasibility inequalities from eligible mass e and successful eligible mass s.
2. Given m=1/4, x=0 or 1/4, prove a single randomized q cannot ensure more than 5; distinguish oracle and robust value.
3. For a perfect F report compute the minimum exposure to beat E=21/4. For r>=15/16 and m>=1/8, derive a lower bound.
4. Explain why a free routine P matching only the technical qualification rate need not dominate K; derive the value when all transitions match.
5. Compute E under shared staffing and explain why retaining R matters. Propose a two-stage lawful audit and its denominator.
