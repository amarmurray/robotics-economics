# Student exercises — information versus admitted capability

All numbers are hypothetical, in normalized **complete captured customer production** units. All continuation actions are physically approved by assumption for the math exercise; no real robot safety inference.

1. With equal H,L, F=(0,0), specialist S=(3/4,3/4), G=(3,-1), and T=(0,-6), compute the optimal hidden-state and perfectly informed gross values.
2. Suppose training changes G to (3,1), leaving all else unchanged. Explain why perfect information has zero incremental gross value even though the training changes technical performance. Compute the controller × information interaction.
3. Instead training changes T to (4,-6), leaving G unchanged. Derive the q threshold at which a positive report should switch from G to T; derive W(q) for q≥1/2.
4. With source+admission cost 3/10 and report cost 9/10, compare retain, report only, train only and both at perfect signal. Solve the minimum q for both to beat retaining.
5. Explain why independently randomized full-network means can identify the controlled policy interaction without identifying a cross-controller individual success overlap. Give two reasons why a factorial controller/report study would *not* identify information-vs-capability components.
