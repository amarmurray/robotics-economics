# Source data, grounding and embodiment option — exercises

1. In the original two-regime equal-prior customer network, R=(2,2), EA=(4,0), EB=(0,4). A report is correct with symmetric probability alpha in [1/2,1]. Derive W under R only, R+EA and R+EA+EB and the marginal value of enabling EB.
2. Add an independently approved robust E0=(7/2,7/2). Determine the information-accuracy region in which extra paired source plus the report yields no incremental current gross contribution. Explain why it does not follow that a free optional paired source has negative economic value.
3. Construct a negative value-of-information/menu cross difference and explain what it says about generalist-versus-specialist choice. Which part relies on the timing of physical policy selection?
4. Suppose only one specialist body can be locked before the report, but R can safely be chosen afterward. Derive expected optimized contribution. Repeat with an irreversible commitment that also disables R. What exact signoff must a field study obtain?
5. State a prospective factorial study that can separately identify source-to-joint-qualification transfer, original-customer market response, report value and embodiment flexibility. Explain why isolated benchmark marginals and selected customer survey returns cannot complete this evidence.
