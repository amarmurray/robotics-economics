# Prospective identification and engineering audit — causal network envelope

**18 Nov 2026.** This is an empirical *design*, not observed robot/controller/customer evidence. It extends [the predecessor protocol](APPROXIMATE_NETWORK_EMPIRICAL.md) rather than creating a second unrelated study.

## Audit first, no unsafe testing arms

Record (i) exact controller checkpoint ancestry, access/replication permissions, training and replay sources with type, embargo and version; (ii) human operator reservation, global queues, abort modes, travel overlap and backup recovery; (iii) original customer demand segment, orders including zero, substitutions, joint accepted-service qualification and opportunity-window expiration; (iv) material/other production network constraints and embodiment changes. Graph nodes can be global. Unknown links remain **unknown**, not zero. Every proposed whole-network policy must pass independent physical safety review before operational study. No paid training/customer access is authorized.

For each target policy family including routine, target grounding, changed embodiment, retained and qualified specialist, pre-register **complete histories** with all possible version upgrades, new workcells, customer segments and operator events. Build a table of target-history support: policy, θ proxy or admitted uncertainty class, prefix, upstream perturbation, downstream next-state kernel, whether actually safely matched, and source of bound. A C1 interval must hold for all supported relevant histories, not just where experiments happened.

## Separate four evidence types

1. **Architectural zero:** offline checkpoint isolation, no shared replay, verified no message/dispatch/customer path; source: versioned engineering access/graph audit. An architecture zero may be policy conditional and must be reassessed after updates.
2. **Authorized matched perturbation:** safe whole-network randomized or credible controlled reallocation of checkpoint sharing/target grounding with common pre-action customer denominators and propensity logging. Measure changes in **joint** operator event laws, held-out jointly accepted recipes and all originally eligible customer orders (including zeros), not only robot success.
3. **Bounded but unverified link:** maximal conditional effect inferred from an explicit physical latency/control/channel constraint, supported matched samples or independently justified worst-case envelope. Report the structural premise and error separately; mean spillover confidence intervals do not certify a sup nor hard safety.
4. **Unsupported:** no way to bound a new route's distant customer substitution, latent transfer or rare recoveries. Set the certificate to unknown or vacuous, not numerical zero.

Each per-node w must come from feasible *captured customer production* under matched demand, rights, output, service-quality threshold and constrained alternative. Where V is solved by a production LP, check that any shadow-price certificate is valid over the entire bounded perturbation interval and matches the true qualified recipe menu; complementary new bundles can make local gradients worthless. Keep accepted/attempted distinction and avoid mapping benchmark percentages to money.

## Falsifiable intervention pairs

- **Shared vs isolated controller lineage:** matched approved routes holding source acquisition/embodiment as constant as possible; test whether far B customers suffer transferred-model regressions or net order substitution when A data are replayed.
- **Routine field vs deliberate grounding:** same approved calendar and recovery/operator cap; measure failures/recoveries/teleop state distributions, source rights/compute costs and follow-up *held-out accepted whole recipes*. Public, simulated, synthetic and pretrained inputs are distinct arms or covariates, not assumed fungible.
- **Serial admission vs candidate unstaged plan:** do *not* run an unsafe plan. Compare validated serial to approved retained and specialist routes, observing refusal-to-start, stopped work, missed customer orders, induced learning loss, operator peak and downtime. A previously observed free productive-data stream is a serious counterfactual.
- **Customer conditional demand:** match original eligible customer strata/zero orders, then measure route-dependent conversion, price/capture and substitution. A positive controller-performance metric with negative order capture is a discriminating rejection of benchmark-as-value.

## Evidence sufficient to narrow the decision

The toy serial policy beats specialist by only .01675 **if** its local economic inputs, C/b support bounds, customer weights and specialist *upper* are valid together. To validate a real analogue, obtain a simultaneous uncertainty set for the lower serial whole-network contribution and the **upper** specialist contribution, not two separately favorable point estimates. Include training/curation and physical operator opportunity costs only once and report both conditional captured private contribution and broader output value separately. If confidence/support remains insufficient, preserve the safe fallback and the rank as unresolved. Any nonzero unreserved recovery path invalidates a *hard* operator support premise irrespective of its estimated probability.

**Official production atlas:** BLS 2025–35 occupation/industry and BEA industry IO numerical tables require verified vintage, exact numeric extraction and industry crosswalk; none are retrieved here. Sector wages or tasks are not qualified robot orders. No employer information, unapproved site, hardware experiment, request for access or benchmark-to-dollar assumption is used.

**Next data task:** trace and version an actual complete controller/demand/operator topology from a permitted primary-source setting and identify which proposed C/b bounds can be architectural versus merely observational; use it to choose one safe, high-value full-service validation comparison.
