**Concavity and service prices.** P_A=a−2x−γy, P_B=b−2y−γx; F=a x+b y−2x²−2y²−2γxy. Hessian −4[[1,γ/2],[γ/2,1]] is strictly negative definite when |γ|<2. At incumbent (x,y)=(1/2,1/2), parameters a=9+γ/2,b=7+γ/2 give P_A=8,P_B=6 and gross F=7, *regardless of γ*. The technical caps, customer accepted-service counts, prices and receipts at that single mix are identical.

**Global optimizer over all γ∈[−1,1].** Strict concavity and KKT suffice. Under R, x≤1/2 and x+y≤1, the derivative along the binding operator line y=1−x is 2 at x=1/2. At the proposed solution the marginal contribution of adding y is positive and x's cap is binding, so (1/2,1/2) uniquely maximizes F. Under G, x≤1/2 and y+(1/2)x≤1; at (1/2,3/4) the operator multiplier is F_y=4−γ/2>0 and the residual marginal gain along the operator line is F_x−(1/2)F_y=5−3γ/4>0, validating the A cap. Under E, at (1/2,1) both F_x=7−3γ/2>0 and F_y=3−γ/2>0, so the individual caps bind. Under X, x+y=1, x≤1,y≤1; substituting y=1−x gives
F_X(x)=5+γ/2+(6−2γ)x−(4−2γ)x².
Stationarity yields x*=(3−γ)/(4−2γ), y*=1−x*. This is in [2/3,1]×[0,1/3]. The common operator multiplier is nonnegative; a directional increase in q at the maximizer is profitable, so the operator remains binding. These KKT conditions with strict concavity establish all claimed global optima. Substitution yields V_R=7, V_X=5+γ/2+(3−γ)²/(4−2γ), V_G=(65−γ)/8, V_E=9−γ/4.

**Operator-saving envelope.** Write h(q,δ,O)=F(q) subject to (u_A−δ)q_A+q_B≤O. Lagrangian L=F(q)+λ[O−(u_A−δ)q_A−q_B]+other constraints. Under standard constraint qualification, locally unique solution/multipliers and differentiability, envelope gives ∂_δV=λq_A and ∂_O V=λ. If the cap c_A binds and the response stays in the same active region, lowering u_A releases c_Aδ units of effective operator capacity, and V may exhibit decreasing differences with an expansion in O. Outside that active regime the sign is not fixed.
