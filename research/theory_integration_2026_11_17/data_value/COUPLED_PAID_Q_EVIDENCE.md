## Support requirements and rejection rules

1. **Eligibility and market.** Predeclare original buyer/order/no-order cohort, offer prices, willingness-to-pay observations, calendar time and service opportunity. Keep unaccepted, refunded, delayed, failed and never-ordered cases in the denominator. Capture program-caused future orders as outcomes, not post-treatment reweighting.
2. **Action-lawfulness.** Mark each action and history as (a) certified safe/physically capable and licensed, paid-supported; (b) certified but paid-unsupported; (c) unapproved/rights-uncertain; or (d) forbidden. Only (a,b) enter a lawful uncertainty set; (c,d) are excluded, never coded as missing Q=0. Legal train/retain/transfer/deploy rights are separate.
3. **Shared-learner coupling.** For a claimed d_A=theta-alpha, d_B=theta-beta relation, use *the same trained checkpoint lineage*, original-market buyer classification, operator opportunity and allowed data rights across matched paid interventions. Estimate complete-service conditional Q, including future learner-generated qualified service, settlement/refund and recovery. Explicitly test slope equality, stable intercepts, nonlinear departures, and shared customer/operator interference; never infer these from ML task accuracy.
4. **Learning provenance.** Distinguish actual field success trajectories, failures/recoveries, teleoperation time, simulation, synthetic generation and public/pretraining. Record marginal compute, curation, licenses, embodiment and operator displacement costs once. Preserve free optional raw availability versus costly/forced training.
5. **Information versus skill.** G_info reveals permitted buyer information before dispatch without changing true physically certified menus; G_train changes grounding/qualification Q and possibly legality. Compare only approved interventions. Record which customer order/history was observable at each decision and any later adaptive observation.
6. **Embodiment counterfactual.** E must be a separately approved *paid service* on the same original buyer network and eligible horizon. If E shares learner states or demand response, carry the actual joint support; do not subtract independent fabricated scalar bounds. If no E approval exists, retain an unidentified investment ranking.
7. **Network and horizon.** Record complete robot/edge/queue/operator and shared learner influence, cross-customer response, future induced service, rights expiration and settlements. One-site paired paid outcomes cannot identify a pooled global program where common learner parents touch all sites.

## Decision procedure

For each lawful decision history, construct buyer-conditional complete paid-Q intervals. Use **rectangular** outer bounds when no credible cross-buyer structural relationship is supported. Sharpen to a **joint feasible segment/polytope** only on the basis of validated shared learner, operator, rights, customer and settlement constraints. On a two-buyer/two-action cell, partition the feasible d_A,d_B polygon at zero-action differences and p*d_A+(1-p)*d_B=0. The resulting exact extrema are conditional on the whole-support assumptions. For unavailable E or unidentified global continuation, report the SG/E **identified set** without choosing a winner. A source-enabled new recipe can invalidate an R-sufficient state aggregation; audit histories backward from paid settlement through legal dispatch.

An economic transfer factor is **not** a benchmark-to-dollar conversion. The specific rejection test for the one-factor construction is whether measured buyer-specific *paid relative-Q differences* lie on the same assumed slope-one line at matched checkpoints and comparable market/resource/rights states. Rejection broadens the joint support. No experiment should be conducted merely to force positivity on an uncertified robot action.
