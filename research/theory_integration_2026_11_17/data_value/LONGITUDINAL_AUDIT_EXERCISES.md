# Student exercises — longitudinal economic data-value identification

Assume the strictly synthetic environment and rights menus of [the chapter](LONGITUDINAL_AUDIT_AND_POLICY_SUPPORT.md). Work with whole-program net original buyer production, not benchmark success or an independent data premium.

1. **Chronological estimator.** With program assignment probability (p_0), report inclusion probabilities (rho_1,rho_2), and intermediate action probability (e_1), derive the inverse-weighted evaluation formula for a policy that sees the first audited buyer signal before choosing its second action. Explain why the second reporting draw must not enter the preaction decision.
2. **Physical worker constraint.** Prove that whenever the new job (J_2=1), terminal accepted buyer production is zero in the stipulated three-date model. State exactly what extension invalidates treating the buyer outcome in that state as economically irrelevant.
3. **The G/E reversal.** Under uniform z,w, calculate the true net value of licensed G/probe and independent E/adaptive and then the selected complete-case G/probe mean. Explain why an actual field conclusion cannot be drawn from these figures.
4. **Rao–Blackwell improvement.** Replace (S_2/rho_2) by 1 only for known zero terminal service. Derive why second moment cannot increase when the audit itself does not affect rewards or future control. Does this imply globally minimum variance?
5. **No action overlap.** Suppose target (pi) requires a safe, licensed intermediate validation branch with positive target probability but (e_1=0) for that branch. Construct two potential-outcome laws with identical logged records yet different (pi) value.
6. **Rights and long-run feedback.** If a future learner uses the unserved buyer's demand or failure/recovery record, identify which audit omission and value equation must change. Would a perfect off-policy propensity model alone identify counterfactual physical intervention outcomes without action overlap?
