# Prospective pre-action target shadow audit and economic support

**Status November 19, 2026: no actual customer orders, robot approval, validated reporter or new field sample. Research protocol, not authorization.**

Before any audit, version-pin the whole approved controller/embodiment, reporter, dispatch, operator network, service acceptance and safety recovery, trained-policy snapshot, rights-cleared source data and qualified retained specialist. Test that the shadow report is **nonactuating**, creates no shared compute/operator bottleneck and does not change customer orders. A changed source-trained controller, physical target grounding or new embodiment is a different whole-policy experiment, not a row of this frozen kernel.

Register original **pre-action** customer-order opportunities before reporting: state z independently verified, timestamp and site, dispatched and undispatched orders, accepted, failed, displaced, expired, missed and recovered service, incumbent option and resource costs. For each stratum, preserve n_z *all sampled original opportunities*, k_z high reports and known audit inclusion scheme. Report absent rows as unobserved, not zero success. Selected accepted jobs, operator interventions, source demonstrations and benchmark robot trajectories are not the denominator.

For complete approved action a, record statewise y(a,z): original-order accepted service/captured receipts, displaced service and customer outside option, operator attention/dispatch/supervision/recovery, scarce fleet/tools/resources and margins. Estimate p_z and R_z only from appropriate population evidence, not wages, benchmark percentage or an IO sector's gross output. Use precommitted inference or valid time-uniform confidence sequences for adaptive audits, and cluster-aware methods if sites/jobs are dependent.

Run two prospective comparisons: (1) frozen qualified policy with/without an inert shadow report, and (2) separately physically approved source, grounding and embodiment policies on independent policy-closed networks with joint qualification and original-order panel. The former identifies information value under appropriate K support; the latter is needed for physical transfer, customer demand changes and body-specific y/A. Do not invent a difference-in-differences customer counterfactual or assume physical consent.

Public DROID and Open-X Embodiment remain routes for versioned technical source datasets, not approved commercial service orders. Official BLS output and BEA input-output routes are preserved as *unfilled numeric backbone*, not evidence about customer-level robot data economics. Neither outreach, paid sampling nor private customer data access is authorized. The next real evidence question is the existence of one genuinely independent, approved target report/original opportunity sample; otherwise preserve the confidence/identified-set theory without fictitious calibration.
