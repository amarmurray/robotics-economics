# Endogenous lawful data acquisition: producing today or collecting deliberately

**22 November 2026 — When Capabilities Become Production; same Models A/B, scientific gate OPEN.** The preceding [lawful report theorem](LAWFUL_REPORT_OBSERVABILITY_AND_IDENTIFICATION.md) optimizes dispatch after an *exogenously available* report. This chapter endogenizes **which report/data/learning state is produced**, without introducing a second premium for future data, a finance-only problem, a new numbered Q queue, or a claim of measured deployment returns.

## State, action and value

At date t, the full history h includes: eligible original-buyer offers **and no-orders**, price and acceptance/settlement; qualified recipes and retained policies; physical embodiment, robot/operator/coordination resources, learner checkpoint and source lineage; rights to record, train, pool, transfer, disclose and use for dispatch; predecision observation and the timestamp at which it actually becomes legal and available. Latent unmet demand and a delayed, forbidden or inaccessible signal are not in the acting policy's information filtration F_t.

Let a lawful compound production/data action a include P (paid service producing field success/failure/recovery data), D (deliberate teleoperation/demonstration or authorized experimentation), S (licensed field, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic or public/pretrained source inclusion), G (target grounding or report licensing), E (new embodiment), or retained R. A candidate can yield paid production **and** trainable data simultaneously. Each action has immediate *all-affected-buyer* finally accepted-and-paid contribution r_t(h,a), additional data/curation/compute/license cost c_t(h,a) **not already inside r**, and a legal physical stochastic transition K_t(h'|h,a). K includes changed qualification menus, future lawful reports, customer demand, robot/operator capacity, rights revocations and shared learner spillovers.

The finite-horizon producer value is the usual constrained Bellman objective

  J_t(h) = max over lawful F_t-adapted a of {r_t(h,a)-c_t(h,a) + beta sum_h' K_t(h'|h,a) J_(t+1)(h')},  J_T=0.

Every contribution generated by later learned production is already in J. There is **no extra data dividend** appended to a Bellman value. For consistency with existing resource-price certificates, resource shadow values may give dual upper bounds, but must not duplicate real expenses or buyer service forgone already reflected in r and K. Sharing across sites is not assumed independent.

## Proposition 1: data acquisition is a *compound* lawful action

Suppose acquisition d now yields a signal z later in the same epoch, updates the lawful recipes/rights/market state, and permits contingent dispatch a(z) with no strategic commitment obstacle. Then maximizing sequentially over d and each feasible z-adapted continuation **equals** maximizing over all lawful compound actions (d,a(·)) in the Bellman problem.

**Proof.** Backward induction gives the best permissible continuation at each actual post-acquisition observation and legal successor state. A finite perfect-recall contingent plan maps each reachable observation to the same action; every such plan can conversely be implemented sequentially. Maximization across these plans and d is therefore identical. If d changes customer ordering, operator occupancy, ability, permissions or tomorrow's learner state, it changes K and/or the feasible menu and cannot be valued by adding a standalone static "report premium" to an otherwise fixed production optimum. A signal produced by today's service cannot retroactively inform yesterday's dispatch. QED.

## Proposition 2: productive versus deliberate opportunity-cost inequality

Take two physically lawful date-zero acquisition actions, P and D, with complete immediate all-buyer contributions r_P,r_D and uncounted acquisition costs c_P,c_D. Write W_P and W_D for *their respective optimized* future paid-production continuations, including all rights, recipes and capacities. Then

  J_P >= J_D  iff  (r_P-c_P)-(r_D-c_D) >= beta (E_D W_D - E_P W_P).

**Proof:** Subtract the two complete arm-specific Bellman values. QED.

Under a **common** post-acquisition latent customer market, recipes, physical state, legal report-use rights and rewards, if D's signal Blackwell-dominates P's signal, the right-hand continuation difference is nonnegative: the D agent can legally garble its richer observation to simulate P. But comparing a richer D report that *does not qualify* an essential recipe with a noisier productive P that *does* qualify it changes the market menu, so the pure-information ordering proves nothing about their program values. Likewise robot/operator time diverted from a paying customer, induced customer orders, delayed learner updates, or one arm's unavailable consent break a static-information ranking.

**No double-counting:** If P produces current accepted paid output and D forgoes it, the difference r_P-r_D already captures that opportunity. Subtracting that same forgone output again as "D opportunity cost" inside c_D is incorrect. Explicit additional D fees, compute and legal expenses belong in c_D once. If D also displaces a *second* buyer using a shared slot, its lost receipts must be reflected in total program r and future K, not invented as a constant price independent of fleet state.

## Proposition 3: conditional marginal economic data value and its domain

For data intensity x on an interval assume a finite, **unchanged** lawful history-policy set, smooth bounded rewards and complete conditional kernels K_x, and no abrupt change in support or admissibility. For a fixed policy pi, let d_t^pi(h,a) be its full-network history/action occupancy and U_(t+1)^pi its complete remaining paid contribution. Differentiate its Bellman recursion:

 dJ_pi/dx = sum_(t=0 to T-1) beta^t sum_(h,a) d_t^pi(h,a)
   [partial_x r_t(x,h,a) + beta sum_h' partial_x K_t(x,h'|h,a) U_(t+1)^pi(x,h')]
   - C'(x),

where C is an additional acquisition cost not already inside r. For finite x-independent policy set, J(x)=max_pi J_pi(x) has **right directional derivative** equal to the maximum of these derivatives over currently optimal policies; if one optimizer remains uniquely optimal locally, ordinary differentiation holds.

**Proof:** Differentiate one-step expected value under a fixed contingent policy, then recursively unroll the derivative of downstream values. Occupancy weights follow from the chain rule along every feasible complete history. Maximizing finitely many smooth fixed-policy values gives the right envelope derivative (and potentially a kink at a policy switch). QED.

The derivative can be positive or negative independently of a source data benchmark gradient because it weights licensed learning, new qualification, market demand and scarce coordinated throughput by actual downstream paid value. If E changes the embodiment or hard safety gates cause discrete recipe/menu jumps, these smooth fixed-policy assumptions fail. Compare complete J across discrete programs instead. This proposition is standard MDP sensitivity/envelope calculus adapted to the joint production ledger, not a new mathematical scaling law.

## Exact two-date conditional counterworld

At the second date an original buyer orders A or B with probability 1/2 each; this fixed order distribution is a **stipulation**, not a field assumption. The retained R pays 1/2 regardless; lawful specialist A and B each yield 1 paid unit when dispatched to the matching order and zero otherwise. A binary licensed predispatch report has symmetric accuracy alpha, and the agent may keep R. With both specialists qualified and alpha >= 1/2, optimized date-one expected accepted-paid contribution equals alpha.

**P**: perform one current paid service (receipt 1/4) that creates a legally usable but imperfect field signal alpha=3/4; no extra data cost. Complete J_P=1/4+3/4=**1**.

**D**: forgo current service; create a perfect target report alpha=1 with a real additional teleoperation/curation cost of 1/8. Complete J_D=1-1/8=**7/8**.

**E**: purchase/use a generalist embodiment yielding 7/8 irrespective of customer order at cost 1/16. Complete J_E=**13/16**. Retained R alone yields **1/2**.

In these conditional values P weakly wins D precisely when immediate productive paid receipt is **at least 1/8**; if P earns zero current receipt D wins (7/8 versus 3/4). The threshold is a mathematical consequence of *stipulated normalized paid production*, not a robot business price. The opportunity created by the more accurate D report can be less valuable than commercially productive learning.

**Qualification/right counterfactual:** If D's perfect report qualifies only A specialist, not B, its later optimum with fallback R is 3/4 and whole value falls to **5/8**. The less informative no-receipt P with both specialists then beats D (3/4 versus 5/8). If D's perfect signal cannot lawfully reach the acting dispatcher, two-specialist expected continuation without any usable report is only 1/2, regardless of the stored raw signal. This distinguishes technical data fidelity, ability to perform an accepted buyer recipe, and legally usable dispatch information.

**Cross-complement:** Let a source change report accuracy from 1/2 to 3/4, and target grounding separately qualify both specialists (otherwise only R exists). Source data alone or grounding without a distinguishing signal each adds zero optimized paid contribution; the combination adds **1/4**. This is a conditional *economic* source × target-qualification complement, not a claim of intrinsic transfer complementarity. More generalist E can instead substitute for demand information; higher-order buyer and capacity coupling may change the sign.

## Economic and empirical discrimination

An original eligible-buyer panel must be defined **before** assignment to P/D/S/G/SG/E, retain no-orders, refused/canceled/refunded service and accepted/finally cleared settlement, trace data lineage and all use rights, log exact times of signal availability versus dispatch, and track shared learner, operator and robot capacity. A current-paying-buyer P episode cannot be compared with D by selecting only completed trajectories; such conditioning deletes the sacrificed production in D. The technical effect of teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and field failures must each be traced to newly **qualified** original-buyer recipes, not assumed from source-data volume. A more informative D report may affect future buyer willingness to order, requiring a program-specific demand kernel.
