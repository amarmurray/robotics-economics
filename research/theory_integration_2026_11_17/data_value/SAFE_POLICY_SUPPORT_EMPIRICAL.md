# Safe-support and network estimand field contract — prospective only

**18 Nov 2026. No live robot intervention, customer permission, or safety certification was obtained.** This is the next actual measurement that would distinguish source data from economically valuable production.

**Registry and assignment units:** Define whole interference components before treatment from every controller/training checkpoint lineage, replay membership, downstream policy broadcast, shared dispatch and operator reservations, customer-order competition and other demand channels. Freeze immutable IDs, qualifying service recipe and operator safety envelope. If graph connects every pool, acknowledge one interference unit rather than pretending robot episodes are independent. For each original prospective customer/site pool record base demand stratum, eligible service window, lost/zero orders, signup/cancellation and new entrants; use original opportunity denominator rather than selecting only post-treatment fulfilled jobs.

**Safe eligibility first:** independent engineering admission/safe-stop proof for every complete policy being compared, including fallback, qualified specialist, target grounding and *only independently safe* embodiment variants. Record support and exclusion reasons for every pre-action customer stratum. Do not randomize an unapproved recovery branch or infer pathwise safety from a high mean or low observed error frequency.

**Assignments and interventions:** Among truly eligible independent networks and within pre-action customer strata, randomize complete, version-pinned policies (including old-controller/no-retraining baseline, routine productive data byproduct, deliberate teleoperation/grounding, simulation/synthetic/public pretraining mixtures and feasible new body). Vary training/collection rights and compute/curation as explicit costs, not as labels of identical data quality. Freeze baseline weights and held-out target qualification tests, intervention gates, safety envelopes, evaluation calendar and assignment probabilities. For rare failure/recovery states measure gate selection and missed events via independently approved audit; do not coerce dangerous exploration.

**Outcome:** sum customer-captured contribution from complete accepted orders by original window, minus real service, operator, hardware, validation and learning costs, plus *only* nonoverlapping future qualified production; record calendar time and original zero-order cases, subsequent customer demand and competitor spillovers. Joint accepted service (not separate marginal tasks) is the economic outcome. Record fallback/specialist output on unsupported sites if safely available; this anchors missing-outcome bounds. Do not convert ML benchmark percentages directly to contribution.

**Design diagnostics:** compare controller isolation and original network; if isolation changes customer allocation or source mix, it is a different policy. Register downstream customer pricing, dispatch competition and common operator recovery peaks. Direct policy means do not identify a cross-world mismatch event and an architectural graph cut is not observed because correlations disappear. Examine target share/eligibility selection for transfer regime drift. Report same-world population-mean contrast separately from worst-regime guarantee and pathwise safety. For each unsupported embodiment or customer segment, report bounded missing outcomes, not assumed mean transfer.

**Decisive negative control:** If a technically better grounding/embodiment policy does not increase *qualified accepted whole-service contribution* over a competent specialist and productive routine baseline on the same safely eligible population, the economic claim fails there. If policy-induced demand or controller transfer creates network spillovers, the no-interference bound fails and the policy must be evaluated jointly at the actual network boundary. Replication and physical validation remain future evidence gates.
