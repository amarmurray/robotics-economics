# Prospective matched qualification and paid-production identification (not field authorization)

**21 November 2026. Design only. Companion to JOINT_QUALIFICATION_DEMAND_AND_EMBODIMENT_CHOICE.md.** There are no real robotic trials, signed policies, source archive contents, buyer receipts, or numerical BEA/industry transactions in this tranche.

**Interventions.** Declare one controller/training checkpoint and original buyer/task cohort. R retains old permitted policies; S adds genuinely independent licensed source episodes at a specified *admitted* update budget and fixed learner architecture; G-train reweights/alines an existing licensed archive or adds grounded target observations, separately from G-observe's predispatch signal at a fixed learner; SG combines S and G-train with the same total time/compute/cost accounting. E invests in an embodiment and its manufacturing, site, controller, operator and permitted recipe; do not code E as a simple accuracy multiplier. Within each arm log field/failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public pretrained source origin separately with cluster genealogy; distinguish resampled or relabeled copies from independent environmental episodes.

**Randomization unit and interference.** Treat shared weights, fleet-level updates, operator pools and simultaneous buyers as potential spillover channels. Assign and analyze *whole learner/operator/customer-network clusters* or explicit exposure mappings; isolate held-out target orders and fixed precommitted sampling rules, time windows and service definitions. A naive episode-level split can leak clustered information; independent A and B benchmark runs do not identify \(h=\Pr(Q_A=Q_B=1)\).

**Joint qualification record.** For each predeclared target order and cluster version, preserve paired \(Q_A,Q_B\) (including neither, A-only, B-only, both) measured under one legally signed permitted controller/operator/safety envelope; time horizon, fallback/recovery authorization, rights, training snapshot, modality admissions, contact/trial refusals and all denominators. Record policy support and no-action alternatives. Report fractions and uncertainty for \(p_A,p_B,h\) by arm and buyer/market; do not condition away failed or unattempted orders. The high/low synthetic worlds have identical marginals; the economically decisive unknown is **paired joint success** and the source×grounding contrast, not an imagined universal accuracy-to-dollar coefficient.

**Actual economic outcomes.** For the *original offered buyer cohort* (not only accepted jobs), record contract/offer, signed required task bundle, acceptance threshold, fee, refunds, settled payments, human intervention cost, operator/robot/compute/resource use, outside paid orders displaced, production constraints and legal training/data rights. Separate verified conditional customer paid contribution from abstract resource LP dual prices and non-customer benchmarks. Establish E's alternative service capability and manufacturing + access cost independently; the numerical 5 and costs from the illustration are **assumed** and should not be transported. If joint receipt counterfactuals or cross-report outside opportunities are unknown, use the previous sharp partial-identification envelopes, not fictional extrapolation.

**Falsification.** Test whether the joint qualification matrix is feasible and whether the network's optimized original-customer table is invariant across programs as required by the simple fixed-\(F\) theorem. Reject fixed \(F\) when changing E or target demand alters signed recipes, operator availability, outside orders, customer offers, rights or price; estimate a demand-specific \(F_{e,d,N}\). Reject claims of source/grounding complementarity when 4-arm paired \(h\) is no greater than predicted by the observed counterfactuals after selection/cluster adjustment. Test archive inclusion against matched proposal/admission/compute budgets and prospective locked target tasks. Preserve real negative findings.

**Decision and data needs.** For the illustrative numbers only, \(h^{SG}>13/40\) makes SG beat the specified E-only alternative; if a valid uncertainty interval straddles that line no decisive recommendation follows. In reality derive the threshold from legally signed and accepted-paid customer policies, observed manufacturing costs and demand. A useful table has rows by original offered customer/task bundle and columns for R/S/G/SG/E alternative whole-network policy support, joint qualifications, settled receipts and omissions. All missing cells should be explicitly marked, not imputed.

**Production-network atlas boundary.** The customer→integrator→robot manufacturer→operator chain and cross-end-market data transfers can be mapped as typed edges; official BEA/BLS I-O industry transactions are a distinct numerical sector aggregation and need real downloaded vintaged cells plus consistent crosswalk. Another vendor deployment announcement is not a substitute. No employer-confidential data, outreach, autonomous trial or public publication is permitted.
