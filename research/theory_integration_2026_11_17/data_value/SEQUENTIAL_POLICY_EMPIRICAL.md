# Prospective source audit and measured-service design (no field experiment)

**Verified public primary routes, 19 November UTC, 2026.** DROID official https://droid-dataset.github.io/ and https://droid-dataset.github.io/droid/the-droid-dataset document in-the-wild teleoperated manipulation trajectories; dataset documentation identifies RLDS and raw versions, 100-episode sample (~2GB) and full RLDS (~1.7TB). First-party training/evaluation code at https://github.com/droid-dataset/droid_policy_learning provides controllable dataset mixtures, checkpoints and physical evaluation hooks. Open X-Embodiment official https://robotics-transformer-x.github.io/ documents 1M+ trajectories from 22 embodiments and technical cross-embodiment policy training. These are **technical source/transfer evidence routes**, not actual industrial A/B accepted-service customer ledgers, cash receipts, signed deployment approvals or independent network causal trial units. No data downloads, GPU training, purchases or physical deployments were run.

**Technical tier:** version pretraining, embodiment action frame, controller source hash, DROID/OXE dataset/mixture membership, target task/scene split, train compute and curation; compare permitted source data, target-grounding set, source+ground, and redesigned embodiment where separately possible. Keep field successes, failures/recovery, productive teleoperation, passive production logs, simulation, synthetic and pretrained data separate. Report both step accuracy and *joint complete service* plus changed visited target support and recovery, avoiding task percentages -> dollars.

**Physical/customer tier:** independently approve complete target service and retained qualified original/specialist fallback. Register the original B customer order universe, eligible A orders, demand deadlines, accepted, displaced, unattempted, operator/dispatch resource costs, recovery and direct customer capture. A public lab success rate is not a commercial qualification certificate.

**Causal tier (only prospectively):** establish policy-closed independent clusters including shared learners, dispatch, human labor and order markets. Apply identical source-first test, record failure and both alternative physical approvals BEFORE randomization. Randomize the *complete* eligible networks to buy/validate target grounding versus retain qualified fallback, tracking all whole-horizon original A/B orders and costs. The direct intention-to-treat identifies the **policy** choice, not the latent technical Q of an unpurchased policy. Changed body requires separate approved, version-pinned acquisition path and complete manufacturing lead time. No randomization of individual robots in one connected market; no post-random success conditioning.

**Falsify or restrict:** safety approval absent, E not pre-randomized, customer/service denominators missing, B original-order displacement ignored, cross-network model updater or common operator pool, changed baseline demand/commercial horizon, missing data rights, specialist better than grounding, training succeeds but customer orders expire. None of these is remedied by synthetic r. Keep the official BLS–BEA numeric sector-production backbone missing until an actual comparable-vintage source table is accessed; no vendor announcement substitution.
