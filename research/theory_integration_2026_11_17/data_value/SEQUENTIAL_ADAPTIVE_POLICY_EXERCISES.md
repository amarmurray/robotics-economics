# Exercises — longitudinal economic influence (student)

Use the chronology and variables defined in SEQUENTIAL_ADAPTIVE_BUYER_WORKER_POLICY.md. Work from the joint law, not separate demand and clearance averages.

1. **Full counterworld.** Enumerate the four (U,V) atoms and compute the t4 paid C coincidence under observed S and under each M regime. Show why D4(S)≥D4(M) for every atom does not identify the optimal recovery action.
2. **Support.** The firm's full historical G panel contains only S dispatches; the original offer registry is complete even for unserved buyers. Does that point identify G/M's value? What if separate prospective studies establish M's D and L marginal rates but never pair the reports? Give the sharp bound.
3. **Regret and safety.** With R=19/10, P=21/10, E=11/5 and G/S=2, calculate the nonanticipative minimax-regret mixture over E and G/M for the two M regimes. Compare with maximizing guaranteed contribution.
4. **Sequential transport.** Write the g-computation expression for an adaptive t3 action rule. Identify one post-treatment variable that must be integrated rather than held fixed at an R historical distribution. State the additional requirement if a new t4 dispatch action is proposed.
5. **Data acquisition.** If the old G/S model, action and training corpus remain eligible at zero additional cost, can free optional access to an unrelated source reduce the optimal program's value? Distinguish that fact from a compulsory, paid new training run.
