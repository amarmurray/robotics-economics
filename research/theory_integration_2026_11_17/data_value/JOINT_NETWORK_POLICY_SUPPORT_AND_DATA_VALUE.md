## 1. Original production treatment is a whole buyer–robot–operator policy

A preassignment cluster i consists of an original procurement parent, all of its original eligible orders (including refusals), the shared operator pool, robot/controller/sensor/build, time window, customer prices and quality terms, dispatch, and lawful recovery. A policy a must be independently admitted **as this complete joint configuration**; separate qualified grounded A and grounded B robots do not prove that running them simultaneously is physically or contractually legal. Source-trained X remains **excluded** from live consideration without signed observation-closed post-failure recovery; future counterfactual learning can still enter dynamic J.

Let Y_i(a) be complete original-network capturable contribution after accepted qualified services, actually collected payments, refusal, credits, scarce operator displacement and once-counted costs. Optimized V selects among feasible whole policies; a dynamic J already incorporates acquisition and continuation production. Market, physical and statistical support are different: a lawful policy can have missing original buyer response, and even a known buyer response cannot sign a physically unsafe robot policy. Technical accuracy cannot be mapped directly to dollars.

For an independently assigned **whole-network** arm A_i with known conditional propensity pi_i(a)>0, predeclared eligible network clusters and full original order/receipt outcomes, the standard inverse-probability contrast is

\[
\widehat{\tau}_{ab} = \frac1n\sum_i \left\{\frac{1(A_i=a)Y_i^{obs}}{\pi_i(a)}-\frac{1(A_i=b)Y_i^{obs}}{\pi_i(b)}\right\}.
\]

With preassignment ignorability/randomization, consistency and independent/noninterfering clusters, E[hat tau] equals the finite-population average of Y_i(a)-Y_i(b). **Proof:** conditional on the fixed potential outcome, each inverse-weighted indicator has expectation 1; subtract, sum. This is existing causal experimental theory, not identification of physical safety or of counterfactual outcomes without treatment overlap. If global model updates, procurement or operators span clusters, a larger cluster/exposure design is required.

**Important failure of coordinate positivity:** each order's marginal grounding probability can be positive even though the *whole* G-A+G-B policy has zero probability. Causal positivity must apply to the relevant policy/exposure, not to its individual robot labels. This is a concrete application of established partial-interference/exposure mapping theory (Hudgens & Halloran 2008; Aronow & Samii 2017).

## 3. Strong negative result: per-order assignment and infinite routine data can leave joint value unidentified

Suppose the *only* experimental assignments are RG and GR, each at probability 1/2 per original procurement cluster. Then each order separately has G marginal probability 1/2, but whole GG and RR probabilities are zero. Every observed original-network receipt is 4 in either world, for either arm. Even with original orders, prices, complete telemetry and infinite repetitions of this **same assignment rule**, joint GG demand, GG−E investment ranking and the actual value of simultaneously qualified G recipes cannot be learned without an additional transport assumption. All RR historical logs and hypothetical E outcomes are also identical under the construction.

With independent original parent budgets and prices as stipulated, **and** a truly optional feasible RR fallback at the preaction investment stage, the counterfactual *optimized gross* GG mean is conditionally bounded between 4 and 11/2. The budget-6 group can purchase at most one service at posted prices; the budget-8 group might purchase B with A or not; the incumbent option gives floor 4. H and L attain endpoints, so the interval is sharp under this restricted model, and crosses E=9/2. If parent budget, product price, contribution bounds, old-policy availability or demand menus change, **do not reuse the interval**.

A complete GG policy may be physically available but statistically unsupported; a policy physically unsigned is not an allowable treatment merely to gain positivity. The real scientific prerequisite is a signed combined G policy *and* an original buyer exposure that actually covers its joint quality/price menu.

## 4. What a lawful complete-policy study would identify

**Hypothetically only**, suppose RR, jointly certified GG and E are all physically approved, contracted and independently assigned to original-network clusters at known propensity 1/3 each. With no cross-cluster interference, the same HT expression unbiasedly estimates their average original-order contribution contrasts. The committed exact JavaScript executable enumerates all 3^4 = 81 synthetic allocations over the four clusters. It obtains:

| World / contrast | True constructed contrast = expected HT | Exact design variance for four clusters |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| H: GG−RR | 3/2 | 141/4 |
| L: GG−RR | 0 | 24 |
| H: GG−E | 1 | 41 |
| L: GG−E | −1/2 | 113/4 |

This is **design expectation**, not four observed experiments or a statistically precise estimate. For fixed potential outcomes and independently randomized whole clusters, the variance is exactly

\[
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\tau_{ab}) = \frac1{n^2}\sum_i \left[\frac{Y_i(a)^2}{q_a}+\frac{Y_i(b)^2}{q_b}-(Y_i(a)-Y_i(b))^2\right].
\]

**Proof:** only one arm is observed per cluster; the squared HT summand has expectation Y_i(a)^2/q_a + Y_i(b)^2/q_b, its expectation is Y_i(a)-Y_i(b), and independent cluster variances add. If a third arm uses positive propensity, the **oracle**, generally unimplementable, variance-minimizing ratio among remaining propensities is q_a:q_b = sqrt(sum_i Y_i(a)^2):sqrt(sum_i Y_i(b)^2). The very large toy variance makes clear that positivity guarantees identification in expectation under assumptions, not low cost, precision or safe deployment. Stratify recorded preassignment buyer/quality types prospectively; never use a post-treatment buyer purchase to define strata.

## 5. Data acquisition support is not a fungible trajectory quantity

Let latent theta be whether a buyer accepts the *joint* GG menu. Suppose all available production actions belong to S={RR,RG,GR,E}, and the **complete conditional observation kernel** of every action in S, including technical reports, operator trace, original orders, refusals and collected cash, is exactly the same under H and L at every possible attained history h. For any finite-horizon **adaptive** policy selecting only actions in S from its logged history and independent randomization, its entire data-history likelihood is identical under H and L.

**Proof by induction:** starting histories have identical law; the action-selection rule, conditional on a history, does not depend on inaccessible theta; and both worlds have the same conditional observation kernel for each chosen on-support action. Hence the next-history laws coincide; iterate. Any prior odds on theta are unchanged after such data; the gross Bayesian decision information value *specifically about theta* is zero irrespective of routine sample count. This is standard experiment-equivalence/Blackwell decision logic, not a new universal impossibility theorem. It **does not** imply free optional R data is worthless for robot reliability, source learning or other unknowns.

A **deliberate target** original-parent, legally binding GG-menu commitment might distinguish H and L without touching a robot, provided its relation to actual purchase/quality is valid. A nonbinding survey need not. An approved whole GG assignment can distinguish the states but cannot be run if the joint robot/operator configuration is unsigned. Source field trajectories, teleoperation, failures/recoveries, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained inputs may improve task qualification, transfer, or embodied model selection, but they do not automatically measure an unoffered buyer's joint procurement preference. Data transfer and data value must be mediated by source-to-joint qualification, lawfully admitted policies, customer response and the optimized V/J; no benchmark-point-to-dollar mapping.

A secondary **invented** equal-prior H/L illustration prices GG certification at 1/2 once, E already includes its 1-unit body cost, and RR remains optional. Without target evidence, expected GG net is 17/4 versus E=9/2, so choose E. A free, perfectly discriminating **binding** preinvestment buyer signal makes optimal conditional choice GG in H (net 5), E in L (9/2), with expectation 19/4. **Gross signal value is 1/4 normalized unit**, not a measured data price; real acquisition cost, selection/strategic change, loss of seller rights, or invalid signal transport can eliminate it. This value is already part of dynamic J if J models the signal and subsequent optimal policy, not an added continuation premium.
