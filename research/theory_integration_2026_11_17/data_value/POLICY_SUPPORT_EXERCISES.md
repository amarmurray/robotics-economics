# Student exercises — policy-induced support and production value

**Separate from instructor key.** Synthetic normalized contribution, not robot dollars.

1. Prove the first-exit bound K+[L,U]p from an equality of full supported *joint* history kernels across possible worlds. Which assumptions establish its sharpness? State why component-wise skill marginals cannot substitute.
2. F stays at s0 indefinitely; A reaches certified c with 3/4 probability and unsupported u otherwise. Explain why unlimited F source histories do not identify A's complete accepted service, even with a characterized first A step. How could a validated cross-body bridge or approved safe stop narrow the identified set?
3. With c=2, u=y∈[0,4], A cost 1/10, S=2, F=1, calculate the base interval. Separately compare fixed-occupancy source-trained c=3 with total cost 3/20, target-grounded u∈[3,4] with c=2 and total cost 3/20, and a fully supported new body gross 11/5, cost 1/4. Which robustly dominate S?
4. If the source-trained A policy instead reaches u with probability 1/2 and y can be zero, recompute its minimum return. Explain what matched real observations would invalidate a fixed-occupancy certificate; why cannot the economic result authorize exploration of u?
