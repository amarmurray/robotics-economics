# Exercises: customer demand and economic data value (synthetic)
1. Derive the exact quantity and capacity-price dual for uniform-price receipts \(q(\alpha-\beta q)\) with \(0\le q\le C\), and explain why physical throughput alone cannot cap economic value.
2. One observed X-qualified sale has q=1 and price8; compatible linear slopes β∈[2,4]. Find the exact range of maximum X revenue when E raises capacity from1 to2. Does optimal production always use the second slot?
3. With R inverse price 8−2q, costs K_X=3/2, K_E=1, calculate net R, X, E and X+E in β=2 and β=4 source worlds, plus the gross source/E economic interaction.
4. Prove that raising a fixed captured-demand intercept by δ≥0 is weakly more valuable with higher capacity; give a plausible robotics change to G that invalidates this result.
5. Compute the perfect equal-prior preinvestment audit EVPI in the two worlds, and construct a decreasing inverse-price curve matching price8 at q1 but exhibiting unbounded off-support gross receipts as its unobserved small-q price increases.
