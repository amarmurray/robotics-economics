# Exercises — joint lineage conversion

1. Prove the joint potential inequality by conditional expectation. Explain why a negative interaction coefficient cannot be paired with an upper bound on its feature alone.
2. Derive R and G for a known compatible pair with certain successor success, and for incompatible A,B. Compute the interaction term and the effect of one total procurement slot.
3. Derive L(x), its feasible interval and exact upper and lower for fixed q,m,s. Why is the perfect-report upper not a policy guarantee?
4. Reproduce the two worlds at q=1/2,m=s=5/16; derive the G/E rankings after acquisition cost. What audit discriminates?
5. Find a uniform m cap that guarantees E weakly dominates G for every report. Explain the difference between optional free routine data and forced acquisition.
