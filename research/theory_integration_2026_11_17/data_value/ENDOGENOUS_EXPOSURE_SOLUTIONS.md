# Instructor solutions — endogenous exposure and joint demand
**Separate from student exercises.** Conditional mathematics, not empirical calibration.

1. Write cells as: 111=t; 110=x−t; 101=y−t; 011=z−t; 100=a−x−y+t; 010=b−x−z+t; 001=d−y−z+t; 000=1−a−b−d+x+y+z−t. Nonnegativity yields L=max(0,x+y−a,x+z−b,y+z−d) and U=min(x,y,z,1−a−b−d+x+y+z). Every t in the interval defines a valid probability law, proving sharpness.
2. Each event is present in exactly two of four equiprobable states, so each marginal is 1/2. Every event pair occurs together in exactly one state, so every pair is 1/4. But U never has 111 (t=0), whereas V has 111 with probability 1/4. Pairwise independence does not imply mutual independence.
3. J_X=1+2+8t−2/5=13/5+8t. J_G=3+3/5=18/5, J_E=7/2 and incumbent J_I=3. J_X=J_G when t=1/8. U favors G and V favors X. Robust lower value for X is 13/5, versus 18/5 for G, so maximin G.
4. Before revelation, E[J_X]=(13/5+23/5)/2=18/5, tied with G. Perfect world information yields (max(13/5,18/5)+max(23/5,18/5))/2=(18/5+23/5)/2=41/10. Difference 41/10−18/5=1/2 *normalized contribution units*. A physical label is a noisy and selected observation with costs and cannot generally identify the population distribution; its net value requires the whole D1 design.
5. Need common payoff-relevant original-order state space, same legal/safe continuation menu, same f∈[m,M], and independently defensible TV(P,Q)≤epsilon for the **complete** joint state law. Then |E_P f−E_Q f|≤(M−m)epsilon. New embodiment creating a new task service, demand under source training differing in definition, or cross-operator interference destroys these conditions; matching marginal skills cannot repair that.
6. Register all original eligible customer orders before dispatch and version controller/source/report, sample at independent policy-closed networks, measure A,B,D on the **same linked order under approved X** plus complete-service/alternative displacement. Preserve failed/unaccepted and nonrecorded orders, shared operator/safety load and demand feedback. Independently sampled network-level assignment and physical approval are required for causal whole-policy contrasts. Accepted-only samples condition on success; they do not identify missed demand or the joint counterfactual under X.
