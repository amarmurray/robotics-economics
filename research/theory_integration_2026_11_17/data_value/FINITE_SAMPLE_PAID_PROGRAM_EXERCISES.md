# Exercises: finite-sample original-buyer SG/E economics

1. Under J_SG=12k−1, k∈[0,1/2], and J_E∈[3,5], derive where perfect k can certify E weak dominance, SG strict dominance, or neither. Contrast k with the perfectly known joint technical qualification h when willingness/settlement is coupled.
2. Suppose lawful independent whole-network SG observations X_i and E observations Z_j are Bernoulli and payoffs 12X−1 and 8Z−1. Derive a two-sided fixed-n Hoeffding confidence interval for Δ and a confidence-qualified regret bound when selecting the empirical winner.
3. Derive the range-optimal SG:E sample split for N total equal-cost independent clusters. Generalize to budget c_S n_S+c_E n_E=C. Explain why this allocation may not be economically optimal or physically lawful.
4. In a matched pair of equal-weight clusters with SG potentials (11,−1) and E potentials (7,−1), enumerate the two fair-swap observed differences, their mean, and a valid bound for m independent pairs. Compare to the iid unpaired range radius for 600 clusters.
5. Explain how shared online weights or buyer procurement interference invalidate simple iid evaluation. Propose a complete customer/order/qualification/paid receipt and data-use-rights ledger without counting continuation value twice.
