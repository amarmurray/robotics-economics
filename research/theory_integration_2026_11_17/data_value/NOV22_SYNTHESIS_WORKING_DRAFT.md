## Proposed central claim at the right level of abstraction

When robot capability improvements become economically productive is jointly determined by: (i) which **complete** services become qualified and safely supported, (ii) whether those services complement existing production recipes and match actual customers, (iii) which physical, human, data and organizational resources remain bottlenecks, (iv) how deployment produces both accepted service **and** valuable observations, and (v) which future customer orders and embodied capabilities the current deployment itself brings into reach. Raw cross-embodiment benchmark accuracy is not a measure of captured customer value. This is a **candidate economic synthesis**, not an established novel general theorem or evidence that robotics adoption follows a particular measured curve.

The canonical sequence is:
\[
\underbrace{(x_t,g_t,h_t,\theta)}_{\text{source, target alignment, body, regime}}
\ \xrightarrow[\text{intervention, safe support, rights}]{\text{learning/validation}}
\ Q_{t+1}
\ \xrightarrow[\text{scarce resources, qualification bundles}]{V(Q;\omega)}
\ \text{accepted customer production}
\ \xrightarrow[\text{market response}]{}
\ \omega_{t+1}
\ \xrightarrow[\text{physical/financial constraints}]{}
\ \text{next acquisition choice}.
\]
This is a causal/economic architecture, not a plug-in data premium. Reports and corrections affect both inference about \(\theta\) and actual controller competence. Demand and visited state support depend on the chosen policy; post-treatment states cannot serve as unconditional transport strata.

## Theory spine already established conditionally

1. **Static production:** LP dual/resource-price certificates explain when a new qualified recipe changes optimized output; O-ring/complementary services can produce large joint returns despite weak standalone returns. These are standard optimization/production theory foundations. The richer \(f(Q;\omega)\) is required when acceptance is bundled or shared bottlenecks bind.
2. **Data and learning:** DDA already endogenizes future data and production in its §5 and data aggregation in Appendix B. The project's conditional source-to-qualification map, Hessian and customer-conditioned mixtures re-express familiar comparative statics in the robotics context. Target grounding, feedback and embodiment can create positive, negative or demand-irrelevant technical transfer; free optional data weakly expands the available menu, forced training may hurt.
3. **Endogenous acquisition:** Productive routine field trajectories, failures/recoveries, deliberate teleoperation, simulation, public/synthetic inputs and source mixtures have different selection rules and costs. First corrections change subsequent safe visitation and information. A gated observation is not a random sample of latent errors. Qualified acceptance and independent recovery audit are required.
4. **Identification and physical feasibility:** Marginal skill rates do not determine joint accepted recipes. Matched same-world policy constraints can bound investment differences; report × recovery positive support, not expected operator use, can rule out a policy. Source-study bounds cannot be transported to another customer order population without valid conditional transport and positivity.
5. **Dynamic demand and ambiguity:** The latest [full-history analysis](ENDOGENOUS_DEMAND_PERSISTENT_TRANSFER.md) permits actions to change follow-on customer orders and eligible observation support. Under an uncertain **one-time** prior over a persistent regime, paired comparisons are affine initial-prior endpoint problems with known conditional kernels, not stage-rectangular robust Bellman games. An explicit example differentiates best pure maximin action, same-world dominance and the value of feasible fleet-level mixing. R-only logs cannot point-identify G/L outcomes absent a bridge assumption.

## Exact Model A / Model B reductions and accounting test

Model A is the special case of a single divisible accepted service \(V(Q;\omega)=\rho\max\{0,\max_{0\le q\le K(Q)}[(a-c)q-bq^2/2]-\Pi_0\}\). When qualifications add only capacity, a marginal benefit is the **difference** in this customer contribution, which varies with \(a,c,b,K,\Pi_0,\rho\); it may be zero even with impressive technical transfer. A customer service requiring two jointly qualified recipes is not automatically divisible and needs the general \(V\).

Model B assumes deterministic postdeployment \((a_i,c_i,K)\), collected \(m_i\), and one binary financeable expansion. With its timing, cash feasibility and provider capture reproduced exactly, its policy value is \(m_i+\rho S_i(K)+\max_{x\text{ feasible}}\{x[\rho(S_i(K+\Delta)-S_i(K))-I]\}\). The fully dynamic \(J\) reduces to this only with known transitions, matching calendars and one count of every collected flow. No claim that the 18 November synthetic G/R example re-estimates the prior H/L cash example. The new work generalizes which joint qualified configurations and demand states can appear, not Model B's already-known finance arithmetic.

**Accounting rejection test:** A proposed proof is wrong if a period's customer contribution appears in both \(m_t\) and terminal \(V\), if future data value is added after its effect already enters \(Q_{t+1}\) and \(V\), or if a lender transfer is counted as new aggregate customer surplus. Preserve actor-specific receipts and rights separately only when the relevant financing constraint actually binds.

## Strongest novelty objections — unresolved, not hidden

- DDA has production/data feedback and complementary source accumulation; claiming this concept as a new discovery is incorrect.
- Process flexibility, LP duality and O-ring qualification chains have mature prior literatures. The finite marginal/Hessian decomposition is algebra, not scientific novelty by itself.
- Conditional transport, statistical generalizability, missing-support bounds and interference already have formal literatures (Pearl/Bareinboim, Stuart et al., Manski, Zivich et al., Hudgens/Halloran).
- Rectangular robust DP and recursive multiple priors are mature tools (Iyengar 2005; Epstein/Schneider 2003; Nilim/El Ghaoui 2005); a persistent regime and nonrectangular ambiguity *alone* are not original robotics economics results.
- Recasting a selected synthetic parameterization as a universal economic mechanism would be wrong; unmeasured customer demand and actual robotic recovery matter.

The **potential contribution** is a precise, identifiable robotics enterprise production/deployment proposition connecting *accepted customer service* to jointly qualified skill and actionable data acquisition under common physical constraints and adaptive demand. To establish distinctiveness, it needs a comparison against actual robot deployment technical papers, recognized process/production organization work, and a field-data design that can distinguish its causal mechanism from a specialist or entirely productive-data alternative. These are not accomplished by existence proofs.

## Two discriminating empirical designs and their negative counterparts

**Design A — source/grounding/embodiment mechanism.** For matched base customer windows and approved robot safety budgets, factorially assign selected source mixture and target grounding, with a frozen retained policy and qualified specialist control; where allowable, compare embodiment redesign. Report independently audited target-state exposure, missed gating, joint held-out accepted tasks, peak recovery, calendar/curation costs and customer order outcome. The primary estimand is *additional accepted whole-service production* under identical demand/opportunity resources, not reported task-completion percentages. Strong null: productive field experience already covers all valuable states at no incremental cost, so paid grounding has no advantage.

**Design B — policy-induced order response.** On a common pre-action customer opportunity registry, vary initial rollout mode by independent learning pool (or document credible assignment) and follow **all** customers, including zero future orders. Compare route-induced future demand/price/capture and service mix, qualified controller state, and actually approved operator use. Strong null: demand never depends on route once base site/customer factors are controlled and qualification outcomes remain stable; then the simpler conditional fixed-\(\mu\) certificate may be sufficient. Missing G/L safe access remains a partial-identification problem, not an excuse for an unsafe trial.

## 18 November adversarial addition — partial knowledge is not independent shocks

The previous endpoint theorem is a valid *known conditional-kernel* special case. The new [coupled response-type layer](COUPLED_IDENTIFICATION_AND_AUDIT.md) explicitly exposes its measurement dependence: the common law must jointly govern latent transfer, policy-induced customer order, held-out accepted service and pathwise recovery. For fixed adaptive policies, partial observed moments permit a linear same-world paired-value bound and an interpretable dual moment certificate **only relative to the response-type set admitted**. If controller interference, persistent transfer likelihood products or cross-site order-response invariances cut that polytope nonlinearly, the linear bound is a conservative relaxation, not claimed sharp for the physical model.

The current synthetic dual/primal demonstration is deliberately decision-relevant: both transfer alignment and baseline high-value customer prevalence equal 1/2, but their unidentified co-occurrence changes R−G from −3 to +2. At the population level a valid calibrated safe report can potentially tighten the co-occurrence bounds; the required diagnostic classification, site transport, recovery support and future *zero-order* denominator are **not available observations**. More testable source logs of only R may fail to identify the missing G route, and apparent perfect accuracy does not make a report safe.

A useful negative result refines the value-of-data claim: when independent sites permit 1/5 routine and 4/5 grounding allocation, that maximin hedge already matches the worst economic value of a hypothetical oracle revealing only the population coupling. Such an oracle creates no additional maximin guarantee, while a pure indivisible fleet gains 0.4 normalized contribution. Minimax-regret instead chooses 2/5 routine. This is an important design objection to treating every mathematically informative data collection as an economically necessary robotics deployment: portfolio flexibility, relevant information level, objective and genuine operator feasibility determine its value. This is standard decision theory and cannot be the sole novelty claim.

**Revision obligation for November 22:** harmonize theorem naming and assumptions without suppressing negative results; distinguish demonstrated **finite-response-type** sharpness from unverified physical-kernel sharpness, externally validated field report from a made-up H label, and random-assignment *mean* effect identification from unit-level counterfactual coupling. Include safe specialist, no-retrain and free productive byproduct controls. Do not mark an audited customer service unit, actual BEA/BLS matrix, prior-art distinctiveness or independent reviewer complete.

## November 18 network-coupling objection to the independent pool synthesis

The [network interference continuation](NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_THEORY.md) tests the assumption hidden in the safe-pool factorial and selective customer-stratum certificates: nominally distinct pools may share controller updates, hard human recovery capacity and customer opportunities. These are three **different edge types**, not one transfer coefficient. The complete network history, rather than the pool, becomes the experiment and economic decision unit when any live edge can change production, qualification or demand. The mean approved full-network policy can still be identified by a registered network-level randomized design with positive vector support; sequential off-policy use also requires positive history-conditioned assignment probabilities. This is prior causal-inference mathematics, not the paper's novel estimator.

**Direct correction to a previous positive numerical certificate:** isolated supported A improvement of 0.5 at 70% share produces gain 0.35 only if the remaining B customers are unaffected. With unknown spillover s to the 30% group and rollout cost c, the paired target policy difference is 0.35 - 0.3s - c. Conditional s in [-0.4,1.5] changes the full value interval to [6.90-c,7.47-c] versus old 7, so the earlier 7.35 point is no longer a robust connected-network guarantee. A resource-justified upper bound s_U< (0.35-c)/0.3 would restore a strict certificate (and pathwise safety remains separately necessary). Demand substitution can conceal improved controller qualification; matched frozen-demand acceptance and all original orders are needed to separate mechanisms.

**Safety objection:** identical per-site 10% recovery rates and 0.2 mean operator load do not determine whether jointly deploying two otherwise approved routes breaches a one-operator instantaneous cap: one coupled world has positive simultaneous recovery support, the other has none. Treating independently approved routes as jointly approved is mathematically invalid. No suggested experiment here licenses an unsafe route.

**Gate implications:** reconcile production, data, operator and customer-network notation in Models A/B; choose whether first-paper scope can credibly include this network layer or should use it as a formal boundary. ## November 18 bounded-network synthesis: economic approximation is not safety authorization

[Approximate network remainder theory](APPROXIMATE_NETWORK_REMAINDER_THEORY.md) extends the prior full-network spillover correction rather than declaring all remote edges negligible. A common persistent transfer regime θ and the **entire policy-dependent** order/controller/qualification/recovery history remain the counterfactual objects. If every feasible matched prefix admits a bounded transition error εθ,t between true and radius-r complete kernels and matched current-service mismatch δθ,t, finite-horizon coupling bounds each policy's customer contribution error by \(E_\theta(\pi)=\sum\delta+\sum\epsilon M\), with M the remaining bounded qualified-production value span. A policy π strictly beats a retained or specialist alternative π0 under these assumptions only when the surrogate **same-world** lower difference exceeds both error budgets for each θ; a scalar radius correlation or independently minimized values is not enough. Strong structural locality gives a finite causal cone; global checkpoints, adaptive orders or operator dispatch can defeat locality.

The earlier selective A policy's net lower gain becomes \(.12-.3\eta\) if B loss from measured local links is at most .6 per B order, remote additional loss ≤η, own-A unobserved loss ≤.02 and coordination cost .03. At illustrative η=.25 this would certify +.045 **on economics only**; η≥.4 destroys strict certification. A separate two-regime finite history demonstrates that the transition-TV envelope can be too wide to certify a real small adaptive grounding benefit, even if exact synthetic kernels show it in both regimes. These are sensitivity inputs, not measured market externalities or independent novelty.

Hard safety is a discontinuity: for arbitrarily small η>0, switching a disjoint recovery law to a law with η probability of simultaneous two-operator need violates peak capacity one while altering its distribution by TV 2η and preserving all individual marginal recovery probabilities. Neither a positive economic lower bound nor 1,000 apparent safe trials is a hard-cap certificate. A tested safe admission/abort option is a *different* complete economic policy, not an adjustment that deletes rare outcomes for free. This affects the **physical feasibility** of Model A/B specializations, not their cash algebra; retained old versions, competent specialists, nonlinear learning interactions and endogenous demand remain first-class alternatives.
