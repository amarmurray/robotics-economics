# Original buyer / learned-source report-sufficiency evidence protocol (unpopulated)

**21 November 2026 UTC.** Prospective research specification only. No customer/robot access, signed deployment, randomized intervention, learner training, financial contract or real trial is authorized by this document. This is the empirical counterpart to [ECONOMICALLY_SUFFICIENT_REPORTS_AND_DATA_VALUE.md](ECONOMICALLY_SUFFICIENT_REPORTS_AND_DATA_VALUE.md), inside the same X/G/E/R and qualified-original-service research.

## What must be measured, by level and timing

| Level | Required fields | What it can identify | Critical failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw source episode | content hash, family/collision signature, source modality, action/provenance and rights, success/failure/recovery, timestamps | reproducible source composition; independent failure family audits | episode count is neither unique information nor admitted updates |
| Learner ingestion | controller and dataset revision, prefilter proposals, admitted transition count, language/goal-image eligibility, update budget, compute, shared model lineage | effective dose and support of X/target variants | proposed mixture differs from admitted training; same family copied |
| Shadow target predecision reports | immutable context, task truth when measurable, report vector S, compressed M, G observation, generation time relative to action | technical information, whether detailed reports actually distinguish actions | postfailure observations treated as if predecision; report shifts qualification |
| Whole qualified service | original buyer/order and refusal, operator/fleet/access capacity, physically signed A/B/E/R and applicable recovery, joint qualification Q, chosen dispatch, completion, acceptance, refunds, captured paid receipt | complete-policy economic response under supported assignment | action not approved, missing original refused orders, R not recoverable |
| Allocation/competition | shared buyer, operator, model-update and robotics network cluster, contemporaneous alternative capacity and order spillovers, assignment propensities | policy-level causal contribution with interference boundary | per-robot randomization cannot support joint network policy contrast |

For the pure within-X report experiment, the **controller, embodiment, original buyer population, A/B/R menu and production response law are held fixed**. Only the information made available **before** signed dispatch changes. Full S and compressed M can be recorded in shadow mode without changing actual dispatch, but their counterfactual economic values require causal support for the alternative signed actions in each report cell. If an action is not physically signed, it is absent; no experiment should force it to manufacture positivity. For X-source vs G-train vs E comparisons, randomize or identify **whole network programs**, not the report bit alone. Retain right-to-reuse, compute/curation, licensure and full-market variable costs separately, without repricing technical accuracy into dollars.

## Pre-specified estimands

1. Fixed-learner report value: E[max_d E(U_d|S)] - E[max_d E(U_d|M)]. Estimate conditional complete paid-service response vectors only where signed action support and original-buyer coverage permit. Report interval bounds otherwise.
2. Training/development total effect: J(X)-J(G), J(X)-J(E), J(a)-J(R) on full signed original-network potential outcomes, including altered reports, qualification, customer behavior, recovery and resource use. Do not call it an isolated source transfer mechanism.
3. Mechanism diagnostics: original task-label loss; admission/update counts; independent episode family coverage; same-target report and joint qualification; target-operator context. A better benchmark without a changed action ranking or new qualified production does **not** establish economic value.
4. Physical/economic feasibility: signed A/B/E/R action space and recoverable old R per complete observation history; raw and priced operator/fleet capacity; original customer offers/accept/refuse/paid/returned service; interference closure.

Compare V of the optimal **whole-network feasible** program, with and without reports, and then costs of source/G/E. If distinct customers compete for one operator, the decision variable d is the full feasible allocation; per-customer maxima are invalid. A calibrated LP dual can interpret a measured capacity bottleneck only at its supported basis. Log registered safety or privacy redactions and affected support.

## Two decisive negative controls

- An observation of target label θ that improves task prediction but leaves all action-specific complete-service outcome rankings unchanged should have zero **within-controller dispatch** value after costs.
- An ex ante operator/physical-context report independent of task θ but changing which signed action earns accepted original-customer service can have positive value even when label accuracy is constant. Check temporal availability, protection and full paid receipt response. Do not infer the second from the first using a task benchmark.

## Public-source boundary and scientific gate

BridgeData V2, DROID and Open X-Embodiment provenance may inform *technical source* selection and experimental logging, subject to individual content rights; none of the currently inspected public records provide original buyer orders, signed whole-network policy dispatch, joint Q×S×buyer receipts and valid production assignment. No signed comparative learner, no paid receipts and no official BLS/BEA quantitative industry cell were obtained. The November 22 Model A/B scientific acceptance gate remains open. The next constructive instrument is a **schema and physically approved support map**, not a fabricated company/warehouse dataset. A primary-source official interindustry matrix should only enter the atlas after actual numeric retrieval and version concordance.
