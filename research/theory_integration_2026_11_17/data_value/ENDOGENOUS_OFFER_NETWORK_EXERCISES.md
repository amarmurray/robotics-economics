# Exercises — joint customer demand, training data and physical capacity

All figures are synthetic accepted-and-paid net service values. Attempt without looking at the [separate instructor solutions](ENDOGENOUS_OFFER_NETWORK_SOLUTIONS.md).

1. With A and B each optionally offered, derive the one-operator value for nonnegative v_A,v_B and the two-independent-operator value. Explain why two robots with the same recovery worker still have one effective capacity slot.
2. Set Pr(Z_A)=Pr(Z_B)=1/2. Show the sharp joint opportunity interval. Re-derive the three whole-program P,D,E values from the chapter (including the P first-date paid service net of cost, the D data cost and E physical cost). Which arm wins each policy-induced order-overlap counterworld? At what common overlap x do P and E tie?
3. Construct worlds with exactly the same selected paid P=A success record but different unobserved B original offers and therefore opposite P/D investment comparisons. What legal, properly timestamped predecision information distinguishes them?
4. Derive sharp one-arm terminal contribution bounds with known m_A,m_B and service values. Why is a difference of two *individually* sharp intervals not necessarily jointly sharp under a common learner/customer population?
5. Derive the full-history add/subtract reference identity in the chapter. What does approved whole-connected-network randomization identify directly, and why does causal decomposition into source transfer versus induced order demand need additional assumptions?
