# Fleet-generated data and the migration of scarce complementary resources
24 November 2026. Evidence extension of [Policy-Closed Economic Data Value](POLICY_CLOSED_DATA_VALUE.md) and the [first-paper evidence matrix](COMMON_CHECKPOINT_EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS.md). Not an isolated new candidate contribution.

## Primary technical evidence
Source: Collective Experience Learning (CEL), Tsinghua University, AgiBot and Infinigence AI, arXiv:2610.25736v3, displayed 30 October 2026, https://arxiv.org/html/2610.25736v3 . Read selected §§1, 4.1.4–4.5.2, Appendix D.4, H.1 Table 11(e), H.2 on 24 November; no PDF equivalence, full replication or external review asserted.

After expert initialization, FERT uses robot-produced successful actions, with failures directing retrieval. Humans still label, monitor and reset. Phone packaging: 95/120 successes at initialization; after training with 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 and 96 robots, respectively 61, 85, 104, 111, 114, 118 successes out of 120 per evaluation, on two held-out robots of the same platform. The first two configurations regress. This is not a universal scale law; collection time per robot is fixed, not aggregate data/compute/capital; no independent paid program versus continued human correction. The one 96-robot run reports 9,834 episodes; 65 robots executing on average; 8.3 robot-hours of incident losses (upper estimate); 10:37–18:30 less one-hour lunch. Appendix D.1 specifies 48 cloud GPUs. These are collection and test metrics, not customer acceptance or human staffing clocks.

## Derived denominator reconciliation, not a counterfactual experiment
The non-lunch clock is 413 minutes. Scheduled exposure = 96*413/60 = 660.8 robot-hours; approximate executing exposure = 65*413/60 = 447.42; remaining = 213.38. Executing share 67.71%, versus approximately 98.7% local-fault availability. These are different measures. The 8.3 upper-estimated fault hours are only 3.89% of the implied non-executing hours; the remainder must NOT be classified as all labor, reset waste or available spare capacity. The average active count is rounded. Do not scale episode output by this ratio or equate scheduled time to paid human time.

If we freeze the other blockers, task schedule and demand, and remove only the recorded robot-local fault intervals, the direct increment in potentially executing exposure cannot exceed 8.3 robot-hours (illustratively active fraction at most 68.96%). This is a conditional accounting ceiling, not an actual achievable intervention or bound on total adaptive learning value. In particular, a fault-free trajectory can induce later learning and change reset or task durations, violating this frozen-clock counterfactual. No claim that the paper measures such a causal intervention.

Proof: in common robot-time coordinates let F denote recorded fault mask, O all other blockers, I=F union O. Removing F with O fixed releases exactly F minus O, whose measure equals measure(I)-measure(O), between zero and measure(F). Both endpoints can arise by changing overlap if O unmeasured. Only if other eligibility, scheduling and demand coincide can all released time become execution. For technical data yield one also needs admissibility, labels, rights, and matching to target failure contexts. No lower bound above zero follows from the fault trace alone.

## Constructive mechanism: bottleneck migration from expert action to eligibility infrastructure
In a restricted single-window Model A suppose all potentially collectible episodes are homogeneous, safe and equally valuable in a known source class; a is the number of robot episode slots, l the number that can actually be labeled/reset/verified under staffed and licensed support, and pairing can be freely scheduled. Then q=min(a,l) is the exact maximum eligible count. This is not a measurement of CEL's human time; label/reset units are episode equivalents, not hours. For nonnegative expansions f of physical episode capacity and h of label/reset capacity,
    q(a+f,l)-q(a,l) = min(f,max(0,l-a)),
and the cross-difference
    q(a+f,l+h)-q(a,l+h)-q(a+f,l)+q(a,l)
      = max(0,min(a+f,l+h)-max(a,l)).
Proof: express the first increment as length of [a,a+f] below l. Increasing l to l+h adds precisely the intersection with [l,l+h]. For integer slots, endpoints have zero measure and length equals count. Complementarity can be zero or as large as min(f,h). At a=l=4 and f=h=2 each isolated expansion adds zero, the bundle adds two. This illustrates why eliminating expert corrections need not eliminate marginal scarcity of other complementary inputs. It is a standard Leontief/resource-incidence special case, not a new theorem of production economics.

The assumptions fail with heterogeneous episode durations, uncertain failure contexts, noninterchangeable operators, joint rights, and dated queues. In Model A retain the existing qualified recipe matrix; replace homogeneous capacity by dated eligible source recipes with actual label/reset/compute resource columns. In Model B let source exposure and future qualification respond jointly to the feasible policy, with the old learner retained. A technically smaller source corpus can dominate if it covers the binding failure contexts. A positive cross-difference in eligible quantity is not necessarily a positive cross-difference in economic J, because target production values, costs, future demand and substitution may change. A single recovered rare trajectory can unlock an arbitrarily valuable new service in a model with no bounded terminal reward; hence the 8.3-hour ceiling does not bound economic data value by 1.3% of anything.

A conditional dynamic certificate does survive: let baseline optimal V_t be defined on all augmented-program reachable states (including those created by new data), with a common transition law for common actions. Augment the action set only at histories where the repaired capacity makes a genuinely additional action feasible; assume old actions remain available. For any augmented policy pi, its net reward difference from baseline optimal at the initial state equals the expected discounted sum of baseline Bellman advantages A_t(s,a)=r_t(s,a)+beta E[V_{t+1}|s,a]-V_t(s), with common terminal value. Proof: telescope along pi. Common actions have A<=0, additional actions can have positive advantage. Thus if a verified upper bound B_t(s) covers the positive advantage of every added action, the optimized increment is at most E under the augmented optimum of sum beta^t B_t(s) times indicator of a chosen additional action. If cost already enters r do not subtract it again. Otherwise subtract incremental fixed cost once and keep the baseline as an optional branch. This certificate is conditional on verified reachable-state economic advantage bounds and cannot be populated from lab rates. Standard Bellman performance-difference reasoning, extended here as an evidence requirement, not claimed novelty. Related prior work: CEL itself develops failure retrieval and local fault containment, DDA §5 future data and Appendix B complementary data, and the prior dated reserve and policy-closed chapters.

## Measurement and first-paper decision
This provides a stronger technical rival to deliberate collection: same-platform fleet experience can improve a task without new expert actions after initialization, and small-scale forced updates can hurt. Preserve the free optional old-policy principle. For each independent full-program arm, separate original service order / robot-clock denominator, eligible episode and retained data lineage, human expert correction, nonexpert label/reset/safety clocks, cloud cost, downtime cause/overlap, and later recipient acceptance/receipts. Observe original orders even when not attempted, and record independently evolving checkpoints. Compare additional grounding, routine reuse, and independent embodiment under common service rights and budgets. If routine reuse can match the grounded feasible dated service at no higher full cost, the policy-emulation bound rules out a grounding premium. Otherwise measure the uncovered service and economically weighted continuation, not just performance gains. No measured matched paid program, customer rights or dated human clocks in this source; no new contemporary official input-output edge. Evidence supports the mechanism as a technical possibility only.
