# Student exercises — joint qualification, information and embodiments

These exercises use only the **synthetic** complete-policy economy in [theory](JOINT_QUALIFICATION_TIMING_AND_DATA_VALUE.md). No source-data performance, customer market or robot safety is measured.

1. With p_A=3/5, p_B=3/5, recover the entire feasible interval for joint development qualification q by deriving the four-cell event table. Explain why setting q=p_A p_B is an additional assumption.
2. An original buyer is equally likely to request A or B. The retained policy earns 2; the matched qualified specialist earns 4, otherwise zero. A target report is right with probability 3/4. Derive the gross value of flexible F, prelocked L (with certified R), and joint-certificate B, as functions of q.
3. If the qualified reconfigurable body costs an additional 1/5 normalized unit, determine for which q F beats L. Can the decision be established from marginal qualifications alone?
4. Construct two joint distributions of (Q_A,Q_B,θ) sharing P(Q_A)=P(Q_B)=1/2 and P(Q_AQ_B)=0 but reversing the economic usefulness of the available specialist under a perfectly revealing target report.
5. Explain why the computed values need qualified complete policies, buyer purchases, operator/resource costs and payment outcomes. Give one reason that source-training log counts or separate technical success rates cannot identify original customer captured contribution.
