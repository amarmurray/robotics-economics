### Explicit proof gaps

- The physical qualification transition conditional on selected labels is a **primitive**, not a learned or validated structural law.
- Exposure e and gate g can respond to H, robot policy, observed history and customer demand; I1 is not a result for all such adaptive experiments.
- The exact two-date example does not solve full multiperiod safe control, multi-robot simultaneous production, cash constraints, or endogenous optimal embodiment manufacture.
- Customer-qualified production payoffs are synthetic; they cannot be read from ThriftyDAgger, IntervenGen or benchmark percentages.
- Closest policy-induced sampling, active querying, Bayesian information value and process-flexibility theory precede the present integration. Scientific contribution is not selected.

Endogenize **sequential exposure support**: a first commercial episode may repair the policy, change which states are subsequently visited, and alter whether a second deliberate experiment is informative or redundant. Derive an adaptive retention/correction policy or an impossibility/counterexample using a shared production/safety budget. Empirical complement: independently audit detection sensitivity and held-out complete-service qualification rather than fitting transfer to correction counts alone.
