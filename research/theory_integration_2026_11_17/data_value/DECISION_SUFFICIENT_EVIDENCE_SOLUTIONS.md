# Instructor solutions: decision-sufficient robotics program evidence

Companion to [student exercises](DECISION_SUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_EXERCISES.md).

1. Worst positive SG−E gap is 5−3=2 and worst E−SG gap is 5−(−1)=6. For P(SG)=p, worst regret max{2(1−p),6p}; equating yields p=1/4 and regret 3/2. Deterministic SG incurs worst 6 while E incurs worst 2; choose E with regret 2.
2. Given a, if a≤3, E dominates; for 3≤a≤5, residual mixed regret (a−3)(5−a)/2, maximum 1/2 at a=4; pure residual maximum 1. Given b, mixed residual (5−b)(b+1)/6 with maximum 4/3 at b=3, pure maximum 2. Net worst losses including test cost: observe a 1/2+1/2=1; observe b 4/3+1/8=35/24; no test 3/2, so a-test ranks first *under the specified hypothetical oracle and feasible randomized-cluster menu*. The difference is reduction in worst-case regret, not Bayesian expected cash gain or learned policy production.
3. Concordant joint law has Pr(Q,C) over (0,0),(1,1) each 1/2, so k=1/2, SG=5>E=4. Discordant has (0,1),(1,0) each 1/2, so k=0, SG=−1<E=4. Marginal Q and C remain 1/2; even their individual reports do not supply their joint paid event.
