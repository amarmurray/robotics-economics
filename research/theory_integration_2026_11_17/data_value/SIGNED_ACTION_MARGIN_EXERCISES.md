# Student exercises — partially identified economic value of robotics reports

**Companion to** [the proof](SIGNED_ACTION_MARGIN_SUPPORT_AND_REPORT_VALUE.md). These are hypothetical normalized original-customer production units, not field prices or measured robot probabilities. Do not consult the separate instructor solutions until attempting proofs.

**1. Signed-action gap.** For two permitted joint service actions A and B, with fine Y, coarse Z=φ(Y), and conditional original-customer contribution margin δ_y=m_A(y)−m_B(y), show directly that gross optimized fine-versus-coarse value is Σ_z min(P_z,N_z) where P_z is the coarse-cell probability-weighted positive advantage and N_z its negative analogue. Explain how a task-label predictor can improve while this value remains zero.

**2. Incomplete support.** All three predecision reports compress into a single coarse category, with p=(1/2,1/4,1/4), and interval margins [1/4,1/2], [−1/2,−1/4], [−1/2,1/2]. Prove a nonzero lower bound and an exact upper bound. Construct economic potential response means m_A,m_B∈[0,1] attaining both endpoints. Is point identification necessary for a positive gross value certificate?

**3. Falsify naïve component valuation.** Add a third fully licensed complete embodiment E that has the highest original-customer contribution in every report cell, independent of reports. Is the incremental full-menu value of the fine report still at least the two-action lower bound? Identify the invalid inference.

**4. Rectangularity.** Prove the formula for the minimum when each δ lies independently inside an interval. Find a simple two-report example where a cross-report physical constraint δ_2=δ_1 means treating the two intervals as independent gives a false strictly positive upper bound.

**5. General menu LP.** Let \(\mathcal A\) contain all signed fine-report complete policies and \(\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A\) contain those using only coarse reports, and let response vectors lie in a compact feasible polytope. Derive a family of LPs that computes the sharp lower and upper economic information value. State why you cannot maximize each customer's value separately under one shared operator.

**6. Which evidence next?** In exercise 2, a prospective fixed-program observation costs c=1/8 units. Is the investment conclusion identified? What exactly must be measured (and where) to tighten the decision while respecting physical approval and original customer order denominators? Distinguish a source-archive retraining intervention from a free report refinement.
