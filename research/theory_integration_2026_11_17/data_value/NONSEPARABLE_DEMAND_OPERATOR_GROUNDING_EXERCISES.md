# Student exercises: cross-demand, data transfer and operator economics
19 Nov 2026. Questions only; [separate instructor solutions](NONSEPARABLE_DEMAND_OPERATOR_GROUNDING_SOLUTIONS.md). Hypothetical normalized units only.

**1. What does the customer actually reveal?** Two qualified service categories face P_A=a−2q_A−γq_B and P_B=b−2q_B−γq_A. Under a=9+γ/2,b=7+γ/2, the retained policy serves q=(1/2,1/2). Compute both prices and total captured receipt. Explain why an accepted-order observation cannot identify γ. What prospective measurement would discriminate γ=−1 from +1, and what causal difficulty remains?

**2. Three investments in one qualification window.** Resource operator constraint is u_Aq_A+q_B≤O, q_B≤1. Incumbent R: q_A≤1/2,u_A=1,O=1. X source learning raises q_A cap to1 and costs1/4. G halves u_A and costs11/10. E raises O to3/2 with a certified body-plus-recovery package and costs11/5. Only one of X,G,E can occupy the engineering validation window. Derive exact gross V_R,V_X,V_G,V_E as functions of γ∈[−1,1]; compare net policy choices at γ=−1 and +1. Is the top choice identified from one baseline observation?

**3. Shadow value versus benchmark improvement.** With a differentiable qualified-production optimum, write the operator capacity dual multiplier λ. Derive the first-order value of reducing u_A by δ without changing q_A qualification, and the first-order value of increasing O. Under what conditions might G have positive economic value with unchanged task qualification?

**4. Sign of complementarity.** In the two-service model, show G+E gross equals E gross and calculate Δ_GΔ_E V. Now consider a single complete customer job requiring A and B together, q_A=q_B=z, p=8, resource requirement2z, O=1, demand cap D=1. G saves1/2 operator unit per complete job and E raises O by1/2. Find all four gross values and interaction sign. Identify the assumption behind the reversal.

**5. Robust policy selection.** If γ is unknown but fixed in [-1,1], calculate the maximin **pure** engineering intervention (including R). Show that an ex-ante equal-probability X/G lottery has expected worst-case return 561/80 under a common policy-invariant γ. Explain the distinction between expected and realized safety and name an interference mechanism that invalidates the guarantee.

**6. Measurement and theory.** Propose a prospective, rights-cleared original customer order/quote/refusal design to distinguish cross-demand, source-to-joint qualification, and operator savings without equating robot benchmark scores with customer dollars. Explain why the same price schedule need not survive new embodiments.
