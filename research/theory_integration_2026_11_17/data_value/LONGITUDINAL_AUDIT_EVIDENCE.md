# Prospective original-buyer / learner / worker causal support and sampling ledger

**Status: research design only, unpopulated; no real buyer/firm data or permission to run tests.**

**Causal unit and interference closure.** Assign at the connected cluster of shared robot, learner checkpoint, original customers and recovery-operator pool. Log site/service/time, whether an independent embodiment truly brings an additional unit of robot time and operator capacity, and any spillovers to other sites. Do not count each site as an independent experimental subject when its learner or workers are pooled.

**Predispatch record.** Register exact program arm (P source field, G licensed target grounding, E independently supported embodiment, R retained controller), prior eligible original customer offers *including no-offers and turned-away orders*, qualification and contractual acceptance criteria, customer identity under approved privacy safeguards, task/recipe, measured robot-capacity and signed version/hash of the learner and agent-policy lineage. Record preaction history, rights permitted for teleoperation, simulation, synthetic/public pretrained or field-originated data separately, requested action and its registered assignment probability, actual action, pre-/post-operator queue, worker-duration and rework events. Keep costs attributable to data acquisition, curation, compute, new embodiment, manufacturing and organizational coordination.

**Preoutcome randomized audit.** Before viewing the original buyer result, register reporting inclusion (S_t), lawful probability (rho_t), selection variables and exclusion. Even if a queue blocks service, sample the *eligible* original offer/no-offer without silently substituting accepted invoices; store safe reason if a counterfactual original offer cannot be observed. Report timing relative to learner update, billing, recovery, and dispatch must be explicit. A customer survey that changes demand is an *intervention*, not an exogenous audit.

**Physical accounting and true value.** Separate technically qualified attempts, safety clears, customer acceptance and finally cleared paid receipts; subtract marginal fleet/worker/compute/curation and cost of licensed rights only once in the relevant decision accounting. Explicitly mark unobserved, denied, masked, opted-out and genuinely absent as distinct outcomes. Record throughput displaced elsewhere in the same constrained operator/robot network, otherwise an apparent paid gain can be internal cannibalization.

**Identification gates.** Preserve action-propensity positivity for every legally feasible policy branch, or report the unsupported set and bound it. Preserve preoutcome audit positivity and known randomization, or retain the selected-history identified set. Validate consistency of original buyer definitions across arm/site/embodiment, no unknown time-varying confounder where not randomized, and no unrecorded cross-cluster broadcast or operator interference. Verify regulatory, privacy, contract and customer opt-out permissions before any field registry. Repeated observations under an unchanged controller cannot stand in for all unexecuted adaptive actions.

**Prospective discriminating comparisons.** P source-only field trajectory vs same buyer-service recipe under licensed G grounding of old source data vs independently resourced E, with same customer window, joint qualification gate and operator constraint. Stratify by source and target task mix and customer demand; report the full cross-program joint transition after accepted production, recovery and delayed original offers. If baseline controller R remains viable, keep its entire paid contribution. Match on *accepted service definitions*, not benchmark percentages.

**Current gaps:** all real randomized assignments, invoices/no-offers, true (rho/e), signed cross-site rights, measured incidents, separate E staffing, official numeric IO coefficients, and physical customer approvals missing. No replacement of the official production backbone by another vendor announcement.
