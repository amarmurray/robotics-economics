# Student exercises — cross-arm qualification and demand (solutions separate)

1. For two complete-service qualifications with four interventions R/S/G/SG, prove (A) assuming each task's potential success is nondecreasing in the intervention lattice. Explain why retaining an old checkpoint does not prove the same condition for a particular new learner.
2. Prove (B) when each task satisfies pathwise meet/join modularity, and explain why \(\Delta p_A=\Delta p_B=0\) does not imply this assumption.
3. For marginal qualifications (2/5,1/2,1/2,3/5) for each task and joint R/S/G success (1/5,1/4,1/4), compute the marginal-only, monotone and pathwise modular hSG intervals. Explain how to certify sharpness from rational response-type matrices.
4. Using F=(0,2,2,12), S/G costs 1/2 each and E-only net 4, find the net SG bounds under each assumption. Is one capital program robustly preferred?
5. For hSG=9/20, compare the paid bundle with the single shared-slot table F=(0,2,2,2). If 1/4 of offered orders change type and E's independent contract remains net 4, what is the preferred program? State the critical demand-support qualification.
6. Derive the total-variation composition bound with one fixed reference offer distribution and discuss why it is invalid as a complete correction when total eligible offer arrival intensity changes.
