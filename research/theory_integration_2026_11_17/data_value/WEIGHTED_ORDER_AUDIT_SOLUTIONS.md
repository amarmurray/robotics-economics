# Solution — economically weighted audit
The three smallest sum to 6; the three largest to 51. After auditing two, j can be 1,2,3, so worst width occurs at j=2. Leaving 1,2,3,10 gives 13-3=10; leaving 1,2,20,21 gives 41-3=38. The audit translates the interval by the known contribution and may resolve the decision earlier or later depending on that contribution. k must be timely accepted recipient outcomes, not upstream successes. Unequal audit costs, known threshold proximity, uncertainty about weights, dependence restrictions and adaptive auditing require a different criterion. No commercial inference from synthetic numbers.
