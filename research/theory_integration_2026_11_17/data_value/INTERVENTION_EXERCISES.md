# Intervention-selection exercises — reconstruction and adversarial tests
Private student packet. Work through the calculations before reading the separate solutions.

**1. The missing correction.** Let H have prior 1/2, p_H=3/4 and p_L=1/4. Routine policy exposure is e=1/4 and the gate detects every would-be error. Compute Pr(C=1), Pr(H|C=1), Pr(H|C=0). If X=0 is logged, what does that observation say about H? Explain why C=0 is not evidence of tested recovery.

**2. Pricing a probe inside production.** A specialist target recipe yields T=5. Source reuse pays 9 in H and 1 in L, plus 1 if a useful correction was collected. Routine earns 6 today and has exposure 1/4; safe supervised probe earns 5 and has exposure 1; an alternative specialist earns 6.3 today and creates no relevant correction. Determine the global two-date optimum and show how (a) no persistent update and (b) a delayed non-safety report change the choice. Do not call the terminal increment a per-label market price.

**3. The attractive but false identification.** World A has e=1,g=1/4,p_H=3/4,p_L=1/4. World B has e=1,g=1/2,p_H=3/8,p_L=1/8. Show the logged (X,C) distributions match but the expected uncorrected would-be-error probabilities differ. Identify one independent, safe instrument that would distinguish worlds and one quantity the correction counts alone cannot identify.

**4. Probe versus new body.** Repeat exercise 2 with target-only terminal T=25/4, all else unchanged. Explain how one change in accessible qualified production can reverse an intervention policy without changing intervention informativeness. Construct a free-routine-byproduct countermodel.

**5. Challenge a monotone-data argument.** Prove, for fixed p_H,p_L, that q=1 correction reports Blackwell-dominate q=1/4 reports. Explain why this does not imply that the q=1 policy is the maximizer of economically optimized production under a shared scarce operator-hour and safety budget. Discuss whether an optional improved robot with fewer corrections can reduce the enterprise's optimized value under retained rights and policies.
