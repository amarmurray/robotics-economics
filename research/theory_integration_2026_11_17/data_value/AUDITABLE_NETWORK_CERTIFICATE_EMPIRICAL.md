# Prospective evidence contract — can the network certificate be made non-vacuous?

**18 November 2026. Protocol only; no confidential customer access, field trial, or approved safety clearance.** One observational row per pre-action opportunity **including zero orders and not-run jobs**, linked across sites, operator pools and controller genealogy. Keep full accepted service and customer cash/capture separate from each individual skill score.

**Architecture/edge audit.** Required versioned fields: robot embodiment and actuator/sensor config; controller checkpoint/source hash, training/replay membership and freeze date; source modality and usage rights; grounder version; model dissemination scope; operator roster, reservations, abort dependencies and peak simultaneous recoveries; customer original eligible opportunity, offered recipe, order/no order, price, competing alternative; date/time and other shared market inputs. Each claimed structural zero needs an explicit counterfactual intervention, all upstream/downstream paths checked, verified lack of update access, and assessment of market substitution and global coordination. Unknown or historically unvisited edges stay unknown, never zero.

**Experimental estimands.** Compare independently **approved** whole-controller-pool policies: retained controller; specialist; routine production with incidental field data; target grounding with deliberate corrections; body redesign (only if its own physical envelope is approved); optional controlled replay/isolation. For every route: accepted whole customer-service contribution in the same post-assignment horizon, original-order denominator, actual physical/organizational resource use, independent held-out joint qualification, subsequent demand, and attributable curation/computation/access costs. Randomization at a lower level than shared checkpoints or operator resources does not identify the full-pool policy effect without an exposure mapping. Never randomize a potentially unsafe recovery policy as a purported scientific control.

**Two noninterchangeable routes for bounding influence.** (1) *Architecture*: prove a causal cut and pre-danger physical exclusion with versioned design and independent inspection; treat safety as its own certificate. (2) *Matched evidence*: compare full-pool qualified accepted-service outcomes under safe assigned interventions to identify a population mean or finite response-type bound, not a cross-world coupling probability without a justified bridge. A zero-trigger binomial bound for a C1 residual is usable only if a separately validated trigger **provably dominates** that mismatch for each relevant policy/history/latent type, with iid independent trials and prespecified finite strata. Never infer a bound on a rare, unsupported, partially observed θ from pooled zero events.

**Decision audit:** test whether there exists any safe and empirically covered policy with lower customer contribution beating a same-world specialist upper *after* all local, remote, demand and isolation losses; if not, report interval overlap and the next safest missing measurement. The synthetic 0.00558 threshold illustrates precision pressure, **not a robotic reliability target**. A controller cut alone does not remove market substitution.

**External methodological locators:** Aronow & Samii (2017), DOI 10.1214/16-AOAS1005, network exposure-based design/estimands; Basse & Airoldi arXiv:1705.05752, statistical concentration limitations under arbitrary interference. Their proofs have not been independently reconstructed here. The official 2025–35 BLS/BEA quantitative crosswalk and any real matched order-service panel remain outstanding and cannot be replaced by these theoretical calculations.
