# Formal proof ledger — joint menu/exposure response transport
**20 November 2026 UTC | conditional finite-population propositions, not claimed novelty**

**Setup and admissible coupling.** On a common original eligible H population, baseline C0,Q0 and target C1,Q1 are binary. Baseline law d,b,a,t indexes (00),(01),(10),(11). Let admissible joint potential-state couplings satisfy upper budgets on each bit's change probability u_C,u_Q. No conditional independence of C and Q, either within or across policies, is imposed. All four baseline cells are realized in the same complete-service denominator; selected-only purchased customers are inadequate.

**Lower bound.** Set S0={C0=Q0=1}, S1={C1=Q1=1}. Every case in S0\S1 must change at least one bit. Hence P(S0\S1)<=P(C1!=C0)+P(Q1!=Q0)<=u_C+u_Q and P(S1)>=max(0,t-u_C-u_Q). For attainability, take disjoint baseline 11 masses of sizes min(t,u_C) and min(t-min(t,u_C),u_Q), flip C on the first and Q on the second. Both conversions remove membership from S1. No other change is needed. Thus L is sharp. Note this proof does not require free availability of the previous production policy; that option matters for *economic* monotonicity, not probability bounds.

**Upper bound and sharpness.** To create a new 11 from baseline 00 requires both a C and Q bit flip. From 01 only C is necessary; from 10 only Q is necessary. No change to baseline 11 can improve the upper objective; no change from another state to a non-11 target can improve it. Let z mass of converted 00. Then z<=min(d,u_C,u_Q). At most min(a,u_Q-z) from 10 and min(b,u_C-z) from 01 can be converted. This supplies the stated upper bound. Conversely every feasible z together with these two conversions can be realized by partitioning each baseline atom continuously and flipping only the necessary bits. Therefore no higher conditional P(C1Q1) can be attained and the bound is sharp for this *restricted* class of couplings; for integer counts, an integer z attains the finite-population maximum. Additional restrictions (including monotone effects, fixed initial eligibility, capacity-dependent first sales, legally unavailable offers or shared physical constraints across customers) can tighten the identified set; sharpness should NOT be asserted after those restrictions without solving their new constrained problem.

**Observed marginals insufficient.** If only p_C=P(C0=1) and p_Q=P(Q0=1) are known, t itself lies in [max(0,p_C+p_Q-1),min(p_C,p_Q)]. Separate treatment marginal success rates do not upper-bound P(C1!=C0) or P(Q1!=Q0) for same original cases without a substantive joint potential-state coupling assumption. Repeated customer records under different menu assignments identify distributional mean differences under valid randomized network exposure; they do not observe both counterfactual responses of one case. It is wrong to interpret a small difference in means as a small flip budget.

**Economic interval and crossing.** At q=P(H) unchanged and legal prices {4,6}, the narrow fully-optimized G contribution is max(6q,4[q+s1])-k. It is nondecreasing in s1. By monotonicity and the sharp support for s1=qP(C1Q1|H), the stated G interval follows. With q=1/2 and k=1/2, G>X=3 iff s1>3/8. For case A, U=14/20, s1<=7/20 and G<=max(3,17/5)-1/2=29/10. For case B, L=(18-1-1)/20=4/5, s1>=2/5 and G>=max(3,18/5)-1/2=31/10. E and R values are not inferred from these inequalities; full policy ranking needs valid E,R bounds.

**Joint-world versus data transfer.** A data intervention changes a conditional joint law of original buyer choice, controller qualification, operator recovery and network demand. If the original eligibility H, case distribution or legal offers differ across policies, the change-budget coupling must either cover that population displacement too or be conditioned on a justified common original registry with a separate mass bound. A complete study cannot select only states visited by the better controller. A shadow qualification that lacks physical authorization is not a safe deployable Q=1. Pricing, costs and captured payment must be separately audited. A two-bit transfer bound is not a measured learned capability-to-revenue mapping.

**Diagnostic execution.** The standalone exact-integer model enumerates all 165 four-cell distributions of denominator 8 and 25 ordered budget pairs (u_C,u_Q) in {0,...,4}, comparing the one-dimensional maximum formula to an independent exhaustive feasible (x,y,z) conversion enumeration (4,125 cases). Tests are arithmetic diagnostics, not mathematical proof. Other production assumptions remain untested empirically.
