1. J_SG^cut=4, J_E^cut=3, each free optional own-program pooling gain lies in [0,3]. Derive and attain the rectangular sharp interval for pooled SG−E. Why does gross data option monotonicity not rank programs?

3. Build two worlds identical on every four-arm one-site SG/E×cut/pool observation with per-buyer contribution 2 but having opposite two-site pooled rankings due to complementary A+B source learning.
4. Compute the uniform influence bounds for each binary data edge in F(x,y)=2+3x+5y+7xy and compare the complete graph gain with the sum of empty-graph edge increments. Explain why an edge triggering 20 downstream customer payments cannot be assigned its local one-unit influence.
5. Two tasks each have qualification probability 1/2 and a complete service pays 8 only when both qualify. Construct opposite joint laws and compute the two expected contributions.
