# Prospective two-embodiment original-buyer and worker audit (unpopulated)

This is an *evidence protocol*, not collected customer or employee data. It operationalizes TWO_EMBODIMENT_DECISION_SUFFICIENT_AUDIT.md, maintaining the earlier SEQUENTIAL_PAID_DEMAND_EVIDENCE.md obligations.

**Cohort and assignment.** Original eligible customer A and C, service contract and budget ledgers before any program announcement; original precommitment policy candidates R/P/G/E; signed allowed data/operation rights and G validity/qualification; approval and randomization probabilities by physically closed buyer–robot–worker–model-sharing cluster; independence of E's machine, technician and dispatch eligibility; exact t0 commitment date and unannounced customer response. If G assignment cannot lawfully occur, mark the G response *unsupported* and retain sensitivity bounds; do not infer positivity.

**Ordered measurements.** At t1, original order offers and paid qualified outcome, deadlines, unit economics, quality, budget depletion and two independently identified repair obligations. At t2, original offers/no-offers before dispatch, worker availability and time-resolved clearing, actual allowed t2 trial and its acceptance/payment, field/synthetic/simulator/teleop data lineage, labels, validation compute, model identity and qualification timestamp, newly caused jobs and their creation times. At t3, original A and C offer/no-offer even when blocked or rejected, remaining buyer budgets, shared worker occupancy and second-embodiment technician eligibility, actual Q gate, accepted units, contractual receipts, fallback and refusal. Record buyer demand feedback from service quality, prices and public availability. Record who received signals *before* each approval/dispatch; separate selected invoice/incident reports from the full original-offer register.

**Minimal conditional audit for the exact example.** Together with verified fair pairwise A/C/W moments and fixed program-invariance/exclusion, estimate one additional contemporaneous original-buyer/worker moment t=Pr(A=1,C=1,W=1), conditional on the original order and permission stratum. Each independent sampling unit must link both customers to the same worker pool and decision window. Isolated single-buyer surveys, binary paid invoice receipts, pairwise robot metrics and generic Q benchmarks do not recover the triple. An independently randomized audit mask with documented eta>0, conditional independence and original exposure denominators permits E[M ACW/eta]; check cluster-level variance and interference. A report from a later cohort is not known before an earlier commitment unless a justified stable population transport model is stated.

**Identification checks and falsification.** Separately estimate whether P source qualification Q is independent of (A,C,W), whether G and E leave the late original-offer/worker law unchanged, whether G target data and embodiment qualifications are valid, and whether E independently supplies a second accepted service slot. Test those restrictions with matched supported program assignment; a change in demand or repair under G invalidates simple R-to-G audit transport. A rejected old policy must remain a comparator only if feasible and safe. Record cluster spillovers and service displacement, not only accepted invoices. When interference crosses the proposed cluster boundary, expand or model the boundary.

**Outcome.** Full program net captured contribution and original accepted units by end market, technical Q by embodiment/task, worker and compute use, rights/coordination cost and customer surplus as separate metrics. Never multiply a held-out benchmark percentage directly into a dollar margin. Do not credit future data a second time when it already raises production in the continuation value.
