# Instructor solutions: lawful signal timing and recursive production

Separate from [student exercises](LAWFUL_SIGNAL_TIMING_EXERCISES.md). These are exact hypothetical mathematics, not measured robot outcomes.

1. The per-report maximum and maximum of its sum give sum_y max_a B_a(y)>=max_a sum_y B_a(y). Any fixed program and pi can ignore a costless report, implying W_late>=W_none. Equality in the first iff some common a attains each per-atom optimal program value for all positive-mass y; a may use a different pi after each y. If sensing forces retraining, removes rights or occupies a scarce worker, the set of feasible policies and future network transitions changes: the old ignore-Y policy is not necessarily contained in the new menu, so the second inequality is inapplicable.

2. Equal-mass reports yield posterior Pr(Z=1|Y=0)=1/4 and Pr(Z=1|Y=1)=3/4. On Y=0, E capacity expected net is 11/5+(23/10)(1/4)=111/40, exceeding P R=5/2. On Y=1, P pilot yields 3/2+(7/2)(3/4)=33/8, exceeding E capacity=157/40. The early program value is (111/40+33/8)/2=69/20. P-locked controller value is 53/16 (retained R on low, pilot on high), E-locked is 67/20; W_late=67/20. No report E capacity gives 67/20. The early signal's exact gross value is 1/10, so cost c<1/10 makes deliberate purchase worthwhile, c=1/10 indifferent. E capacity involves a one-time purchased embodiment and cannot be used by a P-only owner without paying E.

3. On the high report, P pilot exceeds E capacity iff (3/2+7q/2)>(11/5+23q/10), i.e. q>7/12; on the low report, E capacity always exceeds P policies over q in [1/2,1]. Hence W_early=max(67/20,3+3q/5). Under P lock, if q>=2/3, R wins low and pilot high, producing 11/4+3q/4, which exceeds E-locked 67/20 iff q>4/5. At smaller q, P pilot always or is otherwise inferior to E; W_late=max(67/20,11/4+3q/4). The experiment is profitable only after its net full cost is lower than the appropriate gross value.

4. At theta=1/4, high Y produces posterior (1/4*3/4)/[(1/4*3/4)+(3/4*1/4)]=1/2, below the early P-vs-E switch 7/12. Both reports favor E capacity, so W_early(1/4)=111/40. Nature may select theta=1/4, bounding every precommitted report policy's minimum by 111/40. E capacity irrespective of Y yields 11/5+23 theta/10, increasing on [1/4,3/4] and attaining 111/40 at its lower endpoint, proving the sharp maximin. Under Bayesian p=1/2, the high report does justify a P pilot and yields 1/10 gross gain: the criteria and conditional distributions differ. A teleop-assisted trial can occupy the single shared rescue worker, delaying an original customer's accepted service and changing the learner state. Its reward and next-state kernel therefore differ from an observation-only report with a subtraction c.
