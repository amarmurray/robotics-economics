# Learning that creates the next economic risk set

23 November 2026. Extension of [history-conditioned improvement](HISTORY_CONDITIONAL_QUEUE_IMPROVEMENT.md), within *When Capabilities Become Production*. Conditional finite theory, not observed robotics returns.

## From fixed histories to endogenous eligibility

The earlier chapter evaluates G/K after a shared predecision history. Here the first acquisition changes which later acquisition is even lawful and economically possible. A field-failure trace, a completed recovery trajectory, a routine successful field record, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained data remain typed, potentially different learning objects. Legal reuse, source lineage, full recipe qualification, worker time and original buyer authorization are separate constraints. The firm does not receive a speculative data premium: the source changes the feasible transition into future Model A production.

Let b be a feasible baseline continuation, including a fallback on candidate-reachable off-path histories. A common finite structural law theta determines all legal action-conditioned histories, the original buyers' orders and accepted payments, complete joint qualification, queue evolution, rights and source generation. For a fixed early acquisition/dispatch rule k followed by b, let B_theta(k) be its expected discounted contribution. At the next date, let H_k be the observed eligible report including its lineage; a zero report denotes ineligibility. Let D_theta(k,h) be the conditional net paid continuation contrast of an additional lawful action g versus b (with fixed feasible continuations in both). The action may itself change later orders, queue and qualifications. For a second decision q(h) in [0,1], with q=0 on ineligible histories,

    J_theta(k,q) = B_theta(k) + beta^t sum_h q(h) M_theta(k,h),
    M_theta(k,h) = Pr_theta(H_k=h) D_theta(k,h).

This is exact by iterated expectation, including all future contributions once in D. For more decision dates use the preceding chapter's performance-difference identity with candidate-policy occupancy, not the incumbent's. In particular an unvisited report is not a zero *conditional* effect; its incumbent occupancy is zero, while its candidate occupancy may be positive. Conditional effects are undefined at zero mass; the weighted contrast is zero. Write M as the difference of two unconditional, history-masked potential continuation payoffs under the same early rule, so no division by a potentially vanishing risk-set probability is necessary.

### A sharp finite common-law certificate

Suppose a finite, explicitly complete set of lawful structural types i=1,...,N is stipulated. A type includes the outcomes of all feasible action paths, not just individually selected data samples. Its unknown population weight lambda_i is nonnegative and sums to one. Observable audit constraints are linear equalities/inequalities on type weights, including bounds on conditional moments after multiplying by the appropriate positive eligible-history mass. With fixed admissible nonanticipative policy pi, type payoff w_i(pi) is the sum of its actual accepted contribution and actual costs, averaged over independent policy randomization if any. Then

    L(pi) = min_{lambda in Lambda} sum_i lambda_i w_i(pi)
    U(pi) = max_{lambda in Lambda} sum_i lambda_i w_i(pi).

Proof: every admissible law on the complete type set is a weight vector in Lambda, and every weight vector is realized by drawing the type at cluster entry and following its feasible trajectories. The objective is expectation, so each endpoint is attained on a nonempty compact closed polytope. These are sharp *relative to the stipulated complete set and audits*. A ratio constraint is meaningful as a conditional audit only on positive-mass strata; either impose an evidenced positive lower mass or treat it as an unconditional inequality with no information at mass zero. All dynamic flow and capacity constraints must hold within a type; a collection of independent stage-specific marginal kernels is not automatically a common law. If policy choice precedes the unknown law, maximize L over feasible policies, not min_theta max_pi. For a finite menu and an independent ex ante policy lottery, the robust optimization is a linear program over policy weights and a lower value bounded by payoff at every vertex of Lambda. The hidden type is not an observation and cannot select policy branches.

For a sequence of interventions, an equivalent sufficient bound follows from the baseline performance-difference identity using signed occupancy-weighted moments at each date. Summing separate lower bounds on these moments is conservative, but sharp only if their minima are jointly achievable. The exact type certificate enforces shared customer budgets, deadlines, rights, physical worker queues and cross-date eligibility on a common law. This is finite partial identification, not an efficient general solution for exponentially large histories. Under supported sequential randomized cluster assignment, the policy-value contrast is also identifiable via iterated g-computation or weighting: the expected observed total contribution times the product of candidate decision probabilities divided by logged assignment probabilities at each visited decision equals candidate value (consistency, positive support, independent assignment given recorded history, policy-closed clusters and full outcome follow-up). The product is over eligible decision nodes only; no pretend assignment to a prohibited action.

## Two consecutive learning choices with a conserved worker slot

Use 64 equally weighted synthetic clusters for numerical replication; the proof allows continuous probabilities. At t1 every cluster accepts original service A, contribution 3. At t2 one shared worker slot may complete existing original C, contribution 2, or an alternative composite validation/dispatch action G. An early K recovery-origin acquisition consumes 1 unit of real incremental curation/compute (or an equivalent measured cost) without changing A. K creates a lawful second-stage report H in {F,K,none}: a failure-origin lineage F or completed-recovery lineage K with valid rights and a new original buyer's separately authorized B order, or no eligible report. These are distinct usable lineages, not interchangeable modalities. H is observed before the second decision; a report includes a genuinely available offer and not its future qualification result. The single final slot is sufficient for the stipulated G validation plus B dispatch if qualified. If not qualified it is consumed by validation and no unsafe service is delivered. C and B cannot both be delivered with this slot. B contributes 8 on qualified accepted completion, 0 otherwise. For simplicity no extra cash cost of G beyond that scarce slot; costs could instead be put in the net outcomes. No other terminal data premium. R uses A,C and obtains 5. K followed by no G obtains 4.

Let total eligible mass be e=1/2 and joint eligible-and-G-qualified mass s=1/4; these are stipulated identified early-K/full-G program moments, not transported offline benchmark scores. The report F has probability m in [1/8,3/8], and x is its joint qualified-and-paid B mass. The K lineage has eligible mass e-m and paid B mass s-x. Feasible laws are exactly

    max(0,m+s-e) <= x <= min(m,s).

Proof: allocate masses x,m-x,s-x,e-m-s+x and 1-e to the five types F-success, F-failure, K-success, K-failure and none. Nonnegative masses are equivalent to the inequalities, and the physical histories described above realize each type. The final worker is allocated once, and each original customer payment is counted once. The same early-K eligible rate and full-G paid histogram can coexist with all these laws. Where m is measured exactly, hold it fixed; otherwise the stipulated interval is all the report-frequency information.

For second-stage probabilities q_F,q_K, normalized net value is

    J(K,q) = 4 + q_F(8x-2m) + q_K(8(s-x)-2(e-m)).

Both terms are occupancy-weighted gains, not conditional success rates. G in both lineages yields 5 for every admissible law. G only after F yields 4+8x-2m, with sharp envelope [7/2,11/2]. To prove endpoints: for m<=1/4, minimum x=0 gives 4-2m >=7/2; for m>=1/4 minimum x=m-1/4 gives 2+6m >=7/2. Maximum x=m when m<=1/4 gives 4+6m<=11/2, and x=1/4 otherwise gives 6-2m<=11/2. Both extremes occur at m=1/4.

At m=1/4, x=0 and x=1/4 produce identical full-G contribution and report frequencies but opposite profitable lineage-specific rules. Each known-law optimum is 11/2. The average of the two endpoint values of any randomized q is 4+(q_F+q_K)/2<=5; hence no common q guarantees more than 5. Full G attains 5, as does retaining R without early K. The oracle value 11/2 in the endpoint subfamily is not implementable without information identifying the law. An independently supported E comparator worth 21/4 beats robust K but not the informed targeted program.

### The economic exposure threshold

For the fixed F-only rule the exact condition to beat E is 8x-2m>5/4. If an audit certifies r=x/m=1, this still requires m>5/24. Two admissible laws with perfect conditional F qualification and equal full-program paid histograms have m=1/8 (targeted value 19/4) and m=1/4 (targeted value 11/2). Thus even a perfect second-stage result on the eligible lineage does not certify funding the first acquisition that generates it. Conversely the joint audit x>=15/64 and m<=18/64 gives the sharp lower bound 4+120/64-36/64=85/16>21/4. The bound is attained at x=15/64,m=18/64. A conditional audit alone at r>=15/16, without stronger exposure information, gives only 4+(8*15/16-2)/8=75/16 for the F rule; feasible at m=1/8,x=15/128. This does not beat R or E. The decision-relevant audit is an occupancy-weighted paid contrast over the original early-action population, not only among its selected eligible survivors.

## Two structural falsifications

Free routine P countermodel: suppose already generated routine field records, legally usable without incremental acquisition cost, reproduce exactly the early K report, full joint qualification/payment transition and worker/offer state, and old policies remain available. Then P obtains 5 before optional G rather than 4, and its all-eligible G rule obtains 6 in every admissible law, beating the stipulated E. In this model every K rule is dominated by its P counterpart by 1. This is not a claim that free raw bytes suffice, or that routine data always match recovery trajectories: equivalence requires rights, conditional evidence, qualification, resource and customer transitions, not benchmark similarity. If free P is merely additional optional input while K remains possible, the feasible policy set expands and the optimum cannot fall; mandatory costly use is different.

Shared E countermodel: independently staffed new embodiment E adds an accepted original order of 9/4 and costs 2 for its launch. With a distinct slot it contributes 3+2+9/4-2=21/4. If the supposed distinct worker is actually the same final slot, committed E can at best contribute 3+max(2,9/4)-2=13/4; an optional firm chooses R=5 instead. No independent E value can be inferred from the price of an order without staffing, physical capacity and costs. The rank reversal is a production-constraint change, not a financing result.

## Interpretation and prior foundations

The unit of economic data valuation is the first action's exposure-weighted creation of the *next lawful opportunity to learn and produce*. Earlier source acquisition can raise conditional technical quality but shrink the number of paid reachable opportunities, or expand that number without making later training worthwhile. The production-generated learning loop can be valuable even when its unconditional full-commitment policy is not; its value is the option to choose on legally observed histories. In the witness the additional first-stage cost is recovered only by sufficiently prevalent economically productive selection. A deployment atlas should therefore record original candidate clusters, new eligibility entry and rejection causes, shared worker capacity, distinct source provenance, and paid dispatch, not merely training successes.
