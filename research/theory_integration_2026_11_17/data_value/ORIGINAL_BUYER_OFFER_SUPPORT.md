# Original-buyer offer support, policy quality drift, and qualified-production value
**19 November 2026 — extension of _When Capabilities Become Production_. Conditional finite-market mathematics; no observed robotics returns or independent novelty claim.**

## The question carried forward

The preceding policy-specific demand analysis bounded cross-market substitution under a **matched price anchor**. That anchor is not licensed by incumbent sales: source training X, target grounding G, and a changed embodiment E may alter buyer quality, alternatives, captured price and the set of eligible offers. Instead of another quadratic-demand parameter sweep, use an **original eligible-buyer offer register** and fixed, qualifying complete-service recipes. Ask when actual accepted/refused offers plus bounded policy quality drift produce an actionable investment certificate.

R is a qualified retained controller; X is heterogeneous data-driven training that qualifies A service; G uses target alignment/grounding to reduce scarce operator input for B; E adds a qualified embodied/recovery configuration. These physical interpretations are not empirically established by the toy example. The economic object remains one optimized accepted-service production value, net of source rights, data/compute/curation, recovery and capital costs exactly once. Field trajectories, failures, recovery, simulation, teleoperation, synthetic and pretrained inputs are separate possible causes of X/G, not individually monetizable by benchmark percentage.

## Model: nonparametric posted-offer demand

For every **original** buyer/order i and policy p, define a posted, enforceable provider offer t_ip for a specified quality of complete service; a latent maximum buyer payment w_ip **net of that buyer's best alternative**; and direct service cost k_ip. Conditional on legally/physically deliverable service and fixed offer protocol, acceptance is a_ip=1{w_ip≥t_ip}. Actual provider contribution if accepted is c_ip=t_ip−k_ip, not w_ip and not gross customer surplus. Joint/substituted services must be represented by complete recipes; if accepting one offer changes another's willingness to pay, the stable-threshold assumption fails.

An incumbent or matched-standard offer register bounds baseline willingness to pay by L_i≤w_iR<U_i when credible repeated accepted/refused quote tests establish a highest accepted L_i and lowest refused U_i. For p∈{X,G,E}, separately justified policy-quality/alternative drift |w_ip−w_iR|≤δ_ip and a known committed t_ip imply:
* **Certainly accepted:** t_ip≤L_i−δ_ip.
* **Certainly refused:** t_ip≥U_i+δ_ip (strict upper support matters).
* **Ambiguous:** anything between those cutoffs.

This is a *margin condition*, not a differentiable demand curve or a discovered robotics transfer coefficient. It preserves original buyer identity, opportunity eligibility, posted-price comparability, stable outside options up to measured drift, complete service and contract rights. One observed accepted-only order cannot establish U_i, and one refusal cannot establish L_i. When customer quality moves more than the evidence-supported δ, these classifications disappear.

Let F_p be the physically qualified feasible set of **complete** binary accepted-order bundles under robot, recovery, human attention, time, rights and integration constraints, with the incumbent recipe retainable only if genuinely feasible. With known nonnegative conditional order contributions c_ip and a set C_p of certainly accepted buyers and P_p of possible accepted buyers, the policy's best gross production V_p obeys

  V_p(C_p) ≤ V_p(actual acceptance) ≤ V_p(P_p),

where V_p(S)=max{Σ_i∈S c_ip x_i : x∈F_p, x_i≤1}. The inequalities hold state by state with market substitution and correlated qualifications as long as the acceptance sets and F_p are valid; the endpoints are **sharp only if** simultaneous all-certain-only and all-possible acceptance patterns are attainable under the same admissible joint demand state. With independently varying threshold valuations, fixed offers and no cross-offer effects, this rectangular support condition holds. Do not label the endpoints sharp under correlated customer substitutions or dynamic dispatch.

For a linear-programming relaxation A_px≤b_p, 0≤x_i≤1, the upper physical and captured-contribution envelope admits the certificate

  V_p(P_p) ≤ inf_{λ≥0}{λ·b_p+Σ_(i∈P_p) max(0,c_ip−λ·a_ip)}.

This follows by Lagrangian weak duality; it is an **upper bound** for integer complete jobs, not an equality unless the relaxation is integral or otherwise exact. It requires a bounded, independently substantiated eligible register, unit captured receipts, rights and resource maxima. Finite robot hours alone **do not** imply a finite revenue ceiling if off-register buyers or policy-specific payment can be arbitrarily large. For unavailable offers, unknown payment capture, or unbounded quality drift, return an inconclusive comparison rather than setting missing demand to zero.

Paid investment net J_p=V_p−K_p. The same production continuation value is not counted again as a "data value" premium. Optional free source files that leave old policies feasible cannot lower optimized gross value; forced or costly updating can. If new software invalidates R or shares operators with it, remove the fictitious fallback.

## Exact counterexample without a quadratic inverse-demand anchor

One previously accepted original order C yields contribution **6** under retained R. Two further original eligible orders A and B have standard complete-service posted price 7, variable delivery cost 2 and conditional contribution 5 each. Initial credible baseline offer tests report accepted at 5 and refused at 9 for both: 5≤w_AR,w_BR<9. Suppose |w_ip−w_iR|≤1 and physically certified policies are:
* X qualifies incremental A alongside C, one fixed cost;
* G qualifies incremental B alongside C, one fixed cost;
* E qualifies both A and B alongside C with sufficient independently qualified physical/operator capacity, fixed cost seven.
R remains possible without investing. Source/grounding qualifications and capacity are **assumptions**, not inferred from buyer offers.

The resulting net menu is J_R=6, J_X=5+5a_A, J_G=5+5a_B, J_E=−1+5(a_A+a_B). Initially A/B acceptance at price 7 is entirely ambiguous. Two states with w_R=(8,5) and (5,8), respectively, with no policy drift, have identical tested baseline quote bands; in the first, (J_X,J_G)=(10,5), and in the second (5,10). Consequently a perfect reading of the same coarse accepted/refused *baseline* quote support cannot identify X versus G. Over the entire rectangular interval, R=6 is the pure maximin menu action: X and G each bottom at 5, E at −1. A precommitment lottery is a distinct expected-value object and cannot be advertised as a guaranteed realized customer job.

Now add two credible, non-disruptive **original-buyer target offer observations** under an unchanged standard: A accepted at 8 and B refused at 6. They narrow A to 8≤w_AR<9, B to 5≤w_BR<6. Under the separately justified drift cap 1, every possible A valuation under X remains ≥7; every possible B valuation under G or E is <7. The actual accepted-service net menu is then **R=6, X=10, G=5, E=4** for all admissible valuations. Source training X becomes a robust winner even though no estimated elasticities, generic benchmark-to-dollar conversion or normalized customer-price intercept was introduced. If the offer register or δ is invalid, this ranking is void.

Under **an additional synthetic equal prior** restricted to the two counterworlds and no adverse action costs of the audit, the best ex-ante pure policy is X or G at expected 7.5; perfect discriminatory buyer information permits value 10, an information gain 2.5. That is only a two-state Bayesian EVPI arithmetic check, **not** the guaranteed price of teleoperation, routine logging, simulation or a field audit. A real audit spends time, may affect demand and must be charged once inside the two-date production problem. The equal prior is not inferred from quote bands.

## Why the register matters and where it fails

1. **Positive technical transfer does not ensure positive economic value.** If X's newly qualified service has no accepting buyer at its captured-price/quality, X loses its fixed cost despite higher technical capability. Conversely the smaller grounding action can win if it unlocks a real accepted-service bundle with scarce recovery resources. A new embodiment competes through actual bundle feasibility and service pricing.
2. **The economically valuable datum is a decision margin.** A targeted acceptance at 8 and refusal at 6 relative to a posted price 7 and a justified quality drift 1 distinguish feasible service. Repeated generic incumbent sales that leave those margins wide need not.
3. **Original denominator must be registered before selection.** Quote refusals, unattempted orders, alternative specialist quotes, customer eligibility and policy-specific controller/quality lineage all matter. Accepted-service-only reporting falsely treats low-demand buyers as nonexistent.
4. **Substitution and interference can defeat sharpness.** Buyer valuations may depend on which other offers are fulfilled, retailer rollout, competitors, shared recovery pools or perceived service quality. In that event use a joint accepted-order set and the LP outer bound (if valid); do not multiply individual acceptance rates or assume rectangular endpoint realizability.
5. **There may be no finite upper value without a true captured-payment cap.** Add a policy-only eligible buyer i* whose maximum captured contribution M is unconstrained and whose qualified robot/operator slot exists. It produces no additional R observations. For any proposed finite upper bound choose M larger. A finite order count or physical slot limit alone cannot bound policy receipts.

**Decision implication.** When full-market support, credible price cap and qualified operator capacity are known, certificate-based investment selection may be possible without point identification of a technical transfer kernel or smooth demand curve. When those prerequisites fail, the correct result is a partial order and a physically competent fallback. This is standard threshold-demand/partial-ID and LP logic applied to the common qualified-production framework, not claimed new economics.
