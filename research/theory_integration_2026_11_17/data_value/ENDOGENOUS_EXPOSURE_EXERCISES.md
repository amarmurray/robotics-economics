# Student problems — data generation, joint qualifications and demand
*Work from the maintained qualified-production objective. No empirical robotics rates are given.*

1. Let A,B,D be binary events with known a,b,d,x=P(AB),y=P(AD),z=P(BD). Derive the eight-cell table in terms of t=P(ABD) and the sharp endpoints without assuming independence.
2. Prove that U={000,011,101,110} and V={111,100,010,001}, uniformly distributed, have identical all one- and two-way moments but different t.
3. In the toy one-order model with date-0 routine 1, incumbent date-1 2, source X cost 2/5 and additional completed-service contribution 8 on ABD, grounding G adds net 3/5 and embodiment E adds net 1/2, determine the exact source/grounding threshold and choose under maximin.
4. With prior Pr(U)=Pr(V)=1/2, compute the hypothetical value of *perfect revelation of the world before investment*. Explain why it is not the value of one physical audit label.
5. State sufficient conditions for a total-variation payoff transport bound between a productive routine source policy and changed physical investment, and give a failure case caused by changed demand support/operator networks.
6. Propose a prospective linked original-order protocol that would distinguish this triple-interaction explanation from independent skill transfer. Specify the independent randomization unit and why accepted-only trajectory samples fail.
