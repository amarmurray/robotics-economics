# Instructor solutions — coupled economic data transfer and network ambiguity

**Separate from student exercises.** These are mathematically conditional synthetic results, not operational or field robotics advice.

1. The same *exogenous before assignment* Z distribution is used for the entire connected original-buyer/learner/worker graph. Treatments affect K_(a,z) and full paid response v_(a,pi,z), not the distribution of Z. If a data-acquisition treatment itself changes what is called Z (e.g. Z labels a trained checkpoint), it is posttreatment and cannot serve as an invariant common mixture.

2. J_P*=max(2+2t, 3/2+7t/2): equality 2+2t=3/2+7t/2 implies t=1/3. J_E*=max(19/10+2t, 3/2+3t): equality implies t=2/5. Evaluate the affine gap on intervals [0,1/3], [1/3,2/5], [2/5,1]. At t=0 and 1/3 gap=1/10; at 2/5 gap=1/5; at 1 gap=1/2. The max of finitely many affine policies has no new kink except pairwise line intersections. The difference is affine between combined kinks, so global extrema are among their endpoints. Exact range [1/10,1/2].

3. Min J_P*=2 at t=0; max J_E*=9/2 at t=1; rectangle gives −5/2. This combines *different* whole-network regime distributions across arms, contradicting the stipulated shared theta. It is an outer conservative bound, not the attainable minimum in C. If interventions change factor prevalence or a global checkpoint crosses treatment clusters, a single theta may no longer be justified and the coupled value range must widen.

4. At theta0 P mixture contribution 2−rho/2, E*=19/10, so rho≤1/5. At theta1 P mixture 4+rho, E*=9/2, so rho≥1/2. Incompatible. Pointwise program optimizers differ by theta; the operator cannot implement an unknowable theta-specific controller. A lawful timely report or a separate robust Bayes/minimax criterion is required, with report cost and altered learning/demand inside J.

5. The alternative E extra-capacity row (11/5,9/2) exceeds E R at both regimes; E*(t)=11/5+23t/10. At 0, P−E=2−11/5=−1/5; at 1, P−E=5−9/2=1/2. For t≥1/3, the source-pilot P row controls. Set 3/2+7t/2=11/5+23t/10: t=7/12. The trained source remains optional and the old P controller available, so this investment contrast says nothing about forced negative transfer.

6. Before intervention: original buyer offers, blocked/no offers, deadlines and customer market, whole-network factor and assignment. Throughout: joint checkpoint update broadcasts, robot/operator simultaneous recovery, lawful use/retention and safe independently qualified recipes, true capacity/displaced customer work, data type, and accepted cleared paid/refunded receipts, including not-served denominators. Cross-arm conditional means require valid cluster randomization and policy support, not cross-arm same-robot measurements. Training success is a mediator/collider candidate, not preassignment type.

7. Conditional law of total expectation still makes each fixed complete adaptive policy value affine in a finite exogenous mixture vector if conditional regime laws are invariant to the mixture. The simplex is partitioned into polyhedral regions by within-arm policy-value equality hyperplanes. Difference of maxima is affine in each cell, so enumerate cell vertices (including simplex boundary intersections), evaluate and take extrema. For a continuum of state types, parameter-dependent within-regime K, policy-dependent initial Z, or a robust adversary reselecting Z by history, the simple finite affine algorithm fails. A theta-specific post hoc optimizing policy is different from a single predecision Bayes or minimax legal policy.
