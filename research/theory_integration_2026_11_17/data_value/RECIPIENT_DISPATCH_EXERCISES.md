# Exercises
1. A frozen cohort has 100 eligible orders, 80 successes and 20 removed. Derive the range of removed successes and the admitted success range. Can original robot output rise?
2. Compute lawful and oracle value in the equal-state model with fallback .5 and dispatch 2/-1. Repeat with discounted licensed continuation 2 in failure.
3. Explain why hourly realized congestion is not necessarily an admissible call-time signal and identify the records needed to evaluate an actual forecasting treatment.
