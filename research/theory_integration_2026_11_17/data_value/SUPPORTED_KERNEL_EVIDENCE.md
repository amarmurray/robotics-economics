# Prospectively supported joint qualification and paid-service observation

23 November 2026. This is a proposed, **unpopulated** evidence protocol associated with FROM_HETEROGENEOUS_EVIDENCE_TO_PAID_PRODUCTION.md, not an authorization to intervene in customer operations.

**Sampling frame and legal gates.** Register clusters closed under a single buyer-order response system, shared robot/model weights, recovery queue and learner. Original offers include zero and declined work; store buyer budget remaining, intended accepted-service recipe, deadline and real contribution basis. Version R/P/G/E program and the predecision F/T provenance signature. Retain distinct modality/source task/embodiment and complete signed retention/training/transfer/deployment rights. Eligibility for S and M requires independently certified safe actions and available labor/compute. Prohibit use of private employer or customer material not independently authorized.

**Assignment before outcomes.** Conditional on a G,T cluster and legal qualification, prospectively assign M/S with known e_i, optionally varying independent target-grounding, source data or embodiment selection in separately powered arms. Hold source model, training budget, validation protocol and hardware matched. Never substitute a G/S or R observation for an untried G/M response. Record treatment take-up, noncompliance and actual delivered work. If rights or worker safety block M, label it impossible, not merely missing from the selected dataset.

**Linked event ledger.** At t3 collect original buyer opportunity/no-offer, buyer budget and receipt, accepted output/quality, failure/recovery ID, active human effort and occupation intervals, new job creation, rights, model release and evidence family. During t4 qualification record train/validation provenance, donor and target, matched compute, independent recipe tests and *joint* arm+mobile qualification. Record original kit order and guaranteed fallback as separate obligations, both receipt or nonreceipt, worker/free/busy, displacement and payment at final deadline. Link cancelled and unresolved cases; avoid falsely labeling censored recoveries resolved.

**Observation sampling.** Pre-register an independent joint audit of the original kit buyer order/no-order, joint skill qualification and worker availability, including failures. Record inclusion η_i, censoring, and why an observation is hidden. Audit assignment must not change customer orders or available service slots; otherwise treat it as a causal action with own cost/outcome kernel. If joint validation occupies the scarce worker, arrange prior independent qualification, charge its cost, or redesign the feasibility map. For each supported (z,h,a) cell estimate P(W) and P(QA QC D W), with separate confidence intervals and joint finite-sample uncertainty. Avoid mechanically multiplying marginals. Estimate alternative program values on their own supported histories.

**Identification and failure tests.** Check e_i>0 and η_i>0 within every history targeted by the policy. Estimate the action-specific joint rate using preaudit inverse propensity weights; audit covariates must predict inclusion without conditioning on unseen buyer outcome. Check whether original buyer demand and worker clearance shift after M; record rejected programs, price and site changes. Randomize at clusters to avoid spillovers from shared model updates or pooled support. Verify independent E worker access, contractual rights, and that the fallback and kit do not consume the same exclusive slot twice. A no-kit-budget history is a structural zero for kit benefit but may still generate learning affecting later periods.

**Economic reporting.** Show technical target transfer and *accepted paid joint recipe* as distinct outcomes. Price the extra qualification by contribution net of incremental compute/curation, labor, hardware, and displaced fallback. Credit saved capacity only if it enables another accepted job or a feasible cash reduction. Never add a second data premium to a Bellman continuation already recognizing learning. Present unsupported cells as intervals/sensitivity scenarios. No actual customer data, signed rights, grounded operator outcomes or BEA numerical IO coefficients are presently populated.

**Fixed empirical comparators.** Include safe R, source P, best fixed lawful G/S, G/T with prospectively supported M where feasible, and independently staffed E. Compare to a precommitted source mixture as well as a responsive controller on matched future customer opportunities, held-out target qualification, and joint worker/offer outcomes. Separate external validation from original production service.
