# Prospective graph exchange audit

Enroll original buyer/robot/operator interference clusters before acquisition. Record baseline feasible schedule, dated capacity slots, parent budgets and separate order IDs, signed recipe safety/qualification, rights, controller and lineage versions, every relevant alternative including refused/not attempted, and actual accepted net contribution. Distinguish creating a qualification, observing a latent edge, creating customer authorization, changing quality/price and changing report availability. Record probe costs and displaced current services, whether future procurement is consumed, and no-service/failure paths.

For the toy identify q and x=P(A)P(B|A=1, second intervention). If approved, randomly assign the second probe with known positive propensity in A=1 clusters, record A before assignment, B after intervention but before dispatch. Under valid matched intervention/randomization this identifies the needed joint moment; no need to probe A=0, and no unsafe or commercially prohibited probe. Separate technical marginals cannot identify x. If uncertainty affects q,c,g,x, minimize the same predeclared feasible policy's net contribution over simultaneous supported bounds. Verify retained fallback and independent E staffing/buyer budget; compare X/E on original populations. For large graphs measure complete connected exchange components and parent rows; distinguish feasible realized exchange lower bounds from valid upper coverage of unobserved alternatives. No actual customer data, approvals, outreach or new quantitative BEA backbone.
