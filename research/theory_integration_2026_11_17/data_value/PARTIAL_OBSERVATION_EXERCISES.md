# Learning problems: partially observed data transfer and customer production
*Student problems. Solutions deliberately in a separate file.*

1. **Beliefs and operator support.** With H=A/B equally likely, G reports H correctly with probability 0.99 and uses 1 operator unit. A target technique matching H costs 1 extra unit, while a wrong technique needs 2 for approved recovery. Budget is 2.1. Compute the posterior after report A and show why selecting A is prohibited by a hard almost-sure budget. Does 2.01 expected total use justify it?
2. **Competent baseline and redesign.** In the two-slot model take incremental accepted joint-bundle value Delta=8. Calculate the optimized route values R, G under perfect diagnosis, G under 99%-accurate diagnosis, E and S. Explain why the preferred route changes without any inferred “value per teleoperation demonstration.”
3. **Alternative risk policy.** If a separately approved probability-of-overrun threshold is 2%, can G with accuracy 99% use its target technique? What is its expected payoff? What if the risk threshold is 0.5%? Explain why the relaxed answers cannot be substituted for hard feasibility.
4. **Joint-qualification ambiguity.** With routine p_B=p_C=3/4, embodiment p_B=p_C=9/10, routine current accepted contribution 1 and body current 0, Delta=8, derive the sharp Frechet intervals. Construct **two distinct** compatible joint laws yielding opposite preferred route.
5. **Blackwell objection.** Explain exactly why “a more informative signal can always be ignored” does not prove that expensive target-grounding dominates routine production, then state a sufficient condition under which the monotone-information theorem *would* apply.
6. **Policy study.** Describe an approved, non-hazardous matched production/grounding comparison that would separate signal accuracy, policy-induced state visitation, gate misses, operator-load support, retained technical improvement, jointly qualified customer production and customer demand. Identify at least one object that a public vendor claim cannot identify.
