## Production environment

Within one shared learner, operator and original-buyer market, define U,V as binary indicators of two **complete finally paid** qualified customer services. A zero includes failures, refusals, absence of qualification, refunds or unsettled orders; exact original orders/acceptances must also be retained. Let L indicate that the *joint trajectory* has valid provenance and rights to be retained, trained upon and lawfully used to enable a new service at the later date. L is episode- and checkpoint-specific; it is not inferred from technical success or a universal right to scrape field data. Shared capacity and rights state control the legally feasible action menu.

A third original-buyer service is newly qualified only following C=UVL, and needs one shared operator/service slot. Its attempted cost is c=1/4 in normalized net-contribution units and the conditional probability of an ultimately paid whole service is q; the controller can decline the new order. This is a **different customer receipt**, not a duplicate of U/V. The same full-program J accounts for every receipt, training/curation cost, operator opportunity and eventual licensed data-generated future service *once*. F field success/failure/recovery, T licensed teleoperation, M simulation/synthetic and public/pretrained sources remain separate potential data inputs. The resulting law of C is not automatically inferred from any source modality or benchmark.

## Proposition: pairwise distributions leave a sharp third-order economic interaction

For t in [0,1/4], put a probability mass t on each **odd-parity** binary state 001,010,100,111, and mass 1/4-t on each **even-parity** state 000,011,101,110 of (U,V,L). This is a nonnegative normalized family P_t. For all t, each single marginal is Bernoulli(1/2); all three pairwise 2-by-2 distributions equal those of independent Bernoulli(1/2). But P_t(U=V=L=1)=t. Therefore even fully observed population pairwise tables do not identify how often newly qualified paid production is **both** technically possible and legally trainable.

**Sharpness proof.** Set p111=t. Each observed pairwise 11 probability 1/4 forces p110=p101=p011=1/4-t. The individual 1/2 probabilities force p100=p010=p001=t; normalization yields p000=1/4-t. Nonnegativity gives exactly 0<=t<=1/4; all those distributions are attainable. The t=0 and t=1/4 worlds have disjoint parity supports despite precisely the same pairwise facts.

### Exact full-horizon economic sufficiency, not merely technical interaction

Fix a legally signed complete policy pi and assume its conditional expected **entire current-plus-future net paid contribution** f_pi(u,v,l), including adaptive choices, follow-on buyer demand, training costs and legitimate recoveries, is unchanged across the counterfactual family P_t. This invariance is a substantive assumption, not justified by pairwise observational data. For any f on the eight states,

    E_t[f] = (1/4) Sum_{even states} f + t D3(f),
    D3(f)  = f111+f100+f010+f001-f000-f011-f101-f110.

This follows by direct substitution of the eight masses and is exact. Consequently the signed fixed-policy expectation is identified from pairwise evidence over this family **if and only if** D3(f_pi)=0. Otherwise its sharp sensitivity range is |D3(f_pi)|/4. The distinction is between higher-order *technical* learning statistics and the higher-order **economic continuation**, which can be nonzero because future paid service requires a licensed joint history.

If all lawful policies share a fixed feasible menu Pi and their conditional f_pi remain invariant across t, J(t)=sup_{pi in Pi}[A_pi+t D3(f_pi)] is convex as a supremum of affine functions. A retained old feasible policy contributes a lower floor. A sufficient—not necessary—criterion for pairwise identification of optimized J is D3(f_pi)=0 for every eligible pi; policies with nonzero interactions could be dominated. Convexity in this **joint-distribution parameter** t says nothing about monotonic returns to acquiring more raw data, and cannot transfer across program-dependent rights, policy menus, hardware or customer response without checking the entire history. No independence or second data premium is imposed.

## Exact retained-policy SG-versus-embodiment counterworld

Take the first two finally paid services to contribute expected 1 in each world. The separately licensed old controller R uses its own historical valid trace, with t0=1/8 and future whole-service conditional paid q0=1/2, net after attempted cost c=1/4. Therefore J_R=1+(1/8)(1/4)=33/32. Grounding G raises q on that old trace to 3/4, so J_G=17/16. S gives access to a new source-trained recipe with triple-event probability t and q0=1/2. SG makes both source and grounding available, q1=3/4, while retaining the old controller as a *free optional* fallback:

    J_R=33/32,
    J_G=17/16,
    J_S(t)=1+max(1/32,t/4),
    J_SG(t)=1+max(1/32,t/2).

A **separately lawful and safely qualified** embodiment E is stipulated to have J_E=35/32 net whole-program paid contribution inclusive of its costs. Its performance is not imputed from any task benchmark. No actual embodiment has been measured.

At t=0 SG falls back to 33/32 and E wins by 1/16. At t=1/4 SG reaches 9/8 and beats E by 1/32. S and G individually cannot outperform E anywhere in the example. Thus the sharp pairwise-only investment difference is

    J_SG-J_E in [-1/16,+1/32],

and the exact SG/E indifference occurs at t=3/16. At t=1/4 the source×grounding economic cross-difference J_SG-J_S-J_G+J_R is +1/32 despite all individual and pairwise first-service paid/rights statistics remaining fixed. This is a *lawful access to future customer value* interaction, not an assumed intrinsic neural transfer synergy.

A genuinely supported uniform upper t<3/16 would choose E; a supported lower t>3/16 would choose SG **only if** the conditional q, the E whole-program paid value and every legally reachable comparison action remain as stipulated. No such bounds have been observed. Forced unhelpful S training at t=0 does not contradict optional free-source monotonicity: the old controller remains feasible. Under program-specific continuation or customer demand, t alone cannot settle anything.

## Connection to recursive-firm state abstraction

The preceding module certificate uses projected learner/customer/operator states. This example shows why retaining all pairwise empirical moments is not equivalent to conditioning on every complete jointly paid, licensed history. A projected potential based on U/V pairwise marginals and independent rights cannot sharply bound a follow-on module whose legal action becomes available only at C=1. A sound projected Bellman upper inequality must include the C/right/checkpoint/qualified-service menu or dominate the worst omitted complete histories and actions uniformly. Merely averaging logged transitions is inadequate; robust upper envelopes may also be loose if they effectively recombine mutually impossible hidden histories at successive dates.

The practical next theorem/evidence discriminator is *economic continuation sufficiency*: can a prospective shared learner/operator/buyer/rights abstraction preserve all valuable qualified production paths, including licensed production-generated training, future newly possible services and source-conditioned buyer demand? If not, do not claim a sign certificate from source-pair experiments or from a dashboard of task success rates.
