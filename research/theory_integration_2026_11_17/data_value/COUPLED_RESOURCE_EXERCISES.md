# Student exercises — coupled economic report value

1. Starting from three signed complete-network A/B services with p=(1/2,1/4,1/4), one operator slot, and common outside original paid opportunity λ∈[0,1/2], derive the δ-vector from the table in [the chapter](COUPLED_RESOURCE_REPORT_VALUE_AND_ECONOMIC_SUPPORT.md). Compute fine/coarse optimal production values. Give a policy implementable without knowing λ and explain what is assumed about the outside order.

2. Prove the sharp common-λ bound on L(λ), then discard the common-λ restriction and use only its three marginal δ intervals. Obtain the sharp rectangular bound. Why is an individually forced positive report not necessary for a positive lower value under the coupled set?

3. Fix gross observation cost c=1/8. Determine when the report strictly covers its cost. Construct a report-dependent outside-order counterworld satisfying all marginal bounds but violating the claimed coupled positive lower value.

4. For δ_y(θ)=a_y+b_y^Tθ with a compact polytope Θ, explain why L is concave on a fixed sign orthant and give an LP for the upper endpoint. State the assumptions under which this says anything about G-observe, G-train and X/E investment.
