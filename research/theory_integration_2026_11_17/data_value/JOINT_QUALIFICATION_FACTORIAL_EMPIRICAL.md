# Prospective matched accepted-service identification protocol: source, grounding and body

**Private, 18–19 November 2026. Design only, not a field experiment or safety authorization.**

**Question.** Does optional B-source copying *used in training* improve A's **joint qualified whole-service** contribution, and does its effect depend on approved target grounding, while retaining B customer orders and all resource/dispatch/market responses? Compare a changed embodiment only as its own validated complete production intervention.

**Unit selection.** First map a **policy-closed** network for each prospective independent customer market: every controller-update/feed link, operator shift and peak recovery budget, dispatcher queue, charging/maintenance/tool resource, customer substitution link and demand response reachable under any arm. If markets share any such link, do not call them independent randomized clusters; merge the components or explicitly model saturation/interference. An industry or task is not an experimental unit by itself.

**Pre-treatment registry.** Freeze complete eligible A/B customer opportunity and order IDs, promised service recipes, deadlines, alternatives, accepted/rejected/unattempted orders, customer-specific capture and price, provider costs, market outside option, and customer-cluster selection. Capture missed-service opportunity alongside accepted tasks; count every B lost order. Record contract/calendar vintages and whether an order can shift across clusters.

**Four-arm design (only if individually physically approved).** 00 retained training recipe/no extra grounding; 10 same with prespecified rights-cleared source-use training intervention; 01 target grounding without that source; 11 both. Fix training/validation computational and human resource budgets or record their differences as treatment costs. Retain approved policy F and competent specialist S as within-arm menu choices; do not treat unsuccessful qualification as authorization to test an unapproved controller. Randomize at the complete independent network level with known positive assignment probabilities and horizon-aligned demand windows.

**Mechanisms to log without mistaking them for effects.** Source dataset/content hash, source type (routine field trajectory, recovery/failure, teleop, simulation, synthetic, pretrained/public) and rights; passive availability versus actual training ingestion; curation/compute and embodiment/tool/controller hashes; version-pinned baseline and learned target occupancy; independently approved physical target support and recovery; **joint** held-out qualification successes and failures; operator demand even on simultaneous-recovery tails; observed A accepted services; original B fulfilled/lost/unattempted orders; customer demand/cancel/capture; replacement and hardware resources; all within-window costs and collections.

**Primary estimand.** Whole-network captured contribution for each complete policy over the pre-registered common customer opportunity horizon. Prespecified economic contrast: E[Y_11]-E[Y_10]-E[Y_01]+E[Y_00], with retained F/S alternatives and qualification failure outcomes already included. Report arm means and their uncertainty first; do not infer value from task-success benchmarks alone. Independently report source/no-source at fixed grounding, grounding/no-grounding at fixed source, learning and policy-induced occupancy, qualification joint tables, and lost B orders.

**Fallback if physical approval or overlap fails.** If only offline training is approved, estimate a *development* target (joint qualification under independently admissible test conditions) without claiming full-service J. If 11 is not admissible, do not expose customers or operators; maintain a missing-arm bound for a hypothetical estimand and compare only actually qualified options. If there is a single interconnected market, pursue controller/dispatcher/original-order architectural audit and the prior conditional eta bound or an appropriately modeled interference design; do not pretend independent robot/site assignments solve the problem. If target states have no safe support, mark the production effect unidentifiable rather than manufacture a residual from the old F pose.

**Threats to validity.** Training data reused across arms; cross-site policy updates; eligibility changes after assignment; hidden customer substitution; capacity displacement from A to B; controller- or embodiment-specific recovery; learning within observation windows; assignment-correlated dates/markets; selected study customers who differ from the intended market; outcomes counted twice as customer revenue and future data premium; significant physical approval/cash feasibility changes. Every trial needs an independently established physical safety approval and a prospectively identified legal customer/test environment.

**Current evidence docket.** Public DDA, PMLR SPIBB and BLS/BEA methods explain parts of the theoretical and data-source context but provide no target-site source-to-Q estimate or completed enterprise accepted-service panel. Attempt to retrieve the current BLS 2025–35 National Employment Matrix XLSX from its official link was not successful in this session; the existing BLS HTML and BEA 2026 input-output code/NAICS route are *metadata*, not a verified numerical industry crosswalk. No new source numeric rate is claimed. Next usable public step is a **verified numeric** download with compatible vintage and reproducible parse, not another vendor announcement.
