# Exercises — correlated data and recursive production (students)

Use the synthetic two-customer environment of [CONDITIONAL_COMPONENT_CERTIFICATE_AND_COPULA.md](CONDITIONAL_COMPONENT_CERTIFICATE_AND_COPULA.md). This is independent practice, not an empirical assignment.

1. Given two final-paid Bernoulli services with marginals 1/2, obtain the complete joint distribution as a function of w=P(U=V=1). Derive the sharp allowable interval for w without independence.
2. Rights permit one later paid buyer service only after U=V=1. Grounding gives q=3/4 and attempt cost c=1/4; old shared controller yields w0=1/4. Derive optimized SG including the legally retained old controller. Compare with E=19/16.
3. Evaluate the optimized R, S, G, SG menu when w=1/2 and q0=1/2. Calculate the source-by-grounding cross-difference. Explain why marginal task success does not identify it.
4. Suppose a prospective signed w upper bound is 1/3. Produce a conditional upper certificate against E. Then suppose a feasible SG policy is established with w at least 2/5: prove the opposite strict ranking.
5. State and prove a componentwise conditional super-solution upper bound for a fleet of modules sharing one operator budget. Which premise fails if the common learner's future state is left out of all module projections? Explain why the resource multiplier is not a second real operating cost.
