## Prospective 2 × 2 learner-wide inclusion and conditioning design

Randomization unit: connected model-weight/update cluster with shared replay, shared operators, site and demand interference recorded. Randomize X = a **fixed, licensed, version-locked archive inclusion arm** on/off, and G = goal conditioning/grounding mechanism on/off, only where both controllers and input modalities are technically implementable; distinguish later new source generation X_new as a separately randomized, *feasible* intervention. Archive source generating method and archive inclusion are not the same treatment. Include a no-new-data image-goal baseline if language-label support is absent. Same seeds/configuration versions are blocked and logged, not proof of independence.

Pre-register **two separate policy estimands**:

1. `B` fixed proposals / fixed compute or wall-clock: the intent-to-train effect of assigning X/G, allowing actual admitted updates `K(X,G)` to vary. This is a production-relevant equal-resource policy comparison, including admission and preprocessing overhead.
2. `K` fixed accepted updates: the effect of implementing each X/G **as a complete feasible sampling/stopping rule** that achieves exactly K, with actual proposals, latency, licenses, semantic checks and opportunity cost recorded. A zero-admission treatment has no supported fixed-K counterpart; do **not** drop zero-K clusters or impute their success.

Never condition solely on achieved K or qualification success to identify a controlled-dose effect from the fixed-B trial: K and qualification are postassignment and may select different difficulty/lineage strata. If effective sampler support cannot attain K, report the missing cell, bounds or redesign rather than fabricate overlap. Predeclare full factorial policy mean `J(0,0)`, `J(1,0)`, `J(0,1)`, `J(1,1)`, and the total policy interaction `J(1,1)-J(1,0)-J(0,1)+J(0,0)`. Under valid whole-cluster randomization, observed complete-policy means identify this *policy* interaction, not unique neural mediation or per-source Shapley values.

Distinct technical tests: accepted context A/B marginal coverage; predeclared independent B recovery families, verified by raw provenance and scene/controller diversity; held-out joint qualifications for actual accepted-service recipes; safety regressions, old R fallback at reachable physical states; failure/recovery outcomes. Match source length weights and all retraining compute. Report both `Q_C` and `Q_N` as **diagnostic hypotheses** rather than asserting the learner literally requires these thresholds. Tests and customer service must involve compatible original task and embodiment versions; no cross-customer benchmark-to-dollar inference.

Economic arm: separately signed, allowed complete R/X/G/E controller–hardware–operator policies for actual original buyer cohorts. Record all offers, refusals, displaced/manual service, completed and returned units, charged/invoiced/**paid** receipts, refunds, downtime, technician/operator attention, customer quality and shared site bottlenecks. Production value is captured contribution (and continuation already in J), not generic external demand. Joint customer demand and signed physical permission are independently necessary; assignment at robot level is insufficient if model and operators are shared.

## Conditional falsification and next research action

The central fork is empirically discriminating: if holding accepted K and gross A/B fraction equal, learned qualification responds to independent episode-family diversity (and the X-minus-G difference persists), the novel-family channel may matter; if qualified service responds to contextual mass but not additional independent families, the simple G-resampling mechanism may suffice. Null results under one recipe do not exclude other modalities or source architectures. A new embodiment E changes the feasible task/dispatch menu and should be compared as an alternative *complete production policy*.
