# Student exercises — policy-occupancy source-data value

1. Two-stage qualification occurs with probability q under P and Q. After qualification P pays with probability p and Q with probability s. Derive the occupancy-bound contribution of the second-stage kernel. When is it tighter than a worst-history uniform bound?
2. Q offers an SG safe service with paid probability .4 and risky service with 0; pooled P retains safe and improves risky to 1. E earns .55. If Q's logging policy always chooses safe, compute the optimized SG/E comparison in both worlds and explain why the observed kernel error is insufficient.
3. Now Q logs risky with probability .01. Compute m, the P-optimal policy's occupancy density ratio and the valid certificate. Is the ranking identified?
4. P=Q on all empty/singleton/pair coalitions and the SG/E paid difference there is −.15; the full P mixture yields SG=1, E=.55. Compute lower-coalition extrapolation, full difference and three-way dividend. Does free optional source access rule out the observed reversal?
5. Prove the common-menu correction when P and Q have different policy sets and a shared Pi0 with nonnegative opportunity gaps e_P,e_Q.
