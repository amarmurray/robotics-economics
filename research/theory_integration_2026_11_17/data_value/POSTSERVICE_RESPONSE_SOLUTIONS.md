# Instructor solutions: post-service response

1. For nonnegative x,y, max(x,y)=x+y−min(x,y). Binary D,Z,Q and budget (1−Y) imply min{b(1−Y)D,v_C QZ}=min(b,v_C)(1−Y)DQZ. Y prevents a duplicate A purchase; W prevents booking simultaneous service while the sole worker is occupied; the overlap subtraction prevents counting two jobs through one robot-time slot.

2. Write V_G−V_R as early receipts minus costs plus ∑p_G μ_G(Q_G)−∑p_R μ_R(Q_R). Add/subtract ∑p_G μ_R(Q_G), then ∑p_R μ_R(Q_G), giving the qualification, mediated-history and conditional-response terms. For transport the G early H law and target Q_G must be genuinely established, every G-reachable H must be present in full original R histories, and buyer mechanisms must be equal conditional on the complete H; rights, interference and deadlines must support the equality. Replacement order is conventional; do not interpret these algebraic components as uniquely identified natural causal effects.

3. Early paid contribution is (1/2)(4/5)=2/5. Good late contribution is (1/2)(3/2)=3/4; idle is (1/4)(2)=1/2; blocked is 0. Sum 33/20. Each free history's max−min is 3. The bound is ±δ[(1/2)3+(1/4)3]=±9δ/4; δ=1/4 gives ±9/16, or [87/80,177/80]. It is sharp: for good, move δ probability between Z=0 and Z=1; for idle, move δ between 00 (payoff 0) and 01 (payoff 3). Each has enough baseline extreme mass for δ<=1/4. Blocked W=0 so all terminal actions yield zero, even though its original C order could matter to t3 learning if the horizon were extended.

4. Put E=168/80; low Gearly=87/80, high Gearly=177/80. Low-world regret q(81/80) and high-world regret (1−q)(9/80), equal at q=1/10, worst regret 81/800. Without a lawful early informative report, that hindsight policy is not executable.

5. Under R, Q_R=0 and all paid C invoices are absent regardless of original C demand. Set Pr_R(Z=1|good)=0 in one world and 1 in another, holding D, quality, worker and all R payments fixed. Under the strong K_G=K_R conditional exclusion, Gearly good-state later C contribution differs by p_G(good)*3=3/2. A lawful original C offer/no-offer registry under R, including rejected and unserved offers, separates the worlds if good H is observed with positive support. At t3 an early blocked worker may clear backlog; C demand can reappear or expire, so terminal blocked-state audit irrelevance does not extend automatically.
