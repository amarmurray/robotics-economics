# Prospective empirical protocol — distinguishing passive data value from grounded target support

**18 November 2026. Protocol only; no robot trial or approval obtained.** This extends the [shadow-data protocol](SHADOW_LEARNING_EMPIRICAL.md) without creating a competing research track.

**Estimands.** (A) Developmental effect: with customer order mix, rights, physical resource cap, body, target validation rules and controller version fixed, difference in *joint held-out qualification and complete accepted customer service* of A trained with versus without a rights-cleared read-only B source copy. (B) Informational effect: for a truly fixed A/S menu and qualification, the conditional report distribution P(Z|pre-action customer stratum, persistent regime proxy, versioned action/recovery state) and the resulting improved selection decision; the regime proxy requires validation and is not directly observed. (C) Embodiment effect: new set of service-relevant action/recovery dependencies E(a,h'), physical eligibility and captured whole-network V after customer/dispatch adaptation.

**Prospective arms, only where independently approved:** retained F; F+read-only shadow copy; A trained with target-only support; A with shadow+target grounding; A with source-only shadow; changed body h' (after separate certification); credible specialist S. Record failed qualifications and zero orders, not only survivors. Never randomize an unapproved A action to force support. For shared controller or customer networks, assign complete policy-closed components, not individual robots; where infeasible, report partial identification.

**Required version-pinned ledger:** customer opportunity ID and before-policy deadline, order acceptance/rejection and value captured, original B order displacement, alternative supplier S, site/cell task recipe and *joint* acceptance, robot body/firmware/policy checkpoint, action/context/recovery support, physical safety approval scope, human operator peak concurrent calls, deployment hours, data type (field/routine, failure/recovery, teleoperation, sim, synthetic, pretrained), observation-censoring gate, permitted rights and replay mode, training cost and calendar latency, demand/price/dispatch response, same-network linkage. Record whether a source record was *available* versus *used* by the learner.

**Tests to separate mechanisms:** matched source volume increase on unchanged support; matched paired target/source analogies; target-only grounding at fixed source stock; embodiment switch at fixed data/resources; freeze A controller and vary only a diagnostic report; freeze report and vary only the training transition. Estimate confidence intervals for report error by *pre-action target stratum*; pooled classification accuracy cannot establish uniform diagnostic informativeness. Audit joint policy acceptance and operator tails separately from average economic difference.

**Discriminating hypotheses:** (i) passive logs can improve held-out A service without reducing uncertainty about unsupported A actions; (ii) target grounding creates decision-relevant support not obtained by extra F trajectories; (iii) changed morphology eliminates a critical unsupported action but creates another recovery demand; (iv) free production data already covers all useful target recipes and expensive grounding has no net benefit; (v) source representations provide an externally validated invariance that defeats naive literal action-overlap criteria.

**Status and source provenance:** no target physical approval, signed control/data separation, complete accepted-order panel, whole-network randomization, or empirical signal law.
