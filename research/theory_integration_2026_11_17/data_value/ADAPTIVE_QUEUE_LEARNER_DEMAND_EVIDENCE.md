# Prospective causal evidence protocol: action-generated recovery jobs and transfer

**22 November 2026. UNPOPULATED.** Companion to [adaptive queue learner/customer theory](ADAPTIVE_QUEUE_LEARNER_DEMAND_COUPLING.md). No customer, fleet or robotics data were accessed. All example values are stipulated synthetic net customer-production units, not measured prices or ROI.

## Unit, admissibility, intervention and clocks

Use a *connected* original-buyer/robot-fleet/shared-learner/recovery-worker cluster as the interference unit, not an independent robot episode. Prospectively timestamp lawful training data rights and source lineage (field production trajectories; failure/recovery; deliberate teleoperation; simulation; synthetic; public/pretrained), legal restrictions on transfer and retention, model broadcasts and license revocation. Before any experimental assignment obtain physical safety approval, signed customer consent/rights and a rollback-safe qualified R recipe. Never infer legal or qualified availability from a model benchmark.

Distinguish R continuation, optional P site-1 production-learning pilot, G target grounding from lawfully retained source with matched training compute/curation and signed permission, and E new independently staffed robot + operator. Randomize P/G/E across disconnected clusters or explicitly estimate interference when connected; exposure mapping must track shared learner updates, worker and robot slots and customer offer displacement. Do **not** claim the four arms are already approved.

Record t1 original eligible customer offers, P adoption/nonadoption and net program costs; initial incident queue, worker-specific service time/capacity and t2 remaining duration D. At t2 record **before dispatch**: original site-1 buyer eligible offer/no-offer, site-1 task qualification with safety gate, worker queue at decision time, R and T prospective contracted expected net, chosen action/propensity, worker and robot-time occupancy, legal report used by policy. Record post-service success/failure, new incident admission J2 and service duration, learner checkpoint/site-2 Q3 qualification causally linked to the action, release/worker reassignment and actual resource costs.

At t3 record original site-2 buyer **eligible offer/no-offer, including unserved offers**, before dispatch, and contemporaneous worker occupancy generated by the entire job history. Separately record realized accepted service, refunds, downtime, invoices and cleared customer receipts *after* dispatch. Keep action-specific t3 demand and qualification measured under both t2 T and retained R, including no-offer/unsupported recipe states; otherwise z and the safe continuation counterfactual are not identified. Keep distinct new jobs arising from T and the earlier D1 carryover, plus any competing jobs on other sites.

## Statistical estimands and sensitivity

Critical conditional event z = Pr(U3^T=1, J2^T=0 | D1=1, U2=1, do(T at t2), lawful rights and qualification). Its T-action marginals are insufficient. Measure the R-action paid eligibility event Pr(Q3^R=1,U3^R=1,worker free | D1=1,U2=1,do R) separately. Record whole original eligible-offer cohorts and policy propensities; selected paid-service successes alone induce selection bias.

If a reliable joint conditional z is unavailable, retain the sharp conditional Frechet interval and report whether P/G/E ordering is unidentified. Do not replace missing original buyer prices with benchmark success percentages or another supplier launch. For more than one new recovery job, estimate action-conditioned transition support for B_(t+1), not independent datewise worker-free Bernoulli cells; include queue discipline, priority jobs, censoring, shared staffing and cross-site learner broadcasts. Distinguish a *known physical law* value envelope from a fixed robust implementable policy absent an early legal report.
