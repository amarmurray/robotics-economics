## 1. Economic premises

A source archive can resolve which control method worked on its original embodiment without telling the firm which mapping makes that method valid at the recipient. A local calibration can resolve the mapping without supplying a useful source method. Neither report is the same thing as qualification for paid work. The firm must choose whether to refine the source, investigate the target relationship, or manufacture an alternative body, using the same dated technical labor that commissions other robots. Accepted work in those different markets then generates different effective records for the next investment. Treating the first-stage learning gain as a permanently positive deployment multiplier ignores this selection.

Prior art: Chen and Waggoner, [Informational Substitutes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08636) (March 2017 abstract), formalize decision-value information complementarity. Anunrojwong, [Structure and Design of Informational Substitutes](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/1bf75cbb-60fb-456f-9ac0-2ad745440cc8) (2018 abstract), explicitly studies XOR structures. Thus neither XOR information synergy nor Bayesian decision theory is claimed novel. Wang and Fang, [KITE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22113) (June 2026 abstract), report decoupling interaction intent from embodiment-specific motor control and adapting an action decoder from a new embodiment's kinematic model without recollecting demonstrations; this motivates separate transferable and local components, not the assumed binary parity, actual costs or economic returns. Abstract-level checks 25 November 2026; no full-paper audit or commercial calibration. DDA section 5 and Appendix B already treat endogenous data and complementarity.

## 2. Information, qualification and dated choices

After ordinary production and all baseline reports, let source method theta and recipient alignment eta be independent binary states with P(theta=1)=a>=1/2, P(eta=1)=b>=1/2. They persist through this installation window; the source archive is represented by its posterior a, not by the count of records. Two candidate target policies are indexed by z in {0,1}; the one matching theta XOR eta can pass a safe local qualification test. This is a deliberately exact binary abstraction of a shared calibration relationship. A test is not a deployment, and a failed test is never used in paid production.

The firm may do nothing extra, take one extra source signal of theta with symmetric accuracy r, obtain a target diagnostic of eta with symmetric accuracy q, or commit an independent native embodiment E. There is one preparatory opportunity before the qualification cutoff. Either signal takes one crew slot; the test occupies one slot and successful B commissioning one more. At most one B test can be attempted before expiry; no test without enough time to commission if it passes. E's native configuration is assumed qualified after a known three-slot install, uses the same B customer order, and has additional net cost F relative to B. It is not a second B order. These are explicit engineering restrictions, not general claims about the feasibility of alternate robots. Two A installations each occupy two crew slots and generate net contribution 4; B contributes B>0 once, net of acquisition and operation; E contributes B-F. There are H slots in this dated window, no other funding or parts constraint, and all other candidate orders remain available until the window ends. Dispatch can use any still feasible old route. Include all costs of extra signals in a general application; here the acquisition burden is entirely the crew opportunity cost. Independent terminal service opportunities have positive values and cannot be duplicated. No unsafe attempt, free forced adaptation or benchmark-to-dollar conversion.

Let x=P(theta=1|reports), y=P(eta=1|reports). With separate conditionally independent measurement channels, posterior parity 1 has probability x(1-y)+(1-x)y. Thus the best technical qualification probability is

    p(x,y) = (1+|(2x-1)(2y-1)|)/2.

When b=1/2, another source observation cannot raise p above 1/2, however accurate. A symmetric target signal with q>=1/2 yields |2y-1|=2q-1 for either report, hence p_T=1/2+(2a-1)(2q-1)/2. Its controlled technical return rises with archive quality. If a=1/2, the target report also has no standalone predictive value. When alignment is already known, source refinement may matter and the target report is redundant. If a and b are correlated, use the joint posterior; the product formula is not valid. If the target contrast comes from existing instrumentation at no incremental cost, or an equivalent native recipe is already available, the acquisition comparison must reflect that alternative.

## 3. Solve the actual portfolio, not just p

After any preparatory report, choose whether to test and which policy. Update the joint belief after the safe qualification outcome. On success either commission or decline B; on failure use remaining slots for A; the option to skip testing altogether remains. The final choice maximizes net accepted contribution plus discounted successor value. Backward induction on these finite histories gives the optimal policy: maximize final schedule conditional on lawful information, sum across test outcomes, maximize over test/skip and policy choices, then sum across report outcomes and maximize over initial routes. This is exactly the best fixed state-contingent procedure given the known law, not a claim that an adaptive agent beats a competent fixed controller.

For H=4 and b=1/2, without successor effects (or within the regime in which these same choices remain optimal), no preparatory signal yields either both As (8), or a test at p=1/2 followed by B+A on success, A on failure. Test value B/2+4. A target signal reduces remaining slots to 3, so testing yields B alone on success and A on failure. Its current value is p_T B+(1-p_T)4, compared also to skipping (4). An extra source signal has the same crew occupation as the target signal but p=1/2. E yields B-F. If B>8 and both test routes are selected within their arms, the target-minus-no-preparation current payoff is

    delta_current = (p_T-1/2)B - 4 p_T.

Proof: subtract the two contingent payoff sums. At equal information value, losing a crew slot can destroy an otherwise complementary A installation. In particular, improved technical qualification does not alone establish higher value, and the minimum accuracy for a profitable diagnostic depends on the alternative customer's margin and crew incidence. At arbitrary H, demand, diagnostic cost or E cost, use the exact finite decision tree, not this restricted formula.

## 4. Successor evidence is a distribution induced by the selected portfolio

At the next generation, suppose a lawful useful record can qualify a single additional original C opportunity before expiry, with independent funding and dated acceptance capacity already sufficient. The opportunity earns net w>=0 if the record is available and the best program admits it, otherwise it is not admitted; a common ordinary fallback is omitted from both values. The record is not itself a dollar reward: w is the distinct incremental successor service after the cost of the next installation. For the solved restriction the earlier archive and diagnostic do not independently qualify C; only a new eligible record, including ordinary capture, can. If current evidence also has direct successor use, include the full posterior and retained archive in the successor state and reoptimize.

Let ordinary capture probability be u0, and per commissioned A, B or E useful-record probabilities u_A(theta,eta), u_B(theta,eta), u_E(theta,eta). Conditional on latent state, the selected portfolio and lawful capture rights, independent new draws give

    P(e'=1 | theta,eta,n_A,n_B,n_E)
        = 1-(1-u0) product_j (1-u_j(theta,eta))^n_j.

Integrate this expression under the joint latent-state/selected-portfolio law of each optimized program. This conditional independence restriction can be removed by specifying a joint record kernel; using unconditional average per-body rates or average commissioned counts inside the product is generally wrong. The current safe qualification outcome and selected portfolio are correlated with the latent state; preserve that conditioning. If C's continuation value depends on the latent state or posterior, integrate its conditional optimized value rather than multiplying by constant w. Private returns use captured contribution, whereas a real-surplus comparison uses resource costs and final service values without internal transfers.

**Proposition (signed second-generation propagation).** For the fixed service branches in section 3, with state-independent hazards u_A,u_B and p=p_T, the change in the probability of inducing C is

    delta_C = (1-u0) [ p(u_B-u_A) - (u_B/2)(1-u_A) ].

Proof: no-preparation miss probability is (1-u0)(1-u_A)(1-u_B/2), because one A always operates and B operates on half the histories. Target-preparation miss probability is (1-u0)[(1-p)(1-u_A)+p(1-u_B)]. Subtract in the opposite order to obtain the displayed formula. Consequently delta_C is negative when u_B<=u_A except degenerate equalities, despite B expansion. It can be positive when B teaches sufficiently more than A. With u_A=u_B>0 and <1, the effect is negative because the diagnostic displaces joint exposure. The induced C installation difference equals delta_C only under the stated binary successor admission and common positive net payoff; otherwise optimize the successor schedule. Even with a positive current recipient effect, the signed next effect is not determined by the current commissioning vector without knowing the joint record law.

The target-minus-direct program value within this regime is delta_current+beta*w*delta_C. If delta_current>0 and delta_C<0, a sufficiently valuable successor opportunity can reverse the initial decision, provided the regime remains valid and no other route wins. Thus the firm's choice to improve target transfer can depend on which future markets the displaced source environments would have taught. This is a specific mechanism of endogenous propagation, not merely a generic feedback matrix. Outside the regime solve the full dynamic policy comparison.

## 5. Worked economies, after the mechanism

Synthetic parameters: H=4, a=.9, b=.5, q=1, B=20, F=6, u0=.2, beta=1, w=2. All options, including native E and ordinary operation that learns, are available to both programs except the focal optional target diagnostic. Both use the best feasible state-contingent response. A and B produce independent conditional record draws, not automatically proportional to throughput.

| Outcome | Without target option (optimized) | With target option (optimized) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Current captured contribution | 14 | 18.4 |
| A installations | 1 | .1 |
| B installations | .5 | .9 |
| C installation probability, u_A=.8, u_B=.1 | .848 | .336 |
| Value including C | 15.696 | 19.072 |

The diagnostic expands B by .4 but reduces A by .9 and C by .512. Swap the two hazard rates, leaving all first-generation technical and economic parameters fixed: both programs select the same respective current routes at w=2, yet C rises from .568 to .784, an increase of .216. Current technical and production observations alone do not identify propagation. In the first environment the target premium is 4.4-.512w. The strict switch against direct testing occurs at w=275/32=8.59375, within the branch regime (the A-only, extra-source and E alternatives are inferior around this threshold). At w=25 the full decision tree selects no preparation. These values are illustrative, not field estimates. Raising the successor value changes the current deployment/diagnostic decision without changing its engineering effectiveness. A cheap external diagnostic or lower capture rate at A could eliminate this reversal.

Selection counterexample independent of that numerical economy: two worlds share P(B commissioned)=1/2; a persistent latent state gives potential B-record probability .9 in one state and .1 in the other, each state equally common. In world 1 B qualifies only in the high-record state, in world 2 only in the low-record state. The unconditional potential record rate is .5 in both; with no other captures the realized contribution to successor admission is .45 versus .05. Equal installation and unconditional record potential marginals do not identify the joint effect. Observing usable records conditional on actual qualified installations can distinguish the worlds, but estimating the full intervention effect needs matched support under the alternative policy, including refusal/expiry histories.

## 6. Research and empirical implications

Compare paid source refinement, recipient-only diagnostic, their combination, and the native embodiment against retained ordinary production. Use matched lawful source checkpoint and original customer roster, identical available crew calendar and separate actual fees/rights. Assign at the shared crew/release catchment; report omitted as well as completed orders. Record source confidence, recipient mapping tests, qualification attempts, dated occupation and old policy retention, actual commissioning and accepted services by embodiment, then new lawful evidence and qualified successor original service. A benchmark gain only prices technical success. Follow unchosen and canceled source sites using authorized reference coverage when feasible, not fabricated counterfactuals. In repetitive handling the question is whether mapping or archive repetition is limiting; in multistage manufacturing it is whether a calibration slot displaces another commissioning opportunity; in maintenance/inspection it is whether the missed rare-state exposure can be recovered from an outside test before expiry. In digital exception handling a schema alignment can play the role of eta, but no physical equivalence is assumed.

Next: retain the joint posterior and source-specific archive across repeated hardware generations, allow alignment drift conditional on redesign, and solve whether the firm should keep a reference configuration or purchase a new contrast under separate shared validation and production resources. Test against a capable fixed contingent policy and fresh field evidence; do not treat policy-dependent record counts as transportable. This chapter establishes a conditional two-generation system, not a fully endogenous long-run equilibrium, commercial data valuation, or scientific novelty.
