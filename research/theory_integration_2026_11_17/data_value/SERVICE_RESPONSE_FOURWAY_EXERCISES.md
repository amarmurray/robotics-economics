# Student exercises — four-way causal paid-service audit

Use the definitions and complete policies in \`SERVICE_RESPONSE_FOURWAY_CAUSAL_AUDIT.md\`; no answers here.

1. **Nonanticipative SCM.** Starting with independent H,U,V fair bits, derive \(H\oplus A\oplus C\oplus W=\theta\). Explain why W's structural equation can be evaluated at t2 even though A,C offers materialize at t3. What is the t2 repair job and why can it not be cleared in the same t2 window?

2. **Why triples fail.** Prove that the even- and odd-parity laws have the same distribution on *every* subset of at most three coordinates of (H,A,C,W), but different \(\Pr(H=A=C=W=1)\). Show that the full R original-offer history is identical across the worlds.

3. **Economic union.** Prove \(E[W(A\lor HC)]=E[WA]+E[WHC]-E[WAHC]\). Compute R,P,G,E values from the stipulated paid services and actual resource costs. Identify the counterfactual that an invalid additive transfer premium would double-count.

4. **Investment under ignorance.** Derive the sharp G interval from the parity-mixture family. Solve the minimax-regret G/E mix *without* assuming the allocator sees \(\theta\), and contrast it with maximin production.

5. **Audit design.** With all G one-/two-/three-way moments known from lawful assigned cohorts, derive an audit of original joint A/C offers with positive observation support only on H=W=1. Explain why the same audit under R cannot identify G's causal response. Explain why invoices only are not generally a substitute.

6. **Extension.** Add an optional t4 buyer repeat order after a t3 worker job can clear. Construct one history where H=1,W=0 gives zero t3 paid output but a strictly positive value of a t3 buyer report for the t4 dispatch. State why the narrowed H=W=1-only audit is not sufficient for the extended horizon.
