# Prospective observation and provenance schema: economically sufficient histories

23 November 2026. This is **unpopulated** design documentation, not licensed field access or approval to conduct an intervention. Companion to WHAT_MUST_THE_FIRM_REMEMBER.md.

**Unit and assignment:** Buyer–worker–model-sharing cluster; distinguish original buyer offers from bids and selected paid invoices. Version program R/P/G/E, action S/M, worker support pool, eligibility and lawful assignment probabilities before an intervention. Preserve nonparticipants and original no-offer dates. Register cross-cluster data/model sharing and any interference. Independent E hardware and support must be *actually* checked rather than stipulated from a sales slide.

**Time-ordered ledger:** At baseline: original buyer rights, remaining budgets, prices/margins and accepted-service recipe, physical cells, worker job IDs/queue. Before each decision: known prior signals, allowed decision set, diagnostic permissions and response deadline. After each t3 action: paid accepted output/quality, new failure and recovery claims, operator minutes, source events actually produced, censoring and failed collection. Before t4 validation: retained records by modality (field success, production failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained), origin task/site/embodiment, episode ID, distinct fault-family count, model release, rights for retention/training/transfer/deployment and target grounding version. During validation: one slot/worker assignment, corpus selected, training compute, failed qualification, joint A+C outcome. At t4: original offered and declined service, worker availability and jobs, full accepted buyer payment and any necessary real incremental cost. All categories are separate; a human label is not assumed to be the unique valuable evidence unit.

**Minimal information-only treatment:** Randomly and lawfully hide/show a versioned packet's provenance to the scheduling/validation rule *without changing physical records, rights, training and validation availability*, conditional on a supported decision stage. Compare realized accepted orders under a fixed eligibility set; a prior safety gate cannot be hidden. Withhold-after-decision reports are invalid for this price. An alternative treatment actually changes source collection/maintenance and must be analyzed as a distinct causal policy.

**Target validation:** For each source-to-target link, use matched training budgets and frozen starting models, with versus without a signed source contribution; evaluate native and cross-embodiment qualification on target recipes and the **joint** complete order. Separate technical accuracy from accepted paid throughput and the availability of the worker and buyer budget. Suppression of recovery events after an upstream model upgrade may alter collection exposure; audit a lawful independent subset of successes and failures to avoid incident-selected-only data.

**Decision record:** For a candidate compression T, record feasible actions by T-cell, estimates or justified bounds on q_z(h,a) for each supported action, the common-optimal-action test, and the gross value-of-information bound net of actual report/validation cost. Do not infer G/M outcomes from P or G/S histories when those actions lack support. Keep an explicit unsupported cell flag.

**Current empirical status:** No customer consent/right, G/M randomized observation, paired original-order and repair histories, verified E staffed capacity or full verified interindustry numeric input-output table. These are planned fields; not observed facts. All synthetic outcome values in the chapter are stipulations.
