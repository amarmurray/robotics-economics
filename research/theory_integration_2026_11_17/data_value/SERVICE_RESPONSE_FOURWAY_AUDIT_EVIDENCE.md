# Prospective early-service, four-way original-buyer/worker audit

**Not populated. No confidential, proprietary or hypothetical-as-observed records.** This specifies how the four-way causal-response proposition in \`SERVICE_RESPONSE_FOURWAY_CAUSAL_AUDIT.md\` could be falsified. An R-only log is never relabeled G support.

**Original unit.** A physically closed cluster: two original customer order streams A and C, one shared repair worker/qualified late slot, independently eligible E staffing where applicable, and every model-weight/robot-support edge that can interfere with its outcomes. Register offered/no-order, refused and served buyer actions before seeing trial response. Track separate repeat budgets (A one; C two in the illustrative case) and each buyer's actual payment/acceptance conditions.

**Program assignment at t0.** Record prospective, approved and randomized R/P/G/E assignment with propensity and sampling unit, G target-grounding lineage/license/retention/evaluation/deployment rights, exact old-policy fallback, P source acquisition/qualification recipe, E machine/operator independence and capital cost. Do not run a G trial absent physical and legal authorization. If G probability is zero, explicitly report no G response identification; use sensitivity/robust policy bounds instead.

**Time-ordered physical and commercial fields.** t1: original buyer offers, accepted units and remaining budgets, previously owed individual repair jobs and clearance. t2 before action: original offers, authorized worker slot, source/type of selected field, failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic or public/pretrained material, model/embodiment/version, compute and validation. t2 after action: accepted **paid** C quality H, price/contribution, costs, customer awareness, new distinct repair job J with creation time *after* clearance, repeat hazard and transfer qualification. t3 pre-dispatch: original A,C offers/no-offers, late buyer budgets, shared W, independent E technician availability, technical/rights qualification and actual observable reports. t3 after dispatch: chosen qualified recipe, whether one shared slot or independent E slot used, accepted paid units, refused units, invoices, rework and escalation.

**Additional economically sufficient report for this fixed synthetic menu.** First establish G-specific E[H], E[WA], E[WHC] and baseline R/P/E margins on supported assigned cohorts. Then measure original A and C orders *together* on a randomized positive share of G-assigned H=W=1 opportunities, including unserved demand. This is the \(\zeta_G=E[HACW]\) fourth-order audit; H and W themselves must be observed throughout. Record the inclusion mechanism \(\eta(H,W,X)\) **before** looking at late unreported orders. For term HWA C, reporting positivity outside H=W=1 is unnecessary; for other dynamic/continuation questions it may be indispensable. Report violation when invoice-triggered selection or worker/quality-dependent missingness is not adjusted for.

**Causal/information validity.** G is a t0 treatment; H is post-treatment early accepted service and conditioning on it does not create counterfactual R-to-G exchangeability. Preserve original network assignment, task mix, potential cross-cluster model sharing, late demand, total worker obligations, nonresponse and censoring. Precommitment G choice cannot use t3 audit observations. Time-stamped reports may improve choices for later cohorts, with their own fee and delay. Compare measured technical target qualification on matched source/grounding/equipment/compute panels separately from captured customer contribution and complete accepted output.

**Failure conditions.** Reject the narrow model if early t2 paid C service exhausts actual repeat budget, an attempted G trial cannot generate J when H=0, late A/C offers cannot change with t2 service, E is not independently supportable, quality fails target acceptance or t2 job creation/clearance differs. A four-way audit matters only if the corresponding late shared slot, buyer demand and qualification are physically reachable. Record unobserved G support, unverifiable original offers, signed rights or BEA numerical tables as missing; do not substitute task accuracy or promotional vendor reports.

**Roadmap crosswalk.** Joint original orders identify demand interactions. Worker occupation identifies cross-fleet support congestion. Qualification measures source/grounding transfer. E independence identifies embodiment supply and capital complement. Observed invoices versus eligible offers identify capture and selection. Add actual official industry interdependencies to the separate atlas only after obtaining BEA numerical table cells with year, table, units, and industry codes; verified landing/API documentation alone is not a numeric backbone.
