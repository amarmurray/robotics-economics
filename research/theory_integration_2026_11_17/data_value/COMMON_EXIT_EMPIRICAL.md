# Prospective empirical design — can two learned policies share an unsupported residual?

**18 November 2026; protocol only, no target field evidence or physical approval.**

**Unit.** A policy-closed whole customer order/dispatch/operator/controller network with a fixed, pre-treatment service opportunity census, time horizon, captured-value/cost accounting and contract/right versions. Do not assume independent robot assignments in a shared market. Include original B accepted orders and counterfactual losses, customer substitutions, rejected/unattempted work, recovery and operator peak calls, and non-robot specialist alternatives.

**Arms.** Frozen retained F/B policy; independently approved no-copy A checkpoint; same-body, same-budget rights-cleared source-copy A checkpoint with pinned source-availability versus source-use; independently approved target grounding/report arm; combined source-plus-grounding arm when actually qualified; certified new-body candidate; competent specialist S. Track source modalities separately: routine field, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained. Record training/curation/rights costs and data-plane impacts on incumbent service.

**First-stage technical outcome.** Joint held-out acceptance of complete customer recipes by source-copy/no-copy trained A under matched budget/checkpoint, with variant-specific policy-induced states and qualification correlations. Record failures and recovery paths rather than only isolated scores; no benchmark-to-cash mapping.

**Second-stage causal/economic outcome.** For each frozen policy version, record its *own* safe target first-exit history, p_ij, identified exit-prefix and no-exit contribution K_i, and remaining full accepted original-B/A service and customer demand. Prespecify labels j using customer/opportunity ID, action/embodiment, controller lineage, recovery/operator demand, dispatcher version, market/time and contract; split heterogeneous labels instead of borrowing a scalar u across them.

**Direct falsification of common residual.** Test the post-exit **complete order-level** residual under policy changes that are independently safe and share an exact conditional state: compare B-order substitution, qualified target service, operator resource peak, dispatch routes and contractual captured margins. A source-training-induced recovery or changed demand-policy effect invalidates common u even when aggregate residual means look similar. If safe matched interventions cannot be conducted, say common u is unverified and use separate first-exit intervals or a justified partial-epsilon sensitivity interval, not cancellation.

**Identification and uncertainty.** Estimate signed exit occupancy differences d_j from approved version-pinned target transitions, not F source distributions. Account for customer selection, missing order attempts, simultaneous network interactions, persistent latent demand and validation-window expiry. Use a joint confidence/identified set for K,p,u, not the exact point-parameter formula as a data-driven confidence interval. A common persistent theta across all comparison arms forbids independent worst-world selection. If grounding only diagnoses u, record the signal law, cost, physical approval and whether it changes market outcomes; if it trains a new policy, remeasure K,p and qualification.

**No empirical claim.** Public logging/RMF primitives are not actual connected-fleet isolation or approved customer experiments. The required actual source-copy/no-copy evidence, whole-order panel, physically approved target transitions, exact conditional equivalence and numeric BLS–BEA sector crosswalk remain unavailable; the comparison theorem is a research design, not a deployment authorization.
