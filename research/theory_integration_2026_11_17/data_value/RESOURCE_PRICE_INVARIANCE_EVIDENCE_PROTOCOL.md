# Prospective evidence: when does a shared operator opportunity remain stable across reports?

**21 November 2026. Design only: no authorized field policy, buyer data, or receipts.** Companion to [the approximate-invariance theory](RESOURCE_PRICE_INVARIANCE_AND_DATA_VALUE.md); extends the [earlier exact-invariance protocol](COUPLED_RESOURCE_EVIDENCE_PROTOCOL.md) without replacing it.

**Target object:** An original order cohort, known before Y is used for dispatch, with paid outside-job alternative λ_y attainable under the *same* legally signed time/resource/rights envelope. An operator shadow price, advertised price or unaccepted conditional quote is *not* λ_y. State explicitly whether the outside job remains physically qualified, available, accepted, and paid in every report stratum.

**Versioned row schema:** original buyer order/offer/denominator and prechoice Y and coarse Z; competing original outside buyer/order, offer and refuser status; paid/refunded/settled contribution and expected net operating cost; signed robotic controller/embodiment/operator shift and slot use; recovery and fallback permissions; chosen *whole network* A/B/R/E action, assignment propensity at the closed shared-learner cluster level, attempted delivery, joint qualification, acceptance and payment; training checkpoint, rights, source episode family, admission and curation budget when X/G-train is studied. Capture durations and whether an apparently unused slot can genuinely serve the outside job within the same deadline. Preserve original customer nonrespondents and altered offers rather than rebase to accepted jobs.

**Discriminating audit:** (1) Check the same contract/eligible outside buyer and recoverable slot for each prechoice Y. (2) Give defensible report-specific *attainable* paid opportunity intervals [l_y,u_y], acknowledging unobserved alternative receipts. (3) Solve the smallest ε and common λ compatible with all intervals **only when the joint coupling of alternatives is supportable**; naive overlap of intervals is not proof. Distinguish seller/report effects on outside arrivals, prices, acceptance, durations, operator permissions and original customer offer. (4) Validate fixed A/B whole-network signatures and service contributions; if they vary, abandon equations (3)-(6), recalculate broader polytope report-value bounds. (5) If ε<1/4 can be *defended* jointly, a positive lower within-program report-value certificate is available under the theorem; cost and E-dominance still decide whether the information is worth buying. If not, return to the noncoupled [signed margins](SIGNED_ACTION_MARGIN_SUPPORT_AND_REPORT_VALUE.md) and current negative dominance result.

**Safe experimental boundary:** Propose independent legal opportunity-cost evidence from actually settled comparable orders and versioned terms, or prospective closed-network randomized complete approved policies where safety and buyer consent are independently obtained. A counterfactual displaced outside order is not itself an observed receipt. Never assign an unsigned controller solely to fill a missing Y×A outcome. Report-dependent outside availability is a real mechanism, not nuisance error to be forced into common λ. Collect a complete natural original offer panel, not only robot attempts.

**For Model B:** Whole-learner X archive inclusion, collection of genuinely new source episodes, G-train, and embodiment E are interventions that change joint technical qualifications, source opportunity costs, resource recipes and potentially customer demand. G-observe at fixed qualified policies is a different causal estimand. Match held-out *joint* target service qualification and full original accepted-paid customer order contributions; separate source rights and compute/curation costs. This protocol licenses neither real trials nor outreach and supplies no field evidence. Quantitative BEA/BLS production-network edges and full independent replication remain open.
