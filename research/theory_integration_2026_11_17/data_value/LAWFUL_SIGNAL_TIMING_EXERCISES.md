# Exercises: ex-ante source/grounding report and embodiment commitment

Student exercises, 22 November 2026. Synthetic accepted-and-cleared original-buyer contribution units only. Companion to [the complete proof](LAWFUL_SIGNAL_TIMING_AND_RECURSIVE_DATA_OPTION.md). Instructor solutions are maintained separately.

1. **Information-order proof.** Starting from B_a(y)=max_(pi in Pi_a)sum_z Pr(z,y)v_(a,pi,z), prove W_early>=W_late>=W_none. Characterize equality in the first inequality including possible ties, without assuming one unique physical controller. Explain why the second inequality might fail if acquiring Y forces re-training, loss of rights or operator occupancy.

2. **A timely licensed report.** Use complete contribution rows P/R=(2,4), P/pilot=(3/2,5), E/R=(19/10,39/10), E/capacity=(11/5,9/2). Let Pr(Z=1)=1/2 and a binary symmetric predecision report have accuracy q=3/4. Derive both posteriors, the optimal report-contingent program, W_early, W_late, W_none, and the maximum cost of an early signal. Why is E capacity not a free old policy under P?

3. **Analytic policy frontiers.** For q from 1/2 to 1, show precisely where the report changes the early program decision, and when a report arriving after commitment improves the best program-locked value. Verify both piecewise closed forms without fitting a new parameter grid.

4. **Unknown prevalence and active acquisition.** The producer knows only theta in [1/4,3/4] and the same q=3/4 signal likelihood. Prove the maximin absolute whole-program value of an optional free report equals the no-report value. Identify why a positive Bayesian gain at p=1/2 is compatible. Then give one concrete physical counterexample to treating a deliberately generated operator-assisted experiment as an exogenous cost-only Y, and name the original-buyer evidence needed to distinguish the two.
