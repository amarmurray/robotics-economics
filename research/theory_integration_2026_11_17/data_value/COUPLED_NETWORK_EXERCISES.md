# Student exercises — coupled economic data transfer and network ambiguity

Companion to [nonrectangular connected-network chapter](COUPLED_NETWORK_ECONOMIC_ENVELOPE.md). Synthetic normalized buyer-paid contributions. Do not treat numbers as field data. **Instructor solutions are in a separate file.**

1. **Common exogenous regime.** Define precisely what it means for the same latent preprogram regime prevalence theta to govern source-trained P and new embodiment E, even though their conditional buyer-order/learner/recovery responses can differ. Give one plausible counterexample in which using the same theta is invalid.

2. **Optimized policy envelope.** With P conditional net rows (2,4),(3/2,5) and E rows (19/10,39/10),(3/2,9/2), calculate both policy switchpoints. Prove that the sharp possible optimized P−E gap on theta∈[0,1] is [1/10,1/2]. Do not infer a general theorem from numerical grids.

3. **The overconservative rectangle.** Compute min_theta J_P*(theta)−max_theta J_E*(theta) and explain exactly why it is not the sharp common-theta difference. What would have to fail for the common-theta approach itself to be invalid?

4. **No deployable uniform controller.** Suppose an operator must select an unchanging probability rho of piloting with P before learning theta. Show that no rho in [0,1] matches E's theta-tailored optimum at both theta=0 and theta=1, notwithstanding the positive optimized P/E envelope. Explain the role of a timely legally usable regime report.

5. **Counterexample to automatic common-factor dominance.** Replace E's capacity row by (11/5,9/2), keeping E's retained row. Determine the sign of P−E at theta=0 and 1 and derive the interior zero. Why is this not a negative-transfer result about optional P source data?

6. **Research/identification.** Specify original-buyer, qualified-service, rights and whole-network exposure variables required to estimate v_(P,pi,z) in an authorized cluster trial. Explain why conditioning on a successful post-training checkpoint is not a valid substitute for a preassignment regime.

7. **Beyond a two-type law.** For three exogenous network regimes with unknown mixture weights in a simplex, propose a finite exact candidate algorithm for sharp comparison of maxima of finite affine policy-value functions. Explain where full-history learner dynamics do or do not invalidate affineness, and distinguish an ex-post optimizer from a robust fixed policy.
