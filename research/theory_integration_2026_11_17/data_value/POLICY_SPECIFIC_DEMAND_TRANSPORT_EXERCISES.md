# Student exercises — incumbent audit versus intervention-specific buyer demand
19 Nov 2026. Use hypothetical normalized contribution units, not dollars. Separate [instructor solutions](POLICY_SPECIFIC_DEMAND_TRANSPORT_SOLUTIONS.md).

1. Starting from a_p=9+gamma_p/2,b_p=7+gamma_p/2 and q=(1/2,1/2), derive P_A=8,P_B=6 for any gamma_p. Why does one retained service mix not identify gamma_R or gamma_X?

2. With J_X(g)=19/4+g/2+(3-g)^2/(4-2g), J_G(g)=(65-g)/8-11/10 and J_E(g)=9-g/4-11/5, show that under fully unrestricted rectangular post-policy gamma_p∈[-1,1], perfect knowledge of gamma_R cannot raise the pure robust investment value above retained R=7. Could the audit still be valuable under a Bayesian prior?

3. Explain why a 50/50 lottery between X and G has an expected worst-case value above 7 under a single common gamma, but fails under a rectangular uncertainty set for gamma_X,gamma_G. Prove the latter for general X probability t.

4. Impose r=gamma_R∈[L,U], |gamma_p-r|<=delta with independent feasible policy shifts and fixed price anchoring. Derive the sharp interval for J_X(gamma_X)-J_G(gamma_G). Explain why it might be conservative under correlated policy changes.

5. Let delta=1/4, r=+1/2 or -1/2 exactly. Compute full-menu upper/lower values of X/G/E and R. State the conditional safe action and gain above R **before audit acquisition costs**.

6. If incumbent r is measured only to epsilon error, find a sufficient X>G criterion in terms of gamma* and delta. Derive the two-quote T_A=P_A+2q_A slope formula and error bound 2eta/|h|. Name the commercial qualification and customer selection conditions it requires.

7. Propose a source-conditioned information acquisition experiment comparing baseline R quote data with prospective X/G/E original-buyer offer/refusal outcomes; explain why target-specific data may matter economically even with fewer trajectories and why physical execution is not authorized by this mathematical analysis.
