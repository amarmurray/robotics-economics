# November 22 working Models A–B: joint choice and priced menus

**Working bridge; acceptance gate OPEN, 19 November 2026.**

The same core model V(Q,omega)=max complete accepted-service contribution subject to robot/operator/integration/rights constraints and J=V−K continues. Adding this chapter does not rename the first paper or replace the earlier robust joint qualification and dynamic data-acquisition material.

**Model A (static production certificate).** Original procurement group i chooses at most one substitute service j from an ex-ante posted menu. A qualified complete-service assignment faces A x≤b and original-buyer Σ_j x_ij≤1; observed offered/refused choices, policy-quality drift and independent captured-receipt/physical resource envelopes deliver outer choice sets and a buyer-aware LP dual. Constructively guaranteed, safely reserved buyer choices produce a lower accepted-service bound. Interactions (source X + grounding G + embodiment E) enter both qualification and physical requirements; optimizing posted price and offered assortment is a genuine economic decision, not an exogenous historical anchor.

**Model B (dynamic enterprise/market policy).** Approved source acquisition, learning, recording, target grounding, robot production and embodiment upgrades change time-indexed service quality, qualification, operator recovery and buyer choice/price policy. The admissible policy set contains retained R only when truly feasible. The complete history H includes jointly evolving buyer demand, rival substitutes, data visitation, controller versions and shared resources. Whole-network randomized policies identify mean J directly under verified cluster closure and physical admission, while Model A certificates may rank investments under less direct experimentation only if its uniform envelopes hold over Model B histories. Dynamic learning data continuation belongs in Bellman value once; no extra dataset premium.

**First-paper candidate scope.** The credible focus remains a source-to-qualified-service production bridge with endogenous customer production and scarce resources; buyer-aware pricing is an identification/economic-value extension, not a claim to have invented choice-based revenue management. An indispensable empirical contrast is technical success/target-qualification rates versus realized accepted **complete service** at the customer offered-price/choice-set level, with operator resource reservation and retained-controller control. Failing this matched panel leaves a theoretical/measurement program, not a measured law.
