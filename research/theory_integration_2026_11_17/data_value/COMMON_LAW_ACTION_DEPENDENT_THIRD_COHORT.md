## Question and contribution boundary

The earlier three-cohort identity prices the redundancy of source G (licensed field-failure grounding) and source K (worker-generated recovery traces) through the probability that both qualify *the same payable, worker-available* service. Its witness assumed acquisition did not change the buyer's later original order or the recovery worker's availability. Now acquisition itself changes both. Can old R data, full G-only and K-only production laws, and even paired technical qualification data identify the value of jointly offering G and K? **No.** The information missing in a physically connected economy is a *cross-program coincidence* linking G qualification to the K-specific worker displacement and final customer response.

This is a synthesis/application of standard potential-outcomes, finite-state decision, and event-intersection mathematics. Farboodi, Koh and Xia, [*Data-Driven Automation* (2026)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35320), already make data endogenous and forward-looking, including spillovers; this chapter does not claim invention of future data valuation or complementary data. Our narrower economic object joins an actual qualified customer production recipe, a single original buyer budget, provenance-conditional learning, and an action-dependent physical worker.

## Physical timing and common structural law

A buyer has one unfulfilled ticket with sufficient authorized remaining budget for *at most one* accepted later sale. At date 1, the incumbent attempts it. With probability \(1-p\) it succeeds, earns \(r_1\) and closes the ticket. With probability \(p\) it fails: no payment, one genuine worker repair job, and a typed field-failure trajectory \(L\). Lawful rights to retain/train on \(L\) determine whether G is feasible. Successful repair creates a *different*, provenance-stamped recovery trajectory \(K\); a failed repair does not. Neither source is a proxy for teleoperation, simulation, synthetic or public-pretraining inputs, which remain separate potential inputs in the general source ledger.

Conditioning on a failed first service, let an exogenous, not necessarily independent common type \(U=(B,S,C,M,H,T)\) specify baseline original-buyer willingness \(B\), buyer response to a credible G-grounding commitment \(S\), actual worker-job clearance \(C\), remaining dispatch capacity after K-specific recovery/training time \(M\), licensed G complete-recipe qualification \(H\), and K complete-recipe qualification \(T\). Take all components binary. \(T\) can only be valid when a recovery trace was actually produced, so \(T\le C\); in the witness \(T\le CM\). These variables are **not** interchangeable success percentages. Their joint law \(\pi\) must represent one physically possible population and its paired counterfactuals, not independently chosen marginal laws for each program.

The lawful precommitted programs are R (retain old service and recover), G (compute-only licensed failure-origin grounding, retain the fallback), K (learn from recovery trajectory and occupy worker time), GK (retain independently selectable G and K checkpoints after paying for both), and E (new target-qualified embodiment with genuinely independent staffing and its own buyer response). In a tractable submodel the potential original buyer offers and free-worker indicators are

\[
D_R=B,\quad D_G=D_{GK}=B\vee S,\quad D_K=B,
\qquad W_R=W_G=C,\quad W_K=W_{GK}=CM.
\tag{1}
\]

The buyer response \(S\) is a potential response to an actionable, disclosed target-grounding program—not an observed exogenous order increase attributed to robot accuracy. K training can prevent final dispatch even after completing the inherited repair. Equation (1) is a **tested hypothesis to be falsified**, not a general law: G can itself occupy a worker, K can affect customer expectations, and training can improve clearance. For the displayed construction G and K technical checkpoints can be retained independently, so \(Q_{GK}=H\vee T\); forced joint training does not inherit that OR identity. Both old fallback and upgraded checkpoint can serve only **one** order.

Write \(m_0\) for the contribution of one accepted old-recipe service, \(m_1=m_0+\Delta\) for an accepted upgraded same-ticket service, \(c_G,c_K\) for *entire costs per original failed cluster* of the relevant acquired program, and \(C_E\) for the full precommitted E build/staff/validation cost. With a valid law, the full original-cluster economic values are

\[
\begin{aligned}
J_R &= (1-p)r_1+p\,m_0\mathbb E[BC],\\
J_G &= (1-p)r_1+p\{\mathbb E[(B\vee S)C(m_0+\Delta H)]-c_G\},\\
J_K &= (1-p)r_1+p\{\mathbb E[BCM(m_0+\Delta T)]-c_K\},\\
J_{GK} &= (1-p)r_1+p\{\mathbb E[(B\vee S)CM(m_0+\Delta(H\vee T))]-c_G-c_K\},\\
J_E &= (1-p)r_1+p\,m_E\mathbb E[D_E]-C_E.
\end{aligned}\tag{2}
\]

The last line requires legally and physically verified \(E\) capacity, a qualified recipe and the program-specific original buyer response \(D_E\); it does not borrow R's worker or count the same ticket twice. For general dynamic states replace the terminal expectation with the *optimized continuation production value* carrying buyer budget, queue, learning lineage and rights. **Do not add a second data premium.**

### Source/worker displacement proposition

Subtracting \(J_G\) from \(J_{GK}\), under (1), gives the exact event decomposition

\[
\boxed{\frac{J_{GK}-J_G}{p}
=\Delta\,\mathbb E[(B\vee S)CM(1-H)T]
-\mathbb E[(B\vee S)C(1-M)(m_0+\Delta H)]
-c_K .}\tag{3}
\]

**Proof.** Split GK's payable worker event into \(CM\) and its complement inside G's payable \(C\) event; on \(CM\), \((H\vee T)-H=(1-H)T\). The first term is newly qualified K service on the *same eligible original order*; the second is accepted old or G-upgraded production displaced by K's worker occupation; the third is the incremental real acquisition cost. This is a whole-program difference, not a dollar conversion of benchmark accuracy. QED.

Consequently, **technical complementarity need not be economic complementarity**, and the economically decisive K marginal may be negative despite retaining both checkpoints. More source data that can be *ignored at no incremental cost and without changing the physical/legal action set* cannot lower optimized value; committing to process that data can. If instead the customer requires an arm-and-mobile bundle with AND qualification, substitute a validated complete-recipe qualification event, not the OR formula. This change in production topology can reverse the overlap sign.

## Exact physically coherent common-law counterexample

All following numbers are **normalized synthetic contributions**, not robot cash estimates. Condition on the actual first-service failure and take eight equally likely types indexed 0–7. Set

* \(B=1\) on 0–3 and \(B=0\) on 4–7; \(S=1\) precisely when \(B=0\), hence all G and GK original buyers renew while only half of R/K buyers do.
* The real inherited repair clears for everyone, \(C=1\). K training then consumes the final worker slot at atoms 0 and 4: \(M=0\) there and \(M=1\) elsewhere. This does **not** imply that no repair job was owed or that there is a recovery trace where clearance fails.
* Recovery-qualified \(T=1\) on atoms 1,2,5,6 and \(T=0\) otherwise. Thus \(T\le CM\). G qualification \(H=1\) on exactly one of \(\{0,3\}\) and one of \(\{4,7\}\); every G-only arm has the same joint \(B,H,D_G,W_G\) law. Every K-only arm has the identical \(B,M,T,D_K,W_K\) law, and **all** worlds have the identical paired technical law \(\Pr(H)=1/4,\Pr(T)=1/2,\Pr(HT)=0\).
* Early success probability \(1-p=1/2\), \(r_1=1,m_0=1,\Delta=4,c_G=c_K=1/4\) per failed ticket. Independently staffed E has \(D_E=1,m_E=4\) and *whole-program* cost \(C_E=5/8\), so \(J_E=15/8\). E costs are paid even if the first service already closed the ticket. All programs retain old policies subject to their precommitted resource use.

There are precisely four compatible deterministic common laws in this **specified restricted eight-atom family**. Their distinct \(H\) assignments and sharp supported values are

| H-qualified atoms | \(\theta=\Pr(H=1,T=0,M=1\mid F)\) | R | G | K | GK | E |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| {0,4} | 0 | 3/4 | 11/8 | 17/16 | **13/8** | 15/8 |
| {0,7} | 1/8 | 3/4 | 11/8 | 17/16 | 15/8 | 15/8 |
| {3,4} | 1/8 | 3/4 | 11/8 | 17/16 | 15/8 | 15/8 |
| {3,7} | 1/4 | 3/4 | 11/8 | 17/16 | **17/8** | 15/8 |

Every separate R/G/K *complete arm distribution* and the paired G/K technical qualification distribution are invariant. Only the unreported association of G success with K's **actual occupied worker slot** differs. K adds the same technical successes in every world. Thus the joint-program ranking reverses without a change in either individual technical score, source-pair technical overlap, buyer renewal law, separate arm's realized paid output or total worker-occupancy frequency.

For the stated source, buyer and recovery marginals,
\[
0\le \theta\le\tfrac14,\quad
J_{GK}=\tfrac{13}{8}+2\theta,\quad
J_E=\tfrac{15}{8}.
\tag{4}
\]
These bounds are sharp **within the defined common-law family**: the endpoint worlds attain both, and mixtures of these coherent types attain every intermediate moment. The E/GK switch is exactly \(\theta=1/8\). A precommitment to fractions \(\alpha\) GK and \(1-\alpha\) E of **independent otherwise comparable eligible clusters**, evaluated by maximum regret across these endpoint economies, has regrets \(\alpha/4\) and \((1-\alpha)/4\), respectively. Its conditional minimax-regret choice is \(\alpha=1/2\), worst regret 1/8. Maximin contribution chooses E. These allocations have no field decision authority.

**Proof of sharpness and observation equivalence.** The recorded R law is indexed by \(B\). The G law records \(B\) and exactly one \(H=1\) within each four-atom buyer group. K records \(B,M,T\) with losses at 0,4 and T at 1,2,5,6. Requiring the paired shadow technical joint event \(HT=0\) restricts H to non-T atoms 0 or 3 and 4 or 7. There are four allowed choices. The all-arm histograms are identical under each, yet exactly zero, one or two H successes lie in K-available non-T atoms 3 and 7; hence \(\theta\in\{0,1/8,1/4\}\). In (2) G pays a success on every H, while GK pays H only if M; the source plus worker occupancy difference gives (4). Mixing entire valid type laws makes the interval sharp without independently rearranging each program's potential outcomes. QED.

### Common-law partial identification and feasible control

More generally for a finite exogenous type set \(\mathcal U\), let each program's causal history, legal rights, original buyer budget, worker queue, learning lineage and accepted terminal service be a **deterministic or conditionally randomized map of the same U**. Let \(\pi_u\ge0\), \(\sum\pi_u=1\) be the common latent law. Actually supported, lawfully recorded treatment-conditional moments impose \(A\pi=b\) and physical/rights constraints impose \(F\pi\le g\). For a *fixed* precommitted program a, \(J_a=\kappa_a+v_a^\top\pi\). Thus the exact supported common-law identification interval is

\[
\left[\kappa_a+\min_{\pi\in\mathcal P}v_a^\top\pi,\quad
\kappa_a+\max_{\pi\in\mathcal P}v_a^\top\pi\right],\quad
\mathcal P=\{\pi\ge0:\mathbf1^\top\pi=1,A\pi=b,F\pi\le g\}.
\tag{5}
\]
The endpoints are attainable only if \(\mathcal P\) encodes **all** assumed common structural restrictions and is nonempty. A projection over arbitrary policywise marginals is generally an **outer**, not sharp, set: the same worker and buyer cannot take mutually incompatible potential histories in one type. Optimized adaptive choice requires information-feasible (nonanticipative) policy \(a(h)\), and the lower optimized value is \(\min_{\pi\in\mathcal P}\max_{a\in\mathcal A_{\rm law}}J_{a}(\pi)\), not a sum of separately optimized counterfactual receipts. Economic ranking is certified only if the *common feasible* payoff difference has a strict sign throughout \(\mathcal P\). A zero-support action cannot be point-identified by randomization performed only on R/G/K.

## Minimal lawful audit and empirical discrimination

In the restricted example, having the entire R/G/K arm law plus valid paired H/T checks leaves exactly one relevant residual statistic: \(\theta=\Pr(H=1,T=0,M=1\mid F)\). A **single joined, correctly denominated risk-set cross-tabulation** of licensed G complete-recipe qualification, K complete-recipe failure, and K-induced worker availability within original failed tickets identifies the comparison, *if* the cross-program shadow measurements are jointly lawful, physically realizable, consistently timed and G does not change M. Selected accepted-invoice samples, benchmark aggregate accuracy and G-only worker observations cannot supply it. In the less restrictive real model, program-specific original D, clearance C, training occupation M and complete qualified service must all be recorded jointly under authorized GK assignment; direct GK original-ticket paid output can identify whole-program value but not by itself separate transfer learning from customer/worker responses. Randomize at noninterfering original buyer/worker clusters when feasible, log no-orders and failed clears with the original denominator, and measure actual new recovery jobs and opportunity cost. Do not impute a K clip when a repair never clears.

This empirical design would discriminate (i) beneficial additional qualified recipes from K, (ii) loss of G-qualified paid capacity because K occupies the worker, (iii) demand induced by credible G deployment, and (iv) purely technical overlap that lacks an actual buyer. If same worker handles multiple customers, replace the single-job event with a conserved queue and log spillovers; if no such lawful support exists, retain only conditional bounds. Different labor skew, original order patterns, and ability to obtain rights across warehouse picking, replenishment, mobile-arm kits and software/physical workflows should yield different economically relevant joint moments.

The official [BEA input-output tables](https://www.bea.gov/itable/input-output) and [guide](https://www.bea.gov/resources/guide-interactive-industry-input-output-accounts-tables) can identify upstream/downstream industry exposure at documented aggregation levels. They do **not** estimate a robot's complete paid recipe or this source/worker coincidence. No new verified numerical BEA IO coefficients are claimed in this tranche.
