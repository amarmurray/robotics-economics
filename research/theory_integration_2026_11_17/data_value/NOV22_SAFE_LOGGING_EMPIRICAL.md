# November 22 empirical gate: safe policy support and original-order outcome closure

**Design only, 20 November 2026 UTC. No customer, robot, employer or private-provider data accessed; no outreach or trial authorization.** Accompanies [the integrated identification chapter](NOV22_SAFE_LOGGING_DATA_VALUE_IDENTIFICATION.md).

**Unit and denominator.** An original eligible customer order–shift–operator–procurement cluster must be enumerated *before* policy assignment or robot success filtering. Save a stable cluster ID and separately record offers, refusals, purchased and declined complete services, quoted price/quality/SLA, acceptance and rejection, credits, realized remittance, maintenance, human or specialist displacement, robot/teleoperator time, parent procurement cap, WMS/WES routing and shared operator queue. An accepted robot pick is not a completed paid original order. Contract rights and data-use restrictions are part of feasible policy membership.

**Physical permission comes first.** For R/X/G/E and bundles, version complete hardware, controller, grounding sensor, observed information partition, complete disturbance/safety transition support, recovery clock, operator allocation and lawful failover actions. Mark each *whole* policy: not evaluated / theoretical admissible under stated assumptions / independently signed physically admissible / approved for market experiment. Never infer hard transition closure from zero faults or high mean classifier accuracy, and never assign an unsigned X proposal to original customers to obtain positivity.

**Supported outcome inference.** For each original eligible cluster, capture preassignment Z and policy/menu exposure A, exact assignment probabilities by stratum, all complete-cluster economic outcomes regardless of robot acceptance, and spillovers across operators or customers. The analysis object is E[Y(a)] for each predeclared *complete policy*, not a task-level weighted benchmark. If sharing crosses cluster boundaries, enlarge the cluster to a closed exposure component or use a justified network interference design; naive independent-cluster inverse weighting is invalid.

**Learning input separation.** Record source lineage as field routine successful trajectories, field failures/recoveries, teleoperation, deliberate target tests, simulation, synthetic or public/pretrained inputs, distinct rights and curation/compute spend, policy-created exposure/recording propensities and matched held-out joint original-order qualification. Shadows or simulation can guide *hypotheses* about X or G but do not directly identify live captured receipt or absent hard failure support.

**Acceptance rule.** A Nov22 Model A comparison needs at least one independently closed original buyer–robot–operator cluster, physical admission or explicit exclusion of each candidate, complete outcome definition, and adequate supported arm/stratum comparison or reported partial-ID bounds. Model B additionally needs an identified or transparently hypothetical source-to-joint-qualification transition and endogenous data observation/rights plan. No row here is populated with field evidence. Official BLS employment/output slice is not a BEA interindustry flow; do not classify it as one.
