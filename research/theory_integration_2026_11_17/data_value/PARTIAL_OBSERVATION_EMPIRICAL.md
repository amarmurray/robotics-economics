# Partial observation, pathwise resources, and joint service: discriminating evidence design
**Prepared 18 November 2026 UTC; design only. No deployments, trials, outreach, privileged customer data or risk authorization.**

## Unit and counterfactual
The matched unit is a site × initial controller/embodiment × target customer-service bundle × order window. Compare approved production-generated R, controlled target diagnostic G, and target-safe changed-body E from the **same initially qualified incumbent**, as well as a qualified operator/specialist S. Keep calendar horizon, customer demand, available human recovery, safety review, compute/curation, rights, cash and site rules comparable. Conditioning only on episodes that generated a correction selects on the gate and exposure, so the design needs an *eligible exposure denominator* and a separate gate audit.

## Measurement matrix
| Object | Measurement and identification test | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source and target alignment regime H | Auditable proxy (domain shift/actual grasp-contact equivalence/validated target-body response), plus regime-labelled challenge set and confusion matrix of y conditional on H | H never observed means only partially identified mixtures, not automatically identified \pi(H|y) |
| Safe eligible-state opportunity | Pre-specified approved candidate set, state occupancy, unvisited states and non-correction exposures under each retained controller | Correction counts cannot reveal missing support or changes in gate recall |
| Human recovery load | **Pathwise peak**, total operator-minutes, multiple concurrent robot emergencies, delays, handoff availability, approved fallback, quantiles and failure branches | Means hide overload; a model of a 1% tail is not proof that a fallback will exist |
| Data inclusion and training | Provenance-separated field/recovery/teleoperation/simulation/synthetic/public sources, rights, model and intervention version hashes, sample selection, curation and compute | A simulated video or one assisted success is not automatically a licensed corrective training label or qualified real service |
| Joint B,C acceptance | One held-out end-to-end **customer** protocol recording 00/10/01/11, recovery, uptime, tooling, warranty/access and rejected work | Independent marginal skill scores leave the Fréchet interval open |
| Economics | Site/customer-specific demand, accepted quantity, operator support cost, lost current work, specialist/output alternative, f(Q;\omega) under exactly one continuation boundary | Neither a benchmark percentage nor a per-demo price equals accepted production value |

## Identification and ethical design
First identify *selection* by logging eligible states even when no correction arrives, and independently auditing gate misses. Second, in already approved operating envelopes only, compare report accuracy and conditional recovery-use distributions under matched initial control/configuration; measure the full joint observation and resource kernel, not just accuracy about an artificial H. Third, use held-out **end-to-end** joint customer acceptance after matched transfer or embodiment interventions. Randomized inclusion of already licensed data can help isolate training transfer if safe approvals and rights allow; do **not** withhold an urgent recovery or deliberately violate the operator cap.

For H-proxy classification, report confusion-matrix uncertainty and posterior set, not a point mass merely because a finite evaluation recorded no error. Conditional on incomplete H identification, estimate **identified intervals** for the joint B,C payoff rather than an invented scalar. If a recovery event is unobservable because the policy cannot enter its state, report missing support and assumptions for transport. No finite number of benign episodes can prove a structural zero probability or a perfect regime signal.

## Three pre-specified discriminating contrasts
1. **Information versus physical access:** A target diagnostic improves report accuracy but consumes scarce crew time, whereas an embodiment interface change enables a state safely at a different total cost. Estimate both action menus under *realized-path* resource caps; do not choose by accuracy alone.
2. **Joint accepted bundle versus marginals:** Holding p_B,p_C and starting hardware approximately matched, estimate 00/10/01/11 and the increment in optimized customer service. A high marginal score with anticorrelated failures can be economically inferior to a lower marginal score with compatible qualifications.
3. **Hard versus average constraints:** Audit worst supported recovery branches and scarce operator concurrency, plus the documented acceptance of any chance-risk threshold. Never silently convert an infeasible hard-budget route to an expected-cost feasible route.

## Source and atlas boundary
This theoretical experiment draws on established Blackwell, safety/POMDP and dependence mathematics, not on new customer-access evidence. Existing public case records can identify organizations, dates and types of work but cannot determine the required controlled transfer, gate recall, joint service or recoveries. The official quantitative BEA/BLS production backbone remains explicitly missing; a robotics press release or guessed network matrix is not an acceptable substitute.

**Next observation obligation:** independently collected conditional full-joint held-out acceptance under matched target policy, and approved recovery-cost *support* (or defensible statistical tail bounds) at the site. Without them a robust-policy rank cannot be interpreted as an operating recommendation.
