## Tests and identification

Primary conditional synthetic-model estimand is Gamma_P(D,U2,U3) under a properly supported P pilot, not Pr(D) times Pr(U). Actual policy choice at t1 is before D/U and cannot use future settlement. Test no-new-job assumption with forbidden free-then-busy pattern, explicit timestamped queue duration, source-candidate target qualification, and customer no-offer paths. Measure full-history action occupancy when t2 dispatch changes future jobs; do not infer it from logged-R runs. Reweight/censor only where exposure is real and legal; if long durations are censored by a shift boundary, report bounds from its survival information rather than assigning it short duration.

Negative controls: blocked broadcast rights must not cause cross-site qualification; safety-ineligible recipes cannot produce paid original-buyer service; unserved eligible demand must still appear in the source order panel; a purported independent E must not consume the P queue's worker or robot-time. Stratify by embodiment, target task, incident type, deadlines, sources and joint buyer path. If signed rights or physical intervention cannot be obtained, keep nonparametric bounds and the Nov22 science gate open. Nothing here is an observed original-buyer or robot deployment result.
