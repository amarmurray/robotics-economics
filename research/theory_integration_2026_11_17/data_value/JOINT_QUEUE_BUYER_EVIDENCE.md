# Prospective evidence for coupling validation, queue capacity and customer renewal

23 November 2026. **Unpopulated protocol, not authorization for a field trial, data access, purchasing or outreach.** See [the causal queue theory](JOINT_QUEUE_BUYER_FEEDBACK.md).

**Cluster and rights.** Define one eligible original customer ticket A after actual first-service failure, its still-valid authorization, inherited genuine recovery job and all workers/robots sharing the remediation and dispatch queue. Record the failure-origin trajectory G and distinct successfully completed recovery-origin trace K with their **separate** legal rights to observe, retain, validate, train on and redeploy. Record rejected/expired rights as exclusion; do not pool teleoperation, field failure/recovery, simulation, synthetic or public pretrained inputs. Other buyers' genuine orders require new unique ticket ids and distinct budgets. Do not infer order arrival from paid-invoice samples.

**Programs and source intervention.** Prospective eligibility for R, productive evidence P, G only, K only, GK and independently staffed qualified E must be established before any comparison. Log hardware, checkpoint version, source lineage, validated complete recipe, acceptance criteria, operator minutes, compute/curation/validation costs, and whether old checkpoints remain selectable. An untried regime has no effect estimate merely because another regime was logged.

**Event timeline.** First attempted A service and any unpaid failure; inherited recovery job, its completion and source generation; timestamp of G validation flag V; timestamp of a legally feasible K decision; K validation flag Z if performed; *separate* new safety/repair job ids, lengths, precedence and actual clearance; available slots at the true A acceptance deadline; A offer/no-offer, quality, accepted service and receipt; B's new authorized **offer including zero orders**, timing, worker capacity, acceptance and payment. Preserve no-invoice clusters, workers blocked by backlog and customer refusals.

**Discriminating assumptions.** Test whether each G/K remediation-job flag stays invariant when both interventions run. Test whether two genuinely available t3 worker slots exist, whether validation repairs obligatorily precede A, whether B order arrival causally depends on accepted A, and whether cancellation can occur after delays. An observational comparison of A-success versus A-failure cannot establish a causal renewal effect if customers select into acceptance. Test if E really has an independent qualified employee and hardware service channel. Keep source technical qualification distinct from accepted **paid original-customer production**.

**Minimum lawful support.** Separate G-only and K-only complete arm records estimate Pr(V) and Pr(Z), **not** Pr(VZ). A lawful, same-cluster paired safety/validation study could identify z only under defended intervention-invariance of each flag. Otherwise use whole-regime GK assignment (and any supported sequential K action conditional on an earlier observed V) and jointly log job creation, clearing, acceptance and later distinct B offers. Randomization, if authorized, is at the **shared queue and customer interference cluster**, not per overlapping worker. For a sequential policy identify K potential outcomes *within V strata* with real positivity, not a post-treatment invoice subset. If support is absent, retain set identification and a conditional robust decision, not a confident performance claim.

**Missing evidence.** No signed rights, prospective intervention support, original buyer paid/no-order panel, quantified actual queue, validated E staffing, causal renewal identification or official numerical BEA interindustry coefficients. Do not misstate the synthetic example as observed robotics returns.
