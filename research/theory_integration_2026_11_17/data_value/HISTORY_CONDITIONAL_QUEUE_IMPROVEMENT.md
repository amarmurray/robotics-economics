# History-conditional improvement in action-dependent multi-ticket queues

23 November 2026 — continuation of [variable-duration renewal](VARIABLE_DURATION_RENEWAL_AND_CUSTOMER_RESPONSE.md), within When Capabilities Become Production. Conditional theory, synthetic values, not measured returns or established novelty.

## General certificate

A finite common structural law theta generates all action-conditioned histories: legally observed typed source data, remaining worker jobs, original buyer orders and budgets, embodiment-specific full recipe qualification, rights and future source generation. Static qualified production v(Q,D,b) is the inner optimizer. J is expected discounted net accepted contribution; count all costs and any terminal value once. G denotes failure-origin target grounding, K recovery-origin learning, P extra production, R retained incumbent, E independently staffed new embodiment. Distinguish routine field, failures, recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained sources. K can alter the *remaining* G work, not already elapsed time. Free optional data preserve old policies if feasible; resource-consuming acquisition need not.

Fix lawful baseline b and candidate pi. For each history h let V^b_t be baseline continuation and Q^b_t(h,a)=E_theta[r_t+beta V^b_{t+1}(H_{t+1}) | h, do(a)], with A^b=Q^b-V^b. The exact performance difference is

    J_theta(pi)-J_theta(b) = sum_t beta^t E_{theta,pi}[A^b_t(H_t,A_t)].

Proof: substitute conditional Q and telescope expectations under pi; common initial law and common terminal valuation cancel. This requires a feasible baseline continuation even on candidate-reachable off-path histories; define a common admissible fallback there, not transported old outcomes. Occupancy is the candidate's, not baseline's. A sufficient robust certificate is nonnegative lower advantage at every candidate-reachable state and selected action. More precise bounds jointly constrain candidate occupancy and advantages under common theta.

For one K/skip decision after common G history, with fixed feasible subsequent policy in each branch,

    J_theta(q)=J_theta(b)+sum_h mu_theta(h) q(h) Delta_theta(h),
    Delta_theta(h)=E_theta[Y^K-Y^skip | H=h].

Y counts all later contribution including learning-generated value exactly once. q is a lawful history-measurable acquisition probability; mu is common predecision law, not invariant across arbitrary first-stage programs. A lower certificate for known mu is lower J(b)+sum mu q inf_Theta Delta(h). This follows termwise; it need not be sharp unless minima jointly attainable. Sharp bound is inf over common theta of full expression. Known law optimum is baseline plus sum mu positive-part Delta. Implementable robust policy maximizes over q before taking inf over laws. Missing treatment support requires skip unless lawful feasibility and independently justified structural bounds establish the contrast; a separate old K-only or R arm cannot supply it. Prospective assignment within eligible shared-worker/customer clusters and strata identifies Delta subject to consistency, sequential exchangeability, positivity and complete observation. The comparison is cluster-level, not independent robots competing for one worker.

## Multi-ticket witness

One worker, minute 1 consumed by common G preliminary diagnostic. Two original unpaid accepted-service tickets A and C each require one minute; A pays 3 by minute 4, C pays 2 by minute 5. H=0/1 observed preliminary diagnosis, equiprobable. G-only remaining job takes 1+H minutes, then A then C pay 5. K costs 1 incremental curation/compute and one validation minute; joint program changes G's remaining work to zero in good latent states or two minutes in bad ones. Assume safety validation must finish before dispatch; abort would require a distinct supported action. Joint G/K legally usable recovery data qualify a B service on another embodiment, not under G or K alone. B is a genuinely new separately authorized order paying 3 by minute 6 after A accepted by minute 3 in good states; no B receipt otherwise.

Good: ready at 2, A at 3, C at 4, B at 5, net 7. Bad: ready at 4, A deadline missed, C at 5, net 1. All feasible ticket orders can be enumerated; no double worker allocation or invented renewal. Retained old checkpoints still consume scarce capacity. R/P retain only A (3); K-only A less 1 (2); comparator E net 11/2 is an assumed independently supported contribution, not an inferred buyer price. No terminal data premium in the witness.

All laws share isolated G/K histories, paired technical qualification profiles and the unconditional joint *outcome* histogram (half good, half bad; not the histogram linked to H). Let p=Pr(good|H=0), Pr(good|H=1)=1-p. Common latent U uniform [0,1] with these thresholds implements each law; no action changes H. Thus J(G)=5, J(GK)=4, conditional gains Delta0=6p-4, Delta1=2-6p. Select K only at H=0 gives 3+3p, only at H=1 gives 6-3p. Known-law optimum:

    V(p)=5+([6p-4]_+ + [2-6p]_+)/2.

V>E iff p<1/6 or p>5/6. At p=0,1 V=6 but opposite actions needed. At p=1/2 V=5. Even full unconditional joint paid outcomes do not identify adaptive acquisition. For any randomized (q0,q1), the endpoint gain is min(-2q0+q1,q0-2q1)<=-(q0+q1)/2<=0; skip achieves maximin 5. Even restricting to endpoint worlds, oracle inf V=6 while implementable robust value stays 5. A lawful audit p>=11/12 certifies fixed short-only K >=23/4>E, without point identifying p. One conditional joint outcome mean plus whole-arm mean and known two-stratum weights determines the other contrast, not the other action's legal feasibility.

The result is about history linkage, not simply source correlation. It requires matched joint remaining-work, paid production and buyer renewal after the common report. Removing B authorization makes good K worth 4, so no selective K beats 5; free routine P yielding the same qualifications and demand without K resource cost could reverse the comparison. E loses its stipulated value if its staff actually share the bottleneck. The certificate generalizes to successive acquisition only using candidate-policy occupancy of new eligible reports and jointly bounded transitions; baseline occupancy misses K-induced histories. This is the next proof gap.
