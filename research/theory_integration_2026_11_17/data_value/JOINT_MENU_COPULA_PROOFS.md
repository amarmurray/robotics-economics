# Proof ledger: demand coupling versus qualified service value

**All conditional standard results, 19 Nov 2026.** No independence assumed for observed buyers, but strong service/contract feasibility required.

1. **Sharp one-slot Fréchet envelope.** For c_1≥⋯≥c_n≥0, W=max_i c_i A_i. Write c_(n+1)=0. If highest accepted index is j, Σ_k(c_k−c_(k+1))1{any A_1,...,A_k}=Σ_(k=j)^n(c_k−c_(k+1))=c_j=W; zero if none. Union probability is at least max_(i≤k)p_i and at most min(1,Σ_(i≤k)p_i). Nonnegative coefficient gaps yield L≤E[W]≤U. Nested A_i={U<p_i} simultaneously attain all lower prefix probabilities. The arcs A_i=([Σ_(j<i)p_j,Σ_(j≤i)p_j) modulo1) simultaneously attain upper prefix probabilities, since union of consecutive arcs before wrap occupies min(1,total length), and afterwards whole circle. Sharpness assumes no other joint-law restrictions. Not a novel theorem.

2. **Two buyer margin and investment sign.** With p_A=p_B=1/2 and t=Pr(A∩B), Fréchet gives t∈[0,1/2]. If c_A≥c_B, one-slot ex-post priority yields E W_X=c_A p_A+c_B(p_B−t). Two safe G slots yield g_A p_A+g_B p_B without independence. In general J_X−J_G=(c_A−g_A)p_A+(c_B−g_B)p_B−c_B t−K_X+K_G, conditional on **the same** joint buyer demand across policies. In the synthetic (6,4)/(5,5), K_X0,K_G1, difference1−4t. A nested demand coupling yields X3, a disjoint coupling yields X5, while G4. E2 from two slots with gross5−K_E3, R2 separately qualified. A different menu or customer quality invalidates shared p,t and requires a new joint history.

3. **Information option.** A known ex-ante prior 1/2 on t0 and t1/2 gives E J_X=4=J_G and J_E=J_R=2. Perfect prechoice t signal yields (max{5,4,2,2}+max{3,4,2,2})/2=4.5; gross EVSI=0.5. A signal with identical distribution across both regimes, including exact unpaired p measurements, leaves the posterior unchanged and EVSI0. Under genuinely optional free information and retained feasible policies, the decision-maker may ignore it, proving nonnegative EVSI; acquisition and implementation costs are separate. No field report is represented.

4. **Finite response-type LP, and scope.** For policy/menu p,m and complete response θ, V_p(m,θ) is the full feasible net-of-operating-cost complete-service value; upfront K_p deducted once. If Π={π≥0:1'π=1,Hπ∈[l,u]} is a supported feasible joint-law polytope, minimizing Σ_θ π_θ V_p(m,θ) is an LP. Choosing menu before observation gives max_m min_π, not min_π max_m. All policy potential outcomes must coexist in the **same** θ when proving paired comparisons. An incumbent-only quote distribution cannot bound future menu-specific p_i if new service qualities/rivals unrestricted. With unbounded eligible price/captured payment and no demand cap, even one physically fixed robotic slot can produce unbounded hypothetical revenue. A guaranteed floor additionally needs legal right to reject unselected buyers, no unmodeled liability and a reserved safe slot.

5. **Computational audit is not proof.** The [self-contained executable](joint_menu_copula_audit.js) uses integer population ticks, exhaustively enumerates 6,435 joint eight-atom laws for three sorted contributions (6,4,2) with total mass 8, aggregates all 729 attainable marginal vectors, and exactly checks observed extrema against the formula. Finite tick verification cannot establish the continuous result; the event constructions above do.
