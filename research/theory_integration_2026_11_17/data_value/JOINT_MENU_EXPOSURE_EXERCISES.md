# Exercises — paired original-order qualification and source-data choice
**Student version; no solutions inline**

1. An original eligible H population has conditional (C0,Q0) cell masses (00,01,10,11)=(3/10,1/10,2/10,4/10). A legal post-grounding regime may change original parent second-service choice C on at most 1/5 of these cases and joint safe qualification Q on at most 1/5. Derive sharp upper/lower bounds on P(C1 AND Q1|H), allowing arbitrary dependence of the changes. Explain why t+u_C+u_Q is not sharp in general.

2. With P(H)=1/2, price menus 6 and 4, a qualified first order for X and G, G cost 1/2 and no other constraints, bound net G. Is G preferable to X=3 in every admissible world? Compute what changes if baseline conditional (00,01,10,11)=(0,1/10,0,9/10) and each switch budget is 1/20. Keep R as an actual verified outside production option, not an automatically assumed fallback.

3. A randomized intervention identifies P(C1=1)=P(C0=1)=1/2. Does this imply u_C=0 or even u_C<=1/10? Construct a counterexample with a common original population and discuss what additional assumptions identify a cross-policy flip budget.

4. State why a grounded controller that passes a held-out technical benchmark but has no independently authorized two-order safe dispatch cannot create a credible Q=1 observation. Explain how selection of only purchased customers biases joint success evidence, and specify a minimally feasible future approved observational unit.
