# Prospective evidence and lawful data-route audit — no field outcomes

**21–22 November 2026.** This is an admissibility/identification specification, not authorization for robotics experimentation, private customer data collection or outreach.

**Target:** on the *same original eligible buyer population*, the whole-program net finally-paid contribution difference between source-plus-target-grounding SG and newly acquired embodiment E, with optional retained old controller R, pooled learner/operator/rights network and demand feedback preserved. The one-step menu–information result is a testable structural restriction, **not** the whole estimand when data production changes future customers or actions.

**Record per offered buyer service, including no-order and unserved/declined orders:** order type, price/value and observation timing, offered service recipe, qualified recipe/robot/embodiment/checkpoint, source provenance (F field success and recovery, T teleoperation, M simulation/synthetic, public/pretrained separately), licensed retention/reuse/deletion/expiry, permitted current and future actions, shared robot and operator slot, attempted dispatch and recovery, service acceptance, refunds, **finally cleared receipt**, future linked newly qualified customer orders and settled payment. Keep cost of deliberate acquisition, ongoing curation/training/teleoperation and opportunity capacity, without an additional data option-premium line.

**Decision-refinement witness:** for two histories with the same candidate summary state s but distinct buyer orders or licensed checkpoint/rights state, first verify legal action-menu equality. If not equal, split. If equal, compare action-conditional whole paid contribution and transition to separately supported next history cells. For a specific constrained one-step policy compute the Jensen regret gap L(s) only where every required action is actually supported; otherwise label the missing counterfactual **not identified**. For a full-horizon quotient, demand uniform action-by-action conditions over *all physically and legally reachable histories*, not an average zero error along R logging.

**Discriminating paired comparisons:** measure source menu growth and *qualified*, not benchmark-only, new recipes; compare source gains without versus with target demand report; evaluate source plus grounding against E with identical original eligible demand population and common resource accounting. Check whether source learning changes demand or outcomes (breaking fixed-p/fixed-w assumptions), whether new embodiments alter menus, whether data rights expire, and whether multiple customers or sites share learner updates/queues. Free optional observations can be ignored; expensive forced learning or processing can lower net return.

**Policy selection:** if approved reports are missing or unsafe, the sign of source–information interaction and the SG/E economic ranking remain unknown. A supported complete old-policy floor can be combined with an independently defensible complete *future*-service Bellman/resource upper price; never use a static first-period capacity ceiling when successful production generates new allowed services. Keep joint paid episodes and rights events rather than independent marginals. Use matched original-buyer demand and exposure clusters, not a benchmark improvement multiplied by an invented average wage or dollar coefficient.

**Bounded official source check, November 21 UTC:** [BEA Input–Output Interactive Tables](https://www.bea.gov/itable/input-output), [official BEA industry account descriptions](https://www.bea.gov/data/industries/input-output-accounts-data) and [BEA API](https://apps.bea.gov/api/) are genuine U.S. primary-source routes for make/use and requirements data. Official [API instructions](https://apps.bea.gov/API/docs/) say a registered UserID is needed for requests; no key was used, table was downloaded, or quantitative BEA/BLS industry pair was extracted during this tranche. Thus **no numerical production atlas coefficient** is asserted. Do not substitute vendor announcements or synthetic menus for official production relationships.
