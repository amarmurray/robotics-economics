## Observational unit and denominator

Predeclare an **original eligible-buyer-service-opportunity network** for each time window. Retain orders and no-orders, offered menu, buyer demand response, price, accepted/refunded service and actually cleared settlement. Do not condition only on successful customer orders or completed robot tasks. Record service timing, throughput and cancellations, since data acquisition can displace economic capacity. For each original buyer follow subsequent demanded and paid services induced by the program, including learning-generated qualification; do not count continuation again as a separate data asset.

## Testable nulls versus alternatives

**Null A (policy sufficiency):** for every history reached under an admissible report-blind policy, its action is full-Q optimal. Falsify by a reachable licensed buyer report at which a different approved controller has strictly greater fully paid continuation. A report prediction improvement without a paid-Q action gap does not falsify this null.

**Null B (transport of occupancy):** the report-blind policy induces the same relevant buyer/learner/operator/rights history distribution as the matched permitted logging/reference support. Reject if source training changes operator bottlenecks, rights compliance, failure recovery, future order supply or qualification in the omitted strata. Only then may a measured occupancy-weighted regret be transported.

**Null C (investment sign identified):** coarsened SG-E plus validated program-specific regret bounds excludes zero. Failure to exclude zero implies partial identification, not a tie or E win. A paired *one-factor* learner restriction may tighten ranges only after paid response is verified on the original cohort.

## Practical admissibility decision tree

1. If a physical action lacks certification/rights, exclude it from the lawful comparison. No unsafe exploration for a statistic.
2. If the aliased histories have no common legal controller (possibly a certified fallback), refine the state. No fictitious abstract policy.
3. If the common legal controller is demonstrably optimal along the policy's entire reached horizon, its coarsening regret is zero even if unvisited histories differ.
4. Otherwise form original-buyer full-Q intervals and a **policy-specific** conditional occupancy envelope; apply the telescoping upper certificate. Missing downstream learning-generated services, joint rights or market-induced demand make the certificate invalid, not zero.
5. Report SG/E only as an identified interval unless the full-program lower or upper sign is strict. Leave the November22 gate OPEN absent empirical support.
