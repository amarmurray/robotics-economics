# Exercises — lawful support-zero economic transfer

1. Under the observed buyer table (reported Y=1 mass 1/4, reported Y=0 mass 1/4, missing 1/2), prove that p is in [1/4,3/4]. Explain why calculating p=1/2 from the observed conditional sample requires an extra assumption.
2. For P=p−1/8 and G=max(p/2−1/16,θ−3/32) with 0≤θ≤p, prove the sharp G−P interval and construct the extremizing physical histories. Compare the worst-case deterministic investment with E=9/20.
3. Derive the exact two-LP representation of the difference between two optimized finite menus under one coupled law. Why is optimizing each arm against a different adversarial law generally too pessimistic?
4. Suppose a complete lawful Y audit yields p=3/4. Find the robust investment. Which dimension remains unidentified with e_T=0, and why cannot another old-action buyer survey eliminate it?
5. For missing binary payoff weights (6,4,3,1)/16, forbidden audit of entry 0, budget two and unit costs for other entries, identify a minimum-width audit. Explain when a shared queue invalidates this knapsack reduction.
