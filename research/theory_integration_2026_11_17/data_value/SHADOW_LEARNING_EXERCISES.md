# Learning exercises — shadow production data and customer qualification

Student version. Work from the same service-value environment as When Capabilities Become Production; assume any stated candidate is physically approved solely within the hypothetical.

1. **Prove the pre-admission invariant.** State the B-side causal closure required to copy field trajectories to a separate A training job without changing retained B accepted orders. Explain why pinned weights alone cannot protect B if there is a shared operator queue.
2. **Data availability versus forced training.** Prove that an extra free optional training file cannot lower optimized gross production value when old policies remain. Construct an example where forced training costing 1/4 lowers net outcome.
3. **Partial identification.** The low-demand stratum has probability 3/5 and qualified A mean contribution 1/2. The remaining 2/5 has unidentified A contribution between 0 and 2. Compute the gross and cost-1/4 net range. When does A beat a net-2/5 specialist?
4. **Joint qualification.** Two required competencies have marginal qualification probabilities 1/2 and 3/4. Find the sharp joint range without independence. With two eligible service periods and 1/4 in total cost, compute net contribution range and the independent special case.
5. **Demand window.** Re-do exercise 4 when a two-period validation delay exhausts all prospective service dates. Explain optional old-policy versus sunk investment accounting.
6. **Empirical design.** Identify a counterexample to valuing a read-only dataset from offline benchmark scores alone. Specify the minimum evidence that would identify accepted service for an unsupported target action, without treating mean recovery as physical safety.
