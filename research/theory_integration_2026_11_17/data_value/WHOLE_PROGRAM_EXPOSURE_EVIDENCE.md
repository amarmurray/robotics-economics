# Prospective whole-program exposure and paid-service audit

**21 November 2026. Protocol, not executed physical research.** This extends the existing SG/E finite buyer-paid evidence design rather than introducing a new program.

**Preassignment causal graph:** Enumerate full-horizon causal parents in three typed layers. **Learning:** all robot/fleet weights, version hashes, checkpoints, replay, rights and admitted source episode genealogy (routine field trajectories, failures/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public). **Operations:** shared operators, recovery queues, backup policies, embodiment, manufacturing, access, verification and physical safety policies. **Market:** original eligible buyers including nonbuyers, procurement/owner organizations, jointly required services, displaced orders, contract prices, cancellation/refund and settlement. Draw a directed arrow if intervening on a parent's SG/E assignment can change a child's whole-horizon paid outcome through any permitted data/update/physical or buyer path; compute transitive ancestor sets `H_i` *before* treatment. Shared policy updates may make every buyer belong to one exposure component.

**Authorized comparator:** Establish independent legal/physical SG and E policies and complete service recipes; do not infer E approval from manufacturer benchmarks. Preserve feasible old R controllers and signed outside orders as genuine alternatives. Define `Y_i` by a frozen original-buyer accounting rule, not only successful robot attempts. Allocate shared operator displacements and refunded customer transactions exactly once. Include time-zero and production-generated future information only in actual subsequent qualified paid output, not as an auxiliary price for data.

**Measurement:** Predeclare p, baseline customer parent/risk set, signed hardware/robot/controller recipe, data admission/retention terms and censoring; observe offered, declined, dispatched, rejected, recovered, accepted, refunded and paid orders with time, prices, operator minutes and model version. Validate `H_i` by intentional *permitted* shadow/witness probes and legal causal lineage, not by finding only visible nearby physical sites. Where cross-learner updates or buyer substitution make `H_i` unbounded, call the test globally coupled and prefer closed whole-network variation if lawful and operationally realistic. Do not use a post-treatment graph as a fixed randomization proof.

**Analysis:** If admissible independent Bernoulli assignments and full-horizon exposure sufficiency are supported, estimate all-policy SG/E original-buyer contribution by inverse exposure weights, subtract only costs outside the measured contribution and report exact assignment probabilities and effective exposed counts. Report graph size `d`, overlap `M`, per-unit signed payoff bounds and a randomization-based uncertainty interval; conservative Chebyshev may be vacuous. Handle continuous contracts and negative refunds with precommitted outcome shifts and finite ranges. Do not use the previously claimed independent-cluster Hoeffding interval on dependent site-level assignments. Where assumptions fail, no full-market ranking is certified.

**Falsifiers:** One unobserved global data aggregation/retraining edge; a single cross-arm operator queue not in `H_i`; unregistered original buyer displacement; market price feedback beyond the declared contract closure; simulated/teleop domain shift; adaptive policy change during a 'frozen' experiment; no approval or positivity for E; noncontractible data-retention rights; missing/late settlement tails. These may invalidate identification, not merely increase error bars.

**Open data backbone:** Preserve typed customer→integrator/operator→robot maker→compute/data and manufacturer supply edges and the original BEA/BLS sector-vintage crosswalk request. No numerical official input-output coefficient obtained; vendor announcements are not a replacement.
