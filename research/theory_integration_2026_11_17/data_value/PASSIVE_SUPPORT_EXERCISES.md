# Student exercises — passive learning, active target support and embodiment choices

**For the November 22 theory synthesis.** All examples below use synthetic normalized *captured qualified customer contribution*, not benchmark-to-dollar conversion. Keep your working separate from the [instructor solutions](PASSIVE_SUPPORT_SOLUTIONS.md).

1. **Indistinguishable F data.** Two persistent regimes generate exactly the same distribution of all retained-policy source records. Approved A minus approved S pays -L and +H in the respective regimes, L,H>0. A firm may randomize before admission. Prove that any report-conditioned policy can be replaced by one unconditional probability p of choosing A without changing either regime's expected payoff. Derive the minimax regret and explain why this proof does not establish zero learning benefit from data.
2. **Reuse the previous chapter, don't invent a new calibration.** With V_A(x)=1/20+(2/5)x, x in [0,2], and V_S=2/5, derive the A/S threshold, optimal passive admission probability and worst-case regret over the *entire interval*. Explain why component benchmark success rates do not identify x.
3. **A noisy but safely authorized report.** Assume just the endpoint worlds of Exercise 2. A diagnostic has P(+|A bad)=1/5 and P(+|A good)=4/5. Let a=P(A|+) and b=P(A|-). Derive both regret functions. Find a minimizing rule using an adversarial mixture/dual argument. Compare the deterministic rule that chooses A only for +. Compute the maximum fixed-horizon diagnostic plus opportunity cost that can be justified under the stated minimax-regret criterion.
4. **Learning versus information.** Suppose free, optional F data do not change the report likelihood at all but an independently validated training transition raises A's joint qualified customer contribution by delta in both regimes. At what delta does A weakly dominate S? Give two real-robot reasons this cannot be inferred simply from having more source trajectories.
5. **Embodiment as a change of required support.** An old A body requires joint action/recovery events {u,v}; only u has a validated source-to-target support bridge. A proposed h' body replaces v with event w. Distinguish the cases (i) w independently tested with a complete customer recipe; (ii) w only seen in simulation; (iii) w is absent from all records. Under which conditions can the new body resolve the missing-evidence issue? Why does none of these observations itself authorize field deployment?
6. **Accounting and interference.** A proposed experiment copies B data, retrains A, measures A target success, and after deployment changes both the B order queue and new customer demand. Critique an analysis that adds “value of the data” after discounting new A sales, uses the diagnostic regret improvement as additional cash, applies one-robot treatment effects to shared customers, and calls expected operator demand a physical safety approval. Specify a viable matched evidence record.
