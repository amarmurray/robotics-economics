# Exercises — stochastic original buyers and the value of qualified data
**20 November 2026 UTC | student work only**

1. With actual complete-service order count N_h conditional on an announced capacity h, prove the exact first-to-second-slot tail identity. Specify the condition under which it equals r Pr(N>=2)-ΔK, and construct one reason this collapses under menu-dependent customer choice.
2. A common procurement parent has budget8, first high-state valuation7 and second valuation either3 or5; high state probability1/2, otherwise no orders. Check utility maximization at prices4,5,6. Compute optimized X (one safe slot), G (two qualified slots, cost1/2), E (independently safe second body, cost2), and R (2) in both worlds.
3. With q=1/2, conditional probability a of a true payable second price4 purchase and two guaranteed G slots, find the exact strict crossing a for G to beat X. Why is an untested 'willingness to pay' survey not enough?
4. Let C be an original payable second service with probability2/5, Q safely qualifies G's second concurrent service with probability1/2, q=1/2 and G cost1/2. Find sharp support for s=P(C∧Q). Compute G/X/E/R for s=0 and s=2/5. State the causal/observational unit necessary to measure s.
5. Prove or disprove universal diminishing returns from successive operator slots under heterogeneous contracted products and a shared parent budget. Explain why assigning data a premium in addition to the already optimized Bellman value double-counts the same information.
