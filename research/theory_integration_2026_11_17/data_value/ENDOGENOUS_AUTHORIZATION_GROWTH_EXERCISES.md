# Exercises — endogenous opportunity lineage

1. Derive the stock-potential inequality when two parent types are jointly consumed to create a single distinct successor and a worker constraint binds. Why is adding the two independently measured birth counts invalid?
2. Under the batch model with c=1/3, compute the broad typed and sharp batch upper, then add q_AB=0. Which bound relies on actual batch structure?
3. Show that identical forced-AB histories cannot identify the G/E decision using a common latent uniform variable. Derive the threshold for the fixed A rule.
4. For beta=1/2 and M columns A→2B, B→1A, with unit receipts, find w. If beta rises to 0.9, does failure of the resolvent imply infinite attainable value when at most two unit services occur per date?
5. Explain why a licensed free routine input can change the choice even when a costly grounding action loses, and why a shared-worker E must not be priced as independent.
