# Prospective joint buyer/operator audit — design only
19 Nov 2026. No physical trials, purchases, customer/private access, consent or outreach.

The [nonseparable demand chapter](NONSEPARABLE_DEMAND_OPERATOR_GROUNDING.md) demonstrates that knowing both offered service classes' current accepted prices and volumes at one mix is insufficient to rank X source training, G operator-saving alignment or E separately qualified embodied/recovery capacity. An actual study must register **original eligible buyers, not only accepted robot jobs** and must observe substitution between classes A and B, competitor specialists and nonpurchase.

Before any allowed controlled study, independently approve the policy-closed network: shared controller/training updates; sites sharing operators/recovery queues, robots and mechanical maintenance; all customers who can switch among offered service classes/providers; rights to quotes, data and trained policies. A complete original opportunity record needs account/time, eligible set, outside options, simultaneous order classes, both posted/negotiated quotes and refusals, delays/cancellation, required complete-service recipe, actual qualified success, operator intervention minutes, payments collected, discount/penalties, relevant costs and controller/data lineage. Nonorders and unsuccessful attempts must retain their denominators; simply observing post-training closed deals conditions on endogenous success.

For γ identification under a **hypothesized stable inverse-demand system**, preauthorized exogenous joint price/menu variation across the two service classes (or credible experimental instruments with independent demand accounting) is required to recover own/cross customer response at eligible opportunities. Differentiating P_A in q_B at fixed q_A is a theoretical identity, **not** an immediately feasible randomized intervention or proof that matched accepted tickets identify the cross derivative. Compare the same customer/time/horizon under physically qualified menus, with actual controlled quote opportunities and rival choices. Test policy-specific γ after X/G/E: training may change quality, product differentiation, available customer classes or bargaining power, invalidating structural transport.

For λq_A grounding evidence, record controller-versioned recovery time and true busy recovery/dispatch resources, including failures and recovery tails for **all offered** opportunities. Randomize only independently approved G/no-G interventions and verify that the released resource yields other complete accepted service rather than merely an idle minute; measure actual value and operator shadow constraints. For embodiment E, certify new body's whole-service and shared recovery feasibility; a new robot alone may not create any additional qualified operator-equivalent O. One engineering qualification slot and all costs are synthetic decision constraints, not deployment facts.

Randomized X/G robust hedging is **not approved by this theoretical result**: validate the common underlying γ and absence of cross-arm controller/operator/customer spillovers before using the expected-lottery bound. In a single market without approved randomization, the pure maximin retained policy R remains the conditional reference. A stochastic expected value does not certify every realized customer a retained complete-service option.

The discriminating estimands remain complete market policy J_R,J_X,J_G,J_E,J_GE and (if permitted) combinations under the same future customer opportunity and operator network; also separately record joint accepted two-step bundle qualification q_A=q_B. Report off-support uncertainty bands, spillover/closure failures, version-pinning and paid rights/curation costs. Do not infer economically useful learning from benchmark pass rates or automatically value every teleop, field, failure, simulation, synthetic or public input alike. When the proposed customer and resource ceilings are not validated, the honest X/G/E investment ranking remains unidentified.
