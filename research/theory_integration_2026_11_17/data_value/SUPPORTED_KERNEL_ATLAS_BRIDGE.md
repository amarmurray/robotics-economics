**Common prospective observation schema.** Version offered and declined jobs, remaining buyer budgets, qualified complete service, actual payments and customer operating surplus separately, paid labor and fallback, worker blocked intervals, model and embodiment, source modality and rights, joint pass/fail on customer recipe, and next evidence-source arrivals. Compare within matched demand/worker/compute cohorts and report interference across model-sharing pools. The synthetic G/T kit is a *test harness* for this accounting, not a claim that any particular market uses its 20-atom distribution.

**Official national production backbone.** BEA's Input-Output Accounts describe interindustry supply/use and requirements tables; the official guide distinguishes supply, use and requirements and details annual 71-industry and benchmark more-detailed classifications.
