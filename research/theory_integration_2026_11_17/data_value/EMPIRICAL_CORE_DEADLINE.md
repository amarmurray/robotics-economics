## What can be measured at the economically relevant endpoint?

Gervasoni et al., Clinical Chemistry and Laboratory Medicine 64(4), 832–837, online December 10 2025, print March 2026, DOI https://doi.org/10.1515/cclm-2025-1336, publisher abstract and PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41364097/ inspected November 24. The single-laboratory historical comparison follows glucose specimens during weekdays 08:00–16:00, May 13–23 2025, versus the equivalent 2024 period. Its clock runs from accession to technical validation in the laboratory system, a **downstream completed analytical stage**, rather than just courier arrival. Reported samples: 6299 manual-era and 5759 robot-era; mean clock 122.64 vs 106.72 minutes; medians 112 vs 101 minutes. The mean difference is 15.92 minutes (12.98% relative to manual). The authors describe reduced spread and fewer delays. Only the abstract was accessible; exact empirical CDF, original accession population including all censored samples, downstream recipient deadlines, manual/robot clock allocation, cost, training lineage and assignment are not established by that reading. Calendar-year comparison is not an independently randomized data program. A 120-minute deadline below is illustrative, **not** an asserted clinical standard.

Kirschling, Rough and Ludwig (2009), AJHP 66(19) 1754–1762, DOI https://doi.org/10.2146/ajhp080184, PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19767383/ abstract accessed Nov 24. The reported 125-day courier pilot used about half of 1750 possible runs; 79 dispatched runs failed, reported conditional success 91%, and satisfaction declined after introduction; the deployment did not extend beyond two pilot units. If the 50% is rounded to the nearest whole percent, dispatched count 867–883 and completed count 788–804 yield original possible-run coverage 45.03–45.94%; actual denominator not recovered. These are potential scheduled runs, not proven original patient orders. This older negative case is retained to guard against equating improved robot reliability with economically adopted service. It is not pooled with the 2026 studies.

A 2021 Australian pharmacy time-motion study, https://doi.org/10.1002/jppr.1708, publisher abstract inspected Nov 24, compares five-day periods at implementation and 15 months later. Orders increased from 774 to 887 with unchanged staffing; staff movement decreased while dispensing rates and turnaround time did not change significantly. Only 46% of packs were robot-stored. This is a different dispensing technology and pre/post design; no causal labor saving, paid incremental production, or data acquisition effect follows. Useful negative evidence against automatic wage savings and against treating the terminal robot clock as a universal performance measure.

## A minimal conditional deadline certificate (finite support; not a field calibration)

Let T be nonnegative elapsed time to actual accepted terminal service for a fixed cohort, with support [0,B], mean mu, median m in the weak sense P(T<=m)>=1/2 and P(T>=m)>=1/2. Let m<t<B and p=P(T<=t), an economically relevant deadline CDF. Necessarily

    max(1/2,(t-m/2-mu)/(t-m)) <= p <= min(1,(B+(m-t)/2-mu)/(B-t)).

Proof: median and t>m imply p>=1/2. To minimize mean at fixed p, put at most half below m, then p-1/2 at m and remaining 1-p just above t. Formally at least p-1/2 timely mass has time >=m since at least 1/2 of the entire cohort is >=m and all late mass is >=m. Thus mu >= (p-1/2)m+(1-p)t. To maximize mean, put half at or below m, remaining timely p-1/2 at t and late mass at B, so mu <= m/2+(p-1/2)t+(1-p)B. Rearrangement gives the stated inequalities. Extremal constructions with atoms at 0,m,t+epsilon and m,t,B, respectively, and convex mixtures, prove sharpness in closure for interior feasible moments; the lower extreme with positive late mass is generally an infimum not attained because late is strictly above t. No assumption of continuous density, independence, or exponential tails. With no defensible finite B the upper bound from B is unavailable (upper 1). With t below or equal to m use a different partition; do not extrapolate this formula.

Illustrate only the information in the reported means/medians. Under hypothetical common cap 200 min and illustrative t=120 min, manual p in [0.5,0.917] and robot p in [0.5,1], giving comparative outer bounds [-0.417,0.5]. Neither cap nor the common cohort assumption is verified in the study. A useful direction-of-effect claim would require actual deadline counts, not the assumed cap. Even knowing reduced variance does not fix the sign: probability laws with weights /100 and support within 200 minutes:

They are counterexamples conditional **only** on those numeric summaries and the direction of variance, not purported fits to the authors' complete distributions or proof their reported qualitative tail claim is false. Under equal fixed positive marginal timely-service contribution, equal other costs and no downstream interference, the ranking of forced transport programs reverses. No dollar value is assigned to a minute of clinical time.

## From a terminal-stage cohort to a whole-program estimand

If a measured terminal-stage cohort represents fraction r of all original orders and its deadline fraction lies [L,U], original-order timely rate lies [rL,rU+1-r] when unresolved records can be either timely or late; if every unresolved record is independently known not timely, the interval tightens to [rL,rU]. Proof is the law of total probability and range [0,1] on the unobserved stratum. These endpoints are sharp with no linking restrictions. r must come from the original order register, not a ratio between different calendar periods. Count safely executed manual fallback, approved retry and already accepted on-time service once. No automatic penalization of manual delivery merely because a robot did not perform the task.

For fixed, independently followed, approved program a in R,P,X,G,E and a cluster c, define J_ac as discounted net captured value from the complete original-order and induced-authorization stream minus actual incremental labor, equipment, curation, compute and training costs. If the observation horizon stops before the economic horizon, add one terminal continuation value, or extend the horizon, not both. Denote the supported observed subtotal by O_ac; if the unobserved residual has a justified uniform interval [l_ac,u_ac], the randomized fixed-arm mean comparison has conditional bound

    E[O_G-O_P]+E[l_G-u_P] <= E[J_G-J_P] <= E[O_G-O_P]+E[u_G-l_P],

where each expectation uses its assigned arm population. This is an accounting/partial-identification certificate, not an estimate of the optimized adaptive-policy difference. Complete-arm randomization identifies mean program returns with interference handled at the learning/worker/order catchment, even without individual cross-world pairing; common-checkpoint subexperiments diagnose local mechanisms but do not identify complete-program effects. If original demand changes, keep induced/newly authorized orders separately rather than condition away that channel. No wage-times-task-time shortcut: observed downstream contributions and realized payroll changes only, without double counting. Lower prices for purchasers need not increase provider capture. Unknown residual value without a defensible bound leaves the decision unresolved.

## Compact first-paper evidence map and prospective discriminator

- Source composition: Robin fixed-size data replacement supports target coverage as a technical object; VLAC-Cut common-checkpoint and LWD method comparisons show that training lineage and independently evolving exposure matter. CEL clarifies active robot hours versus fault availability. None prices an accepted service.
- Physical qualification: Vulcan attempt versus original assignment, stage-score bounds, motor-cell QC and hospital eligibility/escort evidence establish distinct denominators; prospective dispatch requires reports actually available when chosen.
- Downstream economics: the specimen study measures a later accepted technical stage, with different annual cohorts; the older courier pilot shows adoption and recipient satisfaction can lag robot success. Pharmacy time-motion observations and costed hospital models are not controlled data-source returns.
- Missing matched contrast: common baseline at independent learning/worker/recipient catchments; P routine logging and optional reuse, X more source, G target grounding/forecast, E changed physical access or independently staffed body. Keep retained R and approved fallback in all appropriate menus. Pre-register original order ID, arrival/deadline and eligibility, original and induced authorizations, controller/data provenance and rights, prospective signal timestamp versus retrospective label, actual acceptance/refund/quality and technical validation timestamps, operator identity and paid/active/idle clock intervals, resource interference, costs and next checkpoint. Record all censored or rejected records and censoring reasons. Measure both original timely coverage and total captured contribution. Report arm-level means and uncertainty, not a selected success sample. No field experiment, customer contact or data purchase authorized.

Falsifier for the restricted emulation argument: if cheap lawful P reproduces G's dated accepted service, useful resource occupancy and successor production at lower total cost, G lacks incremental economic value in that menu; if it does not, identify the weighted original-order deficit and actual costs instead of imputing benchmark prices. Fixed-arm acceptance differences alone need not establish superiority to E or optimized R. DDA's future data value and aggregation mechanisms remain prior art. This is elementary moment/partial-ID mathematics applied to the existing policy-closed mechanism, not independently established novelty.
