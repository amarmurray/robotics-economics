# Endogenous exposure, joint qualification, and customer demand
**19 November 2026 — continuation of *When Capabilities Become Production*.** This extends [the two-date acquisition chapter](TWO_DATE_DATA_ACQUISITION.md), not a new question, model family or claim of original probability theory. No observed robotics/customer outcomes or approved physical experiment exist.

## Economic object and substantive question
Productive autonomy simultaneously (i) completes original customer orders, (ii) selects which target situations are visited and recorded, (iii) produces heterogeneous learning inputs, and (iv) changes future customer/operator activity. More source trajectories, an independent target audit, physical grounding and a changed embodiment therefore cannot be ranked by dataset count or pairwise robot skill scores. They are feasible **whole policies** in the common date-0/date-1 objective (D1).

Write the physically approved history-dependent action k's transition as
\[
T_k(dq_1,dd_1,de_1,dr_1,dx_1\mid h_0,\theta_0),
\]
where q is the **joint** qualified customer-service recipe vector (not a per-skill metric), d includes the pre-admission original-order process and demand response, e is physical target exposure, r is recording/selection, and x includes operator/recovery resources and remaining budget. The retained qualified controllers, legal rights and noninterfering data reuse are explicit admissible options. Evaluate c_0(k)-c_k+\delta E_{T_k}V(Q_1,D_1,x_1), where V is already optimized accepted complete-service contribution. There is **no separate data premium**. R versus A is a Blackwell comparison only if relevant physical/market state transitions and continuation menus are actually coupled as in the preceding chapter; selected R reports alone provide no such coupling.

The exact next identification gap is **higher-order economic alignment**: source X may individually qualify two components and generate original demand, but what matters is whether both components work *on the same customer order that appears under X*. If the report mechanism selects successful production and adoption changes who orders, measuring each margin, even all pairwise intersections, need not determine economically qualified capacity.

## Sharp three-way opportunity certificate
For a **fixed, physically admissible intervention k** and fixed definition of pre-action original customer orders, let A be a validated target manipulation/competence component, B the recovery/coordination component, and D a paying original customer opportunity eligible under k. They are binary potential outcomes **on a common policy-closed network unit**, not unlinked trajectory labels. Put a=P(A), b=P(B), d=P(D), x=P(AB), y=P(AD), z=P(BD), and t=P(ABD). The complete eight-cell law is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
(A,B,D)&111&110&101&011&100&010&001&000\\
P& t&x-t&y-t&z-t&a-x-y+t&b-x-z+t&d-y-z+t&1-a-b-d+x+y+z-t.
\end{array}
\]
Consequently, whenever these six supplied moments are jointly feasible, the **sharp** identification interval is
\[
\boxed{L=\max\{0,x+y-a,x+z-b,y+z-d\}\le t\le
U=\min\{x,y,z,1-a-b-d+x+y+z\}.}\tag{E1}
\]
**Proof.** Nonnegativity of the eight unique cells yields precisely those bounds. Conversely, every t in [L,U] makes all eight cells nonnegative and summing to one, so no narrower interval follows from those six moments. This is elementary probability-table inversion / Fréchet-type partial identification, not a scientific novelty result. If D, A and B are not simultaneously definable under intervention k, even E1 is not an identified policy-specific certificate; do not fill the missing structural assumption with a model score.

For a single incremental complete-service opportunity whose captured contribution above **retained production including operator displacement** is g>=0 only if ABD=1, with a fixed incremental cost c_k paid exactly once, its expected added contribution is in [gL-c_k,gU-c_k]. For a fixed competitor with measured policy increment v, choosing X is robust only when gL-c_X>v; rejecting X is robust only when gU-c_X<v. Otherwise the six moments do not rank the investments. With multiple orders, shared operators, nonlinear demand or finance, this scalar corollary must be replaced by the full V(Q,D,x) or a certified bound on it; E1 alone is not an LP network shadow-price equality.

## Observational-equivalence construction with endogenously matched original demand
Consider an **illustrative, not calibrated** two-date system. At date 0 retained routine R earns 1 and observes routine logs of its own safe eligible customer orders. At date 1 retaining the incumbent produces 2 in either world; the date-0 logs and date-1 retained customer outcomes have the same distribution in both worlds. An approved alternative source-trained X would create two required qualifications A,B and face **X-induced** original customer demand D. We generously suppose the six one- and two-way post-X moments are known exactly, even though in practice they require difficult linked counterfactual validation:
\[
a=b=d=1/2,\qquad x=y=z=1/4.
\]
No historical R-only log, however numerous, measures the counterfactual post-X triple on its own.

Two compatible physical-demand transition laws are
- **World U:** (A,B,D) in {000,011,101,110}, each probability 1/4. Then t=0: skill-pair successes and original customer orders never coincide all at once.
- **World V:** (A,B,D) in {111,100,010,001}, each probability 1/4. Then t=1/4: some target demand coincides with **both** qualified components.

Both worlds have precisely the same **marginal qualification rates, all pairwise qualification–demand moments, aggregate target demand, retained production and unlinked/pairwise routine evidence**. The unresolved difference is a three-way alignment of learned competence with actual service demand. Economic capture cannot be imputed from pairwise technical transfer.

Assume *within this synthetic eligible, safe one-order network* that a complete X-qualified target bundle yields additional **net** captured service contribution 8 relative to the incumbent, already accounting for displacement/operator requirements, and X adaptation costs 2/5. An alternative approved grounding G yields a measured hypothetical incremental net contribution 3/5, a new embodiment E yields 1/2, and retention 0. All share the same date-0 routine contribution 1, date-1 baseline 2, and a one-acquisition-slot budget that makes each feasible but not simultaneous. Then
\[
J_X(t)=3+8t-2/5=13/5+8t,\quad
J_G=18/5,\quad J_E=7/2,\quad J_I=3.
\]
At U, X gives 13/5 and **G wins** with 18/5; at V, X gives 23/5 and **X wins**. The six moments imply t in [0,1/4] and J_X in [13/5,23/5]; they cannot decide whether source training or grounding has greater whole-service value. The switch occurs **at t=1/8**, not at a benchmark success percentage. Under maximin choice G is selected. If, solely as a hypothetical belief illustration, U and V are equally likely, a planner without discriminating information has best expected value 18/5, while perfect knowledge of the world before investment yields (18/5+23/5)/2=41/10: an **upper information value of 1/2 normalized units** before information costs/displaced production. This is not the expected value of one audit label and not a measured robot return.

Why this is an *endogenous-exposure* extension rather than a generic three-skill problem: D denotes orders that exist and qualify **after** X's controller/dispatch/customer response; A and B are also X-specific. Their *marginals can stay constant even while the joint composition of arriving demand and executable competence shifts*. Production R samples a different controller-induced opportunity process. Perfect pairwise statistics therefore do not identify the cross-policy production value, much less accepted-only logs. The parity construction extends earlier nonidentification examples by making **post-policy original customer demand** one of the indispensable factors, and yields a sharp robust investment certificate.

## A narrowly conditional transport result
When two approved policies *do* share the exact same customer-order space, terminal service function f and feasible subsequent decision menu, and their complete joint (qualification, original-order, exposure, operator) transition laws P,Q satisfy TV(P,Q)<=epsilon, then for any fixed feasible rule with whole-service payoff in [m,M],
\[
|E_P f-E_Q f|\le(M-m)\epsilon.
\]
Because the inequality is uniform over rules in the **same** menu, the difference between their optimized terminal values is bounded by the same quantity. Proof: center and scale f to [0,1], use the variational definition of TV; then take suprema both ways. This is a standard total-variation decision bound. It does **not** apply across X/G/E if production function, physical approval, rights, operator use, demand definitions, or policy menus differ. Neither recorded data volume nor matching pairwise moments supplies a useful epsilon: U and V have disjoint triple supports and TV=1 despite agreement of all six moments. For the incremental 8·1_ABD example, the much tighter economic ambiguity width from E1 is 8(U-L)=2, not the generic span×TV=8.

## What the result accepts, rejects, and still cannot say
**Accepts conditionally:** the same training marginal and post-admission market share can coexist with opposite investment decisions; matching all pairwise qualification and demand moments does not replace a *joint complete-service* comparison. Free noninterfering optional data still weakly enlarges the approved policy set; forced costly X can lose to G even without negative per-skill transfer.

**Rejects:** independence of A,B,D by default (that assumption would set t=1/8 and impose a tie); treating accepted-only R production as representative post-X opportunity support; mapping percentage point learning gains into money; describing a routine-log count as a causal exposure-transport certificate; or adding an extra future-data premium to J.

**Open:** identify a genuine target policy-specific joint law and original-order denominator from version-pinned *approved* matched network interventions, or else maintain [L,U] and choose among only safe feasible alternatives. When source learning changes visitation, reporter choice, operator recovery or demand at each step, extend the fixed-k E1 certificate with full-history support sensitivity or direct whole-policy economic estimands. With correlated customer networks the unit is the policy-closed interference cluster, not a robot rollout. Do not force a single triplet model onto large multipayer task graphs; E1 is a transparent diagnostic slice of the broader V(Q,omega), J theory.
