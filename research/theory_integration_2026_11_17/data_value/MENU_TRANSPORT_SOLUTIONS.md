# Instructor solutions — original-menu information versus contract intervention

For the separate [student problems](MENU_TRANSPORT_EXERCISES.md). These conditional finite examples are not a validated robotic system or independent scientific discovery.

**1.** theta_N∈[3/4,1] because the response is in [0,1] and within 1/4 of theta_C=1. Since G increases affinely, V_G^natural∈[7/2+9/8,7/2+3/2]=[37/8,5]. Since 37/8>9/2, a **joint physically admitted** G wins against E under the stated transport restriction. The contract might change buyer utilities, deposits, warranties or quality so the restriction is unsupported. The interval describes natural G, not Y(G,C).

**2.** Let natural high-budget theta_N=0 in L and theta_N=1 in H. Under both, C offers a distinct quality guarantee that induces theta_C=1, and all RR/RG/GR/E observations agree as in the existing complete-policy counterworlds. Natural net G is 7/2 in L and 5 in H, versus E 9/2. No quantity of records generated exclusively under the same on-support C/R mechanisms identifies the response to a different unoffered menu; this is conditional observational equivalence.

**3.** (i) No: whole joint X requires independent observation-closed safe transitions, resources and signed recovery under every modeled disturbance. (ii) No: contract C changes buyer/menu intervention and is not a validated natural purchase kernel without transport. (iii) Potentially: Y(G,C) can be evaluated if the C contract itself is legally enforceable, physically admitted and all receipts, costs, buyer selection and resource displacement are recorded; it is not the natural-G policy automatically. X technical improvements and market demand belong to different identification objects.

**4.** Baseline E=9/2 because expected G=(5+7/2)/2=17/4. Under a positive report, posterior P(H|+)=alpha; G conditional value=7/2+(3/2)alpha and beats E iff alpha>2/3. Under negative report posterior 1-alpha≤1/2, so choose E. Both signals have unconditional probability 1/2. EVSI=1/2*max(0,(3/2)alpha-1)=max(0,3alpha/4-1/2). At alpha=3/5 the report is informative in Shannon/likelihood terms but does not change the chosen investment, so its decision value is zero. Report cost would reduce net value once.

**5.** For perfect state revelation, choose G in H (5), E in L (9/2). Expected value 9/2+p/2, minimized at p_low. Without report, an ambiguous prior worst-case evaluates E at 9/2 or G at 7/2+(3/2)p_low; maximizing gives max of these. Difference is p_low/2 when p_low≤2/3 and 1-p_low when p_low≥2/3. At p_low=0 the robust improvement is zero; at p_low=1/2 it is 1/4. This presumes the true prior is chosen before the signal, the signal has perfect accuracy and the physical feasible set is unchanged. Minimax regret instead compares each policy to an oracle action within each state and can yield a distinct policy.
