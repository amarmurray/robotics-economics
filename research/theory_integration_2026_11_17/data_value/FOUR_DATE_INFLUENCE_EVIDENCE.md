# Prospective four-date original-buyer and repair-clearance evidence protocol

23 November 2026. **Unpopulated design, not a field dataset or authorization to collect customer data.** Paired with FOUR_DATE_ECONOMIC_INFLUENCE_CLOSURE.md.

**Registered unit:** A physically closed customer-support interference cluster: original buyer streams A and C, fixed and mobile robot recipes, one shared support worker/repair queue and all coupled models, with independent E worker and slot proven rather than assumed. Follow each cluster from t0 commitment through t4 customer settlement. Preserve customer A's one unit and C's two-unit lifetime illustrative budget in the synthetic test; field budgets require observed terms.

**Assignment and legal access:** Define approved t0 assignment probability for R/P/G/E, with G rights separately stating retention, training, technical validation, target adaptation, deployment and consented customer-order reporting. A G program with zero lawful assignment support cannot be valued from R-only histories. Record fixed and adaptive policy actions, signatures/permission, price, interference group, and physical fallback. Retain all failures and unassigned eligible opportunities in the prospectively approved population.

**Clock and fields:** t1 original A/C buyer offers/no-offers, accepted-unit budget and inherited worker jobs. t2 before action: qualification and original offers, active worker assignments, evidence source type (ordinary field success, failure/recovery, teleoperation, simulator, synthetic, independently licensed public/pretrained), version, model/embodiment, compute and validation. t2 after action: paid accepted C outcome H, actual invoiced value, quality and costs; new distinct repair job creation/time. t3 pre-dispatch: original A/C offers including refusals and no-offers, worker availability W and completed/blocked support jobs. t3 after dispatch: accepted paid unit and actual remaining customer budgets. t4 before service: NEW original C offer D even when rejected or unserved, actual repair clearance L with times and whether an eligible operator slot is free. t4 after service: accepted paid output, downtime, rework, repeat job and settlement. Link repeat C offers to an actual remaining prepaid budget; do not count prior t3 expired requests as new paid receipts.

**Lawful audit design:** First register prospective G assignment (prefer random within approved physical/safety constraints at whole interference-cluster level). Record H and W across the full G cohort. Randomly include original t4 offer/no-offer documentation in the H=1, W=0 risk set with known inclusion probability eta(X,H,W)>0 fixed before unreported D and independent of D/L conditional on observed pre-audit X. Record L for the risk set whether or not D is selected. For the fixed menu only, estimate E[H(1-W)DL] from full cohort weighting; original t3 H=W=1 buyer-offer audits must still cover the separate t3 term. Missing original offers must not be inferred from paid invoices. Report sample coverage, censoring, attrition, and deployment-cluster interference.

**Negative controls and cross-modal checks:** Matched fixed-panel technical qualification for G vs source P and unchanged replay, matched curation/compute and embodiment; signed directional transfer; independent data sources; fault/recovery counterfactual worker-state tests; audit of offered but refused t4 buyer work. A pure increase in technical success with no increased accepted complete service is not economic transfer at the target. A t4 receipt without recorded original offer, qualification and the prior worker occupancy is not sufficient for the proposed identification.

**Failure/identification conditions:** Assignment support absent; worker clearance unmeasured or endogenous to unrecorded selected t3 recovery action; audit assigned after staff see D and L; shared model versions contaminate nominal control clusters; buyer prices/capture change across programs; or t4 service policy differs from the specified one. In any such case, do not report the fixed witness's [9/5,51/20] interval or audit estimator as a field estimate. Use a new joint-history model or state precise nonidentification. Current repository contains no such approved G cohorts, signed rights or payments.
