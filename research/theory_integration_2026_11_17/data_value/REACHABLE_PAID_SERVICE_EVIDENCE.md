# Reachable paid-service: prospective, unpopulated evidence specification

**No field observations or signed rights have been supplied.** This defines the minimum defensible *original-buyer* audit for the chapter REACHABLE_STATE_BUYER_VALUE_AND_ZERO_G_SUPPORT.md. It does not authorize contacting customers, training, trials or collection.

**Unit and timing.** A connected physical operating cluster/customer purchasing unit, not trajectory frames treated as independent rows. Before choosing P/G/E, record inherited original-worker queue L0, shift capacity, pending work severity, season/market and *actual* buyer offer terms. Verify commitment precedes the next worker/service shocks and that each exposed buyer appears in a complete offered/no-order/paid ledger, including rejected and failed service, not only successful invoices. Track job arrivals, start/completion and censoring to validate whether L1=max(0,L0+J-1) holds. Timestamp all decisions and signals relative to commitment and dispatch.

**G-specific mechanisms.** Only rights-cleared, separately authorized, valid interventions can estimate G Q, demand response, J or QD/J dependence. Record learner checkpoint/embodiment/recipe version, safety acceptance and fallback; customer original purchase choice, price, SLA and cleared payment; G-induced recovery jobs with severity and the *actual* staff roster. A P-only job rate or static skill benchmark does not identify G's incident probability or its same-customer correlation with QD. Distinguish known pretrained/public/synthetic inputs, teleoperation (including paid work and intervention cost), incidental field trajectories and failure/recovery episodes by provenance, retention and legal use. No modality is presumed universally substitutable.

**Right-to-use gates.** Dataset origin, provenance, approved source→target task and embodiment scope, model-update allowance, customer contract and revocation; operating permission/worker safety; signed right to reuse. Forbidden G is excluded; legally allowed but unassigned G is an unidentified arm. Independently verify E capacity, manufacture, staff, energy, time to deploy, and buyer acceptance rather than inserting E's free extra worker.

**Economically discriminating measurements.** Joint per-cluster (Q,D,J,L0), not just accuracy/Q and aggregated order rates; buyer contribution v_l after realized paid collection, refunds, SLAs and displaced production; current P paid throughput and learning consequences; program costs once. Distinguish QD=0 due to unmet qualification from no buyer order (and from unobserved order); record original buyer/no-order denominators at every reachable state. Record G reports legally available *before* irreversible program choice to value information without an oracle assumption. Censor/missingness policy, support zeros and reasons are explicit columns.

**Failure tests.** Compare actual L1 to the asserted unit service law; check whether G changes worker service rate or predecision backlog. Trace if multiple concurrent incidents, retry backlogs, customer budget coupling or adaptation across dates cause cross-period dependence. If so reject one-slot certification and estimate a common path law instead. Check whether a purported probabilistic G job upper bound follows from a physical theorem, externally transportable validated mechanism, or an unjustified guess. Treat unapproved G service as forbidden, not as unpaid or zero-quality G.

**Identification ladder.** (1) P-only inherited queue and original buyer data yield a physical envelope and no G qualification floor; (2) externals may justify explicit G-specific q/j sensitivity bands, marked assumptions; (3) lawful randomized G/P mechanism interventions in matched connected clusters with verified complete original buyer history may identify conditional G joint success if interference and exposure are addressed. Neither (2) nor (3) exists in current records.

**Other roadmap.** Maintain original end-market/customer/value-chain atlas; numerical official BEA/BLS flow is not yet verified. No vendor announcements substitute for quantitative interindustry coefficients. No confidential PE data, outreach, public posting or purchased/scheduled experiment.
