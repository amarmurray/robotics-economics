# Prospective dynamic influence and qualified production registry
**Design only, November 19, 2026. No robot/customer access, safety approval, outreach or purchases.**

Purpose: determine whether the dynamic qualified-production exposure theorem can be safely calibrated at all, not to fabricate a robot-specific network influence matrix.

**Pre-policy accounting frame.** Fix a market catchment and two-date customer window before any source-training assignment. Preserve every offered, rejected, nonattempted, accepted and displaced complete-service order, timestamps, requested acceptance criteria and complete verified recipe. Record competent G/specialist alternatives, buyer substitutions, original and policy-induced demand, captured contribution with exact cost convention, and customer opportunity denominators. An accepted motion or a vendor benchmark is not a substitute for complete service. All cross-site reallocations have a consistent single owner in W_i; global total is independently reconciled.

**Versioned causal graph.** Blocks are not necessarily sites: include an explicitly *joint* shared-model update/gradient and controller lineage block; pooled recovery and teleoperation calendar; dispatch and battery/tool resources; customer order/pricing/referral responses; qualification/held-out acceptance; data reporter, recording selection and rights. List lag direction and maximum response window for every purported edge. Document an opt-out/fallback mechanism and whether copy-only data is genuinely nonactuating. Mark operator→buyer, buyer→dispatch, learner→remote controller and source rights→eligible adaptation paths. Missing a graph edge is a falsifiable claim only when there is an actual negative-path certificate, not merely lack of events in selected logs.

**Separate source interventions.** Predefine field trajectories, failures and recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic data and public/pretrained representations with their own acquisition units, costs, rights, controller weights/checksums, source windows and target-task qualification panels. Hold a competent retained qualified policy feasible. Under X, measure source training then post-training qualification and all original-order production; under G, measure target grounding with unchanged source data; under E, record the *new* joint kernel (new geometry might alter operator time and qualification); under A, distinguish independent shadow report calibration from physical controller improvement. Do not subtract a second learning option value after continuation production.

**Evidence needed to invoke (D3).** A physically approved intervention with exogenous pre-policy assignment, *full state blocks rather than only marginal operator or robot histories*, and supported variations of each candidate cause. Estimate or bound conditional kernel TV shifts from j intervention (b), from prior block discrepancies (A), and the **whole qualified-service contribution spans** (L) over a fixed horizon; use a held-out population and simultaneous uncertainty control. State whether paths are confined to truly independent markets, only within-network, or unknown. Safe operator reservation, existing controller rollback and no-interference across independent experiment closures must be version-approved. If no admissible variation in source broadcast/pooled recovery is available, the coefficients are not measured or zero.

**Prespecified negative checks.** Compare complete original-order customer demand across intervention histories, not successful-service demand. Test if a source update from one network changes another controller; if operator failure/recovery changes acceptance or buyer referrals at later dates; whether rare failures are recorded; whether per-coordinate qualification marginals mask a joint success loss; and whether the same original order is double-counted after dispatch. A latent common learner/operator innovation requires a joint-kernel bound or an expanded block. Robust policy comparisons must include X/G/E effects on opportunity set, acceptance and learning—E is not assumed capacity only.

**Selection and external validity.** Accepted-only or success-only records do not identify full-law TV, reference demand or whole-service L; positivity of training and order strata matters. Very rare all-neighbor exposure yields unstable inverse-probability weighting even if correct. If the graph is too connected or safety approvals exclude whole-market assignments, keep the competent fallback and report an interference-agnostic value range. Separate true data acquisition costs (curation/compute/rights) and scarce operator/capacity opportunity cost; no confidentiality or payment authorization is implied.

**Current actual status.** This run obtained no original-order panel, real block-kernel samples, robot safety approval, verified rights, bounded b/A/L, independent cluster count or numerical BLS–BEA official production crosswalk. Source material used for methodology: Aronow–Samii (2017), https://doi.org/10.1214/16-AOAS1005; Leung (2022), https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA17841 . The latter's approximate neighborhood interference is prior statistical theory, **not** a validated spatial decay claim for a shared model.
