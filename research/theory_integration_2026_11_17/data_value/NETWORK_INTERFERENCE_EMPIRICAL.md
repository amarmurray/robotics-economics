# Prospective matched network intervention and opportunity atlas protocol

**18 November 2026; a design, not an executed field experiment.** Companion to [NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_THEORY.md](NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_THEORY.md). Authorized public evidence only; no private customer, employer, new training, outreach or safety experimentation.

## Identify the true independent network before sampling

Construct three *separate* adjacency/flow layers for candidate connected robot deployments. Controller edges: shared trainable policy lineage/checkpoint, replay buffer, compatible data rights and joint updates. Human/organization edges: recovery dispatch queue, overlapping operator shifts, requalification/validation personnel and peak support constraints. Market/physical-production edges: common procurement/account, jointly accepted service bundle, substitutable customer orders, material flow, transport, upstream/downstream recipe. A mere same vendor or same city does not prove a shared edge. Record each edge's source, timestamp, scope and unknown status. Close under *known* edges to determine the conservative experiment cluster; incomplete mapping is explicitly a limitation. Examine remote indirect interference rather than truncate by convenience.

## Minimal linked observation table

At **preassignment network** level, record network_id, parent holding/customer organization (if public), deployment windows, pre-action customer stratum, complete eligible order registry including zero future orders, qualification rubric/whole accepted service and specialist/old-policy menu. At pool level record physical body and tools, local service slots, controller checkpoint hashes, source dataset lineage (field routine, failures, recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained separately), legal rights, annotation/curation and validation expenditure. At joint network-time level record actual assignment vector, inclusion/propensity probability, safety approval coverage of whole vectors and supported recoveries, abort/fallback rule, shared human availability, realized recovery peak/cumulative load and dispatch conflicts. At full-history level record customer attempts, orders/zero orders, accepted whole bundles, assisted vs autonomous units, failures/returns, gross contribution by actor and nonduplicated curation/operator/robot/capital/coordination costs. Record new customers induced by treatment, not only orders from those who stayed.

## Counterfactual and experimental design

Compare fully approved network vectors only. If possible, independently randomize whole **interference-closed** networks within *pre-action* demand strata. Test mixed routine/grounding at fixed starting versions versus old policies, specialist and target-body update under matched calendars, rights and qualified production demand. Use no-train and frozen customer-demand qualification arms as learning-mechanism diagnostics; separately compare isolated versus shared controller checkpoints with equivalent compute and actual isolation cost. Keep absolute qualified whole-service accepted quantity and net captured contribution separate. Follow every eligible network even if it produces no orders. Do not use post-treatment orders as a stratification or regression control for a total policy effect.

For dynamic policies, preregister approved support-aware action probabilities conditional on state H_t. Positivity is for *complete* network histories with allowable operator budgets, not just for per-robot R/G flags. Report sparse/unsupported histories rather than extrapolating. If real operators cannot safely run a vector, do not randomize it and do not infer its safety from policy-value bounds. Use fixed saturation and no-train alternatives in the allowed action support.

## Safety and source-value tests

Independently review possible *joint* recovery overlap across sites: separate means and marginal fail rates cannot certify that two rare recoveries do not coincide. Document calendar overlap, dispatch queues, certified abort-before-attempt outcomes, worst-case shared peak and cumulative resource support. A field experiment cannot be used as a substitute for independent technical certification.

To distinguish interference mechanisms, simultaneously measure (1) held-out frozen-order complete qualification for old and new controllers; (2) realized customer opportunity including the zero denominator and substitution/induced orders; (3) operator capacity used and lost downstream accepted services. To distinguish different data sources, apply matched, rights-cleared controlled interventions with fixed training recipe and compute/curation budgets; record confidence and raw technical trial outcomes without converting percentages into dollars. To evaluate a redesigned embodiment, redo eligibility and safety support and hold the customer service unit fixed.

## Structural inference limits and useful negative findings

Network total effects can be estimable even if the latent transfer regime is not labeled. Controller-specific data influence requires another matched contrast; total-only observations may equally reflect demand cannibalization. If whole-network randomization is infeasible because every nominal pool shares one model, operator or customer, treat it as a single experimental network and bound unidentified effects, rather than manufacturing i.i.d. standard errors. If near-neighbor mapping is only approximate, record the unobserved long-range remainder and sensitivity scale, grounded by negative-control/far-site diagnostics.

**Production/atlas bridge:** A BLS occupation or BEA input-output edge is an economy-wide supply/use relationship and *does not by itself* identify robot controller, operator or customer interference. The numerical 2025–35 BLS/BEA backbone remains missing, and the existing official source indexes have not been mistaken for extracted data. Align such macro flows to specific accepted-service tasks only with an explicitly audited conversion and evidence chain. Keep candidate customer/market links as hypotheses until verified.

**Decision rule:** The earlier supported A-only rollout earns 7.35 vs 7 only if cross-site impacts are zero. In the connected model require externality bound s_U and verified additional coordination cost c satisfying .35 > .3*s_U + c, and complete pathwise safety. Otherwise the sign remains unverified; retain old approved versions or specialists rather than classify a benchmark as a positive deployco ROI.
