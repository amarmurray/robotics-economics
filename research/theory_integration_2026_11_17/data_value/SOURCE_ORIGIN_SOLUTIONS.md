# Instructor solutions — intervention support and economic data value

These are kept separate from [the student exercises](SOURCE_ORIGIN_EXERCISES.md).

1. Inclusion holds a *specific archive* fixed and randomizes its use in a frozen learner, so the held-out outcome at any test task is a potential outcome of that data inclusion regardless of where source episodes originated. Generation compares counterfactual **new** data produced by different methods at the same context and needs those methods physically admitted and assigned or exchangeable in that context. The first estimates a fixed-data, fixed-learner conditional effect; the second concerns the distribution of future source acquisitions. Shared updating requires randomizing the update-connected network, not nominal robots.
2. Write \(\Delta=\rho d+(1-\rho)d_0\), where \(d_0\in[-1,1]\). Therefore \(\Delta\in[\rho d-(1-\rho),\rho d+(1-\rho)]\). Set, on missing contexts, all \((Y(S),Y(T))=(0,1)\) or \((1,0)\) to attain endpoints. Restrictions coupling outcomes across missing contexts (e.g. a proven monotone source generator, fixed known transfer law or physical impossibility of one option) invalidate unconstrained sharpness.
3. With \(\rho=1/13,d=1\), lower \(1/13-12/13=-11/13\) and upper \(1/13+12/13=1\). These are hypothetical bounded per-context *source-generation* effects; BridgeData's actual task weights, same-state overlap and measured \(d\) were not obtained. A learner may transfer knowledge from pick/place samples to any suitably evaluated unseen task; test-support positivity is separate from source-generation support.
4. Fix rights, curation and compute; independently test complete joint task/failure/recovery qualification; require a safe signed controller/body/dispatcher/operator policy with fallback and a market version; assign whole original-buyer networks with valid propensities and measure unchanged offers, refused/unserved/accepted/completed/paid service, scarce resources, displaced jobs and net costs. Then compare \(J\) values under source, target-grounding and embodiment alternatives, counting future data once. Technical success might be on a service with zero willingness to pay or constrained by the same shared operator; no dollar conversion follows.
5. Publisher CC BY covers data subject to its actual release and attribution conditions; MIT applies to published source software, not automatically to every archive and checkpoint. Record archive/version and individual episode provenance or third-party/consent limitations; independently audit language/task labels and camera/action data alignment, and any downstream artifact rights. The open language-label issue is a user report, not proof that all samples lack labels.
