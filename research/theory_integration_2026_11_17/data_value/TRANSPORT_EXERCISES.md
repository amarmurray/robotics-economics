# Learning packet: data transfer, target demand, and policy feasibility

Student exercises for [TRANSPORT_AND_SAFETY_IDENTIFICATION.md](TRANSPORT_AND_SAFETY_IDENTIFICATION.md). These are synthetic mathematical examples, not directions to test physical robots. Keep [solutions](TRANSPORT_SOLUTIONS.md) separate.

1. **A transport reversal without changing technical marginals.** At both sites A and B, each individual task B,C qualifies with probability .75 under routine R and .9 under redesigned E. Complete-bundle acceptance probabilities are \((q_R,q_E)_A=(.75,.80)\) and \((q_R,q_E)_B=(.50,.85)\). Prove all four joint distributions exist. A source validation cohort has 90% A; a target customer order population has 40% A. If routine preserves one incumbent unit and a complete extra bundle contributes eight units, calculate both R−E differences. Identify exactly which attempted transport fails.

2. **Identification bounds and target overlap.** The fraction \(1-\varepsilon\) of target paid orders with supported matched comparisons satisfies \(q_E-q_R\le .1\). Unsupported orders have only the same marginal task probabilities as Exercise 1. Show the largest possible E-minus-R complete-service gain on unsupported orders; derive the sharp robust *lower bound* on R−E as a function of \(\varepsilon\). What is the strict-certification threshold? Does a negative lower bound prove E is better?

3. **A separate demand shift.** Keep joint acceptance \(q_R=.7,q_E=.8\) stable and incumbent R current gain 1. Calculate the incremental customer-bundle contribution at which R and E tie. Compare \(\Delta=8\) and 12. In the Model A single-service case let \(a=10,c=4,b=2,\Pi_0=0,\rho=.8,K_0=1,K_1=2\). Compute \(G\) at each capacity and incremental captured value. Is this a general formula for a complementary B/C service?

4. **Marginals versus hard safe support.** A report is good with probability .5. A potential target action has high recovery demand with probability .1; low requires 1 unit after an initial 1-unit diagnostic, high requires 2 after the diagnostic. Budget is 2.1. Under W0 the good/high cell is 0 and bad/high is .1; under W1 good/high is .1 and bad/high is 0. Construct each full 2×2 table with nonnegative cells; explain why a report-good-only target attempt can be allowed under W0 but not uniformly under \(\{W0,W1\}\). Does increasing budget to 3 settle real-world certification?

5. **Boundary test and prior art.** Write down what must be known or bounded to replace the special single-bundle formula with an optimized production function over a joint mixture of services; specify how unobserved demand, state-visitation selection, shared-controller retraining and a free qualified specialist can each undermine a simple price-per-trajectory rule. Identify which parts are standard transportability/partial identification and which are still testable robotics-economic hypotheses.
