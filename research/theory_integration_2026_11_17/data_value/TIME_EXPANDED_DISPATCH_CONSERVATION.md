# Time-expanded dispatch conservation: accepted production, backlog and licensed successors

24 November 2026 UTC (23 November Eastern), continuation of [endogenous anchor drift](ENDOGENOUS_ANCHOR_DRIFT.md) in *When Capabilities Become Production*. Conditional mathematics, not measured returns.

## General theorem: economic flow quotient for fixed lawful policies

Fix two implementable rules G and E. A common finite structural type specifies potential histories under both policies on the original interference-cluster denominator, including authorization, accepted completion, deadline, staff/robot capacity, jobs, data lineage and rights. Exclude physically or legally infeasible types before constructing any expected moment constraints. For divisible backlog, B_(t+1)=B_t+arrivals_t-v_t, 0<=v_t<=B_t+arrivals_t, and u_t+v_t+idle_t=C_t (service, remediation, idle, available effort). For indivisible jobs use actual schedules and remaining job states. New authorization refers to a distinct order ID, not a repeated receipt. Data provenance and usable licenses constrain feasible later policies. No additional data premium when continuation already includes its economic effects.

Let F have one column per common type, containing discounted differences in economic activity counts between fixed G and E trajectories. For common coefficients p, contrast equals p'Fq plus known prefix constants, q the type law. Let the existing population audit be Mq=m, with physical equalities appended where valid; let L be the span of differences of feasible q. A price family with linear span represented by the columns of P has minimum additional unrestricted *linear* audit dimension rank(P'F restricted to L). Proof: measure a basis for the restriction's row space. Agreement on these rows implies agreement for the whole price family. Any smaller collection has a kernel direction in L that is not in the payoff kernel. A sufficiently small displacement along that direction around a relative-interior law is feasible, giving a changed contrast. This is not a sample-size claim or an assertion about nonlinear encodings. A single known price needs only the fixed-policy net contrast. State-dependent prices require weighted features or bounded residuals; reoptimized policies need new action exposure evidence.

Known per-trajectory conservation equalities let one eliminate economic count features. In a signed comparison, only equal resource and boundary balances cancel; changed arrivals, capacity, expiry and terminal backlog must appear explicitly. For arm-specific contribution drift, bound the signed residual on actually occupied activities; common invariant paths cancel on paired types, and mutually exclusive activities cannot each incur a full-slot residual. Jointly minimize on common-law support if coefficients and exposure are jointly uncertain. This is established linear algebra and flow accounting applied to the heterogeneous data-value problem, not new generic MDP theory.

## Three-period physically feasible witness

One worker per original cluster in every date. No discounting. A is a qualified retained service paying net 6 in t1. Grounding G costs 1/8 and supplies a qualified, licensed, authorized B alternative on a preobservable stratum S of mass 1/2; fixed G dispatches B there for 5, A otherwise. This committed policy is not necessarily optimal. E dispatches A everywhere plus an independent staffed contract paying 2 at cost 15/8, net 1/8; no later spillover from that contract. The unaffected complement of S has common t2 accepted A renewal paying 4 and t3 fallback paying 1, by construction only.

In S, response to A includes new A authorization R_A and worker clearance W_A. If both, accept A renewal for 4. If clear but no authorization, accept independent authorized fallback C for 2. If not clear, remediate a real carried job for expense 1, consuming the whole slot. Response to B includes new B authorization R_B, clearance W_B and licensed usable qualification report L_B. If all three, accept B renewal for 8; otherwise fallback if clear, remediation if not. Under this technology remediation completes the job; all workers clear for t3. Report measured by approved technical/rights audit; operational report is usable only when clear. Unavailable is distinct from negative qualification.

64 original probability units, 32 in S. A potential (W,R) atoms 11,10,01,00 have masses 20,4,4,4. B potential (W,R,L) atoms 111,011,101,110,000 have masses 14,5,5,5,3. Each clearance and authorization margin is 3/4 conditional on S; B report margin also 3/4. Original-denominator a=5/16 displaced accepted A renewals, b=7/32 added accepted B renewals, d_A=d_B=1/8 remediation masses. Draw potential arm responses jointly conditional on S (product is one common-law witness, not an identifying assumption).

At t3, fallback C3 earns 1. A distinct authorized D order earns 3 and requires a licensed successor report with qualified complete recipe. A-origin remediation K_A, B-origin remediation K_B, or accepted B renewal P_B may generate it; no other t2 action does so here. These sources remain distinct. For each eligible lineage the marginal rates of usable report and D authorization equal 1/2; both imply actual qualified accepted production in this witness only. For overlap z in [0,1/2], its four atoms (both, report only, authorization only, neither) have conditional masses (z,1/2-z,1/2-z,z). Observe report and authorization lawfully before t3 decision; take D iff both, else C3. B renewal and remediation cannot share a t2 worker slot; neither can D and C3 share t3. No counterfactual reports used.

Let q_A=d_A z_KA, q_B=d_B z_KB+b z_PB. Then q_A ranges [0,1/16] and q_B [0,11/64]. Every point is attainable by selecting the separate conditional tables and a common initial structural law. These worlds have identical t1/t2 outcomes and successor report-only and buyer-only margins by lineage but different jointly qualified payable successors.

## Exact conservation reduction and sharp bound

For generic common accepted contributions a1,b1,a2,b2,c2,c3,D3 and remediation expense k, write s=Pr(S), Δd=d_B-d_A, Δq=q_B-q_A. Every path obeys
    t1 A+B=1
    t2 A2+B2+C2+remediation=1
    t3 D+C3=1.
Eliminate A1, C2, C3 differences using conservation:
    ΔJ = -cost_G-net_E +(b1-a1)s -(a2-c2)a +(b2-c2)b -(k+c2)Δd +(D3-c3)Δq.
The backlog term k+c2 includes displaced fallback holding other counts fixed; its learning-generated downstream effect is already in Δq. A recovery job is therefore not universally an economic loss of k+c2. There is no separate data premium. Five contrast features suffice across common linear price families preserving the fixed policy. If all five induced coefficients can vary and moment margins are not fixed, this rank is minimal: locally vary S, A and B joint intersections, B clearance (by changing nonpremium clear versus backlog mass), and successor overlap in a strictly positive atom law. These five directions independently change the five features. With earlier measured moments fixed, only Δq is missing. Common backlog cost cancels when total backlog masses agree even though the underlying recovered source histories may differ.

At baseline E=83/8+2q_A and G=165/16+2q_B, hence ΔJ=-1/16+2Δq. Sharp interval [-3/16,9/32] with attainable opposite rankings and threshold Δq>1/32 for this fixed comparison only. A lower first-period margin can be compensated by licensed production-generated successor demand, or not; separate technical and demand measurements do not decide.

For a supported audit Δq>=1/8, let common c3 lie [7/8,9/8] and D3 [23/8,25/8], with earlier exact synthetic contributions unchanged. Allow arm-specific realized t3 drift +/-1/16 per occupied slot in S; the unchanged complement's drift cancels. Since each arm occupies exactly one slot per cluster, adverse drift <=(s+s)/16=1/16, not a separate bound on every candidate recipe. Common gap >=7/4. Thus ΔJ>=-1/16+7/32-1/16=3/32. Sharp at q_A=0, q_B=1/8 (z_KB=1/4,z_PB=3/7), c3=9/8,D3=23/8, G drift -1/16 and E drift +1/16 on S. Prices preserve t3 choice. Audit is a population restriction, not a confidence interval.

## Countermodels, prior art, next proof gap

A free routine source reproducing the *whole* lawful G transition including accepted qualification, customer/worker response and report timing earns G+1/8 by avoiding prefix cost. File availability alone implies no such equivalence. Free optional availability cannot lower optimized value when old policies remain feasible; committing resources can. If E shares the t1 worker, its contract and A cannot both pay; in a countermodel with no successor spillover, committed E retaining A pays sunk 15/8 rather than earning net 1/8, exactly 2 below independent E. Optional E can be declined.

Fixed-policy price transport is not reoptimization. Changing b1 can change t1 dispatch and the subsequent eligible customer/worker/data histories. Next derive support-function certificates over feasible policy feature vectors with lawful action support and optional no-acquisition policies; test when fixed-rule moments cannot transport across prices and queue durations.
