# Student exercises — cross-date worker occupancy and data value

**Problem set, 22 November 2026.** Study [the four-date chapter](WORKER_CARRYOVER_NETWORK_AND_VALUE.md) first. Do not view the [solutions](WORKER_CARRYOVER_SOLUTIONS.md) until you have written your own derivations.

1. **Dynamic dispatch proof.** At t=2 an ordered, signed-safe target T is eligible with H2=0. Suppose old R yields r2R and target yields r2T, and optimal t=3 value is r3R+d3 1{F3(S31 or S32)}. Derive the exact t=2 target-versus-R decision using two action-dependent transition kernels. Explain which variables the t=2 controller may observe and why future cleared payment cannot be used.
2. **Worker shadow cost versus market qualification.** Express the t=2 value increment using q_a=Pr(S31 or S32|do a) and j_a=Pr(H3=1,S31 or S32|do a). Construct two admissible kernels with the same q and marginal H3 but opposite t=2 choices by changing only the joint coupling. Specify the current paid target premium required.
3. **Full Bellman audit.** With the chapter's B pilot: calculate the t=2+3 return when (a) H2=1, (b) H2=0 and S2 is 00 or 11, (c) H2=0 and S2 is 10 or 01. Derive C_B=5-(1/2)h+(5/4)z and the optimal full P value with Pr(L=1)=1/2.
4. **Sharp missing-cell support.** If the singleton-site original-buyer probability is s=1/2 and the busy-worker marginal is h=1/2, prove the exact attainable range of z. Evaluate the B policy and full P investment value at both endpoints, retaining the old R option. Compare a physically independent E program valued at 209/32.
5. **Identification and falsification.** Explain why a dataset containing only accepted t=2 target episodes cannot identify z. Draft the shortest adequate connected-cluster event ledger covering t=0 source, t=1 B/R pilot, t=2 eligible/no-offer and worker, t=3 learner/recovery, and post-dispatch cleared payments. State one legal/physical falsifier invalidating the four-date construction.
6. **Extension.** Replace the single worker with two workers but retain a single robot-time slot. Does the pairwise operator-overlap deduction necessarily disappear? How does your answer change with a genuinely independent robot-time slot and two workers? State which resource-price certificate, if any, can be evaluated before estimating the joint customer order law.
