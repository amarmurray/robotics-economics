# Reoptimized economic policy feature frontiers: prices, queue durations, and licensed successors

24 November 2026 UTC (23 November Eastern). Continuation of [time-expanded dispatch conservation](TIME_EXPANDED_DISPATCH_CONSERVATION.md) in *When Capabilities Become Production*. Change the estimand from the economic value of two fixed rules to the feasible policy frontier. Conditional mathematics, not measured robotics returns.

## 1. Economic successor features and a price frontier

Fix the original interference-cluster denominator, finite horizon, common law of complete action-dependent histories and an admissible set Π_i of nonanticipating whole-program policies in investment menu i. Qualification, customer authorization/acceptance/payment, shared worker/robot/capital balances, rights and deadline/report timing are enforced in Π_i, not assigned a monetary safety penalty. Policies can generate new licensed data and orders through production. No duplicate receipt, no unobserved future report in a current decision. Retain the no-investment rule where feasible; an old checkpoint does not ensure its old resource schedule remains feasible after sunk investments.

Let f(h,a,h') be a finite vector of discounted economic activity counts (accepted service by receipt class, actual resource consumption, acquisition/curation expense, salvage). Let m_i(π)=E_iπ[Σ_t β^t f_t], where f_t denotes undiscounted features. Coefficients w encode cash receipts/costs, including a constant coordinate when needed. Policy-induced changes in occupancy are in its own expectation. Later learning-generated payments already enter f; no separate data premium.

For fixed transition law and fixed admissible policy menu across a coefficient region W, J_i(π,w)=w·m_i(π). Define K_i=closed convex hull of {m_i(π):π∈Π_i}, assumed bounded. Then V_i(w)=sup_π w·m_i(π)=h_{K_i}(w). Proof: linearity and continuity leave the supremum unchanged under convexification and closure. Finite histories/actions give a finite set of deterministic nonanticipating policies, so a maximizer exists in that set when they are the admissible menu. Convexification does not itself authorize randomized deployment: mixing requires separate feasibility, divisibility or admissible randomization. Conserved activities may be removed modulo known pathwise flow balances.

**Price-family sufficiency.** Two candidate laws with K,K' imply identical optimized values on W iff their supports agree in each direction w∈W. If W contains every direction, equality implies K=K': a point outside the other compact convex set is strictly separated. A restricted price family need not identify the full hull. For finite policies, only the exposed upper envelope on W matters; a supported upper bound may exclude an omitted policy without measuring its full vector. Even all incumbent features and its exact optimality at a single price do not identify this envelope. Nor do optimized values at a basis of directions suffice: conv{(1,0),(0,1)} and {(1,1)} have equal coordinate supports 1 but sum-direction supports 1 vs 2.

**Audit certificate.** Feasible audited policy vectors provide an inner support bound L_i; a justified outer set containing all feasible vectors gives U_i. Thus L_G−U_E ≤ V_G−V_E ≤ U_G−L_E. Positive superiority needs an attainable G lower bound and valid E upper bound. If fitted compact hulls are within Hausdorff distances ε_i in norm ||·||, the contrast error is at most (ε_G+ε_E)||w||_*: for each point choose a nearby point in the other hull, apply Hölder, and take suprema in both directions. The contrast of two support functions is not the support of their Minkowski difference (which maximizes both terms separately). An outer relaxation is not an implementable policy.

For a fixed incumbent π0 optimal at w0 and every feasible mπ within distance D of m0, regret at w is at most D||w−w0||_*. For every π, w0·(mπ−m0)≤0; apply Hölder to the remaining difference. More sharply, a certified old-price gap g_π and distance d_π exclude that competitor when g_π>d_π||w−w0||_*. The distance must encompass future learning-generated accepted activities, not only present outputs. This gives a potentially cheaper local price certificate than identifying every source transition.

## 2. Common-law uncertainty and implementability

Let q range over an audit-compatible set Q of distributions on finite complete original-cluster potential histories. Each fixed rule's feature vector is affine in q under this full-type representation, not in arbitrary reused Markov-kernel parameters. A proposed G rule π is robustly superior if

    min_{q∈Q, σ∈Π_E} w·(m_G(π,q)−m_E(σ,q)) > 0.

With finite competitor rules and polyhedral Q, this is a family of LP inequalities on the *same* law restrictions; it can exploit common-world cancellation lost by independent bounds. Do not infer a feasible robust G rule merely from a positive min_q[max_π J_G(π,q)−max_σ J_E(σ,q)]. That permits G to select with knowledge of the unidentified law.

Counterexample: q∈[1/4,3/4], alternative rules earn 4+4q and 4+4(1−q), baseline 5. Pointwise optimized G≥6 with equality at q=1/2; each fixed pure rule has worst-case ≤5. E earning 11/2 wins pure maximin despite its lower law-informed frontier. A fair lottery yields 6 in expectation only if additive, lawful randomized or independent divisible allocation is available, not a conclusion of the hull argument.

## 3. Two-date conserved production witness

A new finite example, not a reparameterization of the preceding fixed-rule chapter. One incumbent worker and robot per original cluster; one program authorization excludes X/G/E combinations over this horizon. R remains available by declining. Retained t1 service earns 4 net; t2 qualified authorized late fallback earns 1. A distinct early premium order earns 1+p instead of fallback, with p≥0 known at t1. Only one t2 service (energy/contract quota), and a carried repair may consume the early worker slot but completes before late fallback. All candidate physical schedules are stipulated feasible, not actual safety approvals. Assume p is a receipt coefficient not changing customer authorization, duration, rights or technical law.

* R earns 5. E adds an independently staffed contract net 1/2 (receipts 2 less expense 3/2), no subsequent spillover: 11/2.
* X buys extra source data at external acquisition/compute cost 3/2, while retained t1 service earns 4. Usable fully qualified and accepted premium successor occurs with probability 3/8: 7/2+3p/8.
* Grounded paid upgrade B earns 7/2 before grounding expense 1/2 in t1 rather than retained service. Its distinct t2 premium requires joint licensed usable report L, new authorization A and early clearance C, plus stipulated complete qualification and acceptance on that intersection. The t2 rule observes availability and chooses premium iff feasible, otherwise fallback. Value 4+pq with q=Pr(L∧A∧C). A separately approved audit could measure report qualification even when queue-blocked; unavailable report is not a technical failure.
* Deliberate target collection D consumes the entire t1 worker slot with no customer receipts, cost 1/2. It produces a usable, timely, accepted successor with probability one by stipulation. Value 1/2+p. This is an intentionally strong countertechnology, not a measured data efficiency.

Optional G envelope=max{5,4+pq,1/2+p}, X=max{5,7/2+3p/8}, E=11/2. For B all L,A,C margins are 3/4. Inclusion and the union bound give sharp q∈[1/4,3/4]. A lower witness puts mass 1/4 on 111,011,101,110; upper puts 3/4 on 111 and 1/4 on 000. Mixtures attain the interval. These laws can coexist with identical other potential-policy outcomes in a common original population, no counterfactual report used operationally.

At p=1, G uniquely declines in both worlds (B≤19/4, D=3/2), and E wins full menu. Exact incumbent features and G/E values plus the three separate off-policy margins do not distinguish the worlds. At p=4 G spans [5,7], X=5, E=11/2. B beats E iff pq>3/2, i.e. q>3/8 at p=4. Thus identical old-price audit permits opposite reoptimized investment ranking. A supported original-denominator joint audit q≥1/2 guarantees B≥6, strict G advantage at least 1/2, sharp. At p=6 D=13/2 can beat B in the low-q world and E. The optimal data lineage can change with the receipt coefficient; this is not simply a change in value per byte.

## 4. Queue technology and customer response

Shorten B repairs so C'=1 while the same potential L,A events, costs, qualified menu and fallback remain unchanged. The explicit common-law bridge gives q'=Pr(L∧A)∈[1/2,3/4], both attained by the same endpoint laws, and q'≥q pathwise. At p=4 B gives [6,7], robust 1/2 advantage against independent E. Extra intervention cost k must be deducted once; strict superiority holds only for k<1/2. A lower *mean* repair duration does not establish this bridge; correlated delays, data rights, new job load, qualification and buyer response may change.

Generally K_i(θ) changes when θ changes transitions or feasible policies. A uniform complete feature-distance bound on a common lawful menu yields the same support-error certificate; differing menus need regime-specific valid inner/outer sets. A customer-facing price may change the order law itself: V(p) need not be convex. One optional order pays p when p≤2, refused otherwise; R=0. V(p)=p on [0,2] and zero above 2, not convex. Exogenous weight transport cannot be invoked for this demand response.

Free optional routine P reproducing the *entire* B transition and costs except avoiding the 1/2 grounding fee earns 9/2+pq. At p=4 low q ties independent E; high q strictly beats. Free file availability does not establish equivalence; retaining optional old policies ensures nondecrease in optimized value. If E instead shares the t1 worker, it cannot collect both A and its own contract; E alone contributes 1/2 before fallback, hence optional E reverts to R=5 under the no-successor stipulation. Independent staffing must be verified.

## 5. Prior foundations, evidence and next gap

Barreto et al. (2017), *Successor Features for Transfer in Reinforcement Learning*, separates reward weights from invariant dynamics and studies generalized policy improvement; NeurIPS primary abstract checked 24 Nov UTC: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/350db081a661525235354dd3e19b8c05-Abstract.html. Feature expectations are established in Abbeel and Ng (2004), https://doi.org/10.1145/1015330.1015430; occupation measures in Altman (1994), https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.19.1.169 (publisher abstracts checked, not full theorem reviews). DDA §5 and Appendix B already value future data and complementary aggregation, per preserved earlier readings. The support theorem is standard convex analysis, not new RL theory. Candidate synthesis is the combination of conserved *paid* production features, heterogeneous lawful lineages, endogenous opportunity generation and common-law economic comparisons.

Next proof gap: construct a decision-relevant frontier without enumerating every new dispatch/learning rule. Derive a lawful Bellman/occupation-measure upper certificate from action-specific feature and transition bounds on reachable histories; test whether local price gaps suffice when a currently dominated action unlocks previously unvisited rights-cleared customer and learning states. Keep endogenous buyer-price response distinct from exogenous weight transport. Actual original-order panel, signed rights, independent E staffing, numerical official production network and external scientific review remain unavailable.
