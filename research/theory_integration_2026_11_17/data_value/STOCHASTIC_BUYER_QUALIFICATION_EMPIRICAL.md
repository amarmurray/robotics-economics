# Prospective buyer-choice / joint-qualification evidence design
**20 November 2026 UTC | design only; no customer contact or physical intervention authorized**

**Unit:** original eligible parent × period × full menu and policy exposure × requested original service pair × robot/controller/operator network. Source sample includes declined/no-sale periods and buyer outside alternatives, not accepted-only receipts. Retain all original divisional requests before dispatch and any capacity-censored quotes.

**Buyer choice:** exact parent-verified *budget ceiling* and period, price4/5/6 and any service quality/contract variations, lawful offer menus and observation timestamps, precommitment, purchased bundle {none,A,B,A+B}, refused offers, truthful enforceable purchase commitment, actual collected receipts and provider avoidable costs. Budget8 makes two purchases possible at price4, not inevitable. A price4 hypothetical survey answer is not a signed sale. Never imply that unauthorized offers or misleading availability are approved.

**Matched qualification:** source category/version/rights, retained training alternative, target grounding protocol, held-out complete customer service recipe, joint two-slot safe recovery deadline and operator minutes, controller/checkpoint, embodiment and physical-safety authorization. Q means the *joint additional paid service* can actually be fulfilled, not an isolated skill benchmark. Record Q and C on the SAME parent-period network draw to estimate s; reporting only P(Q) and P(C) does not identify s. Do not apply an independent binomial model across network sites sharing learner, operator, procurement parent or recovery capacity.

**Experiment support, if independently authorized:** legally and ethically offer actual menus to a randomly sampled original eligible parent panel with fixed upfront assignment; measure each original choice (including no purchase and outside vendors) before capacity censoring, match with safe versioned controller/grounding joint tests, and distinguish real paid accepted service from declared intention. Preserve menu probability/positivity, time-order, budget verification and data rights. A rollout requires cluster/network units and consent when exposure spills across parents. Without approval, assemble schema and identification logic only—do not conduct outreach or physical trials.

**Evidence decision:** credible same-network lower s>3/8 under q=1/2,k=1/2 supports synthetic G over X; the actual threshold must be recomputed from documented prices, costs, competitors, allowable menus and physical capacity. Certified parent budget alone, cross-source transfer benchmark or independent demand rate is insufficient. If no matched s or defensible network spillover bounds, report X/G ranking inconclusive and preserve eligible R. Use original-order records for the first-paper empirical discriminating panel and eventual production atlas; BLS and BEA aggregate series inform potential tasks/value chains only and do not identify s. No numerical official crosswalk has been verified.
