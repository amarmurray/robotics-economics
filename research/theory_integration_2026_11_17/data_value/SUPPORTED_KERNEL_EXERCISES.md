# Exercises — supported kernels and economic qualification

1. A firm can use old G/S after receiving a teleoperation/recovery trace. Let Pr(W)=4/5, Pr(QA)=Pr(QC)=3/4 and Pr(D)=4/5 under *hypothetical separately justified* G/M evidence. A valid joint kit pays 6 instead of fallback 1 and M costs 3/5. Derive the sharp interval for paid G/M contribution, construct both extremes, and explain why multiplying all four marginals is not a theorem.

2. F and T signatures occur equally often. F must use safe S worth 21/10, and T can choose S or M. Using the result of (1), derive the G investment value as a function of joint success p and the action switch. Compare it to an independent E worth 12/5. Does free optional training data ever require taking M?

3. For a generic supported finite action kernel with linear restrictions A q=b and q≥0, give a sufficient condition for q·u to be identified. Prove a robust action certificate and explain why independent per-action worst-case laws may be invalid when a shared customer or worker mechanism couples actions.

4. In a prospective G,T experiment, M is randomized at propensity 1/2 and the joint original-offer/worker/qualification audit is independently selected at propensity 1/2. Derive an unbiased estimator of p from n_T eligible clusters and its single-cluster variance. Explain what goes wrong when reports are selected based on the buyer's hidden offer.

5. Extend the fixed terminal comparison to an additional t5 original buyer opportunity. Explain why discarding recovery provenance can now reduce continuation value even in a t4 history with a physically blocked worker, and specify the new state variable needed for a valid compressed-state Bellman equation.
