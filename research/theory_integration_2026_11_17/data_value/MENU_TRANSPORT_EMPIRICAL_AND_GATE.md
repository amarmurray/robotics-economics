### Which comparisons would identify what?

* **Original natural GG demand**: only actual version-consistent GG offers/assignment to original eligible procurement parents with complete rejection/receipt observation and lawful independently signed joint G; or a separately defended transport restriction from another version.
* **Contract C demand**: versioned offers and legally verified terms may identify demand for C among the appropriate sampled original parent population, not natural GG without an epsilon argument. A signed contract creates a new potential outcome Y(G,C).
* **Physical qualification**: whole controller/build/sensor/shared-operator transition and observation-closed signed recovery; a commercial reservation/commitment does not establish this.
* **Technical transfer from X or heterogeneous data**: matched training-policy/body/resource/target held-out qualification and failures/recovery, source provenance and selection, negative-transfer comparators, rights and compute; these are separate from the causal consumer menu response.
* **Production-generated versus deliberately acquired data**: for each accessible policy log selection/exposure kernels and future destination qualification, but identify absent GG menu response only if some authorized, independently relevant target evidence has support.
* **Common V/J**: date/index current receipts, costs and continuation once, compare a separately deployable E option, legally available old R and physically admitted target G and future X without assigning intrinsic prices to observations.

### Falsification hierarchy

1. Test whether an independently signed **joint** GG and safe recovery configuration exists. If not: physical gate fails; report cannot create GG admissibility.
2. Test whether the same original buyer group faces the same prices/quality/rights/response conditions across C and natural GG. If not: perfect transport (epsilon=0) is unsupported; record differences.
3. If a bound epsilon>0 is proposed, identify an independent matched menu audit or enforceable structural restriction with predeclared confidence coverage. No estimate of epsilon can be inferred from C-only observations; commercial changes can make it 1.
4. Check buyers absent from solicitation and zero-sale/refusals, sampling probabilities, operator interference, and altered model versions. Exact finite-population theta_C is not observed from a finite selective sample without a sampling/inference argument.
5. Compare [lower,upper] net G against actual E/R, with certified feasibility and once-counted expenses. If intervals overlap, report no investment ranking.
6. Challenge implied independence of joint purchase and technical qualification; report original-order receipt and qualified-network joint events, not just marginal benchmark scores.
