# Prospective original-order demand/quality and qualification register
**No study has been authorized or performed.** Design only, 19 Nov 2026.

**Unit and denominator.** Original eligible *customer opportunity*, with immutable original-order ID, buyer/market/alternative, acceptance specification and feasible specialized nonrobot substitute, standardized service-quality attributes, offer and refusal history **including zero deliveries**, price/contract currency/date, willingness-to-pay information interval and source/quality provenance. A future controller-induced new buyer population is an *off-register category*, not a zero-valued one.

**Policy lineage and matched intervention.** Capture X source type (routine trajectories, failed/recovery trajectories, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, pretrained/public), rights and actual training/curation/compute expenses; G target grounding and operator-saving qualification; E body/tool/control/recovery revision; R retained qualified controller. Frozen versioned service specs, acceptance gates, complete accepted customer output and actual collected cash under each policy. Independently validate whether |w_ip−w_iR|≤δ holds for the matched buyer under alternatives, service quality and rival supply. Do not infer δ from a model benchmark or retrospective self-report.

**Customer offers.** Register posted, feasible and enforceable terms, whether offer was seen, acceptance/refusal, withdrawn/requoted terms, off-policy quality changes, opportunity validity and price timing. A refusal at 6 and acceptance at 8 must refer to the same stable standard and genuine purchase availability: sequential counterfactual quotes to the same buyer can create learning, strategic choice and interference. If repeated-offer invariance fails, use experimentally approved independent matched buyer strata or partial support with explicit wider bounds. Never assume unaccepted offers would have converted if the robot performed a motion benchmark.

**Production and interference.** Record pre-specified entire service bundles; physical robot-hours, charge, autonomous vs supervised recoveries, human dispatch, transport, integration, safety stops, waiting and customer scheduling; qualification result before commercialization; signed policy admission; shared operator/learner overlap and rival robot/specialist displacement. Link prices/refusals to *original* buyer orders and realized full service rather than accepted-only success rates. Record alternative customer output/foregone production, lost paid work during data acquisition and value capture rights.

**Identification choices.** (1) Nonparametric quote margin classification with separately validated bounded policy-induced quality drift and fixed enforceable offers; (2) conservative complete-service LP upper envelope with certified eligible-market receipt maxima and resource prices, no assumption of a quadratic inverse demand; (3) approved whole-market policy randomization where qualified and isolated enough, counting all original orders, off-policy demand and operator interference. If these assumptions cannot be met, preserve wide intervals and competent R; no fabricated price or quote support.

**Falsification gates.** Does a matched acceptance status change unexpectedly under the proposed δ? Do lost/refused offers alter quote rankings? Does E add genuinely incremental certified recovery capacity? Are X/G old-policy fallbacks still feasible after learned-controller updates? Do actual payment/rights ceilings bind the claimed dual? Can new original buyers enter or alternative suppliers exit under rollout? Are safety and measurement inclusion jointly valid? A single failure blocks narrow certification, not necessarily all useful data collection.

**Atlas discipline.** Official quantitative 2025–35 BLS–BEA sector/value-chain crosswalk still missing; only incorporate verified official vintage-specific input-output/labor data, not an unrelated robotics vendor press release. No customer panel, experiment, physical intervention or outreach in this tranche.
