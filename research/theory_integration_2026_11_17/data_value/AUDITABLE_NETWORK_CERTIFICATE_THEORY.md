# When Capabilities Become Production — what evidence would certify a network-dependent deployment choice?

**18 November 2026.** A continuation of [causal influence and admission](CAUSAL_INFLUENCE_AND_ADMISSION_THEORY.md), within the same qualified customer production objective. **A synthetic auditability result, not a measured bound, a robotics safety certificate or a scientific novelty claim.** The question is whether versioned controller/dispatch/customer evidence can justify a *policy-uniform* residual envelope for new grounding and embodiment histories, rather than just for the retained route.

## 1. Complete-policy value and an engineering cut

Retain the earlier complete dynamic state, persistent transfer regime θ, action-dependent qualification Q, original eligible customer order registry, real accepted whole-service captured value J, and separate physical admission. The current conditional coupling claim is e(t+1)≤C(t)e(t)+b(t). Its crucial quantifiers are **all** reachable complete histories, all θ in Θ and all independently admissible candidate policies. A fitted mean from incumbent traffic is not such an envelope.

An **architectural cut** is a set of verified disabled causal edges in the *time-unrolled, complete* controller/replay, operator/dispatch and market/order graph. If every directed path from a change in one source policy to a downstream state component crosses a genuinely disabled edge, and the causal state includes all shared innovations, the two intervention worlds agree in that downstream component when exogenous innovations are held fixed. In the assumed structural model the corresponding influence entry is exactly zero. This is a graph-tracing consequence of structural equations, **not** a test for non-significant correlations. It needs immutable lineage identifiers, controller checkpoint hashes, access/replay permissions, operator reservation/abort state and explicit customer-order/price links. A new grounding recipe, embodiment, re-used checkpoint or customer substitution channel may restore a path; the cut must be checked again over the new policy support.

Conversely, an isolated controller is **not** an isolated economic deployment. Even when controller/replay paths are mechanically cut, a customer may reallocate orders, shared operator dispatch may delay another accepted service, and qualification bundles may reprice the same scarce capacity. Market and operator networks are independent edge families. A new embodiment can establish a fresh route to a global model update or demand segment, so locality inherited from the old body's data is insufficient.

## 2. A quantitative economic *certificate frontier*, not a new dataset price

Continue the exact synthetic case from the preceding chapter: old production 7; staged/admitted route **lower** qualified contribution 7.09675; competent specialist **upper** 7.08, conditional on the same demand calendar, captured-value definitions and resource constraints. The existing positive margin is m=0.01675=67/4000. That margin has already deducted the earlier local and remote uncertainty allowances.

Suppose **additional, previously unbudgeted** source-to-B discrepancy can be triggered by a controller/replay path with worst-case conditional probability q_C or by a market/order path with probability q_M. Let the bound on its *whole accepted B-service* payoff swing be M_B=3 (30% of the original order opportunities times 10 normalized contribution per B opportunity). Suppose an extra verified isolation or audit action sacrifices at most c in further captured contribution, and the specialist bound is unaffected. A union bound on the two additional discrepancy events gives

\[
J_{\rm serial}-J_{\rm specialist}\ \ge\
\frac{67}{4000}-c-3\min\{1,q_C+q_M\}. \tag{D1}
\]

This is a **sufficient**, conservative, same-world certificate, with no assumption that C and M disturbances are independent. A strict positive guarantee needs c+3min(1,q_C+q_M)<67/4000. For c=0 and only one unidentified path, its worst-case probability must be **strictly below 67/12000≈0.00558333**. The previous 7.09675 figure is not re-estimated: this is an extra uncertainty allowance against an already specified bound. If the specialist itself is approximated, its own residual allowance must also be subtracted.

With q_C=q_M=.002 and c=0, the serial lower falls to 7.08475, leaving a 0.00475 margin against the assumed 7.08 specialist. Cutting q_C to zero does not help if demand feedback q_M is wholly unknown; the resulting conservative bound is only 4.09675. This does **not** say the true route loses or that q_M=1 in a robot fleet; it says the earlier narrow *certificate* is uninformative without a demand-path argument. If an auditable complete cut makes both additional terms zero, any extra isolation cost c<.01675 preserves the comparison **conditional on all previously assumed bounds and physical admission**. In reality a cut can alter learning, qualification, order demand and throughput, so c must be measured on the *complete isolated policy*, not assigned as a constant without validation.

D1 is a special application of bounded differences and a union bound. Its content is the falsifiable accounting of which network pathways require structural or statistical evidence to preserve a qualified customer-production ranking, not a new universal probabilistic theorem. Neither success rate nor technical transfer is directly converted to dollars: M_B must be justified by the actual production/qualification optimization over the original customer opportunity denominator and available alternatives.

## 3. What can zero observed triggers prove?

Let s denote a **predeclared supported audit cell** (complete controller lineage, operator dispatch regime, pre-action customer stratum, embodiment and policy class). Assume for each s a binary observable trigger E dominates all *unmodeled joint-state discrepancy events* under a justified, shared-innovation coupling; formally mismatch implies E on every supported matched history in s. Then q_s≤P(E|s). This **dominance premise is demanding**. A live treated robot and a different live control robot do not reveal a joint potential-outcomes mismatch; conventional randomized outcome comparisons generally identify a mean effect, not E. A simulator replay with paired seeds only supports this premise if its structural faithfulness to the real conditional law has been independently established.

If n_s independently replicated identical audit exposures in a fixed s produce zero E events, the **one-sided** exact binomial upper bound is U_s=1−(α/K)^(1/n_s), simultaneous for K prespecified cells by Bonferroni at confidence 1−α. Therefore a finite-class statistical certificate for q_* = max_s q_s requires every θ/history relevant to the policy-uniform bound to be covered by a valid cell and each within-cell parameter to be truly bounded by the audited E probability. With α=.05, a single cell needs **536 independent zero-trigger exposures** to get U_s<67/12000; four cells need **783 per cell** for the same strict inequality. These are arithmetic illustrations of what a **credible trigger** would require, not a recommended live-robot experiment or a physical safety rate. Repeated episodes from one shared learning pool, shifting demand strata, adaptively selected rare states and unobserved persistent θ violate the simple iid/cell-coverage premise.

A formal **no-support obstruction** is immediate: if some new embodiment/target history h is reachable under the proposed policy but never under the observed/audited policies, two complete structural laws can agree on all audited histories and assign q(h)=0 versus q(h)=1. No amount of incumbent-only zero-trigger sampling identifies a policy-uniform q_*<1. Likewise, pooled calibration across θ cannot certify each θ-conditioned supremum. The identified-set interval must remain wide unless architecture, conditional transport or independently safe intervention justifies the missing cells.

This argument contrasts **two real research inputs**: (i) engineering-based removal of actual causal pathways, and (ii) appropriately supported complete-pool matched measurements that upper-bound residual opportunity loss. It explains why source observations can be highly predictive of the *average* technical response and still economically insufficient to underwrite an adaptive rollout.

## 4. Physical safety is not D1

The bound in D1 is about **captured production**. Even a 0.0001 discrepancy upper probability does not make a two-operator simultaneous emergency admissible under a hard one-operator cap. The prior chapter's exclusivity and verified pre-danger safe-stop invariant must be established separately over **all** retained and new robot/remote dispatch activity. Sampling cannot turn an unverified safe-stop into a mechanical guarantee; an audit that deliberately provokes an unapproved rare recovery is outside this program.

## 5. Countermodels and the actual empirical discriminator

A specialist may beat serial admission once measured real opportunity/price or full isolation costs replace the synthetic constants; a free productive routine-data byproduct may remove the incremental need for paid grounding; a redesign may open customer states with no safe common support, precluding comparison without a genuinely certified alternative. Complementary shared-controller data could increase full service under the same resources; an imposed controller cut could destroy that gain. In all cases compare **complete available policies**, retained old version included, and count continuation value only through future accepted production.

The first discriminating deliverable is therefore an **evidence inventory**, not a new fitted coefficient: for each policy, hash controller and embodiment versions; trace rollout/replay permissions and actual operator-resource graph; register all pre-action customer opportunities including later zero orders; document each cross-site policy-exposure/qualification path and which architectural links provably cannot occur; classify all unknown edges and supported cells. Next, use a safely approved matched complete-policy evaluation of qualified accepted services and original customer demand. If no provably dominating E trigger and no covered structural cut exist, do **not** reuse 536 as a field sample-size prescription or claim the serial-specialist ranking is established.

**Prior-art boundary:** Graph/sequential-coupling and union-bound arguments are established. Aronow & Samii (2017, *Annals of Applied Statistics* 11:1912–1947, https://doi.org/10.1214/16-AOAS1005) already analyze causal estimands under exposure mapping and interference; Basse & Airoldi (2018, arXiv:1705.05752, version check outstanding) explain why large network experiments can fail to concentrate under arbitrary interference. The zero-event bound is a classical exact one-sided binomial inversion. None is claimed new. Their relevance is to *which* network effects can actually be measured before turning qualified robot service and data acquisition into a deployment investment decision.

**Precise next proof/identification gap:** Can actual controller/access/dispatch architecture establish a complete structural cut **including** customer demand and shared operators for an admissible grounding/body policy; failing that, can a safe target-stratified audit bound the jointly qualified downstream service response without an untestable matched-world trigger?
