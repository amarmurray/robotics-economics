1. For each z, policies measurable in Z are a subset of those measurable in Y. The difference is the conditional Jensen gap E[max_d m_d(Y)|z]-max_d E[m_d(Y)|z]≥0. If a common signed maximizer d_z is pointwise optimal for all fine reports inside z, equality holds. Conversely a maximizing coarse d_z with zero expected nonnegative regret must maximize almost surely within z. A different controller/qualification kernel or missing old action destroys nestedness.
2. Write max(m_A,m_B)=m_B+(Δ)_+ and x_+=(|x|+x)/2. Conditional subtraction yields [E|Δ||z|-|EΔ|z|]/2. In the independent target-label, physical-context case, A pays 8 when Z=0 and 2 otherwise; B reverses this. Coarse optimum is 5, full 8, gross advantage 3 even though task-label prediction remains chance.
3. For majority M of three bits, P(correct original regime|M)=p=13/16+2t ≥ 13/16 >3/4. The fine-report Bayes decision differs only on split k=1 patterns when t<1/64. Compute q_full=55/128-t/2 in that range, and =13/32+t thereafter. Their difference is max{3/128-3t/2,0}.
