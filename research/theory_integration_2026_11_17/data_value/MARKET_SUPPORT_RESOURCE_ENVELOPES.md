# Policy support, physical production envelopes and the value of source data
**19 November 2026 | One coherent continuation of *When Capabilities Become Production*. Conditional standard mathematics; no calibrated robotics economics or independent novelty.**

The immediately preceding [dynamic-exposure chapter](DYNAMIC_EXPOSURE_PRODUCTION.md) derived a qualified-customer-production bound from a versioned **joint** learner/operator/customer transition kernel. No such joint kernel, original-order customer panel or independent network closure has been physically validated. Rather than declare missing operator-to-buyer edges zero, ask what investment comparisons survive **without** that kernel. The same investment menu retains productive R, independently informative target audit A, heterogeneous source X, target grounding G and changed embodiment E, with data rights and all continuation value charged exactly once.

## Original-customer policy value and the unit of support
Declare C genuinely independent *closed catchments*, each including the entire shared controller update lineage, operator/recovery pool, dispatch resources, downstream buyer demand and original offered/refused/reallocated/completed customer-order window. For an approved full-network policy p, Y_c(p) is the capturable **whole-service net contribution**, including continuation after learning and exact costs. The target is M(p)=C^(-1) sum_c Y_c(p), or a declared value-weighted equivalent. If one broadcast learner, common staffing pool or customer market spans two catchments, they are **not** independent. Recombine; if the entire market is one cluster, an X/G site experiment is not support for all-X adoption.

A reported source-transfer percentage, accepted-only success or per-robot margin is not Y_c(p). Observing X on one robot while another is G identifies a mixed-network policy, not the entire shared market under X. Complete original-order accounting includes unmet demand, specialist alternatives and policy-induced orders. Pre-X order frequencies cannot be assumed to describe post-X endogenous demand.

## Proposition 1: truthful policy-support interval
Let S_p be the catchments actually assigned a *complete physically approved policy* p, and suppose their whole Y_c(p) is recorded without selected-order omissions. For unsupported c, suppose an independently defensible envelope l_c(p)≤Y_c(p)≤u_c(p). Then

\[
 L_p=\frac{\sum_{c\in S_p}Y_c(p)+\sum_{c\notin S_p}l_c(p)}{C},\quad
 U_p=\frac{\sum_{c\in S_p}Y_c(p)+\sum_{c\notin S_p}u_c(p)}{C}. \tag{S1}
\]

M(p) lies in [L_p,U_p]. With **only** independent rectangular restrictions on the missing potential outcomes, both endpoints are attainable and the interval is sharp. If demand/capacity across markets is linked, (S1) may remain an outer bound under valid independent potential-outcome definitions but is not necessarily sharp. If policy outcomes themselves are not closed due to interference, applying (S1) with nominal site catchments is invalid.

For two policies, [L_p-U_q,U_p-L_q] is a valid *outer* contrast interval (not necessarily sharp under cross-policy compatibility). A robust p-over-q ranking needs L_p>U_q; p is rejected if U_p<L_q. Adding full-policy support for catchment c reduces the arithmetic p-interval width by (u_c-l_c)/C, but **width reduction is not the expected economic value of purchasing data**. This does not infer a value for unknown policy-induced future orders from accepted-only samples.

## Proposition 2: production upper bound without a learning coefficient
Under any admissible post-X history h, let y≥0 denote **complete qualified accepted-service recipes**; operating allocation maximizes v(h,p)^T y subject to resource/operator A(h,p)y≤b(h,p) and original-order demand/rights B(h,p)y≤d(h,p). If nonnegative multipliers λ,ν meet
\[
 A(h,p)^T\lambda+B(h,p)^T\nu\ge v(h,p)\quad\text{for every admissible history }h,
\]
then LP weak duality gives
\[
 V(h,p)\le\lambda^Tb(h,p)+\nu^Td(h,p).\tag{S2}
\]
Uniform, physically audited outer bounds on capacities, customer demand, captured service contribution and **all recipes that X could qualify** therefore supply U_p even with an unidentified source-learning kernel. A statewise dual can replace common λ if justified. E changes physical resources, and G can qualify a new accepted recipe, so never transport an X-only dual across those regimes without rechecking feasibility. If future new demand or service value has no credible ceiling, (S2) supplies no finite useful policy bound. A *lower* economic certificate requires a genuinely feasible retained G/R policy and safe rights/customer/operator continuation, not merely stored old model weights.

## Exact counterworld and an economically decisive bottleneck
Synthetic two-site complete-market values are gross G/G=6, X/G=G/X=7. Two histories agree on all three policies but have unsupported full-X/X gross 0 (forced incompatible joint controller) versus 7 (qualified single-slot production). With paid X/X conversion cost 1/2, net full-X minus G/G can be -13/2 or +1/2. Even exhaustive *mixed-policy* observations cannot rank full adoption. If a verified rollback preserves *the same actual market-demand and operator law*, full X/X gross≥6 and the conditional net interval becomes [-1/2,+1/2]. A passive, free, nonactuating copy that leaves every old physical and market policy feasible cannot lower optimized gross output; paid forced updating can.

Here one actually qualified shared recovery/operator slot can serve at most one original order worth no more than 7. This cap is a **synthetic physical assumption**, not a real fleet measurement. It yields full-X gross≤7 whatever source-data transfer achieves. With G/G feasible gross 6, incremental forced X conversion cost K>1 strictly rules out paid full X, **without identifying any controller-learning parameter**. An actually qualified new body E might expand operator/robot capacity and must be evaluated under a new cap. The result says something about economically usable transfer, not that X data are bad.

## One observed whole-X policy is not universal evidence
For *three genuinely independent closed catchments*, suppose retained G contribution is verified 6 everywhere, X gross is physically bounded [6,7] under operative fallback, and only one catchment has been exposed to complete X (observed gross 13/2). Then full-register mean X gross belongs to [37/6,41/6]. At X cost 1/2 per catchment, net X-minus-G is [-1/3,+1/3]. A positive whole-X observation is insufficient to assert full adoption gains. If K>5/6, even the most favorable missing policy completions cannot exceed G. Observing one additional supported full X catchment shrinks *arithmetic* width by 1/3; whether that observation is worth buying depends on policy responsiveness, likelihood, value and timing.

## Interpretation, limits, and prior art
X can add technical qualifications but leave a fixed operator/service cap binding; G can unlock a previously useless trained representation; E can expand both the qualification set and embodied capacity. A can be economically informative only when independent reporting/measurement constrains an actual decision variable. Productive R can generate data without buying a separate audit but selection on accepted service may not identify X's entire future network. Field trajectories, recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretraining inputs stay separate data-source technologies.

This is standard partial identification, LP duality and robust choice *composed* with the existing optimized qualified-service framework. Manski's treatment-rule analysis (https://www.nber.org/papers/t0242), Sävje–Aronow–Hudgens's unknown interference (https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.06399), and Schröder et al.'s exposure misspecification partial ID (https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03459) are relevant prior art. Robust mathematical programming was developed long before this example (Mulvey–Vanderbei–Zenios 1995, https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.43.2.264). DDA §5 and Appendix B already price future data and source aggregation (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10127). Abstracts/metadata examined November 19, not full independent originality adjudication.

**Falsification and next scientific gate.** Measure complete original offered/refused orders, whole-network closure, versioned learner broadcasts, shared operator/robot resources, new E/G-qualified recipes, and captured payoff upper spans. One unaccounted profitable qualified service above the proposed upper cap refutes that dual certificate. Unknown market-wide interference refutes nominal independent support counts. Without verified closure/caps, keep an uninformative interval and competent baseline rather than manufacture calibrated coefficients.
