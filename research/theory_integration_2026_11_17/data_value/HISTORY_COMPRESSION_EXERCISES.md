# Exercises — economic sufficiency of heterogeneous robot-production evidence

1. **Economic Jensen gap.** For a finite terminal action menu A and a compression T=f(H), prove V(full)>=V(T). State an exact condition for equality, including ties. What changes if some actions are unlawful in some raw histories grouped by T?

2. **Production-data count.** Use the F,T,FF,FT table in WHAT_MUST_THE_FIRM_REMEMBER.md. Compute optimal action and expected value conditional on N=1 and N=2. Why are separate average technical success rates not an economic sufficient statistic for the joint kit?

3. **Minimality.** Find the coarsest partition of the four histories that preserves V(full). Explain why F and FF can merge but F and T cannot merge under the given customer-payoff table.

4. **Information and investment.** The optional independent E alternative has net value 3. Find the highest permissible additional report cost at which the provenance-aware G policy still beats E. Does optional free data availability ever lower an optimizer's value?

5. **Action timing.** For the earlier t3 S/M two-signal example, show why a report delivered after the irreversible t3 choice cannot restore the 11/2 value.

6. **Causal support.** Suppose every historical G site has only operated with S, while engineers fully record its F/T outcome and t4 invoices. Does the common-optimizer criterion identify a policy using M? Design the minimal lawful evidence treatment and explain which joint buyer/worker outcomes remain indispensable.
