# Conditional component certificates when learned production creates shared services

**21 November 2026 — same November 22 Models A/B SG-versus-embodiment decision. Scientific acceptance OPEN.** This extends the full-horizon Bellman certificate in [DYNAMIC_PAID_RESOURCE_CERTIFICATE.md](DYNAMIC_PAID_RESOURCE_CERTIFICATE.md), rather than treating data as a separate option premium. The new constructive step is an auditable *componentwise* whole-horizon upper certificate that **does not require independent robot qualification, independent customer demand, or independent source outcomes**. It does require conditional moment inequalities valid under every admissible shared learner/operator/buyer history and every legally selectable action. That distinction is central to scaling from individual experiments to pooled production.

## Economic environment and question

Let h_t be the complete network history: original-buyer orders, declined/refused services, finally cleared payments/refunds, model/checkpoint and source provenance, jointly qualified recipes, device embodiment, shared operator inventory, future recipes learned from production, source-retention rights and their expiry. A program a in {R,S,G,SG,E} has a finite-horizon lawful nonanticipating policy menu Pi_a. Distinguish field/failure/recovery F, licensed teleoperation T, simulation/synthetic M and public/pretrained sources; data acquisition by deliberate testing differs from by-product data generated by accepted production. Changing the program can change the admissible actions and the *complete* event distribution, including new future buyer demand.

Write the **one** accounted net original-buyer contribution as
\[
J_a=\sup_{\pi\in\Pi_a}\mathbb E_\pi[\sum_{t=0}^{H-1}\sum_{i=0}^m y_{it}+\sum_{i=0}^m y_{iH}],
\quad \mathbb E_\pi\sum_{t,i}g_{kit}\le b_k.
\]
Module 0 includes genuinely shared learner, operator and contract terms; each actual training/curation cost and finally settled receipt enters a single module **once**. Resource loads g are nonnegative. The full state need not be Markov in a small representation: use complete histories where necessary. The question is whether potential functions on smaller projections can upper-bound all globally admissible adaptive policies despite correlation.

## Conditional component certificate (weak duality without independence)

Fix nonnegative resource prices \(\lambda\). For each module i and time t, propose a projected state \(s_{it}=\sigma_{it}(h_t)\), a deterministic function \(\phi_{it}(s_{it})\), and deterministic audit tolerances \(\epsilon_{it}\ge0\). Suppose the true **complete conditional law**, for *every legally reachable complete history h_t and global action u_t*, satisfies
\[
\phi_{it}(s_{it})+\epsilon_{it}\ \ge\mathbb E[y_{it}-\lambda\cdot g_{it}+\phi_{i,t+1}(s_{i,t+1})\mid h_t,u_t] \tag{1}
\]
and \(\phi_{iH}(s_{iH})\ge y_{iH}\) pathwise. Then
\[
\boxed{J_a\le\lambda\cdot b+
\mathbb E\sum_i\phi_{i0}(s_{i0})+
\sum_{t=0}^{H-1}\sum_i\epsilon_{it}.}\tag{2}
\]
If there is an independently identified lawful supported complete policy \(\pi_0\), its paid contribution yields \(J_a\ge V_a(\pi_0)\). For the same original-buyer market, separate valid SG and E upper/lower certificates imply a strict investment ranking only if the intervals do not overlap.

**Proof.** Sum (1) across i. Conditional expectation is linear even under perfect source, learner, qualification, demand and operator dependence. Conditional on the actual full history and global chosen action, the sum is a full-network super-solution inequality. Iterated expectation telescopes all \(\phi\) terms; terminal domination closes the sum. Add \(\lambda\cdot b\) using the true resource bounds, then take the supremum over *all* feasible adaptive policies. This requires neither a product transition law nor independent buyers. Validity is lost if (1) was checked only at logged average histories, a single training recipe, or a subset of physically/contractually admissible actions.

The projection can be compressed only when every hidden global history sharing its projected value is covered by the claimed conditional inequality. Shared checkpoint, operator occupancy, rights, buyer-demand regime or simultaneous qualification may therefore have to enter several projected potentials or a common module. If such uniform bounds cannot be verified, use the earlier full-history certificate or report partial identification. The resource-price term is an upper-bound device, **not** an incremental cost charged again to customers. No strong duality, rectangular ambiguity, independent qualifications or small source-tail assumption is asserted. This is a conditional application of standard approximate/separable dynamic programming and weak duality.

**Connection to the existing resource-price certificate.** If service/learning states do not change dynamically and the legal production columns are closed, each module potential is a static accepted-and-paid resource-price row. With recursive learned columns, (1) must hold for every permitted successor, including qualified new embodied actions and post-failure recovery. Thus this is a constructive decomposition of the prior full-horizon proposition, not a second additive value-of-data term.

## Exact two-customer coupled-data counterworld

All figures below are **hypothetical normalized paid-service contribution units**, not dollars, robot field measurements or claims about data scaling. At date 0 two original-buyer services produce finally-paid indicators U,V with P(U=1)=P(V=1)=1/2. Each order uses its own already-available first-date slot; expected initial net contribution is 1 under every compared data recipe. The source-conditioned joint probability \(w=P(U=1,V=1)\) need not equal 1/4. Its **sharp** Fréchet range from these marginals is \([0,1/2]\), with complete joint mass table \(P(00)=w, P(01)=P(10)=1/2-w, P(11)=w\).

A licensed joint-success trace may qualify one distinct date-1 original buyer service, with one **shared** operator slot. Target grounding determines its conditional complete finally-paid probability q; an actual attempted service costs c=1/4 whether or not it settles. The robot may skip it. It is a different customer order, so its receipts are not duplicates of the two date-0 services. With rights, the **forced trained recipe** has value \(1+w(q-c)_+\). Without lawful data reuse this future action does not exist and its value is 1. The first-date pair can be positive/negative correlated with identical single-service benchmark statistics. No buyer independence was assumed.

Keep a physically eligible old source/controller policy with \(w_0=1/4\) and q_0=1/2. A stronger G target-grounding method has q_1=3/4 and the same old w_0. New source program S changes dependence w but not either date-0 marginal. Training new data is *optional*, not forced to replace the old controller. The feasible maximized contributions are:
\[
J_R=17/16,\quad J_G=9/8,\quad
J_S(w)=1+\tfrac14\max\{w,1/4\},\quad
J_{SG}(w)=1+\tfrac12\max\{w,1/4\}.\tag{3}
\]
A separately feasible E embodiment has stipulated **complete** value \(J_E=19/16\), not derived from model accuracy or data percentages. The support interval from unchanged single-service marginals is
\[
J_{SG}\in[9/8,5/4],\quad
J_{SG}-J_E\in[-1/16,+1/16].\tag{4}
\]
Two worlds with the same original first-paid marginals and legal old-policy fallback reverse the economic ranking. World L: w=0, the new forced recipe loses, is skipped, SG=9/8 and E wins by 1/16. World H: w=1/2, SG=5/4 and wins over E by 1/16. At w=1/2, source-only S=9/8 and grounding-only G=9/8 each loses to E, yet their combination wins; the exact source-by-grounding cross-difference is 1/16. The interaction is from joint *economic occupancy* of a later service, not necessarily an intrinsic neural technical-transfer interaction.

A legitimate **conditional bound** w<=1/3 for every feasible new SG recipe yields \(J_{SG}\le7/6<19/16=J_E\), strictly favoring E. A legitimate bound w>=2/5 for an actually feasible qualified SG policy yields \(J_{SG}\ge6/5>J_E\), strictly favoring SG. The decision boundary, with the old option preserved, is w=3/8. This boundary is *not* a proposed field estimate or a preapproved safety threshold. The same two single-service marginals cannot provide either certificate. Complete rights, joint first-paid U/V, q conditioned on U=V=1, and the original buyer's offered/accepted/finally-settled follow-on service are needed.

### Explicit module construction

At date 1 set the common learner/rights/service potential \(\phi_{0,1}=1_{\{U=V=1,\,rights\}}(q-c)_+\), with no terminal value; first-buyer modules each contribute their known date-0 conditional mean 1/2. Before the source choice, if all new trained recipes obey a certified upper w<=u, take \(\phi_{0,0}=\max\{u,w_0\}(q_1-c)_+\). Summing first-buyer and common modules gives \(J_{SG}\le1+\max\{u,w_0\}/2\). The constant price \(\lambda=0\) suffices because the one shared future operator slot is already encoded in the legal action menu. Under a valid conditional resource budget across many future buyers, nonzero \(\lambda\) can instead provide a separate weak-dual bound. Crucially, leaving the joint U/V successor or legal rights out of the common module would make a misleadingly tight certificate.

**General implication.** Even when source learning improves neither single task's final-paid marginal, its effect on *which joint histories become productive* can be economically consequential. Source diversity, target grounding and embodiment choice cannot be ranked without demand- and rights-conditioned qualification bundles, constrained future services and the joint learner/customer production regime. Conversely, observing an unhelpful forced retraining recipe does not violate free-optional-data monotonicity.
