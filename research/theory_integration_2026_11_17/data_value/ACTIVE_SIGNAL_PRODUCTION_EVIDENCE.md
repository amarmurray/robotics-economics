# Prospective active P/D/SG/E causal data-value evidence ledger — unpopulated

**22 November 2026.** Companion to [active signal and production](ACTIVE_SIGNAL_PRODUCTION_KERNEL_AND_ECONOMIC_TRANSFER.md). No private buyer records, training rights or robot access were obtained. This is a preregistration-style identification and falsification *design*, not an experiment already run.

**Unit** — whole connected original-buyer cluster (all simultaneous eligible orders **and no offers**, buyer/site/time horizon, shared fleet checkpoint, failed work/recovery, support staff, retained controllers, model broadcasts, licensed data, E hardware lead-time and costs). Shared broadcasts or workers spanning nominal sites require the larger connected exposure unit. No individual episode-level selected success can stand in for full original-buyer demand.

**Preassignment** — record source version/lineage; exact mutually exclusive conditions P paid field data, D approved safe teleop/grounding, SG, E genuinely independent capacity, R retain; assignment timestamp; customer outside option/pricing/acceptance; source train/retention rights; approvals and exclusions; safety qualification recipe; current workload and order opportunity; available technician/robot/operator slots and capex/manufacturing. Any unapproved action has no numeric potential paid outcome. No employer or confidential information.

**Transition and reports** — track a physically approved pre-dispatch Q test with exact task and version; each report Y provenance, signal clock, legal viewer, latency, acquisition expense, precision conditional on preassignment Z (not a posttreatment proxy), and whether it actually changes training/recovery/demand. Track B=original buyer eligible order/no order regardless of served status, acceptance, deadlines, refunds and cleared revenue. Track F=operator free **at the relevant reserved service window**, competing site breakdowns, failures, incidents, rescue duration, operator identity and time, rollback. Avoid calling H=worker free when a second embodiment shares its supervisor. Measure conditional joint q_a(y), s_a(y) directly; marginal Q, B, F and aggregate Y accuracy alone are insufficient.

**Matched interventions** — compare P and D with common original-buyer order opportunity and pre-specified cluster exposures, not selected successful robot episodes. To separate action's K and information L require legally permitted **factorial/report variants** or defensible structural restrictions; off-policy K_D×L_P and K_P×L_D cannot be inferred from two observational arm means alone. Preserve failure/recovery, false negative target acceptance and unserved orders. E must have genuinely nonshared capacity plus independent recovery/lineage measured at simultaneous demand, procurement and maintenance costs. Shadow comparisons are observational unless actual assignment and outcomes give causal identification; intercluster spillovers may invalidate naive randomization. Never deploy unsafe or unapproved robot policies.

**Empirically discriminating tests:** Conditional on Q=1,Y=y, T reservation requires a grounded buyer-and-worker probability greater than its opportunity-cost threshold; a favorable aggregate Q need not cross it. In a design that holds K fixed by construction, garbling a legally free report cannot improve optimized paid return. In a design that alters recovery occupancy or buyer timing, same precision can yield a different ordering, including opposite interaction signs. If report-generated treatment breaks conditional independence of X and Y given Z, estimate K_a(X,Y|h) rather than imposing K_a(X|Z)L_a(Y|Z). If measured buyer offers or signed safe qualification menu are absent, write **unidentified**, not zero.
