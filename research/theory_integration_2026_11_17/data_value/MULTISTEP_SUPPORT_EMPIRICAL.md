# Multi-step support frontier — matched safe empirical identification design
**Design only, 18 November 2026 UTC. No customer access, robot deployment, training, human recruitment, or industrial parameter estimate.** Builds on SEQUENTIAL_SUPPORT_EMPIRICAL.md and INTERVENTION_EMPIRICAL_PROTOCOL.md.

The estimand is the causal difference in *customer-accepted joint service production* when the same eligible starting controller and site use an ordinary production policy, deliberate safe target grounding, or permitted new/changed embodiment subject to identical time, operator, validation, safety and cash budget caps. Analyze both the induced policy distribution and the policy chosen after the first update, with the same qualified incumbent A and competent specialist S.

**Required panel columns:** demand window, target accepted service bundle B=(components including recovery, tooling, site safety, operator coverage); incumbent f(Q;omega) and specialist output; q0/e0 and q1 version+hash; site and consent; first approved state-exposure opportunities, actual visited states, gate decisions, independent false-negative safety audit, received corrections, licensed training inclusion and update/qualification check; second safe candidate-state eligibility, exposure *even without correction*, held-out component-wise and **joint** acceptance, rejected attempts and downtime; operator minutes, latency, calendar days, compute/curation, hardware retooling, rights and costs. Separate production-created field trajectories, supervised recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained records.

**Interventions, only after independent approval:** across matched site/embodiment/starting-policy seeds, randomize optional *inclusion* of already licensed relevant corrections for safe retained controller training, then randomize the second **permitted coverage policy** within each controller arm. Separately compare a target-only qualified specialist and a reworked body under the same real operator/customer budgets. This is a proposed causal design, not permission to enter risky states, disable safety gates or withhold urgent human corrections. For safety in actual experiments use external sign-off and record any policies precluded by a safety invariant.

**Estimators and bounds:** estimate the statewise conditional transition p_j^R and p_j^G from eligible exposure denominators and audited misses, rather than log-count fractions; learn the full joint Q acceptance distribution and conditional H proxy regimes. Confidence bounds near zero must distinguish "not observed" from structurally impossible: a zero count does NOT prove p=0 in an unbounded population. If a B-relevant state is never safely reachable under any audited policy, report support-failure and nonidentified target transition rather than assume zero or fit a dollar-per-demo coefficient. Map accepted service to demand-specific f(Q) via actual orders/rework/alternatives; preserve invoice/receipt provenance as a *separate* economic stage.

**Three discriminating comparisons:** (i) first controller improves skill score but changes second-target visitation (changes p_1) versus same controller with changed coverage policy; (ii) same marginal B/C acceptance but different joint 11 frequency (tests bundle complementarity); (iii) regime where routine byproduct reaches comparable B safely and cheaply versus one where only G/E safely opens support. If routine reaches the supposed zero-cut state on an approved matched attempt, the toy's cut assumption is falsified for that population. Customer demand and specialist output can still make G/E unprofitable even when technically more effective.

**Evidence boundary:** Public DAgger and safe-exploration literature explain why these controls matter, not the value of real robot deployment data. Current repository lacks matched field joint acceptance or official numeric production network backbone. No vendor demonstration, announced robot count or raw training-hour total is a substitute.
