# Prospective demand-stratified safe whole-pool qualification design

**18 November 2026; no field study performed.** Links to [the exact theory](SAFE_POOL_FACTORIAL_THEORY.md) and [proof ledger](SAFE_POOL_FACTORIAL_PROOF_LEDGER.md). This specifies the smallest possible observations that would discipline the prior speculative shared-controller interference sign and the new three-way term.

## Target and units

Use pre-registered customer opportunity cohorts including zero future orders, existing specialist outside options and all price/capture terms. Define d **before assignment**, based on customer order type, lighting/surface/access, complete service demand and target embodiment. Fix starting controller SHA/version, rights, allowed data source and model-update recipe. Measure the full *shared controller + operator + downstream customer interference neighborhood* as the randomization unit, not each robot. If cross-pool model training or operator dispatch is shared, do not claim independent pool randomization; enlarge clusters or accept the nonidentification.

Let one approved n-slot pool deliver complete follow-on accepted-service contribution, net of actual curation, validation, CPU, operator, body and commercial costs. Report both total policy effect (including induced orders, zero-order denominator) and the **secondary frozen-demand held-out qualification** effect. Only the latter can isolate control learning from demand/marketing response.

## Eligibility and assignment

1. Independent safety engineer/operator governance must approve pathwise peak and cumulative recovery, certified abort-before-attempt routes, controller fallback and service-level obligations for **each complete vector**; expected operator time and zero observed accidents are not substitutes. This research does not itself approve an action.
2. For a three-slot pool, separately pre-register feasible GGG; exactly-one-R with randomized position; exactly-two-R with randomized position; RRR **only if all its supported paths are independently approved**; and isolated-controller, specialist, no-train and alternate-embodiment alternatives under commensurate customer resources. Do not treat staged two-slot safety approval as approval of RRR.
3. Randomize full approved vectors at independent pools *within pre-action customer stratum* with recorded p(a|d)>0, date/version and assignment compliance. For a fixed saturation k, randomize identities/positions of the k R slots but prohibit all disallowed vectors; position assignment need not be independent Bernoulli. Check actual exposure mapping because intervention gating and route-induced visited states can change what each robot experiences.
4. Audit the complete original customer registry through the pre-specified horizon, including no orders, unassisted/assisted accepted units, failures, recovery distribution/tails, requalification delay, returns, teleop, simulation, public/synthetic contributions and rights. Separately track learned-controller/held-out task-bundle acceptance and customer surplus/capture so a robot benchmark never becomes dollars by assumption.

## Proposed table and estimands

For each d, controller lineage, date and complete approved route vector, record **number of independently assigned pools; randomization probability; certified safety envelope; observed peak/cumulative operator resources; eligible base and new orders (including zero); jointly accepted whole-service units; net captured contribution and qualifications by frozen controller comparison**. Estimate \(\mu_d(a)\) and design uncertainty using the pool/cluster assignment and follow-up, not within-pool robot-level i.i.d. standard errors.

For the minimal n=3 symmetry check, compare f0,f1,f2 and, only if approved, f3. The pairwise-only extrapolated f3 is 3f2-3f1+f0. A materially different observed f3 identifies a third-order *economic* contrast conditional on the design. Repeating a two-robot task benchmark does not estimate it. If f3 unapproved, do not fit a model to assert safety; restrict rollout to certified saturation levels and report the missing support.

To assess source data independently of commercial marketing response, randomize allowed field, recovery, deliberate target, simulation, synthetic or public-source additions separately with matched compute and exact rights inside a fixed policy-update recipe. Use frozen production demand and held-out end-to-end qualification to estimate genuine learning; repeat *commercial* full-policy follow-up to estimate total customer value. A causal mean route contrast requires a stable preassignment cohort and follow-up; it need not reveal latent H or its conditional diagnostic accuracy.

## Partial coverage policy rule

For an approved but unobserved treatment in pre-action segment B, carry a transparent external resource/customer bound on contribution; label it **unverified** until appropriate cluster coverage. Form target-weighted partial intervals without treating post-treatment customer orders as baseline weights. If the observed positive A effect clears real segmentation cost, a safe A-treatment/B-fallback rollout may be justified while the global-treatment difference still straddles zero. If B is unapproved, no global treatment comparison is feasible—do not use statistical extrapolation to bypass the safety gate.

## Primary falsifiers and limitations

- Independent controller replication makes all observed mixed-pool effects disappear, at affordable true resource cost: shared learning interference is a contingent deployment configuration, not unavoidable.
- Mixed exposure improves a benchmark but no new held-out whole-service bundle, accepted order or capture: technical gain is not yet economic source value.
- A third concurrent robot creates a new coordination or error-recovery tail: two-slot pair scaling is invalid and might be *unsafe*.
- Customer A/B composition shifts due to selection or treatment, so target weights are unstable: conditional transport fails, demanding complete dynamic policy response measurement.
- All observed routine production already supplies relevant data at no increment or old policies can be retained for free: an expensive deliberate-data or conflicting retrain strategy may be dominated.
- The operator cost/support tail is not auditable: mark pathwise policy eligibility unresolved, not automatically eligible or ineligible.

**No confidential employer/customer access, robot training, outreach or purchased data is authorized.** This is a prospective protocol; no actual acceptance panel, experimentally measured interference, BLS/BEA numeric production network or economic qualification estimate is claimed. It advances the measurement plan while the Nov22 synthesis and Dec25 final gates remain open.
