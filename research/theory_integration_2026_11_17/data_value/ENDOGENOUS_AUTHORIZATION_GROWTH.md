# Endogenous authorization growth: an economic lineage potential beyond a fixed pool

24 November 2026 UTC / 23 November Eastern. Continues [dynamic occupancy audit dual](DYNAMIC_OCCUPANCY_AUDIT_DUAL.md) in *When Capabilities Become Production*. The earlier common-population cap can fail when a learning program creates new buyers and licensed opportunities. We retain the original cluster denominator but no longer require a fixed terminal order pool. This is conditional theory and synthetic arithmetic, not estimated robotics economics.

## 1. Accounting object and economic environment

A token is a distinct, dated, scope-specific economic opportunity: a customer-authorized original or successor service, with its relevant qualified controller/menu, lawful data-access status and deadlines recorded. A lead without authorization is a different type from an accepted order; a data file is not itself a paid-service token. A token is not a transferable legal right or a claim that a buyer must pay. Distinct IDs, mutually exclusive service alternatives, joint buyer budgets and worker/robot calendars prevent double counting. Record renewal as a fresh authorized successor (or an explicitly dated residual entitlement), not copying a parent's authorization. A learning intervention can alter the stock of such opportunities; a newly identified customer has to grant its own applicable consent. Types may encode bundles when their receipts or rights cannot be separated. Source lineage labels distinguish routine field work, failures/recovery, teleop, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained and deliberate target grounding; these are not assumed interchangeable or intrinsically monetizable.

Let S_t be the nonnegative vector of outstanding opportunity stocks after external arrivals, measured per original interference cluster. Actions are whole-network, lawful, nonanticipating schedules, represented by nonnegative counts x_{ta}; action a consumes vector n_{ta}, creates random distinct new opportunity stock D_t, uses scarce resources l_{ta}, and has incremental net economic receipts. No physical or safety permission is inferred from the algebra. Require pathwise sum_a n_{ta} x_{ta} <= S_t; expiry E_t is nonnegative; and

    S_{t+1}=S_t-N_t x_t+D_t+B_{t+1}-E_t.

B is separately accounted exogenous arrival, never the same order as a child in D. A joint action can consume several parent types; an action needing no parent must be represented with its other scarce resource or bounded external opportunity. No unlimited unpriced generation. Physical resource restrictions L_t x_t <= K_t can be replaced by valid unconditional expected bounds, but pathwise feasibility is still needed for lower policies. We assume finite integrable quantities.

For each feasible predecision history and joint schedule, suppose conditional expected net incremental reward is at most r_t·x_t and conditional expected new stocks at most M_t x_t componentwise. Columns may depend on full joint action and stage; a bound on isolated parents is not a bound in a simultaneous multi-parent schedule unless it holds under every relevant sibling selection. If reward/birth depend on latent selection, the bounds must hold conditional on that selection's lawful report and history. Baseline cash or resource charges outside this representation must be included once, in a separate bounded residual; if retained output is displaced, include its loss in r or the residual, not both. Terminal payoff either zero or bounded above by w_T·S_T plus separately recorded residual. Dates and rates fixed for comparison; buyer-facing prices may change all of these bounds.

## 2. Lineage potential certificate

Choose nonnegative deterministic vectors w_t (already in time-zero value units) and nonnegative resource prices lambda_t with

    w_t >= w_{t+1},
    beta^t r_{ta}+w_{t+1}·m_{ta}-w_t·n_{ta} <= lambda_t·l_{ta}

for all allowed columns and dates. The first inequality prices carryover; a stock that can mature with time must have its maturation represented in an action or in a finer type/time conversion. If there is a nonidentity carryover conversion A_t, replace w_t>=w_{t+1} by w_t>=A_t^T w_{t+1} and account for how consumed stock is removed before conversion.

Then every lawful policy under every common law satisfying those inequalities has incremental discounted value at most

    w_0·E[S_0] + sum_{t=1}^T w_t·E[B_t] + sum_{t=0}^{T-1} lambda_t·E[K_t],

plus the separately bounded baseline/residual. Any deterministic upper on expected B or K can be inserted because weights are nonnegative.

Proof: conditional on the predecision history and selected action, add the expected change w_{t+1}·S_{t+1}−w_t·S_t to beta^t times incremental receipt. By stock conservation it is at most (w_{t+1}−w_t)·(S_t−N_tx_t) + sum_a x_{ta}[beta^t r_{ta}+w_{t+1}·m_{ta}−w_t·n_{ta}] + w_{t+1}·E[B_{t+1}|history,action]. The discarded expiry term is nonpositive, and unconsumed stock is nonnegative. Thus the total is at most lambda_t·K_t plus external-arrival term. Take expectations and telescope, bounding terminal payoff by w_T·S_T. This is weak duality, not a claim that any minimizing potential exactly represents the optimal policy. With deterministic envelopes one can minimize the displayed bound by a linear program in types, dates, represented action columns and resource prices; it avoids enumerating complete contingent policies but may still require difficult separation over joint actions. A relaxation optimizer need not be a lawful policy. Unlike the earlier fixed-pool cap, this upper admits genuinely new authorizations as long as their growth has a valid uniform bound.

**Economic recursion.** In the simpler stationary setting, each processed parent consumes one type-i token, produces children with conditional mean bounded by column i of a nonnegative M, has incremental receipt bounded by r_i>=0, and can optionally carry. For 0<beta<1 and spectral radius rho(beta M)<1, set w=(I−beta M^T)^(-1)r=sum_{k>=0}(beta M^T)^k r. The series converges by finite-dimensional matrix theory, is nonnegative and satisfies w=r+beta M^T w. Use date prices beta^t w: both action and carry inequalities hold with lambda=0. For each finite horizon the bound follows, and for nonnegative actual rewards the infinite sum follows by monotone convergence (for signed rewards assume convergence/integrability, or bound the positive parts with a nonnegative receipt envelope). Any independent bounded external arrivals enter with discounted prices. This is not an independent-offspring branching model: conditional mean dominance suffices even with dependent histories. A large individual source's outgoing multiplier is not the system reproduction rate; a type with two downstream opportunities feeding back only one-eighth has rho(M)=1/2 in the two-type off-diagonal model. Conversely, rho(beta M)>=1 merely defeats this particular unconstrained resolvent proof; finite horizon, saturation, rights expiry, procurement budgets and physical capacity can still bound value. If every date's incremental net positive receipt is capped by K and beta<1, K/(1−beta) bounds value regardless of reproduction. With beta=1 and no external inflow, rho(M)<1 still gives a finite bound on nonnegative receipts via the same finite-horizon telescope; indefinite carry cannot make a second receipt from one token.

The factor w_{t+1}·m is a potential for future production already included in the overall objective. It must never be added as a second premium to a solved production value. A change of data mixture, grounding, model or embodiment can alter consumption, reward, reproduction, external arrivals, resource capacity and observation legality simultaneously. A large reproduction coefficient is not sufficient to rank an investment.

## 3. Exact counterexample: the forced program suppresses its own descendants

One original cluster, two parent customer opportunities A and B; common baseline R=6. Grounding G replaces a date-0 retained contribution by one lower contribution, committed baseline 5. At date 1 the firm can verify neither, A, B, or both. Verification uses one scarce slot per parent (two are available) and displaces 1 of baseline per parent; the verified parent service subsequently earns incremental 1 per verified parent. This cancels the displacement exactly. All parent qualification, consent and paid outcomes are identical across the following common laws, even under forced both. Incremental child service slots are separate from the baseline and have zero omitted costs in the synthetic example. Any shared staff variant must subtract lost baseline.

At date 2 the joint verification schedule may create exactly two new authorized B-type successor orders, or none. These are not the original parent B. At most two child services are available and each, if performed, is accepted and paid 1 net. Each creates one distinct authorized C-type follow-up with permitted data lineage. At date 3 a shared worker permits at most one of those follow-ups, which pays 2 net. Reports of actual child availability and qualification precede their respective dispatches; no latent predecision information about the birth event is assumed. If the batch exists, optimal continuation performs both child services and one follow-up. No speculative revenue from unused authorizations.

Let q_A,q_B,q_AB be the probabilities of this two-child birth under the three joint verification actions, and q_empty=0. A common law of all potential outcomes exists: draw one uniform latent U before assignment and use the event U<=q_j for action j (the zero-probability boundary is immaterial). There is no access to U when choosing the verification menu. Different q values are structural action response, not independently selected worlds during a policy. Conditional on birth, all dated reports, qualifications and receipts specified above follow. Expected committed value of a fixed root action is 5+4q_j. Optional G therefore equals max(6,5+4 max_j q_j).

Low law (0,0,0), high law (1,0,0): all forced-AB histories, including actual authorized births, licensed report availability, customer payments and physical schedules, are identical. Yet the optional values are 6 and 9. An independent new embodiment E adds 1.5 to retained production, value 7.5, and thus ranks above G in the low law but below G in the high law. Mechanism: simultaneous verification can exhaust a customer/curation liaison's ability to arrange the next introductions even though initial paid parent services still finish. The high-law conditional response is an assumed mechanism, not evidence about a real fleet. A forced-program audit of zero descendants does NOT upper bound selective production. No post-selection latent oracle is needed: always choosing A works in the high law. Extra source X with cost 1 and incremental premium at most 1 cannot exceed R under the assumed independent menu; this is only a countermodel, not a general X ranking.

## 4. From a growth audit to a decision

Suppose a defensible uniform bound c on expected B-type child orders *per consumed parent* holds across all lawful root menus (including the joint menu), so 2q_A<=c, 2q_B<=c, 2q_AB<=2c. The parent net receipt is zero including displacement. Use w_C=2, w_B=1+2=3, parent potential 3c, in undiscounted units at the relevant dates. Carry prices can be chosen constant before the respective expiry. There are two initial parent tokens and no external arrivals. The typed potential gives G<=max(6,5+6c); no fixed final pool is assumed. At c=1/3, G<=7<7.5=E. This cap must hold for the newly generated orders under selective schedules, not merely under the forced AB intervention.

The two-child batch and one C slot imply an additional exact coupled constraint: the actual paid continuation from the two children is at most 4 rather than 6. Treat the batch as a composite opportunity worth 4 (two parented B plus jointly constrained C) or use the original-denominator relation C served<=B born/2 under the specified batch model. Then G<=max(6,5+4 min(1,c)). This bound is sharp over compatible laws: q_AB=min(1,c), q_A=q_B=0; the fixed AB rule attains the committed part. If a separate forced audit identifies q_AB=0, the sharper sharp envelope is max(6,5+4 min(1,c/2)). At c=1/3, the generic typed upper is 7, the batch upper is 19/3, and with forced audit plus the uniform cap it is 6. Do not apply the batch relation if the births can arrive singly, if an independent C service exists, or if child types consume different resources. In those cases the broader potential remains valid under its own conditions.

For a lower decision certificate, an independently supported single lawful A rule with q_A>5/8 achieves strictly more than E, with all costs, follow-up acceptance and shared C capacity as stipulated. If only q_AB is measured there is no inference about q_A without a genuine causal/structural bridge. If exogenous arrivals contribute extra value or the bound shifts with price, replace the corresponding envelope rather than using the previous threshold. The broad cap gives strict negative comparison for c<5/12; a verified batch relation gives it for c<5/8. These are synthetic thresholds, not calibration targets.

Free optional routine data P that exactly reproduces the G transition and menu without the lost initial contribution adds 1 to committed G. At q_A=1/2, q_B=q_AB=0, optional G=7, P=8; E=7.5. Exact transition equivalence includes induced customer orders, lineage rights, queue and qualification, not just identical raw sample counts. Free optional P cannot lower optimized value if old lawful policies remain executable. Forced costly training may. If E instead displaces 2 retained net units to provide its 1.5 increment, committed E=5.5 and optional E=6. Shared staff invalidate the assumed independent E comparison.

## 5. Identification, boundaries and prior foundations

The minimum useful audit is an original-cluster accounting of joint actions and distinct parent-child authorized order IDs, support before dispatch and joint realized accepted/paid service, including declined or inaccessible children. To establish a uniform growth cap, a signed structural issuance rule can limit all admissible parent/sibling contexts, or approved randomized/supported joint program contrasts can give simultaneous conditional bounds over a declared policy class. Aggregate forced-arm rates and isolated conditional technical qualification are not substitutes. If a candidate visits unmeasured contexts, carry an explicit conservative bound or leave the comparison unresolved. The matrix is not a claim that legal rights replicate; new authorization must be separately verified. See [evidence protocol](ENDOGENOUS_AUTHORIZATION_GROWTH_EVIDENCE.md).

The potential proof is standard linear Bellman super-solution/resource-price telescoping applied to an endogenous economic lineage stock. Finite LP and multi-type mean-matrix methods are prior foundations.
