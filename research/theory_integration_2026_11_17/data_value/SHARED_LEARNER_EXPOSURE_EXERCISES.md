# Exercises — shared learner and qualified source-policy exposure

1. Under P(GG)=1/2, P(XG)=P(GX)=1/4 and P(XX)=0, compute each slot's P(X) and explain why individual positivity does not support XX policy IPW.
2. Given J(GG)=6 and J(XG)=J(GX)=5, set J(XX)=z. Derive Gamma, both conditional X effects and the XX-vs-GG ranking. Classify integer z in [1,8].
3. Under two service gross ceilings 4 and 5, known program cost1 and certified preattempt old R=2, prove a conditional bound on net XX and identify precisely when the fallback lower bound fails.
4. Suppose signed whole networks are randomized to each of four profiles with p=1/4. Derive an unbiased HT contribution for XX and explain why pooled updates across nominal clusters invalidate it.
5. Give an example of economic source complementarity without technical learning complementarity, and explain which matched experiments can isolate field, recovery, teleoperation and simulation effects.
6. Show mathematically why free *optional* data cannot hurt the optimized production value when the feasible policy menu is unchanged. Does a forced expensive training program contradict this result?
