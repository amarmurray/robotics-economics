# Prospective original-customer demand and resource audit (NOT executed)
19 November 2026. No physical experiments, customer access, consent, purchases or outreach.

Declare a complete network catchment before policy selection: every site sharing controller updates, robot/operator pools, recovery dispatch and downstream buyer substitution. Fix the eligible customer population, including non-requesting accounts, and log **offered and refused** quote opportunities, varying quoted service price, competing specialists, canceled/unaccepted/newly induced orders and actually collected provider receipts. A selected accepted-order sample does not identify the price–volume slope. Track complete accepted-service quality, robot/controller lineage and every jointly qualified physical/operator/rights recipe. Separate field failure/recovery, teleoperation, sim, synthetic and public pretrained inputs, permissions and curation costs.

An actual test of D1 requires documented upper resource use by all prospective source/grounding/body recipes, full customer payment/margin span and verified capture/rights restrictions under X/G/E and shared demand. Any safe E expansion requires independent physical signoff and must not promise unavailable service. To discriminate the two X price worlds, preauthorized price/quote variation or an externally validated demand curve must identify **off-support** quantities; nonpurchase exposure may require sampling the eligible account register, not merely inbound leads. No hypothetical quote should be treated as collected cash.

Predeclare full-network estimands J_X−J_R, J_G−J_R, J_E−J_R, J_(X+E)−J_E and interaction J_(X+E)−J_X−J_E+J_R, including two-date continuation and operator/demand spillovers. Test D3 only when G's change in price intercept holds slopes, rights, human recovery resources and physical configuration stable. Any observed qualified customer receipt outside the proposed demand ceiling, source-caused new recipe beyond the cap, changed slope after G, or nonzero cross-cluster interference invalidates that particular certificate. Do not substitute a robotics-company announcement for a production panel or numeric BLS–BEA backbone.
