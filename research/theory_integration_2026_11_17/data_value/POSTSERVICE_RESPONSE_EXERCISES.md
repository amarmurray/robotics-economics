# Exercises: causal buyer response under qualified robotic service

These are student exercises; consult POSTSERVICE_RESPONSE_SOLUTIONS.md separately.

1. Derive the paid single-slot identity f=W[b(1-Y)D+v_C QZ−min(b,v_C)(1−Y)DQZ] from max{b(1−Y)D,v_CQZ}. Explain which term is a customer-budget effect and which is a physical capacity effect.

2. Derive the four-term decomposition (3) starting from full V_G−V_R. State conditions under which conditioning on early paid quality H permits transport without historical G assignments, and why the decomposition is not a uniquely identified natural indirect causal effect.

3. In the three-state synthetic witness, compute the R-transported value of fixed G-early. Let the free-history G conditional buyer laws lie within TV radius δ<=1/4 of independent fair D,Z under R. Derive the sharp value range, and show precisely why the blocked state's radius does not matter for terminal service.

4. E contributes 21/10, P and safe G-skip each 2. At δ=1/4 derive the G-early/E minimax-regret lottery chosen **before** observing the G buyer-response law, and prove E is maximin. Why is taking max{2,V_Gearly(world)} at each counterfactual world an invalid uninformed policy?

5. Construct two worlds with identical selected paid R invoices but different C original offers conditional on good early outcome. Set Q_R=0 and conditional G/R buyer-mechanism invariance. Compute the implied G-early paid-value difference and identify which lawful additional observation would resolve it. Explain why the result changes with a nonterminal t3 continuation.
