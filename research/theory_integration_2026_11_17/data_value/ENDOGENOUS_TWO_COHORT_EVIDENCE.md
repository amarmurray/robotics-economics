# Unpopulated evidence protocol: two-cohort endogenous acquisition and paid service

23 November 2026. Linked [conditional theory](ENDOGENOUS_TWO_COHORT_SOURCE_AND_DEMAND.md). **Not an authorization to use private customers, deploy machines, change training or acquire licensed data.**

**Program and causal unit.** An eligible closed robot/customer/worker cluster with one original customer-order stream and auditable physical recovery support. Register lawful P productive service (with subsequent field record only when executed), G deliberate typed source acquisition and target grounding, independently staffed E embodiment, and R retained fallback. Log randomization/assignment before outcomes; preserve positive program support within pre-action F/provenance stratum. If worker pools or buyer price/order behavior cross cluster boundaries, enlarge the cluster or report exposure; no sitewise independence by fiat.

**At the start:** original buyer offer/no-order opportunity, remaining budget, service deadline, actual arm/mobile/other embodiments, joint task recipe, F source identity and type, model/hardware version, intended customer quality requirements, ex-ante qualification, workers and outstanding jobs. Record legal rights separately for observation, retention, training, cross-site/model transfer, teleoperation and target redeployment, with expiry, revocation and exclusions.

**After randomized action:** H accepted early original service and paid receipt (including rejection/retry), whether a production-generated field trajectory/failure/recovery actually exists, T deliberate source acquisition assignment and result (including negative/blocked), model update and target validation version, compute/curation expense, cleared and new worker jobs and occupied time, original subsequent customer offer/no-offer/cancellation before dispatch, and matched complete target recipe acceptance and paid receipts at the deadline. Store both **original offers** and **delivered invoices**, not invoices alone. Avoid repeated clips from one fault as independent source records. Distinguish teleop, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained categories from field work rather than collapsing source modes into T.

**Two sequential causal tests.** (i) Within rights-approved P and G clusters stratified on F, audit F/T and target full-recipe qualification with held-out tests at matched budgets. Observe potential selection: P may generate a new field clip only if H=1. (ii) In every chosen G/market risk set record the *same-cluster* Q_G,D_G,W_G joint acceptance/payable event; separately record any t3 recovery work from attempted grounding and whether the customer originally ordered. A pairwise F/T association identifies late value only under independently defended G conditional buyer/worker invariance. Compare the factorized prediction Pr(Q)Pr(D)Pr(W) against measured joint coincidence and preregister falsification/coverage.

**Minimum welfare/accounting denominator.** Normalized expected paid contribution per eligible **original** order/cluster, including zero orders, unsafe/failed attempts, displaced qualified incumbent jobs, shadow prices for common workers, signed license, model curation/compute and embodied capital at comparable deployment dates. Do not double-count worker payroll plus its shadow value. Completion quality and cash receipts must be recorded; benchmark success cannot be priced directly. The E comparator requires independently verified actual staffing/build and qualification delay, not a scalar assumption.

**Identification/decision.** If a G arm has zero rights-approved assignment support, report it as unidentified. Source-only, accepted-only, unpaired original buyer, and selected recovery logs remain insufficient. Prospectively randomized complete cluster regimes can identify whole policy value without decomposing each data pathway; paired source and original-order/worker audits test the mechanistic model. Build a joint conditional confidence region for paid coincidence and compare the *entire program* to P/E/R only after causal support and resource price certification. Sequential audits may change policy/data supply; freeze timing and precommit decision rules. A report observed after dispatch cannot be treated as an early investment signal.

**Atlas mapping.** Candidate service denominators: original warehouse tote order, mobile replenishment request, workcell pick-and-place kit, digital-physical order release. In every sector track customer downstream value and outside process, worker occupation, alternative capacity and rights. Aggregate BEA sector-flow tables could contextualize output exposure if a versioned official numeric extract becomes available; they cannot infer robot task qualification. There is no verified numeric interindustry coefficient or real matched original-order/paid robot panel from this work.
