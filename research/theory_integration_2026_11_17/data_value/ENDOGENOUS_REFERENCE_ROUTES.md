## Economic premises and relation to the common system

A field trajectory, a laboratory reference, a target calibration and a diagnostic procedure are not identical goods. An available archive may be usable only after a local contrast; a new diagnostic can either provide that contrast without the archive or make the archive cheaper to interpret. Installing a sensor or a dedicated reference embodiment occupies capital, while installing and maintaining a diagnostic occupies dated engineer or compute resources. Neither guarantees complete accepted work. The firm chooses these means of acquiring qualified capability jointly with the next physical fleet, against ordinary operation that also learns. In particular, a more effective reference technology can reduce the private incentive to deploy a source robot while expanding the recipient market.

This extends the preceding [coupled evidence and capacity model](COUPLED_EVIDENCE_CAPACITY.md) from a fixed reference route and one prospective body to a shared optional setup, heterogeneous recipient investments and independently evolving source choices. ## 1. Qualified installation as a portfolio

There is a finite original roster j of candidate customer installations across end markets and embodiments. An admitted j uses one new body of its specified type, and earns net captured lifetime contribution v_j>0 after its body price and all common service costs. For tractability it has one installation window t no later than deadline T_j, with full contribution discounted by beta^t; there is no terminal reward for an uninstalled body. Each opportunity appears at most once, and all funded candidates are known, with no further binding resource except dated commissioning/qualification crew H_t. A crew slot cannot be used twice. Route-specific money costs are equal within j in the solved restriction; setup cost F is separate, paid once. In reality use the full costed schedule menu when routes differ. Accepted service, commissioned units and cash are separate outputs. v_j includes actual incremental captured service against the matched fallback, not the buyer's entire revenue.

At the end of a source shift, usable record e in {0,1} is observed lawfully. Ordinary operation can produce e without additional source investment. Each candidate has available stable external reference + commissioning duration a_j; a donor record permits a potentially faster grounded route b_j. A prepared new reference technology t=1 offers external and donor-conditioned routes c_j and d_j. It occupies setup s crew slots at the first date and costs F>=0, with reusable access for the remaining horizon. Setup must be completed before any first-window admission. Integer slots and simultaneous availability within a window suffice; jobs cannot span windows in this restriction. An unavailable route has infinite duration; using a new route is optional and old routes remain available after setup. Native qualification is a special case with commissioning-only duration. Different compatibility across recipients is represented by different route durations or absent routes. A digital diagnostic, a dedicated fixture or an additional reference embodiment can instantiate the new technology; do not assume their costs, physical outputs or ownership are equivalent. All routes meet the same safety and acceptance requirements before commissioning. Funding is already sufficient for the stated menu; if not, include dated funding and manufacturing constraints before maximizing.

The shortest admissible duration is

    h_j(e,t)=min{a_j, b_j if e=1, c_j if t=1, d_j if e=t=1}.

At a single decision window, gross value W_t(e) is the maximum over subsets S of sum_{j in S} v_j with sum h_j(e,t) <= H - t*s. The multiple-window extension allocates disjoint sets S_l under each dated crew budget, eligible deadlines, and the discounted objective. It is exact in the restriction because one can schedule the selected shortest routes sequentially inside the budget, and any feasible schedule uses at least their durations. It is not an LP approximation for indivisible bodies. Given observed e and enough setup capacity, the optimized value when the additional reference option is available is

    V(e)=max{W_0(e),W_1(e)-F}.

If setup does not fit, its branch is infeasible. The old feasible policy is retained; optional reference technology cannot lower V. In the more general model where the reference body itself produces paid work, add its incremental contribution and occupation on that same branch; count its commissioned type separately rather than treating it as free or as a recipient installation.

## 2. Exact reference-option interaction and source investment

Let G_e = W_1(e)-W_0(e) be the gross value of committing the reference setup, including its crew displacement but before the separate money cost. The old donor premium is D_0=W_0(1)-W_0(0). The new premium D_1=V(1)-V(0) obeys

    D_1-D_0 = [G_1-F]_+ - [G_0-F]_+.                 (R)

Proof: write V(e)=W_0(e)+[G_e-F]_+ and subtract. Positive part is monotone and 1-Lipschitz. Thus G_1>=G_0 implies D_1>=D_0 with increment at most G_1-G_0; reverse inequalities hold when G_1<=G_0. Strict interaction requires at least one of the larger gains to exceed F and G_1 != G_0. Where both are below F the technology is irrelevant. Optional evidence itself preserves old schedules, so D_0,D_1>=0 even when (R) is negative. A shorter route is not itself evidence of complementarity: setup may consume the slots it saves, and the reference may be used only in histories where the donor is absent. One cannot identify G_1-G_0 by comparing only the duration of one route at one target.

Now choose before e is observed whether to add one source body. In the controlled restriction the only downstream effect is to change the usable record probability from p_0 to p_1>p_0; ordinary capture remains p_0>0. Its incremental direct net cost k includes source acquisition and current net service difference, once. Hold recipient demand, cash, crew, evidence rights and conditional state fixed; beta discounts from the source to recipient decision. The source body is purchased iff beta(p_1-p_0)D_r>k (ties retain old source) in reference regime r. This follows by taking expected optimized value differences. Hence an available alternative reference can reverse source entry in either direction. If setup must be committed before e rather than after, the exact additional expected value is [E G_e-F]_+, not E[G_e-F]_+; that latter is the contingent-choice value. The source effect then differences the former expression across p_1,p_0. Both alternatives can condition on lawfully available information and can use the old routes. No perfect hindsight. More general source changes in H, demand, rights or native readiness require independently optimized laws J_1,J_0; the scalar p effect is a controlled special case, not a mediated causal estimate.

## 3. Endogenous signed first-generation response

For each recipient type j and installation date l, let n^r_jl(e) count the selected commissioned opportunities at the optimized decision, with a predeclared deterministic tie rule or report the range over ties. The first-generation response to one extra source body in the controlled restriction is

    g^r_jl=(p_1-p_0)(n^r_jl(1)-n^r_jl(0)).

This follows from differences of conditional expectations, with no descendants attributed yet. More general g is E_1[n_jl]-E_0[n_jl] under the two evolving programs. Distinguish forced added source capital (a finite policy impulse) from whether it is actually selected. Because the maximizing subsets can differ, g has no positive sign restriction even when D_r>=0. The same route shortening can reallocate an existing crew from many smaller to one larger opportunity. A pure measurement of physical MPK at fixed commissioned stock, qualifications, demand and a specified dispatch rule remains separate. Installation counts are not accepted service units; private net value is not necessarily real surplus. If customer/rival prices move or ownership assigns different shares, replace v by the appropriate captured net values for that decision, and calculate real surplus with transfers canceled on the resulting allocation.

An exact portfolio test for a new reference design is whether some feasible post-setup schedule earns more than the best ordinary schedule plus F, conditional on lawful histories. This tests digital inquiry, grounding and embodied reference assets against the same scarce capacity and opportunity menu. With only one divisible dated input, identical proportional time compression for all eligible positive-valued opportunities, no setup occupation and no fee, the value-maximizing fractional schedule orders projects by unchanged value/time density (fix tie order). It consumes a prefix, and compression increases the uncompressed crew budget, extending that prefix weakly. Every type's admitted quantity is nondecreasing under that tie convention. Proof: exchange any lower-density allocated mass with equal input at a higher-density unsaturated opportunity. This yields the ordered fractional prefix, with truncation at total demand. The negative response therefore requires a break in the conditions, e.g. heterogeneous compression, setup occupation, indivisible starts, multiple constraints or changing demand. It does not follow from universal technological progress alone.

## 4. Constructive mechanism comparisons

All quantities synthetic, not commercial data. One window H=4. Two type-A candidates each earn 4 and require 2 crew slots. One type-B candidate earns 12 and needs 5 via stable external reference. The new diagnostic costs 1 and occupies one slot before any commissioning. A routes are unchanged. All v include the actual body outlay; there is no duplicate data premium. Assume the ordinary source generates a usable donor with probability 1/4, and an added source body changes that to 3/4. Source current net costs below differ between experiments only to put each demonstrated switch strictly inside the analytically established interval.

| Reference technology | B duration without / with donor | No-donor optimized value (A,B) | Donor optimized value (A,B) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Old only, substitute case | 5 / 3 | 8 (2,0) | 12 (0,1) |
| Optional new standalone diagnostic | 3 / 3 after setup | 11 (0,1) | 12 (0,1), old route |
| Old only, complement case | 5 / 4 | 8 (2,0) | 12 (0,1) |
| Optional new archive-grounding diagnostic | 4 / 1 after setup | 8 (2,0), old route | 15 (1,1) |

In the substitute case the donor premium falls from 4 to 1. At k=1.5 and beta=1, the extra source net return falls from +0.5 to -1. The extra source changes the recipient installation vector by (-1,+0.5) with only the old route, and (0,0) when the new option is available. Yet even without an added source body the new option increases the B installation rate from 1/4 to 1, displacing A. The optimal program switches from buying to not buying the additional source. Its total value increases from 9.5 to 11.25; its expected number of recipient bodies falls from 1.25 to 1. The new reference is digital in this illustration, not counted as a commissioned robot.

In the complement case the donor premium rises from 4 to 7. At k=2.5, the extra source net return rises from -0.5 to +1; the optimal program begins purchasing it. The controlled recipient response changes from (-1,+0.5) to (-0.5,+0.5). The new diagnostic cannot make B fit without the donor; together they free enough slots to preserve one A alongside B. This isolates an archive-conditioned technical relationship, not simply a new parameter for an always-positive transfer rate. In both cases the option value calculation includes the best ordinary contingent response. In particular, if the same useful contrast can be obtained externally at identical total resource and money cost, a duplicate reference has no gross advantage.

## 5. Acceleration is not extra adoption

For the substitute reference technology, now isolate B with two crew windows (4,5), e=0 and beta=0.8. When B persists through both windows, old reference produces one installation in the second, worth 9.6; the prepared diagnostic produces one in the first, worth 11 after the fee. Thus the dated difference is (+1,-1) for B, but its sum is zero. When B expires at the first window, old value/installation are zero, while the diagnostic still earns 11 and adds one B; the dated difference is (+1,0). This is a conditional comparison between separately optimized programs with the same original offer and deadlines within each case. In the full law, retain opportunity identity and report cumulative commissioning by date. The sum of signed dated differences is the final cumulative difference over that roster; equal totals do not establish same customers or same service histories. Follow through catch-up when possible, and record expiry and unfulfilled orders. A signed displacement kernel is not the positive constant reproduction matrix in the common paper. Do not apply the latter's Perron threshold to the signed vector.
