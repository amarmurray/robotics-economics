## Routine-data emulation as a falsifiable null

For every G policy suppose a feasible P policy can be constructed using only lawful P histories, uniformly in m, with a coupling of their full histories under each m. Let tau be the first date of economically relevant emulation failure (missing qualified recipe, too-late report, unavailable rights, worker, altered buyer response); tau=T+1 for no failure. P must be feasible on all histories including after failure. The coupling is a proof device, not operational foresight. Suppose the discounted cumulative gross disadvantage of P strictly before tau is bounded by A, possibly random; on tau<=T the remaining gross difference discounted to tau is bounded by nonnegative B_tau. Include terminal value at T and all payoffs in the prefix if tau=T+1. Then

    J_G(pi_G,m)-J_P(pi_P,m)
      <= E_m[A + 1{tau<=T} beta^tau B_tau] - (c_G-c_P).        (1)

Proof: pathwise split the discounted sum at tau; use the two bounds, take expectations and subtract committed cost difference. If the expectation is uniformly <= epsilon over G policies and m, take sup to obtain V_G(m)-V_P(m)<=epsilon-(c_G-c_P). Uniform emulation also implies sup_piP inf_m J_P >= sup_piG inf_m J_G + c_G-c_P-epsilon: apply (1) to each fixed G policy and its common P emulator, then take infimum and supremum. If emulation only holds with a law-specific P policy, this latter robust implementability implication is invalid.

If prefix loss <=a and suffix bound b_t on tau=t, epsilon<=a+sum_t beta^t b_t Pr(tau=t) under a uniform bound on that expression. This is the economically weighted first failure of emulation, not merely probability of technical mismatch. Exact emulation with zero loss makes extra paid grounding redundant. Different data modalities can emulate each other's economic opportunities despite different technical distributions. Conversely identical eventual qualification or marginal readiness does not prove emulation. Free optional data preserve feasible gross optimum by menu inclusion, not positive transfer in all training recipes. A late technically identical dataset can fail to emulate an early one. Fixed identical physical/menu/rights dynamics and causal garbling of P reports to reproduce G's conditional report process give the familiar information-order sufficient condition, but not when the report acquisition changes the physical or buyer state. Safe fallback must exist, not be assumed. This elementary stopping decomposition is not a novelty claim; its proposed economic use is to test a routine-data rival on whole qualified paid production.

## Sharp restricted countermodel

Two dates after a common retained production prefix whose value cancels. One paid program slot is available; each retains optional P. P obtains routine data with zero incremental acquisition/training/curation cost in this stylized model. All programs ultimately qualify the same target recipe; P's jointly licensed validated controller is ready before the date-1 customer deadline only when L=1. H=1 denotes an original distinct authorization for net 8 conditional on actual qualified on-time service. L,H observed before dispatch; a free safe worker and no competing use are stipulated. G costs 1 and makes the recipe ready at the deadline in every history, without changing H. X adds a distinct net contribution 1 beyond P after costs; E adds 2 beyond P with its own staffing and buyer budget. No simultaneous paid programs allowed; no duplicate sale.

With h=Pr(H), l=Pr(L), j=Pr(H,L):
    P=8j, G=8h-1, X=8j+1, E=8j+2,
    G-P=8(h-j)-1, G-E=8(h-j)-3.

The emulator dispatches when L=1, otherwise foregoes H. Failure event H=1,L=0, not all late data. Equation (1) is tight with beta=1, A=0, B=8. Four atoms have probabilities (1-h-l+j,h-j,l-j,j); j ranges sharply from max(0,h+l-1) to min(h,l). For h=l=1/2, j=0 gives P=0,G=3,X=1,E=2; j=1/2 gives P=4,G=3,X=5,E=6. G beats E iff j<1/8 and P iff j<3/8; equality ties. Equal eventual technical transfer, marginal readiness and marginal demand do not determine ranking. Optional G means overall optimum cannot fall ex ante. If G changes authorizations, E borrows existing worker, or P incurs cost, recompute full J; these formulas are not transportable by default.
