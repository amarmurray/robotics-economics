# Exercises: economic data value

Attempt before reading SOLUTIONS.md. These are mathematical exercises, not empirical robotics claims.

1. Let a qualification's success probability be p_i=1-exp(-sum_s T_is n_s). Explain why multiplying T_is by the isolated profit from qualification i is generally insufficient to value source s. Derive the correct conditional marginal term.
2. Compare an accepted service worth 1 requiring both qualifications with a service that either qualification can supply. Give their value tables and the sign of the economic interaction. Can identical marginal qualification probabilities price the two production systems identically?
3. In the two-source model with epsilon=1/4 and budget R=2, derive the optimal mixture when the incumbent contribution u is zero, then when u/v=1/2. What happens as u/v grows? Distinguish this objective from maximizing the average qualification probability.
4. Show why increasing epsilon may reduce source diversity despite improving both sources' technical usefulness. What breaks at epsilon=1?
5. Replace the learning curve with p=z/(1+z). Derive the global optimum using L_1+L_2=H. Which conclusion survives and which numerical threshold should not be reused?
6. Write a source-data versus target-grounding factorial experiment. What must stay fixed to isolate technical complementarity? How would a separate customer-demand comparison reveal economic complementarity? Explain why four sets of average benchmark scores do not necessarily identify joint accepted-service value.
