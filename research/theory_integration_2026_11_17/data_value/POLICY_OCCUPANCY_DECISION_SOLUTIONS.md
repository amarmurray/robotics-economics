2. Sufficiency for the optimal policy concerns **reached histories and chosen actions**, not equality of every unused action's return or transition. At t=0 choose a paid pilot that discloses A/B for t=1; both policies choose pilot. At t=1 SG's optimal a for A and b for B disagree inside a report-discarding row, creating nonzero regret. A stronger uniformly Markov-equivalent quotient is sufficient but unnecessary for that original market and policy.
