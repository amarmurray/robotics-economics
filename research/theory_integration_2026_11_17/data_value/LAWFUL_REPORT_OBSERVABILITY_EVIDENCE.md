# Prospective original-buyer protocol: lawful predispatch telemetry, paid service, and shared learning

**22 November 2026. Planning only.** No customer contact, collection, deployment, confidential records, purchases or robot-control changes authorized.

**One linked, original opportunity unit.** Eligible original-buyer offered orders and observed no-orders (market denominator), timestamped actual order and quoted accepted service/SLA, original prices/discounts, attempted qualified robot recipe, accepted/rejected/refunded completion, final cleared settlement, cost and terminal customer/producer contribution. Do not select only successful trajectories or only customers who chose a pilot.

**Distinguish data lineage and legal functions.** For each checkpoint: production field success; failure/recovery; teleoperation; licensed simulation; synthetic; public/pretrained. Preserve site/customer rights to record, retain, train, pool, transfer, disclose *and use the report for dispatch*. Record revocation and time/version changes. A stored bytes field with no dispatch-use right is excluded from the lawful z map. Unobserved customer intent is not an available signal.

**Before-action information architecture.** Observational event ledger must distinguish buyer order/offer at t0, physical sensing at t1, checkpoint qualification at t2, legal authorization at t3, controller/operator receipt at t4 and actual dispatch at t5 (actual event order may differ). A report arriving after t5 cannot refine the earlier dispatch. A legally unusable signal cannot form an atom. Physical menus M(h), signed controller IDs, embodiment capacity, recovery and operator/coordination limits must be frozen to the decision epoch.

**Arm-matched Q and decision support.** Estimate or bound joint accepted-and-paid *whole-horizon* Q for R,S,G,SG,E, including alternative lawful actions, canceled work, rescue, denied orders, displaced productive operator/robot time, maintenance, and future paid services learned via *permitted* production data. Do not infer unsupported actions as zero or impute payment from simulation scores. Where some actions lack outcomes, retain a joint feasible set Θ of buyer/learner/rights/settlement kernels; do not present fabricated independent confidence intervals as verified. Estimate reported signal z under each candidate regime before making claims of statistical or causal identification.

**Three design diagnostics.** Compare (a) unrestricted action-cover oracle, (b) lawful observation atoms and their irreducible common-argmax regret, and (c) feasible reporting/rights acquisition net of cost. A coarsened report might be sufficient under R but insufficient under SG when new recipes become usable. Check whether target-report value can be realized with existing assets and whether E avoids that information bottleneck at additional capital/operator/capacity cost. An actual licensed additional signal is a legally nested intervention only when the old rule may still run unchanged.

**When fixed-stage theory fails.** If reporting changes order probabilities, consent, future checkpoint training, operator scheduling or follow-on service, use endogenous Model B whole-policy J and occupancy—not static W(z). Physical SG/E and buyer Q interventions require authorization not presently held. The original eligible population and joint network effects must be stable or explicitly modeled. Never double-count learned continuation.

**Missing at this writing.** No approved or paid SG/E original buyer study, predispatch legal signal ledger, matched conditional whole-paid Q, original-buyer no-order cohort, legal sensor disclosure rules, signed shared-learner interference law, or numerical BEA/BLS interindustry input-output coefficient. BLS 2025 sector chained output exists separately as official *context*, not an industry-to-industry requirements matrix.

**Falsifiable discrimination.** The legal sensor may be physically predictive and still unable to change paid production if the actual dispatch actor cannot use it. Target report can generate value absent additional training; source learning can create decision-use for a signal previously redundant under R; a generalist E might lower the signal's opportunity value. These require jointly matched buyer paid Q and authorized information, not model accuracy scores.
