# Solutions: reserve contingency

1. max(B,K)-B=(K-B)_+ pointwise; integrate then subtract the fixed cost k, irrespective of call frequency. If idle reserve changes B or report distribution, the formula requires an explicit drift term.
2. Put S_s mass entirely on normal paths (j=0), or entirely on overload (j=1/4); D_s mass 1/4 can be placed anywhere on normal paths without further restrictions. G=11/4 or 15/4; GK=21/8 or 23/8; R+K=25/8; E=3. Low chooses reserve, high chooses G. All values are net increments over R=4.
3. S gives 5+4h versus 8, so h>3/4. The forced marginal is a mixture over both report cells, not the conditional h. Record both original report and eligible successor under an assigned safe S, or the original-denominator joint selected outcome.
4. A trained body can qualify a previously inaccessible order worth 5 only with a reserve worker; old body cannot qualify even with reserve. Neither input alone earns the new receipt, combined earns 5 before costs. Shared reserve requires overlapping call timing, feasible assignment, actual response, displaced orders and action-specific licensed successors, not independent call means.
