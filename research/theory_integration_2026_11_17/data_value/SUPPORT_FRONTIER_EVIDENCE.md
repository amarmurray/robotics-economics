21 November 2026. Nothing here authorizes a trial, customer access, purchased training, or public research publication.

**Unit.** Start with original eligible offered customer orders including declined, failed and zero orders; trace checkpoint lineage and rights from field success/failure/recovery F, approved teleoperation T, simulation/synthetic M, public/pretrained input, deliberate training and later authorized production-generated information. Attach whole-network robots/embodiments, human operator allocation and recoveries, acceptance, refunds, finally cleared payment and end-market price/order responses. Declare full horizon, cohort, all shared learner/customer/operator ancestors and real counterfactual exposure.

**Policy menu.** Enumerate whole physically and legally permissible strategies, not controller buttons: SG safe, SG source plus grounding, E embodiment permitted actions, fallback after failure, dynamic learner upgrades, interactions with production data, other buyer opportunities and scarce manufacturing resources. Unsupported legal policy is NOT physically illegal, and an untested controller is not automatically admissible. Confirm actual signatures, licenses, safety and controller rollback rights before any field execution.

**Known-floor evidence.** For each genuinely tested policy seek matched original-buyer accepted-and-paid contribution with credible sampling/assignment support; track unsupported histories separately. Reuse policy-specific Q-reference full-event occupancy bounds only with independently valid P/Q kernel measurements and complete market transport.

**Unknown-policy ceiling.** Record signed full-horizon eligible order caps d; maximal capturable paid unit values v net of discounts/refunds; robot, operator, manufacturing and coordination resource b, per-service consumption A; rights and unavoidable costs c_min; and newly learned embodiments/recipes that can emerge during the horizon. An LP dual multiplier is an arithmetic upper certificate only if every possible legal strategy's production lives inside the verified constraint set. Do not assume fixed demand where it reacts to deployment quality.

**Falsifiers.** A lawful post-failure rescue, new future data-enabled customer service, additional operator supply, source-rights change, new embodiment capacity, missing buyer orders, program-induced demand shifts or global learner interference destroys a static or isolated cap. Recompute or leave the decision partially identified.
