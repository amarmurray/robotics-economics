# Instructor solutions — networks, grounding and economically qualified production

**Keep separate from [student exercises](POLICY_CLOSED_NETWORKS_EXERCISES.md).** All values are normalized synthetic complete customer contribution, and all causal claims are conditional.

1. Status-quo undirected connected components are \(\{A,B\},\{C\},\{D\}\). Unioning approved-policy potential dispatch edges produces \(\{A,B,C\},\{D\}\). Under G, B can move an order to or away from C, so separate randomization of C may alter the measured A/B policy outcome. Overinclusive union components cost statistical power; underinclusive components invalidate consistency.
2. For each component i, random assignment independent of its fixed full-policy potential outcomes gives \(E[1\{Z_i=p\}Y_i(p)/\pi_{i,p}]=Y_i(p)\); subtract q and sum. The expectation argument does not need independent exogenous outcomes across clusters, but statistical inference, external population transport, regime-specific bounds and operator safety require separate assumptions. Missing true cross-cluster policy paths invalidate the potential-outcome simplification.
3. Difference \(=(7+B-1/2)-(5+5)=B-7/2\), so [-7/2,13/2], sharp over unrestricted B in [0,10]. Under verified isolation B=5, the difference is +3/2. An asserted controller-only cut is insufficient because demand, dispatch or operator links can change B's accepted production.
