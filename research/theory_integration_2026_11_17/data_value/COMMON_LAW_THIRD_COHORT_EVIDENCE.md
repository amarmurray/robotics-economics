# Prospective evidence: action-dependent G/K buyer renewal and worker occupancy

**Unpopulated protocol, not approval to run an experiment or access confidential customer data.** Based on [the theorem](COMMON_LAW_ACTION_DEPENDENT_THIRD_COHORT.md).

The experimental unit must be an original, budget-valid customer order plus *all* workers and robots sharing its recovery/dispatch queue. Pre-register legally supportable R/G/K/GK/E assignment, the old fallback, the independently staffed E's actual readiness, and whether checkpoints can coexist. Require the signed right to observe, retain, train on, validate and redeploy each failure-origin G and recovery-origin K datum; refused/expired rights exclude a treatment. Log source lineage, task embodiment, data collection controller, training/curation/compute and recovery time separately; do not combine teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained, field failure and recovery clips into a generic human-data count.

Observe the first attempted **accepted** service, original ticket id, authorized budget, receipt or quality rejection and post-failure job creation. Record repair initiation and completion, worker availability before/after learning, occupied operator minutes, new jobs induced by service, late original buyer offers **including no-orders**, acceptance criteria, actual invoice and budget decrement. Never classify an original ticket's repeat exposure as a new payable buyer. Capture both source-specific and *complete recipe* joint qualification at the deadline with matched hardware, validation horizons and model checkpoints. Preserve source-missing-by-construction on uncleared repairs.

In the limited theorem, the additional decision-sufficient joined statistic is Pr(G-qualified, K-unqualified, K worker available | original failure): the conditioning set is not a sample selected on accepted invoices or cleared workers. In real program-dependent responses, a matched H/T technical test is insufficient. Either establish and challenge the restrictions that buyer demand from G and worker occupancy from K transport to GK, or obtain lawful **whole GK regime** support and jointly register original buyer, repair job, worker state, G/K qualification and paid output. Recording only treatment marginals or G-only service data cannot identify the missing common-law overlap.

Prospective cluster assignment or justified natural assignment with measured pre-treatment confounders and interference boundaries is required for causal claims. Monitor adverse events and obtain actual physical/customer authorization before any study. Estimate direct whole-policy effects and incremental decomposition separately. Without that support, report the set of compatible common causal laws and a robust decision bound, not an observed gain from robot learning. No signed permissions, source-to-qualification experiment, matched paid buyer panel or independently staffed E evidence exists in this repository.
