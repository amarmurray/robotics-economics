# Proof / negative-result ledger

1. Distinguish static factorial arm means, unobserved cross-policy qualification overlap t, endogenous after-source technical r, and the **eligible complete-policy continuation value** delta. A direct stage-two randomized economic design identifies the last only, without making t/r observable.
2. With common approved first stage, pre-random history H and eligibility D,E, random B and complete consistency, E[Y|B=b,H,D=E=1]=E[Y(b)|H,D=E=1]. Integrate over eligible H; policies identical elsewhere imply population effect pi*delta. Classical dynamic treatment regimes, not a new general theorem.
3. The observational unit is the full connected potential intervention network. No cross-unit interference is needed; intra-network customer demand/order and operator response are **inside Y**. If there is one single shared dispatch/learner/customer system, independent cluster design cannot be asserted.
4. For bounded independent arm outcomes, a Hoeffding deviation R sqrt(log(4/alpha)/(2n_b)) has probability at most alpha/2. Union bound and triangle give the sum radius. The finite-population assignment enumeration in the executable verifies unbiased mean algebra, **not** the independent-superpopulation confidence result.
5. Synthetic net: Y0=2, Y1=1.4+2Q, so r=3/4 -> +9/10 and r=1/5 -> -1/5 conditional; with pi=1/2, +9/20 or -1/10 population. A qualified old policy is the default; cost/delay of unsuccessfully *testing* a grounded policy is allowed to destroy value, despite retaining that controller.
6. Direct economic randomization neither identifies separate technical causality nor an untested changed embodiment's value, and cannot extrapolate to physically ineligible site histories. Prior art: Murphy (2003), Hoeffding, Frechet bounds, DDA (2026), operating duality and process flexibility. No novelty, safety or field estimate is claimed.
