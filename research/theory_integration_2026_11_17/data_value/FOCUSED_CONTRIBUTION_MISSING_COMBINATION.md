# When Capabilities Become Production — focused contribution and missing-combination certificate
24 November 2026 Eastern. Compact first-paper candidate. Historical manuscript and broader agenda retained; scientific gate remains open.

## Abstract and question
When should a robotic producer deliberately acquire data, adapt a target, reorganize existing production, or acquire a new embodiment? Data has economic value when its use changes attainable customer-accepted production or its continuation, not merely a technical benchmark. A lawful routine-learning program is a competing investment, not a frozen baseline. Reorganization can either displace deliberate grounding by generating useful routine trajectories or complement it by releasing scarce resources. We give conditional decision certificates, exact counterexamples and a prospective whole-program design; no commercial causal estimate or independent novelty claim.

## 1. Common production and learning object
Model A optimizes accepted net contribution over jointly qualified recipes. In the divisible fixed-menu special case maximize v(h)'x subject to A(h)x<=K(h), B(h)x<=d(h), x>=0, where A contains embodied, worker, digital coordination, compute and other dated resource use; B contains original buyer procurement/demand rows. Omit forbidden recipes. Net settlements and incremental costs, not gross merchandise value, define v. Dual nonnegative multipliers give valid resource/buyer upper certificates; indivisibility, queue carryover and deadline constraints may require a time-expanded or integer feasible set.

Model B chooses complete programs R retained, P optional routine reuse, X additional source, G target grounding, C coordination change with independently evolving routine learner, E new embodiment/access, and lawful combinations. J_g(pi,m)=E_m^{g,pi}[sum_t beta^t r_t + beta^T phi_T]-c_g for feasible nonanticipating policy pi and controlled law m of original opportunities, qualifications, resources, rights and source histories. r_t is accepted net paid service, phi_T distinct successor production, c_g committed incremental costs. V_g(m)=sup_pi J_g(pi,m). No double-counted data premium. Distinguish field trajectories, recovery/failure, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained inputs. Transfer is an endogenous joint qualification law, not a universally positive fixed matrix. New bodies alter both executable recipes and future source distributions. Demand, competition, staffing and manufacturing can change the controlled law. See [policy-closed formulation](POLICY_CLOSED_DATA_VALUE.md) and [broader theory](DATA_VALUE_THEORY.md).

**Result A — economic emulation.** If every G policy has a feasible lawful P (or C) emulator with discounted gross loss uniformly bounded by epsilon over supported laws, V_G-V_P (or V_G-V_C) is at most epsilon less incremental cost difference. Proof: subtract the costs and take the supremum after the pointwise emulator inequality. A law-independent emulator also supports robust optimization; a law-specific one does not. The earlier stopping decomposition builds epsilon from the first economically consequential emulation failure. Free optional data retaining old feasible policies with no added obligation weakly expand gross optimum; forced training can regress. Neither a benchmark nor an eventual checkpoint match proves emulation.

**Result B — competing coordination mechanisms.** In the restricted two-date model in [coordination and routine exposure](COORDINATION_ROUTINE_MODEL_AB.md), H denotes authorization, L baseline timely readiness, S additional C-induced routine readiness, T additional G readiness, W0<=W1 usable capacity. For a single target of contribution v>=0, let R=H(1-L)T. With additive costs and no demand drift, CG-C-G+P = v E[R(W1-W0)(1-S)] - v E[R W0 S]. The first term values newly usable capacity still lacking routine qualification; the second is redundant grounding on already feasible service. The economic overlap, not a technical transfer coefficient, determines complementarity.

## 2. Result C — sharp missing-CG decision bounds without measuring latent R
Suppose P,G,C have been independently followed but CG is unavailable. Maintain Result B's structural restrictions: common original-order law, no competing target orders in the service window, stable authorization and nonnegative policy-invariant contribution, W0<=W1, observed timely readiness before dispatch, OR-composition of L,S,T. All programs are lawful and physically feasible; no demand, cost or qualification interaction beyond those stated. This is NOT implied by free optional data, randomization or monotonic average skill.

Let F0={H W0=1}, F1={H W1=1}. F0 is a subset of F1. Paid-service events:
 A_P=F0 intersect L;
 A_G=F0 intersect (L union T);
 A_C=F1 intersect (L union S);
 A_CG=F1 intersect (L union S union T).
Let p,g,c,z be their masses; f0,f1 are the F masses; d=f1-f0. Use probability and common v, or the weighted measure mu(A)=E[v(omega)1_A] for common nonnegative contributions (then omit scalar v below). Feasibility requires 0<=p<=g<=f0<=f1 and p<=c<=f1. The sharp identified interval over unrestricted divisible measures is

    max(g,c) <= z <= min(f1,c+g-p+d).                 (3)
    max(0,g-c) <= z-c <= min(f1-c,g-p+d).             (4)

Proof. CG contains G and C. Inside F0, newly added G service beyond C must be in A_G minus A_P; outside F0 it has mass at most d. Total cannot exceed f1. For sharpness, let u=mu(A_C intersect F0) in [max(p,c-d),min(f0,c)]. The two sets A_C intersect F0 and A_G both contain A_P; outside A_P they can nest or overlap minimally. Newly added service inside F0 ranges from max(0,g-u) to min(g-p,f0-u). Outside F0 it ranges independently from zero to d-c+u because T there is not constrained by the G-only observation. Thus

    max(0,g-u) <= z-c <= min(g-p,f0-u)+d-c+u.        (5)

Both the minimum lower endpoint and maximum upper endpoint occur at u=min(f0,c); substituting gives (4). Represent each construction by setting L=A_P, S=A_C minus A_P, T=A_G minus A_P plus the desired additional outside-F0 grounding events, and unobserved bits zero. Convex mixtures preserve the moments and reach intermediate points. Equal-weight finite count grids attain integer endpoints; unequal indivisible weights can make these bounds conservative. Negative contributions, endogenous demand or additional cross-program interactions require the complete J or justified alternative restrictions.

With additive incremental grounding cost k after C, no other changed payoffs or continuation, J_CG-J_C is v times (4) minus k. Reject if the upper endpoint is negative; adopt relative to C if the lower endpoint is positive. For full-menu adoption compare to every rival, including X/E, using complete values or valid upper bounds. Bounded costs can be subtracted at appropriate extrema (sharp only if jointly feasible).

The economic interaction interval, in probability units before common v scaling, is

    p-min(g,c) <= (CG-C-G+P)/v <= min(d,f1-c-g+p).   (6)

The lower bound is nonpositive: three program marginals plus ceilings cannot establish strictly positive complementarity under this model. They can establish strict negative complementarity if c+g-p>f1. If d=0, interaction is weakly negative. This is a qualitative structural test, not a new calibration. Unlike the earlier R-conditional result, the missing-arm certificate uses arm-level original-order acceptance and capacity ceilings. A valid baseline-capacity classification u measured in C histories can tighten (5), but a latent counterfactual must not be labeled observed.

## 3. Matched-marginal reversal
Eight equal-mass histories, v=8. f0=6/8,f1=1,p=2/8,g=4/8,c=5/8. CG gross contribution ranges from 5 to 8. With k=1.5 and C early net contribution zero, P=2,G=2.5,C=5,CG in [3.5,6.5]. Synthetic independently staffed alternatives X=4,E=4.5 do not change the reversal: C wins at one endpoint, CG at the other. Both stored witnesses have u=4/8; even knowing this stratum need not identify the missing combination. The executable enumerates the nine admissible event types, all count compositions through ten equally weighted histories, checks both interval formulas and every feasible integer point. Numerical agreement is not proof. No actual vendor or dollar figures enter this example.

Preferred test: prospective cluster assignment of independently evolving lawful P/G/C/CG to comparable original opportunity catchments, common starting checkpoints and separate later histories. Include X/E where feasible. Freeze C's coordination algorithm, not the ensuing learner. A frozen-controller diagnostic can distinguish immediate mechanical release from exposure but is not the C economic rival. Record original opportunity/refusal/no-service, accepted receipts, deadline, dated worker/body/compute occupation, recovery and downstream acceptance, source rights and lineage, training/curation and all costs in the [compact validation protocol](COMPACT_WHOLE_PROGRAM_VALIDATION.md).
