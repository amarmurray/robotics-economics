# Student exercises — original-network exposure and economic data value

Read [the chapter](JOINT_NETWORK_POLICY_SUPPORT_AND_DATA_VALUE.md) before attempting; instructor [solutions](JOINT_NETWORK_SOLUTIONS.md) are separate. All money units are **synthetic**.

1. Two original orders A/B have contributions/prices 4/3. A parent budget is 6 or 8 and operators have capacity2. R uses2 units per service, G uses1. Derive whole-network RR/RG/GR/GG feasible captured contribution, and explain why G-A and G-B signatures alone do not imply GG admission.
2. In H the parent with budget8 buys both G; in L it vetoes B only on the GG menu. All RR/RG/GR/E logs and prices are the same. Derive GG, E and RR means for equal budget frequencies and show the conditional sharp optional-GG gross interval; list assumptions needed for sharpness.
3. Randomize only RG/GR with equal probability. Derive per-order G propensity versus **joint GG** propensity. Prove observational equivalence of H/L even with arbitrarily many such shifts under the stated kernels.
4. Suppose whole RR, GG, E are actually signed and assigned independently per original network with probability 1/3. Derive a correct inverse-propensity finite-population GG−E contrast and its variance under no between-network interference. What does it not prove about physical safety or estimation precision?
5. Under prior H/L each 1/2, E net9/2 and G certification cost1/2, derive the gross value of a perfectly binding preinvestment GG-buyer signal. Explain why more R-only trajectories, nonbinding survey and double-counting J are invalid replacements.
