# Prospective evidence protocol — complete-policy network closure and economic data usefulness

**No live robot, approved intervention, private customer data or target-fleet architecture obtained on 18 Nov 2026.** This is the smallest falsifiable measurement and decision protocol behind [policy-closed theory](POLICY_CLOSED_NETWORKS_AND_DATA_VALUE.md). The earlier [safe-support protocol](SAFE_POLICY_SUPPORT_EMPIRICAL.md) remains valid under its isolation assumptions.

## A. Architecture and enrollment audit before any allocation

Create versioned *pre-assignment* records: \`market_id\`, \`candidate_supernetwork_id\`, \`unit_id\`, \`controller_checkpoint_digest\`, \`training_lineage_id\`, \`data_access_rights\`, \`source_type\`, \`broadcast_subscribers\`, \`robot_embodiment\`, \`dispatch_namespace\`, \`task_bid_competitors\`, \`shared_operator_reservation_group\`, \`customer_id\`, \`original_order_window\`, \`pre_action_segment\`, \`eligible_policy_mask\`, \`safe_admission_certificate\`. Capture the possible pathways under **each approved complete policy**, not only realized fallback logs. Mandatory network paths include controller updates, telemetry/learning, emergency human recovery, cross-fleet dispatch, demand substitution and customer order cancellations. Directed time-stamped edges and exclusion reasons are auditable facts; a fully closed graph is an assumption until independent engineering verification.

The policy-closed *union* graph must be recomputed for source-only R, target grounding G, competent specialist S, and new embodiment E **only if separately physically admissible**, including controller isolation/freeze variants. A global demand/dispatch edge can collapse many robot pools into one assignment unit. Report this honestly. If there is no independent network replication and no credible reset, a switchback under permanent learning is not a full-policy randomized experiment.

## B. Treatment and policy version pinning

For each randomized policy arm, fix controller starting digest, training algorithm/version, legal source-data mix (field, recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained distinct), sample selection and intervention gates, compute/curation outlays, target alignment, qualification tests, controlled checkpoint broadcasts, recovery admission, operator budget, embodiment and old-controller fallback. Freeze opportunity/measurement windows *before* assignment, retaining zero-order cases and subsequent churn/entry. Public or synthetic data are not accepted service. Distinguish data availability while the old policy is retained, data trained into a new pinned checkpoint, and safe deployment of that checkpoint.

Only independently approved complete policies enter the randomization. A positive mean accepted-service change cannot justify unsafe simultaneous recoveries. If isolation changes market access or controller quality, treat it as a separate complete policy; do not transport from a shared-policy experiment without testing.

## C. Outcomes, intervention identification and decision ledger

Measure per original opportunity window: attempted orders (including none), customer participation/pricing, cancellations, fleet routing, complete joint qualification, accepted whole-service completions, value captured by the provider, operator recovery hours and simultaneous peak, support/validation rights, capital/compute/curation/training/coordination costs and any nonoverlapping terminal contribution. Build one complete-horizon \`Y_network\` from these records; never substitute task success, teleoperator minutes or individual skills for customer service dollars.

Pre-register (i) mean full-network G-vs-qualified F and G-vs-S, (ii) frozen-versus-shared G total policy contrast, (iii) technical held-out joint qualification before/after training, (iv) customer order displacement and operator feasibility, (v) supported target weights and unsupported bounds, and separately (vi) robust regime-specific comparisons if enough regime data exist. Apply valid known randomization probabilities to **network-level** complete outcomes. Confidence intervals require independent units or a justified correlation model; zero observed recovery overloads do not prove hard capacity safety.

A missing customer architecture record or a policy-dependent dispatch link contradicting the proposed union cut is a **falsifier** of isolated SS2 transport. A measured negative full-network change despite positive eligible A technical qualification distinguishes economically harmful spillover/market substitution from simple skill transfer. A safely pinned frozen-controller branch is the explicit counterfactual for "free data available but not forcibly trained." A competent specialist and retained old policy are not intentionally handicapped.
