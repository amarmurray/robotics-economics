# Solutions — economic sufficiency of heterogeneous robot-production evidence

1. The max of conditional expectations is no larger than conditional expectation of the max. The gap in a T-cell is zero exactly when an action maximizes q(h,a) almost surely throughout the cell: choose a conditional-mean maximizing action, then its nonnegative shortfall has zero mean iff it is zero a.s. If feasible sets differ, use only common feasible actions in the coarse cell, or encode eligibility into T.

2. N=1: F favors arm at 3, T favors mobile at 3; a single count-based choice yields mean arm 1.5, mobile 1.5, kit -1, retained 2. Choose retained, worth 2. N=2: FF/FT each favors different actions; arm's mean is 3, kit's mean is (-1+7)/2=3, mobile 1.5, retained 2. Choose arm or kit, worth 3. Total=(2+3)/2=5/2. A joint kit is productive only in FT even though FF has the same count.

3. The unique maximizers are arm for F and FF, mobile for T, kit for FT. Merge only F and FF (three cells F/FF, T, FT). Any merger of T with F or FF or FT, or FT with F/FF, loses the ability to select a common maximizing action. Full-history value remains (3+3+3+7)/4=4.

4. Gross G with provenance equals 4; E equals 3. A report cost c paid solely by G preserves the strict preference if c<1; at c=1 it ties. The report's stand-alone gross value over the N-only rule is 3/2, but its *investment ranking* threshold is only 1. Free optional information with old rules feasible cannot reduce optimized value; forced use and costs differ.

5. Predecision signal-specific maxima: (5+6)/2=11/2. Choosing one action before signal arrival yields max((5+1)/2,(0+6)/2)=3. A delayed report cannot affect a committed single-worker decision; no subsequent action with equivalent payoff was granted in the example.

6. No. The common-optimizer test assumes q_G(h,M) is supported or independently justified. G/S histories alone cannot identify G/M response, even when they include original buyer offers and worker occupations. Use prospective lawful G/M assignments among G-eligible reachable histories, independently record original offers/no-offers plus the *joint* paid-service, recovery and worker-clearance outcomes, and maintain versioned technical qualification and signed data-use rights. If assignments are infeasible, report partial bounds rather than a point value.
