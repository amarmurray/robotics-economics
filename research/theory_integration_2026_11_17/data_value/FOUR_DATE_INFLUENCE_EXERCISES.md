# Exercises — dynamic economically sufficient information (student copy)

Use the four-date common-law G/R/P/E model in FOUR_DATE_ECONOMIC_INFLUENCE_CLOSURE.md. No solutions in this file; see FOUR_DATE_INFLUENCE_SOLUTIONS.md.

1. Derive G's entire fixed-policy expected contribution from t2 accepted H, t3 original A/C offers and W, and the new original t4 C offer D plus clearance L. Show which terms are paid receipts and why counting t3 and t4 C service on W=0 does not double-count a customer budget.

2. Starting from the physical risk set B={H=1,W=0}, prove the sharp bounds on m=Pr(B,D=1,L=1) given Pr(B)=1/4 and conditional d=l=1/2. Explain why separate t4 order and clearance marginals are not enough, even if all (H,A,C,W) histories are observed.

3. Work through the two structural worlds L=Z and L=1-Z on B, with D=Z. Verify that the previous t3 H=W=1 audit observes the same records but the preferred investment switches. Determine the exact m at which G ties E.

4. Assume no early lawful report about the hidden regime. Compute the minimax-regret split between G and E and compare it with the maximin choice. Explain why a t4 audit of a finished cohort cannot be used to select that *same cohort's* t0 investment.

5. State the finite linear row-space identification criterion for a fixed policy g. Construct a null direction that preserves observed t4 conditional D and L marginals but changes their conjunction. What changes if the only unknown perturbations lie at a boundary of the feasible polytope?

6. Write a prospective lawful audit estimator for m using G assignment propensity p_G and a known randomized t4 offer audit probability eta on B. Explain why conditioning on B as though it were a baseline treatment covariate would not identify R-to-G causal effects.

7. Change the continuation policy so that, in addition to C, an originally declined A t3 order can be reoffered at t4 if the worker clears. Which new joint original-offer/qualification/worker moments might become relevant? Describe the recalculation rather than proposing to reuse the present scalar certificate.
