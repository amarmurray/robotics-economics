# Multi-step safe support — exercises (student packet)

1. **Support versus value.** Prove the positive safe-path characterization for a finite controlled Markov model with pathwise resource budgets. Explain why positive mixture support does not imply robust support in every H, acceptable failure probability, or positive captured customer contribution.

2. **Joint customer bundle.** For outcome order (neither, B only, C only, both), compare R=(1,7,7,1)/16 and G=(7,1,1,7)/16. Compute both marginal skill pass rates, the complete-bundle expected return when f(both)=16 and proper subsets have zero value, then subtract 4 of G production displacement. Compare against a specialist worth 2. Repeat when each singleton contributes 8 additively. What measurement discriminates the two economies?

3. **Selected support.** A two-slot service has first validated progress chance 1/2; ordinary second production has p_1^R=0. Explain why two routine slots cannot qualify the new bundle, even though they generate some useful current production. Calculate bundle qualification probabilities for G with conditional success 3/4 after first success and E with unconditional second success 3/5.

4. **Adaptive choice.** Slot-1 incumbent production is worth 1. In slot 2 R=1, S=2, G=(3/4)Delta-1/4 (only after update), E=(3/5)Delta-2 (either history). Prove the Bellman maximum and obtain both breakpoints and all three pieces of V(Delta). Calculate V at Delta=7, and the values excluding G and excluding E.

5. **Free production byproduct and transfer.** If routine second-slot qualification after first success becomes r while retaining one unit current service, derive exactly when it dominates G. Why is higher average source skill not sufficient to prove this r inequality? State the independent gate/exposure/held-out-service observables required.

6. **Statewise stochastic dominance.** Give a coupling proof for a sufficient routine replacement of a deliberate policy under monotone f. Construct a case where two policies have the same positive reachable state support but one provides much lower reliability and another higher current service, so the sufficient condition fails.
