## 1. The economic object

A source record has content about a source task; the relation between that content and a target is another potentially scarce object. A measurement bias is a narrow instance, not the ontology. A deployment type indexes embodiment, task, environment, policy, end market, and dated acceptance rule. Let X be latent reusable source knowledge, M_j the unknown relation needed to interpret it for target j, and theta_j a target-relevant state. A firm can buy source exposure, learn M_j from matched reference/target observations, or acquire direct target knowledge. A controller converts these into proposed work; rights, validation, body, crew, customer demand and release still determine feasible accepted production. Represent the joint posterior of (X,M,theta), not only per-site performance. The observation law changes with chosen source work and diagnostic effort. A competent fixed contingent acquisition/dispatch policy can use all these observations; revising the policy is a separate investment.

The technical transfer relation is not a dollar coefficient. For a scalar target one may use expected variance reduction conditional on the archive, but for complete service use the joint posterior and the optimized feasible service/investment value. An additional report is worth E[V(after)]-V(before) gross when it is optional and does not consume resources; a costly intervention is a difference of feasible whole programs. Keep ordinary operation-generated information on both sides. Ben-David et al. [A Theory of Learning from Different Domains](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10994-009-5152-4) and [Impossibility Theorems for Domain Adaptation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v9/david10a.html) (2010, abstracts consulted November 25) analyze assumptions needed for source-to-target learning, including indistinguishable source/unlabeled-target settings. This is not a new statistical impossibility theorem. Bayesian decision theory, information complementarity, DDA §5/Appendix B and strategic entry are prior art. The candidate economic contribution is the connection between investment in interpretability, the source-capital margin, and the identity of the next production/data source.

## 2. A solvable endogenous transfer technology

Take independent uniform signs X,M in {-1,+1}, theta=XM. A source observation S=X E, a relation observation R=M F, and a direct target report T=theta G have independent sign errors of means a,b,d in [0,1]. These are reliabilities (2 accuracy minus one), not real robot safety scores. Conditional on S,R, the posterior mean of theta is ab SR: independent priors and separate likelihoods give E[X|S]=aS and E[M|R]=bR. The product SR is a binary symmetric signal of theta with reliability ab. A second independent binary symmetric report of reliability d is available, conditional on theta. With two equal-loss signs the MAP decision follows the more reliable report when they disagree, and both when they agree. Thus maximal probability of the correct target decision is

    Q(a,b,d) = (1 + max(ab,d))/2.

This is exact for the binary symmetric known-channel restriction. Additional correlated reports, heterogeneous loss, learning a continuous transformation, and uncertain error rates need the full posterior. No report by itself constitutes acceptance; in the reduced-form production restriction below the correct choice is *subsequently and independently verified* by a complete authorized test, and a known feasible fallback serves unmet demand. No unsafe release or accuracy-to-dollar mapping is assumed.

A paired observation of source and target, taken on a common latent task X, gives S T = M E G. It is a relation experiment with reliability ad, independent of X; fresh independent paired tasks can inform the shared mapping M. Its usefulness depends on the source's observability and the target probe, not just target data count. This makes b a choice-dependent posterior feature. This construction assumes the same transformation class across paired and recipient tasks, known observation error law, and a stable M; without these, more pairs need not transfer. Physical calibration is only one mechanism generating such a comparison. A digital representation, simulator with identified correspondence, teleoperated reference, or matched ordinary service records could provide it.

In the region where the direct route does not dominate, gross marginal source accuracy is b/2, and the cross derivative with respect to a and b is 1/2. At a fixed direct route d, the finite gain from a source investment a0 to a1>a0 is

    Delta_a Q = [max(a1 b,d)-max(a0 b,d)]/2.

It is nondecreasing in b: below d/a1 both max terms equal d; in the intermediate interval only the first term rises, and above d/a0 both rise with positive slope difference a1-a0. It is nonincreasing in d by the same three regions in reverse. It follows that learning the relation can raise demand for source-generated records, while obtaining a direct substitute can eliminate that demand without discarding the archive. An unrestricted qualification or deployment value need not preserve these monotone differences: max over alternative projects, shared validation queues, changing opportunity mix, and strategic entry can overturn them. This is a conditional positive result, not a universal complementarity of data.

For several targets with independent original orders, nonbinding validation/body/cash windows and known discounted net contribution w_j from a subsequent verified success relative to fallback, source investment value is sum_j w_j Delta_a Q_j minus its once-counted net acquisition/operating cost. This is a finite private investment return if weights are captured contributions, a real comparison if they are real resource savings. At a constrained shared window solve the joint program rather than summing isolated target values. Accepted-service MPK_r is a vector at fixed qualified state, not this expected learning return. The induced-installation coefficient adds the probability of funded starts conditional on actual qualification, demand and capital capacity; it cannot be inferred from Q alone.

Synthetic single-target witness: a0=.4, a1=.8, ordinary mapping b=.3, improved b=.8, direct d=.2, w=10. Gross source upgrade returns .2 versus 1.6; at cost .8 the decision changes from reject to buy. A direct report of reliability .7 removes the return even with improved mapping. An ordinary production byproduct with equivalent reliability is a valid no-extra-purchase alternative. For multiple targets with different d, weights, commissioning deadlines and b, the same mapping investment reallocates source capital toward otherwise interpretable and economically actionable target work. A firm that can learn M earlier may advance starts or, with expiring opportunities, create additional starts; without these constraints no automatic effect on the eventual number follows.

## 3. The report's scope and joint law change strategic qualification

Use the preceding two-firm procurement game: one original order, conventional fallback, real savings D=10, separate local validation costs c_A=1.5,c_B=2, only a verified qualified bidder can receive one funded installation, reverse second-price procurement and fair ties. Independent baseline guesses give p=q=.5,j=.25 where j is joint potential success. Both strictly test; ordinary production also teaches and is retained. A licensed interpretation report with a=.8,b=.5 gives .7 marginal correctness; in this synthetic restriction each correct choice passes the separately costed validation. Exclusive A access leaves B's independent guess unchanged: (p,q,j)=(.7,.5,.35). Broadcast with the same underlying state and common interpretation/decision error gives (.7,.7,.7). Independent local reports of identical quality instead give (.7,.7,.49). These are distinct feasible experimental structures, not evidence that every broadcast has perfectly correlated errors. Ordinary guesses are independent conditional on the state. The independent local report law can also be implemented using independent target-specific signal errors of mean .4; same marginal as the shared route.

For entry probabilities x,y, private margins before access fees are x[D(p-jy)-c_A], y[D(q-jx)-c_B]. Expected installations are (px-jxy/2,qy-jxy/2). Let future market X obtain a separate incremental discounted real service saving of 6 per B award in expectation (including probability of a useful lawful record, residual demand, and available successor validation and capital); no such captured payment to A or B. Its ordinary path is held fixed and cancels. Aggregate incremental real contribution is D(px+qy-jxy)-c_A x-c_B y+6(qy-jxy/2), net of actual report resource costs only once.

Ordinary: both enter, awards (.375,.375), A private margin 1, total real contribution 6.25. Exclusive: unique A-only entry, awards (.7,0), A margin 5.5, total real contribution 5.5. Private gross access willingness 4.5, real change -.75 even before reference cost. Independent broadcast: both enter, awards (.455,.455), A margin .6, real contribution 8.33, gain 2.08. Common broadcast: two asymmetric pure equilibria and one mixed; A-only has A margin 5.5, B-only and interior mixed have zero A margin. A's gross willingness relative to ordinary is in [-1,4.5], not a unique number. The independent broadcast yields a strictly higher real contribution than exclusive, yet A would not voluntarily sponsor it at any nonnegative fee. No universal broadcast welfare ranking: common signal correlation and equilibrium selection matter. These comparisons hold under explicit commitment to scope, no redistribution, no forced entry, and no additional buyer authority. A capable prewritten policy implementing these conditional rules gives the same equilibrium.

## 4. Rival suppliers and an endogenous outside diagnostic

To isolate the supply effect without asserting an unrestricted multi-buyer licensing equilibrium, suppose A is the sole potential sponsor, and has a private internal diagnostic of real cost k that implements exactly the exclusive route including the joint law, timing and validation. Its ordinary option remains available. Two external suppliers can implement the same exclusive route at constant real per-contract cost c1<c2 and quote simultaneously; all capacities are available before expiry, one indivisible license is needed. Exclusive supply is credible and no resale. A's maximum external fee is min(W,k), W=4.5 in the witness: above W it retains ordinary operation, above k it uses its internal diagnostic (when viable). The cap is *not* a technical score or the standalone value of the report. With a monopoly the seller can charge the cap if it covers cost. With two suppliers, in the stated complete-information Bertrand game an equilibrium price is min(c2,W,k) if >=c1; at equality ties are awarded to lower-cost external supplier and then external over internal; alternative tie rules give the corresponding epsilon or limiting quote. Proof: the lower-cost supplier can profitably match/undercut an above-cap rival if below A's reservation, and cannot raise price above the other supplier's quote or reservation without losing the contract. The higher-cost supplier cannot profitably undercut a price <=c2. If cap<c1, neither serves and A selects its internal or ordinary option. This establishes existence under the specified tie rule, not uniqueness of all prices/strategies at boundaries. If a cost c1 includes fixed entry setup only when chosen, it is a per-contract avoidable cost; sunk pre-quote entry with uncertainty is a different game.

For c1=.8,c2=1.2: k=2 yields cap2, competitive fee1.2, low-cost supplier margin .4, compared to monopoly margin1.2 if c1 is monopolist. At k=.5 no external provision; internal diagnostic remains privately chosen and reproduces the same socially negative exclusive outcome. If alternative supplier bids implement different signal laws, costs or scope, the same-route Bertrand result does not apply. In particular independent broadcast is socially attractive in the example but A's reservation is negative; making external capacity cheaper cannot by itself elicit its sponsorship. A buyer/beneficiary coalition could pay for that route, but must solve its own participation, price commitment and entry selection; no such contract is assumed.

This shows the distinction between (i) competition to sell interpretability at lower private cost and (ii) the economic direction in which the ability to interpret evidence is deployed. Competition can discipline markups without restoring the displaced evidence-producing sites. Relative interpretability is thus jointly technological, informational and allocative; rights and contracts determine which relation can be exercised. Future work should solve competing *heterogeneous* route suppliers and endogenous beneficiary sponsorship under shared dated capacity, retaining selection bounds for joint report laws rather than assuming away the externality.

## 5. Discriminating evidence and synthesis handoff

Propose a factorial matched original-task experiment: same initial source archive, ordinary retained controller and target eligibility; vary source exposure, paired relation probe, and an independent direct target substitute. Record source and target embodiment/conditions, known common-task link, interventions, held-out joint validation and dated labor/compute. Separate fixed-exposure technical contrasts from independently evolving paid programs, whose source exposure can change after awards. Observe original offers, test attempts/refusals, paired potential qualification across matched held-out work, actual award/installation, fallback, support, service settlement and lawful usable next-market records. Distinguish common broadcast from independent local routes even at equal marginal scores; supplier quotes, availability and internal production substitutes identify the relevant outside option. For multiple target task spaces, vary a mapping intervention on one class and test fresh held-out classes with matched task performance; this discriminates learning the relation from merely improving a source controller. No field panel currently identifies these effects; no new quantitative official production edge obtained here. This is a synthesis/identification advance rather than another unsupported metrology market claim.
