# Prospective atlas and identification design — residual data-transfer network effects

**18 November 2026 | proposed evidence program only; no new customer access or robot operation.** Extends [connected-network protocol](NETWORK_INTERFERENCE_EMPIRICAL.md) to answer whether a partial interference graph can support *complete-policy* economic rankings without making unsafe extrapolations.

## Observation and assignment unit

Pre-register the true cluster as the union of checkpoint lineage/replay/train permissions (C), human operator/dispatch capacity and concurrent recovery calendars (O), and customer order substitution/joint service/production input flows (M). An unknown edge is not coded as zero. For each complete network record site, customer stratum, time, order-eligible original denominator (including no orders), rights, embodiment, old policy, source type (routine field, failures/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public), data curation/computation, recipe acceptance, qualified controller version and all action propensities. Record stopped/aborted operations and safety resource reservations even without an accepted customer order.

The learning intervention should distinguish **shared vs physically isolated checkpoint**, **routine data** vs **deliberate target grounding**, and **redesigned body** vs retained specialist, with the same customer service requirement, rights and approved operator constraints. Measure held-out whole-recipe qualification under frozen demand in parallel with actual customer orders/accepted service; otherwise negative observed customer production might be model interference, capacity loss or market substitution. Keep the conditional target-demand segment for each observation.

## What evidence could justify an omitted-influence bound?

1. Engineering lineage: exact version/replay/edge access map; verified isolated checkpoint builds with no cross-update or hidden global replay; an architectural causal cone is stronger evidence than a regression finding no remote correlation.
2. Matched economic perturbation: among already approved safe complete network assignments with known positivity, measure remote-site accepted output, customer substitutions and zero orders when near policies change. Examine *far* sites and staggered predeclared windows; do not condition on post-assignment order receipt.
3. Uniform rather than average: estimate/explain bounds for all **plausibly reachable** joint history prefixes, including rare target-qualification states, demand expansions and persistent transfer types. Average radius-r spillover near zero is insufficient for the supremum in A1.
4. Negative controls: nonsharing versions, markets lacking order connections, and nonoverlapping supervisor shifts; deviations indicate the map is missing links. Controls are diagnostics, not a proof that all unmeasured remote influence is bounded.
5. Dynamic policy test: compare registered histories under the baseline and proposed retained/adaptive training rules with traceable opportunity and controller transitions. An old-policy bound is not automatically transportable after an embodiment or grounding intervention creates new states.

Report evidence separately as (a) directly observed/replicated, (b) engineering upper envelope, (c) model-dependent structural assumption, and (d) wholly unsupported. Propagate uncertainty through A1/A2; label a threshold crossing only if the **whole** confidence/sensitivity set is on one side and actual safety is independently authorized. Never map a benchmark percentage to customer dollars or infer source quality from one modal human-teleoperation count.

## Operator safety cannot be certified by small distant-effect estimates

Log the **joint timestamped** need for intervention, travel/response/hand-off and abort feasibility, not just mean hours. A rare simultaneous event can make hard shared B=1 infeasible despite arbitrarily small TV distance to a disjoint law. Independently certified reservation, staged admission or verified abort-before-service is a candidate physical control; test its separate accepted-service, customer dropout, data collection and cost outcomes. Unsafe policy vectors are **not** proposed experimental arms. If only mean evidence exists, mark the policy unverified under a hard cap, regardless of a positive A2 lower bound.

The official BLS 2025–35 and BEA industry input-output quantitative backbones are still missing in this program. Their sector employment/input-output values are neither realized customer robot orders nor certified robot work. Continue only an actual authorized official numerical route, audit revision and crosswalk, and flag absent sector-to-service conversion. A linked field atlas first needs [cluster, controller genealogy, demand stratum, whole order type, accepted service, cumulative and peak operator resources] with consistent timestamps. Do not substitute provider announcements or payroll estimates for this identification problem.

**Pre-registered falsification:** reject a local-radius certificate if (i) long-range controller or customer effects exceed its residual, (ii) proposed complete policy enters undocumented opportunity/embodiment states, (iii) a concurrent recovery path exceeds hard budget, or (iv) stable preassignment target customer weights cannot be defined. Retain old/specialist service and narrow the radius assumption or restructure verified safe network allocation instead of claiming a failed field test validates the model.
