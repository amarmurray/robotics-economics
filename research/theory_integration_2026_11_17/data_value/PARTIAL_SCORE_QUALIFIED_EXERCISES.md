# Exercises — rubric and qualified service

1. For n=6 and mean .92, calculate sharp strict and permissive full-stage bounds. Are they actual commercial acceptance rates?
2. At n=5, construct two programs with mean .9 and .8 such that the lower-score program has greater strict complete service. Reverse the ranking without changing means.
3. For a fixed homogeneous v=12, c=1 and p_G in [.4,.8], p_P in [.2,.5], give the conservative incremental contribution interval. Does optional P guarantee a strictly positive return?
4. Why is the LWD RECAP comparison insufficient for an economic source-only return estimate? What additional records distinguish routine data, grounding and independent embodiment?
