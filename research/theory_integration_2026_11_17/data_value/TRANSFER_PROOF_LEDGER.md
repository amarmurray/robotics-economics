## Negative results retained

1. The source/grounding share for a **single** service at a **fixed** expenditure is invariant to a positive multiplicative customer value. Do not falsely claim demand-conditioned mix from (G4) itself.
2. The sign of the data-ground cross-partial can reverse along learning in the scalar threshold model. A constant positive learned-transfer complement is not justified by that model.
3. Increased source stock can make a new body more valuable, but a universally monotone redesign advantage was **not** proven.
4. Gaussian measurement precision does not identify supported robot policy competence, full order reliability or provider economics.
5. Pure transferability diagnosis can change the best policy without changing technical skill; do not double count it as both information and qualification progress.

## Exact next gap

Derive and challenge the choice of a **productive deployment that generates source observations** versus a **deliberate target alignment/diagnostic experiment**, with source relevance learned endogenously. Retain dated demand, complete customer recipes, old policies and a serious specialist outside option. Compare against a competent profit-only policy and a fixed technical-mixture policy; do not grant the learning-aware planner free information or insert a second data premium. A controlled cross-embodiment experiment must distinguish target-body exposure from strictly unseen-target transfer. The November 22 gate must integrate this reasoning with Models A/B and the empirical backbone, rather than merely counting derived identities.
