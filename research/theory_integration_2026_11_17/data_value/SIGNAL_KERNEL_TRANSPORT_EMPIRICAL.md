# Prospective shadow-calibration and approved whole-service contrast — target signal support

**19 November 2026. No target robot experiment, customer access or controller/report approval has been obtained.** This extends SIGNAL_ADMISSION_EMPIRICAL.md without changing the original-order denominator.

**Core comparison:** keep the *entire* independently approved controller, dispatch, operator and customer-order network as the statistical/physical unit. Pin the controller hash, embodiment, software, report-generation and training snapshots, safety-approved actions including specialist, telemetry and customer order universe **before** treatment. For each original order include unattempted, rejected, failed, recovered, displaced, fulfilled, canceled and expired states, accepted price/captured value, outside provider and required operators/peak load; do not condition on accepted work only.

**Shadow report evidence first:** run a rights-cleared, nonactuating source reporter under a separately approved target observation path while retaining the actual approved controller; record source-policy and target-policy state/report joint support, z-label provenance and label timestamp. Evaluate source conditional kernel K_0(s|z) and target kernel K_1(s|z) for the same *pre-action commercial opportunity z* and report meaning. A robot-local perception label is not by itself a commercial-demand state label. Record source modality (autonomous field, errors/recoveries, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained), filtering, curation, compute and rights, plus sample exclusion to prevent test contamination. Freeze target model before held-out validation.

**Two controls distinguish mechanisms:** (i) hold qualified controller and complete customer opportunity fixed while *report policy only* changes under genuinely approved information access—identifies decision/correctness contribution without altering physical learning; (ii) independently admit a target-qualified source-trained controller with a common versioned nonactuating report if technically feasible—tests physical transfer, joint qualification and occupancy. An independently approved new embodiment has its own resource and manufacturing/acceptance trajectory. Do not force cross-play when reporter support is missing; record the unidentifiable cell instead of imputing its value.

**Target transport falsifiers:** source/target state coverage gaps; controller-induced report timing or semantics; failures whose z becomes observable only after action; changed customer demand or operator calls; physical-report data-plane leakage; shared model updates across randomized networks; baseline specialist being displaced; hidden report quality difference by target customer stratum. Distinguish estimator error from action-policy regret. For each approved report action compute the *joint* economic margin D(s,a), not just classification accuracy; a report can be accurate and decision-irrelevant, or inaccurate yet change a costly scarce-resource assignment.

**Identification hierarchy:** real source and target conditional kernels with common support may produce the conditional-TV decision bound, subject to an independently justified whole-service payoff span. Approved full-network randomized policy arm outcomes may identify average *complete* J despite unidentified cross-world qualification coupling. When target reporter/physical policy approval or outcomes are absent, use an explicit identified set and a retained approved fallback; no artificial numerical TV estimate. Randomize whole policy-closed demand, learning, dispatch and operator networks only with independent authorization; robot/rollout assignments sharing dispatch are not independent units. Register pre-treatment order census and service substitution.

**Official empirical program:** public DROID/Open X-Embodiment are versionable technical data-route hypotheses, not price, demand, joint customer qualification or approved robot production. The previously identified BLS/BEA official route still lacks a comparable-vintage downloaded **numeric** crosswalk. Do not replace it with an isolated vendor anecdote or infer value from tasks-per-hour, wages or offline benchmark pass percentages.

**Next qualifying evidence:** an actual versioned, rights-cleared and physically approved shadow report and held-out target opportunity labels for one policy-closed network, or a deliberately conservative target-kernel uncertainty set with a real qualified customer service contribution span. Neither exists in the repository as of this theoretical tranche.
