# Economic value of learning-induced resource incidence changes

24 November 2026. Continuation of [qualified graph discovery](GRAPH_EXCHANGE_DISCOVERY.md) in *When Capabilities Become Production*. The controller may make the same customer recipe consume a different dated operator vector, may move a queue completion before a customer's deadline, or may qualify a new recipe. These are not interchangeable with discovering a pre-existing edge. They change feasible production, not merely the weight of an edge. This chapter supplies a common accounting certificate and a counterexample where identical marginal technical and worker-time improvements conceal an investment reversal.

## 1. Environment: a common catalogue, not a common feasible set

At a lawful predecision history h, let C_i be the finite nonempty set of structurally admissible binary selections from a union catalogue of versioned recipes for regime i=0,1. Include the empty schedule. A recipe specifies its customer, acceptance standard, body/controller, timing, and licensed use; alternate versions serving one order are mutually exclusive. Qualification and rights not known at the decision time cannot be silently admitted. Enforce worker/robot/queue/dated procurement capacities through A_i x <= b_i, with nonnegative resource incidences A_i and capacities b_i. Set w_i to accepted net contributions inclusive of actual variable costs (not an imputed shadow cost of an already budgeted operator); V_i=max w_i'x over feasible selections. A controller can both change A_i and C_i. For instance, shorter remediation releases capacity at one date but may increase later inspection. If the old version is still executable, keep it as a separate alternative. If a training intervention forces retirement, consumes a license or changes the buyer window, the old policy need not remain feasible. Distinguish structural safety from resource relaxations.

For any new feasible reference z, exact accounting gives
    V_1 - V_0 = (w_1'z - V_0) + max_{x feasible in 1} w_1'(x-z).
If z is a transported old optimum, the first term records its contribution drift and any omitted service; the second records all feasible rearrangements. Proof: subtract and add w_1'z. The transport must itself pass time, safety, rights and procurement checks. If the old optimum is not transportable, an explicitly feasible repaired schedule gives a lower bound on the difference, not proof of monotonicity. No economic value is owed to an unexecutable counterfactual. An optional intervention with old feasible policy and unchanged receipts preserves gross optimized value, not necessarily net value after acquisition.

## 2. Finite-change resource-price certificate

For any nonnegative vector p, define
    m_i(p) = max_{x in C_i} (w_i - A_i' p)'x,
    U_i(p) = p'b_i + m_i(p),
    g_0(p) = U_0(p)-V_0 >= 0.
Then
    V_1-V_0 <= g_0(p) + p'(b_1-b_0) + m_1(p)-m_0(p).    (1)
Proof: for any feasible x, p'(b_i-A_i x)>=0, so w_i'x <= U_i(p). Substitute U_1-V_0 and add/subtract U_0. This is valid for indivisible recipes, with the old Lagrangian gap carried explicitly. No differentiability, unique shadow price, or exact LP relaxation is assumed. A supported upper bound on m_1 and lower bound on V_0 can be used if exact maxima are unknown. Prices are certificates, not additional paid receipts.

If C_0=C_1=C (e.g. all common structural recipes have separately verified rights and qualification), set
    t_p(x) = (w_1-w_0)'x - p'(A_1-A_0)x,
    d_p(x) = m_0(p)-(w_0-A_0'p)'x >= 0.
Then exactly
    m_1-m_0 = max_{x in C} [t_p(x)-d_p(x)] <= max_{x in C} t_p(x).  (2)
Proof: substitute r_1(x)=r_0(x)+t_p(x) under the maximum. A low-priced resource release does not necessarily fund a new service; and a large resource release on a poor schedule must overcome its old relaxed opportunity deficit. In particular, estimating the release on the incumbent alone gives neither a universal upper nor lower bound on this maximum. For changed structural menus use (1) directly, or use a common expanded catalogue with explicit availability constraints; do not use (2) across unsupported new columns. A single multiplier fixed before reporting can give a looser bound than a history-dependent multiplier. A hindsight multiplier yields an upper bound on a hindsight relaxation, not an executable schedule.

An exact difference follows when a new feasible reference attains U_1 and the old optimum attains V_0; more generally the width between the feasible lower bound and (1) equals U_1 minus that reference's contribution. This is an operational criterion for whether more graph evidence is needed. The relaxed maximum retains common structural exclusion; relaxing a parent budget for two independently priced local blocks can grossly overstate value, even when each block is accurately measured.

## 3. Dated release and downstream buyer alignment

Consider a single original interference cluster and two dated worker slots, each of capacity one. The retained A serves one buyer for contribution 4 and requires one worker slot at each date. A separate authorized B pays 3 and consumes one slot at its specified date Q in {0,1}. There is no alternate B date in the baseline. Both are fully qualified. The parent procurement cap is two distinct accepted services. Without investment the firm chooses A (4), rather than B alone (3). Target grounding G costs 1/4 upfront and creates a qualified variant of A that still pays 4 but releases its worker requirement at date L in {0,1}. Retain old A as an option; it cannot be sold twice. Assume L and Q are safely verified before dispatch and jointly distributed under G, with Q's marginal law held fixed across arms. This is a time-expanded service bundle, not two separate paid parts of A.

If L=Q then choose updated A and B for 7; otherwise retain A for 4. Let a=P(L=Q). The incremental net value of optional G is
    G(a) = max(0,3a-1/4).
No price is assigned directly to an hour saved. Even a perfect measurement of one hour released per cluster and unchanged 100% technical B qualification cannot identify a. With both L,Q uniform on {0,1}, each of the anti-aligned, independent and aligned laws has the same marginal observations. They give G=0,5/4,11/4. Compare source X strengthening A by 1/2 (net and no change in constraints) and independent embodiment E with separate staffing and procurement, adding 3/2 net. E wins under independence; G wins under alignment. Strict G>E iff a>7/12; the joint audit is necessary and sufficient for this comparison within these stated laws. Equivalently if x=P(L=0,Q=0), then a=2x for half marginals, and x>7/24 suffices. These are synthetic values.

Certificate (1) is exact here without knowing all technical transitions: set p_Q=3 and the other two resource prices (other date and parent cap) to zero. The structural menu includes B and the mutually exclusive A variants. Old m_0=1, U_0=4; new m_1=4 if L=Q, else 1, because B has zero residual. Thus U_1=7 or 4 and the feasible witnesses attain it. Conditional economic resource prices depend on the binding downstream buyer date, not merely worker wages. This upper bound uses a complete two-recipe catalogue; unobserved alternatives can invalidate it. Price 3 is a valid certificate, not a unique marginal operator wage.

Countermodels:
* A parent cap of one makes G's gross increment zero despite identical worker release and qualification; enforce this row instead of adding local surpluses.
* If B is flexible across both dates and the firm can lawfully choose after seeing L, G's net increment is 11/4 under every L law. Customer flexibility is an economic input, not a technical metric.
* If L is hidden at dispatch and both L states are possible conditional on Q, a pathwise safe one-shot schedule cannot combine updated A with B; separately feasible latent schedules do not constitute an admissible policy. The above expected aligned value is an upper bound for the poorer observation structure. A safe abort/retry with enough time could change that conclusion; it is not assumed.
* If E shares the original worker or parent cap, it must be placed in the common menu rather than treated as an additive 3/2. If a free optional routine-data pathway reproduces G's qualified variant and report before dispatch, its incremental value is 3a without a deliberate-data premium. Retained policy feasibility remains essential.

Thus the release date and the demand date are economically complementary even when their marginal distributions and total resource utilization are unchanged. Creating an edge, observing one, and decreasing its incidence have different intervention/measurement requirements. More source data can improve an existing weight, G can release a dated resource, and E can add a capacity row or a new body-specific recipe: all compare in one production optimization. None is assumed intrinsically preferable.

## 4. Dynamic interface and minimum evidence

At each reachable lawful history use the dated graph value or its upper certificate as a potential F_t; the prior Bellman residual bound requires an actual conditional bound on E[r_t+F_{t+1}|h,a]-F_t(h), and terminal F_T dominating terminal receipts. Telescoping bounds full discounted policy value (discount included in rewards/potentials). A resource released now may allow more data collection and generate an entirely new later buyer. That effect must enter the successor state and potential, never as a second additive data premium. A static incidence certificate cannot by itself bound an unknown successor kernel. In particular, a measured duration improvement on selected completed jobs is not a counterfactual release distribution for the original population; failed, delayed and non-admitted jobs matter. [Prospective evidence](RESOURCE_INCIDENCE_EVIDENCE.md) specifies matched dated resources, original orders and predispatch support; it identifies the joint aligned mass in the finite example if approved interventions and exchangeability hold. For a wider graph, certified feasible witnesses and conservative residual envelopes can give partial identification without full law recovery. Model A is the qualified resource/buyer menu; Model B selects the intervention through its controlled transition over menus. Field trajectories, recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and pretrained data remain distinct sources, with lawful rights and real compute/curation costs.
