## The economic question
A production relation becomes an investable opportunity only after embodiment, learned control, authorization, complete recipe qualification and dated capacity jointly support a customer's work. Because production itself changes the evidence that future controllers can use, data capital is a derived complement to those objects, not a universally priced stock. The focal problem is whether deliberate target grounding has incremental economic value **after the best feasible routine-learning and coordination alternative is allowed to evolve**, in competition with extra source data and new embodiment. The question is meaningful even in a fixed final-demand economy, and more important when demand, source visitation or qualification change with deployment.

## The hierarchy and null models
Primary economic mechanism: C may enable paid capacity requiring G, or generate useful routine evidence making G unnecessary. The exact single-target interaction (1) is a derived implication, not a universal assumption. Its two terms correspond to independently distinct real production phenomena. Its positive and negative signs survive without stipulating positive/negative technical cross-derivatives. General history interactions are handled by B, not extrapolated from (1).

Primary decision object: incremental net whole-program value of G, conditional on the feasible menu P/C/X/E and combinations. The relevant counterfactual is a retained or cheaper program with its own evolving data, not an ablated frozen learner. Economic emulation is a constructive rejection certificate; a feasible incremental witness and upper bounds on competitors support adoption. Three separate nulls discipline the argument:

* **Fixed flexibility null:** data only changes one exogenous assignment edge, learning has no effect on future exposure. Then standard finite production flexibility/LP sensitivity explains the capital result. Network geometry is an appendix, not standalone contribution.
* **Learning-only null:** capacity and demand are fixed and additional routine experience can exactly emulate grounding. Then G cannot justify a positive incremental acquisition cost. If routines change qualification but not usable capacity, the restricted interaction is nonpositive.
* **Coordination-only null:** C releases capacity but does not alter any relevant routine readiness. The restricted interaction is nonnegative. If data does not change any complete recipe or future opportunity, it has no incremental gross production return in B, even if technical scores improve.

A fourth, strongest comparator allows both routine adaptation and reorganization, with equal real budgets and the same feasible legal intervention vocabulary. If the full model supplies no different decision or identifiable mechanism relative to that comparator, reduce the novelty claim to an applied measurement framework. Comparing to an intentionally static baseline is not sufficient.

## Evidence needed for scope choice
Fixed intervention assignment identifies program ITT, conditional structural bounds screen a missing combination, and feasible-policy lower/upper certificates address optimized choices. Neither actual dispatch records nor qualification marginals alone identify the portfolio value. A record for the whole service, date, demand, cost and lineage is more valuable to this paper than another isolated benchmark or vendor announcement. A pre-registered four-arm comparison with evolving learners is the highest priority; without it the first paper can only present conditional theory plus an executable measurement design. The Kobe, Vulcan, hospital and Amazon evidence illustrate denominators and resource mechanisms, not matched returns. The warehouse human baseline and official contemporary production backbone remain unfilled. The choice between a theoretical/applied measurement paper and a causal empirical paper remains open at the December 6 gate. No scientific novelty accepted by deadline alone.
