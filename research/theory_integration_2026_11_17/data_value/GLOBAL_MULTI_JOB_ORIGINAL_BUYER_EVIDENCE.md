# Connected-cluster original-buyer, rights and multi-job evidence design

**22 November 2026; PROSPECTIVE AND UNPOPULATED.** Companion to [globally coupled multi-job queue theory](GLOBALLY_COUPLED_MULTI_JOB_PRODUCTION.md). No robotics customer records, authorized interventions, signed licenses, cleared receipts, operator data or original quantitative industry I/O edges were accessed. Synthetic unit values are not benchmark-to-dollar conversions.

## Unit and lawful intervention

The unit is an **entire connected exposure cluster**, not an isolated successful robot episode: original customers and unserved order opportunities, robot-time slots, pooled recovery workers/operators, learner checkpoint recipients, intervention rights and any competing site. Prospectively define R retained safe production, P paid field source/learning with optional R/T dispatch, G grounding and verifying **already legally reusable** data, D separate deliberate teleoperation, and E a new embodiment with **independent robot and worker**. Record task- and embodiment-specific safety/qualification and customer acceptance before classifying an action as feasible. Cross-customer transfer, retention, model broadcast, report timing and license revocation require distinct authorization; absent permission means a forbidden action, **not** a zero-valued unobserved arm. No outreach or actual intervention is authorized by this protocol.

## Minimum chronological field panel

1. **Pre-t1 connected-cluster census:** pseudonymous original buyer and *no-order* denominator; site/task/end market; accepted-service recipe and Q; R outside service; contractual expected output net, invoices/refund conventions; robot and operator resource overlap; prior shift/time/seasonality; anticipated end-market demand displacement. Include offers that would have occurred if the robot was unavailable.
2. **Data decision t1:** P/G/D/E eligibility, assignment and propensity if an approved experiment is eventually conducted, data lineage for field trajectories, failures/recovery, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic, public and pretrained inputs; training/retention/broadcast license scope and cost; expected quality/curation/compute.
3. **Predispatch t2:** eligible **original buyer offer and no-offer** for safe R and qualified T, task safety gate, worker queue state, available robot slot, legally observed report, admissible actions and expected *contractually net* service return. Treatment choice must precede incidence or future Y.
4. **Post-t2:** actual accepted output and cleared net only *after* service; separately timestamp/identify target-caused J, concurrent other-site H, duration and worker service admission; broadcast learner version and qualified-site transition. A background incident is not inferred from average staffing utilization.
5. **Predispatch t3:** backlog B3 and worker identity, original site-2 customer order/no-order including blocked site, Q3, lawful actions and propensity, what the controller knew; record whether the site-2 target is physically deployable.
6. **Post-t3:** whether K was newly caused by target service, worker completion at most actual capacity, and full conservation B4=[B3-c3]+K; actual task acceptance/settlement/refunds. Missing incident duration and censored repairs must be represented explicitly.
7. **Predispatch t4:** original site-3 buyer eligible order/no-order **even if B4>0 and no service was offered**, Q4, rights, queue and robot opportunity. Postdispatch: real accepted/cleared payments, SLA penalties and recovery costs. Record original-order displacement across customers, not just observed service.
8. **Across arms:** verify *extra* E chassis and independent worker capacity, G's already licensed source access, non-P original offer panels, data-rights compliance, operator queue discipline, exposure interference and customer price/quality terms.

## Estimand and missingness audit

For the toy alone, under an approved and supported t2 T, the critical three-way conditional cell is \(\pi=\Pr(J=0,H=0,Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(T),Q,\text{original eligible cluster})\). With the *explicitly assumed* fair independent pairwise margins, \(\pi=(1-\theta)/4\) and identifies the synthetic parity law's remaining scalar. Those assumptions must **not** be imported into actual observation. Real queues have heterogeneous job durations and service rates, customer demand feedback, latent incidents and multiple worker capacity pools; estimate a supported full conditional path law or retain a structural partial-ID set and an unidentified investment ranking.

Selected successful robot episodes reveal neither the original customer orders when worker capacity was unavailable nor lawful counterfactual T/R, G/E service opportunities. Track unknown no-orders, censoring, nonadoption, safety exclusions, cross-cluster interference and assignment propensities. A pre-registered disconnected-cluster randomization (or a defensible interference-aware causal design) is preferable to treating source/model changes and customer shipments as independent treatments. Without authorized action support, a nominally sharp conditional theorem is not an empirical causal estimate.
