# Instructor solutions — source transfer and operator-pool interference

1. For q admitted X incremental qualified recipes, maximize \(v(x_1+x_2)\) with \(0\le x_i\le z_i,\sum_i x_i\le1+e\). The optimum is \(v\min(q,1+e)\). Add \(2b\), conditional \(\ell1\{q=2\}\) only when a complete eligible incremental order is actually accepted (here at least one if q=2), and subtract \(kq+Ke\). The incumbent qualified production remains feasible; its ability to operate is not consumed by an unapproved transition.
2. At e=0 values are \(2b,2b+v-k,2b+v+\ell-2k\), giving \(\ell-v\). At e=1 values are \(2b-K,2b+v-k-K,2b+2v+\ell-2k-K\), giving \(\ell\); subtract to get v. If added E enables collecting further physical trajectories which alter the learned pooled model, \(\ell_e\) varies with e and the difference is \(v+\ell_1-\ell_0\).
3. When the neighbor is G, own X produces \(v-k\); when neighbor X, focal X yields \(v/2+\ell/2-k\). Average with probability p and obtain \(D(p)=v(1-p/2)+p\ell/2-k\). Full rollout per-site difference is \(T=(v+\ell)/2-k\); thus \(D(p)-T=(1-p)(v-\ell)/2\). With p=1/2, v=4,k=2.5,ell=0, D=.5 but T=−.5. A perfectly unbiased estimator for D measures the *wrong estimand* for universal rollout.
4. Totals \(F_0(0)=6,F_0(1)=7.5,F_0(2)=5,F_1(0)=5,F_1(1)=6.5,F_1(2)=8\). Both X plus independently qualified E is optimal among six; E alone has no extra qualified demand. For independent Bernoulli p with e=0, \(E[F_0]=6+3p-4p^2\), maximized at p=3/8 and equal to 105/16=6.5625; deliberately selecting one X attains 7.5 because it avoids random zero-or-two waste.
5. Uniform per-neighbor bound implies \(|w_i(a,\mathbf z_{-i})-w_i(a,\mathbf a_{-i})|\le\sum_j\epsilon^a_{ij}1\{z_j\ne a\}\). Under Bernoulli p, mismatch rates are 1−p (for a=1) and p (for a=0); averaging two arms and the sites gives (I9). In this model \(\epsilon^1=|\ell-v|/2,\epsilon^0=0\); at ell=0,p=.5 the interval radius is 1 and observed D=.5 differs from market T=−.5 by exactly 1. Accepted-only samples cannot reveal the unaccepted or cross-site-counterfactual response.
6. Weight observed \(w_i\) by inverse known probabilities of own treatment plus its *entire target neighbor exposure*, subtract two target regimes and divide by pre-policy N. Target event positivity and correct exposure mapping are required. A global model update can make everyone depend on assignments outside the documented graph; even nonzero calculated \(\pi\) then weights the wrong potential outcomes, and full-market assignment may be practically unsupported.
7. Free optional corpora cannot remove the best gross retained policy; paid retraining may reduce net value. Reports inform decisions without necessarily changing skills, grounding changes source-to-target applicability, and E modifies qualified physical feasibility. A physically approved X-by-E saturation factorial with complete original customer order accounting and frozen source/model versions could identify cross differences. Interpreting their change as congestion alone additionally requires E leave learning, demand, acceptance, prices and rights unchanged. Technical benchmark change without accepted-service contribution is not a dollar payoff.
