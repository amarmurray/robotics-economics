# Component-level causal and economic evidence ledger (unpopulated)

**22 November 2026, prospective only.** No access to private customer, operator, training, signed rights or paid-service records; nothing below claims an approved intervention or a measured robotics parameter.

| Component/assumption | Minimum independently checkable records | Legal/physical support needed | What it can and cannot identify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preaction state x, H and original demand segmentation | Dated original buyer eligible offers/no-offers/cancellations, site/task-bundle, physical robot/worker and learner checkpoint prior to action | Consent, source ownership, connected-cluster selection masks | Identifies at-risk states and P histories only; selection of accepted orders alone is inadequate |
| Qualification K^Q for P/G | Matched blinded task-bundle qualification, threshold/version, coverage and failure modes, actual lineage of source data | Lawful G data use and accepted test, controlled source/target interventions if causal | Benchmarks without physical acceptance and target support do not determine a cross-arm Q kernel |
| Original buyer K^D(.|x,Q) | Offer/no-offer and order/cancellation timestamps, quoted prices, buyer identity link to eligible physical recipe, demand response to program | Commercial permission and appropriately randomized or externally justified G offer exposure | Historical P buyers cannot identify untried G demand; a model-predicted order is not buyer willingness to pay |
| Recovery vector K^J(.|x,Q,D) | Every incident arrival, severity, job creation/completion, worker assignment, transfer and queue occupancy with timestamps | Physical operator logs linked across sites; capacity and service timing independently verified | Queue conservation is checkable, but G incident rate and joint dependence remain unknown without mechanism or supported G experience |
| Accepted paid service and opportunity cost | Original customer acceptance, captured cleared payment, displaced eligible old service, robot power/uptime and human recovery resources | Original buyer audit rights and invoice-to-service linkage | Prevents benchmark-to-revenue mapping, double counting and survivor-only sampling |
| Independent embodiment E | BOM, incremental fleet unit, lead times, energy, staffing and access, learning rights | Separate hardware and genuinely independent worker capacity | E cannot be treated as a costless extra operator or cloned robot |
| Source data and use rights | Field trajectory, recovery, teleop, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained provenance, metadata and retention/training rights | Versioned licenses, legal constraints on downstream sharing | Different sources need different transfer maps; 'one more demonstration' is not a common physical unit |
| Economic network | Original BEA make/use/direct requirements table, matching year/industry/valuation and crosswalk | Public numerical vintage with preserved row/column totals | Macro diffusion only *after* accepted service is measured; still no numerical observed edge |

**Discriminating experiment design, not requested execution:** In a legally permissible connected-cluster test, pre-register full original buyer eligibility, jointly observe Q/D/job/accepted-payment outcomes and reporting masks, and compare P and G only where each policy is actually assigned and safe. Preserve complete recovery histories and effects on the old policy R. Demand-conditioned and queued-out zero outcomes are as important as successes. If G cannot legally be assigned, report outer bounds and structural assumptions; neither model evaluation nor P-only auditing repairs missing G action support.

**Falsifiers:** Identical learner qualification with divergent buyer response refutes a benchmark-only data-value coefficient. Recovery arrival probabilities invariant unconditionally but dependent on customer demand refute separate marginal multiplications. A G step that clears inherited jobs refutes the prior B_G<=W premise; model additional worker/service resources explicitly. An observed G offer response outside proposed conditional beta invalidates that certificate. A transfer-rights prohibition removes that action, rather than making its contribution small.
