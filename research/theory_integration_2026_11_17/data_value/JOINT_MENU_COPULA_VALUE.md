# Joint buyer demand dependence and qualified robotic operator capacity
**19 November 2026.** Continuation of the common *When Capabilities Become Production* source-X, grounding-G, embodiment-E, retained-R investment comparison. This is conditional standard probability/optimization theory, not observed robot economics or established scientific novelty.

## One original market, not independently additive orders
Previous [joint priced menu theory](JOINT_BUYER_PRICED_MENUS.md) correctly prohibits counting two substitutable services bought by one original procurement group twice. A second dependence matters across different procurement groups: buyers may request service simultaneously and compete for one legally available operator/recovery slot. Fix one supported, precommitted original-buyer menu m, a policy p and complete held-out qualification. Let A_i be indicator that eligible original customer group i chooses its offered complete service, p_i=Pr(A_i=1 | m,p), and c_i≥0 its **actual net captured contribution per fulfilled, safely qualified order**. When precisely one safe slot can be assigned after observing the requests, with right to ration and no compulsory fulfillment of the rejected request, gross customer production is W(A)=max_i c_i A_i. It is not Σ_i c_i p_i. A_i are jointly distributed under a common policy, menu, buyer budget and market regime; incumbent p_i do not automatically transport to X/G/E.

## Sharp dependence bounds
Sort c_1≥...≥c_n≥0 and c_(n+1)=0. Knowing only exact marginal p_i at this fixed menu, the expected complete-service opportunity has the **sharp** interval

    L=Σ_k (c_k−c_(k+1)) max_(i≤k) p_i
    U=Σ_k (c_k−c_(k+1)) min(1,Σ_(i≤k) p_i).

The pointwise telescoping identity is W(A)=Σ_k(c_k−c_(k+1))1{any of A_1,...,A_k}. Boole/Fréchet bounds apply to each prefix union. Both are attained *simultaneously*: for L construct nested acceptance events from U∼Uniform[0,1], A_i={U<p_i}; for U allocate consecutive intervals of length p_i on the unit circle, making each prefix union probability min(1,Σ_(i≤k)p_i). If budgets, customer grouping, shared insurance, regime restrictions or observed pair counts preclude these couplings, the expressions remain outer envelopes but cease to be sharp. One-slot dispatch is an executable production policy only under actual ex-post priority, capacity, recovery and contract rights; otherwise W is an upper benchmark to a dynamic admission problem. These bounds do not supersede the prior complete-recipe LP dual when tasks use multiple scarce resources.

## Joint demand can reverse the data-vs-grounding investment
Two distinct original eligible buyers A and B each request one qualified service with probability 1/2 under the *same* policy-invariant posted menu (a strong synthetic assumption). X source-training qualifies both but its shared approved operator capacity is one order; captured service margins are c_A=6,c_B=4, with X acquisition cost normalized to zero. Write t=Pr(A and B), 0≤t≤1/2. Qualified X gross/net=6(1/2)+4(1/2−t)=5−4t. G target grounding reduces operator-resource requirement from 1 to 1/2 per order, permitting both services at captured margin5 each and upfront K_G=1: J_G=5−1=4 regardless of t. E independently adds a safely qualified slot, uses X's per-order margins but has cost K_E=3: J_E=5−3=2. Legitimately retained R delivers2 from an already safe reserved accepted order. At t=0 (disjoint buyers) X=5>G=4; at t=1/2 (nested buyers) X=3<G=4. Same p_i, same per-service net margins, opposite optimal investment. The X−G difference is 1−4t, crossing at t=1/4. All quantities are hypothetical normalized customer-production units, NOT vendor observed data or benchmark-to-dollar conversions.

Source data improve/enable target qualification; G can be valuable because it frees operator/recovery capacity on qualified target tasks, not because its benchmark gain is necessarily larger. E physically adds slots, with its own costs and demand-quality effects. Distinct field trajectories, failures/recoveries, teleop, simulation, synthetic and pretrained/public sources may alter X's qualification in different directions. Their technical similarity does not determine c_i,p_i,t or robot operator capacity. Preserve free optional legacy policies when genuinely available: offering new free optional data cannot lower optimized gross value, although forced retraining, rights loss and costly training can.

## Economic information acquisition
Under an artificial prior assigning t=0 and t=1/2 probability 1/2 each, no report gives best ex-ante expected J=max{4 for X,4 for G,2 for E,2 for R}=4. A perfect **paired original buyer/order and operator** report available *before* choice permits X in the disjoint regime and G in nested: expected value (5+4)/2=9/2. The gross information option is 1/2, before audit expense and already inside optimized future policy value—do not separately add a data premium. A perfectly accurate **unpaired marginal** survey returns p_A=p_B=1/2 in both regimes, does not reveal t and has zero decision value under these assumptions. The report's feasibility, accuracy, market persistence and cost are neither observed nor established.

## Policy- and menu-dependent response closure
For realistic variable prices, competitor alternatives, buyer budgets and network learner/operator spillovers, define a complete joint response type θ that includes potential choices/no-purchases for each admissible policy/menu, qualified production, capacity censoring, controller updates and captured payments. A supported set Π of distributions over those complete histories yields conditional robust ex-ante menu value

    J_p = max_(m safely admissible) min_(π∈Π) Σ_θ π_θ V_p(m,θ) − K_p.

For each precommitted m the inner problem is a linear program when observed moments and safety restrictions are linear. Do not splice impossible source and grounding potential outcomes across independent uncertainty distributions. The fixed-menu Fréchet bounds cannot be applied to X from only incumbent data; p_i(X,m) must be identified or defensibly bounded. Without a finite original eligible population, bounded collected payments, real approved qualified resource and rights/obligations closure, a physical slot ceiling alone supplies **no finite cash-value bound**; customers may change outside options or quoted willingness to pay after an unsupported policy. Retained R belongs in feasible menus only if its old controller, capital and customer right actually survive.
