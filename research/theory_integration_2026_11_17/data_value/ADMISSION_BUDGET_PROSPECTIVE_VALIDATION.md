# Prospective, unpopulated original-customer source-admission/qualification protocol

20 November 2026. **Nothing here describes conducted training or a signed field study.** This is the next conditional design within the existing source-X/target-G/embodiment-E/retained-R production-value argument.

## Required version-locked unit-of-observation ledger

1. Original publisher/version and permitted use, source-generator provenance (routine accepted field service, failure/recovery, teleoperation, deliberately scripted control, simulation/synthetic, pretrained/public), original episode UID and cryptographic digest, scene and task, timestamp, robot/camera/action schema, collection method and episode/step count. Preserve original and processed manifests separately; source-level license is not proof of each modified artifact's consent or operational safety.
2. Raw natural-language candidate list and validated semantics, absence-vs-empty-vs-mismatched distinctions, `load_language` and `skip_unlabeled` flags, goal-relabelling function/seed and sampled language index, post-processing transition count, rejected-source counts and accepted updates. Explicitly measure both \(r^{episode}\) and \(r^{transition}\), including length–label covariance; record within-episode and cross-source duplicate identifiers.
3. Actual TFRecord/TFDS conversion boundaries and lineage. Publisher 60,096, CoRL abstract 53,896 and TFDS 28,935 are incompatible denominators *until* versions, splits and transformations are checked; no arbitrary inferred drop reason. Track the pinned Berkeley code commit and exact archive SHA separately. Do not substitute a third-party transformed copy for original-publisher episode evidence.
4. Fixed-proposal B, fixed-admitted-update K and fixed wall-clock/compute experiments are distinct arms, not interchangeable fairness labels. Record decoder/filter overhead, accepted training FLOPs, retraining failure, seed, batch schedule, effective source exposure and controller checkpoint genealogy.
5. Training randomization must assign the **whole connected learner/update cluster**, not nominally distinct robots sharing weights, operators or customer demand. Predeclare accepted-dose and budget estimands, source inclusion yes/no and goal-image versus language conditioning where both are implementable. A language arm with zero accepted source support cannot identify the equal-dose treatment without a changed annotation intervention.
6. Independently held-out, disjoint source-family/scene original customer service recipes: joint task qualification and failure/recovery, degradation of R, held-out overfitting, stable physical acceptance, report timing and manufacturing choice E. No training data leakage across robot fleet or jointly trained components.
7. Separately approved source-generation comparison where two generators are genuinely feasible in the **same source context**, with episode error, cost, rights and rare events. Lack of source-generation overlap must not be mislabeled absence of out-of-task transfer from a fixed archive.
8. Whole signed buyer–robot–operator R/X/G/E action menu; pre-commitment customer demand/price and exact offer version, offered/declined/unserved/completed/paid units, shared human attention, commissioning, downtime, original operator cost and refunds. Intention-to-deploy denominator includes all failed qualification and negative field outcomes, not only successes. Preserve original customer identity/contract privacy.
9. Original market-network/BEA/BLS sector product/cost/volume reference, signed classification-vintage linkage and official quantified IO cells where available. Employment and wages are not realized robot sales; no numeric edges imputed from unaudited vendor announcements.

## Estimands and stopped claims

* **Causal archive inclusion:** predeclared complete learner-wide inclusion intervention versus exclusion, holding field deployment timing, budget and R feasible. Assignment-level intent-to-train on joint qualification, then separately on *signed original-market* complete production policy contribution.

Descriptive q/r may explain mechanisms; conditioning on successful qualification or admitted data without an identification design does not automatically recover a pure causal data mechanism.
* **Economic choice:** evaluate E,G,X as feasible complete policies under identical original-customer version and captured value, including one continuation accounting and old controller R fallback only if physically certified at the actual state. If raw archive is available freely, retain old training option.
