# Exercises

1. Derive the sharp q interval at fixed p,h. Why is H missing on blocked paths?
2. At p=3/16,h=1/2 derive G range and E cutoff.
3. A report certifies c=1/2,d=1/4. Compare G with X and E; refinement adds c=1/4,d=1/8. What changes?
4. Show why signed scenario prices can give a slack credit to an empty schedule; explain positive-part penalty and why neither certifies the physical support.
