# Proof ledger — protection–learning frontier

**18 November 2026. Conditional constructions only; no observed robotic service economics.**

1. **Pathwise noninterference lemma.** Recursive induction requires invariant B_P transition/output functions, initial state, and exogenous customer arrivals/prices/contracts/operator availability; exclusion of every A→B directed causal path under every approved action, *not* merely absence of a logged baseline dispatch edge. Physical safety of candidate policies is an independent prerequisite. A change in orders due to shared demand is a counterexample even if controllers are physically separated.
2. **Protection-selection equality.** Substitute P and P∪{j} into the previous order-loss lower certificate and subtract; no envelope theorem needed. Each candidate's A gain must be policy-specific. Constants k, b and residual bounds cannot be silently inherited when changing the protected architecture.
3. **Top-k tightening.** For fixed integer k and nonnegative margins, deleting j from the current top k replaces it with the (k+1)-st highest (or zero), yielding b_j−b_(k+1); deleting an item outside the current top k changes nothing. Ties give the same value. The set function T_k(U) is a nondecreasing weighted uniform-matroid rank, hence submodular; W(P)=T_k(B)−T_k(B\P) is supermodular. This is a *certificate complementarity*, not realized production complementarity.
4. **Synthetic arithmetic.** F=5+(6+3+1)/5=7. G no cut: 12/5−2/5−6/5=4/5. Protect B1: (12/5−7/10)−(2/5+1/5)−3/5=1/2, a decline of 3/10. Specialist upper 9/10 is assumed. Ideal zero-loss pin: 12/5−3/5=9/5; cannot infer its A gain from shared G without separate evidence.
5. **TV/capacity bridge.** For bounded 0≤Z≤M, |E_PZ−E_QZ|≤M TV(P,Q). If at fixed u full outcome-law TV≤δ uniformly in demand stratum and θ, and constrained resource loss ≤Kr under pinned training, the two comparisons imply a_pin ≥ a_shared−Mδ−Kr. A changes in market demand, joint acceptance, operator capacity and qualification must all be included in Z or in an explicitly bounded residual. Pooled marginal success rates never imply TV for joint whole-service outcomes.
6. **Failure of general small-r guarantee.** g(u)=(12/5)1{u≥1} is an eligible indivisible bundle production map; g(1)−g(99/100)=12/5 despite r=1/100. Any asserted K=2 is false. No general smoothness/Lipschitz assumption follows from the original LP.
7. **Identification and independence.** Two hypothetical structural worlds can agree on every F/shared-G observation yet differ in pinned transfer (a_pin=0 versus 12/5) while preserving B. The price of physical isolation does not identify a_pin; a matched safe policy comparison or an independently justified full-outcome bridge is required.
8. **Accounting and prior art.** Data's contribution appears once through qualified, optimized accepted customer production. The old F option creates free-data weak dominance only when it remains feasible at unchanged world/resources; sunk costs and mandatory updates are distinct. Interference, weighted-rank/submodularity, TV expectation and LP bounds are established foundations, not scientific novelty.

**Proof gap and falsifiers:** no actual signed full B structural cut; no uniform pinned-vs-shared full-service outcome TV or resource-response K; no official numeric BEA/BLS sector crosswalk or customer accepted-service panel.
