## What was not proved

- No optimal robot intervention controller or model training algorithm was derived.
- No universally best data modality, embodiment, teleoperation occupation or end market was identified.
- Source relevance signal quality remains a primitive.
- No scalar price per trajectory or competitive wage equilibrium was derived.
- No independent review, empirical calibration, first-paper novelty determination or Christmas acceptance gate was completed.

Distinguish a competence improvement that reduces observed corrections from one that reduces the need for those corrections. Derive a constructive allocation rule and a falsifying countermodel, with the learning transition estimated or bounded at the level of qualified complete service.
