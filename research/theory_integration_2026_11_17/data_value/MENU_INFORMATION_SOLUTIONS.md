1. Gross (R no signal, R signal, S no signal, S signal) = (1/2, 1/2, 1/2, 1). Interaction = (1−1/2)−(1/2−1/2)=+1/2. It follows from jointly matching *known orders* and *qualified menu flexibility*, not a neural learning interaction estimate.
2. Gross (1/2, 1, 3/4, 1). Source gains are +1/4 without information and zero with it; information gain falls from 1/2 to 1/4. Interaction = −1/4. Every old action remains available and gross optimality is monotone under more optional data; therefore the negative economic interaction is not proof of negative transfer.
3. For each action a, E[max_u Q(z,u)]−E[Q(z,a)] is the expectation of nonnegative pointwise regret. It vanishes iff a is pointwise optimal at all positive-mass z. Maximizing over a proves both directions. Future coarsening can induce further loss through altered transition/observation policies; one-step Q permits normal subsequent full histories and does not check equality of rewards or next-state kernels.
