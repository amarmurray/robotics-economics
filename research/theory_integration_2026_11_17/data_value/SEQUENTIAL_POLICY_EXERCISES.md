# Student problems (instructor solutions in separate file)

1. Prove the original customer-population adaptive grounding effect equals pi times the mean eligible source-failure continuation effect if all noneligible histories use the same retained policy.
2. Fallback produces net 2; an approved grounding attempt has total net 1.4+2Q. Compute conditional and population incremental benefits for r=3/4 and r=1/5, pi=1/2. What is the break-even r?
3. Derive Hoeffding/union confidence half-width for independent network contributions in [L,U] in two randomized arms. Calculate equal-arm n for range 4, 95% confidence and desired width .5. Is this trial power?
4. Identify why a shared dispatcher/learning network invalidates robot-level independent randomization, why an ineligible grounding policy is not an observed zero, and what the direct policy experiment does not identify about Q coupling.
