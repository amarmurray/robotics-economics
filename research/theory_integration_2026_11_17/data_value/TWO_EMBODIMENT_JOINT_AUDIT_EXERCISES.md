# Two-embodiment data-value and decision-sufficient audit: exercises

Keep the two-embodiment A/C/W/Q environment and all rights/cost assumptions in TWO_EMBODIMENT_DECISION_SUFFICIENT_AUDIT.md. State all unobserved histories and action timing.

1. Derive the P, G and E exact expected paid contribution in terms of t=Pr(ACW), using only the stated fair pairwise margins. Explain where overlapping original buyers and one shared worker enter.
2. Construct the even and odd parity worlds. Prove that all individual and pairwise A/C/W tables are identical but E[ACW] differs. Is invoice-only R evidence sufficient?
3. Show that E weakly dominates P and strictly dominates R over t in [0,1/4]. Find G/E ranking threshold, maximin investment, and globally minimax randomized policy chosen before learning t.
4. With equal prior on endpoint worlds and a precommitment binary report classifying the regime with accuracy a, derive the minimum reliability necessary to ever select G after a low-overlap report. Price perfect information. What changes if the report arrives after t0 commitment?
5. State and prove the row-space criterion for point-identifying one fixed-policy contrast when the feasible law contains an interior distribution. Explain why a known sign does not require point identification.
6. Show a paired R/G potential-outcome construction in which every complete R original-buyer and worker history is observed but G's effect remains unidentified because G alters t2 repair or customer demand. Give an ethically/physically feasible evidence design that would distinguish those worlds, and identify one quantity that remains unmeasured if it is not authorized.
