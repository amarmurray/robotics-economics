# Prospective measurement: source-selected failures and economic follow-on choice

**No physical study, approved deployment, training or customer access has been conducted.** This is a private evidence contract for the same qualified-production manuscript.

## Four linked layers of evidence

**Population/order denominator:** pre-register an original A/B accepted-service order universe (requested, accepted, attempted, delivered, failed, replaced, displaced, unattempted, expired), timing, customer willingness/captured contract value, competent specialist/F alternative and all relevant organizational/physical resource and paid data costs. Record customer-order generation itself rather than only orders accepted after treatment. Whole policy-closed networks, not robots, are candidate independent causal units.

**First-stage source lineage:** for each regime x, record permitted source mixtures by collection method (routine autonomous field, assisted teleoperation, rare recovery, deliberate target diagnostic, simulation, synthetic, public/pretrained), license/rights, curation, controller/action-space and embodiment hash, training version, test protocol, support map, exact Q vector for *complete qualified service* and pre-choice H_x. The post-source pass/fail denominator includes all opportunities, not only convenient successes. Failure reports may be informative without qualifying the policy.

**Second-stage admission:** separately sign the physical, recovery, data-rights and customer approvals for every feasible F/G/body policy before stage-two assignment. If stage-one source choice itself is an experimental arm, its safety and rights must be approved independently. Do not randomize unsafe actions or treat a success as post hoc eligibility. Hold accepted-service definitions and original-order measurement invariant; record source selection and each regime's actual post-source failure rate, not a pooled r.

**Causal identification:** if independent policy-closed networks and two safe source regimes exist, independently randomize the source strategy and, *among pre-choice eligible source failures*, the allowable F/G/body continuation. Estimate each source regime's baseline J_x(F), eligible fraction pi_x and within-regime d_x(a,H_x) directly. Estimate J_x^* using an explicit policy selection rule learned on separate data or prespecified; do not evaluate a selected maximizing rule as though it were preregistered on the same data. Conditional comparisons concern actual H_x support and cannot be naively mapped to other source failures. Technical paired qualification overlap t, transition r_x and individual-type screening remain separately unidentified without compatible safe matched testing.

## Discriminating tests and countermodels

1. **Selection-only hypothesis:** after baseline qualification and explicit pre-choice type/support strata, does a downstream intervention's joint complete-service effect remain invariant while the source upgrade changes the distribution of failures? If true with valid overlap, use the nested-screen decomposition; if false, quantify genuine changed transfer, recovery or customer response.
2. **Mechanistic learning hypothesis:** compare fixed model family, validated training resources and target/held-out complete qualified service for source x0/x1, and G/body with versioned lineage. A source-mixture change may alter the post-source action occupancy, transfer or accessible customer demand even with the same initial success fraction. Avoid task-percent to dollars.
3. **Diagnostic-substitution alternative:** can a safe, non-actuating target-support diagnostic, pre-choice type test or validated offline eval identify appropriate acquisition more cheaply than first-stage training? Include actual false results, coverage, permission and opportunity cost; do not assume free perfect knowledge.
4. **Independence/safety failure:** if a central learner, operator or customer market links nominated units, randomization must occur at their *entire* policy-closed union or the trial cannot identify the stated independent-network mean. Controller hash separation alone does not close dispatch, attention or demand connections. An original B order threatened under either action remains in Y for both.
5. **Weak-support limit:** no source-arm sample in a target source-failure/history stratum means direct d_x there is unobserved; use bounded partial identification and disclose coverage. An exact policy effect from a source x0 trial is not a field-calibrated x1 effect.
