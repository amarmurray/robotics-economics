# Exercises — original-parent joint production

1. From a=P(A), b=P(B), q=P(A and B), construct all four joint masses and prove the sharp feasible q range.
2. Derive gross expected value and its dependence term for one operator slot with A margin 4 and B margin 3. Repeat for an indivisible customer A+B margin 7.
3. Let a=b=1/2. Construct two natural q worlds at 0 and 1/2. Calculate gross, then net of a 1/4 G cost, and compare a separately admissible E policy value 3. Explain why q_C=1/4 and zero C/N marginal drift need not rank the original G.
4. Given rho=3/4, respondent C marginal rates 1/2 each, and defensible per-service mean drift 1/8, compute sharp natural marginal, q and both production intervals.
5. Specify one approved original buyer/robot/operator measurement that could separate the worlds. Why do additional unpaired teleoperation/field logs not by themselves supply it?
