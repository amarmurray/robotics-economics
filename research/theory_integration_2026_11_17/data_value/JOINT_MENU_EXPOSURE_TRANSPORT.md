# Joined original-buyer choice and joint qualification under policy/menu drift
**20 November 2026 UTC | When Capabilities Become Production | conditional theory; no field estimate**

## Same central economic question
Source learning X, target grounding G, an approved new embodiment E and a genuinely retainable incumbent R compete for resources because their data and engineering changes alter **which complete original customer orders can be safely accepted, sold and paid for**. The preceding stochastic-choice analysis identified the joint event C AND Q, not the independent marginal purchase and qualification rates. But that event was indexed by one fixed policy/menu. If source data, grounding, body, price menu or shared operator exposure changes either original parent choice or qualification, its previously estimated joint rate need not transport.

This continuation investigates **how much matched original-order information survives a specified policy/menu/whole-network change**. It does not assume globally additive transfer, independent skill outcomes, static demand, fixed hardware or a special role for human data. Heterogeneous field failures/recoveries, routine production traces, teleoperation, simulation, synthetic and public/pretrained material affect the learning/qualification law only through a documented, rights-valid versioned intervention. A technical skill score has no revenue coefficient by fiat.

## General qualified-production object
Let a full action a=(p,m,z) contain controller/data acquisition policy p, legally feasible original-buyer offer menu m, and full learner/operator/customer network exposure z. The original eligible buyer set and production window must be defined before post-treatment selection. Let Y_a be the collection of **actually purchased, fully qualified, safely admitted and paid** service recipes, including no-sales and declined offers. Define net decision value

    J(a) = E[V(Q_a, Y_a; capacities, operator, rights, parent budgets) - K(a)]

where V is the previously established optimized complete-service production problem, using actual captured contributions and competent feasible alternatives. The future value of data enters Q and the continuation of J, never as an additional separate data premium. Prices/rights and embodiment eligibility are part of a, not universal constants. When the old policy is truly optional, its gross choice remains; forced training may destroy that option.

For a deliberately narrow diagnostic, condition on an original first eligible buyer order H, with q=P(H) invariant over the compared supported menus and networks, two legally available menus priced 6 and 4, stable captured unit margin equal to those normalized prices, and one safe paid first service. X safely supplies one; G may safely supply two. Let C_a be the original parent's committed second purchase at price 4 **within H**, after budget/alternative-service choice, and Q_a the same-network, rights-valid safe joint second slot. Define s_a=P(H AND C_a AND Q_a). With target-grounding cost k, and no other available profitable recipe,

    J_X=6q,   J_G=max{6q,4(q+s_G)}-k.

Therefore J_G>J_X iff 4s_G>2q+k. This is not a robotics profitability estimate. In the previous synthetic q=1/2,k=1/2, the investment threshold is s_G>3/8. If changing menu also changes first-order purchase probabilities or receipts, use the full menu-aware V and do not transport this formula.

## Conditional transport proposition: paired response bits, not separate percentages
Fix H, one original parent/network stratum, and a legally and physically interpretable baseline comparison a0. Conditional on H, observe or otherwise defend the complete *joint* baseline law of original buyer C0 and safe Q0:

    d=P(C0=0,Q0=0|H),
    b=P(C0=0,Q0=1|H),
    a=P(C0=1,Q0=0|H),
    t=P(C0=1,Q0=1|H),   d+b+a+t=1.

For a contemplated target regime a1, suppose independently justified **joint-world** transport restrictions bound the fractions of original cases where buyer choice changes and where safe joint qualification changes:

    P(C1 != C0 | H) <= u_C;   P(Q1 != Q0 | H) <= u_Q.

These are restrictions on a coupling of potential states for the same original original-parent/whole-network population, NOT assertions derivable from differences in two aggregate rates. They may fail when the buyer registry, physical embodiment/eligibility, offer legality, model lineage, supervision or network exposure changes in untracked ways. A postpolicy joint response rate is s1=q P(C1=1,Q1=1|H).

**Sharp conditional two-bit bounds, without additional H constraints:**
The smallest possible conditional joint success rate equals

    L=max(0,t-u_C-u_Q).

To maximize it, for a chosen mass z converted from baseline (0,0) to (1,1), require 0<=z<=min(d,u_C,u_Q). It is optimal to turn up to min(a,u_Q-z) of baseline (1,0) into (1,1) and up to min(b,u_C-z) of (0,1) into (1,1). Consequently the sharp upper bound is

    U=t + max_{0<=z<=min(d,u_C,u_Q)}
      [ z + min(a,u_Q-z) + min(b,u_C-z) ].

The maximization is a one-dimensional piecewise-linear problem and is exact for divisible population masses. It improves the crude upper bound t+u_C+u_Q when many baseline cases require *both* changes to become paid, qualified second service. Proof and restrictions are in [the proof ledger](JOINT_MENU_EXPOSURE_PROOFS.md). For finite integer populations, restrict z to the discrete counts. An independently measured marginal success rate, or a single unconditional "negative transfer" under one forced learner, cannot replace this two-bit support.

**Economic certificate (narrow diagnostic only).** For q unchanged, s1 in [qL,qU] bounds G by
    max(6q,4q(1+L))-k <= J_G <= max(6q,4q(1+U))-k.
Thus if the upper bound is below the fully qualified X value, X dominates G in the modeled legal menu; if the lower exceeds X, G dominates X. R and E must also be compared under their **own** valid lower/upper supported complete-policy values; neither follows automatically from this one comparison.

### Exact synthetic informative and noninformative worlds
A. Conditional joint baseline counts (00,01,10,11)=(6,2,4,8) out of 20, with C and Q policy-change budgets 4/20 each. Baseline s0=q*(8/20)=1/5 at q=1/2. Direct crude transport suggests s_G<=2/5, which crosses the G threshold 3/8. The exact joint allocation can convert four (1,0) and two (0,1) cases, but no more because the Q-change budget is exhausted: U=14/20, so s_G<=7/20. Therefore G<=29/10=2.9 < X=3. **Supported joint baseline states eliminate an otherwise ambiguous investment**—conditional on the much harder-to-justify counterfactual change budgets and stable first purchase.

B. Joint baseline counts (00,01,10,11)=(0,2,0,18) out of 20, with each change budget 1/20. The conditional lower L=16/20; at q=1/2, s_G>=2/5. Then G>=31/10=3.1 > X=3. Distinct data-generating worlds thus supply opposite rigorous rankings while obeying the SAME structural theorem, not new arbitrary functional forms. R=2 is a qualified benchmark in both synthetic worlds; E requires its own verified policy-specific demand and physical upper bound before declaring a full X/G/E/R winner.

A certificate can be reversed by unobserved first-purchase behavior, invalid margin capture, legal menu changes, parent composition, adverse capacity consequences, a source-updated shared controller, absent safe joint second qualification, or an unbounded change budget. The last objection is decisive: without a defensible link from a0 to a1, both C1 and Q1 can be arbitrary, and even extensive original-buyer baseline data need not decide source vs grounding.

## What learning or acquisition is worth
Learning from an additional original case, a paired buyer/refusal and safe-qualification audit, or a controlled source mix is useful only insofar as it changes the feasible interval of **whole** a-specific J enough to change the optimal decision or reduce its decision loss. A mere reduction of technical uncertainty can have zero choice value when source X remains dominant under all admissible laws. Report selection and operator availability can alter which paired cases are visible. Formal Bayesian EVSI is the difference between *optimized* values before and after the report, less valid actual audit cost once. Do not add EVSI again to the already evaluated continuation value.

A source intervention may improve Q on the same original orders while G saves operator time for those orders; the interaction can be a complement. In a saturated market with no additional purchase, improved Q has no immediate production return. Embodiment E changes service recipes, customer eligibility and possible learning trajectories, so the two-bit capacity example is not a full model of E. This is the narrow identification subproblem embedded in the original multilayer/coordination/manufacturing/value-chain programme.
