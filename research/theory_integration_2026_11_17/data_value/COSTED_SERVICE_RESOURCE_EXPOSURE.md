## Restricted theorem: economic exposure of released worker capacity

For fixed complete lawful programs g=P,G (routine data versus deliberate target grounding), assume identical qualified original service, known common additional opportunity menu in dated skill/location cells t, additive net contribution v_t>=0 per redeployed hour, and independent capacity ceilings u_t. Let r_gt in [0,u_t] be hours that can be redeployed, H_g=sum r_gt. Here r is **usable released capacity** under the stated menu, not just physical labor savings; zero-value slots may account for idle releases. All acquisition, integration and operating costs (including any realized payroll avoidance) are counted once in C_g. Then J_g=J_common+sum v_t r_gt-C_g. v_t excludes wages already netted and is additional captured net contribution, not task value, patient utility or gross price. An incremental data program changing only these release profiles and costs has

    Delta J= v·(r_G-r_P) - (C_G-C_P).

At known H and no other restrictions, lower L(H) fills slots in increasing v order and upper U(H) in decreasing order, fractionally if necessary. Proof: if a candidate maximum uses positive capacity in a lower-price cell while a higher-price cell is unfilled, transferring min(used, unfilled) weakly increases value and preserves H; iterate over finite cells to attain greedy maximum; reverse for minimum. For unconstrained cross-program profiles the sharp difference interval is [L(H_G)-U(H_P)-Delta C, U(H_G)-L(H_P)-Delta C]. Independent extremes attain both; convex feasible sets imply all intermediate values. If a **separately validated** nested release r_G>=r_P holds, H_G>=H_P, the sharp interval tightens to [L(H_G-H_P)-Delta C,U(H_G-H_P)-Delta C]: any extra release vector of that total fits, and one can assign H_P within residual total capacity sum u-H_G+H_P>=H_P. Optional retained P does not imply nesting. Adding same-price uniformly valued hours collapses the interval but cannot justify uniform prices in the field.

Synthetic two-window example u=(3,3), v=(4,0), H_P=2, H_G=4, Delta C=3: unconditional contrast [-7,9], nested [-3,5]. Independent staffed embodiment with incremental value 2 can beat or lose to G under both assumptions; P remains available, so G is not forced when negative. These are normalized illustrative values, not either hospital's monetary inputs.

General production network: v may be replaced by supported dated resource-price certificates from the existing finite-change chapter. With complementary services, endogenous buyers, shared staff, fatigue, opportunity-specific qualification, endogenous learning continuation or changing opportunity menu, this linear formula is not an exact certificate. Need full policy-closed J, or independently verified price bounds on complete feasible production. Released time must be linked to a real, additional accepted order or other cost displacement; no benchmark-to-dollars conversion. Data rights, source composition and compute/curation remain in the program and its transition law. P can emulate G with cheaply generated data, and E can change both service and resource menu; neither ruled out by these cost studies.

## Evidence and falsifier

Use hospital cohorts as a service atlas entry, not as a calibration of data transfer. In a lawful future measurement design: define the original routine and emergency request set before routing; preserve timely correct receipt, manual completions and recipient acceptance; record every worker occupation (recovery, batching, training, cleaning, maintenance), dated released labor, actual paid staffing costs and separately realized incremental qualified clinical/logistics output. Freeze P/G/E intervention definitions with model/source lineage, rights, acquisition/curation, embodiment and independent staff; include rejected/nonattempted requests and changed subsequent service opportunities. A costed fixed-program difference needs exchangeability or a supported alternative bound. If released hours lie entirely in zero-value or unusable windows, or P generates equally usable lawful data in time at lower cost, the claimed G premium fails in this restricted mechanism.
