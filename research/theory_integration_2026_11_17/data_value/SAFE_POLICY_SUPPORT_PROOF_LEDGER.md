# Proof ledger — approved whole-policy support under network interference

**18 Nov 2026.** Mathematical scope conditional; read [the theory](SAFE_POLICY_SUPPORT_THEORY.md) and [experimental contract](SAFE_POLICY_SUPPORT_EMPIRICAL.md). Reuse earlier safe-pool randomized means and complete network production accounting; do not create a data-premium term.

1. **Design identification.** Pre-action D and E fixed. Independently assigned actual interference clusters, positive p(p|D,E), no cross-cluster contamination and consistent complete policy Y. For finite eligible set of N, E[1{Z_i=p}Y_i(Z_i)/p_i(p)] = Y_i(p) under assignment randomization. Summing/N identifies the *mean* for p; subtract for s. The joint Y_i(p),Y_i(s) is not observed and not necessary for this expectation. Does not imply regime-wise or unit-level dominance.

2. **Unsupported bound SS1.** Law of total expectation: J(p)-J(s) = sum_d w_d [e_d delta_d^E + (1-e_d)delta_d^U]. With L_d<=both Y<=U_d, delta_d^U lies in [-R_d,R_d]. Summing gives SS1. Sharp *relative to unrestricted bounded response functions*: keep all eligible potential outcomes, observed assignments and shares unchanged, assign all unsupported Y(p)=L,Y(s)=U (lower) or reverse (upper). More constrained joint structural laws could forbid endpoints. If m_s,U known, delta_U lower L-m and upper U-m, sharper. If p is physically unapproved on U, this is only a hypothetical missing-potential-outcome interval, not a candidate action.

3. **Selective-policy identity SS2.** On U both arms take the same *actual* F and have identical transitions. Under no spillovers and fixed eligibility, their difference there is zero. On E the conditional difference is delta_d^E. Integrate over original w_d,e_d. Strictly this requires the experimental p versus s exposure environment on E to equal the selective rollout environment; changes to controller pool composition or market saturation invalidate transport.

4. **Residual guarantee SS3.** If a separately justified *total* network production perturbation from the isolated selective comparison is bounded below by -Gamma, and extra c is not already part of Y, the real difference is >= A-Gamma-c by addition. Earlier influence-matrix recursion is one *conditional* way to produce Gamma; counting both its included error and Gamma again is invalid. If only a probability of coupling mismatch is known, an envelope on whole-network reward swing is still required.

5. **Endpoint enumeration.** In the synthetic strata: A= (3/5)(3/10)+(2/5)(1/4)(1/10)=19/100; unsupported mass=(2/5)(3/4)=3/10. [0,2] for both policies gives +/- 2 per unsupported unit => [-41/100,79/100]. Measured s_U=1 gives +/- 1 => [-11/100,49/100]. Subtract Gamma=2/25 for 11/100; further c=3/20 gives -1/25. Nine integer endpoint pairs enumerated in the executable include both extrema. All numbers normalized synthetic, no estimated customer values.

6. **Complete-history versus switchback.** G→R 9+13=22 while R→R 10+10=20. The observed R period conditional on prior G is not Y(R|initial) but Y(R|post-G controller). The resulting period-level comparison is not a full-policy causal estimand. Order assignment identifies order-specific outcomes if safe; it does not identify an arbitrary infinite-horizon stationary policy without additional laws.

7. **Statistical confidence is a different layer.** If each pre-action stratum truly has independent bounded Y in [L_d,U_d] in each random arm, a conservative joint (1-alpha) uniform two-arm, K-stratum Hoeffding lower bound is sum_d w_d e_d [hat_delta_d - R_d(sqrt(log(4K/alpha)/(2n_dp)) + sqrt(log(4K/alpha)/(2n_ds)))]. This is not a finite-population randomization interval and is not valid with shared stochastic controller updates, adaptive nonstationarity, correlated market shocks, unaccounted target-selection or hard safety constraints. It is *not* the earlier binomial 536-trigger audit: the two certify different estimands under different assumptions.

**Validation:** exact stored JS source and results for 20 scoped assertion groups, full enumeration of 9 unsupported integer endpoint states and 16 4-pool random assignments, plus independent Python Fraction verification of main fractions. These are arithmetic/finite-example checks, not general proofs; the numbered arguments above supply conditional proofs. No robot trials, controller-link audits, historical CI or peer review.
