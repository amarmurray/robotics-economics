# Solutions: qualified output, demand, and the research/production frontier

1. Deployer cash contribution is pi(q)=[kappa D-s]q-(kappa beta/2)q². Writing m=kappa D-s and d=kappa beta, unconstrained q*=max(0,m/d). With cap C(u)=K(1-delta*u), q(u)=min(C(u),q*). Capacity binds for all u if m>=dK; otherwise there can be an initial slack interval where training has no effect on output. A robot motion benchmark is not automatically an accepted order.

2. With V_AB=W/rho, the pre-B Bellman equation is rho V=max_{u feasible}{g(u)-cu+lambda u(V_AB-V)}. Under a fixed stationary u, solve (rho+lambda u)V=g(u)-cu+lambda u W/rho. For g=g0-Lu-hu², define A=lambda(W-g0)-rho(c+L). By quotient differentiation the derivative has the sign A-2rho h u-lambda h u², strictly decreasing if h>0. Stop if A<=0; choose the effort cap if the sign remains nonnegative; otherwise solve u=[sqrt(rho²+lambda A/h)-rho]/lambda. A linear *hazard* need not imply a bang-bang *effort* choice because accepted output is concave in effort via demand.

3. Subtract the no-learning value g0/rho from the fixed-u value, with W(m)=g0(m)+Delta. The increment equals u[lambda Delta/rho-c-Kdelta(m-dK)-hu]/(rho+lambda u). Its derivative with respect to m is -Kdelta u/(rho+lambda u)<0 whenever u>0 and delta>0. Taking the maximum over the common feasible set yields a nonincreasing premium. Specialist training preserves A capacity, so its incremental value is max{0,(mu Delta/rho-c_sp)/(rho+mu)}, independent of m in this controlled comparison. Changing Delta, training rates or specialist resource sharing invalidates the simple claim.

4. Baseline g(u)=2-2u², so u*=1/2, V=5/2; specialist V=12/5. A hard q>=.9 means u<=.1, so shared V=67/30, below specialist. With a soft penalty p whenever q<.9, the discounted value in that region is [g(u)-cu-p+lambda u W/rho]/[rho+lambda u]. Differentiation changes the sign polynomial to A+lambda p-2rho h u-lambda h u². A higher p increases the conditionally optimal breach intensity (shorter expected penalty duration), but reduces the value and can make the specialist or nonbreaching alternative optimal. For p=.1 shared still wins; for p=.25 specialist wins. A one-off contract termination payment or non-retained qualification changes the problem.

5. The no-success cash identity is C(t)=K0-c integral_0^t u + integral_0^(t-d_cash)+ g(u(z)) dz. For bounded effort and concave g, terminal nonnegative cash plus Jensen gives c*x<=g(x) at every long-run subsequential average x. Thus x* = max{u<=u_max: cu<=g(u)}. If h>0, the positive root of h x²+(L+c)x-g0=0 is 2g0/[(L+c)+sqrt((L+c)²+4hg0)]. Start with u=0 until matured A receipts can pay the constant effort x*, and then sustain the receipt-matched rate; fixed delay is transient. A finite loan changes the finite-time deficit but not this asymptotic capacity/cash bound. A hard SLA lowers u_max. This is a no-success-path frontier, **not** a claimed optimal private discounted policy or a full stochastic invoice-default model.

6. A matched panel needs (i) delivered and paid accepted complete orders, customer outside process and order-value/demand shifts, (ii) simultaneous robot/learning-hardware utilization, supervision and task/exception logs while B is trained, and (iii) dated service-level events, invoices, credits and banked collections. DHL's May 2025 'up to 700 cases per hour' is an issuer-reported unloading speed, not a matched case-picking acceptance rate, a customer demand function or a paid learning-displacement experiment. GXO's October 2026 cumulative tote count is likewise not a causal estimate of specialist-versus-shared qualification cost. A matched specialist comparator needs the same order mix, supervision requirements and legal rights.
