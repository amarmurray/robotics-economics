## Candidate contribution and next falsifying test

The candidate is a measurement-linked explanation of how customer production structure transforms capability learning into economic value. It requires economically grounded qualification units, correct joint acquisition laws, competent alternatives and a dynamic/organizational extension. It is not yet an accepted original paper contribution.

A decisive test changes customer demand or a conventional fallback while leaving technical learning fixed, and checks whether the economically productive qualification bundles change as predicted. A second compares independent versus shared acquisition with the same marginal technical performance. A third observes progress and retention to distinguish adaptive stopping from batch commitment. These tests need qualified complete-service and real cost/payment observations; standalone benchmark scores do not supply them.
