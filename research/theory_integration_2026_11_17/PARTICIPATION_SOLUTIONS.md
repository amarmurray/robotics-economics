# Solutions — payment dependence and customer participation

1. P(Q intersect Y) >= P(Q)+P(Y)-1 = 13/20 and <= min(P(Q),P(Y))=3/4. Disjoint complements give the lower endpoint; nesting Q within Y gives upper. Independence gives 27/40, one interior coupling, not an implication of marginals.
2. h=P(Q AND S)=27/40. First paid-window intersection lies in [13/20,3/4], second in [23/40,27/40]. The total B-receipt interval is 16*[49/40,57/40]=[98/5,114/5]. Payment Y2 and Y3 may be allocated conditionally on the common Q,S space independently of each other to achieve either pair of extremal overlaps; an LP can verify the joint realization.
3. Expected accepted B services are 57/40. Gross B buyer value is (24)*(57/40)=171/5. Its minimum expected surplus before the pilot is 171/5-114/5=57/5=11.4. Invoice nonpayment moves wealth between buyer and provider in the stipulated accounting; it does not make the delivered B service disappear or undo physical production cost. Real legal or bankruptcy losses must be added explicitly.
4. Let D be the initial development gap before buying A consent; the A buyer requires a up-front. A B buyer with fixed nonrefundable pilot can provide at most U_min=Vn-X_high. A lender entitled to fraction theta of actual B collections can provide at most M_min=theta X_low under ambiguity. Thus D+a <= U_min+M_min is equivalent to existence of a split for these up-front funding constraints. Additional requirements include: A contract permits displacement with consent; provider IR, enforceable assignment, postqualification service incentive and cash solvency on all relevant paths. None follows automatically from the aggregate inequality.
5. The route needs D+a=12+10=22. Under payment independence U+M=1197/50=23.94 and might appear financeable. Robust U_min+M_min=57/5+49/5=106/5=21.2<22. Yet total real surplus is 14.35-10=4.35>0. A refundable P=14 makes expected B buyer payment pP=10.5 <= 11.4. A robust L=8 is backed by eta=8/(98/5)=20/49<1/2 of actual collections; lender worst expected repayment eta*(98/5)=8. Both date-1 advances total 22; A gets compensation 10. Worst expected B buyer incremental value is 0.9, provider 0.25, lender 0; sample-path terminal A receipts fund 14 refund on Q failure. No extra productive surplus was manufactured.
6. Example: If the date-5 A receivable defaults exactly whenever Q fails, the previously calculated collateral pool for refund may be inadequate. The derivation of B buyer expected pilot cost pP assumes the entire failure refund arrives as promised. If refund recovery is state-dependent, buyer effective cost exceeds pP and robust IR may fail. To test, obtain dated A collections, B qualification outcomes, pledged claims and actual refund/guarantee priority on the same site and customer, not vendor total-hours statistics. Alternatively, a hard two-A-order service floor makes shared-hardware training infeasible regardless of transfers.
