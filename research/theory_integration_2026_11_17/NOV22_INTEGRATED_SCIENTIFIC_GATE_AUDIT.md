# Scientific integration gate: how source data becomes paid production

Review dated 23 November 2026 for the requested **22 November Models A/B synthesis gate**. Gate status: **OPEN**, not passed retroactively. This audit is part of the same *When Capabilities Become Production* argument, not an independent queue-repair paper.

## One model, two levels

**Model A — qualified static production.** Conditional on a learned set of complete admissible recipes Q, a feasible robot/worker/material capacity vector b and actual original buyer budgets/orders D, let \(v(Q,D,b)\) be the solution of the customer-accepted production LP (or MILP if indivisible tickets and deadlines matter). Different embodiment and coordination bundles qualify different complete services; a single benchmark improvement never fixes receipts. LP dual prices are conditional shadow prices, not general equilibrium robot returns. O-ring complete-recipe qualification and process flexibility are established foundations. Source acquisition changes the joint law of Q rather than adding a second independent revenue stream.

**Model B — endogenous stochastic reproduction of A.** A dynamic policy jointly chooses productive source data P, licensed target grounding G, recovery K, teleoperation/simulation as separately priced and rights-qualified inputs, new embodiments E, verification and accepted dispatch. The transition updates original customer demand, accepted-service history, heterogeneous source provenance, learned joint Q, queue/job durations, usable robot/worker capacity and legal rights. The terminal/substage value is exactly the Model A customer-production optimum. \(\mathbb E[\sum_t\beta^t(r_t-c_t)]\) is the objective; learning-generated option value is in future reachable A subproblems. The old R policy is retained whenever it is actually feasible and freely optional.

**New conditional result that clarifies the relationship.** The latest [timed-queue chapter](data_value/VARIABLE_DURATION_RENEWAL_AND_CUSTOMER_RESPONSE.md) proves that the sign and decision relevance of source integration can differ under the same complete-qualification scores because (a) G/K activate real worker-demanding validation jobs; (b) A pays under one completion threshold while a distinct original B is authorized only under an earlier threshold; and (c) legally observing a G validation duration before K is chosen supports a robust policy that dominates an assumed E comparator even though an immediate all-GK commitment has an unidentified value. This is not scientific novelty by itself: the constructive steps use standard scheduling, coupling and sequential decision tools.

## What the theory establishes conditionally

1. Technical data transfer becomes economically useful only through changes in **joint qualified accepted service** in reachable, rights-feasible customer/resource states. Source overlap can be a substitute or complement depending on the topology of the complete production recipe, deadline and shared capacity.
2. The source-data portfolio's marginal value is customer-demand-conditioned, and joint source qualification cannot generally be inferred from single-source benchmark scores or even isolated complete arm records. The relevant information object may be a small policy-specific paid-service contrast rather than the entire high-dimensional source law.
3. The policy changes the next distribution of field evidence, recoveries, customer orders and resource slack. In this feedback, an additional data source can have negative *forced realized* economic value without violating the monotonicity of an optional, costless admissible dataset.
4. Worker-duration/renewal interactions make single-scalar congestion claims fragile. In the two-deadline construction, positive G/K duration association moves mass toward both-earliest and both-latest completions and *reduces the overlap penalty slope's magnitude* from 4 to 3 rather than increasing it.

The first three are mechanism synthesis across earlier derivations, not established universal theorems under one single identified empirical kernel. The fourth is a restricted synthetic counterexample.

## What is not yet sufficient for scientific acceptance

**Identification:** Historical old, P, G-only or K-only paths do not identify lawfully unsupported joint G→K action-specific durations, joint complete-service qualification, original later B demand, or capacity displacement. A new embodiment E is an assumed option until truly independently staffed and accepted. A causal decision certificate must be linked to a verified legal intervention history, *including customers who never paid*. A prior observed G duration is informative only if it arrives before the K action.

**Contribution:** DDA already values future data and aggregation complementarity. LP duality, multilinear coupling, process flexibility, sequential randomization, Bayesian value of information, finite-deadline scheduling and robust choice all have established literatures. It remains unproved whether the integrated data-to-qualification-to-paid-service causal mechanism provides a theorem or empirical finding beyond a careful composition of those literatures. Do not label successive exact witness instances novel economic laws.

**Data:** Actual original eligible offers, accepted paid service, qualitative safety, cross-embodiment technical tests, worker-time job durations and lawful source rights are absent. Official BEA input-output programs provide real possible quantitative routes, but this branch still lacks verified numerical cells and a vintage/classification join to the sector atlas. BEA flows do **not** measure robot-deployable task revenue.

**Replication:** The current example's 624 scoped V8 assertions and 90 independent Python exact checks verify internal finite constructions, but are not full-repository CI, independent scientific review or a field experiment.
