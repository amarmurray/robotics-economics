# Learning packet — multipayer qualification (separate solutions)
*November 17, 2026. These are model derivations, not empirically estimated outcomes.*

1. Expected accepted B units conditional on qualification = 1+sigma. Unconditional units are p(1+sigma). The provider's marginal net cash per such unit is rR-k. Let K be qualification and d displacement: Delta=p(1+sigma)(rR-k)-K-d. Invoice collection value X=p(1+sigma)rR, buyer surplus before pilot deposit U=p(1+sigma)(V-rR). Lender claims M=eta_bar X, eta_bar=min(theta,1-k/(rR)) if operation is voluntary. X belongs initially to the provider (subject to collection), U to the buyer. They are not automatically transferable between parties.
2. Shared p=3/4, sigma=r=9/10, R=16, k=2, K=13, displacement d=4. Delta=67/100>0, D=12. Expected invoices X=513/25. Eligible half M=513/50=10.26 <12. Hence an all-B-claim nonrecourse loan of 12 cannot break even with the available collateral rights even though a fully funded private investment is profitable.
3. Specialist p=9/10, expected collectible B invoices 3078/125. L=8 requires fractional claim eta=8/(3078/125)=500/1539, below theta=1/2 and the operating incentive cap 31/36. B buyer's expected retained surplus before the advance is 2052/125=16.416, so P=12 leaves 552/125=4.416. P+L=20 matches specialist funding shortfall, and the pathwise dated A receipts cover operating outlays after date 1.
4. Qualification fails with probability 1-p. A refund P on failure decreases provider expected proceeds by P(1-p) and increases the buyer's by the same amount; lender unchanged. Shared: 12.67-3=9.67 provider and 1.68+3=4.68 buyer. Specialist: 13.204-1.2=12.004 provider and 4.416+1.2=5.616 buyer. Joint increments remain 14.35 and 17.62. Refund ability itself needs an enforceable future liquidity path and is not automatically assured.
5. The provider receives an expected retained collection (1-eta)rR per service and pays k. Its operation is privately rational if (1-eta)rR>=k, or eta<=1-k/(rR). Here this bound is 31/36. The 1/2 limit binds instead. A legal rule about notification is not proof of first-priority assignment, contractual buyer assent, or fractional cash waterfall.
6. Observe linked complete-order demand/acceptance, source-target skills and site qualification, training downtime, contemporaneous outside production, seller invoices and timestamped collections, contract/default and assignment rights, explicit advance/refund terms, and supplier/customer payment contributions. Compare with an amply funded matched site (invoice timing and ability to pledge should cease to bind when cash and rights are truly slack). Key objections: default, qualification and customer demand may covary; a customer may refuse ex-post risky deposits; A customer welfare and switching/attention costs may change the ranking.
