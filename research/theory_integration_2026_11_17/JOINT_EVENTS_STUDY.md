# Study: qualification events, useful intermediate states, and optimal financing

Read Result 7 in THEORY_MANUSCRIPT.md and the full proof in JOINT_EVENTS_AND_PARTIAL_VALUE.md. Attempt before viewing JOINT_EVENTS_SOLUTIONS.md. All parameters are synthetic.

1. **Reconstruct the controlled process.** State the stopping value, remaining cash constraint, event map \(S\to S\cup A_j\), and the Bellman equation. Why is the cash constraint a *pathwise maximum expenditure* rather than the expected expenditure? What changes if accepted unloading earns cash while case-picking validation is still being attempted?

2. **Economic order versus names of skills.** Assume two distinct complete-service qualifications A and B are both needed to increase operating value by W. Compare the small-budget gross order when (i) two independently acquired single-qualification events at bounded rates are necessary, and (ii) a single observed shared program event establishes both qualifications. Why would splitting one actual joint qualification into two independent labels be an invalid inference?

3. **Interim value and the investment strategy.** Let \(f=(0,v,0,W)\), with targeted success rate a for A or B and joint success rate μ for AB, each requiring unit cost per unit effort. Derive two sufficient inequalities under which A is optimal first for very low available budget but joint is optimal for very high budget. Check \(a=2,\mu=6/5,v=7,W=10\), including the best long-budget targeted sequence.

4. **Falsification and economic accounting.** The vendor advertises case-picking integration with a warehouse management system. Does that alone validate f(A+B)>f(A) at a specific site, prove that B is a separate qualification, or identify μ? Specify the minimum matched customer-level observation that would change the investment conclusion and one competent alternative to a robot-centric A+B bundle.
