# Teach-back 2 — separate solution

1. Odd powers of the two-block kernel have off-diagonal support; even powers have diagonal support. There is no common path length with positive weight for every block pair. Definition 4 therefore is not verified. A failed sufficient condition does not establish failure of its conclusion; this example does not refute Proposition 5.
2. Composition integrates over two blocks of mass 1/2. W^(2)=[[101/200,1/20],[1/20,1/2]]. Every entry is at least epsilon=1/20, so n=2 works. Ordinary unweighted matrix multiplication is twice this composed kernel. With eta=1/2, sigma=2 and equal f, the source-proof recursion gives a conservative all-time effective-data lower coefficient 1/12800. It is not the exact asymptotic data ratio or a calendar forecast.
3. W<=1 gives A_j<=M, while A_i>=bM gives psi_Ki>=lambda(b)*psi_Kj, lambda(b)=(f_min/f_max)*b^eta. The common labor alternative and capital rental rate imply unit cost chi_i<=chi_j/lambda(b). CES demand y proportional to chi^(-sigma) gives y_i>=lambda(b)^sigma*y_j; integrate over the unit-mass task space. An unpaid gate can prohibit the ostensibly cheap capital technique, and capacity can prevent serving CES-implied quantities. Neither is represented by the baseline unrestricted factor choice.
4. The operator cut has available cash zero and obligation one, hence slack -1. Learning alone does not change it. A permitted and available one-unit advance, or a one-unit bridge loan with an economically supportable repayment contract, repairs that cut. Merely calling the provider's five “consolidated cash” does not transfer it. If the fee falls to zero, the engineering/cost environment has changed instead.
5. Mandatory incorporation gives total optimized values 14,13,8: the last version is not funded. Optional incorporation keeps x=1 and gives 14 throughout. The old version remains an admissible plan at unchanged payoff; more options cannot lower the maximum. Costly acquisition, lost qualification, mandatory upgrade or changed rights can alter that comparison, but each must be stated and measured.

The strongest empirical objection is valid unless actual qualification work, timing, payer responsibility and policy-retention rights are observed. These examples diagnose which observations matter. They do not supply those observations, establish a novel theorem, or prove that the source economy's long-run automation prediction is wrong.
