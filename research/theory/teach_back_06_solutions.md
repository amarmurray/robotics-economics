# Teach-back 06 — Worked solutions

1. X enters the OEM's real production cost and, if passed through, the downstream invoice. L changes same-service adaptation and validation expense only with technically valid transfer and reuse rights. C alters reachable prospective customer economics and/or dated signed demand. BOM, yield and invoice cohorts; matched held-out target-service qualification trials; and dated customer opportunity/contract records are needed respectively. A raw experience count does not distinguish them.

2. Entry I_H=(8-3*kappa)+(7-3*delta)=15-3*(kappa+delta); provider budget is 10. Financing requires kappa+delta>=5/3. If chosen, value net of initial unchanged cash and incumbent baseline is 3+16-I_H=4+3*(kappa+delta). These are normalized synthetic units of money; the knowledge stocks are normalized inputs, not robot counts.

3. V_F=5+22/5=47/5. Hybrid strictly preferred if 4+3*(kappa+delta)>47/5, hence kappa+delta>9/5. Values in [5/3,9/5] fund the specialist yet fail to compensate for sacrificing current cash and displaced incumbent expansion. At equality the specified tie rule chooses the factory source.

4. With delta=1, affordability requires kappa>=2/3; private-source preference requires kappa>4/5. At kappa=81/100, X=1, invoice p=8-243/100=557/100, real supplier resource cost u=7-3=4, supplier gross margin 157/100. Qualification cost is 4, provider total value is 3+16-(557/100+4)=943/100. Provider plus realized supplier margin equals 11, exactly 3+16-(4+4). Do not add a manufacturer's *same* cost saving twice.

5. When B capacity 2 binds and customer participation holds, hybrid target provider profit P_B=(8/5)(a-2). At full rights source value becomes (8/5)(a-2)-6. Comparing factory's 47/5 gives a>93/8. For weak demand, output may be below 2 or the customer may choose the outside option; one must solve the original constrained quadratic optimization. More learning is no guarantee of customer demand.

6. Link actual manufacturing/build cohorts and contract invoices, identical target acceptance protocols with and without source L, and matched customer pipeline/accepted paid output and the best alternative. Record rejected opportunities, credit and cash available when choice occurred. Confounders: product redesign, operator experience, initial site selection, changing final demand, integration/software upgrades, different output definitions and rights. A planned 1,000-unit MOU is an intention, not realized training transfer, physical acceptance or signed receipts.
