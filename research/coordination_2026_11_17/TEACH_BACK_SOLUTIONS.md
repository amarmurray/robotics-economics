# Teach-back 4 — Worked solutions

1. States k=0,...,N represent interrupted units. Up transitions have rate (N-k)λ because only working robots are exposed; down transitions min(k,c)μ for c supervisors. Treating arrivals as Nλ even when all units are interrupted invents repeated exceptions from nonworking robots and changes the congestion process.
2. Detailed balance: π_k/π_(k-1)=(N-k+1)λ/[min(k,c)μ]. Normalize the finite product weights. Effective capacity is r(N-Σkπ_k). Arrival flow λE[N-k] equals clearance flow μE[min(k,c)], at most cμ. Thus E[active]≤cμ/λ. With fixed c, μ, λ>0 and N increasing, servers are almost always busy, giving the limiting bound; adding hardware has diminishing incremental expected active capacity in the regime. This is a repairman queue, not a universal law of robotic scale.
3. Halving λ changes the request incidence and expected fleet downtime; doubling work rate r raises accepted output per active unit without altering arrivals conditional on active exposure in this model. For both investments affordable, compare ρ[G(K_D)-G(K_H)] against I_D-I_H, and also evaluate no-investment and additional-human alternatives. The comparison is only valid under the stated pricing and adoption structure.
4. Write C_K=K_HD-K_H-K_D+K_0 and C_K2=K_HD²-K_H²-K_D²+K_0². Then C_profit=ρ[(a-v)C_K-(b/2)C_K2]. With C_K>0, private complementarity flips at d*=(b/2)C_K2/C_K, assuming the customer adopts at every alternative, output equals effective K and there are no interaction costs. At d below d* (but high enough to keep all configurations capacity-bound), the physical complement may generate negative economic complementarity because extra output has diminishing value.

Fewer exceptions might still reduce service cost, SLA failures, variability, warranty obligations or financing needs; those are different benefits and require appropriate data and explicit terms. Serving another buyer could also unlock demand if qualified access and throughput are modeled.

7. Example: correlated sitewide Wi-Fi failures cause simultaneous pauses, violating independent Poisson exposure. Log incidents by common cause and time and compare observed joint-event frequency to the independent prediction; rerun investment options with correlated downtime and actual recovery procedures. The direction of the investment choice is not predetermined: redundant communications, better coordination, more hardware or a process redesign may win under different customer contracts.
