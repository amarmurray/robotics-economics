## Required pre-execution record

- Request ID, owning paper, proposition ID, author and input main/source SHAs.
- Exact proposition and assumptions.
- Falsification question, potential defeating observation and expected informative outcomes.
- Minimum state/action/parameter domain; comparator, ordinary learning/outside option, observation and equilibrium-selection rules.
- Script/command and dependency hashes; exact or numerical arithmetic and tolerances.
- Resource limit and stopping rule; why a smaller analytical check does not answer the question.
- Destination for result, explicit null, counterexample or boundary.
- Logged-before-execution status and coordinating writer.
- After execution: actual command, scope, output, verification, deviations from plan, budget stops, interpretation and claim-ledger update.

A budget stop, missing pure equilibrium or algorithmic nonconvergence must be reported as such. One registered question does not authorize expansion to larger cash budgets, extra variants or multidimensional sweeps. Registration after looking at results is not preregistration.
