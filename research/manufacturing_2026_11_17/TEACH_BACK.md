# Teach-back 05: manufacturing and deployment economics

**Problem.** A robot provider buys hardware from a separate manufacturer. A new generalist unit costs 6 and a specialized unit costs 9 before manufacturing learning. Two earlier generalist builds reduce the manufacturer's later specialist resource cost by 4 but reduce the **provider's invoice** only by the contractual fraction κ. Market B requires an additional fixed integration/qualification bill of 4, and the provider has 54/5 of immediately available date-one cash after the original source investment. The directly bought specialized unit earns 124/5 in later provider operating receipts; the two source units earn 24/5 per period in each of two periods and originally cost 12.

1. Derive the minimum κ that makes the subsequent specialist purchase financeable.
2. Derive the minimum κ that makes the full source-first investment sequence more valuable than direct B entry, conditional on the same B service.
3. Suppose κ=0 but the manufacturer's real resource savings remain 4. Explain how consolidated surplus may improve without relaxing the provider's immediate cash constraint.
4. Suppose the customer B demand intercept falls so both specialist and generalist capacities are nonbinding. Explain why the interior specialist threshold from the note must not be applied.
5. What source evidence distinguishes manufacturing learning, design-to-cost, contemporaneous utilization economies, and procurement bargaining?
