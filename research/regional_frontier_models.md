# Regional model releases, rights and operating evidence

Regional follow-up, 26 October 2026. This pass adds four contrasting dossiers to the existing K work: RLWRLD, Unitree, AgiBot and Alibaba DAMO/Hupan's RynnBrain. Exa returned 40 search results across three research angles, with 36 distinct URLs before artifact-level follow-up. Primary papers, operator announcements, repositories, model/data cards and license files supply the accepted claims. The [evidence packet](../data/frontier_model_economics.json) retains dated versions and distinctions; this is a selective map, not a global census or a ranking of deployed models.

The decision finding is that **public research access, an executable policy, a compatible control stack, commercial permission and supported customer service are separate conditions**. A code license does not settle weight/data rights. A laboratory score does not supply accepted-output economics. A customer's use of robots and its model partnership can both be real while the exact deployed checkpoint and commercial arrangement remain undisclosed. These distinctions constrain the four-architecture and shared-enterprise scenarios without converting source announcements or benchmarks into numerical coefficients.

The RLWRLD distinction is explicit in the pinned [repository license description](https://github.com/RLWRLD/RLDX-1/blob/cf67c31f7f168ebbca6d06e8c3c23914cb94435c/README.md) and [public model license](https://huggingface.co/RLWRLD/RLDX-1-PT/blob/38fb9498af6650915a79cd44cb3169026072081d/LICENSE.md). The latter limits the model and derivatives to noncommercial use and conditions redistribution on attribution/share-alike terms. It does not disclose a separately negotiated enterprise agreement. An independent operating newco cannot simply treat these public weights as a zero-fee commercial input; an incumbent's private agreement is also not established by the public grant.

Unitree provides a useful counterexample to a single brand-level openness label. The [VLA package declaration](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unifolm-vla/blob/ff6c39aeb0454cfb95418c66aef40ca777f935c1/pyproject.toml), [VLA-Base weight license](https://huggingface.co/unitreerobotics/UnifoLM-VLA-Base/blob/06fee5014922ba6791cfe48d1e4aefac995dd8a2/LICENSE) and [selected dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_Dex1_Stack_Block/blob/0b3905f83f5ef5fe64a44af3a1d829830192e7e5/README.md) differ. Its deployment instructions also point to the separately licensed world-model-action repository already recorded in PHY-E15. That repository's current HEAD still matches the earlier inspected revision. SDK, dataset, weight, controller and hardware layers therefore need separate checks; no change to one layer clears the others.

The [AgiBot-World repository](https://github.com/OpenDriveLab/AgiBot-World/blob/98f136ea6b345db1a08451a2f543d67c1789a809/README.md) and [GO-1 model card](https://huggingface.co/agibot-world/GO-1/blob/0bc37be9caa20b5b3d4a569ec462409072d3e244/README.md) state noncommercial terms. The method introduction in March 2025, repository's September 19 release entry and later model-card snapshots are distinct milestones. The documented Genie Studio and remote-inference workflows identify possible integration routes; their commercial service terms, reliability and realized customer costs remain unobserved.

These are artifact-level observations, not a legal-clearance opinion about a complete system. A publisher may offer different private permissions, support or indemnities. Those agreements must be inspected rather than inferred from public availability or from a partnership logo.

## 2. Experimental metrics require their original task and scoring boundaries

RLDX-1's ALLEX average combines two binary task/selection measures with graded card-manipulation and pouring progress. The paper specifies task-specific fine-tuning and 24 trials per task. Its pouring task uses plastic balls, and the card test uses a restricted height for safety. The reported 86.8 mean is therefore not a complete-job success probability. Partial progress can still be useful engineering evidence, but stages are not equal units of human time, cost or accepted customer service. [RLDX-1 report, sections 6.3 and G.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03269v2).

A separate issue occurs in Table 9. The PDF's stated full/partial/failure weights and printed outcome counts do not reproduce its score column. Direct HTML and visual inspection of PDF page 53 show the same values. The calculation below preserves the discrepancy; it does not decide which printed field should be corrected or supply a corrected empirical effect. [RLDX-1 v2, page 53](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03269v2#page=53).

The count/rule reconstructions are 9 + 0.5 x 8 = 13 and 14 + 0.5 x 8 = 18, versus printed score sums of 16 and 20. The generated report table and [saved source-check summary](../model/results/frontier_model_summary.json) expose these inputs side by side.

The [source-arithmetic implementation](../model/frontier_models.py) retains both versions of the score calculation and marks the empirical effect unresolved. It also prevents a mixed progress average from becoming a whole-job probability. Resolving this table needs source clarification or trial-level records; running a different synthetic scenario would not resolve it.

RynnBrain's evaluation is more explicit about two distinct measures: complete-task success and subtask progress. In its three-task, 20-trial-per-task protocol, the reported per-task and generalist final-success means are 86.67 and 91.67, while process-score means are 91.28 and 94.14. The final percentages are consistent with 52 and 55 complete trials out of 60 under the stated protocol; those counts are reconstructed from percentages, not independently inspected logs. The Qwen-initialization comparison uses matched downstream data and optimization settings, while other baselines use different action-chunk lengths. None of these results is a universal cross-embodiment learning coefficient. [RynnBrain 1.1 v2, sections 4.5 and 5.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17977v2).

The two studies use different robots, tasks, fine-tuning and statistics. Putting their headline percentages into a common league table would erase those differences. The packet deliberately supplies no cross-model ranking and imports no benchmark score into the economic scenarios.

## 3. A model's control interface and serving hardware are part of its productive configuration

The RLDX technical account reports an all-modality median inference reduction from 71.22 to 43.70 milliseconds, approximately 1.63 times, for a specific RTX 5090/Core Ultra desktop setup, input shape and action/denoising configuration. This is evidence about that reported optimization experiment. It does not establish a tail-latency guarantee, embedded-board requirement or complete camera-to-actuator deadline. [RLDX technical account, section 6.1](https://www.rlwrld.ai/en/rldx-1).

The [AWS/RLWRLD infrastructure account](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/physical-ai/putting-dexterous-robots-to-work-how-rlwrld-builds-physical-ai-with-aws/) separately identifies H200 training instances, L40S simulation instances, scheduled GPU capacity and storage/orchestration services. Training infrastructure, simulation evaluation and robot-side serving are different resource uses. Their presence does not disclose total paid compute, per-customer serving cost or qualified onboard supply.

RynnBrain's real-robot tests use an attached RTX 4090 workstation. Its framework has separate coordination and interpolation/control loops; the latter's 200 Hz setting is not the VLA's measured inference rate. Its G1 configuration also uses a separate SONIC representation and hand outputs alongside embodiment-specific masking. A shared action interface consequently still requires a compatible embodiment/controller implementation. [RynnBrain control and deployment protocol](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.17977v2).

The [RynnBrain repository](https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/RynnBrain/blob/5b8641993fd265300dba89f079d9b708ffe19ed4/README.md) and inspected [2B](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-DAMO-Academy/RynnBrain1.1-2B/blob/36069aa841fc58f500d307316cbfdf379777ce8a/README.md) and [9B](https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-DAMO-Academy/RynnBrain1.1-9B/blob/22faf91e92a87a3d72d0b61fdae78cf864445a45/README.md) cards identify released cognition/grounding/planning bases. The exact executed checkpoint for the separately reported VLA experiments was not identified in the inspected release listings. Permissive base weights can contribute to downstream R&D without constituting an identified, qualified customer-service policy.

This distinction matters to H and I: a richer general model can improve a downstream starting point, while action interfaces, serving location, latency distributions, sensor availability and support remain additional inputs. The research does not infer that a larger model fits a given robot's compute, memory or power envelope.

## 4. Collection, simulation and productive service have different denominators

AgiBot World's Beta card reports more than one million trajectories from 100 collection robots and 2,976.4 recorded hours. Alpha has its own updated task/hour description. These variants are kept separate because their overlap and raw corpus quality were not audited. A robot collecting demonstrations is a resource used to build a dataset; it is not automatically an installed robot producing paid customer service. The public cards expose noncommercial and access-agreement conditions. [Beta card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/agibot-world/AgiBotWorld-Beta/blob/2252b3fdc88dc67fa5d5e4b771e1b86f5e71c278/README.md), [Alpha card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/agibot-world/AgiBotWorld-Alpha/blob/128665c9e0244c45d1cbe5c13f5a4706afd24f27/README.md).

[GE-Sim 2.0](https://github.com/AgibotTech/GE-Sim-V2/blob/508c60f1a737a54ad2aaa974e63489c7f0c1b1a2/README.md) is an action-conditioned video/world simulator with separately served policies. Its code/data declarations distinguish imported permissive components, other noncommercial material and upstream policy terms. G01/G02 robot support and simulated rollout clips do not establish a new GO-2 policy release or physical operating exposure. This is a potentially useful robotics-R&D complement whose sim-to-physical validation and component permissions remain part of the adoption problem.

The same distinction applies to RLWRLD's hotel relationship. The coauthored AWS account describes workplace skill capture, dedicated space and multi-year data-partnership discussions at LOTTE, with full-scale deployment aimed at 2030 subject to validation. This can be relevant access to human skill and operational context while remaining different from current productive robot service. The account does not provide an operating robot denominator, signed service duration, accepted throughput or customer margin. [AWS/RLWRLD account](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/physical-ai/putting-dexterous-robots-to-work-how-rlwrld-builds-physical-ai-with-aws/).

## 5. An operating customer can also be an investor, data partner and requirements authority

CJ's September 3 announcement reports two dual-arm humanoid robots placing cushioning material in customer-order packing at Yangji Olive Young. It names Robotis for hardware cooperation, Aidin Robotics for hand technology and RLWRLD for RFM cooperation. This is an operator-reported operating use, with a named place and task. The exact deployed model/checkpoint, hardware configuration, asset title, human support, quality-adjusted throughput and payment terms are not identified. [CJ operator announcement](https://www.cjlogistics.com/ko/newsroom/news/NR_00001386).

CJ's later announcement describes a September 18 MOU, earlier equity participation and logistics-specific co-development, requirements and planned PoCs. Its repetition of the same two-robot deployment is not another installation. Nor does a named RFM partnership alone identify RLDX-1 as the executed policy in those robots. The records preserve both the operating claim and the development relationship without merging their maturity or denominators. [CJ co-development announcement](https://www.cjlogistics.com/ko/newsroom/news/NR_00001393).

This is directly relevant to the customer-side inquiry. An incumbent can supply operational data, define acceptance requirements, control worksite access and participate in a model supplier's capital formation while still relying on external hardware, hand technology and software. Those roles can coexist. Their investment, purchases and service payments are separate flows; the public announcements disclose neither a complete ownership/control contract nor a consolidated economic result.

## Implications and remaining evidence

The evidence changes three decision checks. First, a proposed commercial architecture needs artifact-specific rights and an actual service agreement; public availability alone does not settle access, price or reuse. Second, model comparisons need common accepted-work criteria and a qualified control/serving configuration before resource or cash comparisons become meaningful. Third, operator evidence should distinguish customer authority, data capture, model cooperation and productive use, even when they appear in the same announcement.

The shared monthly model can expose the cost of an unfunded installation, support period or customer obligation. It cannot supply the missing license, intervention rate, deployment identity, retention term or supplier cost. The conditional architecture model likewise remains a mechanism comparison with unobserved target costs and compatibility. The new source records constrain what would need to be measured or contracted; they do not turn either model into an observed forecast.

Further work should test consequential counterparties and configurations, including additional regional developers and the still-unreviewed Generalist/Dyna mechanisms, while retaining the broader end-market, digital-AI, supply and quadruped/drone inquiries. Exact deployed-policy identity, commercial rights, complete support exposure and accepted-output economics remain high-value gaps. No outreach, model execution or GPU/hardware run is implied by this source audit.
