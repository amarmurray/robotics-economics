# Teach-back 08 — separate solutions

1. L_H=binom(n,s)(19/20)^s(1/20)^(n-s) and L_L=binom(n,s)(17/20)^s(3/20)^(n-s). Posterior p_s=L_H/(L_H+L_L). The likelihood applies to held-out **whole-order** outcomes; grasp events omit pallet/WMS/deadline acceptance and may be dependent.
2. E[V_B|s]=p_s*(29/25)+(1-p_s)*(-19/25)>3/10 iff p_s>53/96. With equal priors, L_H/L_L>53/43.
3. For n=8 the ratio is (19/17)^s*(1/3)^(8-s). Only s=8 exceeds 53/43. To finance I_B=2 after .08 test spending, the budget must satisfy B>=2.08=52/25.
4. With B=2 any paid test excludes B-entry, leaving only the incumbent if it remains affordable. But costless perfect information would be worth .5*1.16+.5*.30-.30=.43. The valuation of free information does not finance its collection.
5. Testing updates beliefs at fixed version/site/protocol; training/adaptation changes performance, cost or rights. Record dataset assignment, training and testing splits, matched target failures, validation labor, qualified capacity and bills separately. A rollout that changes the policy is not independent evidence of the pre-update policy's target acceptance.
