## Findings that change deployment judgment

The strongest evidence supports choosing a complete service configuration before choosing a robot morphology. In the cases below, mobility, measurement, interpretation, action and business value are successive conditions. Success at an earlier condition cannot substitute for failure at a later one. PG&E's drone test is particularly informative: an inspection aircraft can fly successfully while producing unusable inspection imagery. A model crediting every successful mission with a completed inspection would overstate useful output before reaching any debate about manufacturing cost.

Quadrupeds have direct productive roles in manufacturing and infrastructure. They need not be justified as a temporary precursor to humanoids. The examined customer accounts support sensing, facility information and remote inspection roles. They do not establish a general replacement rate for production or maintenance workers. A humanoid strategy should therefore distinguish entry into existing sensing services from expansion into manipulation and intervention. The second step changes the accepted output, qualification requirements and physical interfaces; it is not a free extension of the first.

The sources also separate available robots from supported productive capacity. Site mapping, route validation, networking, operator training, human review and spare parts can bind even when chassis supply is ample. A manufacturing announcement does not remove those constraints. Conversely, a more capable policy may widen the feasible service set while increasing onboard memory, power, sensors or support requirements. The same advance can expand addressability and make an existing configuration inadequate.

## Service boundaries and the shared atlas

Cases link to the common atlas without adding independent labor totals. BMW supports the manufacturing anchor `CELL-M05-auto-assembly-US-2025`, but its observed service is UK plant maintenance and information capture, not US assembly output. National Grid, Equinor and PG&E inform `CELL-M03-utility-inspection-US-2025`; Equinor's CO₂ facility is an infrastructure comparator rather than an electrical-utility employment observation. Rwanda informs `CELL-M10-hospital-logistics-US-2025`, with interfacility distribution kept separate from movement inside a hospital. Agricultural application is a bounded service-business comparator within the atlas's agriculture family, not a seventh deep market estimate.

Three rules follow. Keep foreign evidence in its observed geography. Preserve the historical service window instead of treating retrieval in 2026 as current field performance. Allocate an inspection, delivery or treated application once even when several robots and people contribute. A drone observing an asset, a quadruped collecting another viewpoint and an engineer approving maintenance can be one service chain, not three sources of avoided labor.

## Quadruped dossiers

### PHY-Q01 — BMW Hams Hall: repeated customer-reported use

BMW's 9 February 2026 account identifies continuing SpOTTO work since 2024: visual, thermal and acoustic collection supports maintenance and the plant digital twin. Experts use the information for planning and quality work. This is stronger than a launch announcement because the customer describes an ongoing role after introduction. It remains customer narrative rather than an audited mission dataset. [PHY-E01: BMW](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0455457EN/production-milestone-for-bmw-group-plant-hams-hall:-british-engine-plant-celebrates-25-years?language=en).

The economic boundary should begin with accepted observations and their use in a decision. Robot-specific capital, recurring support, inspection acceptance, intervention, downtime and before/after staffing are undisclosed. The plant-wide energy improvement is not a robot treatment effect. A larger or richer digital twin could create value through changed planning while leaving payroll unchanged; that is a different business case from labor substitution.

For an entrant, the relevant complements are access to the plant, its equipment history and decision systems, plus integration authority. Equal chassis access would not instantly reproduce those complements. For the incumbent, pre-existing systems and engineers could lower integration effort, but legacy interfaces could also increase it. Neither direction warrants an ownership multiplier. The next useful observation is the cost and acceptance of one recurring route, including reviewer time and resulting actions.

### PHY-Q02 — National Grid Blyth: a bounded trial, not an established fleet

National Grid's 17 February 2023 primary account reports a three-day North Sea Link converter-site trial, with Roboverse Reply assisting. Visual, thermal, acoustic and lidar collection were tested; results were still being assessed before an investment decision. The direct page timed out/returned 403, but its primary text was recovered through the search index. [PHY-E02: National Grid](https://www.nationalgrid.com/who-let-the-dogs-out).

The trial boundary is a useful negative constraint on interpretation, not evidence that the project failed. There is no verified later continuation, abandonment, accepted inspection count or return on investment in this edition. Do not combine it with US Sandy Pond narratives as if both were one operating cohort. The examined record establishes a test of asset access and sensing under site conditions, not continuous operation.

The possible economic advantage is broader access to information while equipment operates. Valuing it requires the actual alternative: inspection during an outage, remote views, fixed sensing or other access arrangements. An avoided outage cannot be booked merely because a robot entered an energized area. The outage would have to be attributable to the replaced inspection requirement and actually avoided. This is a high-priority follow-up because trial-to-routine conversion is more decision-relevant than another demonstration of locomotion.

### PHY-Q03 — Northern Lights: commissioning and operational authority

Equinor's 13 January 2026 description says Roberta needs local route and site training, virtual and physical testing, and training for the operating team. Sture operators can dispatch inspections. More independent fault detection and mission planning are described as goals. The text does not establish an OEM/model revision, mission denominator or uninterrupted unattended service. [PHY-E03: Equinor](https://www.equinor.com/magazine/svein-ivar-the-robot-trainer).

This case identifies a commissioning production function: the purchased system supplies general ability, but useful output requires site-specific configuration and people with operating authority. The distinction matters for expansion. Reusing a route or configuration at a similar site may save engineering, but differences in equipment, hazards and response procedures can recreate work. Count that reuse as a testable reduction in commissioning hours, not an assumed universal fleet-learning coefficient.

Remote inspection also changes where labor occurs. Fewer trips to the inspected area can coexist with control-room dispatch, review and exception work. A service model must retain those activities. Data rights and operational responsibility are not disclosed, so no learning rent or independent operator margin is assigned. The public source supports a constrained role and required complements; it cannot calibrate a supervisor-to-robot ratio.

Woodside was investigated as an additional case. Its Spector URL now redirects to a news index; a primary indexed excerpt supports image delivery through FUSE, but the full source was not recovered. It remains an access gap rather than a fourth completed dossier (PHY-E22).

## Drone dossiers and the limits of their economic evidence

### PHY-D01 — PG&E: successful flight can fail the service test

PG&E's EPIC 3.41 final report, v1.0 dated 27 May 2025, covers April 2022–May 2025. At Silverado, Vendor A met flight criteria while failing image acceptance; Vendor B encountered communications, waypoint and imaging problems. The report's printed pages 15–17, Tables 3–4 (one-based PDF pages 19–21), preserve the distinction. Vendors remain anonymous. [PHY-E04: PG&E final report](https://www.pge.com/assets/pge/docs/about/corporate-responsibility-and-sustainability/pge-epic-project-3.41.pdf).

Later results are mixed rather than uniformly negative. Vendor A was disqualified before Demonstration 3. Vendor B met the Templeton flight criteria but failed Silverado criteria; image capture was not evaluated because its known sensor hardware remained below PG&E standards. The distribution-sensor alert integration was deferred. These findings appear on printed pages 17–20 (one-based PDF pages 21–24), including Tables 5–6 and section 4.5.

This is a field demonstration with meaningful adverse findings, not evidence that all docked drones fail. The aircraft, sensors, communications, dock placement and inspection requirements jointly determine service feasibility. Later hardware may change that result, but cannot retroactively improve the tested cohort. A drone fleet model should measure accepted imagery separately from completed routes, then include review and any repeat visits.

The permitted transfer to ground robotics is structural: measure the entire service, including sensor quality and workflow. The numerical failure rate does not transfer. A camera on a grounded platform faces different standoff, motion, reach and access limits. Likewise, historical BVLOS approval does not establish economic utilization. Permission, installed infrastructure, technical performance and customer demand remain separate gates.

### PHY-D02 — Rwanda: observed service outcomes, unresolved cost-effectiveness

Nisingizwe and colleagues studied 20 facilities using 2015–2019 administrative data and drone deliveries during March 2017–December 2019. The initial edition used the PubMed abstract; this revision checks the publisher full text retrieved through Exa. Road times are estimated because planned collection of hospital road records was prevented by COVID-19 restrictions. There is no control group. The 67% reduction in expirations is an interrupted-time-series estimate per facility at 12 months, not a randomized treatment effect. [PHY-E05: full article, Methods, Results and Discussion](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/langlo/article/PIIS2214-109X(22)00048-1/fulltext).

The time boundaries materially change interpretation. The reported mean of 49.6 minutes includes order preparation and packaging; the 41-minute median used in the facility comparison excludes it. The principal road comparator is a hospital vehicle making a round trip to collect blood. The paper reports median advantages of 79 minutes against CBT driving estimates and 98 minutes against Google Maps estimates for this arrangement. A hypothetical center-origin, one-way road service reduces the corresponding median advantages to 19 and 28.5 minutes. These are different service organizations and statistics, not interchangeable speed multipliers. Table 1, Table 2 and the adjacent Results paragraphs provide the locators.

The authors also state in the final Discussion paragraph that drone delivery was more expensive than road delivery in Rwanda at publication. That adverse cost direction belongs in the dossier even though the paper supplies no matched dollar cost or provider margin. Faster delivery and less waste could still have health or economic value, but the cost-effectiveness calculation remains unresolved. No accepted clinical-SLA fraction or causal mortality benefit is established.

This case is materially stronger evidence for a useful delivered service than a flight demonstration, but it is not a provider-margin study. The mechanism includes geography, dispatch, perishable inventory and timely availability. Comparing only aircraft travel to a courier's journey omits launch preparation, receiving, reserve capacity and inventory design. Counting reduced waste once in the logistics model and again as a separate platform benefit would overstate value.

The humanoid analogy largely fails at the physical mechanism. Aerial transport can bypass road geometry; better ground manipulation cannot inherit that shortcut. The useful research question is whether a different production network changes service quality and total cost. For US hospital operations, indoor handoffs, interfacility deliveries and stock positioning need distinct scopes. This study does not supply an indoor robot productivity coefficient or a current US health-logistics adoption rate.

### PHY-D03 — Agricultural services: activity and illustrative budgets stay separate

North Dakota's regulator reports licensed unmanned application activity for 2025. This is an exposure denominator, not a profitability estimate, and repeated versus unique treated acreage remains unresolved. Missouri Extension's March 2025 T40 budgets separately illustrate farmer ownership and custom-service configurations. Its modeled costs are preserved as hypothetical nominal USD per applied acre; they are not observed costs for the North Dakota population. [PHY-E06: NDAC](https://aero.nd.gov/studies/aviation-statistics/); [PHY-E07: Missouri Extension](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g1274).

Missouri Table 2 changes the system as well as annual acreage: one versus two aircraft, one versus two crew members, and different driving, cleaning and wage assumptions. Its cost difference cannot identify a scale effect holding the production configuration fixed.

The operating-model contrast is valuable. Farm ownership uses equipment within one production business; a custom operator must obtain customers and schedule work across sites. Higher annual utilization can spread ownership costs, yet narrow application windows, travel and weather can prevent the apparent capacity from being sold or used. A two-aircraft business is not automatically twice the useful capacity of one aircraft if crews, loading or time windows bind.

The source's discussion associates its roughly 980-acre threshold with inconsistent comparator wording; this edition therefore does not adopt that threshold as a validated break-even. A defensible matched comparison also needs agronomic efficacy, coverage, timing and drift conditions. The analogy to inspection-service entrants is conditional fixed-cost spreading under usable demand, not a transferable cost curve or proof that robotics-as-a-service is more profitable.

## Actual configurations and the connected physical value chain

Two configuration maps in the JSON pin the commercial SKU and retrieval date: Unitree G1 base and Go2 EDU. They preserve actuation, sensors, energy, compute, communications and known quantities while leaving supplier identities, costs, production yield and qualified capacity unknown. The base G1 and optional EDU configurations are not merged. Go2's low headline entry price is not used as the deployed price of its EDU configuration. [PHY-E08: G1 parameters](https://www.unitree.com/g1/); [PHY-E09: Go2 parameters](https://www.unitree.com/go2/).

These are real product architectures rather than hypothetical BOMs, but they are not teardowns. Joint counts do not disclose actuator part numbers, per-start scrap allowances, life or interchangeability. A shared vendor is insufficient evidence that two systems draw from one fungible actuator pool. The same caution applies to sensor grades, connectors, thermal solutions and spare assemblies.

Compute needs an equally strict boundary. Unitree's optional Orin references do not identify an exact embedded module or installed memory capacity. The lead's common evidence [HI-003–HI-005](digital_infrastructure.md) owns Orin/Thor/H200 specifications. Embedded LPDDR and datacenter HBM are separate resource classes. A richer policy could require a memory or power upgrade even where nominal accelerator throughput appears sufficient; the effect on accepted output must be measured on the configured workload.

## Matched embodiment comparisons and transfer tests

`PHY-C01` compares one accepted utility condition inspection, with equal coverage, resolution and response interval. A digital-improved human process, fixed sensing, wheeled inspection, quadruped, aerial sensing and a possible humanoid are alternatives only where they meet those constraints. Fixed sensing can be attractive for stable viewpoints; accessible level routes can remove the need for legged mobility. Stairs, terrain, energized access and hazardous-zone requirements can change feasibility. A sensing-only service provides no evidence that humanoid hands justify their additional burden. Adding repair or manipulation changes the service and requires another comparison.

`PHY-C02` compares one usable blood order delivered by its deadline. Road courier plus digital dispatch, aerial transport with ground handling and local inventory/replenishment are competing networks. Ground robots may complement indoor handoffs but are not established substitutes for the rural aerial leg. Road access, urgency, payload eligibility and sufficiently dense demand can reverse the preferred arrangement. Neither comparison has enough whole-system cost evidence for a numeric ranking. The correct present result is a feasibility map and break-even condition, not invented prices.

The five structured transfer tests preserve separate source and target evidence. PG&E supports separating navigation from useful information. Rwanda rejects copying an aerial geography advantage into humanoid labor productivity. PX4/ODRI versus Unitree supports testing artifact-specific engineering reuse while rejecting blanket openness. The [FAA's July 2024 UTM authorization](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/faa-makes-drone-history-dallas-area) motivates shared intentions and operating authority for mixed fleets, but neither airspace rules nor throughput coefficients transfer. Agricultural budgets provide a fixed-cost and usable-demand mechanism, without empirical cost parameters for inspection robots. Each card states a target prediction and falsifier; none calibrates the common model.

## Supply-to-deployment model implications

The common engine owns the time-indexed scenario; this contribution supplies its evidence boundaries. Use distinct compatible component pools, reserve service spares for installed cohorts, apply assembly acceptance loss once, retain lead times and finished inventory, and commission only within site/support capacity. Replacements are not net additions. Qualified receipts cannot be generated from announced annual capacity, and net receipts cannot have external commitments deducted twice.

An explicit supported-fleet target needs a backcast of surviving stock, retirements, replacement and expansion units, required accepted completions, assembly starts, component receipts, service inventory and commissioning/support labor. That is necessary-condition logic. It does not prove orders, customer finance or accepted-service demand. The model agent uses virtual configurations for synthetic coefficients; the named G1/Go2 maps must not be treated as cost-calibrated versions of those virtual systems.

Three decision changes follow. First, product strategy should fund the measurement and workflow bottleneck before assuming extra robot capability creates value. Second, an inspection-service newco needs site access, contracts, review and dense support, not only robot capital. Third, expansion planning should test a support-bound case alongside component qualification and manufacturing delays. The available evidence permits these causal structures but does not establish which bottleneck dominates across the economy.

## Coverage and next bounded questions

This edition supplies three quadruped dossiers across manufacturing/infrastructure, three drone dossiers across utility inspection/medical delivery/agricultural service, two real product maps, six openness cards, two matched qualitative comparisons and five physical transfer tests. Routine use is customer-reported and lacks independent route telemetry. Costs, contractual allocation, manufacturing yields and qualified capacity remain mostly unknown. The report and common dashboard must retain these limitations.

The highest-priority next work is a matched recurring inspection cohort: accepted outputs, interventions, review hours, repeat visits, downtime and full configured costs. Next retrieve the disposition of the Blyth trial and configuration-specific service manuals/BOM compatibility. Then inspect data/repair rights and add non-Spot operator cases with comparable exposure. Actual agricultural-service and medical-delivery economics should be investigated before using either as proof of an attractive operating business. The JSON records precise stopping conditions; additional publicity without those denominators would not resolve the decision.
