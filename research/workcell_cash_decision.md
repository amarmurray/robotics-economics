# Workcell gains must survive preparation and paid commitments

Edition 0.56 · 11 November 2026 · same November 14 campaign deadline.

**A faster station can use more total modeled labor and cost more cash.** The existing workcell engine already tests buffering, component identity, preparation, quality, changeovers and supported replacement. This extension compares those complete executions against a matched human or fixed-cell alternative, counts upstream preparation, and derives conditional customer cash bounds. It does not add favorable robot assumptions or substitute simulation for missing empirical records.

The [method and equations](../model/WORKCELL_COMPARISON.md), [complete replay artifact](../model/results/workcell_comparison.json) and the existing dashboard's Embodiment view expose fourteen derived pairs. Eight meet the matched complete-service/funding gate; six do not. All historical worlds and the edition0.53 evidence matrix are preserved. One explicitly reconstructed human comparator faces the same mixed-family jobs; its qualification remains a synthetic premise. No probability or real cost estimate is introduced.

## What the accepted sources establish

The [automotive source audit](automotive_workcell_decision.md) already distinguishes the BMW/Figure02 loading episode, the supplier's Figure03 sequencing/cart demonstration and Liebherr's separate fixed vision/buffer application. CAP-E001/E002 report a production-connected episode and requirements; CAP-E025 concerns different parts and a different plant; CAP-E026 is a newer demonstration. CAP-E024's supplier fleet retirement and CAP-E027's operator integration/pilot account do not establish a customer's contractual replacement, remaining life or current supported capacity. These records motivate A1–A6 qualification and cost questions; they are not a matched cost trial. No source was fetched again for this comparison, and no source number enters a new coefficient.

In particular, do not impose the synthetic three-component kit on BMW's real workflow, turn a loading target into an achieved cycle-time distribution, or call station output completed vehicles. If the target needs transport, that function needs its own qualified and paid comparator.

## Complete-service comparisons

All monetary values below are **illustrative synthetic USD for one declared window**, not observed prices, staffing or ROI. Delta means alternative minus baseline. Every eligible pair meets the same 12-kit obligation. Station staff remain paid $500 in these inherited fixtures; preparation is a separately paid function.

| Complete comparison | Customer cash change | Station work change, person-seconds | Preparation change, person-seconds | Total modeled work change, person-seconds | Alternative station-staff bill ceiling |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Buffered fixed / human | $236.00 | -300 | +0 | -300 | $264.00 |
| Buffered humanoid / human | $372.00 | -300 | +0 | -300 | $128.00 |
| Prepared-input fixed / human | $336.00 | -300 | +360 | +60 | $164.00 |
| Prepared-input fixed / buffered fixed | $100.00 | +0 | +360 | +360 | $400.00 |
| Buffered humanoid / buffered fixed | $136.00 | +0 | +0 | +0 | $364.00 |
| Refill fixed / matched refill human | $236.00 | -300 | +0 | -300 | $264.00 |
| Mixed humanoid / fast-changeover fixed | $136.00 | +0 | +0 | +0 | $364.00 |
| Mixed fixed / matched mixed human | $239.00 | -285 | +0 | -285 | $261.00 |

Prepared-input fixed reduces station work by 300 person-seconds but adds 360 preparation person-seconds: **420 total versus 360 for human**. A narrow station measurement would reverse the direction of this modeled labor comparison. Its $336 higher cash outlay is not a wage estimate; station staff stay paid and the preparation bill is separate. Buffered fixed uses 60 station person-seconds yet costs $236 more than human in this window. Released task time alone does not fund either arrangement.

The staff-bill ceiling asks how low the alternative's station-staff invoice would have to be for cash parity if every other modeled bill and all service remained unchanged. Buffered fixed would require $264 or less, versus its $500 recorded bill; prepared input requires $164 or less. Neither is an executable roster. A lower price with unchanged funded capacity can satisfy the arithmetic, but reducing staff capacity can lose timely service. Actual wages, minimum shifts, competing duties, cancellation/notice and payment terms must establish whether any reduction is attainable.

Against an already qualified buffered fixed cell, prepared input needs an additional $100 and the humanoid $136 of proven net value or cost reduction. Under the explicit fast-changeover premise the mixed humanoid also costs $136 more with no modeled person-time improvement. This is a conditional challenge to an embodiment premium, not a real product ranking or a claim that flexibility is worthless. Actual part mix, geometry, transport and qualified changeovers can change the service obligation.

## Failed service has no cheap economic bound

| Challenge | Alternative timely kits / due | Late / undelivered | Bound |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| direct misses deadline | 0 / 12 | 11 / 1 | Not computed |
| preparation shortfall | 11 / 12 | 0 / 1 | Not computed |
| quality rework is late | 11 / 12 | 1 / 0 | Not computed |
| support gap | 5 / 12 | 0 / 7 | Not computed |
| qualified replacement still late | 11 / 12 | 1 / 0 | Not computed |
| unqualified replacement | 5 / 12 | 0 / 7 | Not computed |

Quality rework eventually delivers every component in its fixture but still misses a required deadline. A paid qualified replacement likewise cannot erase earlier late service. The result retains replacement cash, ownership and prior work while refusing a cost-parity claim for an incomplete obligation. If the user legitimately changes the deadline or required service, both sides need a newly matched replay; the original failure remains part of its history.

## Payer boundary and residual costs

Customer cash reconciles exactly to receipts by station staff, station resources, preparation and replacement equipment. These external payees are not modeled supplier businesses: **provider resource costs and profit remain null**, not equal to receipts. Opening robot/buffer availability, closing assets and replacement ownership are disclosed but acquisition, financing, taxes, maintenance, integration, terminal value and other plant work are incomplete. There is no all-payer lifecycle ranking.

For cash parity, incremental omitted net costs of alternative minus baseline must be no greater than baseline recorded cash minus alternative recorded cash. Thus fixed/human has an allowance of **−$236**, and prepared/human **−$336**. These are already deficits in the bounded cash record: an additional cost saving or independently evidenced net benefit is needed before covering further omitted burdens. The model does not assume those burdens are zero or invent ergonomic, throughput or maintenance benefits. Better quality/capacity may still matter, but it needs its own accepted-service and value evidence.

## Evidence with decision consequences

Use these results to focus the unchanged [A1–A6 acceptance sequence](deployment_evidence_sequence.md), not to mark it complete:

| Missing packet | Minimum useful join | What it can change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 + A3: service and alternative qualification | Exact parts/presentation/sequence/transport/deadlines, accepted IDs including failures, and a matched human/fixed comparator | Reject incomplete substitution; distinguish a buffer/process gain from a mobility or embodiment advantage |
| A3 + A5: complete work and avoidable payment | Same lot/window's station and upstream preparation, QA/transport/recovery duties joined to paid shifts/contracts and effective notice | Reverse apparent labor savings; determine whether the conditional staff-bill ceiling is achievable without losing service |
| A2 + A4: supported configuration and change | Applicable operating entitlement, old/new serial/policy configuration, paid engineering/installation, downtime/fallback and requalification | Establish whether a new generation is usable and timely; retain failed-change costs rather than granting free continuity |
| A6 with preceding gates: payer and asset closeout | Actual paid dates and legal payers, preparation and integration bills, support/repair/finance obligations and remaining asset rights | Replace the bounded cash allowance with an observed-window comparison; leave lifecycle ranking open when life/support/terminal value remain unknown |

The proposed custodians remain target plant production/quality/logistics, payroll/procurement/finance and the actual OEM/integrator; access and possession are unverified. No outreach occurs. A service-only evaluation can use an existing applicable entitlement; a new purchase is not required merely to observe service. Purchase authorization, executed commitment, payment and accepted output stay separate.

**Next discriminating record:** a permitted A1/A3/A5 packet joining complete accepted output, upstream preparation and residual work to the exact paid commitment that could change. Couple it to A2/A4 support and A6 payer/asset records for an economic deployment claim. Without that join, the warranted conclusion is a conditional threshold and a rejected shortcut from station speed to payroll savings. A new actual target, changed service constraint or defensible cost/rights record could justify another replay; repeating the same fixtures cannot acquire the missing evidence.
