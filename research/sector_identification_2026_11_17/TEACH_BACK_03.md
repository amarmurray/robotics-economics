# Teach-back 03: Aggregate production networks versus robotic opportunities

Attempt without the solutions file first.

1. Two sectors each have 40 units of labor value added. A complete robot service has fee 15 and provider resource cost 12. Derive two eligible/retained task partitions with opposite customer-adoption rankings but identical sector accounts.
2. Given a supplier-row/purchaser-column transaction matrix \`Z=[[5,30,10],[10,10,10],[5,5,15]]\` and gross output \`x=(100,100,100)\`, derive final demand and value-added vectors. State what they account for.
3. What is the Leontief response to a local final-demand shock in a reconciled square fixed-coefficient model? Why is the raw BEA Use table not necessarily the required \`A\` matrix?
4. Explain why a high input-output centrality could coexist with low customer willingness to pay for robots. Which actual task and contract observations could reverse the ranking?
5. Suggest one credible empirical observation that could distinguish the two micro economies without observing all economy-wide tasks. State the assumptions required.
