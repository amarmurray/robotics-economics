# Teach-back: embodiment quality versus the demand window

Read the delayed-embodiment research note and attempt these before reading the separate solution.

1. Derive the customer quantity and provider surplus-sharing revenue for a qualified machine under quadratic final demand and a best feasible outside option. State the assumptions required to use the capacity-binding formula.
2. For K_redesign=3, lag_redesign=2, K_software=1, lag_software=0, T=4 and capture share .8, derive the derivative of redesign-minus-software value with respect to linear demand erosion gamma. Explain its economic meaning.
3. Compute the projects' pairwise crossing from D(0)=31.15. Why is a pairwise crossing not automatically a portfolio-choice boundary?
4. Explain why reported research hand assembly time is different from design lead time, qualification effort, manufacturing ramp and date of first commercially accepted output.
5. Give a condition under which hardware redesign opens a conservative service-eligibility edge that no finite software adaptation on the old configuration can open; then explain why that does not itself ensure profitability or financeability.
