# Where should the next audit take place?

A parcel arm encounters a new packaging lot. The digital dispatcher sends difficult picks to a recovery operator. A mobile carrier works under a newer handoff controller. One engineering hour is available before the release decision. At the arm, the engineer could check whether a proposed demonstration supplies anything missing from the incumbent corpus. At the carrier, the engineer could test whether the proposed change helps or harms delivery. The team could also spend the hour filling orders and keep its usual release routine. What determines the choice?

The last study treated a deployment as both a place to discover a correction and a place to earn on it. This extension separates two uncertainties that each demand skilled time. **An investigation can have low value because someone else already supplied its answer, or because acting on its answer at the recipient would make work worse.** Moving the next hour from source to recipient can be optimal as a fleet grows even when its hardware, unit costs and underlying technical probabilities stay fixed. The common assumption that source novelty and successful transfer are independent is consequential enough to reverse the investment recommendation. ## The decision before the equations

The firm is deciding whether to commission and release an additional correction before a deadline. Its ordinary program can route to people, keep the validated checkpoint, train on existing data and already serve part of the work. Let $B$ measure the **discounted incremental contribution at stake on remaining recipient work**, conditional on a distinct correction. It can include a paid order that would otherwise be lost or reviewer time that can actually be redeployed. Ten machines duplicating a nonbinding stage can leave $B$ unchanged. Use the deciding party’s claims and feasible permissions. Report additional accepted service and labor hours separately when measuring this object in a deployment.

Let $U$ indicate whether the candidate supplies a distinct correction relative to ordinary learning by the deadline. Let $T$ indicate whether this correction would transfer beneficially on a recipient-side reference case representing the work at stake. A local compatibility diagnostic can reveal the recipient regime even when the candidate later turns out to be redundant. Write $p=\Pr(U)$, $h=\Pr(T)$ and $j=\Pr(U\cap T)$ given everything the incumbent team already knows. In the solved restriction, a distinct beneficial correction earns $B$, a distinct harmful one loses $B$, and a redundant one changes zero orders or costs. Commissioning costs $c>0$ in all three cases. Keeping the checkpoint has incremental payoff zero.

One idealized source audit reveals $U$. One recipient audit reveals $T$. Both arrive in time and consume the same slot. Each audit is informative about the unobserved attribute to the extent that the two attributes are associated. These perfect audits give a transparent comparison and an upper bound on a noisier version of the same diagnostic. A real teleoperation hour might also recover the current order or generate its own training example. Credit those outputs separately in the comparison.

## When growth moves the audit downstream

Write $[x]_+=\max(0,x)$. The expected value of the release decision before the audit fee is

$$
V_0=[B(2j-p)-c]_+,\qquad
V_S=[B(2j-p)-pc]_+,\qquad
V_R=[Bj-hc]_+ .
$$

Without a diagnostic the team faces the full commissioning cost. After a source audit it declines every redundant correction. Its remaining positive payoff, if any, occurs on the $U$ branch, which carries probability $p$. After a recipient audit the team declines in the harmful regime. The beneficial branch carries benefit $Bj$ and expected commissioning cost $hc$. Optimizing on these branches gives the stated formulas. Buy the best audit only if its gain over $V_0$ covers the audit fee and the foregone contribution of the hour. Different fees, permissions and queues can change which diagnostic wins.

**Prediction under independent novelty and transfer.** Suppose $0<p<1$ and $(1+p)/2<h<1$. At intermediate stakes the source audit is the first to become valuable. It can avoid commissioning a redundant correction even while the expected value conditional on a favorable recipient test is too low to act. At sufficiently high stakes the recipient audit is better. The first hour at the source can screen out wasted commissioning costs. The recipient hour can also prevent a harmful release whose cost grows with the work exposed.

To see the boundary, put $j=ph$. The source and recipient options first become positive at $c/(2h-1)$ and $c/p$, respectively. Once both are active, $V_R-V_S=p(1-h)B-(h-p)c$. Hence the source option leads from its activation up to $B^*=c(h-p)/(p(1-h))$, and the recipient leads above $B^*$. Equal positive audit costs can create a region in which ordinary production wins. Here $B$ changes while $p,h$ are held fixed. A change in actual routing or retention can move all three quantities.

**Counterexample to treating two dashboards as sufficient.** Even a correctly measured novelty rate and recipient pass rate need not identify which audit to fund. Their joint probability can range from $\max(0,p+h-1)$ to $\min(p,h)$. Across that interval, sharp bounds on $V_R-V_S$ follow by evaluating its two endpoints and any interior points $j=hc/B$ and $j=p(1+c/B)/2$ for $B>0$. Between these points the difference is linear, and every permitted $j$ defines a feasible joint law.

For a range check only, use a commissioning cost of one, $p=0.3$ and $h=0.8$. Under independence the source audit leads by 0.26 at $B=4$ and the recipient audit leads by 0.10 at $B=10$. Holding both marginal rates and $B=10$ fixed, the sharp interval for the recipient advantage is **−0.50 to +0.85** in the same cost units. Near one end, distinct source discoveries occur precisely in the favorable recipient regime, so checking the source resolves much of both questions. A firm can measure each headline rate accurately and still choose the wrong audit. The [replication](check.py) enumerates the underlying decisions independently and checks 1,430 finite-state parameter cases. These are analytic and arithmetic results.

## What makes this a theory of a recursive firm?

Suppose a dispatcher changes which difficult jobs reach the arm and the carrier updates its controller. Both changes can alter $j$. A release that removes familiar exceptions may leave novel cases precisely where the carrier performs poorly. More retained source interventions can then increase confidence in novelty while giving little evidence about recipient harm. Retention rights may allow each team to measure its own marginal rate while preventing it from linking the same cases across teams. Merely funding more of each existing dashboard cannot recover the missing association in this model.

A procedure the firm could learn from earlier production is **when to preserve a matched source–recipient reference case**, even at the cost of one fewer intervention for training. Its value depends on whether a better estimate would change the next source purchase, transfer link or checkpoint decision before expiry. A dispatch agent choosing that hour, a supplier able to retain the trace and an operator controlling the paid delivery can have different incentives even with identical predictions. A fixed split between local and recipient audits can outperform a learned rule when the new controller breaks the association on which that rule was trained. The existing simulation already found a late fixed check winning under an unexpected source convention reversal. [Existing result under drift](../../reference_coverage/README.md#where-joint-funding-pays-and-where-it-loses)

This is the link to the marginal product of capital. Changing the audit location first changes which release the firm uses. The release can change completed orders, recovery labor or the load on a downstream carrier. Measure the marginal physical product of another carrier by varying its available service against that new dispatch and the best fallback. A gain on existing orders can accrue entirely to the host and leave the supplier’s next installation unfunded. The audit-choice formulas alone establish neither a physical return per robot nor a new installed unit. [Network marginal product](../../network_mpk/README.md)

## The discriminating observation

Open X-Embodiment's published comparison supplies an operational reason to separate the two ends. The same Bridge-data addition in the 55B mixture moved success by 33 percentage points on selected emergent skills and seven on the broader generalization evaluation. The study identifies task-specific technical gains for those protocols. It does not observe dated source novelty and recipient contribution on paired original commercial orders. [Open X-Embodiment, Table II](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.08864v9)

Assign a small authorized reference sample **before** routing. For each selected job retain the candidate’s distinct contribution against the ordinary corpus and its effect on the recipient against the checkpoint. Include diverted jobs, refusals and delayed qualifications. Vary source audit, recipient audit and ordinary recovery across independent release groups at equal total resources. Cross that assignment with recipient-only workload, keeping source opportunities fixed. Measure net accepted work, role hours and deadline misses. Separate source-only and recipient-only spending can be compared with a jointly funded paired panel at the same budget. Joint funding adds identification only if it actually obtains linked observations the parties could not already access. Linkage itself does not cure a selected sample.

Forecast the ranking before a held-out supplier lot, controller change or new embodiment. The crossover prediction can fail under dependence, asymmetric harm, noisy diagnostics, unequal delays or a checkpoint the operator can selectively apply. Measure those rivals. A systematic opposite ranking when the maintained conditions and stakes thresholds hold would reject the restriction. To attribute a gain to the organization, carry *only its rule for buying the next audit* to a fresh site. Compare it with the strongest local team, fixed allocation and matched audit calendar under the same executor and budget.

**Next unresolved question.** When recipient validation returns one release late, how much of the association measured on the paired panel survives until the firm can use it? A stale but precise result may lose to current source evidence or ordinary recovery. Estimate that persistence across releases before pricing the next hour.
