# Can a fleet discover which incidents generalize?

The [adapter experiment](../transfer_validation/ADAPTER_REPAIR.md) supplied the partition of cases sharing a correction. Here the organization knows four observable binary case attributes, pays for incident labels and learns which attribute and orientation can predict the correction. It can learn separately by embodiment or infer whether the arm and mobile robot should share evidence. The economic outcome is net contribution on complete orders. An independent family panel measures generalization.

**Result.** With distinct robot rules drawn independently from the feature dictionary, the learned partition improved agreement on untouched families by 8.18 percentage points versus shuffling feature identities. It earned 69.93 more units per fleet than that placebo. A simpler competitor repaired familiar cases immediately and earned 2.15 more than discovery on average. The paired interval includes zero. Generalization is economically valuable relative to a broken representation, yet the firm also has an opportunity to specialize at low cost.

## One unit of scarce learning capacity

Each world runs 72 shifts with two offered jobs per shift. A digital routing agent must succeed before a fixed-arm or mobile stage can finish. A complete job earns 7 or 10 units. An aligned physical action succeeds with probability 0.94 and a misaligned one with probability 0.12. Two digital releases degrade routing. A failed route can fund a digital update toward a 0.96 ceiling for 0.30. A routed but incomplete physical job can instead supply a paired diagnostic label, subject to lane retention of 0.85 or 0.55. At most one update goes through each shift. Eligible robot labels take priority and cost 0.90 inclusive of the write. Buying one can delay recovery of the donor. Successful physical work removes future training incidents. These are fixed assumptions about rights, prices and access.

At shift 12 each embodiment's convention flips on eight of 16 families. The digital controller has its own two shocks. Demand shifts toward previously unseen families at shift 36. Site-specific feature assignments, labels, realizations of orders, routing and completion are shared across comparison arms. Eight families permit training, four different families permit qualification, and four are untouched by both. A physical label is noisy with probability 0.08. Each budgeted validation costs 2 and draws two qualifying cases per embodiment with replacement. It compares keeping the deployed rule, retiring to no correction and installing a candidate. Ties favor the incumbent. Release follows two shifts later. Family-specific immediate repair also competes as a separate governance design, without a release gate. The supplied comparator knows the true original feature and orientation, an information advantage.

The discovery agents consider both orientations of four feature splits and two constant rules. Scores decay by 0.96 per shift. A match adds one and a mismatch subtracts one. The implied weights are tempered evidence scores. They do not correct for incident selection. At each release, weighted predictions determine the candidate. Calibration on 128 worlds selected among 4-, 8- and 12-shift review intervals and between uniform incident selection and priority by predictive disagreement. The separate learner selected uniform selection and eight-shift review. Its matched shuffle uses the same settings, fees and opportunities with a random reassignment of feature identities. Actual label purchases can diverge as releases change failures.

## Held-out production and generalization

We froze the selected designs and drew 1,024 fresh worlds. Each alternative regime uses the same random tapes. Brackets are paired 95% Monte Carlo intervals over worlds, conditional on the calibration, with no multiple-testing adjustment. Net units accumulate over 72 shifts. Panel agreement is averaged through deployment with equal weights across the four untouched families and both robots.

In the first row the separate learner completed 81.28 paid jobs versus 79.13 for immediate repair. With nine release checks, 10.52 paid labels and 19.58 donor updates, it earned 655.27 versus 657.42. More completed work can coexist with lower contribution. A reserved digital slot improved discovery's relative return versus the raw donor by 4.54 [4.01, 5.06], before any fixed cost of provisioning the slot.

## A firm can learn how widely to pool evidence

Suppose a rule has prior mass 1/H across H candidates. Write S(j,h) for accumulated evidence on robot j for rule h. Comparing one common rule to two independent rules with equal prior model odds gives the following operational odds for pooling.

$$
O = H\frac{\sum_h \exp(S(1,h)+S(2,h))}
{\left(\sum_h\exp S(1,h)\right)\left(\sum_h\exp S(2,h)\right)}.
$$

The common and separate predictions receive weights O/(1+O) and 1/(1+O). Agreement on diagnostic cases changes the organizational rule for the *next* prediction. This is a finite hypothesis model with discounted scores and an imposed rule dictionary. In the common-rule regime, the tuned mixture earned **45.88 [42.12, 49.64]** more than separate discovery and **41.74 [35.95, 47.53]** more than immediate repair. Always pooling earned another 4.75 [2.51, 7.00]. With independently drawn robot rules, the mixture earned **13.73 [11.03, 16.43]** more than always pooling and **2.15 less [−5.63, 1.33]** than separate discovery. Holding acquisition and review settings fixed, the mixture gained 44.52 [40.84, 48.21] over separate learning under the common rule and lost 3.54 [0.07, 7.01] under independent rules. The return to cross-embodiment data rights therefore depends on how often the same observed features diagnose the same fault.

## Identification, falsification and the next decision

Changing the governing attribute only on untouched families leaves every acquired training label, qualification score and release decision unchanged under matched tapes. To see why, hold the policies fixed at the start of a shift. The changed families enter neither acquisition nor qualification. Digital routing failures depend only on the shared digital state and draws. The two worlds therefore update the same digital and physical policies, which establishes the claim by induction. Their paid orders can differ. The inference assumes the organization uses only acquired labels and validation for learning. A firm that actively interprets unretained aggregate payouts has another information channel.

This is a concrete failure of transport to a new task group even when the established sites keep passing release checks. The next discriminating experiment is to give the firm a choice between one more incident label and permission to validate at an unrepresented site. Randomize a small target audit allowance across releases, compare with the same number of extra incumbent audits, and allow a competing agent to learn from aggregate payouts. Price targeting, evaluation lag, false alarms and foregone training. The prediction is that target checks earn more under geographically concentrated shifts, especially when aggregate settlements conceal where errors occur. If incumbent audits do equally well despite indistinguishable incumbent records, look for an additional channel such as location-tagged settlements. If target checks detect the shift but fail to earn their price before the next release, coverage has diagnostic value and no demonstrated financial return at that cadence.

These are symbolic controllers and synthetic outcomes. The family encodings and drift process were supplied. No scaling exponent was imposed or estimated here. The [published robot score reanalysis](../learning_dynamics/README.md) measures physical task performance at different data breadths. It lacks the incident, training and settlement records needed to estimate this mechanism. No physical robots or pretrained LLMs were evaluated in this experiment.
