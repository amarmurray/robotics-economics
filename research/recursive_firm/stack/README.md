# The buying power of a teleoperation hour

Recursive Firm Theory · full-stack economic control, bilateral contracts and collective authority · 20 November 2026, US Eastern

A deployer can use an operator-hour to save a customer's present order, qualify a model update, or discover which machine the customer actually needs. An independent model provider, a hardware owner and a deployer can each own part of that opportunity. Common equity can put several decisions in the same hands. A contract or jointly governed organization can also give someone the authority to make them. The test is which transformations of an observation into *paid work* are feasible when the observation arrives, who pays their costs and who can collect the proceeds.

## The estimand

Let $V_G(s,h)$ be the highest achievable expected contribution starting from state $s$ with $h$ available operator-hours under arrangement $G$. The state includes buyer orders and margins, manual fallback labor, installed embodiments, feasible retrofits, outstanding qualification, compute, separately licensed corpora and beliefs. The arrangement specifies who can observe, retain, train, validate, deploy, amend or transfer a right. Origins and destinations matter. The party who pays for an intervention and the party who learns from its consequences can differ.

$$
\lambda_G(s)=V_G(s,1)-V_G(s,0),\qquad
\text{effect of changing the organization}=\lambda_{G'}(s)-\lambda_G(s).
$$

This is gross willingness to pay for the marginal hour after accounting for other operating, qualification and hardware costs. Deduct the price of the operator-hour once to obtain net gain. When the hour is already bought, the relevant cost of redirecting it is its best alternative use. The same calculation can use a particular firm's collectible cash rather than joint service contribution. Fees between participants redistribute cash. Each party has its own participation test. Report the physical quantity effects by end market separately. In the main witness the first hour raises expected downstream plant volume by one accepted unit, leaves paid downstream depot and digital volume unchanged, and adds 16.47 contribution units. The current rescued order is counted once as an immediate receipt. A financial shadow value and a physical marginal product answer different questions.

**A result that generalizes.** Define $g_h=V_{G'}(s,h)-V_G(s,h)$. The change in the price of the hour is exactly $g_1-g_0$. A new machine can strictly improve the organization under both labor endowments and still lower the price of the next hour. For a simple ex ante substitute with fixed value $z$, adding the machine changes the hour price from $V_1-V_0$ to $\max(V_1,z)-\max(V_0,z)$, between zero and its old value whenever $V_1\ge V_0$. A data-dependent retrofit can have a greater gain after a demonstration and increase that price. When equipment can be selected after diagnosis, a bypass can also create a valuable choice about whether to install it. The sign then depends on the timing of deployment. No universal return to full-stack ownership or data retention follows from free disposal.

## The operating economy

The autonomous digital roles dispatch the operator, select a post-training corpus and authorize a capital decision. The physical fleet has an installed fixed arm at a plant and an installed mobile manipulator at a depot. A compatible tool retrofit or a dedicated fixture can be commissioned, subject to the capital menu and deployment deadline. A digital service agent releases manifests for both physical workflows and serves a separate software queue. A paid human desk worker or floor worker can replace an unqualified stage when the complete order remains profitable. The research object is an economics-scale control environment. A production LLM, contact dynamics and real robots were not run.

| Stage | Feasible decisions and information |
| --- | --- |
| Dispatch | In each of two shifts, rescue an urgent one-off order, support a fixed-arm job, support a mobile job, or wait. An operator can work once per shift up to the available hours. Rescue receipts are 5, 2 and 1 respectively. A qualified source stops supplying that kind of unresolved case. There is at most one independent field packet per source family in a batch. |
| Learn | Spend one of two compute slots on independently licensed digital records, synthetic arm data, an admitted arm demonstration or an admitted mobile demonstration. A slot costs 1. The ledger qualifies the digital route. The simulator qualifies the arm with probability 0.5. A local demonstration qualifies its native source. Portable arm data can also produce a mobile retrofit candidate with probability 0.8. Portable mobile data can qualify the fixed arm with probability 0.2. Update and validation outcomes are observed before the next dispatch. |
| Use rights | A sealed deployment yields its current rescue and clears the live work item. It supplies no persistent training record or cross-site diagnostic. Settlement-only adds an independently permitted one-bit batch diagnosis, with no reusable trajectory or model update from that episode. Local adds a source-only training right. Portable adds explicitly licensed cross-site training. The separately funded ledger and simulator retain their own rights. The two physical sources can have different contracts. |
| Settle | The reference order basket contains three digital, two plant and four depot units with margins before manual fallback of 4, 6 and 8. A missing digital stage costs one desk-hour at 3. A missing mechanical stage costs one floor-hour at 10. Decline an order whose completed margin is nonpositive. One quarter of a digital/plant basket also arrives after the first training decision. |
| Deploy equipment | A new depot batch is difficult with probability 0.55. An easy batch works on the stock machine. A hard batch needs a locally qualified mobile controller, a portable arm candidate with the retrofit, or the dedicated fixture. The retrofit costs 3 and the fixture 11. The current plant or depot pilot can return the batch class if permitted. This shared diagnostic is a stipulated informational relationship. |

The first two shifts represent a live service and independently qualified calibration of prospective work. Source eligibility depends on the operating policy, while permission to use the **target** batch class is explicit. Every bid, update and hardware decision uses only admissible histories. The hidden target class, unobserved candidate results and potential outcomes of other policies are reserved for evaluation. The retrofit raises the first-hour value by **4.14** and shifts the best *source* of training. Adding the fixture to that option set raises one-hour contribution by **2.31** but raises the no-hour alternative by **6.60**. The hour is consequently worth **4.29 less**. This explains how an effective physical intervention can depress a robotics firm's internal bid for teleop even as its business improves.

**Robustness and boundaries.** The 128-case factorial varies digital, plant and depot demand, the difficult-batch probability, desk and floor labor costs, the direction of cross-embodiment transfer and fixture cost. Adding a fixture to an existing retrofit menu reduces the hour price in 64 cases, raises it in 22 and leaves it unchanged in 42. The extra fixture never reduces attainable total contribution. The full portable hour price spans 5 to 23.70 on that grid. In one positive fixture case, one digital, three plant and two depot orders, 25% hard batches, desk/floor costs of 2/7 and a fixture costing 6 yield an increase from 14.70 to 15.10. Flexible hardware makes an informative hour valuable in that setting.

## The price of a demonstration the firm never collects

Two independent customers can grant portable rights at their existing deployments. Either customer can keep its work sealed. Take their private costs of granting access to be 6 at the plant and 2 at the depot. With one operator-hour, an arm license alone adds 11.47 to operating contribution above sealed work. A mobile license alone adds 5.02. Both together add 11.47. The underlying service, machines, available model and operator-hours are identical across these offers.

The contracting benchmark uses Clarke pivot procurement. The sponsor commits to choosing the package that maximizes the operating gain it can collect less reported license costs. A selected seller receives its reported cost plus the difference between attainable surplus with and without its offer. Under quasilinear private reservation costs, reporting truthfully is a weakly dominant strategy. The payment rule is designed. Source selection, participation at those payments, and the division of earnings follow from the parties' alternatives. [Vickrey and the economics of incentives](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/vickrey-lecture_laffont.pdf)

| Who can bid and who captures downstream returns | License purchased | Payment to selected customer | Sponsor gain, after paying | Joint gain above sealed service |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Only plant can offer, sponsor captures all returns | Arm | 11.47 | 0.00 | 5.47 |
| Both can offer, sponsor captures all returns | Arm | 8.45 | 3.02 | 5.47 |
| Both can offer, sponsor captures half of returns, residual beneficiary contributes nothing | Mobile | 2.51 | 0.00 | 3.02 |

Merely having a credible alternative at the depot makes the selected plant license **3.02 cheaper**. The depot keeps its records sealed and receives no payment. The firm's bargaining position improves while the selected trajectory, fleet productivity and combined surplus stay the same. That distinction matters when attributing a commercial return to data capital. Appraising deployments exclusively by counting training rows misses the value of an available substitute. Counting that saving as additional fleet production overstates the return.

Dividing downstream claims changes an actual learning decision. When the sponsor captures half, the plant's privately collectible 5.735 falls below its cost of 6, while the mobile license can still cover its cost of 2. The sponsor buys from the depot and forgoes 2.45 of achievable joint gain. Giving the sponsor a contract on the whole increment restores the plant choice, just as common ownership would if it secured the same claim. With no operator-hour, all incremental operating gains in this market are zero and neither license is purchased. Confidentiality costs and the procurement rule are stipulated. Whether sites seek deployment in the first place remains open. [Executable procurement and settlement](experiments.py) · [Both endowments and each party's proceeds](results.json)

## Both forms of recursive organization

The integrated operator, the independent contracting parties and the jointly empowered dispatch agent face the same resources, external licenses and observed states. We compile a complete state-contingent agreement into action lookup tables before running independent forward settlements on all positive-probability latent event histories. A pure change in ownership labels creates no difference. With one hour, timely integrated control and an equally timely bilateral book both earn **56.47**. Case-by-case signatures implemented on time with probability 0.4 earn **54.076**, including the best ex ante fallback. The associated increase in the first-hour price is **2.394**, the same difference in this particular case because the zero-hour alternative is unchanged. That number prices timely authority relative to this comparator.

**Can the parties choose to create that authority?** A hardware/model partner receives 25% of the incremental service contribution, bears a verification charge of 1, and can refuse to sign. The deploying partner receives the rest and can propose quarter-unit transfers up to its own expected benefit. At zero transfer, the hardware/model partner rejects. The first accepted offer is 0.50. The two parties' net gains are 0.0985 and 1.2955, totaling 1.394 after registration. At a cost of 3 the negotiation fails. These are self-interested bilateral acceptances within a supplied bargaining protocol. They do not show spontaneous invention of the protocol or truthful price discovery under all market structures.

**Does continuing authority do something a contract cannot?** Two equally likely public demand regimes provide either 10 digital, zero plant and one depot order, or one digital, five plant and six depot orders. A fixed mandate can react to operational evidence within its allowed rules. When it must use the same policy across demand regimes, its optimal policy is obtained at mean order volumes because each fixed policy's payoff is linear in those volumes here. With one hour that rule earns 67.97. A jointly revisable mandate earns 70.535, exactly matched by an independent bilateral agreement covering both public contingencies. Without the operator-hour the value of conditioning is larger, 4.3625 instead of 2.565. Organizational adaptability can improve the *alternative* to teleop enough to lower the marginal bid for one more hour.

We also replace the founders' representatives in half the batches. A personal agreement needs fresh signatures, available in time 40% of the time. It earns 69.7655. A transferable agreement and a durable joint office earn 70.535 gross. The carryover right is worth 0.7695, or 0.2695 net if registering it costs 0.50. A local transfer makes both parties willing to ratify the office. The discriminating treatment is who can bind successor resources after a surprise. Transferable contracts, collective offices and common ownership can coincide on observed production. New contingencies that are expensive to specify, delay, exit or an actual restriction on amendment are candidates for separating them.

## Economic reinforcement learning in both directions

**Learning where a retained update transfers.** A posterior-sampling allocator starts with equal odds on two rival transfer laws, 0.8/0.2 or 0.2/0.8 for arm-to-mobile and mobile-to-arm. It observes a cross-site result only after choosing, admitting and validating a licensed update. It receives no label for an untried update. Over 24 training batches per seed the sampled law changes the optimal dispatch. The next qualified result changes the posterior. On 1,000 fresh batches per seed, all twelve frozen allocators for each law chose the appropriate donor and matched the known-law controller in this small two-law problem. Paired mean contribution was 56.43 for high arm transfer and 55.64 for high mobile transfer. Always rescuing the highest-current-receipt job earned about 45.02 in each. This is learning among supplied hypotheses with informative licensed trials.

A two-hour paired fork makes the feedback observable within one deployment. Use one plant and two depot orders, no separate software order, and allow a retrofit. Hold the hard batch, mobile outcome and every other input fixed. Both firms use their first hour on the arm. When its cross-embodiment test succeeds, the second hour rescues an urgent order, compute goes to digital release records, and the firm installs the retrofit. When the test fails, the second hour produces a mobile demonstration, compute goes to the native mobile policy, and the existing machine serves the order. The arm demonstration has changed the marginal value of **which data to collect, which existing rows to train on and which embodiment to deploy**.

**Training the lower controllers as well.** A separate executable environment removes the assumed cross-site success probabilities. A digital agent makes a binary manifest decision. A fixed arm and a mobile unit make a binary alignment move from four task families with different arrival frequencies. Three stochastic policies learn actual action logits. Each funded update makes four policy-gradient steps on a one-step reward for the unique successful action, using an independently licensed ledger or admitted demonstration. The highly simplified binary simulator makes both action rewards inferable from a correction. The collector draws from failures of the *current* policy. A legal pool weights eligible donors and then task families. Duplicate cases never create a new family. A source-only update changes local weights. Software records can be replayed while the operator rescues an urgent job. The mobile action coordinate reverses for two families, and alignment hardware can reconcile those coordinates. This is a second microeconomy and does not calibrate the transfer law of the first one.

Over six shifts the upper Q policy can commission local or pooled corrections, replay existing authorized records, use the ledger or dispatch urgent help. It sees remaining shifts, the weakest validation domain and whether an authorized pool exists. Its reward includes current service margin and the change in expected downstream contribution from complete orders, less compute. These changes telescope so the terminal order basket is counted once. The lower policies change the distribution of future eligible cases. Across three demand/embodiment settings and four independent training replications per setting, the economic learner beats the policy that trains the lowest-accuracy domain in all 12 comparisons. It beats the strongest fixed schedule in 4 of 12. The fixed comparator was selected among 49 repeating pairs on separate calibration seeds. All policies are scored on the same 160 held-out worlds per setting. The reported intervals quantify sampling error within those worlds conditional on a trained policy. The training replications show additional variation.

**Degrees of recursion leave different observable traces.** A controller can update how to execute the same task. An allocator can respond to that update by changing which tasks it samples next. A contracting organization can respond to the resulting economic evidence by changing who may pool data, allocate compute, or commission equipment. A successor can inherit the authority to revise those rules again. Those dimensions can move separately. A specialist supplier might learn extremely quickly within one customer and have no ability to change the next site's model. A coalition with transferable authority can act on both sites without buying either one. At the slowest level, a proposed decision rule for changing the lower objective must earn the delayed gains from that change net of evaluation, coordination and switching costs. Varying the interval between reviews relative to model qualification and demand changes is a direct test of the proposed adiabatic allocation rule. The studies here measure individual links and leave that timescale comparison open.

## How to take the mechanism to deployments

Give matched sites the same eligible orders, compute access, operator time and equipment quotes. Within permission and qualification boundaries, separately vary the right to receive a settled diagnostic, local training, transferable updates and authority to act before the downstream deadline. Hold equity fixed for the authority treatments. Compare against a priced contract that can exercise the same rights at the same speed. Record original refused orders, independent source families, model versions, validation failures, complete orders accepted by each buyer, real labor, hardware commitments, transfer payments and external deployment rights. Use the hour/no-hour and organization/no-change contrast on net output and the underlying service vector. A field design that omits the no-hour hardware choice cannot identify how much of the hour's buying power the organization produced.

The research implication for entry is that two equally large customers with identical immediate service margins may warrant different bids. An authorized observation can change the optimal downstream model or hardware investment. If the supplier captures those gains, it can rationally subsidize a low-margin source deployment. If a better bypass later clears the bottleneck without demonstrations, that subsidy can disappear even while fleet productivity rises. The next branch study should let competing firms sign, decline and renew new service deployments, design the rights packages themselves, and face an unlisted contingency. Measure which high-margin sealed and low-margin data-rich sites actually enter, who funds the missing complement and how costly amendment is. Varian's work on versioning suggests allowing customer types to self-select across service, timing and permission bundles. [Shapiro and Varian on versioning](https://inforules.com/models/m-version.pdf)
