# Who pays to keep a fleet observable?

A parcel arm tries a grasp, a mobile robot receives the parcel, and a digital dispatcher decides which cases go to recovery. An operator who sees only retained interventions has two explanations for a quieter queue. The fleet may have improved, or the dispatcher may have changed which failures leave a record. Adding another warehouse can replicate the same ambiguity at larger scale. In Amazon’s Robin study, engineers deliberately collected a subset of picks under a different sampling rule. That establishes that changing how a fleet samples is operationally possible. It does not price a standing audit or establish the cross-firm effects studied here. [Li et al., training and evaluation samples](https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss19/p023.pdf)

The scarce input here is an independent case that someone can validate **before a release**. The reusable procedure is a rule for choosing the first diagnostic at fresh physical installations. A check can qualify a more productive method on the arm or mobile robot, changing the mix of downstream orders that can clear. The two participants share net operating proceeds, while a sponsor may have to pay the whole cost of preserving reference coverage. When will a joint fund keep observing a production process that an individual participant would allow to go dark?

## Why an archive can get arbitrarily large without answering the question

Let the hidden production convention be $m$ and let $y$ denote which of two diagnostics is informative on a fresh case after accounting for its visible marker. The original roster has $\Pr(y=1\mid m)=.75$ or $.25$. For each currently deployed digital routing rule $a$, choose signed admission masses $b_y(a,m)$ and retained masses $w_y(a)$ with $0<w_y<b_y<p_m(y)$. The dispatcher admits a case with probability $b_y/p_m(y)$ and the site retains an admitted case with probability $w_y/b_y$. These propensities are unknown to the learner. The construction represents hidden case features correlated with the eventual signature. Assume the total admission mass is constant across conventions for a given rule.

Then the probability of a retained signed case is exactly $w_y(a)$ in either world. The masses of admitted-but-unlabeled and unadmitted cases also match. By induction, a dispatcher that changes its route in response to past visible records generates the same observable history distribution under both conventions until it buys independent evidence. More copies of the archive cannot identify the convention. This is an existence and identification result. In a real fleet, an extra settlement field or an observed rejection reason can break the equivalence. The leakage control below does so.

An independent reference draw from the original roster has a likelihood ratio of three or one third. Let $H(b)$ be the optimized terminal contribution at belief $b$ over the convention. The value of a single timely test is the expected $H$ after the update less $H$ without it. It is zero when the optimal action remains the same throughout the reachable beliefs. A participant receiving share $s$ will sponsor a test at fee $f$ if $s$ times its gross decision value exceeds $f$. A joint fund can buy when that gross value exceeds $f$. Thus a governance difference occurs in an interval of fees. Both organizations can make the same purchase outside it. This distinction also survives when firms keep separate ownership and grant identical rights to use the resulting procedure.

## What was compared

Twelve source shifts precede a fresh installation decision. The convention flips with probability .035 between shifts. The incumbent knows the site type, its prior (.67 or .33), the drift law and feasible operating options. It can use all ordinary records, keep each embodiment’s native checkpoint, and specialize its diagnostic by site. The reference program pays a separate fee per labeled roster case. Its dispatcher updates its belief and changes which cases it admits or retains. In the ordinary environment an eligible reference is independent of the ordinary archive case conditional on the convention. A validation result arrives one shift later. Each target still needs its own diagnostic. A correct, incorrect or native diagnostic qualifies the improved method with probabilities .85, .25 and .57 respectively. A failed test leaves the original machine available.

For each combination of qualified stages, a production program allocates arm and mobile capacity plus shared handling and technical-review hours between warehouse and supply orders. Each warehouse order requires 1 arm unit and 0.4 mobile units. A supply order requires 0.55 arm and 1 mobile. Orders contribute 3 or 4 synthetic units after routine variable costs. Before tests resolve the firm can buy an arm or mobile block at 2.25, or wait. The primary outcome is total contribution after installation and audit fees. We record qualifications, both final quantities, both gross stage workloads, role hours and the next installation separately. Per-site capital effects force one additional block and compare with no installation while allowing diagnostics and delivered quantities to adjust.

Two governance arrangements receive the same information and technical menu. A common fund maximizes joint contribution. Under unilateral sponsorship the claimant receiving 55% of operating contribution pays the entire audit bill and the 45% claimant can also use the result. The larger claimant is the more willing sponsor at these proportional payoffs. This benchmark omits enforceable cost-sharing side payments. When parties can contract at zero cost on the same shared audit, the joint choice is attainable across a firm boundary.

The standing schedules were selected on 12,000 separate calibration worlds. Matched comparisons use 24,000 fresh worlds per regime and common random draws. The adaptive renewal rule can buy up to six labels and has a separately budgeted Bellman problem. At each shift it can wait or buy, update on the label, subtract the fee and retain one fewer remaining slot. Terminal value lets the firm choose the test rules for both embodiments and the capital installation. We solve the recursion on 2,001 belief points. For a stronger fixed baseline we **enumerate every subset of up to six of the twelve shifts**, separately for each site, integrating the terminal decisions over all possible reports. Another comparator receives the schedule chosen for an independent donor world of the same site type. It has matching timing and workload in distribution but cannot condition purchases on its own reports. The separate-heads control retains native diagnostic routines. The matched archive control spends the same audit fee replaying ordinary eligible records. [Operating model and schedule comparisons](study.py) · [Renewal and full fixed-menu comparison](renewal.py)

## Where joint funding pays and where it loses

At a fee of 0.16, both claimants' preferred precommitted schedule and the joint fund’s preferred precommitted schedule were one late check. Its net gain over ordinary site-specialized operation was **0.448** (paired 95% Monte Carlo interval 0.415–0.480). Assigning the ordinary site rule at the target after buying the label reduced the difference to exactly **−0.16**. The audit fee remained, and the extra information could no longer affect the target decision. Holding the fee fixed and moving the same check to the first shift gave −0.056. An operationally identical label can arrive too early to describe the convention still in force.

| Evaluation condition at fee 0.16 | Flexible joint fund minus site rule | Joint fund minus best ordinary-regime fixed schedule | Joint fund minus independently assigned audit schedule |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ordinary drift | +0.492 | +0.044 | +0.146 |
| Unannounced convention reversal late in the source period | +0.420 | −0.598 | +0.289 |
| Unannounced reversal of transfer sign on the mobile robot only | −0.486 | −0.254 | −0.016 |

Under ordinary drift the joint fund bought two or three audits, averaging **2.384**. The unilateral sponsor bought one. The gain from flexible funding over the best fixed schedule was small (0.023–0.066). At a fee of **0.40**, the joint fund bought one and the sponsor bought none. The joint net gain was **0.208** (0.175–0.240). At a fee of 0.64 both precommitted designs selected zero. Joint funding can matter by financing an indivisible measurement, or by making later purchases contingent on what earlier measurements revealed.

An unexpected late reversal made the single late observation especially valuable. Jointly financed renewal lost 0.598 (−0.618 to −0.577) to this fixed rule while beating the donor-matched schedule. Conditioning the next purchase on one's own evidence helped relative to copying another site's calendar. The maintained drift model still made the entire sequence less valuable than one well-timed observation. When the source convention transferred with the opposite sign to the mobile robot alone, the ordinary one-audit rule raised arm qualification by 9.38 percentage points and lowered mobile qualification by the same amount. Warehouse orders increased 0.240 while supply orders fell 0.198. A larger roster of source labels could reinforce the wrong recipient rule. ## Capital, rights and evidence limits

The ordinary one-audit program added 0.082 warehouse and 0.090 supply orders per terminal period, and raised upstream arm and mobile work by 0.132 and 0.123 units. At site type zero it **reduced** the expected net value of an additional arm block by 0.165 and a mobile block by 0.365. A qualified method releases capacity that the next block would otherwise supply. At type one the arm block gained 0.003 and the mobile block lost 0.134. **The selected installation did not switch in these worlds.** Marginal investment values moved even when the purchase decision stayed fixed. The joint file gives within-site quantiles of both revaluations and service-specific changes in accepted output. With scarce review labor, the qualification gain produced more warehouse orders and fewer supply orders. Claims on contribution describe who could fund the investigation. They give no estimate of a market price or a sector-level return. [Operating, capital and fee results](results.json)

Triplicating the underlying source family reduced the one-check net gain to 0.342. Replicas reuse the same original signature and its date. Extending validation lag to four shifts made the preselected late check expire, costing its entire fee. Allowing ordinary signed receipts to leak information about the convention, and giving the incumbent the correct reduced-form likelihood, cut the extra one-check gain to 0.244. The nonidentification proof applies to the independent, exactly matched ordinary archive. It is not a universal description of firms' records.

These results price a stylized economic learning and control problem. The site rules and post-training effects are analytical abstractions. The program solves target production exactly for divisible orders and simulates independent source histories. It assumes the protocol for interpreting a representative label is known, that shared compute permits one target diagnostic per head, and that no financing constraint binds beyond the audit menu. The donor-schedule intervals condition on the sampled donor pool. Other intervals cover variation across synthetic worlds. No pretrained model, physical robot, measured capital stock or scaling exponent was evaluated here. The previous [published-model reanalysis](../inquiry/README.md) provides the empirical control for static specialization. The [priced transfer study](../transfer_validation/README.md) compares learned checkpoint and retirement rules under drift, and the [network study](../network_mpk/README.md) defines the physical-output and investment measures.

## Discriminating test and next question

Assign a small reference panel from the full original job roster before routing. Link ordinary, reference and declined cases to settlement and a fresh target diagnostic where permissions allow. Preserve the ordinary team's local rules. Randomize procedures at the level where updates are shared. Compare conditional renewal with a site-matched donor calendar and the strongest precommitted late sample, charging all three for coverage and release delay. Track whether installing the retained procedure changes the chosen diagnostic, accepted deliveries and role hours. If ordinary records already identify recipient sign, or extra reference observations change no feasible target decision before expiry, the proposed information premium should disappear after costs. Evidence for a funding effect additionally requires that the parties hold different residual claims and could not implement the same cost sharing already.

**Next question.** Can the organization learn when the source-to-recipient relationship has reversed using a small recipient panel, before a well-funded source panel makes the fleet more confident in the wrong release? Compare spending the marginal validation slot on another source case, a recipient case, or a standing local checkpoint. Preserve endogenous admissions and the independently assigned schedule. The decisive outcome is the value of transfer that the recipient safely retains after charging for both panels.
