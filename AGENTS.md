## 4. Sequential epistemic gates — no skipping

Each paper advances independently through:

**Gate A — Analytical core and deduplication.** Portable propositions have explicit primitives, assumptions, proof, exact boundaries, competent alternatives and scoped claims. Every overlapping branch source is merged, retained as a scoped appendix, routed to another owner or archived. One manuscript owns each claim. Notation and replication coverage agree. An ownership map alone does not pass A.

**Gate B — Hostile-referee pass.** After A, a separate adversarial micro-theory/econometrics reviewer challenges novelty against prior art, functional forms, observability, equilibrium/selection assumptions and external validity. Follow the discipline of reviews/integrated_firm_referee.md. Every objection is resolved by an argument/proof or retained as an explicit claim boundary. Author self-review, a satisfied solver and an old general referee report do not automatically clear this gate.

**Gate C — Empirical anchor mapping.** After B, map at least one concrete operational/commercial case onto formal states, decisions, information and outcomes. Every unidentified parameter is explicit null with a reason and the observation needed to identify it. Separate posted terms, executed terms, actual operations and causal effects. A motivation citation or source list does not pass C.

Evidence collection and early criticism may prepare later gates while A is open, but cannot mark those gates passed. Dates and edition numbers never close gates. A narrower or negative proposition can pass when its proof and boundary are sound.
