**Central question.** When does the best current deployment lose to a deployment that changes future profitable and financeable opportunities?

**Linked questions.** (i) How do demand and contract form determine deployer cash versus customer value? (ii) Which directed learning and qualification relationships change the reachable opportunity set? (iii) Which payer can fund the complementary investment, and on what date? (iv) When do hardware design or coordination change these answers beyond ordinary expansion?

**Candidate contribution, not an established result.** Misalignment among production, learning-transfer and payment networks may change deployment order even at the same aggregate resources and engineering potential. The first example isolates that tension, but its reversal is familiar constrained-investment economics. A defensible paper requires an additional structure-specific result or a rigorous narrower/negative finding. Do not manufacture novelty by naming states after robots.

**Dependency map.** Customer output demand and best feasible alternative → contract surplus and collected cash → actor-specific investment feasibility → qualified configuration/capacity → accepted service → customer output/demand. A second directed path runs from deployment → eligible experience and rights → adaptation/validation → future capability and reachable service. Coordination and manufacturing affect those transitions through explicit costs, delays and shared constraints. This is a conceptual dependency network, not observed topology. A joint service requirement is a bundle/hyperedge when its components are complementary.

**Scope ladder.** A: static surplus, pricing and outside option. B: exact two-period cash/learning/access and investment. C: directed transfer and costly new/modified embodiment eligibility; introduce uncertainty only to study a real information decision. D: coordination, organization or competition only if a core result requires it. Do not call a central optimization decentralized competition. Distinguish a changing edge weight from an actually new reachable configuration/customer/link.

## Research prospectus and argument outline

The prospective paper studies deployment sequencing when initial service changes both future production possibilities and the ability of specific actors to fund them. A tractable service benchmark endogenizes customer output against a matched alternative. A two-period provider chooses a current deployment and a subsequent supported-capacity investment. The first exact example shows that a bridge loan changes the preferred deployment without changing its engineering potential, and that access or learning ablations remove the reversal. This is a diagnostic, not a demonstrated contribution beyond constrained dynamic investment. The next model must explain a consequence of directed transfer, complementary qualification, or contractual cash allocation that a generic option-value narrative would conceal. Empirical cases will restrict feasible edges and parameter bounds, not supply fictional calibrated returns.

Current paper argument: (1) state the puzzle and unresolved prior-art objection; (2) present the exact worked comparison; (3) compare seed mechanisms and generic dynamic-investment theory; (4) specify Model A/B; (5) give the conditional threshold and boundaries; (6) add only the extension that changes the claim; (7) map independently evidenced production/learning/payment relationships; (8) challenge finance, demand, transfer, qualification and baselines; (9) interpret the bounded result and falsification test. ## Theory-discriminating empirical contrast

Compare **within-configuration inspection expansion** with **cross-configuration manipulation transfer**. They expose different missing links rather than simply selecting the old three dossiers. Inspection can have recurring access and a named contracting authority while lacking intervention/maintenance benefits or transferable control evidence. Manipulation papers can report transfer evaluations while lacking customer demand and provider cash records. Neither population estimates the other's transition.

Existing input records, not freshly re-verified source claims: `QOF-E01` in [inspection evidence](data/quadruped_operator_followup.json) connects a configured ANYmal B300 to reported 2018 offshore inspection missions but preserves access/visibility/integration limits. `QOF-E36/E37` record Mount Dora council contract approvals (2012/2016); they do not establish execution/payment, ownership, or learning. These are **different deployments and technologies**, not a joined operating/finance panel. `LEC-004` in [learning evidence](data/learning_data_economics.json) preserves DexWild task-level nonmonotonicity and weaker mean outcomes at a higher human sampling share. `LEC-006` is a negative mapping control: XEWorld held-out embodiment video/motion evaluation is not physical policy transfer.

The first atlas extraction should keep node/relation/direction, configuration, geography/time, units, quantity or null, source ID/locator and observation/inference/hypothesis status. Unknown transfer/payment edges stay unknown. Official BEA input-output and BLS occupation/interindustry matrices provide a sector backbone after vintage reconciliation; they do not provide firm-to-firm/site-to-task links or robot-addressable demand. Separate historical observations from projections and preserve unallocated activity.

**Estimand and test.** For a fixed target service/configuration and matched qualification protocol, compare target accepted output/cost with versus without eligible source experience, holding target adaptation budget and starting policy fixed. Separately observe the provider's dated collections, commitments and access to financing before investment. Outcomes: qualified entry date, accepted target service and payer cash, not episode counts or announced units. Confounders include concurrent model/design upgrades, chosen easy sites, demand growth, operator experience, contractual renegotiation and reverse causality. Public cross-sections cannot identify this causal effect. They can bound which links exist, reject invalid proxies, and specify the minimum matched evaluation/payment panel. A contract approval is not loan capacity; skill similarity is not transfer.
