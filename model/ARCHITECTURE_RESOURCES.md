# Intelligence architectures with dated resources and compatible parts

Edition 0.33. This is a synthetic execution contract, not a measured comparison of real model suppliers. The [analysis](../research/architecture_resources.md), [complete export](results/architecture_resources.json) and [sole-writer review](../reviews/architecture_resources_review.md) describe its decision scope.

## One execution, explicitly composed admission

`model/annual_resource_ledger.py` extracts the existing annual admission rule into `AnnualResourceAdmission`. It creates no actor, invoice, physical unit or service. `_ResourceEnterprise` retains its competitive policy and historical outputs. `_ResourceArchitecture` instead wraps `_ArchitectureEnterprise`; the two business policies are never stacked or executed independently and added together. The shared rule checks remaining annual minutes before the existing atomic admission and commits exactly the actual change in its physical support/qualification and integration counters. Monthly ceilings still apply. Each calendar year has its own opening use, unavailable outside reservations, actual bookings and remaining balance.

`architecture_result` formats one completed physical/Treasury execution. The new runner adds the annual ledger and the existing typed component inventory to that same run. It replaces generic modules explicitly with `TypedComponentSystem`; generic opening stock and receipts are zero. Opening robots are already owned assets and do not consume a second manufacturing BOM or hardware purchase. New starts consume 24 compatible manipulator actuators and one standard onboard board, or two boards under the high-memory assumption. One separately declared replacement requirement per unit needs one compatible actuator from month four. Parts and replacement labor remain priced inside the existing bundled manufacture/support charges. These are hypothetical configurations and replacement events, not a product BOM or annual failure rate.

## Resource and cash contract

Four resources remain distinct: monthly adaptation/validation engineering; monthly demonstrations; physical support and software-upgrade qualification, subject to monthly and annual ceilings; and physical integration, also subject to both ceilings. No common staff identity, substitution or engineering-to-support conversion is inferred. A separate declaration and a redesigned resource contract would be needed to share one employee pool. Avoid summing these four resource quantities as if they were measured interchangeable labor.

Candidate model/integrator fees, role-funded resources and physical booking are atomic. A refused, unperformed qualification consumes none of these candidate payments, architecture hours, annual minutes or replacement parts. The same unit can retry in a later service phase. A manufacturing order that fails at its declared month does not silently move to a new month. Earlier successful manufacture, parts issued for it, fixed staff/site work and other mandatory obligations remain incurred. This model does not execute R&D trials; it cannot erase failed-trial costs or substitute for the separate [learning execution](LEARNING_EXECUTION.md) contract.

The existing architecture mapping preserves logical-role capital and sums it only where roles share a legal account. Customer, operator, OEM, model supplier and integrator payments follow their original contracts. An internal transfer is eliminated, not added to resources. Architecture resource cash is already included in enterprise external outlay; never add those two totals again. Outside reservations restrict capacity but create no expense or outside delivered service. Standard cases have no debt; real consent, rights, prices, qualification and target funding remain unknown.

## Compared worlds and exact switches

All thirteen cases use July 2026–February 2027, the same 500 accepted cleaning zones per month and fixed paid digital-human capacity. Four unreserved profiles preserve the previous architecture cases' complete cash, physical and service outcomes. Four matched commitment cases leave 8,400 physical integration minutes available in 2026. The pool renews in January; unused minutes do not carry and future capacity cannot repair past service.

Two open-adapted cases reduce the assumed adaptation requirement from 200 to 100 engineering hours per unit, holding validation at 100 and capacity at 400 hours/month. This is a hypothetical productivity premise with unchanged acceptance, not observed AI performance. One case has unreserved physical resources; the other has the same 8,400-minute allocation. Remaining cases expose no model-supplier capital, delayed board qualification with an explicitly moved manufacturing order, and the paid digital-human-only alternative. No higher service price or additional demand rewards robot activity.

The licensed profile needs 5,250 integration minutes per new unit. Exactly 10,500 remaining minutes admits both new units in September; 10,499 leaves one waiting until January. The open profile needs 6,300 per new unit. Less adaptation work removes its first engineering obstacle, while integration remains binding. Tests also reduce adaptation to 50 hours: cash cost changes but the physical date does not. This is a conditional critical-path result, not a recommended architecture or a calibrated investment return.

## Reproduction and interface

Run `python3 scripts/export_architecture_resources.py` and `--check`; run `python3 -m unittest tests.test_architecture_resources -v`. The CLI accepts `--case FILE --output FILE` for full replay. `GET /api/architecture-resources` returns controls, summary and one complete reference. `POST` accepts exactly `inputs` or `saved_case`; the latter restores complete plan, Treasury, hardware contracts, enterprise, finance, bridge, architecture, annual resource and part inputs. Derived outcomes and a submitted hash cannot replace calculation. Exact rationals retain string numerator/denominator representation.

Commercial models, Digital AI ecosystem and Supply & production share the same applied case. Controls recompute available annual minutes, monthly engineering/demonstrations, logical-role model funding, manufacturing month and board qualification. Blank qualification means unrealized. Invalid edits or stale responses retain the last valid result; pending edits do not change export. Imported full cases are read-only in shortcut controls until a standard world is chosen. The original annual cell filters do not relabel or recalibrate this cleaning-service execution.

## Remaining limits

This joins four architecture roles to annual support/integration and typed parts for one service. It does not simultaneously execute the other five cells, outside reservations, competitive renewal, acquisition/recovery, shared R&D or endogenous learning. The human alternative is a strong feasible fixed-staff operation with the same digital site work, not a claim of optimal dispatch. Installed starting hardware and terminal assets are not newly bought or valued. Paid resource totals and service dates therefore do not establish full lifecycle economics, financing sufficiency, employment effects or the best commercial structure.
