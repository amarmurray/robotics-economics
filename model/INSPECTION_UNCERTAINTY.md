# Inspection decisions with unresolved evidence

This distribution-free sensitivity experiment extends the existing [inspection engine](UTILITY_INSPECTION.md) and [cash comparison](INSPECTION_PRICE_BOUNDS.md). It does not change either engine or its old results. Its new contribution is **joint evidence conditioning**: which combination of unresolved fields can change a feasible decision, rather than another favorable robot example. The utility pack is the numerical case because it already has an auditable common-service and payer ledger. Hospital and automotive packets inherit the method only; their empirical inputs are not replaced by utility assumptions.

## Possibilities and provenance

[Complete inputs](inspection_uncertainty_inputs.json) inherit the accepted four-window, twelve-point human inspection fixture. All cash amounts, review work, staffing, capacity and field-duty minutes remain synthetic. Six explicitly finite axes generate 96 possibilities and 288 policy executions: paid staffing contract; physical duty; fixed measurement qualification; mobile quality/timeliness; review capacity; and service deadline. Every policy in one state has the identical full due obligation. States are enumerated, not sampled, and are not assigned probabilities, weights or relative plausibility. Counts of winning or failing states are not likelihoods.

The 23/24 review-minute values straddle the existing exact requirement for twelve observations at two minutes each. Mobile capture at minute 61 deliberately crosses the declared minute 60 cutoff; this is not a measured outage duration or failure rate. Deadlines 100/180, a missing fixed qualification and a failed mobile review are explicit structural stresses. A complete late capture receives no remote acceptance. The funded human-only route starts at opening, while monitored fallback starts at the declared cutoff, as before. An earlier prebooked fallback would be another policy and must carry its cost; it is not silently assumed here.

The model permits a subset of the declared values and complete alternative base financial inputs under its documented fixture geometry. It rejects empty, duplicate, unknown and mistyped axis values. An infeasible human baseline stops the experiment; it does not create a zero-cost default. The shipped artifact uses the full stated domain. Refining the domain means assuming or establishing evidence, not learning from its output.

## Decision rule and information

Let `C(p,s)` be a policy's recorded window cash, at either the customer or consolidated external-resource boundary. A feasible policy completes every due window, has no unpaid committed bills, and funds every enabled monitoring booking. A provider that retains a fee while customer fallback completes the job is not admitted as a successfully supplied monitoring option. Infeasible service has no cash-equivalent penalty and cannot win by omitting work.

Within each state, the oracle cost is the minimum among feasible policies. For a policy feasible in every compatible state, its regret is the maximum of `C(p,s) − oracle(s)`. The conditional minimax choice minimizes this worst regret. This is an explicitly chosen decision rule under deep uncertainty, not an estimate of expected profit, utility, risk preference or an optimal real deployment. A human policy may remain the only robust admissible option even though a qualified fixed alternative wins some states.

All 63 nonempty subsets of the six fields are evaluated. A revelation partitions the possibilities by the actual values of those fields; the decision can adapt in each branch. The reported score is the worst remaining branch regret. Full revelation is an ideal costless, perfect-information reference. The reduction in regret is **not expected value of information, willingness to pay for a study, or an empirical acquisition budget**. Evidence can be incomplete, noisy, costly, delayed or selected in reality. Public source records motivate packet fields but do not assign the probabilities needed for an expected-value calculation.

The model evaluates information before choosing a new policy. It is not an option to cancel an existing contract without cost or to recover sunk bills. Historical renewal and cancellation statements need their executed terms. A reported completion percentage does not identify any simulated field.

## Complete payer costs remain a separate condition

The recorded cash ledger is complete for its declared window bills, not for equipment life. The inputs explicitly list unresolved acquisition/replacement, integration, qualification, software rights/renewal, repair, financing, tax, terminal obligation and downstream maintenance costs. Actual target amounts remain null. There is no assumed maintenance saving or outage value.

For a feasible baseline B and alternative A, the customer allowance is `C_B − C_A`. Let U denote omitted customer costs and V proven incremental net customer benefit, both matched to the same boundary and period. The alternative can be cheaper only if `(U_A − U_B) − (V_A − V_B) <= C_B − C_A`. The consolidated equation uses external-resource cash R instead of C. The saved artifact supplies both exact allowances, including negative values, for every feasible pair. No bound is assigned to unmatched/incomplete service. These are conditional algebraic limits, not completed lifecycle rankings. Unlimited unknown residual costs mean no unconditional real-world ranking follows.

At the customer boundary, supplier fees are cash outlays. At the consolidated customer/provider boundary those fees cancel; the external resources they fund enter once. An added support fee and the same support resources cannot both be charged to consolidated cost. Baseline provider cash gain is retained when comparing buyer and consolidated incentives. Recorded supplier net cash is not profit after omitted costs.

Four additional executions test funding timing in the complete favorable fixture. An extra initial external-resource bill equal to the minimum provider cash balance across the unchanged schedule is funded; one cent above it breaks the first booking. This distinguishes aggregate cost recovery from timely liquidity. The additional amount is a derived stress, not an observed repair bill. It changes actual inputs and replays the Treasury; a terminal cash subtraction alone is insufficient. The unchanged schedule may require a different advance, provider liquidity or contingency, each requiring a new funded replay. No commercial action or proposed quote follows.

## Reproduce and inspect

Run `python3 scripts/export_inspection_uncertainty.py`, then `--check`; run `python3 -m unittest tests.test_inspection_uncertainty -v`. Recompute a saved artifact with `--case FILE --output FILE`. Complete inputs are authoritative: forged saved eligibility, costs, decisions or hashes are ignored. Code, Treasury, input metadata and underlying inspection sources are fingerprinted. There is no randomness, network access, third-party package, credential or GPU requirement.

`python3 scripts/build_inspection_uncertainty.py` generates the [decision chapter](../research/inspection_uncertainty.md) from the checked artifact. The existing dashboard links a [local explorer](../dashboard/inspection_uncertainty.html); its controls select the Python-precomputed evidence branches and never implement another economic engine. The edition 0.53 matrix remains byte-identical as its dated acceptance specification; its source-package hashes identify that historical input, not later expanded packages. Current evidence additions and row mappings are recorded separately in the decision chapter and operator package. No empirical requirement is promoted by simulation.
