# Funded learning and physical qualification

Edition 0.22. `model/learning_execution.py` composes the unchanged workcell executor and Treasury. This is an executable decision contract, **not model training, an empirical learning curve or a robot experiment**. References LEC-005..008 motivate the distinctions; no reported score, dataset size, GPU allocation or vendor criterion calibrates a numerical parameter.

## One service boundary and one stock

Two named opening-owned fixed workcells, `retained-unit` and `target-unit`, each owe twelve unique three-component kits in each production cycle. The reference has three cycles and 72 due kits. Each workcell executes its own manipulator picks, buffer, handoff, changeovers, inspection and rework through `run_workcell`. The production windows represent separate unit capacity; their component identities never overlap. The baseline changes product variant after each three-kit run. Its twelve-tick changeover meets four of twelve deadlines per window. The competent conventional alternative assumes an already-qualified one-tick retool configuration, with the same startup charge and staffing obligations. Its availability and implementation burden are hypotheses, not observed vendor equivalence or an investment-cost estimate.

Each cycle consists of a planning allocation, reserved preproduction physical trials when admitted, production windows, then episode collection. Buffer contents and closing recipes persist between windows. A separate finite reset crew must clear staged parts into identified retained inventory and reset the recipe before the next window. Its actual person-ticks and cash are charged; shortage blocks operations while staffing commitments remain. Raw unserved parts remain recorded inventory, and missed prior orders are not silently fulfilled by later demand. Initial setup is retried and charged until funded; it cannot become free in a later cycle. The engineering budget is a shared number of person-hours, not elapsed time or dedicated hours per unit. Physical trial, reset and production ticks are five seconds. The reset crew has its own declared per-unit capacity and expense; it is not borrowed from the engineering pool or created from released operator time. A cycle is a discrete planning/production batch, not a day, month or annual-model period. Treasury `Moment.month` remains an ordered transaction index. This experiment does not silently convert any of these clocks into a payback or annual deployment rate.

## Data and candidate gates

Only completed production handoff attempts can supply observations. A rejection and its later successful retry are distinct attempt IDs on the same job; accepted service remains one unique kit. Repeated copies of the same observation are deduplicated before collection fees, engineering use or eligibility counts. Collection is paid in batches, consumes the remaining engineering allocation, and can fail for lack of cash or capacity. Uncollected observations are unavailable for later training. Sensor validity and assumed training rights gate eligibility separately from collection.

Training requires a minimum number of eligible unique episodes from **earlier cycles**. It incurs engineering and compute expenditure once. Its input episode IDs are frozen and exported. More later data does not automatically increase the candidate's performance. The candidate's proposed target and retained-cohort changeover rates are explicit user-editable hypotheses. The no-learning intervention retains eligible observations and actual funded training/adaptation/trial work but preserves baseline rates. The no-research intervention skips candidate work while retaining the same operating-data collection policy. These are different decisions and have different costs.

A successful target does not update the retained unit. Retained use additionally requires the transfer assumption, a separately paid adapter/module, adaptation and a passing trial. Legitimate shared data collection is counted once; per-unit adaptation and validation remain separate resources. A failed retained trial leaves the existing configuration in service. No fictional data-asset value is credited to cash or output.

## Delivery, trials and support

Each upgrade needs a distinct funded runtime module. Payment creates an order with a delivery cycle; delivery is still separate from compatibility, adaptation, qualification and production activation. An incompatible or late module remains a paid resource/order and cannot create a robot or backfill missed service. Both robot identities remain opening stock throughout. This is bounded qualified-configuration supply, not a complete robot manufacturing or component-ramp model.

Qualification requires the whole reserved per-unit physical window, engineering admission and actual workcell execution with paid trial staff and operations. The default reservation is 156 ticks. At 155 ticks no trial is admitted; its prerequisite training, module and adaptation spending remain. A trial must satisfy its declared timely-output threshold, deliver all test kits within the window, and leave no unpaid trial obligation. Target and retained thresholds are explicit inputs; the reference uses 12 and 4. Trials use disjoint component/job identities and never enter customer service or the training dataset. They share a task pattern with production: this is an identity-disjoint deterministic acceptance fixture, not statistical evidence of out-of-distribution generalization or industrial reliability. A single pass cannot support those empirical claims.

The target is considered before retained adaptation. `support_first` books recurring installed support before candidate work; `candidate_first` reverses that order. Each funded operation consumes the same finite engineering pool. Deferred work resumes in a later cycle without rebuilding the candidate or rebuying an already ordered module. Production on an unsupported unit has no admitted operations, but its staff commitment remains. The allocator is a declared greedy policy, not an optimal planner. Trial resources occupy explicitly reserved preproduction capacity; the model does not estimate displaced customer production outside those reservations. A missing slot defers validation rather than inventing another trial unit.

## Cash and reproducibility

All actor cash resides in one immutable Treasury. Each physical window starts with the current operator balance and executes the original workcell invoices. Its audited invoice/event partition is appended with namespaced IDs and shifted transaction moments. Postings, amounts, obligations, cancellations and operation funding order are unchanged. No local opening balance is added to consolidated capital. Tests verify the partition's actor deltas and the full Treasury audit; the original workcell can replay each window from its complete inputs. To avoid storing duplicate subledgers, physical windows retain inputs, outcomes, events, funding and the original executor hash; the consolidated Treasury retains all financial events.

Operator outlay equals opening minus closing operator cash and the sum of funded charge lines. Staff, physical resources, modules and R&D recipients are explicit counterparties. There is no customer fee, provider markup, financing return, revenue, data premium or assumed removed payroll. Failed conditional work is not performed; unconditional unpaid staff obligations remain. Support-first refers to engineering allocation, not a payroll cash reserve. The one-cent training test deliberately demonstrates that a funded research action can leave later payroll unfunded. A broader solvency policy remains outside this experiment.

Reproduce all sixteen complete worlds with:

```sh
python3 scripts/export_learning_execution.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_learning_execution.py' -v
python3 scripts/export_learning_execution.py --case saved-case.json --output replay.json
```
