# Funded share acquisition and separate growth

[This extension](entry_transitions.py) runs on the unchanged monthly physical, commercial, enterprise, finance and calendar bridge. It uses the bridge's explicit synthetic cleaning-zone contract. The [fifteen saved worlds](results/entry_transitions.json) compare status quo, organic build, share acquisition and hybrid entry. No company is bought, no third party is contacted, and no real transaction terms are inferred.

## What transfers

The supported acquisition is a 100% beneficial equity transfer in the existing incumbent operating company. Before closing its recorded owner is `selling_owner`; after a successful closing it is `platform_buyer`. Its legal actor/account stays `incumbent`. Customer assignments, unique physical unit IDs, leases, title, custody, workflow authority, receivables and liabilities stay with their existing legal counterparties. In the reference the lessor continues to own the robots. The separate ultimate-owner register must not be mistaken for robot title or a grant of model/data rights.

Closing requires an explicit assumed-consent/operating-rights premise and actual buyer funds. Consideration goes to the selling owner. It does not arrive in the target's operating account. Separate target equity, if specified, requires a separate settled payment from buyer to company. No target-cash sweep, dividend, target-funded purchase, asset novation, debt forgiveness or share liquidation is modeled. Share price and terminal value are assumptions/unknowns, respectively; this is not a valuation, legal or tax analysis.

## Dated funding and atomic closing

At the declared month before closing (month zero if closing is zero), the buyer incurs a diligence bill to a distinct resource supplier. The committed bill remains even if closing fails. Each due collection uses actual available buyer cash. In the closing month's phase 1, conditional invoices are issued for share consideration, transition resources and optional target working equity; phase 2 follows actual operating receipts and scheduled growth equity.

The three conditional payments are evaluated on an immutable candidate Treasury. All must settle before any candidate payment or ownership change is accepted. Otherwise the candidate is discarded and the conditional invoices are cancelled. Prior paid diligence and its unpaid obligation remain. The failed closing is not silently rescheduled. A one-cent boundary is tested against the full group, including the target-equity leg. A funded closing never implies that all the target's operating obligations can subsequently be paid.

A declared whole-target interruption begins only after successful closing. During its explicit months the target cannot provide service; its paid fixed obligations continue and its physical units remain. No customer reassignment, free fallback or restoration of already missed service occurs. Interruption is a synthetic sensitivity, not an estimated acquisition effect.

## Build and hybrid

All four routes start with the same $200,000 in the buyer account. The target has a separate $25,000 reference opening balance and two existing leased robots. Technology, annual digital/capability levels, support/integration budgets and supplier terms are common; the former floor-bundle case is not converted or added to this market.

Build and hybrid commit $60,000 of buyer equity to a separate entrant company at month zero and request two new robots through the existing funded manufacture, shipping, integration and support process. Building captures 70 units of existing baseline business and assigns 100 unmet plus 50 already specified new recurring zones in month three. Hybrid leaves the target's 500 baseline zones assigned to the target and assigns only those 150 growth zones to the entrant. The market remains 650 zones per month; acquisition adds no demand or robot stock.

The hybrid growth leg is separately committed and may continue even when the share acquisition fails. This is an explicit contract choice, not an assertion that a real buyer should proceed. No integration synergy, automatic headcount change or automatic transfer of idle parent cash is assumed. Alternative prompt/delayed incumbent customer-return attempts use the same technology access and actual acquisition-resource charges; their timing is a fixed policy, not an optimized competitive equilibrium.

## Accounting and replay boundary

The existing enterprise perimeter reports its actual operating sales, paid costs and received equity. A second, fixed wider perimeter includes buyer, seller and all robot-commercial/operating parties. Share consideration and internal equity cancel inside this wider boundary; externally paid operating and transaction bills remain. The seller's cash receipt is not new physical output. A deliberately supplied opening target payable stays owed by the target; its settlement is reported separately from current-period resource creation. All actors conserve cash and every month's demand assignment conserves the same market inventory.

The wider cash change reconciles to actual end-market collections plus external equity/net financing minus external operating and transaction cash. It is not accounting profit. Uncollected sales, existing/future obligations, assets, unpaid claims and unknown terminal share value remain visible. The reference adds no new term loan; inherited outside-lender facilities can be replayed, while parent/seller loans and unrelated opening invoice contracts are rejected rather than silently misclassified.

`python3 scripts/export_entry_transitions.py --check` verifies deterministic exports. `--case downloaded.json --output replay.json` restores complete opening Treasury, physical plan, contracts, enterprise, finance, bridge and transition inputs and recomputes all outcomes. Saved owners, cash totals and hashes are ignored. Integral JSON numbers are canonicalized and rational numerator/denominator strings preserve exact browser replay. The API `/api/entry-transitions` accepts exactly shortcut `inputs` or a complete `saved_case`. Customer adoption shows terms, operating versus buyer cash, ultimate ownership, unchanged leases, obligations, controls and export/import.

## One funded entry and renewal execution, edition 0.38

It never removes an inherited bill. One invocation of `run_monthly` maintains all unit IDs, physical events, invoices, funds and accepted work.

The fixture has eight months and one conserved market. Standalone and hybrid growth use actual parent equity; the inactive growth company cannot bid in status quo/acquire. Closing remains atomic after prior diligence. The inherited readiness test governs both physical service and renewal offers. Share close occurs in phase two, then funded physical work, renewal in phase five and invoiced accepted work. Target interruption changes readiness and may leave a locked contract under-served; later time does not backfill it.

At successful closing, the policy snapshots target lots before renewal and records ownership reclassification separately. It tracks first platform contract-lot IDs, gross awards from outside/unassigned lots and transfers between commonly owned operators. Regained work is not a new lot. After common ownership, separate declared offer mandates continue; no independent competition or optimized parent pricing is asserted. Parent, target and growth legal accounts remain separately funded. The inherited fixed wide consolidation includes the seller and eliminates share/internal-equity transfers; resource and transaction payments appear once.

Optional `human_efficiency` recomputes whole-zone human capacity from unchanged paid staff, effective annual efficiency and the established wage/hours mapping. The existing mapping validation remains active. This is a synthetic assumption under the same site software price, not measured AI performance. Full replay restores physical, Treasury, business, finance, calendar, competition and transition opening inputs and ignores joint outcomes and submitted hashes.

Run `scripts/export_renewing_entry.py`, `--check` or `--case FILE --output FILE`. `/api/renewing-entry` exposes a checked catalog and one full reference; POST accepts exactly `inputs` or `saved_case`. ## Paid crews in actual renewals, edition 0.41

`model/staffed_renewals.py` subclasses the existing entry/renewal policy explicitly. It adds one separate `Workforce` contract per legal operator, a uniquely named crew and funded outside buyer. Opening staffing is mapped through the existing wage/digital calendar for input consistency; dispatch replaces the independent human scalar with exact conserved crew bookings. Original inputs remain intact for replay; only the current month's issued staffing commitment changes after a binding notice.

Phase 1 issues optional fixed-block overtime quotes. Phase 5 buys a block in full only if then-qualified funded regular minutes do not cover retained work plus the whole-market offer ceiling. It reserves local attention, then indivisible outside jobs, then the lower marginal-minute cleaning method. The inherited renewal loop uses that paid residual capacity, retains locked contracts, and pays onboarding/switch costs atomically. Dispatch consumes the same budget for actual awards. Bids never use later receipts, another firm's crew or parent cash. Paid unused capacity is observable. Fixed purchase blocks and whole-market offer ceilings are policy assumptions, not optimization.

The ledger identity is regular available minutes plus paid overtime equals attention, outside-job time, robot handling, human cleaning and unused minutes. All-payer resource/transaction cash equals the joint entry boundary plus outside customer switching payments and also equals actual outside resource-vendor receipts. Outside-job sales are counted once in existing receipts and separately labeled by service unit. Full-input replay ignores supplied derived rows, balances and hashes. Existing model modules and their prior outcomes are unchanged. This extension does not compose annual resources, typed parts, architectures, asset recovery or learning.

## Qualified support and parts in the crew/renewal loop, edition 0.44

`qualified_staffing.py` composes `AnnualResourceAdmission` with `_StaffedRenewals`; its cooperative `admit` and `close` hooks call the same inherited entry/competition policy. `ComponentInventory` enters the existing `run_monthly` material hook. One completed execution is formatted through the extracted `staffed_renewals_result`; no second fleet, Treasury or customer run is created. The previous `run_staffed_renewals` delegates to that formatter and retains all prior outcomes. The plan ID is preserved so invoice identities also remain stable in the unconstrained comparison.

Qualified manufacturing consumes 24 compatible actuators and one declared standard board per whole paid start. A separately named actuator replacement is due in month 4. Consigned parts and repair labor remain in existing bundled prices; actual supplier financing and independent repair labor are unmeasured. In each pool, opening qualified + released = manufacturing issues + repair issues + closing qualified. Opening quarantine + received = released + closing quarantine. Late phase-4 qualification cannot fund or supply earlier phase-3 support.

Provider support/integration conserve their own annual balances, monthly ceilings and exact physical booking. Site-crew minutes conserve separately by legal operator. Only successful contingent admissions consume provider minutes or repair parts. Staffing notice is still based on the previous completed month; no future outcomes enter its decision. Once effective it is binding, so an adverse later resource event is handled through retained capacity, funded pre-award overtime or actual shortfall. Every payer, service unit and terminal stock remains explicit. Full replay reconstructs all input contracts and ignores saved derived outcomes.

The module accepts `staffed`, `resources` and `components`; absent resource rows use the observed calendar years with no outside reservation, and absent parts use the declared synthetic BOM/replacement fixture. Default target staffing requests one block after qualified notice. Full saved inputs, source hashes and the case hash identify the result. The joined scope includes share entry and actual renewal; four architectures, asset recovery, learning and the other five cells remain separately bounded.
