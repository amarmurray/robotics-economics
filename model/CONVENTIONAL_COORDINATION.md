# Exact conventional coordination over an observed queue

This is a bounded synthetic comparator for Workstreams E/H and the hospital decision pack. It executes no model, network service, RMF installation or robot. [The implementation](conventional_coordination.py) and [twelve complete worlds](results/conventional_coordination.json) use a new unit: **one unique synthetic item request accepted by its declared deadline**. There is no conversion from cleaning zones, historical floor bundles, hospital transports or clinical outcomes.

## Contract and exactness

A window has at most twelve integer ticks, six requests and two unit-capacity resources: courier and robot. Five minutes per tick is an arbitrary reference assumption. Requests state a release time, deadline, mandatory flag and one or two eligible modes. A mode specifies elapsed duration, interval claims on resources and an integer-cent nonlabor resource bill. The reference robot mode includes courier loading and handoff, not just robot motion. Physical eligibility, acceptance quality and authoritative completion are scenario premises. Mandatory priority is an arbitrary service obligation, not a clinical triage rule.

At each tick, dynamic programming enumerates every eligible mode and start time for the **currently observed pending queue**, with fixed reservations for work already admitted. It maximizes, lexicographically: mandatory completions, all completions, negative incremental resource cents, and negative sum of completion ticks. A deterministic action tuple breaks ties. Skipping work is a feasible option. A 300,000-state budget fails visibly without returning an optimality claim. The independent test oracle uses Cartesian enumeration and sets of occupied resource-time cells rather than the planner's masks or eligibility helper.

Exactness applies to this finite observed-queue objective. The planner cannot see future releases or an unobserved outage. It does not solve an optimal stochastic policy, discover physical feasibility, optimize clinical welfare, or choose long-horizon investments. The complete saved world contains future events for replay; these are absent from policy observations. A prefix-invariance test changes future arrivals and an outage without changing earlier policy observations or decisions.

Only actions starting at the proposal's execution tick are submitted. The remaining schedule is advisory and is replanned. With proposal latency, the clock keeps advancing. In-flight proposals reserve their still-feasible declared resources and cash tentatively for local planning. Malformed or now-infeasible packets reserve nothing. Tentative reservations do not confer execution authority or encumber a bank account. Every arriving packet is checked against current observed pending identities, deadlines, resource occupancy, standby constraints and actual available cash. Admission of a contingent action batch is atomic. A duplicate, unknown, malformed, stale or unfunded batch has no partial physical or payment effect.

## Staff, uncertainty and physical effects

The ex-ante courier reserve excludes routine work during ticks 2–4; mandatory work can use it. It is a declared standby obligation, not a discovered future arrival. It can hold paid capacity idle. Both conventional and synthetic proposal policies receive the same constraint. An optional immediate conventional urgent path bypasses proposal delay; its calls are reported separately within the stated prepaid planning-capacity contract.

An observed robot outage blocks its declared interval. If it intersects an active robot claim, that attempt becomes terminal failed: paid resources and partial-work disclosure remain; accepted count stays zero. Only future reservations are released after an **assumed authoritative stop**. There is no automatic retry, reversal of physical effects, or claim of external reconciliation. A funded recovery bill permits the declared return time. Unfunded recovery blocks the robot through the remaining window. Fault observation precedes completion processing at the same tick. Physical claims occupy half-open intervals; a completed robot claim is not retrospectively interrupted.

A unique accepted request is credited once at authoritative completion by deadline. Unserved requests finish as missed-deadline or failed states. Failed attempts do not inflate accepted output. This local harness does not establish restart durability, distributed identity, lookup finality or installed-system authority. Those remain distinct in the [transaction fixture](COORDINATION_TRANSACTION_FIXTURE.md).

## Cash boundary

The common immutable [Treasury](TREASURY_CORE.md) funds every bill. Required window capacities are booked sequentially before execution: retained paid staffing, robot support when applicable, a workflow-integration allocation, planning capacity and verification capacity. Earlier paid bookings remain if a later required booking is unfunded; execution then stays blocked. Integration allocation here is a declared cash charge for capacity in this window, not depreciation or an inferred annual conversion. Planning and verification prices book twelve decision slots, including the optional urgent path. Actual calls are reported; these assumptions do not measure CPU or model expense.

Each attempt pays its full specified nonlabor resource bill at admission; wages are already in the paid shift and are not charged again by minute. Recovery resources are additional. The service operator and external resource supplier conserve cash. Treasury `Moment.month` stores a monotonically ordered **transaction index**, with issue phase 1 and settlement phase 2. Each cost line also records the separate service tick. These indices are never financial months and generate no interest or annual scaling.

The reported cost ratio is all paid window resource cash divided by uniquely accepted requests, including costs of failed work and unused booked capacity. It is null when nothing is accepted. Assets, acquisition, depreciation, long-run maintenance, customer revenue, financing and residual value are outside this boundary. Lower cash spending with missed service is not an economic ranking; empirical whole-system cost remains unknown.

## Replay

`python3 scripts/export_conventional_coordination.py --check` reproduces all twelve worlds. Use `--case downloaded.json --output replay.json` to rerun complete saved workload/controller inputs. Saved outcomes and hashes are ignored and recomputed. The API `/api/conventional-coordination` accepts exactly `inputs` or `saved_case`; inputs contain exactly `workload` and `controller`. Source hashes pin this module and Treasury. The Learning & coordination panel retains the last valid result through pending edits, invalid input and stale responses, and supports complete JSON export/import.
