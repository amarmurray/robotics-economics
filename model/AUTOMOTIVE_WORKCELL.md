# Automotive workcell: physical flow, deadlines and supported configuration

Status: bounded synthetic DE-CF04 mechanism, edition 0.20. All numerical inputs are illustrative. This model is neither a reconstruction of BMW production nor an estimate of a product's cost, reliability or return.

## The common service

One service unit is the complete correct set of three uniquely identified components delivered to its designated fixture/sequence handoff, after loading opens and no later than the due tick. Each of twelve default kits has its own three components and one deadline. The fixed, humanoid and human alternatives share the same component inventory, releases, due times and acceptance rule. They do not share assumed operation durations: those are visible configuration inputs to be challenged.

A tick is five seconds. The default horizon is 156 ticks, with due times 24, 36, …, 156 and loading opening six ticks before each due time. These are invented units and schedules. The supplier's reported single-component feeder timing and Figure's three-part loading/cycle requirements have different boundaries and are not calibration inputs. Welding, vehicle completion, cart transport and line-wide throughput are outside this fixture. A complete accepted kit is not an accepted vehicle.

All 36 components are allocated opening raw inventory in the default case. A material supply tick prevents use before arrival; no manufacturing, replenishment price or new component production is simulated. Every component remains raw, in an operation, buffered or delivered. A rejected first attempt returns the same identities for recovery and retry; it does not create extra parts. No physical scrap, replacement-part consumption or literal double-pick mechanics are represented. Late physical deliveries remain late and cannot repair previously missed service.

## One manipulator and real buffering

One resource performs picking, kit handoff, variant changeover and recovery. Their intervals cannot overlap. With positive installed buffer capacity, actual funded pick operations create buffered components from raw stock. There is no free initial fill or hidden second feeder arm. A handoff atomically consumes all three staged identities. Buffer occupancy includes an in-progress pick's reserved position. The whole-kit staging rule means capacity below three cannot complete a kit in this particular design; it is not an empirical minimum for all workcells.

With zero buffer capacity, the resource must pick all three parts and hand them off within one direct operation after loading opens. Its duration is `3 × pick_ticks + handoff_ticks`. Buffered handoff duration is `handoff_ticks`; prior picking still consumes the same shared resource and cash. A known source-closure interval blocks any pick or direct operation that would overlap it, while a handoff from already staged parts can proceed. This is a scheduled refill, not a model of surprise breakdowns or perfect prediction of them.

The declared FIFO policy reserves the current kit before prefetching. Lookahead follows only its contiguous same-variant production run, so it cannot fill the buffer with a later A run while the intervening B kit waits. When the current kit is complete, it avoids a pick that would straddle the loading release. Changeover consumes resource and attendant time before a different variant can be served. Qualification is a separate explicit configuration list. This is an inspectable feasible policy, not a global optimizer, measured intelligence uplift or proof that no better fixed/human scheduler exists.

The competent human comparator completes all default due kits. A buffered human comparator can also meet every deadline during the same scheduled refill. Both prevent a weak baseline from manufacturing a robot advantage. The fixed mixed-variant witness misses service with its assumed twelve-tick changeover; reducing that input to six ticks restores all twelve on-time kits, while seven yields nine on time and three late. The humanoid's assumed one-tick changeover is a hypothesis, not a measured property of an embodiment. A fixed robot with equally fast validated changeover also succeeds.

## Preparation and retained people

Prepared presentation is a separately funded external resource. Its single preparation worker processes the allocated components in FIFO order, beginning each no earlier than material availability or completion of the prior part. Only completed preparation makes a component available to picking. The cash bill purchases setup and a specified count of preparation slots. Booked person-time, completed parts and actually executed person-time within the horizon are separate outputs; partially executed work counts without creating a finished part. This prevents upstream work from disappearing when the workcell becomes faster.

The shortcut's prepared-input default sets picking to one tick; an explicit `pick_ticks` input overrides that assumption. Preparation does not automatically grant quality, supply or deadline acceptance. Seventy-two preparation person-ticks process all 36 default parts; seventy process only 35 and leave the last three-part kit incomplete. The prepared direct case therefore exposes a staffing and material dependency rather than free input normalization.

All alternatives retain the same declared staff cash commitment. Human operations consume their duration in person-ticks; robotic handoffs consume the stated inspection allowance, and changeover/recovery consume attendant time. A paid capacity limit gates starts. This is aggregate person-time within the window, not a detailed multi-person roster or temporal scheduling model for inspection. The external preparation worker is a different purchased resource and cannot be counted as released workcell labor. Lower used task time leaves committed payroll unchanged. No source establishes actual removable staffing here.

## Funding, assets and replacement

The unchanged [Treasury](TREASURY_CORE.md) settles all cash in exact cents. Staff is a committed invoice; unpaid staff prevents work and its obligation persists. Initial configuration, operations, presentation and replacement purchases are conditional bills. A failed conditional bill is cancelled without a partial resource purchase or free work. Each operation must fit the remaining horizon and supported interval and must actually be funded. `Moment.month` is a transaction sequence here, not a financial month; it must not be added to the separate monthly fleet/loan outputs.

Opening robots, raw stock and installed buffer/fixtures are already owned assumptions. Their acquisition and lifecycle costs are absent. Startup and per-tick operation bills are bounded cash proxies for additional nonstaff station resources; the separate staff invoice already covers the declared attendant or human operator commitment. These proxies do not disclose manufacturing resources, lifetime maintenance, capital cost, power or terminal value. External staff, resource, preparation and equipment payees are separate accounts. Operator outflow equals their aggregate cash increase. They are not modeled supplier businesses, so their revenue is neither profit nor an independently established consolidated resource cost. There is no transaction fee added again on top of these payments.

A support cutoff blocks admission of operations that would finish after support ends. The retained robot does not vanish. A replacement requires an explicitly available unit, assumed rights, actual purchase cash, a separate configuration identity and a commissioning delay. Purchase includes the scenario's commissioning provision; its detailed crew/material process is not simulated. The new configuration has its own qualified variants and support interval. It does not inherit useful capability merely from being newer. Transfer of existing parts/buffer access to the replacement is an explicit common-workcell compatibility premise. The old unit remains owned, with no salvage or refund. No factory supply ramp, spare inventory, contractual entitlement, creditor enforcement or asset resale is implied.

The replacement can activate once ready and the manipulator is idle. A purchased but unqualified replacement produces no service; one that becomes ready exactly at the horizon is recorded as ready but unactivated. The qualified default replacement recovers later work but cannot turn an already late kit into timely service. Retained staffing continues in all these worlds.

## Replay and decision limits

`python3 scripts/export_automotive_workcell.py` saves eighteen complete worlds; `--check` verifies them. Replay complete inputs with `--case saved.json --output replay.json`. `run_workcell` validates full inputs, `run_workcell_case` supplies bounded shortcuts, and `replay_workcell` ignores saved outcomes and recomputes them. Source hashes cover this model and Treasury. Unknown fields, malformed identities and invalid quantitative types fail closed; Boolean values cannot substitute for integer money or counts.

The local `/api/automotive-workcell` GET exposes witnesses; POST accepts exactly `inputs` or `saved_case`. The embodiment view exposes buffer, picking, changeover, preparation, support and replacement controls, full component/asset/cash traces, pending-state feedback, stale-response protection and last-valid export/import. A selected annual cell does not change this independent service boundary.

This model establishes conditional physical and funding counterexamples. It does not choose between products using lifecycle cost, estimate whole-plant employment, qualify cart mobility, learn policies or bridge kits into the annual/monthly fleet. Those decisions require measured distributions, installed process constraints, complete support/rights and a genuinely matched accepted-service record. See [the decision analysis](../research/automotive_workcell_decision.md).
