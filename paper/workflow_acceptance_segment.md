## The complete-service acceptance hyperedge

A capability link does not guarantee an economic entry opportunity. In a warehouse, physical box manipulation must be combined with material handoffs, customer-specific order requirements, digital acknowledgement, exception recovery and saleable output. Inbound trailer unloading and mixed-case picking can use the same robotic substrate while requiring distinct accepted services. For the former, moving cartons to a conveyor does not necessarily complete receiving: scanner orientation, sortation and downstream capacity may remain binding. For the latter, multiple correct case selections and placements plus a WMS-acknowledged pallet form a complementary order-completion bundle. A case-per-hour statistic is not an order-per-hour statistic.

Let a target service require component successes A_1,...,A_k. With known marginal probabilities s_i on the same target trial distribution, complete-service probability g=P(intersection A_i) lies in the sharp interval [max(0,sum s_i-(k-1)), min s_i]. The upper bound follows by inclusion; the lower follows from the union bound on component failures, and both can be reached by suitable dependent failure patterns. With three hypothetical legs each having 95% marginal success, g can range from 85% to 95%; independent legs yield (19/20)^3=85.7375%. This is an established probability fact, not an original statistical theorem. Its economic implication is that even perfect observation of marginal competence can fail to identify the return to a complete service.

Suppose customer final-output revenue at expected accepted quantity q=gA is a*q-b*q²/2, with A attempted bundles, b>0, attempted cost cA and manual rework cost h(A-q). Then gross joint operating profit is [(a+h)g-(c+h)]A-bg²A²/2. The optimal attempted quantity for g>0 is min(K,max(0,[(a+h)g-(c+h)]/(bg²))). The provider pays fixed qualification F if its captured share rho of incremental surplus over the customer's best feasible alternative Pi0 is worth more than F. In the synthetic capacity-binding illustration a=10,b=2,c=1,h=2,K=4,Pi0=16,rho=1,F=2, strict entry occurs if g>(6-sqrt(6))/4≈0.8876. Identical 95% marginal competence is therefore consistent with both entry and no entry; a higher destination-demand intercept can also change the decision without any technical improvement. The revenue function here is applied to expected accepted output rather than expected revenue under realized-output uncertainty.

DHL's dated May 2025 release reports operational Stretch unloading and a planned case-picking expansion; Boston Dynamics' presently accessible product pages market both workflows, including case-picking WMS integration. Gap's unloading report makes a separate downstream scan requirement visible. These sources establish contrasting workflow and product relationships, not a matched source-to-target order-completion distribution or qualification bill. The next paper test must identify the joint target acceptance, retraining/validation investment, commercial customer alternative, collection rights and financing sequence. A high handling rate, an MOU and an advertised feature cannot jointly calibrate that counterfactual.
