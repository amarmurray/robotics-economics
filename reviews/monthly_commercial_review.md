## Deliberate choices and limitations

A settled accepted-unit shipment may fund a later start in the same phase. This deliberately extends the earlier design's conservative exclusion, with explicit event order and a separate counterexample showing that the future sale cannot fund its own manufacture. No external transaction atomicity is claimed. Failed multi-payment candidates are not committed; the returned commercial event records the failed group, while actual Treasury cash remains unchanged.

Lease rent is unconditional while the asset remains installed; service fees require actual support. Both availability fees include the commissioning month without fee proration, despite the quarter output ramp. Commercial default applies within a contract, with the shared physical engine's conservative existing-support priority. There is no automatic cross-default, repossession, recovery, write-off or full-company mandatory-payment scheduler. Deposits are security liabilities and never purchase credit. Cancellation is deliberately restricted to before shipment/installation; attempts outside that boundary fail without changing the supplied Treasury.

The resource subtotal is bundled vendor cash for included physical actions, not a complete lifecycle economic cost. Supplier finance/fixed costs, interest, taxes, residual assets, demand competition, customer acquisition, human payroll, enterprise formation and the annual/entrant/K-architecture bridge remain incomplete. W1-CASH stays partial. Positive end balances, an owned robot or a receivable must not be described as observed profit or financeability of a real deployment.
