## Transfer, arithmetic and publication boundary

The joint cost contrasts are internally consistent: robotics saving against the improved human baseline is `C01 − C11`; total joint saving is `C00 − C11`; `C11 − C10 − C01 + C00 < 0` represents complementary cost reduction. These are accounting contrasts, not causal estimates. Required embedded software is held fixed across the robot row. There is no duplicate addition of the joint benefit to the component benefits in the reviewed contract.

The analogy cards separate source evidence from target evidence and prohibit numerical transfers for the checked headline results. The infrastructure narrative also distinguishes commodity or supplier-level totals from contract-accessible qualified inputs. No source here establishes a robot OEM's chip allocation, a humanoid's required future memory, a fleet coordination gain, or a reduction in full data-center project duration from robotic work.

The first milestone may publish these results with the current scope language. It must not certify all H requirements complete merely because the record counts are present.
