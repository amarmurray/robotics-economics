The lead should retain these qualifications when recording statuses.

The review read the report chapters, evidence packages, model methods/code, dashboard implementation, reproduction instructions, roadmap and source/model/browser reviews. It relies on the independent source-review dispositions rather than mechanically fetching their URLs again. ## What is established at the cutoff

The frozen first-wave packages contain 96 evidence records across six files, 15 market families, six deeper cells across six markets, 15 configured-system records, two customer/commercial comparisons, eight H analogy tests, three H direct-contribution maps, six I/J openness cards, six field dossiers and five physical transfer tests. The current model preserves those corrections. All eight saved scenario exports carry the inspected engine SHA-256 `5ff697ba757c04edf75bda597867e1b17e4b7e854b04210148bf8ed693bd9ea0`. The lead reports exact recomputation of all eight exports, exact agreement of eight report summary rows, 15 passing owner tests, a passing bounded evidence validator and JavaScript syntax check. These are separate receipts, not checks this acceptance reviewer claims to have run.

The generated [report](../REPORT.md) integrates the seven research chapters, including substantial H/I/J content and strategic implications. Its approximately 23,000 words exceed the specification's 8,000–15,000-word guidance; the handoff discloses this. Editorial compression would improve use, but preserving the substantive evidence is preferable to treating length alone as a research failure. [Methods](../model/METHODS.md) and [reproduction instructions](../REPRODUCE.md) are present. Earlier review notes about missing docs/stale exports describe a prior implementation snapshot; the checks above supersede those particular pending items. ## Highest-value continuation work

1. **Obtain one matched operating denominator before expanding the company list.** Prioritize hospital and utility cohorts: accepted output and deadlines, exposure, interventions including duration, review/repeat visits, maintenance and all human work. Keep failed/urgent attempts. Stop at a reproducible cohort or an explicit public disclosure gap; this resolves whether support and workflow integration dominate after navigation succeeds.
2. **Complete an evidence-backed configured comparison.** For one repeatable utility or cleaning service, define the same frequency, quality and response interval across human-plus-digital, simpler automation and mobile alternatives. Recover configuration, integration, support, utilization and contractual retention costs. A price quote or mission total alone cannot rank embodiments.
3. **Connect finance only after preserving the existing accounting invariants.** Extend the strongest entrant case into commissioning/resource reservations and a dated treasury, or preserve it as a separate exercise. Reconcile replacement, receivables, overhead and acquisition cash with funding/retention; do not add its output to the six-cell model before sharing its constraints.
4. **Finish benchmark reproducibility and the moving digital comparator.** Recover exact model/harness/action budgets, failure-inclusive costs, intervention and latency boundaries for the consequential H/BDE benchmarks. Add a separately controlled time path for human-side digital improvement; do not map a professional-task or simulated-agent coefficient into physical service without target evidence.
5. **Trace qualified supply and rights to an actual configuration.** Inspect compatible embedded compute/memory, delivered allocations, qualification and spare/service dependencies, then one non-Spot operator cohort. Commit-pinned permissive code is useful evidence of access; commercial manufacturing, data reuse and uptime are separate questions.
6. **Close the data/UI audit gaps.** Validate the declared evidence contract and typed join dimensions, then add the missing substantive filters and result-specific lineage. Preserve explicit reference scopes. These changes improve inspection; they should not manufacture numerical observations where the source remains silent.

These actions are ranked by decision consequence and dependency, not by the number of missing records. A broad employment atlas, a large vendor list and functioning controls cannot compensate for an absent accepted-output/support denominator. Conversely, a null physical-hours field, adverse source finding or failed analogy is an acceptable bounded research result when its implication and next test are explicit. The milestone can be published with these limitations after the lead completes the publication check; the campaign remains in progress through its original deadline.
