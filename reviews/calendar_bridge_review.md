## Boundary challenges

1. **Unit mismatch:** the bridge requires the new work identity and a one-to-one assumed cleaning-zone mapping. Changing both physical and market IDs back to the historical bundle is rejected. No employment, hospital delivery, component or inspection measurement is converted.
2. **Calendar versus elapsed year:** July–February consumes six months of 2026 levels and two of 2027 levels. Annual path arrays are validated in full. No interpolation, annualized partial-year service or retroactive output is introduced.
3. **Double capacity/ramp:** the annual function already includes uptime/intervention/coordination. Actual cohort ramp applies once. The mechanical ceiling is separate from effective accepted capacity, and annual fleet stock is never added.
4. **Software assigned without qualification:** a changed robot signature needs funded qualification and resources before vintage advances. Frozen cohorts remain frozen. Equal robot levels do not incur qualification solely for a new calendar date; a price change alone does not invent a technical upgrade.
5. **Partial effects on failed booking:** physical support/commissioning, applicable first rent, digital subscription and upgrade charges evaluate on an immutable Treasury candidate. A one-cent software shortfall leaves no paid physical booking, consumed hours or changed vintage. Already completed shipping and fixed staff/site obligations remain separate.
6. **Payroll confused with task time:** monthly capacity must match the declared staff cash, wage, hours/zone and dated digital level. An arbitrary capacity increase at unchanged staffing is rejected. Scheduled staff invoices and actual paid current-period cash remain distinct; payroll removal is null.
7. **Costs counted twice:** catalog manufacturing applies per actual start, with no second yield divisor. Physical operation excludes the added digital bundle before the latter is billed. Digital/upgrade supplier receipts reconcile to payments already included in enterprise outlay. Principal/interest preserve their financing classification.
8. **Unstated resources:** initial integration and ongoing support/upgrade pools are distinct, bounded allocations of annual reference resources. Other annual markets are not simultaneously allocated the same stock/pool. Monthly synthetic component bundles have no inferred actuator/board conversion.
9. **Unsupported cost mapping:** saved physical cost/configuration changes that violate the declared annual mapping fail visibly. The underlying monthly booking requires positive integration cost; a zero-integration annual premise is rejected rather than silently replaced with a one-cent charge.
