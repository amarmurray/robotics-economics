## What was challenged

It exhausts the finite currently observed queue, can wait and replan, and uses the same funded admission/resource/deadline guard as synthetic proposal variants. The independent oracle enumerates Cartesian alternatives with set-valued occupancy, without using planner feasibility helpers. Forty seeded three-request worlds match the objective and deterministic optimal action set. A state-budget overrun returns no optimality claim. Hidden future arrivals/outages do not alter the earlier information or decision prefix.

Nineteen focused tests also cover retained paid staff, mandatory misses hidden by equal aggregate throughput, label-only invariance, latency and urgent fast path, added overhead, failed-work costs, funded/unfunded recovery, capacity failure without free service, malformed/duplicate/stale proposals, atomic batch cash rejection, exact saved-input replay and cash/resource/deadline/identity conservation. In-flight malformed packets were hardened to reserve nothing and fail visibly. Treasury service-tick/transaction-index mapping was made explicit; issue precedes payment, and no financial-month interpretation is imported.

The twelve reference worlds remain synthetic. Planning/verification capacity prices include the optional urgent path and are not measured per-call runtime costs. The mandatory flag is not a clinical priority rule. Local intent and authoritative completion/stop assumptions do not demonstrate distributed durability, RMF interoperability or physical acceptance. Window cash excludes asset lifecycle costs. A separate exact courier scheduler does not correct the earlier fixed monthly investment policy automatically.
