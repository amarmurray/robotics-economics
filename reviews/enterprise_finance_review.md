# Dated enterprise finance review — edition 0.14

Disposition: accepted as a bounded synthetic mechanism checkpoint. Review is by the sole writer under the single-agent instruction, not an independent audit. The active campaign remains in progress through 14 November 2026; fourteen worlds cover only DE-CF11/12, not all required counterfactual families.

## Change and evidence boundary

`model/enterprise_finance.py` adds a policy to the existing integer monthly physical, commercial, customer and Treasury execution. The four underlying model modules remain unchanged. Term loans have funded counterparties, a scheduled draw, an exact monthly interest convention, maturity and explicit repayment/default/cure rules. Later start candidates face actual available cash and a declared reserve. No principal invoice duplicates the Treasury credit claim. Recovery is a cash payment; it neither sells the leased asset nor erases unpaid debt.

The reference case and thirteen alternatives are invented contracts in `WORK-FLOOR-BUNDLE-SYN-02`. They are not target financing quotes, legal priorities or hospital/utility/automotive cost estimates. No new source claim or empirical calibration is added. Existing evidence, assumption values, six-cell definitions and question statuses are preserved.

## Challenges and resolutions

- **Maturity hidden beyond the horizon.** Month-12 maturity preserves more service in the eight-month window and retains the full principal claim. It is not debt forgiveness or proof of sustainability.
- **Debt paid but service unavailable.** $5,300 additional upfront equity clears the reference principal, yet leaves final support unfunded. $5,399.99 leaves $99.99 cash; the $100 full-or-nothing booking fails. $5,400 supplies the same installed unit and demand with support, adding 30 delivered bundles. This is an exact fixed-policy boundary, not an optimal capitalization claim.
- **Recovery mistaken for value creation.** Operations-first and debt-first rules recover different principal while total Treasury cash remains conserved. Claims, unpaid invoices and existing title survive the difference. A lessor owns the entrant's leased robot; the loan policy does not transfer it to the working-capital lender.
- **Cure silently assumed to restore service.** The funded month-5 rescue repays debt under both cure rules. Only the declared restart policy resumes output. Existing contractual overhead/lease obligations are not automatically cancelled by a permanent operational stop.
- **Money confused with capacity.** With a month-2 draw, the earlier three shipments go to the incumbent, whose five installed units then occupy support capacity. Later shipment attempts fail future support availability. The model does not retroactively reallocate that capacity. A sixth funded start also consumes $36,000 without adding service within this horizon.
- **Loan IDs and multiple actors.** Interest invoices are tracked explicitly by loan; IDs sharing a prefix cannot contaminate another loan's totals. A defaulted lessor/provider cannot admit new funded activity merely because the operating customer is solvent. The browser aggregates the tracked actor's loans rather than displaying just the first one, and distinguishes those rows from all-loan headline totals.
- **Replay and invalid controls.** The API accepts exactly one shortcut-input or complete-case payload. Complete replay reconstructs inputs and ignores saved outcomes. Invalid controls retain the displayed/exported case; request sequence IDs reject late responses. Imported ledgers disclose their own unit/horizon and remain fully exportable.

Two initial test expectations were corrected during development: a full-or-nothing principal witness can retain cash at maturity and spend it on later obligations, so its assertion now inspects the maturity month; a lessor default witness must include the lessor's actual rental receipts before claiming insolvency. Those were test-fixture corrections. A browser assertion was corrected to compare numeric service rather than require two displayed decimal places. No financial output was changed merely to meet an expected number.

## Remaining work

No collateral sale, repossession, asset transport/requalification, recovery waterfall, restructuring or legal insolvency result is modeled. Interest is a synthetic fixed monthly rate on opening principal, not a market APR or daily convention. Lender funds are exogenous opening accounts; real credit availability and lender solvency are not inferred. Declared later start candidates are not autonomous investment optimization. Principal maturity outside the horizon is visible but is not simulated past that horizon.

The annual/monthly work-unit, calendar, cohort and intelligence-architecture bridges remain pending. Acquisition/hybrid transitions, substantial H's strong conventional comparator and the other twelve challenge families are still required. The three decision packs retain missing matched whole-system costs, avoidable payroll, support, supply and rights. This checkpoint establishes a mechanism that can reverse service outcomes; it does not establish empirical ROI or full campaign acceptance.
